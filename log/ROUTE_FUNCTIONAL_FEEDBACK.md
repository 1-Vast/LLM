## Route synthesis

### 1. R4–R68: exploratory identifiability and transfer framing

The early rounds established the question and a family of nested comparisons: whether drug/pathway/target annotations, cell identity, and partial history could improve prediction or decision rules under entity-held-out splits. These rounds generated useful decomposition and candidate estimands, but many early statistics were descriptive or resource-specific. They should be read through later claim-status corrections rather than as independent discoveries. The route never established a transferable causal or biological updater.

### 2. R69–R127: reproducible bounded signal, with strict scope

The retained synthesis reports a stable route-level pattern: pathway-level grouping generally outperformed finer target grouping, and adding target labels on top of pathway labels supplied little or no incremental gain. The gain attenuated when pathway levels were unseen; coverage is a threshold-like condition in this resource, not a tunable “more rows always helps” law. Identity/annotation effects were measured under specific splits and model families, with ordinary baselines retained as the primary comparison.

The strongest usable statement is conditional: within the inspected resource and split contracts, pathway-informed priors can improve ordinary held-out prediction when relevant pathway levels are represented in training. It does not establish mechanism, novelty, broad cross-resource transfer, or a generally useful Agent updater. Route reports also record that several broad early interpretations were later withdrawn or narrowed.

### 3. R128–R188: audit, correction, and external-resource gate

This phase converted the exploratory route into auditable evidence.

- **Replication unit correction (R128):** the 3.04% figure is a BATCHIE PAIR same-cell × directed-drug-pair cross-layout hash proportion, not a cross-plate or biological-repeat rate; the 42.8% overlap comes from a different Jaaks GDSC resource. File multiplicity is repeat opportunity, not independent culture.
- **Criterion/metric corrections (R129–R130):** Boolean synergy and ΔEMAX are distinct criteria; four continuous Δ metrics are highly correlated readings of one endpoint. Earlier “redundant criteria” wording was withdrawn.
- **Model-tuning and uncertainty corrections (R156–R164):** fixed λ=1 results were not absolute effect claims after inner-CV showed λ≈54 for the feature count; arm-to-arm rankings were retained where valid, while single-arm magnitudes require λ disclosure. Repeated split intervals cannot be treated as independent task-population CIs when tuning occurs inside rows.
- **Invalid or non-diagnostic executions:** R104, R108, and R113 are explicitly invalid runs (zero-difference/zero-width or saturated-decision signatures); their numbers must not be cited. Feature/effect audit utilities were added after several failure modes, but an audit gate passing does not prove that the scientific question was answerable.
- **External replication gate:** the route required a second resource with connectable identities, at least coarse annotation levels, enough held-out cell×pair units, a non-saturated endpoint, and no entity leakage. ComboCat failed the structural gate. DrugComb v1.4 was structurally promising, but R191’s annotation/endpoint/power pre-gates were not all sufficient; R192’s stage-2 execution was `not_replicated` with a primary CI crossing zero and proxy sensitivity. This is not a universal negative; it is failure to qualify the specified replication route.

The result is a bounded internal observation and a precise re-use contract, not an externally validated algorithm.

### 4. R189–R202: innovation rounds and closure of the feedback route

The innovation rounds are a continuation chain, not fourteen independent studies.

- **R189 / I003:** the proposed decision-focused acquisition constraint was stopped by independent challenge. Its score was rank-equivalent to ordinary decision value under fixed r, its MDE terms were not operationalized, and prior work already covered the acquisition structure. No effect experiment ran.
- **R190–R192:** the cell-state line stopped as a novelty claim. Signal and room existed, but the first MDE statement was corrected (fold-mean 0.240 became a five-fold endpoint MDE 0.112); power/design and target coverage failed before a fair replication. “Qualification” was explicitly separated from replication.
- **R193–R195:** the conditional-shift Q route was tested as an information object. R193-A1 was `inconclusive` at the power gate; R194 found a small condition main-effect share (1.43%) while condition×cell interaction and measurement noise dominated. R195 was `not_qualified` under its large-resource gate because shared cells and cross-study units were insufficient. Later review narrowed these claims: the 1.43% number is only an additive main-effect share, not a general low-rank or feedback result.
- **R196–R197:** audit-level reliability and low-rank structure v2 were both `inconclusive`; C130 was neither externally confirmed nor overturned. R200-D1 then showed that the Q table was 96.735% algebraically redundant and that random holdout could be reconstructed from the same closure, so the earlier 0.7206 cannot be treated as out-of-closure structure evidence.
- **R199–R202:** the condition functional-replacement qualification remained `not_qualified`/data-insufficient because of missing independent endpoints, legal exposure, independent culture/batch and non-reaggregated lineage. O’Neil development work was conditional and internal only; ON3 v1.2 ended `no_increment` with a CI whose upper bound slightly exceeded the practical threshold, and it was non-blind/peeked. R200-D3 and R201-A stopped on precision/null-scale design failures, not biological negatives. R202 external resource qualification was revised after an export key bug: AZ/GDSC technical-repeat structure is recoverable descriptively, but official occasion, independent culture, and numeric orthogonal-function mapping remain unavailable.

The route therefore closes with a useful stop rule: seek a genuinely independent, identity-connectable, non-saturated, independently repeated functional resource; do not continue changing thresholds, aggregations, or model variants on the same resource and call that transfer validation.

## Ordinary functional-feedback route (2026-10-28–30)

The later ordinary-functional episodes operationalized the route on public GDSC/PRISM-like exports. Their reports converge on these findings:

- **Observation and dose structure:** metadata and dose keys can be reconstructed, but repeated exported rows are not independent cultures. The fixed GDSC007 projection contains many duplicate/technical opportunities; source wells, true preparation, and physical cost are unresolved.
- **Local relation and dose-sharing candidates:** local interaction models did not beat the strong additive baseline on the frozen held-out drug tasks (`A−L = -0.0118770`); this specific gain line stopped. Dose-structure candidates remain conditional questions, with no core promotion.
- **Functional index and assay relation:** the H_FI training advantage gate failed before development scoring; relation-identification arithmetic was conditionally valid but reproducible by ordinary joint models and did not establish a new algorithm or drug-effect recommendation.
- **Common-assay and paid-feedback routes:** synthetic/metadata comparisons were computationally consistent but did not demonstrate a real decision gain, independent biological validation, or an advantage over ordinary baselines. The fixed feedback selection lines were stopped under their pre-set gates.
- **Observation pilot:** a small curvature difference was reproduced across implementations (`Δ≈0.000691` with row-block quantiles), but it is a computational/conditional result, not a biological CI or a transferable mechanism.
- **Common-screen co-learning:** a conditional full-history/active-drug graph boundary remained coherent, but ordinary signed-graph/direct-assay rivals and qualified joint-exposure data were missing. A constructed test refuted an unrestricted plain time-exponential formula; it did not validate co-learning biology.
- **Functional rule/context/mechanism routes:** source-owned context and recurrence objects support scoped questions, while static-mixture inequalities and missing exposure/clone/time bridges prevent acquired-memory, transport, or mechanism claims. Multiple routes are parked or stopped pending data contracts.
- **2026-10-30 episodes:** cheap-screen joint learning is exploratory negative/parked; paired functional resource R2 is `data_unavailable`; functional-action principles remain a conditional design. None promotes a core method.

Across this route, “metadata-only,” “data-unavailable,” “design-invalid,” “inconclusive,” “not-qualified,” and “no-increment” are distinct outcomes. None should be rewritten as a biological negative, and none should be upgraded to a positive transfer or novelty claim.
