# Program revision

Pre-registered study on PRISM 19Q4 HTS002, frozen after Stage A (source development data only) and
before the development arms and any future outcome; `verify.py` VERIFIED. Not an independent
confirmation: the future strata come from the same screen.

* **Question.** Does MAESTRO's agent improve future decisions by revising a shared library of
  executable, observably gated response programs on top of a strong low-rank model? No.
* **Prior knowledge helps.** The pre-specified 10-program seed library gives +0.21 hits/line with 8
  probe wells, +0.45 with none and +0.44 in exploit-first campaigns, most on unseen lineages.
* **Revision does not.** Editable minus fixed library is 0.000; stepwise search +0.008; LLM vs
  enumeration +0.004; revision-added programs have ablation value <= 0; joint measurement edits
  changed nothing. About 4,300 development wells per arm are never repaid.
* **Why (post hoc).** The within-lineage development arena rarely contains the gates that pay in
  new lineages, so the frozen rule would reject most useful seed programs. The LLM committed the
  wrong direction for 20-23% of candidates.
* **Transfer.** Seed programs transfer to unseen lineages; shared loadings do not transfer to unseen
  drug structure groups.
* **Campaigns.** Exploit-first feedback beats 8 up-front probes (-0.38 for probes); AFA beats a
  fixed design (+0.22) but needs 10 rounds.
* **Cost.** 0 purchased wells, 0.287 USD deepseek-flash.
* **Next.** Lineage-disjoint revision with curated proposals, confirmed once on PRISM 24Q2 REP.1M
  after authorisation, against the seed library.
