# Context transfer with verified bridges

One shot on Beat AML 2.0 (520 primary AML samples, 479 patients, 16 archived AUC queries, about
112 treatment wells): **CONFIRMED** under the frozen claims, but narrow. The label records protocol
execution; the claims amendment (`CLAIMS.json`) supports only the functional-feedback gain under
the replay. Attribution: `feedback_attribution_20261017`.

* The source-verified agent chose exploit-first bridging in 5/5 folds: 0.850 hits per sample.
  That is +0.073 over the Virtual Cell alone (Holm p .0075), +0.229 over pilot matching and
  +0.108 over the PRISM development procedure carried over unchanged.
* The basal-expression prior beat nearest-context transfer (+0.075) but not the item prior
  (−0.008).
* The agent matched exploit-first and did not beat it (P2 0, P5 trivial).
* The pre-registered expectation was PARTIAL.
* The LLM bridge picker gave no gain (0.2768 USD).
* Post hoc: the rejected lookahead had a greedy-batch baseline defect that uneven measurement
  exposes. The corrected rule ties exploit on Beat AML. On PRISM 19Q4 the correction is neutral
  (+0.025 line, −0.046 lineage holdout).
* The correction is development-grade.
