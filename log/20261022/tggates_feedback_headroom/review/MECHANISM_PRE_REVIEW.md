## Falsifiability and what the headroom gate proves

The numerical gate is deliberately weaker than the scientific claim: it asks whether the best simple/strong feedback baseline leaves even an arithmetically possible development error reduction of the chosen absolute size. If it leaves little, current CEL/state-stack investment is routed away from this exact application. If it leaves more, RNA contribution is merely possible. A high baseline error could be irreducible assay variation, proxy chemistry mismatch, solubility/prescreen design or missing context. Do not call PASS evidence for separable molecular/functional innovations.

The0.02 and10% requirements are engineering choices, not assay-noise or clinical thresholds. The10% requirement cannot salvage a floor-impossible configuration. With baseline error b, perfect prediction offers b improvement; the future minimum would be max(0.02,0.1b). This screen need not estimate a universal noise floor from two repeated individuals. Source/development estimates cannot prove what happens in eight unopened later proxy groups. A wide interval or source/development disagreement stays unresolved; no post-opening threshold rescue.

For the future scientific test, ordinary full-history joint conditioning with the exact same block constraints/training is one implementation of two-innovation repair in the linear case. Its algebraic identity must remain visible. Strong ordinary auxiliary-RNA direct/joint and cross-block shrinkage get complete source late-function/RNA supervision and actual Middle RNA/function. Specific removal/paired-structure permutation of molecular and conditional-function information can establish their empirical contributions; names or a smaller comparison set cannot.
