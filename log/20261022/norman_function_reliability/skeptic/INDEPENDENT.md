## Meaning and failure conditions

Positive reproducible residual structure means a conventional predictor misses a stable component in this screen conditional on its training information. It does not identify biological causality, noise variance, an oracle error floor, a required latent state, or a necessary world model. Two replicates cannot separate shared technical artifacts from biological signal. Residual agreement alone can reflect underfit baseline or finite-training prediction error. Conversely poor agreement or a nonpositive cross-product is inconclusive about learnable biological structure when support is weak or replicate scaling differs.

Identity/mask/scale failures stop the comparison; failed source reconstruction stops linkage to previous scores. Too few common observations produces NOT_IDENTIFIABLE, not a favorable subset chosen by agreement. Large replicate offsets require reporting the original scale; do not learn a corrective transform on held-out pairs. Never select low-disagreement pairs, promising target modules or reliability weights after inspecting held-out outcomes. Report both reliable and discordant support without promoting the better subgroup.
