## What two screens establish

Replicate disagreement describes screen-to-screen instability under shared guide design, preprocessing and endpoint definition. Agreement can reveal stable signal, but shared guide efficacy, off-target effects, normalization bias or selection artifacts can reproduce as well as biology. Multiple guide pairs are not independent biological screens, and pair observations sharing genes are not independent statistical units. RNA cell representation is not an independent replicate count for the bulk growth assay.

Even under the additional model Y_r = mu + epsilon_r with zero-mean independent errors, mean squared replicate difference estimates the sum of error variances; dividing by two assumes equal variances and dividing by four for average-label variance additionally assumes the average endpoint is their equal arithmetic mean. These assumptions are not identified by two replicates. Correlated errors invalidate those conversions; per-pair variance estimates from two observations are especially unstable. Neither a global correlation nor disagreement supplies an oracle ceiling, unique biological/noise decomposition, or evidence that the direct model has reached irreducible error.
