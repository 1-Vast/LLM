# Strongest positive interpretation: use simulations for intervention decisions under biological mismatch

2026-10-22. This addendum preserves the initial seal. Prepared before receiving the new biological reviewer's position. It responds to the updated broad goal; the five-site cardiac selector is not treated as the scope of MAESTRO.

**HYPOTHESIS, not a promoted mechanism:** MetaGenSim supplies a useful operational distinction between estimating the true environment and learning which simulated experience improves a real decision policy. Drug-discovery models are often wrong in details while still useful for ordering interventions. A biological translation could exploit that distinction to learn across drug-switch/washout tasks without requiring a globally correct virtual cell. This is stronger than using a synthetic model to generate more regression labels. The opportunity is real in principle; the current translation has not established a new algorithm or a qualified asset.

## The biological information constraint

Consider choosing the second treatment after an initial drug exposure. Two populations can have similar current cell numbers yet differ in reversible adaptation, delayed damage or selected resistant composition. Different histories can therefore reverse the ranking of continuation, washout, and a second drug. Real treatment branches are expensive and destructive assays may consume the population; imaging may be incomplete and selection can change which cells remain observable. A drug-discovery policy needs the *future functional contrast between actions*, not merely a plausible current image, a hidden-state reconstruction, or accurate average growth.

The 2026 MetaGenSim source optimizes a simulator sampling distribution through real validation performance after training a policy. Its useful operation is allowing imperfect synthetic environments to teach decisions when the weights of those environments are externally corrected. It does not require that the learned parameter distribution be a posterior over the physical truth. The corresponding biological use would train a small intervention policy on simulated adaptation/selection/damage scenarios, then adjust which scenarios it trains on using measured functional outcomes from distinct real experiments. After such selection, the retained simulation curriculum could train or improve a later policy on new drug families or independent populations.

Why this is not merely a new interpretation of CiPA: the proposed decision depends on an action-conditioned temporal continuation, histories that collide in currently measured endpoints, and biologically different continuation responses. Its public-source leads are sequence/step-response studies already authenticated elsewhere in the project. A useful minimal instance need not claim a mechanistic explanation of resistance or demand full cell-lineage reconstruction, but it must genuinely evaluate an intervention decision.

## What extra observations resolve the exact counterexample

The two-world construction in the initial review is deliberately unconstrained: both worlds agree on all observed support and disagree on an unobserved query. There are at least two legitimate ways to remove that ambiguity for a specified decision.

1. **Measure a separating continuation.** On exchangeable populations with the same initial conditions and history, observe the functional response to continuation and an alternative action. With noise and biological variation, independent experimental runs estimate the action contrast and its uncertainty. One perfect repeated cell or deterministic reset is not required. The claim is conditional on valid assignment, measured intervention identity, carryover/handling controls and the population to which the contrast applies. These outcomes constrain both worlds, so their opposite action values can no longer both agree with the available evidence if the contrast is identified with sufficient precision.
2. **Use an independently justified shared structure.** For a simple linearized model with support observations A_S beta and a decision contrast c^T beta, the contrast is identifiable without identifying all beta precisely when c lies in the row space of A_S. Equivalently, every null direction of A_S is also a null direction of the decision contrast. For nonlinear models, require that all environments consistent with observed data agree on the relevant action ranking, or obtain an intervention that distinguishes environments with different rankings. This is classical decision identification/experimental design. It is a useful prerequisite, not this project's novel mechanism.

The additional structure must be biologically and empirically qualified. Examples include independently validated response composition over adjacent exposure intervals, stable measurement calibration, or replicated shared drug-response parameters across related histories. Simply asserting a low-dimensional state, fitting a smooth trajectory, or generating a synthetic outcome does not add identifying information. A violated semigroup property can also reflect changing observation/assay definitions rather than biological memory. Drug switches need not commute; washout is not an inverse.

The goal is not to rule out every latent alternative. It is to identify enough about action contrasts that uncertainty changes a real choice. Full dynamics identification is unnecessarily strong when all admissible models recommend the same intervention. Conversely, good prefix prediction is insufficient if admissible continuations recommend opposite actions.

## What would make a world model useful and its agent role substantive

The world contribution would be tested on a concrete capability: use observed prefixes and *new action compositions* to predict how functional outcomes change under a second intervention, enabling better choices at the same real-measurement budget. Such value cannot be inferred from synthetic rollout accuracy or from a baseline that receives only the last endpoint. Compare an equally informed direct history-and-schedule predictor, ordinary mechanistic/state-space models, a fitted drug-by-time/schedule interaction model, and direct policy learning. The fact that a sufficiently general direct predictor can represent the same mapping is not an empirical disproof of a useful world-model factorization. The factorization must actually deliver better generalization, decision quality or measured cost efficiency under fair comparisons.

The agent would choose which continuation experiment most reduces a consequential ambiguity, or which admissible intervention to deploy after feedback. It must beat fixed schedules and ordinary active/decision-focused design supplied the same model family and information. Reusing MetaGenSim's sample reweighting to improve an intervention policy is a real change of training object, but still close to ordinary bilevel policy optimization. A genuine new contribution would require a specific rule that handles biological features the original mechanism cannot—such as irrecoverable population consumption or intervention-dependent censoring—with a demonstrated advantage over their established statistical/control treatments. No such rule has yet been derived here.

A promising scientific output would be a previously unsupported and independently checked treatment-order or washout benefit with a reproducible boundary of applicability. The model could help discover it without claiming that it identified the molecular mechanism. A public-data replay may test prospective-style selection of held-out measured effects; it cannot certify a previously unknown biological discovery if those effects and treatment schedules were already published and used in development. Independent discovery return must be reported separately from retrospective policy utility.

## Inheritance and open-ended improvement

The inherited object could be a simulator-experience selector that learns which synthetic tasks produce better intervention policies, together with its validation history. To test Level 3, freeze earlier and later selectors and let each improve the same starting policy under matched simulation, real-feedback and search budgets on independent later tasks. Compare fixed randomization, fixed MetaGenSim-style bilevel weighting, ordinary warm starts/continual learning, an equally informed direct gain selector, and equal-budget search. Reusing a better policy or a larger log is not sufficient.

A fixed simulator family and finite schedule menu test bounded transfer, not open-ended evolution. New intervention modules or admissible scientific hypotheses would have to enlarge the meaningful task/capability set while preserving real external checks. Merely generating longer schedules, adding arbitrary latent states or branching an archive is not evidence of that enlargement. The first experiment should not attempt open-ended growth before its biological decision signal and bounded transfer work.

**Current disposition:** retain this as a positive structural opportunity, not a candidate cleared for fitting. It explains what evidence would break the residual counterexample and why action-conditioned dynamics might provide practical decision value. It also exposes the missing independent biological action contrasts and unresolved novelty. No new fit, protected outcome access, author contact or source acquisition was performed for this addendum. Await independent biological challenge before any further proposal or implementation.
