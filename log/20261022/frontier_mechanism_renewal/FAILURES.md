- The original acquisition helper parsed Atom XML with BeautifulSoup's HTML parser and emitted XMLParsedAsHTMLWarning. Exact body retention and ElementTree search parsing were unaffected. The helper now selects the XML parser when the response content-type is XML. Historical raw bodies and receipts are retained unchanged.
- The targeted Test-Time Training End-to-End phrase query returned zero entries. This is a search failure, not evidence that no such work exists.
- Broad searches are truncated to the newest 12 results each. They favor recent October 2026 work and are not comprehensive novelty searches. Irrelevant returned entries remain in SEARCH_RESULTS.json.
- CrossFit search metadata refers to v2 while the pinned full-text acquisition is v1; claims are based on the retained v1. Nested Learning has an author-stated earlier conference version. No earliest-publication claim is inferred from arXiv alone.
- Initial h1 extraction for older arXiv abstracts selected subject headings; abstract text was valid. Final identifiers/titles use citation_title metadata, not that header.
- The residual nonidentification example is a rational arithmetic construction. It cannot establish empirical biology, broad impossibility, lack of information in structured histories, or failure of learned optimization.
- No discovered translation clears a new algorithmic contribution plus indispensable biological decision capability. This is a scoped NO_PROMOTION result, not completion of the full goal.
