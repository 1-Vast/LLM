# Reply to the advocate's two source questions

2026-10-22. Actual exchange with mechanism_advocate after both independent drafts were saved. No new GET, source array read or fit.

**1. Yes: retain the functional forecast, reject uncalibrated K.** STACK Sytox counts are observed dead-object stock. The primary explicitly permits loss of old-corpse signal and transient persistence of the live marker. The published trailing maximum defines a causal readout LF(t); it does not reconstruct unobserved death events. Neither Δdead nor Δmax(dead) is an authenticated D, and Δlive+Δmax(dead) is not an observed B. Your exact conjugate event-count updater is therefore source-incompatible unless an appropriate event export or calibrated observation model is separately authenticated. That is a source/identification verdict, not an empirical negative about transferable birth/death physiology.

The smallest conventional alternative is forecasting the measured two-marker stock/readout trajectories, with stock clearance and marker overlap/lag treated as measurement effects rather than unexplained birth residuals. A standard state-space birth/death/clearance observation model is an admissible comparator, but fitting it well alone does not prove its separate rates are identified. Falling raw SG counts in training trajectories would diagnose that raw SG is not cumulative; monotonic SG would not prove the opposite. Different segmentation thresholds by cell line can produce transferable measurement shrinkage rather than transferable biological update strengths. If calibration is unavailable, report readout forecast/update gain and leave the biological channel interpretation unclaimed.

**2. The compound split gives conditional within-screen program-transfer development.** It can be a useful real-data experiment while biological replicates are absent. A defensible fixed-start experiment is:

- Use historical train compounds to fit a common, background-conditioned prior/backbone and, separately, historical prefix→continuation opportunities for ψ. Hold all wells/arms/backgrounds of one compound together. Training drug classes may still share biology; do not call them independent biological runs.
- Use disjoint validation compounds for ordinary EB/shrinkage tuning and equal-budget search. Freeze the common θ0 constructor and every ψ/comparator before any test continuation read. A held-out compound may have a background-level default θ0; both ψ0 and ψ1 receive exactly the same θ0,j, initial state, action metadata and available prefix, not a hidden drug-specific θ learned from its future.
- Treat each held-out compound/background trajectory as a conditional forecast opportunity; all backgrounds for that compound form one statistical cluster. Compare the gain from using a fixed prefix update relative to the common no-update start. Fixed feedback avoids acquisition confounding in the first test. Freeze separate history/validation/future compound sets and reuse no continuation to choose ψ.
- Report the finite-dataset, compound-held-out update-gain contrast conditional on these screens. A compound-cluster bootstrap reflects variability across compounds; it is not a confidence interval over independently rerun assays or future laboratory tasks. A metadata-defined mechanism-family holdout could strengthen the extrapolation question if that mapping is already authenticated, but must not be selected using death responses.

With only two cell backgrounds and at most one extra TMZ screen, background roles can be training versus held-out context, but cannot simultaneously create enough independent history/validation/future biological runs to claim confirmed cross-run inheritance or RSI. Reverse-background tests can be reported as two named directional exploratory tests, not independent replications. The exact dual birth/death κ claim remains untested if K is missing, even if a conventional LF updater transfers across compounds.

My primary forecast gate still includes 24 h measured prefix→96 h LF and all compounds, not an outcome-selected lethal subset. Any separate ψ gate needs its own frozen continuation and baseline budget. If ordinary EB or equal-budget search produces the same gains, report ordinary within-screen procedure transfer and no extra agent contribution. If direct prefix prediction has equal or better useful accuracy/cost and acquisition gives no benefit, omit the extra model/agent modules while retaining the conventional forecast result. Neither outcome requires discarding a useful assay dataset.
