## Prefix and functional endpoint

The clinical-facing application is early triage of cytotoxic drug screens: predict 96 h lethality from a 24 h live/dead-count prefix, prioritizing later measurements of uncertain drug effects. This is population functional prediction under real compounds, without requiring RNA or a complete death lineage.

Use both marker channels, not the published outcome-selected set of 79 common lethal compounds or fit-success-only subset. On a common 4 h grid, the prefix is 0,4,...,24 h and the primary target is LF at 96 h. The primary scoring equation is LF(t) = max_{s<=t} dead(s) / [max_{s<=t} dead(s) + live(t)]. Its trailing maximum is causal at each t: do not normalize a 24 h prefix with a maximum from the full future trajectory. LFmax, AUC, fitted DO/DR and any 96 h-derived compound selection are forbidden prefix features. Missing/censored raw count values must remain explicit.

Segmentation and marker kinetics matter: mKate2 can persist briefly in dead cells and SG can disappear from old corpses. A latent birth/death world model must include an observation model or face a strong conventional kinetic baseline; a count conservation claim is not automatically valid. The original LED lag-exponential model is a necessary comparator and cannot be renamed as a new contribution.

## Conditional minimal actual-data gate

This is a proposed gate, not permission to read arrays now. Root should first authenticate a version-pinned raw object and identity-only schema, then freeze this protocol before a separately recorded response read. If the object has no raw time series, no stable compound/well/time mapping, or fewer than 400 usable compounds with both backgrounds and the specified prefix/96 h endpoint, stop this exact gate. Do not loosen the endpoint or filter to lethal compounds after viewing responses.

1. Establish one well/population trajectory as the observation unit; retain plate/run/background and compound identifiers. Keep all wells for a compound together. Determine whether true independent replicates exist; absent those, explicitly label the experiment within-screen development. Use all mapped eligible compounds, no selection on future death or successful LED fitting.
2. Freeze a deterministic SHA256 compound-ID split (60% train, 20% validation, 20% test), shared across both backgrounds and TMZ arms if present. This tests held-out compounds with available prefixes, not independent-run reproducibility or unseen-dose pharmacology. If genuine run identities are available, add a predeclared whole-run test rather than splitting replicate wells across sets.
3. Compare prefix persistence, regularized direct prefix regression, a tuned boosted-tree direct predictor, a pooled/regularized LED forecast fit using prefix-only observations, and a conventional birth/death/state-space measurement model. All receive the same prefix and authenticated treatment/context metadata. Use the same validation splits and at most 24 tuning trials per substantive method; allocate equal compute to a simple mixture and proposed world-model method. Strong direct models may use all prefix counts, slopes and time gaps. No preliminary method wins by withholding information from a baseline.
4. Primary score: compound-macro mean absolute error of 96 h LF. Promote only if the candidate beats the best validation-selected direct/kinetic baseline by both >=0.02 absolute LF MAE and >=10% relative MAE on the one frozen test; require the 95% compound-cluster bootstrap interval of the gain to exclude zero. Report low-death and high-death errors descriptively without using them to redefine success. If the useful-gain criterion fails, stop the candidate for this gate; no test retuning.
5. A second frozen exploratory feedback gate may simulate purchasing full trajectories of previously hidden training-pool compounds in batches of 16, total 128 acquisitions, five shared seeds. Predictions are assessed on a fixed disjoint compound test. Compare random purchase+ordinary pooling/refit, warm start, uncertainty active learning, a simple acquisition mixture and equal-budget program search. The offline oracle reveals only measurements that actually exist; it cannot answer arbitrary new doses or sequential interventions. Count one full existing trajectory as a bundle and charge equal revealed scalar data/fit cost. Report learning-curve area and final LF MAE. Do not call this adaptive physical experimentation.

The two screens alone cannot establish inherited agent competence across independent future research tasks. They can establish a real prefix forecast and a limited retrospective feedback benchmark. Separate world-model/agent/weight-or-procedure inheritance controls and an edited-improver psi0/psi1 future-opportunity test remain required before an RSI claim; this source's metadata does not make those requirements disappear.
