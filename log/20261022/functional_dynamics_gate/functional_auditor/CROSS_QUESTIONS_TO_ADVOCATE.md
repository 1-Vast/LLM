# Data-side cross-examination of the updater proposal

2026-10-22. My independent DRAFT.md was saved before reading advocate/INDEPENDENT.md. I now read that draft, and ask the following source/identification questions. These do not demand that one dataset establish full RSI before an exploratory experiment.

1. **STACK's dead channel is not K.** The primary explicitly says old corpses can lose Sytox signal and uses a trailing maximum of dead-object counts for lethal fraction; live mKate2 can persist briefly in dead cells. Thus max(dead-so-far) is an assay correction for a functional score, not authenticated cumulative death-event count. Taking Δmax(dead) as D and ΔL+Δmax(dead) as B would silently assume no event undercount and perfect marker separation. Will you explicitly reject that substitution for the exact Gamma-Poisson updater, while retaining STACK for a prefix→future functional prediction/observation-model experiment? What is the smallest conventional measurement model or directly testable diagnostic that would allow useful L/dead-stock forecasting without claiming birth/death identification?

2. **Latent counts versus observed differences.** Your B and D are exact counting-process quantities under the stated idealization, but interpolated marker-object trajectories can yield noninteger or negative balance estimates. If the actual source only has live/dead stock counts or live-cell counts alone, what claim remains falsifiable without repairing negative B by clipping? Could fixed-start ψ0/ψ1 be evaluated on actual continuation prediction even if residual channels are latent, with an ordinary state-space likelihood as the implementation-equivalent control? Please distinguish source incompatibility with an exact updater from a negative test of the biological transfer property.

3. **Exploratory task flow versus independent-run confirmation.** STACK publicly describes two cancer backgrounds and thousands of compounds, but large-screen biological replication is not authenticated. Your section 8 lists “only one independent run” as a stop condition. Can this be narrowed to stopping cross-run/RSI confirmation while allowing a clearly labeled within-screen, compound-held-out ψ transfer experiment? All records of a compound would stay together across backgrounds, history/validation/future compound sets would be disjoint, and no technical wells would become independent runs. If you reject that exploratory experiment, identify the specific estimand that is untestable rather than using missing runs as a universal stop.

4. **Concrete acquired unit and strongest null.** In an offline assay table, the purchasable feedback is an existing full trajectory or a predefined continuation window, not an arbitrary new drug dose/action or an independently executed intervention. For a minimal 24 h prefix→96 h LF task, would you first freeze fixed feedback for all ψ methods and compare two-channel κ, shared κ, channel-swapped κ, ordinary EB tuning, and equal-budget search with identical historical continuations? What minimum real-data result would falsify the need for a separate agent/module, while still retaining a useful conventional prefix forecast?

The critical point is that absence of a true K channel need not stop functional prediction; it does stop calling a dead-stock balance an observed birth/death reaction count. A successful net-growth or LF forecast is not automatically evidence for the proposed birth/death updater, and an unsupported updater should not force rejection of an otherwise useful source.
