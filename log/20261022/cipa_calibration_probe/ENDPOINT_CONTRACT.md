# CiPA source/development conversion contract

Frozen before running `endpoint_contract.py` on 2026-10-22. This is a zero-fit conversion census, not a scientific result or a novel mechanism. The original XLSX and mixed published supplement are already acquired/designer-exposed. This contract protects computational use of later numerical outcomes, not global human blindness.

## Source semantics and scope

The authenticated Blinova et al. 2018 full author XML (doi 10.1016/j.celrep.2018.08.079) says: arrhythmia-like events were counted/classified A–D; combinations were recorded; spontaneous-beating cessation is Q. Its statistical-analysis section calls arrhythmia binary and calls a drug positive if any well at any concentration has an event. That author-level summary is distinct from our mean of exported row-level EAD entries at one finite condition. We do not infer binary outcomes from `Type_of_EADs`, whose acquired strings additionally include T, 1AP and combinations. No clinical risk labels or event-type strings are predictors.

The author says ddFPDc/ddAPD90c is baseline/vehicle controlled and Fridericia rate corrected. Concentrations with at least 50% arrhythmic included wells were assigned missing duration. This is a condition-level observation rule, not proof that every exported blank was caused by arrhythmia, Q or assay failure. Exported H values span the named platform-specific measure; we retain context and refer to the column as exported ddFPDc, without claiming adult-APD or platform equivalence.

Only physical sites 1–5 are source and 6–7 development. Sites 8–9 are later: identity-only census is allowed, and their G/H cells are never read, decoded or converted by this script. Site 10 is excluded because it used sequential additions. All line/platform contexts remain together within site. No model fits, schedules, train/test shuffles or statistical comparisons occur here.

## Identity, finite query and physical metadata

Read original worksheet OOXML directly. The header must be exactly Drug_Name, Cell_type, risk, Platform, Type_of_EADs, conc, EAD, ddFPDc, site. Only the authenticated punctuation alias `D,l,Sotalol` → `D,l Sotalol` is applied. Each `(site, cell, platform, drug, dose_code)` with released rows is one acquisition bundle; it returns every released row. We do not manufacture well/run/donor IDs or independent replicates. Equal bundle cost is a benchmark assumption, not measured cost. No nonexistent bundle may be queried. All drug/code keys must join the author-derived Supplemental Table 2 nominal micromolar/Cmax strings. Free well concentrations were not measured. This census performs no numerical assay conversion on reserved or excluded sites.

## Exact value validity and targets

For source/development G and H only, absent cell, absent/empty value, empty inline string or empty shared string is missing. Nonempty error, formula, unsupported cell type, malformed decimal or nonfinite decimal is illegal and retained verbatim in the anomaly record. Numeric cells must have OOXML type absent or `n`; nonempty textual lookalikes are illegal, with no silent coercion. EAD must be exact Decimal 0 or 1 (e.g. 1.0 is legal); every other finite value is illegal. ddFPDc accepts every finite exported decimal, without clipping or range-based exclusion. No missing EAD is assigned 0, and no missing duration is assigned Q/EAD or 0. No source-specific convenience filter is added after observation.

Per bundle retain row IDs and denominators and compute: `ead_mean` = sum legal EAD / number legal EAD, null if none; `ddfpdc_mean` = mean finite available H, null if none; `ddfpdc_available_fraction` = number finite available H / total released rows. Retain separate EAD observed fraction, counts and raw source/development records. The availability target denotes exported numeric availability, not known physiological measurability. Aggregated row fractions are descriptive within bundles and do not create independent observations. All mean targets use equal row weights within their released bundle; downstream evaluation must choose weights explicitly. Illegal observations stop eligibility for model fitting; artifacts retain them for diagnosis and are never silently dropped. Missing-value counts alone do not stop the census.

## Verification and outputs

Bind input hashes and this contract hash before execution. Require exact header, unique mapping drugs/codes, all identity joins, legal sites/dose codes, canonical total 1,679 bundles, 8,859 drug rows, and known missing site3/CDI/MCS/Ibutilide code1. Require source/development groups disjoint from later/excluded and reserved/excluded outcome contacts/casts equal zero. Emit source/development bundle numerical JSON and row JSON; emit missing/illegal source/development records and schema/identity-only census separately. Artifact files use exclusive creation. No previous-phase source is modified. A numerical reader for later outcomes would require a separate future protocol and is not provided here.
