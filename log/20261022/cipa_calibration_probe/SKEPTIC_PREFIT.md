The proposed scope is legitimate and useful: previously named drugs at a new physical laboratory, source sites1–5, development sites6–7, eight paid drug/context/dose bundles per target site, later sites8–9 untouched numerically, sequential-addition site10 excluded. This is retrospective finite-menu assay transfer. It does not establish new-drug, donor, patient, kinetic-history, or causal channel inference. ## Mechanism assessment

I have **no sufficiently distinct mechanism to promote** from these source-only facts. A low-dimensional site response, shared residual calibration, joint event/availability/conditional-response heads, hierarchical shrinkage, and acquisition by uncertainty all have ordinary statistical interpretations. A drug-dependent response to a small calibration set is compatible with ordinary random effects or reduced-rank multioutput regression. Calling that a latent cellular world does not distinguish it technically.

The sharpest useful operation for this probe is already an ordinary comparator: estimate target-site residual corrections from the eight paid bundles, shrink them toward zero using source-only leave-one-site-out evidence, and apply the correction to the same drugs/doses elsewhere in the target context. Its classical equivalents include ridge regression on source predictions/context features and a Gaussian random-effects posterior mean under the corresponding design/covariance. It must not be renamed a novel mechanism. A source-only test can decide whether correction transfers across drugs, but success would license a later focused hypothesis rather than novelty promotion. No bespoke mechanism module is required now.

## Query selection and evaluation must identify one task

1. **Use the same eight fixed bundles for the first model comparison.** Select their identities using source-only outcomes and permissible target design metadata, seal the identities before reading target assay values, and give the complete same feedback to every model. A fixed menu is sufficient to answer whether target calibration adds anything beyond source drug/dose response. It is simpler than adding an adaptive agent immediately.
2. If an ordinary AL comparison is included, freeze a common terminal evaluation pool outside the eligible acquisition pool. All policies get the same legal query pool, the same starting information and eight distinct paid bundles; every query's entire released condition belongs to that acquisition. Querying one row does not unlock independent heldout wells at that condition. Include failed/no-continuous-value measurements in the paid budget.
3. Scoring each policy only on its own unqueried complement changes the evaluation population. It can be a declared operational policy score, but a better score can then reflect removing hard cases, not improved prediction on common conditions. Do not interpret it as the same-target model improvement or causal value of the query strategy. A common terminal pool avoids this ambiguity. Another valid diagnostic is to score all competing predictors on the same policy-specific complement, then keep the different policies' population caveat explicit.
4. A final target holdout may not choose the query ranking, amount of shrinkage, feature interactions, solver, score weights, stopping round, or winning seed. Even choosing which outcome to emphasize is adaptation. Sites6–7 can support a disclosed development screen, not a later confirmatory test on the same sites after design changes. Source leave-one-site-out selection should freeze tuning before the first development replay.
5. Two development sites are two physical environments. Drug/dose bundles, technical rows and random seeds do not become independent site-generalization replicates. Report both sites separately and use paired per-drug summaries within site descriptively; do not manufacture a high-precision across-site confidence interval from thousands of exported rows. Random query seeds estimate sensitivity to a policy's randomness conditional on the same biology.

If a finite common query menu leaves too few heldout conditions, disclose that tradeoff and keep a fixed-menu first probe. There is no need to force an AL claim into this stage.

## Endpoints and conditioning

Treat the three outcomes as different prediction targets: bundle EAD fraction under authenticated row coding, fraction with a valid finite continuous measurement, and mean ddFPDc among available valid measurements. If the source does not establish the denominator/coding of a missing EAD field, do not silently replace it by a negative event. Missing continuous values do not imply EAD, quiescence, zero, or an unobserved continuous ground truth.

The continuous mean is **conditional on availability in the released assay export**. A gain on that mean does not establish recovery of latent unmeasured repolarization. Score it only where a valid mean exists, report its evaluation coverage, and score availability separately. Do not condition deployment predictions on the actual target mask or actual EAD strings before paying for that bundle. In particular, observed numeric-cell counts and measurement fractions can reveal the availability endpoint; a parser can retain them for terminal scoring without making them prediction features.

Squared error of a predicted event fraction is a proper score for the mean event rate; the registered target here is the empirical bundle fraction, not an authenticated independent-binomial latent rate. If a fractional log score is used, define endpoint clipping and aggregation before outcomes. Equal-bundle acquisition cost suggests reporting equal-bundle score first. Weighting by exported row count instead answers a different question and gives larger collections more influence; absent well/run IDs, those counts do not prove biological precision. A continuous squared-error predictor targets the conditional mean; MAE targets a different optimum, so describe the elicited quantity accurately if MAE is primary.

Standardization must use source-only scale. Report native endpoint errors as well. Do not pick combined endpoint weights from development wins. A probe can report three separate scores and a preregistered primary endpoint; no invented utility score or clinical-use threshold is needed to estimate headroom. A small numerical improvement is only a screen until a concrete assay decision and meaningful margin are justified.

## Site, platform, line and dose support

Keep all contexts from one physical site together. The source has15 site/line/platform contexts across10 sites; those are not15 independent laboratories or donors. A context may be confounded with its site's commercial cell line, platform, protocol, serum and other unrecorded conditions. Predictive calibration of the joint context is allowed; causal attribution to a platform or laboratory effect is not identified by these exports.

Specify whether the eight paid bundles are per physical site or per context. The stated per-site budget must be split across all its contexts rather than silently multiplying by the number of contexts. A context-specific affine fit may be underdetermined if that context receives no queries; its fallback must be source-defined. Record source support for every target line/platform level and interaction; unseen categorical features must use a declared encoding/fallback, not target outcomes. Include source per-drug/dose curves stratified or shrunk by line/platform where source support permits, so a weak pooled mean does not make an ordinary direct model look special.

Use nominal dose mappings as authenticated, not measured free exposure. Drug×dose categories are a strong legitimate known-drug baseline. Log nominal concentration or Cmax-relative concentration may be additional source-authenticated features, but fitting such features does not establish pharmacological extrapolation. The one absent Ibutilide source bundle must stay absent. Site10 exclusion preserves a common single-dose protocol scope and is not a data-driven outcome exclusion.

## Fair information and budget

- Source drug/dose baseline: pool within physical site first or explicitly report the chosen weighting, to prevent large row/context collections from receiving hidden extra importance. Do not discard source drug identity while giving it to a proposed world.
- Direct ridge/tree: use all legal source data, line/platform and dose information, with source-only hyperparameter selection; source drug×dose interactions are essential for this known-drug application. Never use `risk`, `Type_of_EADs`, actual EAD, actual availability, or actual ddFPDc of an unpaid condition as inputs. Outcome-associated strings can be labels only.
- Ordinary refit: give the same eight target bundles, allow source-tuned source/target weighting and regularization, and include a no-update option. Simply pooling eight target records into a vastly larger source table can handicap the refit baseline. Affine calibration, hierarchical shrinkage and a fixed/simple mixture are necessary low-cost competitors.
- Updates: evaluate the initial source predictor and each queried-prefix checkpoint under the same information. An update is not automatically continuous learning; a fair online comparator can retain/refit prior paid feedback under a frozen source-chosen rule.
- Search: distinguish assay budget from computation. Eight assays per method alone does not establish equal-budget program search if one method evaluates many more fits, candidates or development feedback rounds. Freeze a finite candidate menu and number of permitted source/development evaluations, log those counts, and charge any target assay revealed during selection. Do not use a full-information target oracle to choose a program and call it paid-feedback search.
- Acquisition: random, fixed positive-control/source-selected, standard uncertainty/AL and any proposed query procedure must obey the same bundle boundaries and initial information. Every policy gets feedback on its own paid bundles; source-only uncertainty may be estimated without accessing target responses. A target oracle may be a labeled upper bound, never a comparator receiving an allegedly equal information budget.

## Falsification and continuation

Freeze the input hashes, physical split, canonical bundle identity, query/menu identities, masks/denominators, source normalization, primary score, parameter selection, fits/search counts and terminal set before executing the new probe. A syntax/schema check is not a numerical predictive success.

A positive development result means only that this particular ordinary calibration strategy improves the registered score on these development environments compared with the strongest equal-information ordinary baseline. A tie/loss stops that exact candidate/configuration; do not tune it on sites6–7 and call the rerun a fresh holdout. If all calibration methods lose to the source-only prediction, report that the eight-bundle opportunity failed under this protocol; do not infer that every cardiac transfer task is impossible. Preserve later sites8–9 for one genuinely future frozen test and preserve all historical STOPs/reserves.

No current evidence establishes a world-model mechanism, agent contribution, inherited gain, joint gain, or C4 RSI. Such tests can follow a useful ordinary predictive result; they need not be prerequisites for running this baseline probe. Future world/agent factorial contrasts need not be superadditive. RSI C4 must compare old/new checkpoints of the same improver from the same starting learner and information, rather than compare two different solvers. Two new development sites cannot by themselves establish that improvement strategies generalize across a large research-task population.

**Root decision requested:** proceed with the minimal frozen ordinary probe; keep the innovation status unpromoted. A later observed, reproducible failure mode that survives these strong ordinary controls may motivate one new mechanism and the user-required independent cross-debate. The present source-only record does not yet provide that mechanism.
