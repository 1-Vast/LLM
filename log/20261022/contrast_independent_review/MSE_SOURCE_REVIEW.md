## Endpoint and information semantics

I independently matched all four `MSE_METHOD_CONTRACT.json` excerpts to the corresponding original primary XML sections, ignoring whitespace only. The methods specify **24h** parallel drug/control culture, fixed-cell imaging, apoptotic-cell exclusion, subsequent phenotype classification, manual artifact/outlier exclusions, and replicate averaging.

The per-well definitions are **RCN=1−N_drug/median(N_control)** and **RCF=1−f_drug/median(f_control)**. Raw sheets contain averages of these derived responses. They are not raw counts/images or independent well-level measurements. Positive RCN means reduced classified retained malignant abundance; positive RCF means reduced malignant fraction. RCF is **not healthy-cell survival**, and positive RCF alone need not establish absolute tumor depletion. Shared malignant counts and controls couple these heads; they are not independent corroborations. Averaged nonlinear ratios cannot recover mean healthy-cell counts or denominator uncertainty.

Raw values need not lie in[0,1]; negative responses are permitted. Under ideal nonnegative numerators and positive denominators the reductions are at most1, but those premises have not been numerically checked here. No clipping or zero-filling follows from the formula. The author-scaled sheets divide each sample's negative/positive responses by its panel-wide negative/positive extrema. Those extrema include unrevealed candidate outcomes, so **scaled sheets are inadmissible** for sparse-feedback inference. Raw normalization still relies on the publisher's same-assay controls/QC and phenotype measurement system. A patient-grouped response test would remain conditional on that cohort-trained measurement pipeline.

The composition is a **24h DMSO parallel-control state**, not time-zero state. Parent and subtype columns overlap hierarchically; all13 cannot be treated as disjoint populations summing to one. Neither specimen IDs nor repeated specimens authenticate chronological visit order. A declared algorithmic sequence is permissible, but is not a historical prospective trial or a measured cellular trajectory.

I asked the biological scout directly about timing and endpoint validity. It explicitly accepted: **freeze k probes from source-only information before observing this patient's composition; at one common24h information checkpoint reveal those raw RCN/RCF pairs plus DMSO composition; nominate a disjoint, unrevealed fixed-size shortlist.** Every method receives the same control bundle and paid joint response pairs. It stated that composition is forbidden for choosing this first probe batch. A control-first culture followed by new drug cultures requires unauthenticated material, turnaround and stability assumptions and is outside this gate.

This is a legitimate retrospective information-access budget on an originally fully assayed panel. It tests the decision value of limited revealed information, not prospective adaptive wet-lab assay savings, culture reuse, monetary cost or turnaround. A fixed k batch is one information cost convention; the shared control-state bundle has its own declared cost. Published averaged four-well conditions are the action bundles; individual wells are not extra tasks. Any later sequential stored-label reveal remains information replay.

The scout also accepted source-only validity/headroom checks and a **utility family/calibration procedure frozen before source decoding**, followed by concrete source-derived thresholds frozen before development/later decoding. Its suggested utility is the number of unrevealed shortlist actions meeting both RCN≥tau_N and RCF≥tau_F. Source quantiles would define **source-relative ex-vivo co-depletion**, not clinical benefit. Pareto reporting alone does not uniquely select a shortlist, and hypervolume needs a frozen reference point. No therapeutic threshold is justified by present metadata.
