## Operation A: deletion/restoration transitions for a learned acquisition function

**Scientific decision:** which next genetic perturbation or drug-combination assay yields useful verified hits under a fixed real-experiment budget? This targets discovery directly rather than expression accuracy. TDAL's current tests are genetic screens, including cytokine production, immune killing and growth; target direction matters. Its benchmark treats strong positive and negative effects as hits for several tasks, which is not automatically an appropriate therapeutic objective.

**Exact primary operation.** For measured set D+ and selected already-measured batch A, set D-=D+ minus A. Refit a surrogate on D- to construct state s-; restore the observed batch and construct s+. The virtual transition is (s-, A, R(A), w, s+), where the reward uses existing measured outcomes. A candidate acquisition score is

q_theta(s,x) = p_hat(hit|x,D) + f_theta(s,c(x)).

The acquisition network learns via Double-DQN targets

Y = R(A) + w Q_target(s+, argmax_A' Q_theta(s+,A')),

with w squared equal to the remaining-pool mean Hellinger squared distance between before/after Bernoulli hit predictions. Batch scores add candidate scores; maximizing them is top-B ranking. The operation is concrete and distinct from simply fitting the response surrogate. LBO creates computational training transitions but **no new assays or independent rewards**.

**Biological constraint:** experiment results are costly and irreversible, so only one actual screening history is available. Replaying measured outcomes can enlarge training signal without re-running cultures. Its validity relies on hiding every relevant result and rebuilding model/state under the counterfactual observed set; a fitted model, feature selection, normalization or state summary that retains the removed outcome leaks it back. Shared plates and bundled assays should be removed/restored as actual information units rather than convenient rows. Non-stationary cell stocks or changing assay definitions cannot be undone by deleting a row.

**Closest reductions and unresolved novelty:** ordinary model-based/off-policy active search, replay/experience learning, leave-out training and fixed-model lookahead are close. TDAL itself already supplies the full operation. Merely substituting a drug-combination response model or inheriting its acquisition network is not a new mechanism. Its Hellinger-weighted Bellman objective weights future value by model change; that is not algebraically the ordinary undiscounted total-hit objective. The paper explicitly defines the weighted objective, so the correct concern is alignment with biological utility, not an accusation that its own stated Bellman equation is wrong. A large prediction change may reflect damage to predictions; a small change may coexist with valuable exploitation. Full direct evaluation of real hit/decision value and unweighted/constant-weight controls remain necessary.

**Available task bank:** TDAL v1 Appendix C names four single-gene, three double-gene and one triple-gene task: IFN-gamma, IL-2, NK, Carnevale, Simpson K562, SCHEMATIC, Kuzmin yeast and Horlbeck Jurkat. Eight tasks are not eight independent drug-development replications; individual cells/genes are not biological runs. No executable TDAL repository or actual table keys were authenticated by this scout. Its global top-5% thresholds are benchmark labels derived from full outcomes; they cannot be treated as operationally known therapeutic thresholds without disclosure. BATCHIE's prospective pair catalog below is a more concrete drug-function source, but its coverage must first be qualified.

**Minimum falsification:** demonstrate that deletion/restoration reconstructs the same state/model as an independently trained D- baseline, including all preprocessing and plate bundles; otherwise virtual transitions are invalid. Then compare against a fixed learned-acquisition rule, full same-information lookahead, ordinary active search, constant/unweighted successor values and equal-total-compute search. A retrospective policy must only request measured available bundles; unmeasured grid outcomes cannot be filled by model predictions and counted as discovered hits. No currently specified extra operator survives the exact-source precedent, so this is a close baseline opportunity rather than a promoted candidate.

## Operation B: signed update recomposition on measured therapeutic ranking margins

**Scientific decision:** prioritize drug-dose combinations that inhibit a target cancer population while sparing non-cancer controls. The BATCHIE prospective study has real RPE/BJ control lines and sarcoma lines, making this a more direct functional decision than cross-cell “selectivity” where both groups are cancers. The endpoint remains in-vitro viability; it is not clinical safety or efficacy.

**Minimal hypothetical translation of ExGrad:** obtain three ordinary update directions g_1,g_2,g_3 from source data, such as all-cell, target-focused and control-focused fitting objectives. On *already measured and separately reserved for update selection* comparison bundles j, define an oriented prediction margin m_j(theta)=d_j[f_theta(a_j)-f_theta(b_j)], with d_j the sign of their measured therapeutic-value difference. Probe each update from the same theta, restoring the state each time:

D_ji = [m_j(theta-eta_p g_i)-m_j(theta)]/eta_p.

Choose beta by maximizing mean log-sigmoid of the normalized affine margins subject to 1^T beta=1 and ||beta||_2 <= R, allowing negative beta_i; then update theta'=theta-eta sum_i beta_i g_i. This explicitly transfers the source paper's finite-response probe and signed affine solve. Validation doses/lines/outcomes and all probe compute are charged; margins on unmeasured outcomes derived solely from the model are not externally validated improvement.

**Why the source operation matters:** nonnegative loss mixtures constrain the search to a convex combination of directions. Signed combinations can leave that hull, potentially suppressing a source direction that harms an intervention-ranking objective. The ExGrad source also warns that negative coordinate coefficients do not necessarily imply a negative net projection onto that direction, because candidate gradients are correlated. Its concavity/global optimum guarantee concerns the local coefficient problem, not the exact neural update or biological decision.

**Conventional comparison and current stop:** this translation is a bounded low-dimensional subspace optimization of an ordinary supervised ranking objective. A standard signed trust-region/subspace search on the same directions, direct ranking training, multiobjective fitting and equal-budget coordinate/grid search have the same information and action class. Replacing image robustness with therapeutic margin is a substantive application but does not yet add a new operator or guarantee. Selecting directions or learning beta across tasks is ordinary meta-optimization unless further technical content is specified. No necessity of a world rollout or a scientific agent follows just from this update. It is a HYPOTHESIS for finite-budget utility, not evidence of RSI or an original core.

**Algebraic falsifier:** for collinear directions g=(1,2,3), signed beta=(-.1,.6,.5) and convex beta=(0,.4,.6) both produce update 2.6. Therefore observing a negative coefficient alone cannot establish a capability outside ordinary nonnegative mixtures. A stronger test must compare the resulting directions/decisions with full same-span signed optimization, including actual finite-step outcomes. Any advantage must exceed a preregistered useful decision margin on independent later groups at matched real-data and compute cost. Persistent gains on the same comparison bundle are selection overfitting, not improved improver competence.
