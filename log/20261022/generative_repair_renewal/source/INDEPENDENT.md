## Exact primary snippets and what they establish

`LOCAL_EXCERPTS.json` records source paths/hashes and the following verbatim excerpts with local text line numbers. The publisher-derived SI DOI is10.1038/s41586-026-11001-9.

**SI TableS8A, lines158–165:** “Sample-level metadata for the PTPC dataset, an independent perturbation proteomics dataset generated across five cancer cell lines (A375, HCT116, NCI-H1975, NCI-H2170 and Panc 10.05). Proteomic profiles were collected at 6 h and 24 h after treatment, with control samples assigned a perturbation time of 0 h.” It describes2,217non-pooled sample records,2,021independent proteomic samples and196technical/biological replicates, with90pooled samples excluded from the table. These are paper-level sample descriptors, not independent RSI tasks.

**SI TableS8B/C, lines167–176:** “Compound information for the716perturbation identifiers used in PTPC, corresponding to708unique compound names” and “Matched protein abundance matrix for the same2217non-pooled sample records.” This is concrete positive evidence of a metadata↔proteome correspondence. The five named backgrounds differ nominally from the old breast-lineage list. Actual alias/sample-ID global overlap and condition matching remain to be checked before an independent task is declared.

**SI TableS7A/B, lines133–147:** PTNC has81compounds with Pert_IDs; “cell viability assays ... based on the24h response,” three biological replicates, resistant(N)/sensitive(Y) “according to the efficacy criteria described in the Methods.” “Cell-line-compound combinations with inconsistent labels across repeated experiments were removed; therefore, the resulting compound-by-cell-line matrix is not fully orthogonal.” This establishes PTNC functional-label semantics at the coarse published-call level, and a label-consistency selection rule. It does **not** say S7's PTNC labels are the labels for S8's PTPC samples, nor provide the actual threshold or sample crosswalk.

**SI TableS2A, lines39–48:** mtPTDS metadata describes sampleID, cell line, plateID, perturbation time, treatment category, drug/combinationID, anchor/library drugIDs and doses, instrument and replicate type;11times from0to60h. Its three named TNBC lines are HCC1806,HCC1143,MDA-MB-453-1. These overlap the old lineage universe and do not supply new cross-background evaluation. It is positive evidence that a supplementary metadata table may expose dose/replicate information absent from the seven-file release; actual fields/values were not read.

**SI TableS1C, lines32–37:** original efficacy calls are24h cell viability with3biological replicates; detailed criteria deferred to main Methods. This corroborates the prior coarse label contract without filling its numerical cutoff.

**SI Dose-response methods, lines600–609:** luminescence normalized to untreated control wells; viability transformed to inhibition and constrained0–1; sigmoidal fits,3independent biological replicates per concentration. The paragraph explicitly ties this to Figures5G–I. It cannot be transplanted as the exact assay/cutoff used for every PTDS/PTNC/PTPC binary label. Text extraction also loses some Greek units elsewhere; do not infer concentrations from mangled PDF text.
