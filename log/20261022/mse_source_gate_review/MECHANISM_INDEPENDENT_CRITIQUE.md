**Disposition: no defensible original operation survives the candidate as written.** Its literal correction is undefined from the stated fit; even granting a common signed correction leaves the terminal ranking unchanged. Coherent action-dependent repairs are conventional transformed-response prediction, with empirical value untested. This is an exact-operation critique, not an appeal to universal model expressibility or an exhaustive literature novelty claim.

Let s=sign(RCN-RCF), with possible values -1,0,+1. The specified q(c)=P(s!=0|c) gives P(+1|c)+P(-1|c), whereas E[s|c] is their difference. Conditional distributions assigning probability 3/4 to +1 versus -1 and the remainder to zero have the same q and opposite means. Thus the signed expectation in the advertised residual is not supplied by that binary logistic fit. A three-category classifier or signed-mean regression supplies it conventionally. A two-category repair needs an explicit zero/tie treatment; ignoring zero signs is not automatically legitimate.

For any shared scalar correction m(c), rho_d=bar_s_d-m(c) obeys rho_i-rho_j=bar_s_i-bar_s_j. Exact order, ties and TopK remain unchanged. The declared source-only magnitude tie breaker also remains unchanged. Changing logistic parameters through target probe information cannot change this terminal shortlist through the written operation. Adding drug identity, drug-by-context interactions or drug-specific probe-conditioning would make the correction action-dependent, but that is an additional ordinary supervised model omitted from the claimed operation. If a later repair changes pooled source means using target outcomes, its label access and mechanism must be specified afresh; that is not the literal formula under review.

The identity RCN-RCF=(M/M0)*(T0/T-1), for one drug/control count configuration with positive totals and identical controls, is correct. Positive disagreement can reflect reduced total retained-cell count. It does not label an imaging artifact, denominator noise or a causal nuisance: total count changes can themselves be real biological effects. Real released averages and separate control medians do not license the proposed inversion. There are no authenticated nuisance-versus-biological-truth labels in this corpus.

The synthetic actions A=(RCN .1, RCF -8) and B=(RCN .9, RCF 10/11) are feasible under the stated count construction, and B strictly dominates A on both favorable readouts while its disagreement sign is lower. This refutes a universal preference interpretation for higher disagreement sign. It is not proof that all sign-derived features are uninformative on a population.
