## Decision target and scope

The useful narrow task is nomination of drugs with both lower retained malignant abundance and lower malignant fraction in the same 24-hour ex vivo assay. RCN = 1 - malignant drug count / median malignant control count; RCF = 1 - malignant drug fraction / median malignant control fraction. Raw endpoint sheets are replicate-averaged derived scores. Joint favorable responses rule out treating a pure fraction change as sufficient absolute depletion, but do not measure nonmalignant-cell survival, long-term killing, clinical safety, or efficacy. This remains meaningful assay phenotype prioritization with modest interpretation.

The exact candidate bank is 135 specimens/95 patients and the authenticated 65 single chemical drugs at 10 micromolar, across later designs. Four technical wells are planned, retained numbers unauthenticated. Shared controls, denominator stability, classifier correctness and effective replicate uncertainty remain unverified from aggregate responses. Their absence is not a literal invalid score. No inverse reconstruction of healthy counts or denominator uncertainty is justified.

Choose probes without this patient's 24-hour composition. Reveal probe pairs and the paid parallel-control composition together at one checkpoint, then nominate a disjoint unrevealed shortlist. This is retrospective information access, not prospective culture scheduling. No source endpoint or context value is authorized by this proposal alone.

## Concrete independent design recommendation

Use k=5 fixed probes and L=5 terminal nominations, leaving 60 terminal candidates. Five is a modest fixed information budget and five nominations permit interpretable integer utility; neither is optimized from observed results. Select probes by the five smallest SHA256 hashes of UTF-8 `mse-biological-fixed-probes-v1|` plus the exact authenticated drug identity, with name as a tie breaker. Do not try multiple seeds. A root-selected alternative k is permissible only before freeze/decoding and must be documented; it changes budget and terminal menu, not biology.

Recommend 40 source, 20 development and 35 later patients, assigned by a declared deterministic metadata-only patient hash. All specimens from each patient stay together. This is a capacity allocation, not a power certification or authentic temporal ordering. Root will own the manifest and final allocation. A different sealed predecode allocation is legitimate; there is no biological reason to treat 40/20/35 as privileged.

The zero-fit gate means no predictor, updater, acquisition model or learned embedding is trained and no algorithm advantage is measured. Source empirical summaries and the explicitly declared utility calibration below are allowed; they are not evidence of held-out performance. Later patients remain entirely undecoded, including their control composition.

## Unit validity and missingness

For the 65 common source actions, require paired raw endpoint storage, exact patient/specimen/drug joins, numeric parseability and finite RCN and RCF. Under the author's nonnegative-numerator/positive-denominator definition, each score is at most one; values above one plus 1e-8 are formula-incompatible under those premises and trigger source-contract review. Values in (1,1+1e-8] receive a rounding-warning flag and are preserved, not clipped. No lower bound is imposed: negative and very negative finite scores remain admissible. Report their frequencies and ranges without turning magnitude into a post hoc exclusion rule.

For automatic GO on this exact complete-menu protocol, every designated source response pair must be evaluable. Missing, annotated, nonfinite or formula-incompatible pairs trigger HOLD/FAIL_THIS_COMPLETE_MENU_GATE before later access. Do not zero-fill, impute truth, drop only the poor endpoint, choose a more convenient action menu, or silently exclude patients. An explicit subsequent revision might define a different incomplete-menu estimand, but this gate does not authorize it. This strict rule is an operational coverage requirement, not a claim that one missing record invalidates the whole publication.

Do not demand unavailable per-well denominators as a mandatory eligibility check, and do not mark every score invalid because denominators were not released. Record three states separately: observed score incompatibility, known missing/non-evaluable truth, and unauthenticated measurement premises. The last limits inference and may motivate future validation; it is not an observed data failure. No source-derived outcome mask may be built using unopened patients' numerical values.

Composition requires a separately exact workbook relationship-to-sheet-to-header mapping before freezing any parser. The auditor's just-delivered metadata warning says our predecessor mapping may have confused the ten-column raw subtype abundance sheet with a thirteen-column normalized parent/subtype sheet. This is an unresolved schema-authentication issue at this seal, not evidence that any composition value is invalid. Do not decode composition while it is unresolved. Proposed minimal state is the authenticated raw disjoint categories if their units/partition can be verified, or documented parent fractions if that is what the correct source sheet contains; no arbitrary choice is authorized here. Fraction bounds [0,1] are appropriate only once stored units are source-authenticated, and a sum-to-one rule requires an authenticated disjoint exhaustive partition. Never force all thirteen overlapping fields to sum to one or normalize values to conceal a schema mismatch.

## Source-only utility calibration recommendation

My preferred calibration is a prespecified source-relative rule rather than unsubstantiated therapeutic effect cutoffs. Assign each source patient equal mass, each of that patient's specimens equal mass within the patient, and all 65 drugs equal mass within the specimen. For each endpoint independently, compute the weighted empirical 75th percentile by the smallest observed value whose cumulative weight is at least .75. Let tau_N=max(0,q75_RCN) and tau_F=max(0,q75_RCF). Define a hit only when RCN>tau_N AND RCF>tau_F. The strict sign excludes zero/ties as depletion when the floor is active. This phase explicitly changes the earlier generic greater-or-equal family to a strict predicate; any adopted choice must be frozen before decode.

These are source-relative joint-depletion labels, not clinical thresholds. The source quantile choice is a fixed research policy: do not change its percentile, floor, tie rule or weighting to obtain more hits or favor a method. Freeze the resulting concrete thresholds and their source-only provenance before development/later conversion. Source quantile calibration can create marginal prevalence by construction, so passing a prevalence check is not independent evidence of biological signal. Correlation can still make joint prevalence trivial. A separately justified fixed pair such as root's proposed .50/.20 would also be defensible as an explicitly normative assay policy, provided nobody calls it source-calibrated or clinically validated. My preference is not a veto of such a predeclared policy.

Primary utility for specimen s and fixed shortlist S is U_s(S)=sum over a in S of hit(s,a), |S|=5, with all probes excluded. Average U across specimens within patient, then across patients. Patient-balanced utility and patient-clustered uncertainty are required; drug cells and technical wells are not independent patients. The eventual meaningful gain target proposed here is 0.5 additional joint-depletion nominations per five-drug shortlist, a research usefulness criterion rather than a clinical minimal effect.

## Zero-fit headroom, and what passing means

For each source specimen let H_s be its number of qualifying terminal actions. Oracle count is min(5,H_s), and the exact expected count for a uniform random five-action shortlist is 5 H_s/60. Report patient-balanced means and their difference. Also report all terminal action hit prevalences, the distribution of H_s, the number of patients with at least one mixed-hit specimen, and the number with no hits/all hits. These are descriptive opportunity statistics, not evidence of predictability from probes or context.

An optional stronger diagnostic is the patient-balanced best fixed-shortlist value: sum the five largest patient-balanced action hit prevalences. This is an aggregate optimization over constant shortlists, not an independently validated trained policy. Its gap below the patient-aware oracle describes source heterogeneity not explained by one fixed list. It should be explicitly labeled as an in-source upper envelope; do not report it as unbiased LOPO generalization. If root's zero-fit boundary excludes even this descriptive optimization, use only oracle/random statistics and reserve learned fixed ranking for a later phase.

My proposed advancement rule is valid complete source data plus oracle-minus-best-fixed descriptive headroom at least 0.5 nominations per patient on average; if the stronger diagnostic is excluded, substitute the weaker oracle-minus-random bound with that weakness explicit before decoding. Do not choose between diagnostics after seeing values. Failing this rule stops the proposed personalized nomination scope on this source bank; it does not establish no biological effects, no alternative utility, or an impossibility of useful models. Passing only establishes space for the stated decision gain in observed source outcomes. It neither demonstrates a learnable signal nor promises later performance or adequate power. No active-selection or inherited-improver claim follows.
