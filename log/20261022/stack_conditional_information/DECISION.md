# STACK conditional-information experiment: useful-gain gate failed

2026-10-22. **Decision: PRIMARY_USEFUL_GAIN_FAIL / STOP_THIS_REGISTERED_CONFIGURATION.** A real, qualified single-run experiment found a small, direction-consistent improvement from early SG history, but it did not meet the prospectively registered usefulness margins. Preserve that observed improvement without promoting this fixed-dose24→72h exported-marker application into a world-model/agent/RSI framework. The full research goal remains **ACTIVE / NOT_ACHIEVED**; no novel core is established here.

## Application, registration and data

The application was early fixed-dose drug-response forecasting in the deposited STACK screens:1,833named library entries at5micromolar in T98G+DMSO, T98G+400micromolarTMZ and U2OS. Input: seven live/SG observations at0,4,…24h, with U2OS downsampled from2h to4h. Target: log2(exported live-reporter density at72h+1), predicted as change from the observed24h anchor. These are objects/mm², with the documented NLS/mKate2 operational live-reporter interpretation. They do not authenticate true viability, event counts, original wells/runs or independent biological replicates.

Coverage denominators include every assigned named row. All arms use the same dual-prefix+target-complete scoring panel. ## Scientific interpretation and historical boundaries

The strong direct predictors substantially outperform constant-growth/persistence anchors on this source and endpoint. This shows that those simple anchors are weak here. It does not identify cellular dynamics, establish model portability or make the direct forecaster a new world model. Likewise, the small extra-channel gain is an empirical finite-predictor result, not conditional mutual information, death causality or discovery of hits/cost savings. The absolute0.05margin is a pragmatic preregistered utility proxy, not a measured assay noise floor or clinical criterion.

The source is Forcina et al., Cell Systems2017, DOI[10.1016/j.cels.2017.05.002](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cels.2017.05.002), data DOI[10.17632/3pnv5wh5jm.1](https://doi.org/10.17632/3pnv5wh5jm.1). Exact assets and primary marker/censoring/run limitations remain in the [frozen source qualification](../stack_raw_qualification/DECISION.md). The preceding primary-source method review retains BESTDR2026(DOI10.1158/0008-5472.CAN-25-1181), Gross2023(DOI10.1038/s41467-023-39122-z), and the2026ROS/proliferation-feedback precedent(DOI10.3389/fcell.2026.1756238). They already undermine novelty from ordinary birth/death inference, phase dynamics or adding feedback alone. No exhaustive novelty clearance or new literature retrieval is claimed in this bounded numerical gate. None of those full mechanistic models was benchmarked here.

The previous HTS001 net-growth/shrinkage and exact EB novelty stops remain; the HCT116 gate remains an invalid-target abort without a channel-performance result. This successful STACK numerical qualification does not amend or rescue either historical record. All124+86+69previously inventoried files remain byte-identical.

Stop this exact fixed-dose24→72h, named-entry, complete-dual-observation application under the registered predictors as a basis for framework expansion. Do not lower thresholds, swap target/horizon, add phenotype-selected subgroups or retune against the exposed test to rescue it. Retain the valid source and the small positive errors differences for transparent retrospective work.

A subsequent candidate must have a different scientific justification and a genuinely unexposed evaluation, qualified intervention/state/functional-response identities, and a mechanism whose behavior cannot already be explained by same-information direct prediction or ordinary updating. Before expensive implementation, opposing independent reviews must establish its minimal necessary modules and falsifiable contribution. World, agent, inheritance and joint effects require separate matched-feedback/common-start tests against strong direct prediction, ordinary full retraining, continual/active learning, simple mixing and equal-budget program search. Genuine RSI further needs a revised improver that improves later improvements over a fixed improver on legitimate later tasks. A new endpoint or more trials on these exposed records is insufficient. No next core has yet passed that requirement.
