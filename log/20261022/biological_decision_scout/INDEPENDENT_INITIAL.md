## What state, action, time and outcome mean here

**Actions:** real compound or combination exposure in a named ex vivo assay. The conservative common menu above uses authenticated single-dose designs. Combination names containing `|` denote co-treatment, not a recorded temporal order. This is not a schedule or washout corpus.

**Time:** the author method fixes exposure at **24 hours**. Patient specimens were collected over the clinical study period, with longitudinal samples for some patients, but exact visit dates/temporal ordering were not authenticated in the inspected metadata columns. Do not turn SampleID numbering into chronological time. A predeclared patient task order can support sequential algorithm evaluation; it must not be labeled an actual historical prospective trial.

**State:** the released composition is the mean cell-type fraction in **DMSO control wells after the same 24-hour culture**. It includes lymphocyte/macrophage categories, malignant cells and malignant morphotypes, other and unassigned categories. This is a real parallel-control state, not a pretreatment single-cell trajectory or a free zero-time biomarker. Acquiring this state requires its own control assay bundle, which every competing method must receive at equal cost. The paper reports nested parent/subtype definitions; the 13 columns cannot all be treated as disjoint populations or forced to sum to one. No RNA-state claim is necessary: induced RNA is available only for five LUAD specimens, not the 95-patient common-action population.

**Observed functional outcomes:** per-well quantities are defined in the author methods as

`RCN = 1 - cancer_cell_number_drug / median(cancer_cell_number_control)`

`RCF = 1 - cancer_cell_fraction_drug / median(cancer_cell_fraction_control)`.

They are dimensionless reductions, positive in the direction the authors regard as desirable. RCN measures reduction in identified non-apoptotic cancer-cell abundance; RCF measures reduction in its fraction relative to all retained non-apoptotic cells. Positive RCF favors selective depletion relative to other cells; it is **not** a directly measured healthy-cell survival fraction. The two endpoints share the malignant-count numerator and same-assay controls and cannot be treated as independent evidence channels. A favorable fraction alone does not prove absolute tumor reduction. Nor can nonlinear ratios of per-well counts be inverted exactly from already-averaged score columns to recover a mean healthy-cell count.

Cells are fixed, stained, segmented and classified. Apoptotic cells are removed using a learned classifier, then cell type and morphology are classified; poorly assigned cells become unassigned. Manual sample-specific segmentation settings and outlier-well exclusions are reported. Thus these are image-derived retained-cell response phenotypes, not authenticated cumulative death events, clonogenic survival or patient benefit. Drug-induced marker/morphology changes can affect classification. No full per-well/raw-count bank has been authenticated by the inspected response matrix; it contains averages across technical replicate wells. The available figure workbooks have been inventoried and selected headings read, not certified as a new independent replicate-validation source.

**Critical leakage boundary:** the authors scale each sample's averaged drug responses to [-1,1] by its most negative and most positive observed response across the panel. The scaled sheets therefore use unrevealed candidate outcomes in a sparse-feedback replay. **Only the raw endpoint sheets are admissible for the proposed masked-budget task**, with any additional transformations fitted on source tasks or observed controls alone. “Raw” here means unscaled derived scores, not unprocessed images or independent raw well measurements.

**Other exposure/dependence limits:** the phenotype classifiers were trained on curated cells across the cohort with image-level train/evaluation splits. Hence a later patient split tests response inference conditional on the publisher's fixed, cohort-trained phenotype measurement system; it does not validate a phenotype detector on truly unseen patients. Sample inclusion required sufficient malignant content, and outlier removal can depend on observed images/counts. The release is a selected clinical/assay cohort. Clinical treatment was nonrandomized and physician-directed. Plate well randomization was not authenticated in the inspected MSE methods. Do not infer clinical causal benefit or general population prevalence from retrospective ex vivo rankings.

## Minimal scientific task and mechanism hypothesis

**Application:** with a fixed, explicitly declared number of measured drug bundles in a new patient-derived effusion sample, nominate unmeasured interventions that reduce tumor abundance while improving tumor-selective depletion. Evaluate recommendations only against actual held-out raw RCN/RCF measurements. The common 65-action matrix makes this a possible finite-catalogue replay; one bundle is the released four-well drug condition, not four independent biological experiments. It does not yet establish actual wet-lab turnaround or monetary savings, and all screened actions were physically performed in the original study. The paid-acquisition process would be a retrospective information-access simulation.

**Hypothesis, not a new named algorithm:** feedback on absolute malignant-abundance reduction and relative malignant-fraction reduction has different transport across cell-composition states. A small joint intervention model, conditioned on the measured control composition, may learn how a few drug observations update the remaining **joint** response distribution more usefully than a scalar selectivity score. The agent's nontrivial operation would choose a real drug assay whose feedback changes which unmeasured interventions meet both functional requirements, instead of merely choosing the strongest predicted mean. This differs from MetaGenSim: no synthetic rollout is used as a training label or real-world validator. It is a real-measurement feedback problem with joint biological readouts.

A minimal implementation would have one joint two-output conditional model, one update operator and one finite-menu acquisition rule. No transition graph, virtual tumor ecosystem, extra agent or fictitious compartment mass conservation is justified. A direct bivariate model or ordinary hierarchical multi-output regression with the same control state can express this hypothesis. Shared-denominator observation modeling and contextual covariance are established statistics. **I have not identified an additional operation that clears the goal's major algorithmic-novelty requirement.** Good new data should not be forced into such a claim. The new task provides a testable need for intervention-conditioned functional prediction and consequential assay choices; whether that factorization and active choice outperform simpler methods is an empirical question.

Required same-information controls include a source drug mean with patient-level shrinkage; direct bivariate drug/state prediction; ordinary multivariate residual updating; standard low-rank/nearest-neighbor completion; fixed diverse probes; exploit-first and ordinary expected-value-of-information/active-learning choices; and equal-budget search over the same update choices. Source covariates, observed control state, purchased response pairs and total fitting/search opportunities must match. A scalar-only weak baseline cannot establish world or joint-readout necessity. The new CiPA ordinary multivariate result reinforces this requirement; its STOP does not determine this biologically different dataset's outcome.

## Falsifiable utility, inheritance and stopping conditions

Before any response conversion, a separate reviewed protocol must set patient-level source/development/later splits, permitted information and bundle costs, the shortlist size and the utility of meeting **both** absolute and selective depletion criteria. A sensible target is the number of jointly qualifying interventions in an unmeasured shortlist, with two-dimensional regret/Pareto reporting as supporting analysis. Numeric RCN/RCF thresholds, a useful gain margin and power have **not** been selected here; they require application justification and source-only calibration, not arbitrary numerical invention. Assay confidence intervals cannot be manufactured from averages alone.

First gate: under fixed probes, do joint outcome feedback and the authenticated control state improve actual held-out two-endpoint decisions beyond the strongest ordinary conditional/multivariate update? Then test active assay selection separately under the same total budget. Stop or narrow if strong simple controls absorb practical gain, if the effect is only scalar loss improvement without changed useful choices, or if the publisher's measurement/normalization limits invalidate the intended claim. Inadequate precision is inconclusive, not proof of practical equivalence.

The 95-patient conservative phase permits a real **patient-grouped** development/later task design in principle; the final allocation must follow a registered uncertainty/capacity analysis, not treating 135 samples or thousands of drug cells as independent learning tasks. Patient repeats are grouped. Procedure inheritance can be tested by improving a small updater/acquisition strategy on earlier patient groups, then applying it to independent later groups. To test improved improvement capacity, save old/new strategy checkpoints; reset both world and information to the same start; give identical new task streams, real feedback and search budgets; compare the quality and cost of the subsequent improvements. Retained fitted model weights alone are not such evidence. Ordinary meta-learning/warm starts/search remain controls. A finite 65-action bank supports bounded transfer, **not open-ended evolution**. No RSI or agent advantage is established by having enough rows to attempt this design.
