## Direct answer to root: one defensible information checkpoint

The first task must be a fixed-batch retrospective information-access experiment. Choose a fixed k-drug probe batch using source-task information, before seeing the new patient's assay-derived context or outcomes. At a single declared 24-hour information checkpoint, reveal the raw derived RCN/RCF pairs for that batch together with the patient's DMSO-control composition. Then nominate a fixed-size shortlist of unrevealed drugs from the frozen common menu. All methods receive the same information and are charged the same control-assay bundle and k joint response bundles.

The patient's 24-hour composition is explicitly prohibited from selecting that first probe batch. A control-first culture followed by new drug cultures would need evidence for available material, stability, assay comparability and turnaround that this corpus does not authenticate. It is outside this gate. Sequential reveals of stored measurements could later test an information policy, but would remain retrospective replay of already completed fixed assays. Neither case establishes prospective adaptive wet-lab execution, monetary savings or turnaround savings.

This is a same-assay conditional prediction experiment. Control context and terminal responses share control measurements and the publisher's fixed phenotype measurement system. Calling terminal entries withheld does not make them independently measured biological validation. The common information checkpoint makes their use operationally explicit without removing this dependence.

## Exact endpoint and utility semantics

For an author-defined well, RCN = 1 - malignant retained-cell count under drug / median malignant retained-cell count in matching controls. RCF = 1 - malignant retained-cell fraction under drug / median malignant retained-cell fraction in matching controls. The released raw sheets average these derived scores across retained wells. Positive RCN means lower classified non-apoptotic malignant abundance; positive RCF means a lower malignant fraction relative to the retained population. RCF is not a healthy-cell survival measurement. Both scores depend on the malignant count and shared controls. Their averages cannot be inverted to recover exact average healthy-cell counts. No independent-evidence multiplication, compartment conservation, cumulative death, clonogenic survival, clinical safety or efficacy claim follows.

For fixed thresholds tau_N and tau_F, one transparent terminal utility is U(S,Y) = sum over a in S of 1[RCN_a >= tau_N and RCF_a >= tau_F], where |S|=L and S excludes every revealed probe. This counts jointly qualifying assay phenotypes. The intended unit of uncertainty and aggregation is the patient: average specimen utilities within each patient and then average patients, with patient-clustered uncertainty. Repeats must remain in one split. A sample-weighted alternative would change the estimand and must not be selected after results.

No numerical clinically meaningful pair of thresholds is presently justified. The smallest next gate is source-only endpoint validity and decision-headroom assessment; it does not need a terminal performance claim. Freeze the utility family and the source-calibration procedure before source outcome conversion; freeze concrete resulting thresholds before any development/later outcome conversion. If source quantiles are used, the label is source-relative ex vivo joint depletion, not therapeutic qualification. Do not tune quantiles to maximize an algorithm's advantage. A threshold-free Pareto set alone does not uniquely select a fixed shortlist; hypervolume still requires a declared reference point. Neither silently resolves the utility choice.

The metadata review must precede numerical inspection. Subsequent authorized source-only checks should handle numeric versus annotation fields, nonfinite values, denominator/normalization warnings, and author QC exclusions with predetermined rules. Do not assume scores are bounded below by zero or clip to [0,1]. A stored numeric field is not proof of a reliable measurement. Per-well counts and denominator uncertainty are not supplied by the authenticated aggregate matrix; assay confidence intervals cannot be manufactured. Four wells are planned replicates in the plate design, not a certified count of retained observations after QC. The initial report's four-well acquisition shorthand is narrowed accordingly.

## Actual answer to the frontier reviewer's operation question

No additional operator beyond conventional contextual multivariate prediction and decision analysis is established. Let I contain source data, the paid control composition c and observed probe response pairs. A joint predictor yields q_a(I) = P(RCN_a >= tau_N, RCF_a >= tau_F | I). The ordinary terminal decision selects L unrevealed actions maximizing sum q_a(I). An ordinary Bayesian or regularized multivariate update conditions its joint response distribution on the measured pairs and c; the likelihood must allow dependence and must not pretend the two readouts are independent evidence.

For a later separately authorized myopic policy, define h(I) as that maximal expected terminal count over the still-unrevealed action menu. Choose a next query a by maximizing E[h(I augmented by Y_a)] - h(I), with a removed from the terminal menu after reveal and its joint-bundle cost charged. This is an ordinary knowledge-gradient/expected-value-of-information construction. It is not proposed as part of the first fixed-batch gate. It also does not specify a final executable statistical family, approximation, hyperparameters or search budget; those must be frozen in the eventual design.

Strong ordinary fixed-probe comparators are source drug means/shrinkage, a direct bivariate predictor with the same composition, and a regularized multivariate residual-conditioning model given exactly the same paired probes and context. Eligible source-only low-rank/nearest-neighbor completion can be included in a prespecified conventional candidate set. Model/hyperparameter selection must use source-only patient-grouped validation, not later outcomes. Match fitting, tuning and search opportunities. A scalar-only weak control is insufficient. Source-only control-state ablations can assess incremental prediction value, but cannot identify a causal cell-composition mechanism.

Stop the new-mechanism promotion if no extra operation is specified or if its practical decision advantage is absorbed by the strongest matched ordinary comparator. A useful conventional task remains a valid narrower contribution. If confidence bounds are too wide relative to a preregistered useful margin, report inconclusive evidence rather than equivalence. Invalid normalization or unmanageable endpoint/QC limitations stop or narrow the task before fitting. No reserve-driven threshold rescue is allowed.

## Independent challenges to TDAL and ExGrad translations

I asked the frontier reviewer whether TDAL deletion/restoration hides the entire assay bundle, preprocessing/threshold statistics and inherited target-specific state. It must: otherwise the counterfactual state still contains removed labels. A source-frozen hit definition is necessary for the proposed operational use; a full-panel top-percentile benchmark label cannot be silently treated as known therapeutic utility. Hellinger-weighted continuation is a different objective from expected terminal joint-depletion count. Valuable exploitation can remain when prediction movement is small; prediction movement can also be harmful. There is no automatic value-preservation theorem for that translation. Cross model/updater and acquisition factors to identify which component contributes, without treating an imported acquisition network as a new operator.

I asked what makes ExGrad's three gradient directions and measured ranking comparisons biologically privileged. No such justification is established. Source-frozen target/control/all-cell objectives may be reasonable choices, but direction labels alone do not supply a new biological operation. A full same-span signed trust-region/subspace search receives the same information and action class and is an essential comparator. The affine local coefficient problem's optimum is not a guarantee about the finite nonlinear parameter update or independent later decisions. Charge comparison-label acquisition, all directional probe evaluations, validation use, retraining and search. Reusing the same comparison bank for repeated selection is not evidence of inherited improvement capacity. Negative coefficients alone are insufficient when correlated directions can yield the same net update.

The reviewer answered these challenges directly and agreed that no extra operator or demonstrated utility survives presently. This is a scoped assessment of the explicit translations and source operations, not exhaustive literature novelty certification or a theorem against finite-budget world models.
