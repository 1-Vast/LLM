## What this task can honestly measure

Raw averaged derived scores are:

`RCN = 1 - malignant_count_drug / median(malignant_count_control)`

`RCF = 1 - malignant_fraction_drug / median(malignant_fraction_control)`.

Both favor positive reductions. RCF is relative depletion, **not healthy-cell survival**. Shared numerators/controls make these coupled endpoints. Already averaged ratios cannot be inverted into authenticated mean healthy counts. The endpoints are image-classified retained non-apoptotic-cell phenotypes, not cumulative death, clonogenic or clinical outcomes. A fixed cohort-trained image classifier conditions the benchmark; a later patient split does not validate that sensor on unseen patients.

The publisher's **scaled** sheets use each sample's full-panel positive/negative extrema and would leak unpurchased outcomes. Only unscaled RCN/RCF can enter a sparse-access replay. The 13 control-composition columns contain nested parent/subtype definitions, not 13 disjoint conserved populations. Composition is measured in parallel **24-hour DMSO wells**, not a zero-time pretreatment state.

The agreed first task is a finite-catalogue information experiment: choose the initial fixed probe batch from source-only information, without that patient's later control composition. At a common checkpoint reveal those paired raw responses plus a charged DMSO-control composition bundle; nominate a disjoint, fixed-size unmeasured shortlist. Evaluate only against actual raw response measurements, balancing patients and clustering repeats. All original assays were physically performed; the replay changes information access. It establishes neither prospective adaptive culture timing nor actual material, monetary or turnaround savings. No fictitious repeated reset, schedule dynamics or independent sensor validation is added.

The scientific hypothesis is that joint response feedback and control composition improve useful simultaneous absolute/relative-depletion choices at this information budget. A direct bivariate model, ordinary multivariate conditioning, hierarchical/low-rank completion and standard value of information may fully explain the result. The supplied candidate is **not a distinct new operator**. Data qualification is GO; algorithmic innovation is NO_PROMOTION.

Uncertainty must respect patients and the absence of retained raw replicate outcomes.

Stop the task configuration if source-derived scores fail their declared validity/denominator contract, legal probe/terminal coverage fails, same-time information is used before available, or meaningful joint decision headroom cannot be shown. Do not rescue a failed numerical gate by moving thresholds, designs or splits. A source gate pass permits a conventional fixed-probe comparison; it does not promote a world architecture. If strong simple methods absorb practical utility gains, stop the corresponding world/agent expansion. Insufficient precision remains inconclusive rather than proof of equivalence.

For Level3, freeze old/new improvers and compare subsequent improvement quality/cost from the same world, information and policy start on independent later patient groups, charging all proposal/feedback/search costs. Warm starts, ordinary meta-learning, fixed improved policies and equal-budget search remain controls. A finite 65-action cohort cannot by itself establish open-ended evolution.
