## ALMANAC strongest challenge and narrowed answer

The skeptic asked whether a static pair-by-cellline matrix with Study/Plate but no measured dynamic cell state can support state-updater transfer, and whether complete physical curve bundles must be reconstructed without shared-control or derived-target leakage before any numerical test. It warned against treating combination rows as independent tasks.

I agree that the currently authenticated dictionary does **not** establish temporal state updates, physical experiment rounds, true chronological deployment or RSI. TestDate is planned experiment date. A bounded archive directory/header/design-field qualification is nevertheless justified for an ordinary intervention-conditioned functional surface and finite feedback prediction, without requiring molecular state or a novel mechanism in advance. This must be named at that scope.

I challenged two overbroad requirements:

1. A declared scalar **published-entry reveal** can define a legal finite matrix/surface-completion test even when the true physical dose-curve purchase bundle is unknown. It does not establish actual experiment/query cost, independent scalar assays or lab savings. Those stronger acquisition claims require real plate/panel/control/bundle reconstruction. Unknown physical bundling limits claims rather than invalidating every descriptive archived-entry task.
2. Source cells may legally share a drug pair with target cells if the intended transfer is **known-pair finite-catalog prediction in a new cell background**. Removing all such source endpoints would redefine the task as unseen-pair generalization, which has not been requested. The forbidden leakage is target-cell/query response, target-derived ExpectedGrowth/Score and shared target-control information crossing the declared reveal cut. Cross-cell source endpoints are not automatically leakage.

The skeptic explicitly accepted both corrections and its prior wording about source/target pair sharing was narrowed. It also accepted that absence of molecular state does not forbid a static conditional functional world. Both support one bounded ALMANAC directory/header/design qualification, followed by a substantive decision rather than an architecture build.

## Necessary design information for the next qualification

The official schema's Study, PlateID, screener, compound sample identities, PanelNbr/CellNbr and concentration/unit fields must be inspected together. A cell line can be a target context for cross-cell transfer, but60cell labels are not60independent genetic ancestries/labs. Shared tissue origin, related cell lines, repeated studies and common controls must be reported; where required, higher grouping should supplement ordinary cell-heldout estimates. Lab-heldout evaluation with only a small lab count may be much narrower than cell-heldout.

An acquisition cut should distinguish one pair-dose row, a full measured dose grid, matched single-agent curves and whole plate results. A scalar reveal benchmark must say it is hypothetical masking of published entries; a plate/curve reveal must expose every result in that bundle consistently. Single-agent inputs cannot be treated as free target information for one model but denied to its direct baseline. Do not infer raw biological replicate noise from OD means.

ExpectedGrowth and Score are author-derived from target-response ingredients; they are not independent function labels or safe covariates for the same target combination prediction. Depending on the eventual estimand, known single-agent curves may legitimately supply a prespecified expectation baseline, but no hidden target combination residual may enter fitting or selection. Valid/filtering flags and missing grids must remain explicit; their upstream selection can limit deployment/population claims. Missing combinations cannot be simulated into real test labels.

The most defensible next finite experiment, only after design qualification, would test whether a few target-cell combination entries improve predictions of the remaining declared functional grid over strong same-information direct predictors, ordinary pooling/refitting, standard interaction-surface models and simple mixtures. It would test conventional conditional updating, not new inherited strategy or improved-improver. A failure to identify any honest disjoint task/reveal contract should stop that configuration. Failure to recover exact physical bundles alone need not prohibit a narrower explicit scalar-entry completion task.
