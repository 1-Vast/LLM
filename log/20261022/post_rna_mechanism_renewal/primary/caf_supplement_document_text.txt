a Correlation between CAF subtype abundance and Fges scores within TCGA-BRCA tumor samples. b Correlation between CAF subtype abundance and Fges scores within TCGA-SKCM tumor samples. c-d Evaluation of apCAF (c) and mCAF (d) abundance scored by CIBERSORTx within CMS subtypes of CRC. e ssGSEA-based validation of the robustness of the CIBERSORTx algorithm using the TCGA-COAD dataset. f Correlation between the abundance of apCAFs or mCAFs and xCellcalculated stromal scores in the TCGA-COAD dataset.
