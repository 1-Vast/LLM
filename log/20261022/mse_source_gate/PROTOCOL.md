## Utility and preregistered descriptive gates

No data-derived utility calibration: **hit=1[RCN>=0.50 AND RCF>=0.20]**. These fixed research screening levels precede all outcome decode. Parse literal scalar tokens as exact Decimal values for threshold/range comparisons; do not round to float64. A shortlist's utility is its five binary hits divided by5. Both endpoints are coupled measurements, not independent confirmatory assays. Numerical tolerance applies only to validity, not hit classification.

For each held-out source patient, construct per-drug success rates by averaging specimens within each of the other54patients, then averaging those54patient rates equally. Use exact Fraction arithmetic for all hit-derived rates, ranking ties and the gap gate; float displays are accompanied by exact fractions. The ordinary global reference picks the five highest rates among57terminal drugs; ties use the published drug strings' alphabetical order. Exclude every specimen of the target patient from this reference. Each specimen's oracle succeeds on min(5,total successful terminal drugs)/5. Average target specimen utilities within patient, then equally average55patients; retain each patient's selections and paired gap. Report55leave-one-patient-out label aggregations separately from zero predictive solver fits.

Pass only if all three hold: (1) all required values finite and in declared range; (2) mean oracle-minus-global gap>=0.10success fraction; (3) at least10distinct source patient groups contain at least one specimen with both successful and unsuccessful terminal actions. The latter two are **descriptive source opportunity/resource gates**, not tests of learnable feedback information, model effect size, precision or an independent result. A random unlearnable patient hit map can pass. No replicate-derived confidence interval or power claim is made.

Any failure stops this exact population/menu/split/budget/utility configuration. Preserve values/failures; do not rescue by dropping patients, changing probes, lowering thresholds or moving the margin. A pass permits only preparation of a separately frozen ordinary fixed-probe information comparison; it does not authorize a new architecture or novelty promotion. Patient-level precision, feedback-learnability, robustness and deployment applicability remain open.
