## What quantity is actually needed

For a frozen legal pre-feedback historyH and prospective feedbackZ, an update ruleU produces revised modelM'=U(M,H,Z), downstream acquisitionpolicy pi_U and possibly a futureupdate policy. One useful target is Delta(H)=E[V_env(pi_U)|H]−V_env(pi_0), charging feedback acquisition and all subsequent evaluations. If the acquisition itself changes a living culture, its physical transition belongs in env; a cloned Python model does not clone the culture. If we condition on a realizedZ after selecting it, the target is a different conditional revisionvalue; average exante value, realizedconditional value and selecting the best update on observed auditlabels must not be conflated.

In the simple one-step case with policies frozen independently of evaluation outcomes, Delta=E_H sum_a d(a|H)*mu(H,a), d=pi_U−pi_0. An equally informed strongdirectpredictor plus ordinary policy can realize this target; calling mu a worldmodel supplies no identification privilege.

## Sharper than requiring fullpolicy support

We need support only where the two candidate policies disagree, not every action in thelibrary. For boundedutility[0,1], suppose the responsemean is known on loggedsupportS but unrestricted elsewhere. The sharp conditional interval is

L=sum_{a in S}d_a*mu_a+sum_{a notin S}min(d_a,0),
U=sum_{a in S}d_a*mu_a+sum_{a notin S}max(d_a,0).

Its width is sum_{a notin S}|d_a|. This follows by assigning each unobserved mean independently to0or1 according to coefficient sign. Policyagreementd=0 cancels completely even if that action is never logged. The executed two-world interval[-1/2,+1/2] haswidth1 and opposite signs. This is ordinary partialidentification and contrast-specific support, not a new theorem claimed for MAESTRO. A rule proposing only an unsupported tinyprobability change may have a narrow uncertaintyinterval but cannot claim an identified positivegain unless the lowerbound is positive under theactualcontract.

In one-stepform, sum_a d_a*mhat_a+d_A/q_A*(Y−mhat_A). The average difference in observed campaignutility identifies a policy/updater-package effect. It does not identify individual-cell counterfactualeffect or separate WM from acquisition/search components without additional interventions. Cluster/plate/culture interference requires actualrandomizationunits, not pretending aliquotdata are independent.
