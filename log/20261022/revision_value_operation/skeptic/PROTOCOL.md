# Revision-value identification falsifier

Written before own code execution or current peer reads. No biological values, newdataset, weights, reserve or stoppedmethod restarted.

Test one-step utility of a frozen old/revised choice policy in a bounded two-action artificial assay. Logging selects actiona only. Both constructed worlds have outcomea=1/2, but outcomeb=0or1. Oldpolicychoosesa, revisedpolicychoosesb. Their observed log distributions are exactly identical while revisionvalue has opposite signs. Enumerate ordinary DRcontrast at fixed nuisance predictions: it must give identical answer on the identical logs, and cannot be unbiased in both worlds. This demonstrates absent support rather than generic failure of DR.

Then evaluate legal randomized logging q(a)=q(b)=1/2 by exact rational expectation: standard signed importance-weight and DRcontrast identify both worlds. More generally compute sharp partial-identification interval for any uncovered policy-disagreement mass under utility[0,1]. Contrast policy equality requires no support because its contribution cancels. Include an epsilon-overlap finitevariance example to show mere positivity need not make finitebudget estimation useful.

All calculations exact Fractions, no stochastic simulation/model training. These are conventional identification calculations and a new concrete falsifier, not a novel estimator or biologicalresult. Sourcepolicy learning and global transport remain additional assumptions; randomized feedback does not establish an improvedimprover.
