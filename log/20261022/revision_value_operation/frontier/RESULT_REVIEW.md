## Strongest opposing interpretation and why it limits the claim

The strongest objection is that this is a test of one particular conditional transfer estimator, not a test of the conditional information content of Middle. Equal-weight Early/Middle kernels change feature geometry and shrinkage; extra features can worsen a finite-sample estimator even when they contain useful information. Joint pair holdout leaves at most one source in the target condition, and the one changed condition has none. Other conditions' mapping may not transport biologically. Consequently attributing the observed error directly to an absence of Middle information would overreach.

The sample is also too small and dependent to support a general performance difference: seven pairs are not seven independent culture experiments, the three conditions share source information and are not automatically three independent study replicates, and there is one source study. Macro averaging gives the single Abema_WT pair the same weight as each three-pair condition by design. The method gap is therefore concentrated, not broad consistent deterioration across conditions. Unknown assay fitting errors can change near-orderings; the present result has no error model or uncertainty interval. Missing outcomes may be informative, but their mechanism was not authenticated and cannot now be guessed to salvage the gate.

These limitations constrain scientific generalization; they do not reverse a frozen allocation decision or authorize post-outcome weight/feature/censoring searches on the same tiny set. A future different hypothesis would need genuinely independent evidence and a new contract, not relabeling this stopped configuration.
