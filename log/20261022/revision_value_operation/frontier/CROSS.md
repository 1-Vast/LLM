# Actual cross-review of revision-value positions

2026-10-22. Conducted after all three independent drafts were sealed. Read skeptic/INDEPENDENT.md and biology/INDEPENDENT.md and exchanged concrete questions and replies directly. No new source search, synthetic experiment or response access was needed.

## Frontier versus skeptic

I challenged the skeptic's sharp unsupported-mean interval being read as a finite-data confidence interval. Its displayed endpoints assume the observed-law means on supported actions are known. Actual sparse random feedback still has sampling uncertainty there. The skeptic accepted and committed to distinguishing the population identification region from finite-sample confidence coverage. Both retain the substantive fact that support is needed on the policy *difference*, not the union of all actions used by either policy; canceling common policy mass can remain unidentified in absolute value without affecting a fixed contrast.

The skeptic challenged overgeneralizing my fresh-audit/cross-fitting repair to arbitrary later searches. I accepted: a single held-out split or cross-fit does not license repeatedly selecting the latest updater using its scores. The pipeline must be frozen relative to the audit, or later choices require fresh evaluation/appropriately predictable sequential inference. In my exact selection example the incumbent is fixed uniform; the selected policy depends on source A,Y, but each arm has true mean .5. Therefore the selected policy's conditional and ex-ante value difference are both zero. The fresh independent audit estimates that selected revision; it cannot be reused indefinitely to select further revisions while retaining the same claim.

The skeptic and I agree that marginal action frequencies across histories cannot substitute for reachable-history support in a multi-step policy contrast. This exchange neither proves actual data lack support nor supplies a new estimator. Its purpose is to avoid treating classical identification conditions as empirical guarantees.

## Frontier versus biology

I challenged the biology draft's literal identification of arbitrary realized paired revision value with classical knowledge gradient. With arbitrary fixed U and D and a restricted post-feedback action set, it is paired policy evaluation / a value-of-information contrast. Classical KG is a special case under Bayesian updating and the corresponding optimal terminal decision. The biologist accepted this narrowing and agreed the faithful counterpart must receive the exact same update, selector, available actions and observed feedback; a generic KG name does not establish pointwise equivalence by itself.

The biology reviewer then challenged two conditions in my proposed score. I accepted that q must be conditional on the legal pre-audit history, and d must be frozen before the audit outcome. Learning piU from a feedback record and scoring it with that same record is precisely the selection bias identified by the exact counterexample. Fresh feedback is interpretable as evaluation at the same target history only for static catalogs or comparable independent physical units. If treatment alters a living culture, a subsequent assay on that altered culture is not an unobserved original-history branch; a genuine branch or authenticated dynamics is needed.

I further clarified that the difference-of-DR *algebraic identity* holds for the same realized d even when d is selected using the record. What fails then is the unbiased-expectation argument, not the subtraction identity. The 216 exact expectation checks in my independent audit deliberately use fixed d. All roles accept that externally justified biological structure can add identification, but a world model cannot simply certify its own unsupported outcomes.

## Consequence

NO_CANDIDATE remains scoped to the literal operators audited. We retain useful distinctions between direct use of a measured result and revision of unmeasured predictions, between conditional revision value and whole-update-algorithm value, and between partial identification and finite-sample precision. None justifies another module, reward-shaping revival or protected-data opening. The separate CDK code review is a frozen narrow functional opportunity experiment, not a test of a novel revision-value estimator or RSI.
