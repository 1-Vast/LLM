# Prospective access boundary

Qualification only. Download pinned author processed fitness, single-fitness and GI tables; inspect CSV column names, row identifiers and shape only. Do not convert or inspect assay values. Download exact official GEO filtered identities, genes and barcodes; inspect column names and string perturbation identity membership only, not per-cell numerical fields. Matrix numerical payload acquisition and decoding deferred until protocol. Downloading notebook JSON retains embedded original outputs, but only source cells are decoded for method review; original outputs are not our experiment.

Endpoint candidate is author gene-average double growth (processed_fitnesses), with GI secondary. Single-growth priors must be available to all arms. Matched guide-specific fitness is a separate sensitivity endpoint, not interchangeable with gene-average labels. Primary already publishes transcriptomic side-information Maxide: no novelty claimed for that capability. Data selection is conditional on growth-hit genes; no unbiased genome-wide discovery claim. All unordered pairs, mirrored cells and constituent guide measurements must stay in one split. Genes are seen at baseline in the proposed pair-generalization task.
