## Positive result and exact scope

There is now a credible **development application signal** for predicting the published Jurkat metabolic endpoint from limited revealed entries. On identical unobserved doses 10/100/1000 nM, nested source-selected B0 MSE is 0.0285660 and B1 MSE is 0.0157127, a 44.995% observed reduction. B1 reveals only the published 10000 nM entry. The nested selector chose ordinary Gaussian conditioning for all 42 outer scaffold groups at B1. Thus the signal is not a new agent or world model: it is a strong ordinary source prior combined with target functional information.

The paired mean gain is 0.0128533, adjusted two-budget 97.5% bootstrap interval [0.0020421,0.0300054]. Gains are positive at all three query doses (0.0005639,0.0118436,0.0261525), and 34/48 drugs improve. Removing each of the three largest absolute-gain drugs separately leaves positive mean gains (0.0119595,0.0149813,0.0070840). The result is therefore not eliminated by any one of these three deletions, although their influence remains material. Against the explicitly post-hoc best B0 method the B1 gain also remains positive in the adjusted interval. This is conditional exploratory evidence from 48 curves/42 groups, not independent confirmation, biological measurement-noise uncertainty, or general-population validity.

Biologically, an observed high-dose metabolic response evidently carries predictive information for this dataset's intermediate-dose normalized endpoint after applying an ordinary source-derived curve covariance. That is compatible with shared potency/amplitude structure, common assay normalization or other dependencies; the experiment does not identify their causes. It also does not mean a high-dose measurement can always be copied: the B1 simple support-high constant worsens MSE from 0.0326200 to 0.0596939. The useful object is the ordinary source-conditioned response relationship, not a universal flat extrapolation rule.

## Endpoint meaning and quality limitations

Metabolic Relative spans 0.042--1.254 over these development entries, with mean absolute deviation from DMSO baseline 0.127567. This demonstrates nonconstant published endpoints despite the paper's qualitative 'marginal' description; it does not establish noise-separated biological effects or therapeutic utility. Confluency and Cytotoxicity were assessed only descriptively under the frozen scope and cannot replace the primary endpoint. A value called Cytotoxicity below one is not self-evidently increased killing; its analyzer direction/correction/export semantics remain unresolved. Metabolic change alone is neither immune signaling nor clinical safety.

All relative-zero entries equal one here, but a constructed normalized baseline can do that by definition. It supplies no independent functional replicate/noise estimate. Raw/Relative inferred-denominator maximum relative spread is 0.0084172 for metabolic activity and 0.0012180 for confluency. These are incompatibilities with *exact* constant-denominator arithmetic on stored displayed numbers, not proof of incorrect normalization.

If Relative was rounded to three decimal places, an error of up to 0.0005 is proportionally large near 0.042 (~1.19% per value), so the metabolic maximum spread of ~0.84% is not automatically beyond rounding. Similarly the confluency minimum 0.588 yields ~0.085% one-value rounding uncertainty. But global minima and global maximum spreads may belong to different drugs, so these summaries cannot demonstrate rounding explains the discrepancies. A precise frozen check would use each drug's stored raw A_i and relative r_i: test whether a common positive D lies in every interval [A_i/(r_i+0.0005), A_i/(r_i-0.0005)], with correct open/tie boundaries and any separately authenticated raw rounding allowance. A nonempty intersection demonstrates compatibility under that rounding model, not the actual exporter implementation. No such per-drug test was performed by this reviewer, and three-decimal storage precision must be authenticated rather than presumed universal.

No raw functional repeats were authenticated, so the bootstrap cannot distinguish measurement noise, exported normalization dependence and biological drug variability. Published endpoint predictability remains real within its declared task even while its upstream provenance limits interpretation.
