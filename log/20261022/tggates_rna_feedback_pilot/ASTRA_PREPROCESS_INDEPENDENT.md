# Independent preprocessing decision before numerical RNA

2026-10-22. No real CEL payload has been read or transformed by this role. This independent decision precedes cross-review and is to be hashed before that review.

Use one array at a time with R 4.5.2, Bioconductor 3.22 affy::mas5(normalize=TRUE, sc=500), manufacturer's hgu133plus2cdf probe-set definition. This uses each array's own background, PM/MM correction, robust probe summarization and trimmed-mean scaling, without a shared quantile target, fitted probe reference, or another array. Thus preprocessing cannot mix held-out High or development arrays into training. Joint RMA of all arrays is unsuitable for the stated source-fold boundary. Single-array scaling measures relative normalized transcriptional state, not absolute RNA per cell; cytotoxic global shifts remain a limitation.

Freeze the transform as log2(pmax(signal, 1)). It agrees with log2 above the fixed floor, is defined for every finite negative/zero signal, and limits low-intensity numerical instability. The floor is fixed in MAS5 scale-500 signal units, before any RNA values. asinh would preserve signed low signals but changes interpretation across the entire scale; do not choose between these after seeing data. A nonfinite raw signal is a technical failure: retain receipt, stop the run, and do not silently replace/drop it. Log counts of signals <=0 and <1, raw finite min/max and prespecified quantiles. Retain every finite probe-set; do not select by intensity, detection calls, outcome association, or development performance.

MAS5 detection calls (fixed alpha1=.04, alpha2=.06, tau=.015) are descriptive QC only, never a sample/probe exclusion criterion. All arrays and both replicates remain. Invalid CEL format, wrong chip type, mismatched identity, duplicate/missing probe sets, unexpected package version, nonfinite signal, or corrupt download are hard technical failures, not biological QC selection. Do not automatically substitute a different array. Any correction requires an explicit audit revision.

Use stable manufacturer probe-set IDs as the primary state coordinates, sorted deterministically; no data-driven probe-to-gene selection. If the four-gene comparator is required, fix its symbol-to-probe mapping from an independently versioned official platform annotation before any RNA transformation, and average the mapped probe log signals without choosing the best probe. Annotation absence is an implementation blocker for that comparator, not permission to select probes by outcome. Condition state is the mean of its two replicate log signals minus the mean of the same experiment's two matched 2h control log signals. Retain the shared-control identity and cost. Every strong direct/auxiliary/joint comparator receives the identical state representation and paid information.

Source-trained variable selection, scaling, PCA/dictionary, shrinkage and models remain nested by source proxy group. Preprocessing is array-local and may be cached across folds; learned cross-array representation may not. Source-held-out High must not inform its fold's representation; development has Middle+controls only. No Low, development High, later RNA or later function is allowed. This method does not erase earlier development functional exposure or prove mediation.
