## Why not call ordinary all-array RMA source-only

Freezing only a quantile target is insufficient if median polish/probe effects are then estimated jointly with validation arrays. A prospective frozen-RMA alternative must learn **both** normalization distribution and probe-effect/summarization parameters from each source training fold, then apply them without validation/future arrays. preprocessCore determine.target/use.target provide the quantile step, not the entire frozen summarizer. This is more code/audit work than the single-array route and should not be improvised as an unnoticed optimization.

Default external fRMA vectors offer a mature single-array alternative and can process small batches. Their training provenance/version should be recorded; they are external pretrained parameters, not parameters learned solely from this pilot's source. Lack of perfect provenance is a scope qualification, not automatic proof of contamination. Training new source-only fRMA vectors would require additional packages and batch/variance assumptions on only24source compounds; no need to add that complexity before testing the simplest established route. [fRMA documentation](https://www.bioconductor.org/packages/release/bioc/vignettes/frma/inst/doc/frma.pdf)
