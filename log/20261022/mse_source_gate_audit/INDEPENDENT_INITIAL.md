**Disposition: reasonable narrow descriptive source gate, conditional on precise definitions. No novelty or learnable-information promotion.** A fixed-batch retrospective information experiment is legitimate here; unsupported wet-lab timing/cost claims are unnecessary. The source-only eligibility and headroom gate can be small and useful if its conclusions stay narrow.

## Strongest issues before a protocol is written

**1. Oracle headroom is opportunity, not learnable between-patient information.** With binary joint success h=1[RCN>=0.5 and RCF>=0.2], define shortlist success fraction as the sum of five h values divided by5. A target oracle's value is min(5, number of successful actions)/5. An exact leave-one-patient-out global shortlist is the five drugs with highest patient-balanced training success rates, with lexical ties. The mean paired difference is a descriptive ex-post oracle gap. It can exceed0.10 even if every patient's successful drugs are an independent random set unrelated to permitted state or probe responses. Conversely, patients having both successes and failures demonstrates within-patient outcome variation, not transportable predictive information. Do not call these two checks evidence that control-state or joint-feedback learning will work. A later fair fixed-probe experiment must answer that.

**2. Fix the unit before computing anything.** All retained specimens of a patient stay in one partition and are excluded together in every LOO baseline. A natural convention is to compute a shortlist utility per specimen, average specimens within patient, then average patients. Train global drug success rates with the same patient balancing. The target oracle is specimen-specific if the actual recommendation is specimen-specific; an oracle chosen once for all a patient's specimens answers a different question. Define “at least10 mixed-hit patients” explicitly, for example distinct patients with at least one eligible specimen containing both a successful and an unsuccessful action. This is a structural diversity check, not a power calculation or ten independent treatment experiments.

**3. Before probes are chosen,65-action headroom is only an opportunity bound.** If the first eight probes have not been fixed, compute and label the descriptive gate over the full65 common actions. Do not imply that the same gap holds after those probes are removed from the57 eligible terminal actions. Later source-only probe selection can consume the successful actions and change terminal headroom. Freeze whether the current gate is full-menu only; a later fixed-probe protocol must recompute its own legal candidate/terminal population without moving this gate's threshold. Probe observations are information cost, not terminal discoveries.

**4. Eligibility must reflect the available measurement contract.** Paired finite raw RCN/RCF and an upper bound1+tolerance are defensible under the published nonnegative-count/positive-control-denominator definitions. Negative scores are legal; no clipping, zero-filling, lower bound0 or healthy-survival reconstruction is justified. Freeze the tolerance and treatment of annotations/formulas before conversion. Missing denominators, retained-well counts and detector-independent validation are known unavailable quantities: report them, rather than inventing an impossible validity requirement and then declaring this source invalid. An anomalous derived score may fail this exact score contract, but does not identify the underlying biological/QC failure.

**5. Composition units and hierarchy precede numerical range checks.** Authenticate the chosen sheet/columns and their stored fraction versus percentage convention from source text, headings and workbook format metadata before reading values. A number format can corroborate a convention but cannot establish biological normalization alone. Do not infer units by inspecting the observed source range or silently divide by100 after failure. Parent/subtype definitions overlap; no total across all13 columns should be forced to1. Declare input-field and missingness handling before source decoding. If authenticated composition metadata cannot resolve the convention, defer that state's numerical eligibility; this need not invalidate an outcome-only conventional task. No numerical composition from development/later groups may be read.

## Proposed conventions that are acceptable with narrow labels

The SHA256-deterministic **55source/20development/20later patient split** is reasonable if salt, string encoding, patient-ID canonicalization, sorting/ties and retained-specimen mapping are sealed first. It is a declared algorithmic partition, not clinical chronology. The existing cohort-trained phenotype detector means later response inference is conditional on that fixed measurement system.

The fixed **RCN>=0.5, RCF>=0.2** levels are interpretable research policy: at least50% classified retained-malignant abundance reduction and20% relative malignant-fraction reduction, on the released average-score definition. RCF0.2 is not20 percentage points or80% healthy-cell survival. These coupled thresholds are not independent evidence, per-well significance or clinical efficacy. Freezing them now avoids threshold-selection after viewing response distributions. A gate failure should stop this exact configuration; neither a pass nor failure establishes general impossibility or novelty.

An average oracle-minus-LOO-global gap **>=0.10 success fraction** and **>=10 distinct mixed-hit source patients** may be frozen as resource-allocation heuristics. They do not establish effect precision or the eventual improvement margin for a trained model. Report the actual per-patient gaps and eligibility counts; do not manufacture confidence intervals from four planned wells. LOO count/rank construction is a descriptive empirical baseline, so “zero-fit” should mean zero predictive solver fits, not literally no data-estimated summary.
