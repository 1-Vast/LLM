## Concrete operation and why it matters

Target discovery often asks whether inhibiting a tumor gene would sensitize a tumor to immune attack. A tumor-only IFNγ response or higher antigen presentation can be measured without reproducing all immune-cell interactions, but does not exhaust recognition and co-stimulation. CD58 is a concrete counterexample: the primary abstract reports that its loss confers immune evasion without compromising MHC expression, and that its protein expression is not induced by IFNγ. Thus, a rule that promotes interventions exclusively from MHC/IFNγ surrogate improvements can miss a functionally relevant channel.

The proposed finite-budget operation is: after a limited set of actual tumor-perturbation/co-culture survival observations, select the next tumor perturbation to nominate for **immune-specific sensitization**, accounting for ordinary growth toxicity. Terminal feedback must be relative tumor survival under immune co-culture, interpreted alongside matched tumor-only growth and IFNγ-only conditions. For a knockout target the direction of drug action, target engagement, compensation and druggability remain additional untested questions; KO sensitization is not evidence that a small-molecule inhibitor will reproduce it.

This is a concrete missing scientific capability, but **I have not established a matched-input finite-budget deficit of ordinary prediction/refitting**. The potentially valuable hypothesis is that a representation of recognition routes shares information across perturbations and effector contexts more efficiently. That hypothesis remains empirical, not proven by the existence of distinct biological pathways. The alternative that a direct predictor with the same proteins, perturbation identities and context interactions is sufficient is strong and must be carried intact.

## Explicit state, feedback, update and decision

- State: a posterior or ensemble over maps from tumor perturbation g, measured tumor surface state x and immune context e to surviving tumor fraction (or a rigorously defined relative-fitness screen endpoint); include ordinary growth under no immune exposure. Observed cells are survivors, not longitudinally matched original cells.
- Feedback: a paid bundle contains the gene/guide assignment, the allowed co-culture endpoint, and its actual controls and replicate information. Reading another published contrast computed from the same source counts is not automatically a new experiment.
- Minimal update: condition or refit this map using newly released functional bundles. A putative structural version can route x through antigen presentation and contact/co-stimulation variables, but this decomposition by itself is ordinary structured prediction. No unvalidated claim that 'contact effect = co-culture minus IFNγ' is allowed: cytokine concentrations, exposure and selection differ.
- Decision: maximize expected immune-specific tumor depletion at fixed bundle budget, with a predeclared penalty/constraint for ordinary growth impairment. Before defining a numerical utility, authenticate the released endpoint/normalization and whether quantitative survival or only pooled relative fitness is available. No clinical threshold or current utility margin is invented here.
- Inheritable object: the representation and/or acquisition rule. Transferring its fitted parameters is model transfer. Updating how candidate rules are generated or selected can test Level3 only by old versus new improvers from the same fresh downstream start and equal total search/data/feedback costs.

This is an executable **conventional reference specification**, not a claim of a new algorithm. A novel candidate must supply a different beneficial finite-budget update/decision operation before expanding it.

## Minimal falsification, nearest baseline and counterargument

The immediate reproducible check completed here is metadata/algebra only. `check_algebra.py` uses exact Fraction arithmetic and no biological files: under the synthetic contact model S=1−e·m·c, unchanged antigen presentation m can coexist with changed survival through contact c; no effector removes that difference. **The equally informed ordinary direct interaction predictor reproduces every binary state exactly.** This simultaneous constructive witness and baseline is deliberately stronger than a generic expressivity objection: it demonstrates what must be measured and why biological pathway naming is insufficient to earn method novelty. A further common-denominator raw-ratio identity shows that (C/U)/(I/U)=C/I; it warns against charging redundant published contrasts as separate feedback. It does not assert that MAGeCK scores, separate normalizations or their uncertainties obey that identity. Five recorded assertions pass; two ratio assertions check the same algebraic fact and are not independent scientific tests.

If a genuinely distinct update operation is later proposed, a minimal biological falsifier could mask whole perturbation families, reveal only a fixed number of co-culture bundles, and ask whether its selected sensitization targets beat the strongest equally informed ordinary model on unrevealed functional endpoints. The useful-gain criterion, family split, source/dev reserve and accounting must be frozen before numerical access. A surrogate-only comparator establishes only surrogate insufficiency; it cannot establish world-model benefit. Known CD58 validation is already designer-exposed and can be a mechanism sanity check, never fresh confirmation.

Required nearest alternatives: supervised gene/context interaction models and regularized low-rank models; the same model ordinarily refit after feedback; continual learning; uncertainty/KG/EVSI-style acquisition; fixed or learned mixtures of functional and molecular predictors; and equal-budget program search over representations/acquisition rules. Give every comparator the same protein/RNA features, prior pathway knowledge, functional observations, budgets and selection opportunities. World effect requires matched decisions with/without the structured state; agent effect requires adaptive versus strong fixed acquisition; inheritance requires fresh contexts and conventional transfer/CL controls; joint effect requires a factorial comparison; Level3 requires new/old improvers from identical fresh starts. Three effector ratios in one patient model do not supply independent later patient tasks or eight independent feedback rounds.

Strongest counterargument: CD58 is an already known mechanism, so a direct protein-feature predictor or simple context interaction may solve the task as well as any mechanistic world. This is valid and blocks novelty promotion now. Constructive response: the real target is learning **when a molecular surrogate ceases to transport to a functional intervention**, potentially across immune contexts, using sparse real functional feedback. A finite-budget structural method could help there without uniquely identifying all biology; it deserves a test only after its operator, measurement contracts and strong alternative are explicit. Lack of a current novel operator is not evidence that the biological problem lacks value.
