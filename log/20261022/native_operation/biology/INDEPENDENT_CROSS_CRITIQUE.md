## Composition proposal

The exact second-order cancellation and explicit conventional reduction are coherent within the stated smooth-vector-field model. A factorial measurement of order effects can be valuable without molecular identifiability or true within-cell reset. Thus I do not reject randomized matched parallel populations simply because the same cells cannot be reset.

My strongest concern is **what makes the learned measured-state operation portable**. Drug removal from medium need not remove intracellular drug, target occupancy, covalent binding or downstream adaptive state. Measured marker coordinates need not form a closed Markov state. In that case f0/fA/fB on the chosen measured coordinates are not context-independent fields shared across the six histories. A six-endpoint contrast remains an observable effect and may still be useful, but calling it a transferable bracket field requires a closure assumption beyond confirming its value at one start. A duration check can reject some failures but cannot certify hidden-state closure generally. The same concern can be handled statistically by a strong schedule-history predictor; requiring mechanistic completeness would be excessive, but hiding history from the ordinary baseline would be unfair.

The second gap is the intervention-decision bridge: nonzero marker noncommutation need not improve apoptosis or survival, especially when measurement is on selected survivors or changes in composition. A direct functional endpoint measured in parallel randomized wells can suffice; longitudinal tracking is not mandatory. Utility should therefore be tested directly, not inferred from bracket magnitude or transcriptional novelty. The proposed uncertainty/utility-sensitive acquisition is reasonable but its utility sensitivity is not yet authenticated from a real functional observation contract.

Necessary answer: specify the narrowest legal empirical claim when state closure/washout is unverified; explain which actual measurement would distinguish useful functional order effect from marker-only noncommutation without requiring a fully identified mechanism. No new source search is requested. Given exact ordinary signature equivalence and absent qualified bundles, NO_PROMOTION remains warranted, not a universal failure of order-aware worlds.

## Gradient-improver proposal

The exact ordinary learned-optimizer reduction is decisive against algorithmic novelty in this stated rule. A learned beta can nevertheless meet an operational Level3 capability test; neither code rewriting nor a special representation is required. A patient-group randomized population endpoint can validate it without within-cell trajectories.

My strongest biological concern is **availability and reuse of the outer functional feedback**, not whether the synthetic derivative is mathematically correct. In sparse public replay, outer R is a sample estimate on an already measured panel; its derivative usually comes from a fitted differentiable predictor/loss, not a new exact biological oracle. A two-evaluation finite difference similarly requires two evaluable candidate worlds against an allowed labeled panel or two actual decision experiments, with different information and costs. Their distinction must remain explicit. Repeatedly using the same measured functional panel for many beta updates is legitimate source training, but does not make those queries independent biological feedback; later utility must be assessed on whole untouched biological groups.

Expression strata in selected surviving cells can reward a shift in survivor composition rather than a more accurate causal response. This does not invalidate predictive or decision benefits, and I do not require identifying selection mechanisms. It does require defining the evaluation population and not reading a better postselection molecular fit as improved killing. The candidate already directs training to downstream utility; the necessary falsifier is whether that external utility remains better under fresh functional groups, with the same labeled panel and optimization budget given to ordinary methods.

Necessary answer: distinguish derivative through a surrogate, reuse of measured source endpoint labels, and fresh biological feedback; specify whether the current operation requires any beyond the first two. State a minimum scientifically adequate later-task grouping without asserting unavailable donor/run metadata. Until those contracts exist there is no new fit justified; the current exact rule remains conventional.
