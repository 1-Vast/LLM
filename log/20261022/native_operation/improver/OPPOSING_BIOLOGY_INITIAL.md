Two biological ambiguities need explicit boundaries before treating this as drug discovery. First, CD58loss causing immune evasion supports a signed recognition requirement; it does not nominate CD58inhibition as a sensitization therapy. A sensitizing gene knockout also does not by itself validate a small-molecule inhibitor because action direction, target engagement, compensation and effector-cell effects differ. The immediate result can legitimately be genetic target prioritization, provided it is labeled that way and a future drug translation is separately validated.

Second, immune-specific depletion is not automatically identified by co-culture minus IFNgamma or by a normalized ratio. Co-culture survival integrates baseline growth, perturbation viability, contact/recognition and effector behavior; treatment-dependent growth and selection can change the available target population. Proper matched tumor-only controls support a defined conditional/relative-fitness decision target, but cannot be silently called a uniquely separated killing rate. The common-denominator algebra is correctly scoped to raw ratios and says nothing about independently normalized MAGeCK scores or their uncertainty. Cross-ratio/intervention comparisons require the actual endpoint contract before defining a utility or an oracle headroom gate.
