## Real observation, action and function

- Cells are seeded, incubated24h, simultaneously dosed and read after **72h** by CellTiter-Glo2.0. The primary assay measures ATP-associated luminescence, normalized to untreated/DMSO and blank controls to give inhibitory response. It is a functional growth/viability proxy, **not direct cell death**, cell count or a transition through observed molecular states.
- Nominal menu:109drug pairs,37individual drugs,7×7concentration matrices over a1000-fold range. Paper says82pairs screened across755lines and27across376lines, but also reports68816pair-line observations. The design product is72062, **3246more than68816**. Do not silently assume a fully observed82×755rectangle; QC/missingness definitions must explain this.
- AZD1775-containing matrices were reduced to **5×5** because the two highest doses were judged biologically irrelevant. Do not impute the removed49−25wells or call an arbitrary7×7action available.
- Recommended atomic query is **one measured pair–line matrix bundle**, with every released valid dose and its matched monotherapy references. Counting one well while revealing all49response values would confer hidden extra feedback. A well-level design is possible only after an explicit cost model for shared control and monotherapy wells.
- No query outside the actually measured/censored menu. No imputation used as experimental truth. All policies get the same eligible menu and observable metadata; costs include all newly revealed reference measurements.
- Primary task: for a new cancer-cell background, use at most8matrix bundles to prioritize5remaining measured pair-dose candidates at an agreed dose/clinical-exposure range. Reward can be the mean recorded inhibitory response of5nominations. A secondary, separately frozen benefit metric can subtract matched strongest single agent. Do not use max-over49dose synergy as the sole utility: selecting its maximum from a noisy unreplicated surface creates an optimistic target. Dose constraints must come from metadata, not the highest observed response.
- Seven control lines **A375,HT-29,PC-14,U-2-OS,SW620,C32,MHH-ES-1** were screened in technical triplicate on six occasions (up to18replicates, subject to QC). Do not infer18replicates for the other748lines. Author code averages by`DRUGSET_ID` and cell/drug/screen before replicate correlations. Recover actual occasion/stock/plate semantics rather than relabeling pooled rows independent.
- Baseline multi-omics/genotype may be linked through SIDM to public resources, but exact available versions and train-only feature processing are not locked here. **No matched intervention molecular state is established**. The old claim that MCL1/BCL2/MEK alternative inhibitors necessarily inhabit the common109pair menu is not verified: some are mentioned only as selected validation experiments and must not seed a same-family-rescue design.

## Legitimate tasks and inheritance

An episode may be a previously hidden **cell background** with a fixed measured action menu. A subsequent task is a new background (preferably a predefined cancer-type batch), not another hyperparameter fit, dose permutation, artificial seed or one of five screens. These are simulated knowledge-release episodes over a static observed world, not physical prospective experiments. Same-origin culture stocks and related lines create dependence.

Proposed design-only eligibility gate, to be frozen by root before any response: establish a common **≥64pair menu**, **≥16cancer-type blocks with≥8eligible distinctSIDMs each**, then assign four blocks to development, four to improve-the-improver training and eight to final evaluation by a fixed metadata-only hash. This is a feasibility proposal, not a registered completed split or a claim the source meets it. Keep all occasions/plates/repeated measurements of one SIDM in the same block. Remove known duplicate/misidentified lineage units. Seven replicate-rich control lines are calibration/reliability diagnostics, never manufactured final-task replication. If the gate is not met, stop this exact task; do not manufacture task independence by splitting wells or treating screens as episodes.

All arms see exactly the same released history and final backgrounds in the same fixed order. Distinguish (a) inherited training data, (b) predictor weights, (c) research/update program and (d) improver state. A final inherited program can be copied with predictor/data reset to the common start; otherwise its gains could be additional training. For improvement-of-improver, compare frozen`I1`and`I0`from the **same initial program+predictor**, with the same new feedback and equal edit/search budget on untouched final blocks. A final best program being better does not show its improver is better.

## Minimal world/agent formulation and reductions

For this static source, a permissible world is a latent context model `z_c = E(x_c,D_c)` with `p(y | z_c,a,dose)`; feedback updates posterior/function parameters. It need not pretend that an unmeasured molecular trajectory was observed. The agent chooses the next measured bundle or recommendation under a fixed budget. This is ordinarily **contextual response-surface learning + active experimental design**. Calling its latent representation a world model supplies no new identifying evidence. An equally expressive direct predictor with the same information can represent the same input-output map; a strong empirical win alone would not establish an extra causal state interpretation.

Required separable predictions: world contribution=fixed policy/world versus strong direct predictor; agent contribution=adaptive versus fixed/random/greedy acquisition using the same predictor; inheritance contribution=program carryover versus reset while data/weights matched; joint contribution=interaction in the world×agent×inheritance factorial; RSI contribution=`I1`improves fresh programs more per budget than`I0`, random edits and equal-budget program search. No data-source property alone guarantees any of these.

Necessary modules are only a versioned lookup/missingness mask, one strong predictor, a budget-accounted selector, and, **only if a precise new operator survives novelty review**, an inherited improver. A prompt editor, error bank, mixture selector or learned optimizer must be compared to its ordinary reduction. Do not add modules merely to fill these names.

## Cheapest decisive next test, success and stops

1. **Do the design census before any response.** Lock exact source-file identities, dose menus, SIDM and drug identity joins, missingness, monotherapy availability, matrixQCs, replicate/occasion keys, overlap with exposed Jaaks and existing sources. The sole success is a valid shared action menu and predeclared task partition; no model or novelty claim follows.
2. **Only with a separate pre-response protocol**, open a development-only subset and estimate decision headroom and reproducibility on the actual task. Keep all final responses inaccessible to candidate tuning. Stop if headroom or noise makes the registered practical decision margin unattainable. The prior0.012Jaaks opportunity remains a stopped result, not grounds to retune this source's task after opening.
3. A new mechanism must first survive a constructive reduction test against refit/CL/AL/simple mixtures/equal-budget search. If it is equivalent, stop the novelty claim before spending this new response asset. Do not repeat HTS001's learned shrinkage already matched by fixed mixtures.
4. If a non-equivalent operator remains, root should fix primary utility, practical margin, cluster unit, correction for comparisons and compute/API/labels before final opening. Proposed practical gate: ≥0.02absolute **and** ≥10%relative reduction in recommendation regret versus the strongest equal-budget ordinary arm, paired95%CI lower bound>0 across the eight held-out cancer-type blocks, with separate world/agent/inheritance/joint/improver tests. This threshold is a proposal, not yet a data-informed freeze. Freeze a maximum spend and stop when a constituent claim fails; no post-hoc endpoint or panel rescue.

Overall recommendation: **retain AZ2024 as a concrete, executable static drug-function task, conditional on metadata census and novelty qualification**. It cannot by itself confirm dynamic virtual-cell states or RSI. This source is sufficient to test a useful functional decision and honest inherited-program learning if a distinct operator exists; no further dataset catalogue is needed now.
