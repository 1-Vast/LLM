## A concrete local leakage warning

The saved official PRESAGE data README explicitly lists **DepMap CRISPR Gene Effect, DepMap24Q2** (DOI10.25452/figshare.plus.25880521.v1) among sources of its cached knowledge embeddings, alongside cross-system Perturb-seq. Its advertised functional evaluation includes expression effect size, phenocopy and gene-set scores. Those are not independent measured viability merely because called functional.

Therefore cached gene embeddings must NOT be assumed label-free when evaluating DepMap fitness. A gene vector that includes a gene's effect across the eventual evaluation lines may directly encode those targets even if a downstream regression split is clean. One must verify actual feature construction and held-out line/gene exposure, remove the fitness-derived channel, or use explicitly source-only reconstruction. This is a risk grounded in source documentation, not evidence the old P0 used that cache: its protocol explicitly excluded the response-containing cache. Give strong direct baselines the same legitimate external information, but giving both sides leaked outcomes does not turn leaked evaluation into an independent capability test.

## Which claim is being tested

There are three distinct levels. First, measured perturbation-state features may improve prediction of independent functional outcomes. Second, they may encode **context-specific** response beyond general essentiality/gene identity and baseline cell sensitivity. Third, actual or predicted state may be necessary for effective sequential updating and inherited research strategy. The first does not establish the second or third. An initially small ordinary experiment may legitimately target only the first or second without inventing an original operator or obtaining a complete RSI campaign bank.

The concrete useful application is prioritizing genetically vulnerable targets in a specified cancer-cell background. Genetic repression/knockout fitness is not automatically a drug-response or therapeutic-window endpoint. CRISPRi and knockout perturbations of the same named gene are related interventions, not exact treatment matches; intervention class, timing and line/culture identity must remain explicit. Cross-context or CRISPRi-to-KO transport may be a worthwhile estimand if disclosed, rather than automatically invalid.

## Minimum source evidence

One needs an authenticated multi-perturbation state artifact with gene/guide and context identifiers, processing/control definition and missingness/QC, plus a separately generated functional readout whose identity and numeric scale are known. Full raw single-cell reads and every instrument replicate are not compulsory; traceable target-by-state-feature pseudobulk can suffice. A bulk CRISPR screen can provide an independent assay despite sharing gene targets, whereas a viability label inferred from the same scRNA guide abundance or transcriptome does not supply independent function.

For DepMap, pin release and the actual cell-line identifiers/aliases; verify overlap with the Perturb-seq backgrounds instead of inferring overlap from cancer types. Verify the exact fitness score and its normalization, source libraries and replicate construction. Do not assert a specific release/model correction is independent before checking it. A line annotation match is not proof of identical biological sample, but separate matched-line cultures can support a clearly labeled retrospective transport study. Baseline expression, target state, functional endpoint and candidate-selection covariates have different roles and costs.

Multi-context state must actually cover multiple perturbations in multiple matched contexts; a list of many contexts with nonoverlapping genes is not automatically a transferable panel. Exact effective overlap and line/gene missingness must be determined from identities before score-driven sample selection. A few contexts need not forbid a scoped exploratory test, but they limit held-out-background precision. Thousands of cells do not create thousands of independent contexts.

## Essential-gene selection and survivor/count circularity

Selecting perturbations because of known essentiality creates a conditioned population. It does not forbid continuous fitness prediction within that population. Freeze the selected universe and selection source, retain meaningful nonuniform labels, report missing/failed perturbations, and limit extrapolation. Housekeeping positive controls plus nontargeting controls are not representative broad negatives. Do not tune a panel's essentiality cutoff or drop severe effects after seeing prediction error.

If target selection used DepMap scores, the same release's labels were involved in ascertainment. Distinguish an exploratory conditional association from a blind independent target-discovery claim, and provide source-gene mean/known-essentiality controls. A model can predict large transcript changes or global stress for essential genes without predicting context-specific functional differences. High pooled correlation over gene×line rows may merely recover shared gene essentiality and cell-line means.

Surviving cells' state reflects both direct response and selection. This does not make state predictors scientifically useless: a post-perturbation survivor signature can predict a separately measured outcome. It prevents claiming that the signature represents pre-death state or identifies a causal mediator without further evidence. Low representation, failed assignment and dropout may reflect death, infection, capture, QC or guide efficiency. Missing state is not a zero-effect label. A fitness target constructed from those same counts is a circular evaluation of the claimed independent function, though it could be transparently studied as an abundance-prediction task.

Separate two information contributions. A count/mask-only arm gets cell/guide counts, representation, UMI/depth, assignment/QC and declared sampling variables. A state-value arm additionally gets normalized expression features at matched aggregation/capacity. Compare against relevant gene identity, source fitness, baseline-line state and nonfitness annotations. This tests whether apparent expression benefit survives known acquisition/selection channels; it does not demand artificially erasing all genuine death-related biology. Shared artifacts can remain, so a positive does not certify causality.

## One minimal discriminating capability test, conditional on source qualification

Keep one ordinary regularized predictor and a prespecified held-out-context evaluation over a common metadata-defined gene universe. Source-context independent fitness trains it; target-context state is observed and charged, while target functional outcomes remain evaluative. Compare: the strongest same-information gene/source-fitness + baseline-context + count/mask predictor, versus exactly that predictor plus measured perturbation-state values. Tune only source data. No foundation-model or agent stack is required.

Report target-context functional prediction and target nomination utility with a scientifically defined fixed budget, alongside a context-specific residual comparison against source-gene-mean prediction. Show whether the increment exceeds gene-averaged essentiality/global line sensitivity. If evaluating a context where each gene was seen in source, call it held-out-context transfer, not unseen-gene generalization. A separate unseen-gene claim would require its own grouped gene holdout; requiring simultaneous novel gene and novel context is not mandatory for the narrower test.

A correspondence diagnostic, if registered, should break the gene-context state association while preserving intended nuisance/gene structure and rerun source preprocessing/training. Globally renaming gene IDs consistently is not a signal-destroying null. Such a control can probe correspondence, not prove a biological mechanism or provide an automatic valid p-value under arbitrary context dependence.

The central falsifier is failure of measured state values to improve a predeclared useful held-out functional contrast over those strong controls, or complete explanation by count/mask/gene-essentiality channels. A minimal positive would support bounded state-feature utility; it does not prove predicted states work, latent dynamics, causal mediation, active-agent utility or inherited improvement. If measured target state is used, all targeted perturbation assays must be charged; it is not a free forecast of unperformed interventions.

If source metadata instead show no matched independent function, no useful context/gene overlap, or embeddings contain unrecoverable held-out fitness, stop that particular evaluation contract before fitting. No universal sample-size floor, threshold, outcome-adaptive split or broad no-data theorem is invented here. Once a concrete valid contract exists, a small ordinary capability experiment need not wait for a new mathematical primitive.
