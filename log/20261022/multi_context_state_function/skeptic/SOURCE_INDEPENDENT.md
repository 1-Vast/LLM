## Why this is a real independent-function lead

Passages63 and65 describe K562 biological-replicate pooled growth screens using a dual-sgRNA CRISPRi library, extracted genomic DNA and PCR/deep sequencing of library-element abundance at growth-screen time points. This is separate from RNA profiling, not viability reverse-engineered from scRNA cell numbers. Passage59 identifies K562 dCas9-BFP-KRAB (KOX1-derived); this is compatible at the effector/cell-line level with K562 Perturb-seq rather than a CRISPRi-to-knockout substitution.

The official Figshare description identifies K562 genome-scale day8 and essential-scale day6 state artifacts, with raw and gemgroup-Z-normalized pseudobulk AnnData. It explicitly identifies .obs as population annotation and .var as expression-gene annotation. The author-linked raw genome-scale pseudobulk file is K562_gwps_raw_bulk_01.h5ad (file35774443,374587922bytes, MD5 4570b53c9d62ff6df281e622f0350060); the normalized counterpart is file35773217, MD5 a3dfaa94ea8724217f5ecb1e14a5f0c8. These identities authenticate an available state artifact. They do not establish its internal columns, actual paired overlap or independent-growth column provenance. A pseudobulk study does not require downloading tens of gigabytes of single-cell data first.

FigS1A reports gamma as log2 guide enrichment per cell doubling, biologically replicated with author-reported r=.94 across11056 constructs;50 discrepant genes were assigned missing growth values. FigS1B distinguishes dual-guide scores from historical single-guide growth and explicitly excludes secondary TSS constructs in one comparison. Therefore use exact population/construct identifiers and documented gene/TSS mapping; do not silently join gene-symbol strings or treat secondary-promoter constructs as independent held-out genes. Any replicate/TSS variants of the same target must stay together in gene generalization splits.

## Discrepancies and limits that must remain visible

The primary Methods describe K562 initial day7 and final17, while FigS1A/D describes day6→16. Both support a roughly10day growth interval, but the exact timing is unresolved. Do not pick the preferred account silently, call day8 RNA a prospectively collected pre-screen state, or claim a calibrated early→late trajectory. A retrospective static expression-to-separate-growth association is still meaningful. Sampling surviving cells may predict function but does not identify causal mediation or cell-death dynamics.

RPE1 is not an automatically matched second context. Passage59 explicitly says KOX1-derived RPE1 was used only for growth screens, whereas Perturb-seq used newly generated ZIM3-KRAB cells. RPE1 Methods state day6 harvest and the figure caption day7, with plasmid as reference rather than K562's two cellular time points; RPE1 readout is not described with K562's per-doubling normalization. A future cross-line task must name this effector/assay/time transport explicitly and cannot infer comparable scales from the shared word growth. The present minimum experiment can legitimately be K562-only and must be titled cross-gene, not held-out-context generalization.

## Prior selection and acquisition channels

Passages60–62 document ascertainment: genome-scale K562 includes expressed genes, transcription factors and DepMap20Q1 common essentials; essential-scale K562 is a selected essential population. Guides were chosen partly by earlier observed growth and phenotype, and growth screens were used to increase representation of strong growth-defect constructs during oligonucleotide synthesis. Hence the state acquisition process contains outcome-informed design. This does not erase the independent assay, but state cell counts, representation, guide efficiency and apparent precision can proxy preexisting fitness knowledge. General genome-wide therapeutic discovery and blind screening claims would be overstatements.

Freeze the authentic available candidate population, missingness and group mapping without looking at prediction errors. Include count/coverage/QC/acquisition controls and available prior essentiality/guide-selection information. Do not require unavailable exact manufacturing weights as a universal veto; disclose residual ascertainment. Avoid DepMap-containing cached PRESAGE embeddings unless their fitness exposure is audited or the channel removed. This K562-only primary-screen route does not require DepMap labels as the endpoint.

## A minimal useful test and what it would mean

After actual artifact qualification, an ordinary regularized predictor with target-group holdout can compare the same declared prior/acquisition features with versus without measured expression-state values. Fit preprocessing and any learned dimensional reduction within training groups; if using author's normalized artifact, disclose external/control-based normalization and examine its definition rather than assuming it is split-clean. Do not append all .obs fields indiscriminately: they may include growth outcomes, DEG statistics, prior annotation or outcome-derived metadata. Screen metadata by provenance before labels are used.

State-value benefit must beat count/QC/known-essentiality and simple low-dimensional state summaries such as transcriptome perturbation magnitude or stress/cell-cycle summaries when those are legally constructed. The paper already reports transcriptional response strength versus growth (Spearman rho=-.51) and771 transcriptomically perturbed genes with negligible growth effects; reproducing an univariate correlation is not a novel world-model contribution. A useful held-out multivariate increment is an ordinary capability result, which can warrant the subsequent update-rule study without pretending the baseline result itself is RSI.

Predeclare one evaluation contrast and biologically meaningful nomination budget before opening outcomes; specify a precision/utility stopping rule in the actual protocol instead of inventing a data-adaptive threshold here. A failure stops that frozen contract, not every possible state/function relationship. A positive concerns already measured perturbation states and separately measured genetic growth in selected K562 populations. All measured-state acquisition costs must be charged; there is no evidence of drug response, therapeutic window, prediction of unmeasured interventions, improved sequential updating, inheritance or improved improvers.
