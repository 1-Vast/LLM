## Scientific consequences and stop boundary

Keep the narrow K562 capability proposal valid but unexecuted. Do not call this a negative prediction result, failure of the state hypothesis, or proof a novel update rule cannot work. No fair strong-baseline test has run. scRNA abundance, DEG scores, mitochondrial programs, single-guide historical Horlbeck fitness and cached DepMap embeddings are different targets/information sources; none can silently stand in for the original dual-guide independently assayed gamma.

There is no need to demand complete historical manufacturing weights, raw single-cell reconstruction or new RSI machinery. The blocker is one concrete unavailable-in-inspected-routes functional payload and its assay lineage. Exact published source-data link/filename/accession, or an independently justified newly registered different endpoint, can support a subsequent phase. A different endpoint must be named as a new contract with its own costs/baselines, preserving this NOT_RUN stop and every prior seal. No author contact is implied or performed.
