## What survived independent challenge

The first debate compares ADCC compartment reset and AP-1 update attribution. A fixed-drug semigroup consistency loss has an explicit primary precedent in Deep-OSG, arXiv2302.03358v2. Switching drugs need not commute, withdrawal is not an inverse, and compositional consistency can be satisfied by a wrong model. ADCC's three-anchors→fourth endpoint rank-one relation can be a useful empirical constraint but is an ordinary factorized endpoint model here; identical endpoint integrals allow incompatible within-time hazards. AP-1's published phase-specific biology is existing work, not our innovation. Unpaired state/function marginals do not identify their conditional association; parameter improvement does not establish improver improvement.

The second debate retained one precise empirical qualification question: can founder-lineage mass and missing/dead branches exclude **fixed pre-existing endpoint-response types plus arbitrary selection** after a standardized challenge? Its scalar bound is `max_h Z_h <= min_h (Z_h+delta_h)`, extended to challenge censoring by adding unknown challenge mass. The categorical version is `sum_k max_h q_h(k)<=1`. These are standard missing-outcome/common-domination feasibility constraints. They are necessary and sufficient for their unrestricted aggregate common-population model, while passing does not prove actual absence of history effects.

The primary scientific objections were accepted and retained: finite randomized arms need not have equal empirical latent composition; stochastic challenge requires expectation/inference; complete tree/frontier accounting must include loss; half-split path mass is not tumor burden; changing generations under ordinary treatment-independent spontaneous switching can violate the narrow fixed-type null without a new memory mechanism; state-label or measurement changes need not alter later drug response. A common joint state/response null can also fail while separate marginal tests pass, provided those summaries genuinely refer to the same arm/cohort/denominator. This is a partial-identification problem, not a new module or proof of mediation.

There is no remaining central logical dispute between the actual reviewers. Unresolved facts are the closest primary-theory/application precedents, an authentic full lineage/rechallenge asset, calibrated finite-run inference/power and any empirical or predictive gain. **NONE current new core; retain only a bounded future source/theory qualification opportunity.** This decision does not rely on a universal predictor's expressibility to dismiss known-component combinations; the concrete current operations and missing empirical novelty are the reasons.

## Required next experimental gate and stop conditions

Do not repeat these closed header scopes, reopen stopped outcomes, or inflate tracks/timepoints/technical wells into future research tasks. The next useful step must identify an actual independent intervention unit or a concrete new constraint beyond the known marginal bound. This particular selection claim requires auditable initial-founder/path mass, a nonoverlapping frontier and conservative bounds on missing/dead/challenge-censored mass. Fully observed trees are one sufficient route; partial observations with credible, sufficiently tight unknown-mass bounds can also qualify. These requirements are not universal for every useful cell-world prediction task. A different legitimate state/action/function application may use less demanding authentic population-level pairing.

Before numerical access to a newly qualified task, freeze application, measured target, independent unit/split, available actions, all outcome exposure, model/update/program budgets and practically meaningful success/stop threshold. For the selection question, success first requires a calibrated uncertainty-aware rejection beyond independently justified measurement/loss slack, plus replication on an independent actual challenge branch; a candidate0.05founder-mass margin in the debate is uncalibrated and not an already-valid experiment. Failure of data identity or a vacuous loss bound stops that source; a bound-compatible result is not a universal no-memory conclusion.

Any later performance claim must include strong direct history/prefix prediction, ordinary refit/pooling, standard continual learning, active learning on the same real assay menu, simple mixtures, conventional birth-death/state-switching models where relevant, and equal-budget program search. All receive the same lineage/state/history information and measured feedback; a constrained direct predictor must receive the same known constraints. Logical query counts alone do not establish wet-experiment savings.

World-model contribution requires better real held-out functional prediction/decisions than these controls, with a specifically tested transition/rollout path. Agent contribution requires better feedback/query/program decisions at matched total cost. Weight and procedure inheritance require independent later tasks and comparisons with restart, warm start, pooling and ordinary CL. Joint benefit requires the matched crossed ablations. For RSI, freeze explicit improvers psi0/psi1 and compare the quality or cost of their *subsequent improvements* from common starts on independent future opportunities; theta gains, source qualification and biological memory cannot substitute.

World contribution, agent contribution, weight/procedure inheritance, joint benefit and improver-improvement are all **NOT_TESTED in this phase**. No evidence here satisfies the total goal or justifies marking it complete or blocked. The measured E.faecalis failure changed the direction; this renewal further changes it by closing unqualified AP-1/sequence framework expansion and narrowing the selection proposal to an honest identification/source question.
