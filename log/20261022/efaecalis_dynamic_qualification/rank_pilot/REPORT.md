2026-10-22 local. **STOP_THIS_SOURCE_CONFIGURATION_FOR_FRAMEWORK_EXPANSION.** The one registered opening/run completed in maestro after12 synthetic tests and independent pre-open code review. Exactly240 finite responses were opened; none were excluded or imputed. No test-dependent tuning or second run was performed.

The task is a single three-population verification roster for future days8–13, using days1–6, conditional on a completed historical training cohort and routine prefix monitoring. The source is twenty CRO-selected E.faecalis populations challenged with DOX. The deterministic split contains ten development and ten test populations. Utility is the roster's mean `1 - future daily-midrank percentile`, averaged over six dates. It measures exported relative-readout order, not absolute IC50, treatment success, or clinical effect.
