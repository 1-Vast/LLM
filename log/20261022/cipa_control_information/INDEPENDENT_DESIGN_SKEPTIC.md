# Independent prefit design skepticism: raw conditional control information

2026-10-22. Position: CONDITIONAL_GO for one bounded, frozen, ordinary source-only diagnostic; NO_CORE_PROMOTION. This opinion was formed before reading another current reviewer or the new protocol. I read the preceding frontier-transfer decision, independent application review and actual application exchange, and the calibration-probe protocol/decision/endpoint contract. The old decision exposes published summary results by design; I did not open biological row/bundle/forecast/score arrays, development numerical files, reserves, or perform fits. I inspected only source context identifiers in the old QUERY_MANIFEST and endpoint-converter code. No new biological numerical evidence informs this recommendation.

## Exact question and limitations

Can eight fixed measured controls improve known noncontrol drug-dose forecasts at a heldout physical source site, beyond source lookup, ordinary calibration/refit/mixture/program selection, and the same conditional family receiving only metadata and control availability masks? Direct raw conditional regression is the appropriate cheap diagnostic. It avoids the changing three-site/four-site residual reference and shared source-mean subtraction mechanism raised in the earlier exchange. It does not identify a biological latent state or a novel algorithm.

There are five transport units. The source manifest gives ten contexts: each physical site has two cell contexts. AXG/CDI are the two cell categories; AXN/CLY/ECR/MCS are the four platform categories. Outer training therefore has eight contexts from four sites; inner training has six contexts from three sites. Each per-condition/head regression has at most those sample counts, fewer when that response head is missing. Neither 96 targets nor 288 coefficient vectors increase the number of environments. CLY and ECR each occur at only one source site, so their heldout coefficients cannot be learned in the corresponding outer folds.

## Schema and estimator that would be reviewable

Use a metadata-frozen ordered list of the existing eight controls (four named drugs, doses 1 and 4), retaining the authenticated 96-condition target list and its one known absent source bundle. The measured control block has 24 entries: E, M, and H for each of eight controls. Add eight binary indicators for whether H is observed. With full one-hot cell/platform dictionaries there are six metadata columns, giving 38 predictors plus an unpenalized intercept. The masks-only family has 14 predictors and metadata-only has six. Full dummy coding is acceptable with ridge; do not silently switch reference coding between families. No site identifier is a candidate feature. A separate regression for every target drug-dose/head supplies the required drug-by-support interaction without a larger architecture.

The 32-entry control schema assumes all eight E fractions exist. Verify that prefit. If not, the protocol must either stop or explicitly freeze additional E-imputation/mask columns before fitting; never turn an unavailable E fraction into zero. M is a fraction defined for every nonempty bundle. Missing H inputs may be mean-imputed, but missing H targets are omitted from that head's fit and evaluation. The absence of the known source bundle removes its response row only; it must not remove a context from other targets or invent a target value.

Estimate the H scale from finite training-source bundle means only, using the already frozen IQR/SD/1 rule. Apply precisely that same scale to training and heldout control H and normalized response H. The scale is positive and computed before any heldout acquisition values are inspected. Recompute it in every inner fold. No per-context or per-target scaling. Continuous-input imputation means and any standardization are fitted once on all allowed training contexts, before selecting valid response rows for a particular head. This makes missing target H unable to alter the input representation. An entirely unavailable training control-H column is imputed to zero in normalized units and centered as zero; its coefficient has no training information. Any unexpected heldout observed value in that column must still follow the frozen transformation. Constant columns have scale 1. Prefer leaving binary metadata/masks unscaled and explicitly standardizing the measured continuous columns; either choice is ordinary, but it must be identical across train/test and written down.

Specify an actual objective, for example sum_i w_i(y_i-b-x_i beta)^2 + alpha||beta||^2, where b is unpenalized. Weights and their normalization matter because alpha in {1,32} has different strength under a sum or mean loss. Equal context weights are defensible for this ten-context source and match the existing context-based lookup. If equal-site fitting weights are chosen, define their behavior after response masking and normalize them explicitly. Do not silently renormalize alpha per head. Center X and y using the declared weights, solve the positive definite ridge system with a stable linear solver, then recover the intercept. No inverse, intercept penalty, or numerical pseudo-fit to missing targets is necessary.

One valid training label implies an intercept-only observed value because centered data are zero. Zero labels require a frozen head-global source-only fallback using valid labels from the training sites; if even that head has no valid source labels, stop as unscorable rather than inventing truth. Record fallback incidence. Unknown heldout metadata categories have all-zero learned contributions; global identity-only dictionaries are permissible, but no source outcome statistic may involve the heldout site.

## Nested selection and fair comparators

Select one alpha per conditional family per outer fold using whole-site inner validation over its four training sites. Aggregate E/M/H under the same endpoint convention and equal physical-site rule as the outer evaluation. Do not tune separately on each sparse target condition. Tie-breaking must be deterministic and fixed. Recompute every inner scale, imputer, feature transform, source summary and model from its three training sites. A preprocessing object built on the outer training set must not be reused during inner selection. Retain all inner predictions and losses.

The full, metadata-plus-mask, and metadata-only families must have the same head objective, alpha grid, target coverage, fallback and prediction clipping. Tune each family independently on identical folds. Full must outperform the best of both reduced-input controls; omission of masks from the baseline would confound measured H with its availability. A mean-filled input ablation with masks retained is another description of the same essential information control, not a reason to add an architecture.

The old source-selected configurations cannot simply be reused as if they were independent of the outer holdout: their original five-site CV included it. Retune the prespecified ordinary calibration/refit families inside each outer training set, or freeze configurations explicitly before this run and acknowledge their prior source exposure; nested retuning is preferable. For the ordinary portfolio, inner family choices and its acquired-control folds must never include terminal labels. Use the same eight acquired controls and the same heldout target population for every comparator. Source lookup all/cell/cell-platform, the fixed mixture, and the strong conventional direct refit remain required. Reporting an oracle best comparator after outer scoring is permissible only as a conservative descriptive hurdle; it is not an honest deployable selection rule. Candidate prediction and hyperparameter selection must already be sealed.

No incorrect-context permutation is required to answer the primary conditional-prediction question. If a stress swap is retained, it must be frozen before outcomes, preserve control identity and paired values/masks, and describe cell/platform incompatibility. Sparse strata may admit no exchangeable nonidentity swap. Do not interpret unmatched swaps, permutation counts, or correlated contexts as significance evidence. The raw conditional design removes the need for a mandatory fixed-reference residual sensitivity.

## Proposed progression policy and stop

Before numerical access, choose an administrative practical opportunity margin. My proposal is at least 0.005 absolute reduction in equal-site combined loss versus the strongest prespecified ordinary comparator, and versus the better reduced-input conditional comparator. This number is a research progression policy on the conventional composite, not a clinical meaningfulness threshold or a statistically estimated cutoff. Show the unrounded effect and each component; do not replace it with relative percentage when a baseline loss is small.

Also require no worsening of pooled E, M, or normalized-H against those same reference methods within only a frozen floating-point tolerance (e.g. 1e-12); improvement at at least four of five sites; and positive mean benefit after leaving out each one of the five outer-site contrasts. These are conservative descriptive progression rules, not confidence intervals or independent validation. All sites and losses remain reported even if they fail. The best ordinary comparator and best reduced-input comparator are chosen by full outer mean only for this conservative hurdle; list every paired comparison so a mean winner cannot conceal a different endpoint failure.

Missing groups or a broken feature/label contract stop before fits. A failed margin, mask/metadata contrast, endpoint gate, or concentration gate stops expansion of this exact conditional ridge candidate. No expanded alpha grid, controls, sites, nonlinear encoder or post-hoc endpoint replacement is justified by a near miss. A linear failure is not a universal no-information theorem. Passing supports at most descriptive known-drug conditional prediction on previously exposed source sites; it licenses no neural/RSI claim and no automatic development/reserve use.

Freeze protocol, code, input hashes, metadata schema, comparator grids and all criteria before predictive fits. Seal predictions for every inner and outer model and acquired feedback before separate scoring. Retain fit counts, CPU/wall cost, masks, selected configurations and all fallback counts. Development 6/7 are already design-exposed and must supply no new numerical input here. Sites 8/9, TG later14 and AZ413 remain unopened. These requirements make the next result falsifiable without manufacturing independent evidence.
