## Direct conditional family

Fit a separate ridge for each terminal drug/dose/head using source contexts as training samples. This is equivalent to a multi-output conditional model with drug-by-support interactions; an additive shared support shift is not the comparator. The response is raw E/M or raw H divided by the current training-source scale. There is no baseline-subtracted response or control residual. Thus the proposed information test avoids the3/4/5-reference-size shift and shared-baseline residual artifact as its primary construction.

The fixed metadata dictionary contains cells AXG/CDI and platforms AXN/CLY/ECR/MCS: six onehot columns. Metadata categories are source identities only. A category absent from a training fold has zero centered training variation and zero fitted coefficient; its target identity cannot supply outcomes.

Construct the master38-column input per context: six metadata columns; eight slot-major triples(E,M,H/source_scale); eight binary indicators that the corresponding paid H bundle mean is finite. No E/M indicators are added because the authenticated source contract supplies valid E/M for all purchased bundles. The three variants are raw38, mask14(metadata plus eight H masks), and meta6(metadata only). Their columns and order are frozen in PREPARE_AUDIT. An H-input placeholder is the finite training-context column mean, or0 if the entire training column is absent; its separate validity indicator remains0. This is input imputation only. No response target is imputed.

Fit input column means/population standard deviations on all training contexts before selecting per-head valid response rows. After the declared input imputation, center and scale all columns, including dummies/masks. Zero standard deviation becomes1. Hold these values fixed for every drug/head and for prediction. All training sites contain two contexts, so equal-context preprocessing coincides with equal-site preprocessing. H scale, feature imputation and standardization are recomputed separately inside every outer/inner training fold. No global preprocessing across a held-out site is permitted.

For S training physical sites, each context j receives weight w_j=1/(S*n_contexts_at_its_site), summing to1 over all training contexts. For target drug/dose/head with finite responses V, solve

`min_(a,beta) sum_(j in V) w_j*(y_j-a-x_j*beta)^2 + alpha*||beta||²`,

with unpenalized intercept and alpha in{1,32}. Do NOT renormalize weights after response masking. Thus a sparse response head receives stronger effective shrinkage by design. At one valid response, emit that observed response as an intercept-only prediction; at zero valid responses, use the current training-source global finite mean for that head (H converted to the same scale). Absent source condition rows do not become observed zeros. Log solver fits, constant/fallback dispatches, valid counts, source sites, preprocessing and timing separately. A small exact dual ridge solve is permitted; synthetic tests must compare its predictions to the stated weighted objective.

Independently select alpha for raw/mask/meta variants using inner whole-site CV. Raw is the sole primary candidate; mask and meta are required information controls, never candidates selected after outer scores.
