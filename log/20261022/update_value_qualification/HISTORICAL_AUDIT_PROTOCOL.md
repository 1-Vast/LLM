# Historical two-noise update-choice audit

2026-10-22. Written before this phase reads the per-campaign numerical records. This is a new post hoc analysis of already exposed historical PRISM outputs. It is not independent confirmation, a new algorithm, a deployed selector, biological replication or the complete EG-RPT/RSI experiment. Earlier aggregate scores are already known and disclosed. No original protocol, freeze, result or STOP is changed.

## Exact input and scope

Read only research/feedback_propagation_20261018/records/E_fold{2,3,4}.json, its compare.py/bench.py/loop.py/metrics.py, and the historical EG-RPT NOISE_HEADROOM/THRESHOLDS artifacts. The historical two-noise scores were generated once in that completed study. No source response table, raw assay, new fit, protected tier or AZ reserve is opened.

Use sections A_B3 and C_noise_seed2, arms ridge and R1, kind unmeasured. ## Estimands and fixed choices

Retain every originally complete 64-compound campaign in folds 2,3,4, and explicitly account for incomplete trailing campaigns. Check each section/arm campaign_compounds equals root campaign_sizes and campaign_index membership; recompute every complete campaign's saved hits from hits_c. Verify equal compound lengths and campaign identities across sections. Preserve source hashes before and after.

For each complete campaign i, draw a in {1,2} supplies two fixed-pipeline utilities U[a,i,ridge] and U[a,i,R1]. Choose R1 iff it has strictly more hits on the selection draw, otherwise choose ridge. Evaluate that choice on the other draw. Report both directions, a symmetric mean, ties, strict sign agreement conditional on non-ties, Pearson correlation when defined, the hindsight within-draw best and all individual vectors.

Compare cross-draw selection against BOTH named fixed methods on the evaluation draw. Also compare against the single best fixed method on that evaluation draw, explicitly a hindsight fixed reference, and against leave-one-fold-out fixed selection: for each target fold choose ridge/R1 on the other two folds' selection-draw mean hits, with ridge first on exact ties, then evaluate on the target fold's other draw. Record all selections and per-fold values. The cross-draw campaign chooser itself reads graded outcomes and is an unavailable diagnostic, not a deployable research strategy. Its nominal observation cost is inherited unchanged; extra analysis has zero new measurements and zero new fits.

The historical practical scale is delta*=0.1875 hits per complete 64-compound campaign. Report whether BOTH directional differences against the evaluation-draw best fixed reference reach that scale. This is a descriptive continuation screen only: a pass motivates separate qualification, never establishes biological reliability, a learned strategy or statistical significance; a fail stops expansion based on this exact two-draw chooser, never all possible feedback features/update menus. No thresholds, tie rules or subgroups may change after outcome reading.

## Dependence and limits

Campaigns mix MoA families and share one response source, base worlds, context panel and nominal event labels; these are not independent biological replications. Do not bootstrap the 36 campaigns as if independent or relabel three folds as three independent screens. No confirmatory interval/p-value or population power claim is made here. A same-response two-noise selector can remain correlated through common target difficulty and does not test recurrence of real assay measurements, unseen biological states, unseen chemical families or query-induced feedback changes. Record size/membership uncertainty rather than assuming36 is sufficient for meta-learning.
