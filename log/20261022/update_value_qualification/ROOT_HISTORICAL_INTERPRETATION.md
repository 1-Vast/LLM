# Root interpretation of the historical two-noise audit

2026-10-22. This position is written from the newly computed exported-score audit, before peer independent recomputation and new mechanism drafts. It is not a new primary biological result or the execution of the historical EG-RPT H0: that draft proposed a different lineage-by-compound task panel, independently simulated probe draws and inferential requirements. Here the already completed 36 whole-panel campaigns are audited without new fitting or feedback.

Eight meaningful synthetic checks pass. Fourteen protocol/code/input files were hash-frozen before numerical parsing, and the real score audit ran once. The 36 complete campaigns cover 2,304 compounds; 94 compounds in trailing 29/46/19-compound campaigns are explicitly excluded under the original 64-compound convention, without repartitioning their existing nomination lists. Every saved campaign count was recomputed from its per-compound hits; original sources remain unchanged.

The asymmetric result is retained: one direction exceeds the historical .1875 practical scale; the other loses substantially. Symmetric gain is -.069444 versus the hindsight evaluation best fixed method, or +.111111 versus leave-fold-out fixed selection. Both directions therefore do not clear the frozen descriptive screen. This criterion was written for this post hoc diagnostic; it must not be represented as a preregistered independent confirmatory test or biological failure probability.

Of 24 campaigns with strict non-tied method differences in both draws, 13 retain the sign and 11 reverse it; six selection ties occur in each draw. Correlation of R1-minus-ridge differences is .303930. Do not equate these observed figures with an unbiased estimate of independent assay reliability. The two evaluations share the same published true response matrix, training worlds, nominal positions and endpoints, while the paid feedback only adds synthetic noise. Campaigns also mix families, and the 36 rows are not independent biological replications; no iid confidence interval or p-value is justified here.

The campaign chooser reads full graded outcomes on the selection draw. It is unavailable to a real feedback-only selector. Its lack of consistent practical gain gives no reason to train a learned policy using this specific two-draw argument, but it is not a mathematical upper bound on every feasible descriptor-driven strategy or every broader update menu. Stop expansion based on this chooser, retain the positive reverse direction, and continue the distinct source/mechanism qualification. A useful future idea must outperform ordinary same-information algorithm selection, not merely outperform the weaker fixed ridge.

No new biological fit, provider request, source dataset download, raw response conversion or AZ reserve access occurred in this audit. Baseline preservation verified all 888 inventoried files across the completed phases, including the 224 files of the last performance phase and 204 literature/chemical records. This preserves previous negative evidence rather than reclassifying it.
