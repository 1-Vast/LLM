# Independent method and simplicity review: CDK Middle-feedback baseline pilot

2026-10-22. Reviewer: frontier_distinct. **GO for a small numerical baseline pilot after freezing its contract; no new mechanism claim.** This review read only `next_mechanism/source/cdk_review/ADDENDUM.md` and `S4_ADDENDUM.md`, plus a file-name availability check. It did not read another new review, numerical barcode/IC50 outcomes or pilot results, and performed no fit, numerical experiment, download or API request.

## 1. Qualified application and immediate question

The defensible task is predicting relative barcode composition at the resistance-defined terminal event, under the observed escalation regimen, from a culture's Early composition, optionally revealing its complete Middle assay. Four drug-by-ER selection conditions each have three evolved cultures, all from one common ancestral MCF7 barcode population. These are twelve real culture histories, not twelve independent cell backgrounds. End is not a fixed calendar horizon. Forecasting relative composition is useful longitudinal substrate work, not identification of clone death, total population growth or drug-switch benefit.

The immediate numerical question should be: **does Middle add forecast information beyond Early when equally simple direct methods receive exactly the same observations?** A positive answer would qualify an information opportunity. It would not demonstrate a distinct world-model mechanism, useful acquisition policy, inheritance or RSI. A negative answer would concern this task, vocabulary, fitted baselines and resolution rather than every possible model.

The S4 addendum authenticates resistant-culture labels for a separate terminal IC50 auxiliary task. It supplies no Early/Middle functional response, calibrated fixed-dose utility or clone-specific outcome. Do not use S4 numeric IC50s, terminal RNA, published eight clusters or resistant clone selection in this first composition pilot.

## 2. Split recommendation and ancestry objection

Hold out every measurement and label of an evolutionary trajectory together. Never put its Early/Middle rows in training examples while evaluating its End. At inference its allowed Early or Early+Middle prefix is input, not a training target. Exclude that trajectory's End from vocabulary selection, normalization, threshold selection, distance scaling, tuning and any diagnostic feature. Known drug/ER context and common barcode identity are legitimate predictor information.

I recommend freezing **three replicate-round blocked folds** as the conservative primary numerical comparison: fold r holds out the four treated drug-by-ER trajectories with suffix r; train on the remaining eight cultures. Average the four held-out culture losses within each fold, and show all twelve individual culture results. Also report whole-trajectory leave-one-out as a clearly labeled, less conservative within-family sensitivity analysis; do not select whichever split looks better afterwards.

There is an important qualification. The source calls cultures independent evolutionary realizations from a shared ancestor. It does **not** authenticate that suffix 1 across all four conditions is a uniquely paired physical aliquot or batch. Therefore I cannot call ordinary whole-trajectory leave-one-out proven ancestry leakage. The blocked split conservatively prevents reliance on any unrecognized index-aligned dependence and reduces overlapping related source context. It does not remove common ancestry: all three rounds still descend from the common pool. If suffix matching is merely an arbitrary naming convention, it is still a declared stress test, not a newly identified biological grouping. No split here establishes transfer to an unrelated ancestor or unseen cell line.

The practical benefit of blocking is that global kernel/ridge methods cannot exploit a same-index culture in another condition if that index encodes an unreported shared processing factor. Condition-specific mean/persistence methods remain well defined with two source cultures per condition. Three folds are not a basis for high-confidence population significance; neither are twelve correlated cultures. Report paired effects and fold/culture scatter descriptively. Protein/barcode counts and repeated seeds cannot inflate independent n.

## 3. A legal common vocabulary and other mass

The workbook row universe may have been assembled using later samples. The mere presence of a row in the full workbook cannot authorize treating that barcode as prospectively known. Do not filter by maximum abundance across all times, terminal detectability or the published future-informed eight clusters.

The simplest conservative contract is a fold-specific common identity set U defined **before reading the held-out End**, using authenticated parental identities and/or nonzero barcode identities in allowed training prefixes and held-out Early. Use precisely the same U for Early-only and Early+Middle. A complete ancestral identity list, if independently available at deployment, can also be frozen as U; unequal Palbo passage-3 and Abema passage-10 reference aliquots must not be treated as identical numeric starting compositions.

Prefer no frequency threshold for the first pilot. If dimensionality requires top-K or a count threshold, choose it from training/authorized prefix information only, freeze it, and carry every excluded identity in an explicit **other** category. The same U and filtering apply to all methods and both information conditions within the fold. Every target composition is represented on U plus other; its other mass is the total End fraction outside U. This use of End is evaluation-label construction, not feature selection. Do not discard terminal mass outside U and renormalize the remaining known barcodes: that would hide a real forecast failure and reward incomplete support.

When a held-out Middle assay reveals identities outside the Early-defined U, aggregate them into Middle other. Do not expand U using those identities and then give that expanded vocabulary to the Early-only method: the existence of the new identities would itself disclose Middle information. Holding U fixed is conservative and limits the question to known-identity reweighting plus aggregate unseen mass. It may understate the full practical value of Middle's newly resolved identities; record that limitation rather than rescuing the comparison with an asymmetric vocabulary. A later full-identity task can use a separately declared information/action space.

An identity absent from a prefix count is not a proven dead clone or a newly mutated barcode. It may be below detection or lost in sampling. No method should be required to invent a DNA string never present in legal inputs. Predict aggregate other mass for unidentifiable identities using a legal source-derived model, or let a fixed persistence baseline predict its observed prefix other mass. Exact zero predictions are permissible with the bounded losses below. Missing worksheet cells are not automatically biological zero: freeze the numeric/missingness contract and fail visibly if required values cannot be interpreted.

Use complete nonnegative sample counts normalized by the full observed sample total, or consistently use the supplied fractions if their definition is authenticated. Freeze one primary convention before decoding. Check count/fraction consistency and report discrepancies; do not change denominator to make results agree. Read-depth differences should not make high-depth cultures more important. Each culture's normalized composition is an observation, not one independent sample per sequenced count. Vehicle keys remain `(selection-family worksheet, sample label)`; vehicle features are optional and should be omitted from this minimal pilot rather than introduce the unresolved S4 vehicle join or extra assay information.

## 4. Minimal strong baseline set

Implement the following small set before any special transition or repair model. Every learned comparator must have an Early-only and an Early+Middle version with the same source cultures, fold U, context and hyperparameter budget. The test trajectory's End never selects a model, kernel scale, mixing weight or extrapolation factor.

1. **Same-condition source End mean.** Average the terminal composition vectors of the two remaining cultures in that drug-by-ER condition, giving each culture equal weight. No sequencing-read pooling. This is a strong repeatable-clone/condition prior, not a straw-man global mean. It ignores target Middle in both information arms by design and identifies how much predictable family structure already exists.

2. **Latest-state persistence.** Early-only predicts Early; Early+Middle predicts Middle. This is the essential direct Middle comparator. If a model with Middle only beats Early persistence but loses to Middle persistence, it has not shown useful learned transition skill.

3. **Direct prefix kernel forecast.** Within condition, predict a convex weighted average of the source End compositions using similarity of source/target Early compositions. In the +Middle arm use both Early and Middle distances, with the distance aggregation fixed identically across all folds. Hellinger distance on U plus other is a simple choice. Use a fixed source-only scale rule or a tiny source-only tuning grid. With only two source cultures per condition this is deliberately low capacity. A condition-aware global version may share all eight source trajectories, but it must not replace the simpler condition-specific control merely because it fits better after exposure.

   A conventional ridge alternative is acceptable: regress terminal change relative to the latest prefix using the same allowed compositional inputs, strong regularization and a fixed/simple source-only penalty. Multioutput ridge needs no per-barcode feature selection or neural router. Project its output back to the simplex by a frozen rule, and report the projection. Kernel and ridge are alternatives for the minimum pilot; building many variants is unnecessary.

4. **Simple mean/persistence blend.** Predict `lambda * latest_prefix + (1-lambda) * same_condition_End_mean`. Include a fixed lambda=0.5. If learning lambda, use one shared scalar selected only within source folds from a tiny fixed grid including the endpoints; debit all tried candidates. This control tests whether any improvement attributed to model updating is merely shrinkage toward the robust condition prior. It naturally receives Middle in the +Middle arm.

5. **Low-degree stage extrapolation.** In the +Middle arm use `project_to_simplex(p_M + alpha * (p_M - p_E))`, with one fixed alpha or a tiny source-only grid including alpha=0 (Middle persistence). A log-ratio continuation variant is not necessary for the first pilot. The source provides ordered stages but unequal/unknown elapsed times and an event-defined End; alpha must be called a stage-continuation coefficient, not a physically identified rate or equal-time extrapolation. No quadratic fit to three timepoints may use the held-out End.

Together these test the main conventional explanations: reproducible condition-level winner structure, lack of sufficient change after Middle, nearest-history retrieval, ordinary shrinkage and simple trend continuation. A direct kernel/ridge that sees Middle is mandatory before claiming model-based feedback value. An Early-only model may legitimately be unable to use an E-to-M slope; that informational difference is the intervention being measured, not unfairness. Fairness means that **all +Middle comparators** can use the same complete Middle assay, not that slope features are secretly supplied to Early-only arms.

Avoid fitting a flexible router, learned protein/barcode embedding, large per-condition hyperparameter grids or a mechanistic ODE in this first twelve-culture pilot. None is needed to determine whether an ordinary Middle observation changes predictable composition. If a later candidate learns which assay to buy, compare buy-none, buy-all, fixed-budget random and standard uncertainty selection separately; the fixed-all-Middle pilot is not an acquisition agent.

## 5. Composition loss and what counts as progress

I recommend **squared Hellinger distance** as the primary composition error:
`H2(p,q) = 0.5 * sum_j (sqrt(p_j)-sqrt(q_j))^2`, on U plus other.
It is bounded between 0 and 1 for normalized distributions, handles zero probabilities without arbitrary pseudocounts and does not erase missed terminal mass. Report total variation `0.5 * sum_j abs(p_j-q_j)` as one interpretable secondary. Normalized Jensen-Shannon divergence is a reasonable alternative primary, but freeze one before decoding; do not select it from favorable results. Avoid raw KL as the only primary because legal persistence zeros can produce infinities. Any log-ratio loss requires an explicit source-fixed zero replacement and sensitivity analysis, adding complexity that is unnecessary here.

Report other-category target/predicted mass and its error separately as a diagnostic, along with how much terminal mass lies outside the legally known vocabulary. Other must stay in the primary full-distribution loss. A vocabulary collapsing almost all terminal mass into one bucket can produce deceptively easy forecasts; show this coverage limitation. Correlation, top-ranked clone overlap and visual trajectories are useful descriptions but cannot replace a proper full-composition error. A low mean absolute error divided by millions of barcode dimensions can be trivial; avoid that normalization as the main claim.

Use equal culture and condition weights. The primary information comparison is the paired loss reduction from Early-only to +Middle within each fixed direct method. Also compare every learned +Middle method against Middle persistence and the strongest prespecified source-selected conventional +Middle baseline. A beneficial Middle change in an otherwise poor model is not enough if the condition prior or simple blend already does better. Do not choose the best +Middle method on all twelve target losses and present it as a prospective selected procedure; such an oracle can only be labeled descriptive.

Set a nonzero improvement threshold before decoding. Because these compositions have no authenticated clinical utility scale, describe that threshold as a pilot engineering/usefulness convention, not a clinically validated benefit. The threshold and any follow-up rule should be fixed by the parent; do not manufacture significance with barcode-level resampling. If the gain is inconsistent across blocked folds, loses to simple comparators or is explained by other-bucket coverage, record that outcome and retain the narrow scope. If Middle consistently helps strong conventional forecasts, retain an information substrate for a later specific mechanism experiment; do not relabel this baseline result as a novel world model or RSI.

## 6. Minimal verification before interpreting numbers

Required checks are modest but meaningful: all held-out End values excluded from every fitting/vocabulary path; grouped fold membership explicit; forecast and target vectors share U plus other and sum to one; all methods receive identical observations within each information arm; count normalization and unknown-mass conservation; source-only parameter selection; negative ridge/extrapolation outputs handled by the frozen simplex rule; persistent zero/other examples supported by the primary loss; no pseudo-independent barcode inference. A tiny synthetic test should specifically catch terminal-based feature filtering and dropping unknown terminal mass. All experimental/test code belongs in research/ and all protocol, decisions, errors and outputs in log/.

No additional biological source qualification is needed to begin these numerical baselines on the already acquired S3, once the parent freezes preprocessing, splits, primary loss, margin and finite candidate budget. This reviewer does not require a full functional/RSI dataset before recognizing the source's narrow positive value.
