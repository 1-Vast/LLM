## 2. Primary nested procedures do not establish consistent feedback benefit

Lower loss is better. Gain below is Early loss minus Middle-access loss.

| Split | Early-selected H² | Middle-selected H² | H² gain | TV gain |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Primary complete-culture LOO | 0.15701445 | 0.15595287 | +0.00106158 | -0.00018701 |
| Replicate-index blocked sensitivity | 0.15797882 | 0.15797882 | 0 | 0 |

In LOO, four culture differences are positive, four negative and four exactly zero. The primary average direction is weakly positive in H², but it does not repeat as a positive effect in the declared blocked sensitivity, and secondary TV slightly worsens. Under the pre-result rules this is split-sensitive and metric-sensitive, not consistent feedback validation. No arbitrary practical threshold is needed to reach that limited conclusion, and no post-result threshold is introduced.

LOO condition differences verify the concentration:

Leaving Palbo Mut out gives average H² gain **-0.00143777**. All other leave-condition-out averages remain positive but small. Thus the LOO mean is concentrated in one condition, not an all-condition improvement. Every blocked condition gain is exactly zero.

This zero is operationally explained by model choice: in the blocked scheme both selected procedures choose the same Early-only methods for every culture (eight condition-centroid selections and four Early-ridge selections). The legal Early fallback is actually exercised. In LOO, the Early portfolio chooses condition centroid eight times and Early ridge four times; Middle access chooses Middle ridge eight times, Early ridge once and condition centroid three times. The wider library's source-selected choices introduce both gains and losses; better inner options do not guarantee better outer forecasts.

The direct, predeclared condition-only centroid has H² **0.15443908** and TV **0.39031065** in both schemes. It outperforms both nested portfolios on their aggregate outer loss. This comparator uses source End compositions from the same drug×ER family and the shared barcode identity; it is a strong explanation of what is predictable in this small shared-ancestor setting. It is not evidence for an autonomous agent or an elaborate transition model.

These are slightly below the fixed condition-centroid losses, while the source-selected Middle portfolio does not achieve those hindsight fixed-family averages. Report them as the prespecified ridge family's observed scores, not a retrospectively selected winning algorithm. They warrant retaining a narrow “Middle can help an ordinary predictor modestly” observation. They do not establish large, robust selected-procedure value, a new model update rule or necessity of a world model.

Both shrinkage families reduce to the condition centroid in aggregate here and obtain identical scores. The gain from Middle persistence over Early persistence is much larger (H² 0.86676343→0.70462207), but both persistence forecasts are poor compared with the source-condition centroid. End OTHER has mean **0.00154608** (0.155%) and maximum **0.00859847** (0.860%). Terminal mass coverage by U is therefore high under this cohort definition. It would be incorrect to dismiss the main result by saying that an enormous terminal OTHER category swamped the target.

These coverage statistics do not identify which coordinates caused either the small ridge gain or the unstable portfolio gain. The evaluator preserves losses/selection rows rather than complete prediction vectors, so no exact known-U/OTHER loss decomposition is available in the result artifact. Do not claim either that all benefit came from OTHER or that all benefit concerned known clone identities. No additional decomposition/refit was performed in this review.

The evaluation remains a transductive cohort composition forecast with literal shared ancestry and count-sampling uncertainty. High End coverage does not turn it into unseen-clone function prediction, absolute cell dynamics or generalization to an unrelated ancestor.

## 5. Decision for further research

**Do not expand this selected-feedback configuration into RSI, agent generations, new neural architectures or broad program search.** The prespecified strong portfolio comparison is inconsistent across partitions and metrics; source-condition centroids and ordinary ridge explain the available performance. Nothing here changes the improvement strategy, demonstrates that a successor improves faster, or measures inheritance.

Retain rather than suppress the modest paired ridge signal. Its natural next scientific interpretation is conventional, small-sample conditional prediction, not a novel cell-world-model mechanism. A new technical mechanism would need a concrete distinction from those baselines and a separate falsifiable test; no such mechanism has been validated by this pilot. Do not silently retune on these twelve now-exposed End trajectories or substitute the positive ridge contrast as a confirmatory primary.

A separately frozen resistant-only S4 scalar/rank exploration remains scientifically available if it answers an explicit functional question. The composition portfolio's weak result neither validates nor logically excludes IC50 prediction. Minimum contract remains the authenticated resistant-culture crosswalk, fixed challenge column(s), positive-finite eligibility and unknown-missingness handling for N/A, source-only Early/Early+Middle features, complete-culture nested evaluation and strong condition/direct predictors. Common-unit scalar interpretation must be authenticated, or the endpoint stays within-column rank. There are at most twelve evolutionary units, not twenty-four independent challenge outcomes. Raw curves are unnecessary for that limited rank/scalar task, but required evidence must be obtained before fixed-dose viability, curve calibration, clone-specific response or switching-utility claims.

The immediate research conclusion is a completed and informative conventional-baseline pilot with a weak selected-feedback result and small class-specific signals. It supports neither a core innovation nor expansion to RSI. The original virtual-cell/agent/inheritance/improved-improver objective remains active and unachieved.
