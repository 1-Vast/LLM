## 1. A legitimate, immediately testable task

The source supports twelve genuinely evolved treated cultures: two selection drugs × two ER conditions × three evolutionary cultures from a common barcoded ancestor. These are cultures, not sequencing lanes or technical wells. The target is terminal **relative barcode composition at the resistance-defined endpoint** under the actually observed escalation regimen. End is not a common calendar horizon. Early follows the specified initial dose/passages; Middle is later along the same fixed regimen. No time-to-resistance, alternative drug schedule, absolute clone survival, total population growth or fixed-dose functional utility is observed by these fractions alone.

Primary question: with drug/ER context and the allowed shared-ancestor barcode identity, does revealing one complete Middle barcode assay improve prediction of the same End composition over the strongest Early-only forecast? This is fixed feedback, not active acquisition. There is one additional physical aliquot/library assay per culture receiving Middle, not one query per barcode. Numeric assay prices or timely online turnaround are unknown; report one additional whole assay and do not invent an economical drug-discovery gain.

Use all twelve treated histories and leave each complete culture trajectory out of supervised fitting. Early and Middle of the target culture are deployment inputs under their respective arms; its End must be absent from all fitting, feature selection, normalization choices, tuning and model selection. Source trajectories can provide all their timepoints. The application is prediction for further evolutionary realizations within these four known selection families and this shared ancestor. It does not require an independent ancestor or a complete future RSI partition before this numerical pilot can run.

It is not a simulation of deployment to an isolated unseen culture. Held-out cultures' Early inputs may define U under that contract; held-out Middle/End may not. For every assay, map each observed barcode in U to its coordinate and sum every observed barcode outside U into a single OTHER coordinate. Retain mass closure: the full vector, including OTHER, sums to one. Do not discard unseen mass and renormalize only the known barcodes. ## 3. Strong minimal baselines and fair tuning

Do not begin with a deep state model. The minimum useful benchmark is a small, frozen library with equal access to drug/ER context, source End compositions and the declared barcode identity:

1. **Early persistence:** predict normalized Early composition, including OTHER.
2. **Family End centroid:** average End compositions of the remaining cultures with the same selection drug and ER condition. The primary LOO fold has two such source cultures. This is a strong shared-ancestor direct predictor and is essential; barcode identities may already reveal repeatable winners without any dynamics model.
3. **Early-plus-template shrinkage:** a convex mixture of Early and that family End centroid. Choose the mixture coefficient from a tiny fixed grid on source cultures only. This includes both baselines above and is a practical strong Early-only model.
4. **Middle persistence:** predict Middle composition. This is indispensable. Comparing only against Early persistence can attribute the benefit of a later observation to an elaborate update mechanism.
5. **Middle-plus-template shrinkage:** the identical family-template construction with Middle replacing Early. Optionally include an equally simple direct convex mixture of Early, Middle and source End centroid; constrain its weights and use a tiny declared grid. All weights are fitted/tuned on source trajectories only.

A single ordinary transition comparator is helpful if a dynamics contribution is proposed: estimate smoothed source-family Early→End or Middle→End log-ratio factors and shrink their effect toward persistence, using the same smoothing/shrinkage menu for both input arms. Source-fitted ratio extrapolation is a descriptive transition predictor, not an identified mechanistic growth law; passages and resistance-defined endpoints do not authenticate equal time steps. A frozen Early→Middle trend extrapolator is another conventional comparator if implemented, not a necessary additional condition for the first five-baseline pilot.

Choose any library member/hyperparameter separately inside each outer training fold, via source-culture inner leave-out validation using the same primary loss. Never pick the global best baseline using held-out End scores and then call its cross-validation result an unbiased selected model. Report all fixed candidates as well as the fold-selected Early-only and Early+Middle procedures. Keep the candidate counts/search costs small, explicit and comparable. No tuning over thousands of barcode features or protein/state embeddings is justified by twelve culture trajectories. Shared barcode-coordinate fitting can use many coordinates computationally, but those coordinates do not create additional validation units.

The Early+Middle procedure should include Middle persistence, so an engineering claim about model updating additionally requires improvement over that fixed baseline. The information-value contrast (best specified E+M procedure versus matched Early-only procedure) and the model-value contrast (E+M procedure versus Middle persistence / direct conditional shrinkage) are distinct. If a simple direct baseline wins, retain the useful information result and reject a distinctive world-model mechanism claim.

## 4. Minimal metric, paired summaries and stopping

Recommended primary loss is total variation, TV(p,q) = 0.5 × sum_b |p_b − q_b|, including OTHER. It lies in [0,1], tolerates true zeros without arbitrary pseudocounts and expresses misallocated probability mass. A normalized Jensen–Shannon distance/divergence can be secondary if defined exactly in advance; do not search among loss definitions after decoding. Top-clone overlap, diversity or a plot is supplementary and cannot rescue a failed primary loss.

For culture i record d_i = loss(Early-only_i, End_i) − loss(Early+Middle_i, End_i), so positive means Middle helped. Every d_i must arise from predictions whose own End was excluded. Report all twelve paired losses/differences, the equal-culture mean/median, four drug×ER family means, and the range of family effects. Each family has three cultures, so equal-culture and equal-family weighting coincide for complete data. If parsing/missingness changes this balance, explicitly show the changed denominator and do not silently replace an endpoint or impute a trajectory.

The three common-index-held-out folds proposed by the parent are a reasonable split sensitivity: hold out all four suffix-1 cultures, then suffix-2, then suffix-3, with source-only tuning. Suffix equality across conditions is not an authenticated shared physical donor or matched culture, and the three folds are not three independent experiments. It is simply a blocked partition choice. Keep primary trajectory-LOO and this sensitivity separate; do not average their scores as extra replications or choose the split giving a favorable conclusion.

The twelve cultures share one ancestor and four designed treatment contexts. There is no empirical replication over ancestors or unrelated cell backgrounds. LOO folds also share fitted source cultures. A naive t interval over barcode rows, a test treating all drug×barcode coordinates as independent, or an exact randomization p-value from twelve signs is not justified by this protocol. No randomized assignment to receiving Middle occurred. A small pilot can be fully useful with descriptive paired results and split/family sensitivity, without a p-value. If a culture bootstrap is supplied, state that it is conditional/descriptive and explain refitting and family stratification; it does not manufacture ancestral replication. Do not demand a large new cohort merely to produce this first descriptive calculation.

Freeze a practical engineering margin before count decoding. A defensible default is mean TV reduction **0.05**, i.e. five percentage points less misplaced composition mass. It is an engineering opportunity threshold, not a validated clinical benefit threshold. Prefer this clear fixed threshold to declaring any positive d useful. If another explicit margin is already sealed before decoding, retain it rather than replacing it with this suggestion.

- If the matched E+M procedure's mean improvement is below the fixed margin, stop deepening this particular information-value configuration and report the observed family/split behavior. This is failure to meet the development opportunity gate, not a population impossibility theorem.
- If it reaches the margin, report only a promising conditional predictive information result. Check whether it survives Middle persistence and the direct template baselines before studying a more complex transition/world model. One favorable family must be visible rather than hidden in the mean.
- If primary LOO and the predeclared blocked sensitivity materially disagree, record split-sensitive/inconclusive opportunity. Do not keep changing preprocessing, loss or feature universe until the result becomes positive.

An ordinary conditional model exploiting a later observation can pass this gate without being a virtual-cell world model. There is no improvement strategy being improved here, no successor inheriting that strategy and no changed research program. Therefore this pilot cannot answer RSI, even with large predictive improvement. Those components remain separate obligations for the original research goal.
