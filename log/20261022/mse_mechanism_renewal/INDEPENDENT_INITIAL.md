## What fails before biological fitting

Four exact rational checks ran in `check_operation.py`; outputs are in `ALGEBRA_RESULT.json`. No MSE scalar was read.

1. **Undefined signed correction.** `P(s != 0)=P(s=+1)+P(s=-1)` does not identify `E[s]=P(s=+1)-P(s=-1)`. Two distributions with nonzero probability 3/4 have signed means +3/4 and -3/4. The specified logistic cannot compute the proposed subtraction.
2. **Inert ranking line.** Even granting a signed-mean correction, one drug-independent `m(c)` gives `(bar_s_i-m)-(bar_s_j-m)=bar_s_i-bar_s_j`. All pairwise differences and TopK rankings remain identical. Target probe feedback cannot alter rankings through that line, nor the stated source-only magnitude tie-break. An action-dependent correction would require an additional conventional drug-conditional model.
3. **No artifact identification.** With one well and identical control counts `M0,T0`, algebra gives `RCN-RCF=(M/M0)(T0/T-1)`. It tracks a total retained-count relationship, not an identified control artifact versus true cancer response. Real source scores use separate medians and averages, so this synthetic identity cannot invert their aggregate values.
4. **Wrong-way decision existence case.** Under those same simplifying controls `M0=10,T0=100`, action A with `M=9,T=10` has `(RCN,RCF)=(1/10,-8)` and positive disagreement; action B with `M=1,T=110` has `(9/10,10/11)` and negative disagreement. The proposed higher-sign ranking favors A although B is strictly better on both endpoints and is jointly positive. This refutes a universal joint-benefit interpretation, not an empirical average-value claim about every learned sign-based model.

The second and final call accepts these defects and demotes the candidate. Its repair suggestions introduce ordinary signed-response regression and drug-dependent conditional prediction, not a distinct update. Its two-class-softmax suggestion needs a zero-class treatment if zero signs exist. Agreement by the model is not the evidence: the explicit operation and arithmetic are.
