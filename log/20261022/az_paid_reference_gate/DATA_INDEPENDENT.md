## Exactly what the oracle means

Task: in GDSC007, use two purchased other-pair matrices from the same source/project/MASTER/stock/plate to predict an unpurchased target pair's 72-hour CTG inhibitory-response surface. This is a static functional prediction task. It does not observe molecular state, drug-induced trajectories or direct cell death. Pair identity is known; the new variable is the cell context and its purchased reference response, not an unseen drug pair.

The previous metadata scan reports 17,567 same-plate eligible GDSC007 target matrix instances. They are not 17,567 independent research tasks. The raw release has observed matched `lib1_y/lib2_y`, combination `inhibition`, and separate fitted/derived fields. Use the observed matched mono fields only from purchased witnesses; the target's attached mono fields remain inaccessible. No `yhat`, IC50, slope, error, expected HSA/Bliss or fitted-drug source field is a predictor input.

For a target AB matrix, retain the frozen deterministic pair of other matrices covering A's and B's entire actual dose axes at exact Decimal µM coordinates. All AB repeats are excluded. AC plus BD is valid with different C,D; a triangle is only one sufficient example. Same-plate scope is `(member,PROJECT_ID,MASTER_CELL_ID,CELL_ID,BARCODE)`. Layout IDs and stock expansions are not new cell identities.

A query reveals **all observed mono and combination outputs of the purchased witness matrix**, with explicit missing tokens. With n released combination rows it reveals n combination values plus 2n attached mono fields, including repeated attachments. The two queries cost two matrix bundles, not two scalar labels. Also report physical matrix keys, n1+n2 combination rows, 2(n1+n2) mono field entries, and the number of aggregated drug-dose references. These entry counts are not authenticated numbers of independent original monotherapy wells. For GDSC007, each matrix has 35 or 49 design rows; two bundles therefore normally expose 70, 84 or 98 combination rows and 210, 252 or 294 total observed output entries before missingness.

No target intensity or attached target mono may be read to form an input, choose a witness, determine support, or repair a missing paid reference. Do not treat source-wide monotherapy data as free. All models receive the same purchased fields; a residual model may ignore some paid information, but the strongest direct/refit comparison must be permitted to use all of it.

## Minimum episode and metadata selection

One episode is **one target source-ID pair in one MASTER cell model**, with a selected target matrix and its two fixed witnesses. To avoid inflating duplicated stock/plate measurements, choose at most one eligible target matrix for each `(MASTER,unordered source-ID pair)` by lexicographically smallest frozen full target key. This chooses one actual stock and plate for that episode. It does not average target responses or select a responsive replicate.

Retain every dose coordinate of the chosen target matrix as its fixed hidden function menu. Do not trim high doses, flat mono regions, low-support points, inconvenient response values or noisy-looking backgrounds after opening. All other target-pair repeats remain sealed for this first pilot. A separate reliability study could use them later under another protocol; they are not extra training here.

Each episode starts with the same role-appropriate historical training data and frozen initial predictor. Acquire the two specified witnesses, optionally perform the permitted ordinary update, predict the entire target surface, seal predictions, and score. **Reset before the next episode**: no previous episode's purchased data, target labels, fitted cell embedding or adapted weights carry over. Otherwise the campaign budget is larger than two bundles. Report total episode bundle charges and the union of distinct source matrices separately; duplicate appearance of a source matrix across isolated retrospective episodes does not turn their sum into unique prospective wet-lab cost.

Within-cell episodes are correlated. Compute per-episode well-averaged error, then equal-weight episodes within each MASTER, then equal-weight MASTERs. Confidence intervals and paired comparisons resample MASTERs, not wells, plates, stocks or source matrix instances. A future cell-level campaign that jointly chooses several pairs and reuses paid probes is a different budget/problem, not a free interpretation of this pilot.

Before targets are opened, save a compact metadata manifest with: role; MASTER; exact target/witness keys and source IDs; stock/plate/layout; target axes and shape; purchased row counts; eligible pair counts per model; target counts per pair/role; cancer-type/tissue distribution; and model/source identity mappings. Report the available distribution before imposing any sample-size condition. No universal 16-cancer-type or 64-pair requirement applies.

## Raw mono aggregation: one simple rule

For each **purchased witness matrix × actual drug ID × exact µM concentration**, take the arithmetic mean of its finite observed `lib*_y` entries. Keep values as released; do not clip, winsorize, choose a favorable row, select by source-fit error, or infer independent replicate counts from repeated attachments. Normalization can produce finite values outside the nominal 0–1 range; retain them for the primary analysis and label a Bliss polynomial reference accordingly rather than claiming all inputs are exact probabilities.

Use the designated A witness's aggregated A curve for target u and the designated B witness's B curve for v. Exact dose coverage was already established by metadata; no interpolation or extrapolation is needed for target inputs. Do not pool source-wide same-dose rows, target-pair repetitions, other episodes or unpurchased matrices. If additional paid compounds appear in both witnesses, direct/refit controls may use them, preserving matrix identity and equal-matrix weighting rather than treating repeated attachments as new independent samples.

All finite entries at a paid matrix/drug/dose contribute to the mean. Missing/non-numeric/nonfinite entries contribute zero weight, not zero inhibitory effect. If every entry for a required dose is missing, the required mono coordinate is unavailable. Inconsistent finite entries are averaged and flagged in paid-data diagnostics; they do not trigger a replacement query or a model-dependent panel exclusion. A large inconsistency can defeat the structural hypothesis, but cannot cause an invisible change of oracle.

This mean is a deterministic summary of **released attached observed fields**, not an estimate based on authenticated independent raw monotherapy wells. That distinction is enough for this empirical task.

## Missingness, costs and prediction coverage

Freeze the target/witness manifest from metadata first. Each witness costs one bundle even if some or all values are missing. There is no free alternative witness, no retry selected by its numerical quality and no reading of hidden AB mono to fill gaps. Report both nominal purchased output counts and finite received counts.

Every method must produce a prediction for every predeclared target coordinate. If a required paid mono coordinate is unavailable, use the **same predeclared dose-only historical predictor** as fallback for the effect model and its coordinate comparisons; include explicit missing indicators where the strongest direct method supports them. Never impute missing mono as a real zero effect. Fixed fallback prevents abstention from creating a favorable scoring subset. Strong direct/refit arms receive the same missing tokens and may learn their handling using training/validation only.

A private evaluator reads only target `inhibition`, after predictions are sealed. Score finite target values on one common mask for all methods and report denominators: planned/scorable wells, episodes and MASTERs; models with no finite target score remain unscorable, not zero error. Do not select panels, supports, roles, doses or best models using this target mask or magnitude. Score partial episodes on their available target wells under the already fixed cell-weighting rule, and disclose the missing-target population. Do not claim success on the full planned assay population if coverage is inadequate. Root/statistical review should freeze a minimum evaluable cell count and coverage rule before opening; this is a single stopping condition, not an iterative qualification exercise.

No target-range filter is allowed. No source QC is reconstructed from target values. A later source-quality diagnostic is descriptive and separate from the primary comparison. A failure to achieve the fixed coverage condition stops this pilot; do not replace its panel with more complete contexts after looking.

## Fair controls and the actual claim

The plain hypothesis is that a pair-specific residual in paid mono-effect coordinates, `y_AB = u + v - uv + h_AB(u,v)`, transports across cell contexts usefully. A common pair-specific map is an ordinary statistical model. Its coordinates need not be sufficient: equal/flat u,v can hide different doses and mechanisms. This is an empirical compression claim to test, not a causal identification result.

At minimum include: nominal Bliss; a pair-specific residual in dose coordinates with the same spline/kernel complexity and search budget; the shared effect-coordinate residual; a strong direct predictor receiving pair IDs, actual doses, u,v, their Bliss/product features, all paid witness mono/combination information and the same available background metadata; ordinary refitting on the same two paid bundles; and a fixed/validation-selected simple mixture. All transformations supplied to the new model, including u,v and the nominal Bliss reference, must also be available to direct/refit controls. Deliberately withholding the effect coordinates from the direct model is an unfair baseline.

The dose-coordinate residual may still have the same paid Bliss base; changing only its residual coordinates then isolates the structural restriction. A pure dose-only predictor is a useful fallback/ablation, but cannot replace the strongest same-information direct comparison. No arm receives hidden-pair mono values for training held-out-cell predictions. Pair-specific h can be learned from training cells where AB is historically observed; that is expected seen-pair/new-context transfer.

This gate uses fixed metadata-selected witnesses. It has no adaptive acquisition contribution, inherited update-program comparison or improvement-of-improver test. Parameter sharing across cells is ordinary pooling/transfer. If it helps substantially, it may support a functional representation component; it does not establish the user's world/agent/inheritance/joint/RSI claims.

## Application value and stop rules

Primary measurable evidence can be cell-weighted functional-surface error on the development gate. This is not by itself drug-discovery utility. Include an honest decision-headroom check: within one pair, maximum-dose inhibition may already saturate ranking, so a largest-dose/monotone rule must be an anchor for any dose-ranking/regret claim. A separately frozen minimum-effective-dose or dose-constrained decision may be useful, but cannot be invented after seeing outcomes; raw source dose is not clinical exposure or toxicity.

Root should choose one primary practical margin and uncertainty criterion before numeric opening. A reasonable candidate to debate is at least 0.02 absolute and 10% relative cell-weighted error reduction over the strongest ordinary same-information control, with paired MASTER-level confidence bound above zero. This is a proposal, not a registered result or an inferred power calculation. A method that beats Bliss but ties strong direct/refit has not established a distinct useful structural advantage.

Stop the exact configuration if the fixed observation/coverage contract fails, strong ordinary prediction leaves insufficient decision headroom, the structural comparison fails its prospective useful margin, or benefit exists only through target-mono leakage/different information. Do not add agent or improver layers to rescue a failed property. No chemical dictionary download or proof of original raw monotherapy well independence is necessary merely to run this **source-ID, released-field, retrospective** pilot. Those become necessary for claims about interchangeable compounds, independent measurements, prospective wet-lab economics or biological/clinical generalization beyond the declared source.
