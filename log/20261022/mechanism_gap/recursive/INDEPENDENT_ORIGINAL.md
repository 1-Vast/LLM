## Why biology can require a delayed edit
A measured reduction of resistance after washout may require both adding a reversible susceptibility state and calibrating its recovery dynamics. Adding that state with a neutral initialization can have zero immediate functional benefit. A short-term calibration-only repair may give immediate improvement but preserve wrong later rechallenge extrapolation. Similarly, a clone-selection representation can require separate composition calibration. Expanding failed branches indiscriminately is wasteful; their probability of becoming useful after a specific further edit is the object to learn. This is an experimentally testable search-value hypothesis. A complete refit or directly proposing the two edits together may remove the apparent valley, so complete-program controls are mandatory.

Cell assays differ from code benchmarks: outcomes are noisy, destructive measurements may use different wells, exposure history may change the population, and evaluating another program on an already measured trace is computation, not another independent wet experiment. A validation panel cannot be counted as fresh for each code evaluation. Later utility must use a separately held-out whole lineage/culture campaign. A persistent response alone is not proof of clonal selection, and declining resistance alone is not unique proof of reversible adaptation.

## Exact learned object and update
Store Q_b(c,p,e), an empirical value table/regressor indexed by remaining proposal budget b, currently observable response context c, provisional executable program p and legal next edit e. Store trial counts and provenance; no source final-model weights transfer. Source episode records (c,p,e,p_next,cost,score_next,terminal_score). Base rule Q_0 is terminal accepted-program utility; for b>0 use observed transitions and finite-horizon backward averages:

 Q_b(c,p,e) = mean_source[delta accepted utility + max_e_next Q_(b-1)(c_next,p_next,e_next)]

Costs decrement b by actual edit/evaluation expense; all actions use the same incumbent acceptance rule. Search can keep a provisional branch even while deploying the best already validated incumbent. Later policy picks argmax Q_b from the same fresh p0 for all arms. Neither likelihoods of hidden cell states nor unobserved rewards are required: all credit comes from measured or explicitly synthetic full feedback traces. Missing source edit paths cannot be silently supplied; exact backup here uses an exhaustively measured tiny menu. For larger menus ordinary off-policy coverage/uncertainty controls are necessary, not claimed solved.

This learns HOW to choose/continue future improvement proposals. It is not just saving a final better model or a natural-language memory. Operational Level3 would require that updated policy finds more useful later programs than old policy, fixed search, contextual sequence portfolios and matched ordinary metaoptimization at equal TOTAL source+later costs. A toy change of proposal law is only a necessary witness, not real biological transfer.
