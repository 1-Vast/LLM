## Marked-current branch

The event identity J=delta N_H-B_H+D_H is correct when B_H is the actual net high-state contribution of divisions, pre-death marks are valid, and all population movement is accounted for. It supplies information absent from unmarked aggregate turnover. This is a useful conditional measurement contract. It identifies signed net crossing, not gross crossings, persistent lineage identities, or the same cells reversing. The hidden-cycle construction correctly distinguishes J=0 from no transitions.

A sign reversal between epochs does not prove drug-dependent transition rates. The explicit shared-rate example gives (2,-2,0). With deterministic expected occupancies S and fixed annihilator w=(2,2,-3), W=w^T J is a valid necessary shared-rate constraint, and the varying-rate example gives -2. Yet ordinary two-rate elimination yields exactly the same residual up to scale: W=-3*r. Finite-volume conservation has the same arithmetic input/cost; the least gross crossing |J| is ordinary two-bin flow. This is a complete equality for this proposed operation, not merely a finite empirical tie. W=0 is not sufficient for physical feasibility: the saved negative-rate example lies in the linear span but outside the nonnegative-rate cone. A practical feasibility procedure needs the cone check and uncertainty model.

For stochastic occupations, J-integral(q_LH*N_L-q_HL*N_H)dt is a counting-process martingale under the stipulated intensity model. This does not imply E[J|T]=S q, because realized occupancy is endogenous to the same events. The proposal explicitly recognizes this, and the result explicitly says stochastic_test NOT_IMPLEMENTED. No stochastic false-positive guarantee follows. Independently estimated weights alone do not automatically restore exact nuisance elimination: their nullspace must still match the relevant evaluation expectation/design, with estimation error controlled. No biological adaptation or molecular cause is uniquely identified by a rejected constant two-state model; hidden states, marking errors and other misspecification remain competing explanations.
