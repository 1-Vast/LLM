## Scientific operation and exact scope

Under repeated drug exposure, a population can become enriched for a resistant/cycling phenotype by selective killing/outgrowth or by changes in cell-state transition rates. Endpoint reporter distributions, total births/deaths and later regrowth can fit both stories. This is the existing Watermelon/AP-1 information bottleneck, not a reason to reopen those assets. The question here is a different measurement contract: can a small **state-marked turnover statistic**, rather than full founder reconstruction or a new fitted bridge, supply an experimentally falsifiable constraint on a selection-only explanation?

The useful functional application would be deciding whether an altered regrowth response warrants a washout/rechallenge protocol and an intervention-dependent state-transition model instead of additional endpoint-only calibration. State must have a predeclared relationship to functional regrowth or sensitization; a fluorescent marker shift alone is insufficient. No current source has qualified this contract, and a positive mathematical witness will not claim a drug decision gain.

Two-state notation: N_H(t) is the absolute number of live high-state cells. Over an epoch, B_H is the net addition due to divisions assigned to the state at the event, D_H the removed high-state cells, and J the cumulative signed crossing from low to high. Exact event bookkeeping gives

    J = N_H(t1)-N_H(t0)-B_H+D_H.

Counts must refer to the same physical population with no unrecorded ingress/egress; daughter-state changes must be included consistently in the event contribution. Death marks are the state immediately before death, not altered postmortem staining. The statistic needs no unique initial-to-terminal cell matching. It identifies net crossing only: opposing crossings cancel, lineage identity is absent, and positive/negative flux in pulse/washout does not prove the same cells reverted. Unknown or misclassified event mass requires a declared error bound; no clipped counts are allowed.

The naive initial construction would treat opposite signed pulse/washout J as reversible drug-induced adaptation. That is too weak: unchanged spontaneous switching rates plus different occupancy can reverse net current. **Exactly one substantive revision is specified before testing:** measure state-specific residence times T_L=integral N_L(t)dt and T_H=integral N_H(t)dt, and eliminate the shared spontaneous transition rates across epochs.

Under a two-state continuous-time population model with shared spontaneous rates q_LH,q_HL, arbitrary state/time/drug-dependent birth and death rates, and correctly marked events,

    E[J_e - q_LH T_Le + q_HL T_He] = 0.

More precisely the residual is the state-transition counting-process martingale when the occupation times are predictable; the displayed unconditional moment does NOT imply E[J|T]=S q for arbitrary realized endogenous T. Conditional inference requires appropriate martingale inference or an explicitly valid stochastic model, not ordinary iid regression by declaration. The proposed first execution is deterministic expected-flow algebra only.

For a known deterministic expected occupation design S_e=(T_Le,-T_He), choose a predeclared left annihilator w with w^T S=0. The proposed shared-rate falsifier is W=w^T J. In a stochastic experiment w cannot be chosen adaptively from the same noisy future occupation without a valid correction; fixed design, source-estimated weights with independent evaluation, or valid martingale construction must be frozen separately.
