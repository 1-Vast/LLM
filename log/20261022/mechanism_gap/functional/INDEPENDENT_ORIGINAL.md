# Independent functional assessment — FateMap, 2026-10-22

## Decision

No new mechanistic operation is proposed. FateMap is a real, useful source for clone-conditioned drug-response prediction and for testing ordinary models of action-dependent clonal outgrowth. The inspected public methods and headers do **not yet qualify** the requested intermediate-history → second-intervention → independent later-task experiment. This is a missing authenticated decision-time join, not a demand for unique causal identification, complete lineage trees, identical cells, or a large patient cohort.

The one application assessed is choosing whether to continue versus interrupt/rechallenge vemurafenib in BRAF-mutant WM989 A6-G3 melanoma cultures, using clone/state information to anticipate later resistant outgrowth. No AP1, WM1366, ResponseDynamics, Watermelon, ADCC, or previous moment-bound configuration is reopened.

## Exact legal actions and physical feedback

The real WM989 schedule comparison is 1 µM vemurafenib throughout versus 9 days at 1 µM, 25 days in drug-free culture medium, and 5 days back at 1 µM. Medium exchange is every 3–4 days. A decision at day 9 therefore has two authentic schedules: maintain drug, or withdraw and later rechallenge on that fixed schedule. It is not a dose-per-clone action, an arbitrary optimized holiday duration, or a switch to trametinib after an observed adaptation state.

Other authentic actions are initial treatment assignments: FM02 splits at 1 µM vemurafenib, 100 nM vemurafenib, 5 nM trametinib, and 100 nM vemurafenib plus 5 nM trametinib. FM03 is DMSO versus 4 µM pinometostat pretreatment for five days, followed by 1 µM vemurafenib. Those initial/pre-treatment branches cannot silently serve as observed-history-dependent second treatments. The method's vemurafenib concentration was chosen for growth arrest without overt cytotoxicity: clone abundance measures net persistence/outgrowth, not a death hazard.

Each new action or observation costs a culture aliquot with its assigned treatment, maintained culture interval, harvest, extraction/capture, library preparation, sequencing and analysis. scRNA-seq destroys sampled cells; a barcode connects relatives across split cultures, not repeated molecular measurements of the same living cell. Bulk gDNA is also a destructive pool assay. No free per-clone query is legal. An intervention is applied to an entire aliquot containing many clones.

## Authenticated joins and their limits

1. Methods: GFP-positive barcoded cells are sorted and expanded for 4–5 population doublings before treatment. The WM989 vemurafenib FateMap plan starts each split with 600,000 barcoded cells. Shared barcodes can therefore associate relatives across split conditions. Randomized assignment of aliquots to therapy is **not established** by the inspected methods; the stated randomization is of bulk RNA samples before extraction/library preparation, which is a different operation.
2. scRNA: a side reaction links the lentiviral barcode to the 10X cell-identifying sequence and UMI, joining cell state and clonal label within a captured endpoint library. Low-UMI calls and multi-barcode cells are removed; the method reports retaining barcode assignments for roughly 50–60% of single cells. STARCODE merging uses Levenshtein distance ≤8. These retained calls define a capture/filter-conditional phenotype distribution; no inverse-capture weights or complete natural clone prevalence is authenticated here.
3. gDNA: known F8/D8 barcoded-cell spike-ins are added to pellets before extraction. A linear regression using (0,0) and the two standards converts barcode counts to estimated cell numbers. UMIs do not necessarily label unique DNA molecules. This substantially strengthens the abundance contract for an authenticated sample, but does not automatically calibrate every scRNA library or supply an unmeasured intermediate state.
4. GEO GSE233766 has 22 sample headers. FM01 is two WM989 1 µM sibling splits; FM02 is the four initial action arms; FM03 is two pretreatment arms; FM06 is two untreated sibling samples; FM07 is precisely two schedule endpoint samples (GSM7434421 continuous, GSM7434422 discontinuous). FM06 is not labeled as a pretreatment aliquot of FM07. No day-9 or day-34 molecular sample is listed for FM07. The remaining FM04/05/08/09/10 sample pairs concern different cell lines or untreated melanocytes, not independent repeats of the WM989 schedule menu.
5. The discontinuous GEO sample has `DiscontinuousDose` in its description but `Continuous` in its supplementary filenames. Sample accession plus treatment/description must govern a future join; filename text alone is unsafe.
6. Figshare 22798952 and 22802888 authenticate FM01…FM10 archive names; processed barcode mapping files include FM07. The FM07 ZIP64 directory contains only continuous/discontinuous endpoint barcode FASTQs distributed over sequencing lanes L001–L004. Lanes are technical sequencing subdivisions, not independent cultures or later tasks.
7. Figshare 22806494 processed gDNA directory lists BC01, BC02, BC03, BC04_Transductions, FM02, FM03 and mouse experiments; it contains FM02 `dataFor10X` mapping filenames. It lists no FM07 calibrated gDNA branch. This is an archive-header observation, not proof no external FM07 count record exists. The public inspected records do not authenticate the count/state/time join needed here.

The independent biological decision unit is a separately established culture/split family subjected to the schedule comparison. FM07 authenticates one family and two culture arms; independent later repeats of that same menu are not authenticated. Clone rows provide within-family predictive units and can support finite conditional checks. They do not turn one pool-level treatment choice into thousands of independent later decisions. BC02/BC03 filenames suggest further drug-dose experiments, but names alone do not establish fresh founders, dates, randomization, or repeated withdrawal/rechallenge tasks.

## Useful finite task that survives, and why it is narrower

FateMap can support a within-experiment question: does a clone's captured state/fate in one sibling aliquot help predict its captured fate or calibrated net outgrowth under another **initial** treatment, beyond action, abundance, and pooled expression? The barcode identity is a legitimate cross-sibling join. A held-out-clone evaluation could test interpolation inside that family without identifying whether every cell switched state. It must report conditional capture, missing clone calls, whole-aliquot cost, and its limited within-family scope. FM02 supplies the authentic action menu and scRNA samples, with gDNA mapping candidates in the same experiment directory. Exact barcode normalization, standard quantities per sample, treatment start/end times, and fresh-family replication remain to be authenticated before numerical execution. We did not inspect the payload to assume those fields exist.

For the requested day-9 decision, however, the currently authenticated FM07 observations are after the schedules have already been executed. Training a model with that endpoint state and claiming a prospective day-9 benefit would leak future information. A donor sibling observed after 39 days is a costly completed pilot, not an early feedback query. No universal causal impossibility is asserted: randomized aliquots, appropriately timed destructive sibling feedback and later outcomes could support honest predictive value even with mechanistic ambiguity.

## Strongest comparators and falsifier

Any later legal experiment must compare, on identical available aliquots and observations: (a) direct action-conditioned prediction from current abundance/state; (b) a direct history-conditioned regressor with clone effects and the complete legal assay history; (c) ordinary birth/death or net-growth branching models, with action-dependent state transitions and refitting after each paid observation; (d) FateMap's existing matched-clone comparisons and an expression-plus-lineage transport/transition baseline of the LineageOT/CoSpar type where its required data actually exist; (e) active learning and equal-budget random/grid schedule search whose unit of acquisition is the whole culture/assay bundle. Mentioning those model families is a comparator specification, not a new prior-art search or a claim that unobserved trajectories can be reconstructed from these endpoints.

The finite useful falsifier is a held-out independent culture-family challenge: predeclare a population-level schedule loss based on calibrated later net outgrowth, reveal only paid pre-decision feedback, and ask whether history improves schedule choice over the best direct and ordinary transition/refit competitors at equal assay budget. A mechanism need not be uniquely identified to win that test. The current header contract does not supply that challenge; therefore no mechanistic operator or benchmark is instantiated and no empirical improvement is claimed.

## Retrieval caveat and stopped configuration

ZIP headers were inspected by HTTP206 ranges, with hashes. The first gDNA tail was entirely inside its central directory. The first processed-All tail included uninterpreted compressed bytes before its short directory, and an initial FM07 parser treated a ZIP64 offset sentinel as an ordinary offset and fetched an 8,870-byte non-directory range. Neither was decompressed or numerically decoded. These files and the failed configuration remain preserved. The reader was corrected to retrieve the 22-byte end record, ZIP64 locator/end record, and exact central-directory range; final FM07 listing is from that corrected route. This caveat changes no scientific inference and no target has been opened.
