## Source observations verified directly

The Methods' Drug treatment experiments section specifies the continuous/discontinuous comparison, with withdrawal after day 9 and later rechallenge. GEO accessions GSM7434421 and GSM7434422 identify continuous and discontinuous FM07 arms respectively. Their descriptions distinguish the schedules even though the discontinuous accession's supplementary filenames contain Continuous. A join must use accession and experimental annotation, not filename vocabulary alone. The FM07 directory contains Cont/Discont barcode FASTQ filenames with sequencing-lane labels; these do not establish separate cultures, intermediate observations or independent later campaigns. The inspected GEO metadata does not authenticate a day-9 state assay available before choosing withdrawal. This is bounded absence in inspected records, not proof that no such assay exists elsewhere.

The actual FM02 GEO headers authenticate four initial treatment assignments (GSM7434409-12). The processed gDNA directory contains corresponding analyzed directories and dataFor10X mapping candidates. This is a credible opportunity for matched-clone, action-conditioned endpoint prediction. It does not alone establish a starting absolute abundance for every clone, per-sample standard quantities, assay synchrony, or a fresh family for external validation. Absolute endpoint abundance and change from baseline are different targets.

An additional useful distinction is visible directly in the FM03 directory: it contains both FM03_Naive_DMSO and FM03_Naive_DOT1Li as well as FM03_DMSO_1uM_PLX and FM03_DOT1Li_1uM_PLX analyzed barcode files. The GEO endpoint headers GSM7434413/14 authenticate DMSO versus DOT1Li pretreatment followed by vemurafenib; Methods specifies pretreatment before the main treatment. Thus the archive is more promising than an endpoint-only characterization of all FateMap experiments would suggest: it has candidate pretreatment-associated abundance baselines. But Naive in a filename does not establish harvest time, sibling relationship, whether each baseline is sampled before or after pretreatment, standard amounts, or matching barcode dictionaries. There is also no authenticated choice among different second interventions conditional on that baseline. Both FM03 endpoints receive the same second drug. This candidate strengthens a narrower history-associated prediction opportunity without qualifying an adaptive second-action benchmark.

Methods explicitly say that the barcode side reaction connects the inherited barcode to the 10X cell identifier and UMI, while filtering retains barcode assignments for about 50-60% of captured single cells. Therefore state distributions are conditional on capture and barcode filtering. Unassigned cells cannot be silently treated as zero-size clones or as a random omitted fraction. The destructive sample is a snapshot of relatives in a culture aliquot, not repeated molecular measurement of the same living cell. Shared inherited barcodes provide a valid sibling association, and full single-cell identity is unnecessary for useful prediction.

The gDNA Methods explicitly describe sample-pellet spike-ins of known F8/D8 cells and regression through their standards plus zero. This supports calibrated cell-number estimates where those standards and the sample join are authenticated. It does not calibrate another sample merely because it belongs to the same paper, and does not convert scRNA capture frequencies into absolute live state counts. gDNA UMIs are explicitly not guaranteed unique molecules. No FM07 gDNA sample was authenticated by the inspected directory. Barcode abundance measures net population persistence/outgrowth; neither its fall nor an apoptosis expression score is a measured state-marked death count.

## Scientific versus algorithmic conclusions

The frontier bookkeeping proposition is scientifically intelligible. State-marked births/deaths and absolute state counts could constrain net crossing without reconstructing complete ancestry. Its shared-rate residual could reject a restricted model, while hidden states, measurement error or marker kinetics remain alternatives to a biological adaptation interpretation. The original deterministic tests correctly include spontaneous-rate sign reversal and hidden opposing currents. This is a useful warning about what evidence an assay must supply.

However, the same event observations give the same current by ordinary conservation, and the stated witness is an ordinary two-rate residual. No distinctive algorithmic operation or held-out decision gain is demonstrated. FateMap endpoint barcodes do not provide the required live pre-event state marks or state occupation integrals. Adding a bridge, learned agent or additional fit cannot recover that missing observation contract. **STOP the literal novel-core claim**, while retaining the measurement-contract observation as a scoped scientific result. Even a richer source would not repair the demonstrated optimized-baseline equivalence by itself.

The recursive code implements a real finite synthetic continuation calculation, but its direct refit and complete-program controls already solve the same family. Different amplitudes with the same exactly observable decay law do not establish independent biological later tasks. Recomputing a score is not a new physical feedback observation, and the addendum correctly separates those costs. The toy cannot establish biological procedure inheritance or world-model/agent necessity. **STOP this literal continuation table as the proposed new mechanism.** Missing FateMap joins and ordinary-control equality are distinct reasons, neither of which should be hidden behind the other.

No nearest-literature equivalence was independently searched here; the exact computational reductions already suffice to defeat the present superiority claims. Cross-domain inspiration is real in the supplied proposals, but the biological recasting has not produced a new validated computation. Drug resistance and net-outgrowth prediction have practical scientific relevance. World-model necessity, autonomous-agent necessity, joint synergy and Level3 benefit remain unverified. The leading failure is converting incomplete, late, capture-conditional sibling observations into cheap early independent decisions.

## Decision units, comparisons, and one bounded next step

The legal action is applied to an entire mixed culture aliquot. Clone rows can be predictive units for a conditional within-family test; they are not independently purchased drug choices or thousands of separate schedule decisions. A completed long-duration sibling pilot can inform another culture only after charging its full culture and assay cost and respecting availability time. Direct history-conditioned prediction and ordinary action-dependent net-growth/transition refitting must receive the same paid observations. A useful world-model result does not require identifying a unique causal mechanism, but does require a decision target and honestly available inputs.

The one low-cost step with a concrete chance of changing a source decision is to authenticate a metadata-only sample manifest for the four named FM03 gDNA branches. Required fields are culture/split ancestry, pretreatment and main-treatment timestamps, harvest timestamps, sample-specific F8/D8 standard amounts, and barcode normalization/mapping identifiers linking them to GSM7434413/14. This is a request for experimental provenance, not additional outcome inspection. If it confirms true pre-main-treatment abundance measurements and matched endpoints, FM03 could qualify a small pretreatment-conditioned net-change prediction task, with conditional capture handled separately. If it fails, abandon that candidate join. It would not qualify a varying second-action decision, an FM07 day-9 assay, or independent later RSI, so no such numerical pilot is justified by this check alone. This review does not authorize a new download or fit.
