## ReTTT's strongest positive claim survives the cross

Skeptic challenged any overly broad “RSI not shown” dismissal. ReTTT's adapted reflector changes how subsequent procedural training experience is generated, while the frozen-reflection control retains action-policy adaptation and the memory machinery. The author-reported 63.57% versus 56.43% success difference therefore supports an **in-episode contribution from adapting experience construction**. Cross-entropy/AdamW being familiar parameter-writing operations does not erase that component contribution. I explicitly accept that this can be described as bounded evidence for an improvement-of-improvement component, with the scope stated.

My reciprocal question asked skeptic to preserve this positive interpretation alongside NO_MAESTRO_PROMOTION. Skeptic explicitly agreed: the adapted reflector changes the future training-material-generation procedure, so the ordinary optimizer label cannot dismiss its local value. Its absence of cross-episode persistence limits transfer evidence, rather than refuting the within-episode comparison.

The remaining inference limits are concrete. Action and reflection share the evolving adapter; changes to later actions also change later interaction histories. Aggregate episode success compares complete feedback systems, not the quality or efficiency of a next-generation updater evaluated from a common fresh model state, identical history and fixed downstream evaluation budget. The common-history trajectory example is a useful illustrative intervention point, not a general later-task test. The paper resets adapter, optimizer and memory after every episode. Cross-task inheritance and common-fresh-start later improvement efficiency therefore remain unverified.

Cost must also retain its denominator: approximately 1.085 times worker time is ReTTT-QA relative to **aTTT**, not a documented equal-budget comparison with Frozen Reflection. Fewer gradient steps do not establish lower total runtime, and the inspected source does not provide a matching frozen-reflector runtime audit sufficient for a cost-normalized improvement-of-improvement claim. The reported component success difference should be credited without upgrading that missing claim.

## Biological evidence boundary and actual exchange

Biology's strongest challenge is that trajectory string predicates can certify an observed successful action in the source environments, whereas a viability response cannot certify a mechanistic explanation. A drug with no observed growth effect can have different exposure, target engagement, time dependence, noisy measurement or population composition. “This measured condition had response y” is an admissible observation; “this target is irrelevant” is not entailed by the same scalar result. A language-generated lesson cannot manufacture missing molecular supervision.

I explicitly accepted this distinction. Any future biological adaptation may retain measurement facts and predictions with stated scope, but must not treat consistency of a textual explanation with a terminal value as verification of a causal drug mechanism. No new verifier or biological relation-construction rule was supplied here; direct ReTTT transfer is not promoted.

I also challenged the biological mixture draft's possible overextension: lack of mechanistic identification does not imply that functional decisions can never improve. If source-learned statistical response structures are distinguishable by legal feedback, ordinary Bayesian mixture adaptation may still yield useful decisions, even when the structures cannot honestly be named target versus exposure mechanisms. Biology explicitly accepted in reply and limited its indistinguishability witness to the stated contract: the two hypotheses give identical support distributions but opposite optimal query choices. It is not a general result against sparse feedback. This preserves the separation between a useful capability hypothesis and a new-algorithm claim. The current two-weight likelihood normalization has a concrete standard finite counterpart and does not by itself supply MAESTRO's new core.

## The examined revision gap is not a new candidate

Skeptic agreed with my specific distinction between editable explicit memory and the parametric consequences of earlier entries. It also challenged a second possible conflation: even exact corrected-history replay would verify a **program-state or decision property**, not the truth of a biological mechanism correction. I explicitly accepted this. A real assay revision stream would need independent validity, time stamps and legal later outcomes. The static ALMANAC result is not such a stream, and its failed/unstable decision comparison cannot be relabeled as revised-evidence adaptation.

Exact deterministic replay requires a checkpoint of the model, optimizer state, data order and relevant randomness. Simply negating an old gradient after state-dependent/Adam updates does not implement the corrected history. Conversely, a useful approximate repair need not match exact replay if it makes and validates a different decision-risk/cost claim. This audit proposes neither such a repair nor a new convex downdate: ordinary replay and sufficient-statistic operations are concrete, finite competitors whose actual cost must be counted.

The gap remains a research question with no qualified candidate, not hidden novelty being withheld until a dataset is found. No new editor, lesson gate or deletion toy is justified by this description.

Skeptic's local-source audit provides the exact SPO definition for a finite top-K decision. I accept that objective-level relationship. Invoking the decision-relative objective alone therefore does not supply the missing novel update operation.

For SelectTTRL, the source's current-correctness association and frozen intermediate-percentile selection remain genuine operations with reported domain results. No current development labels may be used to turn the proxy into a new gate, and no cheap update-value estimator with a distinct validated property was supplied. This is a transfer qualification finding, not a claim that the paper's empirical selection gains are impossible.

## Final decision and honest evidence level

What can be borrowed honestly from ReTTT is the experimental contrast: compare an evolving experience-construction procedure with its frozen version while retaining action adaptation, memory operations and feedback legality, and report the full generation/training/interaction cost. Its author-reported positive component result motivates such a distinction; it does not authenticate a drug task, a new biological lesson rule, or inherited later-task efficiency.
