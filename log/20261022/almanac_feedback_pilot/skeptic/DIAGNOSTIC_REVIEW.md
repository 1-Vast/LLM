## Observation and sign

Innovation is observed high-high outcome minus source-only B0 anchor forecast; error is prediction minus observed query. Negative correlation means larger-than-predicted anchors coincide on average with underprediction of queries, consistent with incomplete response to feedback. B0 mean-cell correlations range approximately−0.068 to−0.396; B1 attenuates these but does not eliminate them. Study-excluded B1 remains−0.047 to−0.227. This is a real descriptive residual relation in the saved diagnostic, not proof that it produces a new cross-task update algorithm or future MSE improvement.

No orthogonality theorem requires a regularized nonlinear predictor trained on20 source cells to have zero innovation/error correlation on ten different cells. Therefore nonzero residual correlation is not a special contradiction of direct prediction or evidence requiring world-state dynamics. Ordinary residual stacking with position-specific innovation slopes, kernel changes or regularization can express the relation. Such ordinary operations have not been experimentally ruled out; fitting yet another correction merely to chase this relation is not justified as innovation.

## Legal observability and identification

At target time B1's own anchor innovation is legal: solo input, a source-fitted B0 anchor prediction and the revealed anchor suffice. Query error is not observable before revealing the scored query; its association is estimated retrospectively here. Within-cell centering over887 different pairs is a legitimate diagnostic operation, but does not establish a legal per-pair failure trigger or an inheritable research strategy. It also does not remove shared drugs, different pair/dose designs or assay heterogeneity. These887pairs are not887 independent biological replications.

Ten tune cells were already used to choose B0/B1 hyperparameters. This diagnostic is post-selection exploratory reuse, not a new held-out confirmation. Same-study exclusion reduces a particular source overlap but does not separate target same-plate calibration from a cell's biological response. No new biological state variable, function, causal mechanism or decision benefit has been identified.
