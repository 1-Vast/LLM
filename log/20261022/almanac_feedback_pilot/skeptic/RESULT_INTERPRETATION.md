## Positive result and exact claim

The frozen ordinary-feedback gate passes. Adding one highest-by-highest combination observation per target pair, while keeping the eight scored queries fixed, reduces selected development MSE from 0.009444383 to 0.008495382: 10.048% relative improvement, absolute gain 0.000949001. Fixed-prediction 10-cell bootstrap interval [0.000625074, 0.001285953] is positive. All ten cells improve, relative gains 0.92–26.66%, and every delete-one-cell average remains positive. MAE improves much less, 0.06512891 to 0.06356169 (2.406%). This is useful evidence for ordinary feedback value in known-pair/new-cell surface completion, not a novel update law.

After same-pair source-study exclusion, MSE gain remains 0.000602873 (6.001%), with 9/10 cells positive. This does not establish global independent-study transport: tuning still used its original source/tune relationships, and shared-study structure remains elsewhere. Bootstrap uncertainty conditions on fixed predictions and ten selected cell clusters; it does not incorporate model-search, assay noise or a population of independent laboratories.

## Direct predictors and Gaussian conditioning

Both selected methods are ordinary direct RBF kernel predictors: B0 bandwidth2/lambda0.1, B1 bandwidth2/lambda0.01. A conditional Gaussian world-state interpretation is unnecessary to obtain the strongest result. The best development Gaussian B1, alpha0.5, has MSE0.009195698, worse than selected direct B1 0.008495382. Indeed it is slightly worse than the post hoc best direct B0 0.009130761; Gaussian does not justify a privileged world-model module here. Fixed mix B1 MSE0.009863746 is also worse than selected B1. No WM-over-direct contribution has been demonstrated.

The post hoc best B0 method is bandwidth2/lambda0.01 (0.009130761). Selected B1 still improves it by approximately6.96%, but this is descriptive, not a new confirmatory comparison. The selected portfolio's10.05% gain includes different regularization selected on tuning; do not attribute all of it to a fixed update operation. Matched-method diagnostics nevertheless support real information value: all13 nontrivial learned fixed methods (three ridge, six RBF, three Gaussian, one mix) improve with the anchor. NCI is unchanged because it ignores the anchor; the naive fixed half-anchor mean correction gets worse (0.010228865→0.011407199). Thus the useful object is how strongly and where feedback should affect the surface, not the existence of another agent tier. These thirteen related methods are not independent replications.

## Dose and decision limits

All eight dose-position MSEs improve, but82.45% of total gain comes from the last three flattened query positions (indices5,6,7), adjacent to the highest-dose anchor along at least one axis. Very small gains at positions0 and3 argue against interpreting the result as general global state reconstruction. This supports a testable locality/feedback-transfer diagnostic, but not proof of a new spatial law: standard kernels already produce it.

Mean top89-of887 selection regret drops0.008636980→0.007767149 (10.071%), but only6/10 cells improve. On a PERCENTGROWTH/100 scale, the average regret benefit is about0.087 percentage points of growth; this is far smaller than the relative percentage might imply. Under study exclusion ranking regret instead worsens0.009603202→0.009719452, only4/10 cells improve. Consequently the present evidence supports surface-MSE utility more robustly than catalog decision improvement. There is no reliable drug-decision transport result yet, and this is neither clinical response nor measured combination synergy. An MSE gate pass must not erase these decision counterexamples.

## Recommendation and falsification

Advance one bounded source/development-only error diagnosis, not architectural expansion. The observable improvement target is a compact rule for transporting an observed anchor innovation across dose positions while avoiding harmful shifts on weakly related positions/pairs. Compare any proposed rule with the already successful direct RBF conditioning at identical observations and compute; include naive shift as a negative control. First diagnose conditional residual covariance, anchor-error magnitude and position dependence with legal source-only fitting. Check whether apparent transfer survives source-study exclusion, and whether it improves the explicit catalog decision objective rather than merely MSE. Do not call residual correlation a mechanistic cell state without intervention-based tests.

A candidate core is worth a sealed multi-position review only if it specifies a genuinely different falsifiable rule and predicts a reproducible advantage over ordinary RBF, linear conditioning, retraining and equal-budget search; otherwise keep this as ordinary feedback calibration and stop that novelty route. No new reserve may be opened merely to find a favorable metric, bandwidth or subgroup. The later20 cells remain closed. Neither agent actions, inheritance, repeated research tasks nor improvement-of-improvement have been tested; full research goal remains not achieved.
