## Minimal operation and update

For clone i, culture r, drug d and passage/dose stage k, let p_{irdk} be its relative abundance. A basic mechanistic implementation would update

p_{ird,k+1}=p_{irdk} exp(g_{idk}+b_{irk}) / sum_j p_{jrdk} exp(g_{jdk}+b_{jrk}),

with multinomial/overdispersed sampling for observed barcode reads and vehicle branches informing passage/background drift. This identifies net relative selection, not clone death/division hazards or absolute total burden. k is the actual dose/passage stage; the event-defined final resistance time must not be treated as a shared elapsed duration.

At an endpoint whole-culture assay with test inhibitor a and concentration c, the proposed coupling is

Y_r(a,c)=sum_i p_{ir,end} h_i(a,c),

where h_i is a transferable lineage-response function; if raw live-cell count is used, an appropriate known starting-population/normalization factor must accompany it. Fit shared selection and response parameters from source cultures by the joint barcode and functional likelihood, then update them after each paid whole-culture sample/functional assay. This is a concrete finite-population selection-plus-mixture model. h_i shared across selection histories is an additional hypothesis, not implied by persistent barcodes. The primary's plasticity findings warn that h_i may instead depend on unobserved within-clone state and history. Adding those latent states would create a more weakly identified ordinary hierarchical model, not automatically solve the problem.

An ordinary Bayes/active-design agent would choose the next legal complete assay to reduce posterior treatment-ranking regret. A possible inherited improved-improver object would be an executable repair-selection policy eta mapping source cross-validated residual patterns to proposed revisions of g or h. However, no new update law for eta or nonstandard assay-selection rule has been specified here. Naming that object does not make the above model Level3 or distinguish it from conventional model selection and learning-to-optimize. An actual old learned eta versus new learned eta, swapped into common fresh downstream starts on independent later tasks at total source+later costs, remains absent.

## Why the literal core does not survive

The selection update is the ordinary replicator/exponential-tilting model; the endpoint equation is ordinary mixture regression. Their biological interpretation can be useful without being a new computational mechanism. Equal-information direct/refit controls can use the same early/middle abundances, vehicle ratios, stage, genotype, ancestral barcode identity and source functional curves. A structured conventional estimator can fit exactly the same equations. Nothing in the current candidate gives an additional identifiable observation or different optimizer unavailable to it.

There is a precise information limitation if the promise is transfer of clone-specific collateral response. At a fixed assay condition, write observed endpoint responses as y=P h, where each row of P is a source endpoint clone mixture. If v lies in ker(P), then h and h+epsilon*v give identical source y; for interior bounded response functions choose small epsilon to preserve bounds. A later mixture p* with p* v≠0 has a different predicted response. This applies unless the relevant response vector is restricted by independently supported structure or the later mixture is within the identified row span. No numerical rank was computed, no claim is made about the actual released P, and this is not a theorem against ordinary useful within-family prediction or average transfer under a correct supported prior. It shows exactly why shared ancestral labels plus bulk response alone do not establish the intended clone-law inheritance.

General barcode→transcriptome joining is not available, and one selected sequenced clone does not supply response functions for all ancestral lineages. Endpoint-derived eight-population clusters use all three future stages; they cannot supply prospective prefix enrollment or an uncharged predictor. A direct predictor and a fixed whole-program/feature-selection search must receive any admissible prefix-defined grouping or prior as well.
