## Authenticated design and temporal meaning

Methods `Par53–Par54` establish a common ancestral ClonTracer-barcoded MCF7 population, followed by division into DOX-untreated (ER-WT) and DOX-induced ESR1-Y537S conditions and drug selection branches. Each drug×ER condition was evolved in triplicate; the article explicitly calls each replicate an independent evolutionary realization from the same ancestral population. These are genuine evolved cultures, not three sequencing lanes, while their common ancestry and one engineered cell background limit external generalization.

The planned doses were 50, 100, 200, 350, 500, 750 nM and 1 µM, escalated every three passages. Barcode sampling is expressly defined as Early after three passages at 50 nM, Middle after three passages at 200 nM, and End when proliferation in 1 µM was restored. Vehicle controls were sampled at matched passage numbers. Thirty million cells were plated at each passage to limit stochastic barcode loss. This gives an observed ordering and an intervention-conditioned population history. End is a resistance-defined event, not a fixed common calendar day; do not invent exact elapsed durations or time-to-resistance labels. Four treated selection families (two inhibitors×two ER conditions), each with three evolutionary cultures, are available at the design level. Drug-specific vehicle control branches also need their actual labels retained.

Methods `Par70–Par71` use gDNA from ten million cells, with 2 µg for library preparation. A complete destructive aliquot assay is the physical information unit. Individual barcode queries, arbitrary missing timepoints and adaptive dose paths were not measured. The paper provides sampling chronology; assay turnaround and operational online availability at a decision deadline are not reported. Offline reveal of an intermediate sample is a legal explicitly simulated information-acquisition replay, not a historical closed-loop experiment.

## Functional and state observations

`Par66` describes observed live/dead-cell counting after drug treatment using Hoechst/propidium iodide and Celigo imaging. IC50 is computed from day-5 live-cell responses normalized to vehicle. GR metrics use day-0 and day-6 counts. The primary also reports cross-inhibitor responses in resistant populations and perturbations of ER expression. These supply a real functional application opportunity rather than RNA-only prediction.

However, the exact mapping between an IC50/live-response assay and a particular evolved population, evolutionary replicate or isolated clone must be authenticated before a joint history→functional-response replay. Assay triplicate wells do not become three additional evolutionary trajectories. The one supplementary table requested below is the barcode table, so no IC50 numeric outcome or separate functional-table header has been inspected here. Current joins are therefore stronger for longitudinal barcode compositions than for per-trajectory functional utility.

`Par87–Par88` defines scRNA-seq on parental cells and vehicle/resistant conditions from evolutionary replicate 3, plus the ER-WT PalboR clone 3_15. This is terminal profiling of a subset of trajectories, not Early/Middle scRNA from all three replicates. The non-expressed ClonTracer limitation is explicit in `Par134`: the authors could not connect every enriched subpopulation to a transcriptome. An endpoint population RNA profile may be a terminal target/descriptor in its proper scope; it cannot supply a per-barcode molecular state or earlier predictor.

## Avoidable future-information leakage

Published eight-population clustering uses one parental sample plus all three timepoints across all three evolutionary replicates per model, ten samples total (`Par78`). Low-frequency filtering also looks across included samples. These are legitimate descriptive author analyses, but their future-informed cluster labels and selected clone universe cannot become prospective enrollment or features. A numerical forecast must define a baseline/prefix-only universe, an unseen/unobserved barcode treatment, and training-only population assignments, or work directly with explicit relative compositions under a frozen censoring rule.

## Minimum useful replay

The first defensible minimal task is Early→End relative-composition forecasting under the actual observed escalation regimen, optionally paying for the complete Middle barcode sample. Hold out complete evolutionary trajectories, account for the shared parent, and give every comparator the same shared barcode identity and known drug/ER context. Evaluate ordinary strong direct trajectory predictors and standard transition/refit forecasts before claiming a world-model contribution. Buy-none, buy-all, fixed-budget random and standard uncertainty/active-selection controls are necessary before claiming an agent contribution. Frozen objective and meaningful improvement margins must precede count decoding.

This is a concrete source substrate for a mechanistic candidate, not itself a novel mechanism. It may also support a functional target once exact resistant-population→assay identifiers are joined. It does not establish an observed policy for switching inhibitors during evolution, a full dose-sequence counterfactual oracle, unique molecular mechanisms, independent later-task RSI transfer, or improved-improver performance. Absence of those wider properties is not a reason to discard the narrow legitimate task.
