# Candidate replay boundary before any outcomes

This is a source-qualified task boundary, not a frozen numerical experiment or a new mechanism claim.

The primary source supplies an ordered, observed intervention path: after three passages at 50 nM (Early), further scheduled dose escalations to three passages at 200 nM (Middle), then eventual restored proliferation at 1 µM (End). A relative barcode-composition forecast from Early, optionally purchasing the complete Middle sample, to End is scientifically interpretable within these selection families once actual sample labels are authenticated. It does not require precise calendar days or an unobserved molecular-state trajectory. End is a resistance-defined event; do not label it a common elapsed-time horizon or predict time-to-resistance without extra timing data.

Legitimate grouping is by the complete evolutionary culture trajectory, never random barcode×sample cells. Replicates are independently evolved from the same ancestor, enabling a limited shared-ancestor generalization task, not independent cell-line families. Shared barcode identity across these trajectories must be declared available to all baselines; this is potentially informative shared ancestry, not silently advertised as unseen clone identity. The same parental measurement is one shared observation, not separate assays for each trajectory. Two drugs×two engineered ER conditions give four treated selection families with three evolutionary cultures each; drug-specific passage-matched vehicle controls also exist. The actual treatment/control header mapping must be verified, not inferred from a generic vehicle label.

A potential agent's only directly replayable sequential information choice here is whether/where to acquire a whole intermediate barcode assay, using the observed trajectories as an offline data reservoir. Measurement feedback requires destructive gDNA sampling from a physical aliquot, extraction, library preparation and sequencing; a barcode is not a free query. Methods use 10 million cells for extraction and 2 µg gDNA for library preparation and plate 30 million cells at passages. Those quantities are acquisition costs/requirements, not a measured turnaround-time schedule. No timely online availability or adaptation of the historical dose path is established merely by sequencing the stored early sample.

This could test information value for a forecast or prioritizing which cultures to assay next. It does not itself demonstrate useful drug switching, functional discovery benefit or a new agent rule: buy-none, buy-all, fixed-budget random/uncertainty selection, strong direct barcode-trajectory predictors and conventional transition/refit models are mandatory controls. A distinct new learning/decision rule would still be needed.

The paper also measures day-5 live-cell response/IC50 and day-0/day-6 GR metrics. These provide a concrete functional endpoint opportunity, including drug cross-sensitivity, and must not be dismissed because no full RSI split is yet available. However, the exact functional-table labels must be matched to selection condition, genotype, evolutionary replicate or clone, tested drug and assay repetition before a joint history→functional-response task is legal. The three assay wells in a triplicate are not three new evolution replicates. An IC50 summary is not automatically the utility at a declared therapeutic concentration; a fixed-dose live-cell decision needs the relevant measured curve/values and a frozen utility.

Single-cell RNA is parental/terminal profiling from evolutionary replicate 3 (plus a selected resistant clone), and ClonTracer barcodes are not expressed. Thus no per-barcode early→RNA state join or RNA trajectories are supplied. Endpoint RNA can be a legal terminal descriptor/target in that scope; it cannot be used as an Early predictor. The published eight barcode populations were clustered with all three timepoints and all three replicates. Their endpoint-informed assignments cannot define prospective enrollment/features without re-fitting on allowed training/prefix information. A baseline-defined barcode universe or explicit unseen-barcode category is needed before numerical forecasting.

No biological numeric array has been opened. This boundary preserves a valid narrow within-family task without promoting it to independent later-task RSI evidence, unique causal mechanism identification, or completed framework success.
