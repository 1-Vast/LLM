# Bounded primary-source discovery

2026-10-22. The currently qualified genomic trajectory source covers only one
selecting antibiotic. Root therefore registers two public Europe PMC searches:
recent antibiotic evolution with sequential/switching/longitudinal designs,
and 2026 virtual-cell/world-model literature. The goal is to identify actual
multi-intervention state/function sources or substantive recent methods, not
to reopen stopped project configurations or manufacture an extra task from a
time point. Search results/abstracts are literature/design-exposed. They are
not full-method inspection or experimental validation.

Exactly one GET per fixed query in `search_primary.py`, no retries, alternate
hosts, redirects, author contact or model API; 60-second timeout and at most
3,000,001 received bytes per request, with over-3,000,000 failure retained.
Request body/hash/status and projected title/author/date/DOI/accession/abstract
are stored here. No response tables or sequence files. Failed searches remain
failures, not evidence that the scientific literature or data are absent.

Success is a concrete primary-source lead with an explicit future qualification
gap. A search hit alone never authenticates a dataset, novelty, causal control,
drug utility, inheritance or RSI. All code remains under `research/` and all
source/decision/failure records under `log/`.
