## Root API rectangle proposal

After independent sealing, I read api/RESPONSE.md and root/EXACT_PROJECTION.json. The proposed closest baseline in the API response (an interaction auxiliary loss) is weaker than the faithful conventional counterpart: the same exact equality/slab projection in the same low-dimensional rule space, or ordinary set-membership identification. For a rectangle contrast a=(1,-1,-1,1), projection onto a^T f=b is ordinary least-change projection; the existence of a known convex projection does not preclude useful constrained prediction but prevents claiming this literal operation as a new algorithm.

The root's nullspace witness correctly targets a separate issue: arbitrary main-effect shifts preserve the rectangle interaction certificate while changing absolute utility and potentially intervention ranking. Thus zero certificate error cannot establish functional choice quality. Conversely three known corners plus a genuinely known contrast identify a fourth corner; it would be incorrect to claim rectangles never inform prediction. The response's allowance of a mere single observed difference does not identify the full interaction unless an additional authenticated structure is supplied. A model-filled missing corner is not empirical four-corner feedback. Root was sent these concrete objections and the stronger baseline, with no request to reopen ALMANAC outcomes.
