## Why this action can be worth doing

The stopped primary maps source predictions through a target-fitted multivariate affine correction using eight controls. That model can fail even when the pattern across named control residuals contains useful information about the unqueried drugs. A source-trained support-to-target conditional map asks a different, narrow question: does *which* control departed from prediction help forecast *which* other known drug departs, beyond metadata, missingness, ordinary calibration and conventional refit? This is information worth testing once with a small linear diagnostic. It does not require pretending the prior failure never happened.

The existing fixed mixture's small favorable descriptive result already prevents a blanket statement that feedback contains no useful information. It does not establish the proposed cross-drug pattern. Stop the exact failed multivariate:k32 configuration. Do not convert its rejection into rejection of all feedback or all context models, and do not convert the mixture into an argument for a new architecture.

PhysiCalWorld supplies a concrete training contract: a context clip and a disjoint prediction episode share one authenticated physical configuration (primary text lines199–209; Appendix A lines1056–1058 and Algorithm1 lines1090–1124). Its cached descriptor is reused only while dynamics remain fixed; exploratory contact/motion must reveal relevant variation (lines210–221). The closest CiPA transfer is disjoint control/noncontrol prediction within a site/cell/platform calibration group. It is not a temporal transition model or proof of common invariant physiology. The paper trains on135 configurations in several environments; our five source sites cannot support a similarly expressive learned encoder merely because each site contains many drugs.

## Non-negotiable statistical unit and residual-construction threats

One physical site is one independent transport unit. Ten contexts, eight purchased controls,96 drug-dose targets and repeated exported rows do not create more independent sites. Keep all contexts of a site in the same outer fold, use equal physical-site aggregation, and show every site's difference. No run/well/donor IDs are authenticated. In particular, control and noncontrol observations are not verified different episodes with the same hidden biological parameters; metadata grouping supports a statistical prediction test only.

Nested cross-fitting must be constructed inside each outer fold. For outer heldout site h, every training support residual, response residual, lookup, H scale, imputation constant, feature standardization and shrinkage choice must exclude h completely. For training site j, its baseline must also exclude all contexts of j. Preparing global leave-one-site-out residuals once and then holding h out is invalid: the baseline attached to training j contains h's outcomes and can carry target information into fitted coefficients.

Even correct nesting leaves a real training/deployment mismatch. Outer training residuals use baselines from three sites, heldout support/target residuals use four, and a later new-site use would use five. With an equal-mean baseline and m training sites, leave-one-site-out residuals are m/(m−1) times deviations from their common training mean and sum to zero. The training pairs therefore share baseline estimation error and algebraic dependence. Both support and target residual vectors can covary because the same uncertain baseline was subtracted. Cell/platform fallbacks and separate H validity masks make an automatic scalar correction unjustified. A successful map can be learning correction of a noisy reference, a useful ordinary prediction effect, without identifying a stable new-site state. Leakage prevention alone does not settle this issue.

The cheapest clean stress test is a fixed-reference construction, preregistered before fitting: in each outer fold, enumerate the six two-site reference subsets B of its four training sites; build all residuals relative to B only, train the map on the other two sites, and score the heldout site relative to the same B. Give each physical training site equal total weight and average the six predictions using a frozen rule. Reference sites provide lookup/scales only, not conditional-map training outcomes. Training and heldout residuals then use an identical reference for each fit. This has just two independent map-training sites per fit and is deliberately a severe low-power diagnostic. Six references are correlated alternatives, not six replications. If this stress is unaffordable or unstable, use a single metadata-fixed reference split and label that limitation; do not silently treat mismatched jackknife residuals as deployment-matched evidence. A source result dependent on the mismatched construction cannot justify a latent-state claim.

## Minimal fair falsification

Freeze the existing primary eight controls, target drug-dose population, known absent source bundle, E/M/H definitions, equal-site loss, source-only scales and probability clipping. No dev6/7 numerical use or reserve opening. One source-only run, with no new rank/hyperparameter search after inspection. Final protocol must state the exact linear estimator and penalty; a large tuning grid is not justified with four training sites per outer fold. Retain all source and outer predictions to make rescoring possible.

The essential candidate is an ordinary full-rank support-to-target ridge. An optional rank1 constrained version tests the compactness assumption; it is not necessary to answer whether feedback contains predictive information. A linear encoder c=A rQ followed by linear decoder rT=B c is exactly the reduced-rank map BA. A low-rank map that cannot outperform ordinary conditional regression does not establish a core component. Conversely, full-rank success with rank1 failure would be evidence of conditional information and rejection of this compactness choice; it is not an information-null result.

At minimum compare, with identical allowed information and scoring masks:

- The source lookup and already specified ordinary per-head calibration, strong direct refit, and fixed lookup/refit mixture. No method gets terminal labels or additional queries.
- A same-input direct conditional predictor with source vector, drug/dose, cell/platform metadata, paid support values/residuals and support validity indicators. An additive support vector alone can produce only a shared output shift; include the necessary frozen drug/dose-by-support interactions or an equivalent multi-output conditional ridge. A weak additive-only comparator would fail to represent the very hypothesis being tested.
- A metadata-plus-validity-only conditional control. Replacing support numerical residuals with zero, retaining their masks, isolates the value of measured outcomes from reporting/site identity signals. Actual missing H never becomes an observed zero. A zero-filled *input residual placeholder with a separate mask* is permissible if frozen; H response loss remains absent when unmeasured, and no target H mean is imputed.
- Incorrect-context checks preserving whole physical-site grouping and, where possible, cell/platform strata, query identities and mask/value pairing. A support swap across cell/platform types is a distribution shift stress, not a clean hidden-context null. With only five sites some restricted permutation classes may have no legal nonidentity permutation. Record that fact and do not replace it with an apparently significant unstratified null. Any refitted null must rebuild all fold-specific summaries and must not move heldout target labels into training through support reassignment. Report the legal null contrasts descriptively; no population significance or independence of permutations is claimed.

Disjoint control and target drugs prevent trivial copying but not baseline artifacts or metadata confounding. Both same-input conditional ridge and mask/metadata controls are needed. An acquisition planner, recurrent state, nonlinear encoder, extra token dimensions or query subset enumeration supplies no missing independent sites and is outside this diagnostic.

## Success, stop and application meaning

Predeclare a descriptive information gate: lower equal-site combined loss than the best prespecified ordinary no-pattern comparator, no worsening of the three pooled endpoint scores, benefit beyond the metadata/mask-only control, and correct-context predictions better than the aggregate of legally comparable incorrect-context controls. Show all five site contrasts and a leave-one-site influence summary so that one unusual laboratory cannot silently carry the result. A strict all-five-sites improvement gate is a conservative progression policy, not a significance test; if adopted, freeze it now. A numerical tolerance can exclude floating-point ties, but no clinically meaningful superiority margin is supplied by the current endpoint convention.

Passing that gate permits the sentence: 'source-site controls contain descriptive cross-drug conditional predictive information under this construction.' It does not yet establish useful gain at a new site. The fixed-reference stress must retain the direction of the information contrasts before treating the result as more than jackknife-reference correction. Failure of either gate stops expansion of this residual-transport candidate; no penalty/rank/control change or bigger model rescues this run.

For a compact-rank claim, compare rank1 directly with full-rank same-input ridge using a predeclared predictive or measured computation criterion. Full-rank superiority stops the rank1 necessity claim while preserving any evidence for ordinary conditional adaptation. Beating a source lookup alone is inadequate. Even a successful low-rank map is classical reduced-rank regression. Its factorization can always be collapsed into a direct conditional predictor, so these data cannot demonstrate that a reusable 'world descriptor' is architecturally necessary. The direct collapsed predictor is the decisive ablation.

Dev6/7 have already exposed terminal outcomes and motivated this hypothesis. They may later support one frozen *development reuse* stress test, with the exposure stated, not independent confirmation. Do not involve them in this source diagnostic or automatically proceed to them on a favorable result. Sites8/9, TG later14 and AZ413 remain protected. New independent evidence is needed for a strong transfer claim, and source success alone creates no license to open a reserve.

The expected information gain is enough to justify a bounded linear diagnostic after its protocol resolves the residual contract and legal nulls. If those cannot be made explicit without model escalation or fictitious task replication, the sensible action is to stop at the current result. No world contribution beyond conditional prediction, adaptive agent value, continual improvement, inheritance, joint benefit, or common-start old/new-improver C4 follows from this proposal.
