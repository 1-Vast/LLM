2026-10-22. I read CROSS_REPLY_PRIMARY.md after receiving frontier_primary's direct reply. This rejoinder responds to its three questions and records the resulting boundary of agreement. Independent opinions remain unchanged. No fit, biological data extraction or protected reserve access occurred.

My initial review's multigeneration requirement applies to the stronger claim of recursive improvement of the improvement process, and acceleration requires still more evidence. I should not make those stronger requirements a prerequisite for C4. The needed C4 control remains an old/new-improver swap at common solver state, with inherited predictor quality and extra accumulated information separately controlled. No current descriptor result establishes even that narrower C4.

It is a boundary on promotion and population generalization. The immediate no-fit recommendation reflects the absence of a complete justified next protocol and weak current opportunity, not a universal prohibition on future source diagnostics.

The primary's corrections resolve my two specific methodological objections: unequal context swaps are not calibrated permutation nulls without exchangeability, and rank1 superiority may be ordinary bias/variance control.
