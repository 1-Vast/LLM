## Challenge 1: rank one is not the missing mechanism

The proposed rank1 support-to-target map and same-input ridge are ordinary predictive baselines. With five physical source sites and four available in an outer LOSO training fold, fitting an eight-control/three-head vector to a 96-target/three-head residual vector has extremely limited independent information. A rank1 model can win because shrinkage reduces variance. It cannot establish that one biological state generated both groups, and such a win cannot distinguish PhysiCalWorld's proposed physical interpretation from elementary multivariate regression.

The defensible minimal claim is only: a predeclared function of measured control responses predicts residual outcomes on disjoint drugs of a held-out source site, beyond a matched predictor using metadata/source information and beyond the existing ordinary calibration/refit controls. Rank1 beating ridge is a conservative engineering gate for pursuing compression, not evidence that the rank restriction is necessary or scientifically novel. A linear failure stops this compact linear expansion; it is not proof of absent nonlinear information.

## Challenge 2: an incorrect-context swap is not automatically a statistical null

The addendum proposes permuting context assignments across sites while preserving masks. Contexts differ in cell type and platform; physical sites may contain unequal numbers of contexts. The protocol does not authenticate well/run/donor identity. Swapping measured support across such groups can alter metadata compatibility and measurement availability as well as the alleged site-specific residual signal. Preserving masks alone does not make those observations exchangeable.

If enough admissible same-metadata contexts do not exist, there may be no valid conditional permutation distribution. The proposed operation can still be an explicitly adverse-context ablation: deliberately give a model another site's controls and observe whether prediction changes or degrades. It must not be called a calibrated significance test or proof of shared physiology. An unrestricted permutation's failure can reflect distribution mismatch. The useful negative control is a matched source-only/metadata-only predictor; a swap adds a diagnostic, not independent replication.

## Challenge 3: nested recomputation and masks are part of the hypothesis

Cross-fitting each residual once across all five sites does not suffice. In each outer held-out site, that site's outcomes must be absent from every support baseline, training residual target, feature scale, shrinkage selection, candidate choice and descriptor training step. Training-context residuals therefore need baselines constructed within the outer training set, excluding their own physical site as required. All same-site contexts travel together. Smaller effective training sets are a genuine limitation, not a reason to leak the outer site into baseline residuals.

The exact map must also specify how a missing support H value is encoded without inventing a response, and how heterogeneous target H masks enter training. A row-level reduced-rank regression implemented by silently completing the response matrix may no longer test the stated endpoint contract. Source-conditioned feature imputation plus missingness indicators could be an implementation convention, but it must not supply a fake target truth. These choices are substantial enough to require the fresh protocol already acknowledged by the primary.
