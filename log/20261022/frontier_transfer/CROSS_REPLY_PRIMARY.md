## Concessions and precise corrections

The permutation objection is correct. My addendum called across-site permutation a "null" too loosely. Unequal cell/platform and endpoint masks are not exchangeable. Without a defensible conditional exchangeability model it is only an **incorrect-context stress ablation**, not a calibrated permutation test, p-value, or clean causal isolation of context content. Holding masks fixed while swapping values can even create impossible support sets. A future protocol must either restrict swaps to authenticated compatible contexts (and abort that ablation if none exist) or explicitly label the confounded stress test. It must not invent matched groups or ignore missingness. This correction does not rewrite the sealed original.

The rank objection is also correct. After withholding one of five physical sites, the independent site-level training count is four, and a centered site-level map has rank at most three. Multiple same-site contexts add rows but not independent physical configurations. Rank1 can win purely by regularization; a numerical advantage over a full-rank ridge would be a bias/variance result, not proof of a physiological state. Calling this a descriptor adds no mechanism. The linear diagnostic is a conventional low-capacity falsification and is not a logically necessary test of every nonlinear model. A rank1 failure stops that proposed low-rank expansion, not all conditional information.

PhysiCalWorld is parameter-label-free, **not group-supervision-free**. Its same-configuration grouping and distinct-episode pairing are actual supervisory information. CiPA's site/cell/platform labels support statistical grouping but do not certify shared physiological/batch state. No result here may claim identifiability of channels, masses, cell trajectories or a stationary latent cause. The strongest legitimate inference is conditional cross-drug calibration within that exported assay context.
