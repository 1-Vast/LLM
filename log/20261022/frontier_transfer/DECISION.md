## Candidate, evidence and opposition

The most concrete retained operation is **infer a reusable functional context from a few observed control interventions, then predict disjoint drug responses in that context**. PhysiCalWorld's contribution to this discussion is its precise grouped/distinct-episode training contract, not the word token. A static CiPA version would map eight measured control responses and explicit validity masks into a context summary, then condition the96 noncontrol forecasts on it.

Support: this is an operational response to unconditional source-best ranking and to the question of whether a common affine correction misses drug-specific context. Real control feedback exists; the query/evaluation pools and separate E/M/conditional-H endpoints are authenticated. The ordinary probe's fixed50/50 mixture has a small benefit, so a blanket no-information claim is false. The hypothesis can be falsified on whole-source-site replay without opening a reserve.

Opposition: with linear encoder/decoder, c=A*r_control and r_target=B*c imply exactly r_target=(B*A)*r_control, a low-rank ordinary regression. Nonlinear variants are conditional neural processes/meta-learning. A rank1 win may only reflect shrinkage. Five physical source sites are five independent environments, despite ten contexts and many drugs. Site/cell/platform grouping does not certify shared batch, donor or stationary physiological configuration. The existing multivariate primary update lost at both development sites, raising the burden for a more flexible model. No new biological invariant, improved improver, computational advantage or substantive distinction from the strongest ordinary controls has been shown.

Necessary modules for a future information diagnostic are only the finite feedback API, explicit masks, fully nested source summaries, a fixed simple conditional predictor and sealed evaluation. LLM, expression/video encoder, neural descriptor, learned cellular rollout, two-step planner, open-ended archive and code evolution are unnecessary at this stage. Removing them loses no established capability.

## Decision, open questions and eligible next test

**NO_PROMOTION and no deepening into a descriptor network, planner or RSI platform.** Stop treating these paper operations or their assembly as a new core. Deprioritize the two-step planner in favor of the simpler information question. The separate failed CiPA primary configuration remains stopped; its tiny mixture benefit is retained as a conventional comparator.

One bounded ordinary source-only diagnostic remains justified as information gathering, not an innovation claim: can measured control-response patterns improve held-out-site noncontrol predictions beyond source/metadata/mask-only and strong same-information conditional predictors? Before any fit it needs a separate complete protocol fixing dimensions, missing-value representation, shrinkage, nested selection, the same comparison population, practical opportunity margin, cost accounting, all predictions and stopping conditions.

Every outer held-out physical site must be excluded from baseline construction, training residuals, source summaries/scales, tuning and descriptor fitting. Training-context residuals use fewer source sites than pseudo-deployment residuals; a direct same-input conditional predictor and baseline-only controls must expose that possible artifact. Shared source-mean errors and leave-out residual constraints cannot be credited as transferable physiology. Missing target H is never completed with fabricated outcomes. All contexts at a site remain blocked.

Success would be an explicitly exploratory held-out-site gain over the best ordinary same-information controls, including the fixed mixture, with component scores and site variation retained; correct feedback must beat source-expected/metadata/mask-only inputs. A compatible incorrect-context stress test may be descriptive. Insufficient groups/masks, no useful gain, unstable site behavior or inability to define a fair comparator stops that exact low-capacity expansion. A linear failure is not a universal no-nonlinear-information theorem. A positive result still establishes conventional conditional calibration; innovation would require a separate substantive mechanism-specific advantage.
