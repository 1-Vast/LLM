## Independent review and experiment

`mechanism_review/ADVOCATE.md` and `SKEPTIC.md` were drafted before reading each
other. Root supplied common primary-source leads; this was independent initial
reasoning, **not blind reviewing**. Both then read the other draft, exchanged
specific questions and answers, and saved `CROSS_ADVOCATE.md` and
`CROSS_SKEPTIC.md`, including final responses. Agreement is not treated as
experimental evidence.

1. **Gene-coordinate Gaussian residual writeback: STOP_AS_CORE_INNOVATION.**
   Its induced drug predictions are exactly those of kernel posterior
   conditioning with K=SᵀCS, given the same mean, covariance, features, noise and
   feedback. The preregistered synthetic audit covers 12 batch cases and 24
   sequential-order checks; maximum discrepancy 6.443734434924409e-13. Omitting
   covariance in a negative control produces discrepancy 1.9276159664078754.
   This is algebra/implementation evidence only, with no held-out biological,
   utility, sample-efficiency or inheritance result. It does not cover every
   future nonlinear, sparse, non-Gaussian or representation-learning method.
2. **Haplotype mutation-selection forward model: STOP_THIS_CORE_CONSTRUCT.**
   The written update is an ordinary selection/mutation model; no new coupling,
   identifiable inference method or cost property was supplied. On marginal
   frequencies alone, its proposed haplotype input is unavailable. Both sides
   accepted a same-marginal/different-linkage counterexample even with additive
   fitness, and the advocate withdrew q-forward on that source condition.
   S-scores cannot be exponentiated as calibrated per-generation fitness;
   clone/mixture dose-response is not automatically a linear frequency readout.
   Distinct calibration channels or a defensible joint-identifiability argument
   could be assessed in a future proposal. They are not established here.

The original paper already connects chemical genetics to temporal mechanisms,
including hldE/waaD, waaF and marR routes, and discusses gain-of-function,
essential genes and epistasis. Repeating these explanations is not a new
discovery. Conversely, known components do not logically preclude every novel
combination: the stop concerns the two explicit constructs, not all potential
world models or practical ordinary-model applications.

## Retained hypothesis, required comparison and stopping rules

A narrow unresolved question is whether **early measured genetic information**
improves later AZI functional prediction beyond past IC90, day, actual selection
dose, growth covariates and mutation burden within this protocol. It was already
explicit in both reviews; it is not a third newly discovered core mechanism.
Twelve lineages may yield only an exploratory, wide-interval result. Do not
digitise or train solely to obtain a positive number, and do not buy 16GB of
reads to test a candidate already equivalent to an ordinary baseline.

If an independently useful numerical task is later registered, freeze the
target/horizon, feature recovery, target population, assay budget, practical
margin and uncertainty rule before arrays are opened. Whole lineage histories
stay together; future day5 classes, author colours/layouts, encoded aliases and
lineage-ID lookup cannot become early inputs. Baselines share all legitimate
screen data, states, histories, auxiliary supervision and feedback.

Required direct comparators include persistence/day/dose/history, original
OCDM/chemical-genetics features, regularized linear/kernel/tree predictors and
same-information multihorizon/multitask models. Explicit rollout additionally
faces ordinary transition/state-space/selection models and simple mixtures.
Any agent claim requires identical actual query bundles/budgets against fixed
SOP, ordinary active learning and equal-budget search; pair aggregates cannot
be expanded into fabricated raw assays. Weight and procedure inheritance need
separate common-start controls. Strategy improvement needs m0/m1 (then m1/m2)
from identical model/program/history and equal proposal/evaluation/query cost
on later tasks, including the cost of forming the improved strategy.

Stop the particular feature/transition claim if its required state or mapping
cannot be recovered, while preserving the legitimate narrower asset. Stop
practical gain if the prespecified interval upper bound is below its useful
margin; a wide interval is inconclusive. Exact ordinary-model reproduction
stops an exclusive-method claim, not the ordinary method's application value.
No additional architecture or program search is justified by source eligibility
alone. New multi-intervention source discovery continues separately.
