## Correction: equivalent expression is not proof of published precedence

Both peers and root challenged the most important inference in my independent position: defining g_u=-r_phi e with the same P_phi and the same outer query loss is a constructed re-expression of my candidate, not an authenticated previously published algorithm. I accept this challenge. The equations establish that the local-target expression contributes no extra state update over the direct synthetic-gradient expression. They do not establish that this exact finite teacher, training objective, and biological use were known, or rule out a contribution from the common rule itself.

The corrected statuses are:

- **STOP_TARGET_REPARAM_EXTRA_CLAIM:** calling the operation a local teaching target does not add a second learning mechanism or a demonstrated efficiency advantage.
- **FULL_RULE_NOVELTY_UNRESOLVED:** published coverage of the external-query-trained teacher has not been established. It must not be reported as KNOWN or as disproved merely because the rule belongs to learned optimization.
- **NO_PROMOTION:** no evidence currently qualifies the common rule as a new useful biological core. Its deployment bottleneck, data contract, comparative functional utility, and world-model/agent necessity are untested. This is not an empirical failure finding.

The independent title and the phrase “failed novelty attempt” must therefore be read only as failure to establish and promote the claimed extra mechanism in this study. They are not a priority verdict against the complete teacher rule. This correction supersedes any broader reading while preserving the sealed original.

There is also a precision correction. In exact real arithmetic, (u+r e)-u=r e. The two literal NumPy programs need not be bitwise identical: target formation followed by subtraction can lose a small r e against a large u. The direct-gradient form avoids that cancellation. No numerical advantage of the target form has been shown. Its additional vector addition/subtraction are not literally zero cost; both forms share the dominant matrix products and state. A compiled/simplified version can use the same recurrence. This is not an ideal expensive solver being substituted for a cheap finite program, but it also does not license an unsupported all-state floating-point identity. No new numerical test is claimed.

Outer training has its own contract: the explicit recurrence A'=A+eta_A r_phi e k^T is differentiated in the outer query objective. The stop-gradient applies only to the local loss interpretation. Naively applying a global detach to the teaching target in an autograd program could erase the teacher's outer gradient and would not implement the stated rule. local_rule.py only defines deployment updates and does not implement or verify source meta-training.

## API ESG: response to the two strongest objections

The completed ESG proposal was read only after my independent seal. It specifies a real gate and is not dismissed for family membership. Both API reviewers identified concrete invalid justifications; root has already run exact checks, which I inspected but did not repeat.

**Biology's strongest objection — nuisance does not cancel on the stated union of edges.** If residual includes c(s)=b_true(s)-b_psi(s), same-state/different-drug differences cancel c, but same-drug/different-state differences retain c(s_i)-c(s_j). Freezing b_psi does not certify that g_phi is a drug effect. Root's pure-state witness y=s, frozen base=0, g_phi=phi*s, phi=0 accepts a positive drug-module update even though true drug effect is zero. Thus the claimed cancellation fails under a legal case of the written contract. Restricting edges would change the proposal and is not silently applied here.

**Skeptic's strongest objection — marginal fair-coin agreement is not a binomial sample.** Shared endpoints induce dependent comparisons, and ordered reverse edges duplicate the same agreement event. Root's exact iid continuous vertex-noise witness over six vertices enumerates 720 equally likely orderings: a nominal .05 gate accepts 49/720 with 15 unique unordered edges and 169/720 with 30 ordered edges. This already holds without correlated measurement noise; it is a failure of the binomial certificate, not a biological performance claim. Bonferroni across directions cannot repair invalid per-direction p-values. I also accept the skeptic's separate count-versus-magnitude warning: seven concordant a=1 terms and one discordant a=100 term yield accepted count evidence but S=-93, so the gate's positive agreement interpretation is not the applied signed step.

For nonzero residual differences, the direction derivative of pairwise residual L1 is -sum_e sign(rho_e)*a_e,v. This is a concrete ordinary loss connection and demands a stronger baseline than sample MSE-SGD. However, copying the entire ESG gate into that baseline is no more a published-priority proof for the gate than copying my teacher was for mine. The justified ESG STOP rests on its failed nuisance/statistical claims and lack of validated advantage; the derivative identity separately shows that its update direction has an ordinary pairwise-loss interpretation. Neither proves all future pairwise gates useless. No repaired gate, new data qualification, or revival of old STOPs is proposed.
