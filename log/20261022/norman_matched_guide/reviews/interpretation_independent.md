## Decision

GO for the bounded 180-fit source-only capability comparison. Do not promote this comparison into a new method, virtual-cell world model, autonomous agent, or RSI mechanism. Its useful outcome is an evidence-based decision about the functional estimand and the usefulness of ordinary RNA side information on that estimand.

## What the current evidence supports

The qualification reports 102 eligible genes, 3,107 source pairs, finite selected-guide single and pair values, and numerical agreement between the locally available processed values and the specified raw source cells to floating-point precision. This is local numeric reconstruction of a declared endpoint and `b.mean` single prior. It is not evidence that the entire historical processing pipeline was faithfully recovered, that a chosen guide is an unbiased measurement of gene activity, or that the functional response is less noisy.

The comparison uses a common pair panel, common pair folds, common RNA input, identical parameter-menu size, and endpoint-specific single functional priors. Both alternatives are meaningful observational prediction tasks. Source-only out-of-fold scores can describe how much these fixed ordinary completion procedures benefit from RNA information relative to their no-RNA counterpart in the same task. Reporting absolute improvement, target variance, quadratic-relative error, all five arms, fold consistency, and costs makes the descriptive comparison substantially more interpretable than a raw cross-endpoint MSE ranking.

Pair withholding is interpolation among largely observed genes in one K562 experimental context. Single-gene RNA for the whole fixed panel is available to the representation; this is legitimate for the declared known-single/new-pair task, but must not be called held-gene or cross-context generalization. The pairs and five folds are not independent biological tasks for inheritance or RSI evaluation.

## Strongest alternative explanations

1. Endpoint and single functional prior change together. Any difference in the relative RNA benefit is a joint estimand-plus-prior association. It does not identify a causal contribution from matching the functional guide to the RNA experiment. An improved quadratic prior or changed residual structure could alter the apparent incremental contribution of RNA without a better state-to-function mechanism.
2. The gain is a ratio with endpoint-specific no-RNA error in its denominator. A larger relative RNA benefit can occur with a smaller absolute benefit; a smaller relative benefit can coexist with lower absolute error. The declared additional reporting is necessary. A cross-endpoint delta is not a method-effect estimate.
3. Gene-average and selected-guide outcomes encode distinct distributions of perturbation efficacy, selection, averaging, and measurement variation. Selected-guide agreement with RNA is not equivalent to recovering a biologically correct latent intervention. Surviving measured cells may also be a selected population. The present data do not separate these explanations.
4. A static correlation-derived RNA kernel and nuclear completion can exploit gene similarity and residual low-rank structure without learning a transition model, causal state evolution, or how functional feedback revises a cellular mechanism. That simple explanation is sufficient for any positive outcome of this experiment.
5. The same source research corpus has already influenced task selection. Frozen menus and source OOF prevent immediate outer-fold hyperparameter leakage, but this remains exploratory capability evidence rather than a fresh independent confirmation. Shared genes and functional measurements also limit interpretation of fold-to-fold agreement as independent replication.

## Interpreting either outcome

If `G_chosen - G_average <= 0`, stop the predeclared fixed directional prediction. Do not change the menu, normalize again, add agents, or search a new model solely to rescue it. This result does not show that matched guides are intrinsically unhelpful, that all RNA information is useless, or that all virtual-cell and RSI research has failed.

If positive, retain only a descriptive conditional benefit of these fixed models on the qualified chosen-guide task. The result does not establish causal guide alignment, noise correction, a new algorithm, necessity of a world model, or a reason to add program editors. No confidence interval from these overlapping folds alone would repair the biological independence or joint-endpoint confounding.

## Simplicity and escalation gate

The simplest presently justified computational account is endpoint-matched quadratic functional priors plus ordinary residual completion, with optional single-RNA side information. This experiment already tests that account. No additional NAIAD refit or large architecture is required to interpret the frozen question.

Any subsequent mechanism should first state a new falsifiable capability that static completion cannot provide, name its actual feedback and independent future task, and compare against the same-information direct predictor and ordinary union refitting. A guide-only cross-endpoint factorial with priors held fixed could diagnose numerical decomposition, but the mismatched cells would be diagnostic counterfactual model inputs rather than biologically matched endpoints; it would not identify causal alignment and is not a prerequisite or recommendation to extend this run.

WM escalation needs evidence that updating an explicit intervention-state or state-function relationship changes future intervention decisions beyond ordinary supervised updating. Agent escalation needs a fixed-policy or equal-budget search comparator. RSI escalation additionally needs a changed improvement strategy whose inherited form finds better later improvements on genuinely later independent tasks at matched cost. None of these gates can be passed by the present endpoint comparison, regardless of its score.
