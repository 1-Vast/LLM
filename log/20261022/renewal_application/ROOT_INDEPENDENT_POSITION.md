# Root independent mechanism/application position

Saved before reading this phase's peer drafts. Root has read historical REPORT/EVIDENCE and retained one independent DeepSeek response. No new biological response values or fits were read/run. Existing stopped configurations remain closed.

The provider explicitly declined to claim a new core. Its proposed diagnostic compiles functional-feedback signs/order into candidate constraints. This is not automatically distinct from ordinary constrained learning or program search. Changing candidate order is not itself world/agent contribution. Its use of remaining pairs in one target cell as a later-task unit also does not by itself establish inherited improver benefit across research tasks. These are concrete concerns for opposing review, not a universal theorem against all constraint mechanisms.

Two newly retained full primary papers sharpen a potentially different application: LCD-CLIPR (PNAS2026-10-08, DOI10.1073/pnas.2602630123) learns perturbation-conditioned stationary diffusions and identifies linear-drift causal effects; a SciAdv2026-09-09benchmark (DOI10.1126/sciadv.aed3414) finds distributional variance compression and context-dependent generalization among13methods/25datasets. Full text is evidence of the authors' claims/design, not this project's reproduction. They show that static snapshots can support scoped mechanism tests, but stationary causal regulation itself is an explicit near predecessor.

Root's concrete application lead is **drug-tolerant persister proliferation**: predict the rare cells that remain viable and cycle under continuous targeted therapy, then prioritize interventions against that reservoir. Unlike treating RNA abundance/captured cell number as viability, the Watermelon primary study (Nature2021, DOI10.1038/s41586-021-03796-6) combines expressed lineage barcodes, doxycycline-pulsed H2B-mCherry dilution as proliferative history, and scRNA atPC9days0/3/7/14 underosimertinib. It reports independent parallel cultures from the same barcoded founding population and additional drug/cell models. Source is official NCBI BioC full text after two retained EuropePMCfullTextXMLHTTP500failures. GEO GSE150949 and author repository yaaraore/watermelon are exact author-declared links, but downloadable schema, matching barcode/replicate/sort identities and numerical usability remain to be checked.

**No novelty claim yet.** A distribution-tail update may matter more than mean response for this functional endpoint, but rare-state modeling, unbalanced transport/selection and survival classifiers have obvious predecessors. FAO/ROS/NRF2 mechanisms and preferential persister lineage fate are already reported by this original paper; they cannot be claimed as discoveries here. A world must add predictive/experimental utility beyond same-information early-RNA/lineage classifiers, hierarchical survival models and mixtures. Any improvement strategy over that world must have a later common-start assessment beyond simply receiving another clone label.

Potential technical question, still unqualified: does feedback separating net survival/growth from within-state expression change reveal a transferable update structure that a normalized expression model misses? If normalized composition alone admits many transport/selection decompositions, extra absolute counts or actual proliferation reporter is needed for that interpretation; adding a standard mass-balance identity is not a new core. A real source may support a useful world-versus-direct empirical gate even when the simplest operator is conventional. Do not force theoretical novelty or call observational state/fate correlation causal.

Before any deeper candidate is promoted: authenticate released fields/costs, distinguish days and independent culture/model units, check whether fate labels are source data or inferred from future sampling, and identify a real legal feedback/action menu. Selecting a few clone labels is not a physical assay if all labels arrive in one pooled culture. Two parallel cultures are not enough for a broad RSI claim. Conversely, lack of a full autonomous wet-loop does not bar an honestly scoped retrospective state/function pilot. The overall goal remains unchanged and unachieved.
