There are three distinct endpoints:

1. Expression/state distributions within a measured gate: a legitimate gate-conditional molecular target.
2. Predict the gate provenance of a captured day14 cell/lineage under the released stratified sampling design, conditional on detection: a legitimate retrospective design-conditional label if defined honestly.
