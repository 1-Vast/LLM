## Decision

**Do not reopen the historical MIX-seq early-RNA-to-PRISM/STATE task. Do not execute the purported 433-compound DRUG-seq panel from the evidence currently authenticated.** DRUG-seq supports a useful, modest drug-discovery application: cheaply profile dose-dependent transcriptional mechanism and possible off-target effects in U2OS. It is a molecular proxy application; it does not establish compound efficacy. The primary paper documents a 433-compound, eight-dose, triplicate screen, but its linked public GEO accession currently authenticates a substantially smaller, standard bulk RNA-seq experiment. This is a concrete source-release mismatch, not proof that the large screen is globally unavailable or that RNA-only world-model research is illegitimate.

No new technical core has been established by this audit. A measured-catalog static intervention world could legitimately be developed on an authenticated RNA release. Destructive sampling does not disqualify such a world. It does prevent interpreting static wells as individual-cell trajectories or identifying a synthesis/degradation decomposition without extra assumptions or measurements.

## Alternative primary source and what is actually authenticated

Ye et al. (2018), DRUG-seq for miniaturized high-throughput transcriptome profiling in drug discovery. DOI 10.1038/s41467-018-06500-x; PMCID PMC6192987; PMID 30333485. This is an older, original application source, not a claim to cover the latest field. Primary XML and GEO metadata are retained.

The paper's Results and Methods state: 433 compounds, predominantly known targets, eight half-log doses from 3.2 nM to 10 micromolar, triplicates, U2OS, 12 hours. DMSO controls are used. RNA is obtained through destructive well lysis; barcoded cDNA is pooled after reverse transcription, retaining well provenance. Compound annotation is Supplementary Data 3. The paper's separate CRISPR RPL6 demonstration has day-four confluency and RNA but is not a matched functional outcome panel for the 433 compounds. The paper says RNA-seq is deposited at GSE120222 and other data are available on reasonable request.

The actual GSE120222 series record instead describes standard bulk RNA-seq in U2OS: DMSO plus AML108, AMN107, BGW675, JAA804, LEE837 and LHD510, at 100, 1000 and 10000 nM, 12 hours, 72 listed GSMs. It links one RAW tar. The series is public since 2018-09-20, last updated 2019-03-21. I have not fetched the tar or any count/TPM/FPKM values.

The fourth GET authenticates GSM3396186: title AML108_10000nM_1; cell line U2OS; treatment Pyrazolanthrone; 24-well culture; RNeasy extraction; TruSeq Stranded poly-A library; STAR/RSEM/HTSeq processing; and a per-sample processed genes.results.txt.gz URL. This is positive evidence for an accessible small bulk RNA-seq experiment, and maps one coded compound to a chemical name. It does not authenticate all 72 files, all six chemical mappings, a physical replicate structure, or public availability of the 433 by eight DRUG-seq response matrix. The series-level doses differ from one apparent Methods typo ('1 nM'); retain the discrepancy and use exact sample metadata if a future small-subset contract is frozen.

## Concrete conditional application and feedback contract

Application: allocate a fixed experimental RNA profiling budget to characterize the dose-dependent transcriptional mechanism of a new small molecule in U2OS, then predict its unmeasured dose responses and rank mechanistically similar reference compounds. Utility is molecular profiling efficiency and mechanism hypothesis generation. Any disease reversal or toxicity claim would be a separately validated proxy; viability or therapeutic efficacy requires corresponding measured functional outcomes.

World: a static intervention-conditioned distribution of transcriptomic state given chemical identity, dose, time and U2OS control context. A kinetic latent world is optional and must earn its role. The only time authenticated here is 12 hours, so this source cannot validate longitudinal transition accuracy or recovery after washout.

Feedback unit: an experimentally acquired whole-well transcriptome, charged once as a bundle, with its replicate identity and matched control cost. If acquisition purchases a triplicate dose bundle, all three wells are charged together; it may not buy one gene at a time or count a post-pooling measurement as an independent culture. Shared controls must be budgeted identically for every method. A reported replicate suffix is not by itself a certification of biological independence. This does not prohibit a developmental catalog benchmark, provided its exact unit is labeled.

Later task units would be held-out compounds or chemical/target groups, with no splitting of the same compound-dose replicates across source and evaluation tasks. The currently authenticated six-code subset could support a small, explicitly exposed development/prototype analysis after a complete sample manifest is frozen. It cannot be advertised as the 433-compound task population or as convincing later-task C4 evidence. A numerical sample-size or performance gate must be set from the eventual manifest and target effect before response opening; the present audit does not invent a passing threshold after viewing outcomes.

If the complete screen is authenticated later, the minimal first empirical check is a frozen equal-feedback dose-completion comparison between a static direct compound/dose predictor, nearest-neighbor/dose spline or GP, ordinary retraining, ordinary continual learning, active learning and a simple mixture. If the candidate adds a research agent, its searches/failed proposals/runtime count, with an equal-budget program-search control. The held-out application metric should combine calibrated transcriptomic prediction and predeclared mechanism-retrieval quality, rather than transcriptome correlation alone. Strong controls get the same chemical descriptors, controls, history, labels and update opportunity.

World contribution must compare the nominated world-assisted procedure against a matched direct model. Agent contribution must compare adaptive research choices against a frozen procedure under identical feedback costs. Inheritance requires carrying a specified useful artifact to new compound tasks against reset or ordinary-CL alternatives. Joint gain is a separate contrast; superadditivity is not a universal requirement. C4 compares old versus revised improvers on a common solver, common history and fixed later task/budget, preferably with a factorial replay to isolate improved strategy from a better inherited model. The six-code subset is at most a debugging setting for that design. No such contrast was run here.

## Falsifiability and necessity

The candidate application is useful if the same RNA acquisition budget yields materially better held-out dose/mechanism results, with uncertainty over compound-level units, than the strongest same-information controls. A new core would need an explicit biological constraint and update operation or a surprising controlled empirical effect. Generic memory, a task embedding, value regression, mixtures or relabeling ordinary retraining are insufficient novelty. This source alone supplies neither such an operation nor evidence of improved improvement strategy.

Necessary source conditions for the 433-screen claim: a downloadable response matrix plus complete mapping of well/plate, chemical, dose, treatment time and controls; documentation sufficient to count purchased feedback and define later units. Independent raw biological repeats or same-cell trajectories are not universal conditions for a static developmental benchmark. Any physical generalization claim must match the experiment's actual independence.

Stop the specific 433-panel execution while that release mapping is unauthenticated. Stop efficacy claims without efficacy outcomes. Stop kinetic or synthesis/degradation-identifiability claims on this one-timepoint total-RNA release unless additional identified measurements/assumptions are supplied and compared with ordinary kinetic refitting. Stop a new-core claim if a same-information ordinary method exactly reproduces its proposed operation, while leaving the application-value question open. Do not reopen MIX-seq's old early-RNA/STATE-to-PRISM configuration under a new name.

This audit changes the next action from assuming a large available screen to either authenticating its release under a new bounded source contract or choosing a distinct available dataset/application. It is evidence-gathering progress, not achievement of the overall world/agent/RSI goal or biological performance.
