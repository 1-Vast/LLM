## The operation, with its biological assumptions made explicit

For a fixed gene perturbation g, let z in [0,1] denote latent per-cell survival under one unit of effective immune exposure. In a deliberately narrow passive-exposure model, survival at integer exposure e is

    m_g(e) = integral z^e d mu_g(z),   mu_g is a probability measure.

This represents heterogeneity without deciding which molecular mechanism generated it. It assumes cells retain their latent susceptibility through the assay and increasing effective exposure composes multiplicatively. T-cell proliferation, serial killing, cytokine feedback, exhaustion, density effects, growth selection and time-varying target state can violate it. **A 1:1,2:1,4:1 effector ratio is NOT thereby exposure1,2,4.** The mapping is a testable additional hypothesis, not metadata truth. There is no permission to tune a monotone remapping after the model fails.

Let b_g denote the known matched no-immune control-pool cell fractions, summing to1. Under proportional guide reads, correctly matched total counts and shared physical conditions, observed normalized pooled ratios have form

    R_g(e) = m_g(e) / c_e,

with c_e=sum_g b_g m_g(e), the total-pool relative survival, conditional on correct baseline growth matching and guide-count interpretation. Thus sum_g b_g R_g(e)=1. This equality is a model/input check, not a license to normalize arbitrary MAGeCK scores. The proposed update keeps c_e shared across all genes. It does not fit MAGeCK scores as m_g, divide arbitrary separately normalized scores, or silently fix c_e=1. Shared controls and calibration uncertainty must be carried jointly.

**One-anchor operation.** A single matched absolute total-pool survival measurement c1 at exposure1 makes every m_g1=R_g1*c1 available. With only relative observations R_g2 at exposure2, the shared unknown c2 has exactly the feasible interval

    C2 = [max_g m_g1^2/R_g2, min_g m_g1/R_g2].

This follows from Jensen m_g1^2<=m_g2 and z^2<=z, with m_g2=R_g2*c2. For positive finite R_g2 and 0<m_g1<1, any c2 in this intersection is sufficient: each gene admits the two-point distribution on {0,m_g2/m_g1}, with nonzero weight m_g1^2/m_g2. Hence, with independent unrestricted probability measures per gene and no additional observations, this is the **exact gauge-feasibility interval**, not just a necessary inequality. The weighted pooled normalization equality makes all these candidate distributions consistent with the same c2. Degenerate m1=0 or1 and exact zero counts require separately frozen handling; the prototype only tests the positive interior. Uncertainty in c1 or relative counts gives a wider joint feasible region, not the same plug-in formula.

Given anchored m1 and m2, the operation computes the sharp fourth-moment interval [L,U] directly:

    L = m2^3/m1^2,
    a = (m1-m2)/(1-m1),
    U = (1+2a+3a^2)m2 - 2a(1+a)^2 m1 + a^2(1+2a).

Use the continuous endpoint limits at m1=0 or1. The lower distribution is supported on {0,m2/m1}; the upper distribution on {a,1}. The implemented conservative lower certificate for m_i4-m_j4 is

    B_ij = L(m_i1,R_i2*min C2) - U(m_j1,R_j2*max C2).

Its sign is **survival of i minus survival of j**; B_ij>0 means j is the more sensitizing target in the narrow functional model. L increases with m2; a fixed upper-dual polynomial constructed at max C2 bounds every lower feasible m2 because its m2 coefficient is positive. Consequently the bound is valid uniformly over the shared c2, although using opposite endpoints can be conservative. Tightening to the sharp joint difference leaves only a one-variable cubic optimization over C2; that sharpening is specified but not implemented or claimed verified here. For finite uncertainty, retain the joint region rather than using independent intervals without a proof. New feedback clips the feasible set; a newly observed violation triggers model refusal instead of forcing a susceptibility estimate.

Computationally, the implemented operation is O(G) to form C2, O(1) per nominated pair, and O(G^2) for all pair certificates; it stores O(G) values and has no fitted support grid or latent-mixture optimizer. A naive H-atom joint mixture optimization has O(GH) weights plus shared gauges. An optimized ordinary generalized-moment solver can itself eliminate the measures with these same classical extrema and solve the remaining scalar problem. Thus the meaningful comparison is to that optimized conventional reduction as well as practical likelihood/bootstrap pipelines, not only to an unnecessarily expensive H-grid solver. No empirical speed benchmark or lower bound is claimed. A copied implementation of the same analytic reduction is expected to tie.

Decision: nominate a target for lower tumor survival only if its worst feasible nomination regret clears a separately frozen criterion; otherwise carry uncertainty into the next **legal whole-panel** acquisition. With an authenticated menu, an agent could compare the marginal reduction of worst-case nomination regret from a reference anchor versus another immune-intensity panel. This is an estimand-specific acquisition operation, but standard minimax design is a required control. The present three-ratio source does not yet supply such an authenticated adaptive menu.

## Exactly one substantive revision, not a silent rescue
