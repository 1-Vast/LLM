The candidate's pair certificate is conservative, not sharp for joint c2. The current opposite-endpoint certificate remains valid, and this conservatism should not be called a novel stronger robust estimator. Thus its exactness is conditional on prevalidated legal inputs, as stated in the text, and it should not be exposed as an unconditional function accepting arbitrary pooled scores. Different sequencing depths, guide depletion/dropout, outgrowth, transformed MAGeCK scores or intervention-dependent guide capture cannot be repaired by this mathematical reduction. One global count is one physical bundle including matched control and replicates, not a single free scalar.
