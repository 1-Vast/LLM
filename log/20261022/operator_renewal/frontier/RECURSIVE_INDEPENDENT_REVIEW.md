# Independent opposing review of recursive operator

2026-10-22. Read only recursive ORIGINAL.md, CHECK_RESULT.json and trace_credit_check.py; no current peer critique or cross-exchange was read. My own original and postcheck were already sealed. This review concerns the literal tested estimator, not general impossibility of trace-based improved-improver learning.

The original correctly identifies the bias of replacing full-trace PAES credit by R E[s|Z], and its randomized difference correction is unbiased when the audit is selected with logged positive probability conditional on the pre-audit information. The code's exact rational enumeration and direct derivative are coherent. The current repair is pointwise equal to an ordinary randomized two-level control variate, as the author explicitly tests. This equivalence does not eliminate potential useful runtime/variance tradeoffs, but they have not been demonstrated against a matched optimized implementation.

The strongest concrete weakness is information sufficiency in the supplied toy. R=1+w/2 reveals w exactly. Consequently (Z,R) is equivalent to full H=(Z,w), so E[s|Z,R] equals E[s|H]. Conditioning the posterior on the already observed terminal functional reward removes the proposed partial-trace bias without any randomized expensive trace audit. The strongest comparison is therefore the reward-inclusive cheap posterior, not Z alone. Root has indicated that the author is adding this control; I have not read that result or any review of it.

Biological relevance is a hypothesis: destructive molecular assays genuinely can omit function, but a full action/measurement trace need not require a biological world likelihood to recover the policy posterior when environmental factors cancel. The original explicitly acknowledges cancellation. A future practical estimator would have to identify which costly likelihood/inference term survives that cancellation, and show that already available reward/context does not make it cheap. This request is about finite work, not latent identifiability.

The common-fresh-start expectation after one update uses the same constructed reward law and known derivative. It does not test learned audit allocation, transfer across contexts, changed proposal quality, or the ability of a new improver to find better later improvements. All unbiased methods have the same expected one-step value here; no independent evidence favors inheritance of the audit policy. This is correctly bounded in CHECK_RESULT.json.

Question to author: once the cheap posterior conditions on all already observed information (Z,R), what expensive posterior work remains in the toy and in the intended assay setting? Specify an actual information/computation gap before treating randomized audits as a new useful operator. If the toy collapses, retain its bias diagnostic and stop the literal novelty claim without another rescue.

Decision: the estimator identities are VERIFIED in the exact synthetic law; practical computational advantage, biological usefulness, world necessity and Level3 improvement are NOT_ESTABLISHED. NO_PROMOTION of the literal repair is warranted unless a concrete cost advantage against the reward-inclusive and standard two-level controls is established. This leaves PAES-style credit and biological trace design as valid research directions.
