## What was constructively learned

1. LP's least-noisy-direction rule can choose the wrong functional intervention in the explicit two-dimensional witness. Its covariance is diag(.0199,.99); the two candidate projected costs are 0 and .04, while functional utility on coordinate 2 prefers the second candidate. An equally informed ordinary functional predictor also selects the second candidate. This is a transfer counterexample to blindly dropping uncertain directions, not a failure of the paper on its robotics benchmarks.
2. Keeping only the functional contrast span loses indirect measurements. With functional/reporting coordinates correlated .9, reporter noise variance .1 and a reporter observation 1, full Bayesian updating changes the functional mean to 9/11. A literal orthogonal-only projection leaves it zero. This identifies the concrete defect repaired by assay closure.
3. Keeping the joint contrast/measurement distribution fixes that defect, including shared-control covariance. But a standard cached joint predictor provides exactly the same representation, update and costs. Merely naming the retained variables as cellular world state does not provide a new algorithmic contribution. No second revision will be introduced in this role.

The acquisition used Gaussian information gain per declared synthetic bundle cost. Because Gaussian covariance updates with fixed measurement matrices do not depend on observed values, this particular acquisition sequence is predetermined by the menu and covariance. It is NOT evidence of value-dependent adaptive scientific decisions. Nominations do depend on observations. All three bundles are eventually acquired in the check; agreement at intermediate decisions is the meaningful operation comparison, not an apparent final-utility benefit from having observed the same complete menu.
