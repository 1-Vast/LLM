## Mathematical assessment

For positive interior m1, R2, the intersection max(m1^2/R2)<=c2<=min(m1/R2) is both necessary and sufficient for unrestricted per-gene probability measures on [0,1]. The supplied two-atom distribution has nonzero atom m2/m1 and weight m1^2/m2, so it simultaneously attains the requisite first and second moments. Weighted normalization ensures the whole pool remains consistent. This is an actual analytic elimination of nuisance measures, not an appeal to expressivity.

The fourth-moment bounds and attaining distributions are consistent. A useful simplification of the upper bound is

    U(m1,m2) = m2 - (m1-m2)^2/(1-m1) - (m1-m2)^3/(1-m1)^2.

Its derivative with respect to m2 is 1+2d/(1-m1)+3d^2/(1-m1)^2>0 for d=m1-m2>=0. Thus taking lower i at min C2 and upper j at max C2 is a valid conservative contrast certificate. It is not sharp because those endpoints need not be simultaneously realized under the shared gauge. The author states this correctly. The fixture's one-anchor rank change is a legitimate designed information witness, not invalidated by the fact that equally informed methods can recover it.

## Strongest simplicity/novelty challenge

The conventional generalized-moment check in the code verifies only one per-gene interval at fixed m1,m2. It does not separately implement the full pooled one-anchor normalization/rank pipeline. Hence it cannot alone settle equivalence of the complete operation or prove that previous literature already contains the complete reduction.

Conversely, an optimized ordinary solver for the SAME declared model eliminates each measure using these classical inequalities, intersects the same scalar interval, and minimizes L_i(R_i2*c)-U_j(R_j2*c) on that interval. The objective is cubic in c; candidates are endpoints and at most two real derivative roots. It therefore has O(G) preprocessing and O(1) per pair, matching the proposed dimensional and asymptotic cost while returning at least as sharp a contrast. This is a concrete full-pipeline competitor, not an expensive arbitrary support grid and not a universal predictor argument. A contribution might still be the new complete analytic reduction/feedback contract itself, but a claim to beat an optimized ordinary operation requires actual finite cost or statistical evidence, or prior-art work showing that this full reduction is absent. No such conclusion follows from the current eight check labels.

Question to author: how does the complete operation differ at finite budget from this scalar-cubic optimized generalized-moment pipeline on the same anchor, counts and uncertainty? If there is no implementation advantage, is the remaining proposed contribution explicitly the new pooled-normalization information theorem/application rather than a superior estimator? Please make that claim boundary exact and preserve the positive one-anchor witness without adding another mechanism.

## Biological and agent limits

The old CD58 finding establishes molecular-surrogate failure, but does not establish passive multiplicative exposure or a fixed susceptibility distribution. Effector ratio, time and cumulative exposure are distinct. Active immune proliferation, serial killing and evolving contact/state can violate the model. The source must supply an authenticated mapping and matched absolute total-pool survival before a source-only falsifier; a single-gene validation or separately normalized MAGeCK contrast is inadequate.

Near-unity survival in the third synthetic gene makes the global gauge tight. That is a transparent designed favorable condition, not an observed distributional fact. With noisy counts and anchor uncertainty, the scalar feasible interval may expand or become empty; finite uncertainty must remain joint. Reporting plug-in certificates as calibrated would be invalid. The original acknowledges these limits and prohibits rescue remapping.

The actual legal action is a whole exposure panel/reference measurement. The source presently supplies no qualified adaptive menu or independent later patient tasks. Thus world prediction, agent acquisition, transfer and improved-improver claims remain untested. The positive information witness justifies retaining a conditional estimator lemma; it does not justify opening outcomes or inventing missing assays. No protected data were read in this review.
