# Experiment record, 2026-09-26

> - **Path**: `log/20260926/README.md`
> - **Purpose**: Record two pre-registered blocks and what they changed. Block 1 tested whether MAESTRO's virtual cell and agent carry perturbation-specific biology (with the SciPlex3 label defect it uncovered, the components promoted to `src/` and `tools/`, and the merge of the two data directories). Block 2 tested whether a virtual cell helps the agent choose the measurement that separates two competing mechanisms, including "the perturbation failed" against "the mechanism is wrong", using the SciPlex3 24 h and 72 h cohorts.
> - **Core points**: Block 1: SciPlex3 contains the biology (7 of 10 literature anchors hold), but no virtual-cell arm reproduces more than 3 of the 7 for unseen compounds, the JEPA latent upgrade adds nothing over PCA or retrieval and is not promoted, and retrieval over measured profiles became a tool. Block 2: no world-model planner beat the current magnitude tie-break (separation cards -0.044 correct decisions [-0.083, -0.004]; time-aware cards +0.054, interval including zero), a learned 24 h to 72 h transition beat persistence on the profile (+0.283 cosine) without improving any decision, multi-branch outcome predictions beat a single class mean (log loss -0.599), and cards did not help the language-model planner (+0.006). Nothing from block 2's world model is promoted; a transport defect in both provider clients was fixed with tests. The decisive variable was time: slow-acting mechanisms become separable only at 72 h, which the static rule never chooses.

## 2. Research questions and hypotheses

### Block 1: biological depth

1. Does the virtual cell predict the **compound-specific** part of a 24 h transcriptional
   response for compounds it never saw, or only the response most drugs share?
2. Does it reproduce literature pharmacology (ten pre-registered anchors) out of fold?
3. Does V-JEPA-style latent representation learning (`task.md` section 13) improve on PCA,
   masked reconstruction and structure retrieval? Registered as P1 (JEPA minus retrieval), P2
   (JEPA minus PCA) and P3 (anchors, JEPA versus the repository's current learned rung).
4. Can the agent's reasoning components (DeepSeek, Jev) read mechanism from a measured signature,
   and does a retrieval evidence card help them?

### Block 2: measurement choice for mechanism discrimination

For a held-out compound and two candidate mechanism classes (its own and another), with a budget
of two measurements chosen one at a time from a registered menu of line x dose (x time) conditions:

1. P1: do scenario cards built from measured reference compounds choose measurements that lead
   to more correct eliminations than the current virtual-cell tie-break on predicted magnitude?
2. P2: in A549 with 24 h and 72 h on the menu, does a time-aware card policy beat magnitude and a
   fixed time course?
3. P3: does a learned population transition (24 h to 72 h, and dose and context transfer) predict
   the next profile better than persistence, and does it improve planning (dyn_model)?
4. P4: do the cards help a language-model planner under identical evidence and budget?
5. P5: are validator outcomes better predicted by multi-branch references than by a single class
   mean, that is, do the data support heterogeneous outcomes within a mechanism?

## 4. Experimental design and controls

### Block 1: biological depth

Five-fold cross-validation grouped by structure skeleton and stratified by pathway; every
dose and line of a held-out compound is predicted by a model that never saw its cells (held-out
responses are set to NaN in the training code, which is how one leakage bug announced itself).
Arms: `zero`, `systematic` (mean training response at the same line and dose), `ridge_chem`,
`knn_chem` (Tanimoto-weighted retrieval), `mlp_existing` (the repository's learned rung, trained
as `evaluation/model_validation.py` does), and three latent world models sharing one
pharmacological transition head (two dose-gated directions, exactly zero at vehicle dose):
`latent_pca`, `latent_mae` (masked reconstruction) and `latent_jepa` (latent prediction across
different-cell views with co-expression-module masks), plus `latent_jepa_moa` (declared pathway
annotation as input) and its shuffled-annotation control. Three seeds per neural arm.

Metrics: B1 compound-specific correlation (after subtracting the systematic response), B2
direction-scramble control, B3 mechanism-class retrieval, B4 potency ranking, B5 context
specificity, B6 ten literature anchors scored identically on observed and predicted shifts, B7
uncertainty usefulness, B8 PRISM viability coherence. Agent probe: 40 anonymised measured
signatures, 17 classes, arms with and without a retrieval card.

### Block 2: measurement choice for mechanism discrimination

- **Validator** (final authority on belief; frozen): quality (both replicate wells, at least 20
  cells, matched vehicle); detection by rep1-rep2 agreement above the larger of 0.10 and the 0.99
  quantile of a vehicle-well null (thresholds: A549 24 h 0.157, K562 0.107, MCF7 0.187, A549 72 h
  0.297); **an undetected response eliminates nothing** and is retained at
  intervention-implementation scope; otherwise projected cosine against the detected training
  compounds of each class at the same condition, with floor and margin calibrated per fold by
  inner leave-one-out at a wrong-elimination rate of at most 0.05. Every executed measurement
  passes through the repository's `InterpretationTable` and `EvidenceState`.
- **Episodes**: tier B, 3 lines x 4 doses at 24 h, 17 pool classes, 2160 contrasts over 135
  compounds; tier A, A549 x 4 doses x {24 h, 72 h}, 9 pool classes, 336 contrasts over 42
  compounds. Five skeleton-grouped folds from block 1. Correct elimination +1, none 0, wrong -2.
  Cost: 2 treated wells per measurement, 4 vehicle wells per new line and time, 1 or 3 days of
  exposure plus 5 readout days.
- **Policies**: random (20 seeds), fixed (highest dose first; tier A adds 72 h), `cost_only` (the
  repository selector with no world model), `magnitude` (the current tie-break), `separation`
  (cards through `maestro.acquisition.select_expected_coverage`), `separation_permuted` (class
  labels permuted: the non-biological control), `dyn_ref`, `dyn_model` and the oracle. Provider
  arms on one seeded contrast per compound: DeepSeek without cards, with cards and with permuted
  cards; Jev with and without cards, eight repeats per state, a stable answer executed and an
  unstable one replaced by the separation choice.
- **Transition arms**: zero, persistence, scaled persistence, ridge on a training-only 20-component
  PCA latent, gene-space ridge, a one-hidden-layer MLP and measured-profile retrieval, for time,
  dose and context transfer on held-out compounds.

## 6. Deviations, failures and corrections

### Block 1: biological depth

- **SciPlex3 labels offset by one row (12:42-12:50, after the freeze, before any outcome).** The
  feature table's first entry is a stray header, so every published gene label names the
  neighbouring column. Found when a dry-run signature listed olfactory receptors and
  pseudogenes; confirmed by cell-line markers (12 of 12 at offset +1, 5 of 11 as published). The
  first preparation and a fold-0 timing run are invalid and kept as `INVALID_label_offset_*`. The
  protocol's gene rule is unchanged; `src/evaluation/model_validation.py` read the same labels
  and is corrected (its predictive metrics never depended on them).
- The first preparation stopped by name on a salt spelling; fold 0 stopped once on the NaN
  leakage guard (fixed to project training rows only) and once on a dtype.
- Encoder views average 1, 2 or 4 disjoint chunks so pseudobulk inputs stay in the depth range
  the encoder was trained on; decided before any outcome existed.
- **B1 was weaker than intended.** The zero arm scores 0.130 because most compounds respond less
  than the average drug, so "specific_signal" passes for every arm including one that predicts
  nothing. Post hoc, a direction metric with the systematic axis projected out (zero arm 0.000)
  is reported beside it; the registered verdicts are left as registered.
- **The vehicle null is too permissive.** It called 69% of 10 nM conditions responsive while
  their median replicate agreement is 0.04; A10 (no-target compounds) failed on noise-level
  exceedances with replicate agreement 0.02 to 0.07. A reproducible-condition view (replicate r
  above 0.3; 559 conditions, 122 compounds) is reported post hoc.
- **Anchor names.** SciPlex3 spells eleven compounds with trailing whitespace, so the first audit
  skipped aminoglutethimide (A10) and prednisone (exploratory). Identity is now folded and an
  unmatched anchor compound stops the audit by name; the corrected audit is the one reported,
  and its conclusions did not change.
- The registered B7 correlates spread with error magnitude and passes trivially; a scale-free
  version is reported beside it. The serving informativeness gate (width at most 0.95 of the
  average-response arm's) was added after the widths were seen; it only withholds readouts.

## 7. Interpretation and claim boundaries

### Block 1: biological depth

**Verdict: the virtual cell does not demonstrate sufficient biological depth.** Its
predictions for unseen compounds recover response magnitude and, for classes with strong,
stereotyped programs (HSP90, HDAC, Aurora kinase; direction up to 0.47 for HSP90), some
direction; for DNA-damage agents, PI3K/mTOR, CDK and most RTK or JAK inhibitors they carry almost
none. The JEPA latent world model is not promoted: it neither beats PCA nor retrieval, and on
the direction metric it is worse than retrieval by 0.063 [0.046, 0.082]. The repository's
current learned rung is likewise below retrieval (0.050 [0.034, 0.067]).

Where depth exists it sits in **measurements, not in the models**: the screen itself carries
seven of ten literature anchors, and retrieval over measured profiles names mechanism classes
at 0.500 while neither language model can (0.100 and 0.075). The agent's value in that task is
to follow grounded evidence; its unaided reading over-attributes estrogen-program changes to
nuclear-receptor drugs. Two genuine positives are narrow: declared pathway annotations transfer
biology to unseen compounds, and structural novelty tells the model when not to trust itself.

No result here concerns engagement, viability, clinical relevance or any context beyond three
lines at 24 h, and a class match is agreement with a vendor annotation, not a mechanism.

### Block 2: measurement choice for mechanism discrimination

**Verdict: no virtual-cell component improved measurement choice over the current tie-break, and
none is promoted.** The cards, the time-aware policy, the learned transition and the cards given to
a language model each failed their registered rule. The learned transition predicts the next
profile better than persistence, and multi-branch cards predict outcomes better than a class mean,
but neither advantage reached a decision. The component that protects belief is the validator: it
keeps wrong eliminations near 1-2% per measurement where an ungated reading reaches 26%, and it
refuses to read an absent response as a failed perturbation, which would be wrong on 29% of
measurements.

What decided the episodes was **time**. Several mechanisms (DNA methyltransferase, BET, Aurora
inhibition) become separable in A549 only at 72 h, and the current rule, whose virtual cell refuses
a 72 h query, never chooses one. A fixed time course and a language model's background knowledge
both exploited this, but it was found after seeing the results and is a hypothesis until a
pre-registered test on independent data confirms it.

Boundaries: population states matched by condition, not cells; two time points in one line;
context transfer was tested for unseen compounds in lines the models had seen, never for an unseen
line; a class is a vendor annotation, not a verified mechanism; episodes replay released measurements
rather than new experiments; the language-model results are for one provider, one prompt and 177
contrasts.
