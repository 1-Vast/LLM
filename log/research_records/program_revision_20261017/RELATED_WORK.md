# Related work and novelty-to-baseline map

I read the method sections, and code where it was released, on 2026-10-17. Publication status is as listed by the venue or arXiv on that date. The rule throughout: a capability that already exists in one of these works is a **baseline or a component** here, never a contribution.

| Work | Status checked | What it already does | What this study does with it |
|---|---|---|---|
| NEO, *Learning to Theorize the World from Observation* ([PMLR 306:5064-5106](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/baek26b.html)) | ICML 2026 | Discrete latent primitives (VQ) executed by a shared executor; MDL-style program-length prior; amortised inference; an explicit transferability test of the learned primitives. Synthetic worlds only. | Shared executable abstractions and program-length control are not claimed. Our library is a capacity-bounded set of typed programs, with no identifiers, on top of a strong model. |
| MIST-WM, *Learning Task-Sufficient World Models…* ([PMLR 306:30616-30641](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/feng26aa.html)) | ICML 2026 | Masked minimal task-sufficient state; active probing skills that reveal the state; curriculum. Robotics. | "Measure what identifies the state" is the AFA/probe baseline, not a contribution. Synthetic check S1 is an instance of its premise. |
| L2M, *Learning-to-Measure* ([PMLR 306:59720-59752](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/kobayashi26a.html); [arXiv 2510.12624](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.12624)) | ICML 2026 | A meta-learned transformer for acquisition; greedy conditional-mutual-information surrogate plus a k-step lookahead variant; identification under MAR and positivity. Tabular clinical data (Metabric, MiniBooNE, MIMIC-IV). | Greedy decision-weighted acquisition is our conventional AFA comparator (Stage B and C). We do not claim adaptive acquisition. |
| TAFA, information templates ([arXiv 2508.18380](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.18380) v2, 4 Feb 2026) | preprint | A template library built by a submodular, mutation-guided greedy search; the policy picks a template by loss plus remaining cost. Per dataset. | Fixed templates map to our fixed A-optimal design. Joint arms change the template (swap a probe) only when the change earns value on held-out decisions. |
| Outgrow, *Learning to Outgrow a Theory* ([arXiv 2610.07627](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07627) v1, 6 Oct 2026) | preprint (ICLR 2027 template; acceptance unverifiable) | Joint proposal of a theory edit and an experiment; class-level distinguishability; anytime-valid e-value revision; detection of library insufficiency; typed edit grammar; imitation learning plus RL. ODE and physics domains. | The **closest prior work**. Typed edits, joint edit–experiment proposal and distinguishing experiments are all theirs. We do not claim them. What is tested here is whether this pattern earns decision value in drug-response data against strong static, conventional-search and acquisition baselines at equal wells. Our acceptance rule is a fixed LLR + decision-gain rule, not anytime-valid. |
| OPINE-World ([arXiv 2607.01531](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01531) v3, 26 Sep 2026) | under review, ICLR 2027 | A natural-language + Bayesian + Python programmatic world model; counterexample-guided (CEGIS) revision. | Cycle-of-Science ([PMLR 306:161035-161053](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/zhang26jk.html)) | ICML 2026 | LLM hypothesis → counterfactual intervention → effect score → preference optimisation of the LLM. | An LLM proposing hypotheses tested by interventions is not new. | AFA4CATE ([PMLR 258:4330-4338](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v258/piskorz25a.html); [code](https://github.com/j-piskorz/afa4cate)) | AISTATS 2025 | Acquisition metrics specific to treatment decisions (CATE), via greedy Bayesian experimental design. Synthetic. | Decision-weighted acquisition (weights from the hit-probability gradient) is in our AFA comparator. |

Model class: a conditional low-rank model plus gated additive corrections is a conditional low-rank / mixture-of-experts model with observable gates. Varying-coefficient models and interaction screening in pharmacogenomics, such as drug × mutation ANOVA in GDSC, are standard. The program language adds no expressiveness claim.

## What remains testable as a contribution (no capability claim)

The test is empirical, and could fail. In a real pharmacogenomic screen, with a strong inductive model, measurement, and equal-budget conventional baselines in place, does autonomous revision of a shared program library (with or without an LLM proposer) change future decisions for the better? If it does, does it repay the wells that building it cost? A null result is a result.
