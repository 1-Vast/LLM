# Anchor purchase: qualification and bounded development diagnostic

Frozen before this study opens replicate responses. Historical PRISM outcomes
were already exposed in MAESTRO; resplitting never creates fresh confirmation.

## Questions and stopping gate

Q1 asks whether a purchased anchor increases independently measured terminal hits
after its opportunity cost. Q2 asks whether selective purchasing beats the best
development-selected fixed workflow. The independent-endpoint gate **fails**:
the cached PRISM replicates share treatment-level ComBat and controls; independent
culture starts cannot be authenticated. MTS010 is explicitly a technical redo.
Authentic pools can be enumerated, but all-attempt/control/batch costs cannot be
reconstructed. Consequently the primary status is BLOCKED_INDEPENDENT_ENDPOINT;
VALUE is unestablished and POLICY is not evaluated. No conditional response,
learned purchase policy, second acquisition round or pathway model will be fit.

The executable work below qualifies evaluator signal and the opportunity bound,
not the mission's independent-endpoint claim. It is justified by the changed
assumption of charging information against terminal confirmation budget. Previous
studies do not authenticate these pooled acquisition units and independent ends.

## Population, campaign and endpoint

One campaign is one held-out compound from the existing five-fold P0 split.
Select only HTS002 experimental treatments at exactly 2.5 micromolar,
with X1/X2/X3 columns authenticated in treatment metadata. For each Broad core,
choose the lexicographically first (full broad_id, compound_plate, well) having
all three replicates. Record exclusions; do not use response availability.
HTS002 column IDs do not encode duration; per-treatment duration is unqualified.
The 120H encoding in MTS010 must not be silently assigned to HTS002.
The target population is historically selected secondary-screen compounds,
restricted to the prior split census, not all 4,528 primary-screen compounds.
Known cell contexts require a DepMap ID and passing STR metadata. Exclude A375,
A549, MCF7 and HT29 from every terminal menu before reading responses.

X1 is an observed technical reference profile. X2/X3 jointly define the
development terminal criterion: both finite, mean log2 viability <= -1 and each
< 0. This is a reproducible technical criterion, not independent biological
confirmation or a selectivity claim. Any absent X2 or X3 is unknown, never
inactive. Missing nominations remain in the full campaign denominator and consume
their slots; report [known hits, known hits + unknowns]. No outcome-based menu.

## Frozen methods and information boundary

Three conventional candidates: training-only context means, Morgan-radius-2
2048-bit chemical neighbours, and the unweighted average of chemical and
target/MoA-token neighbours. Reuse the existing RKT neighbour parameters (chem:
k=10, power=2, floor=.3; knowledge: k=10, power=1, floor=0), with no transferred
certificate. Donors are only the eligible secondary population. No pathway
features or old prediction arrays are read. Chemistry and token dictionaries are
metadata-only; no response-dependent scaling. Fit secondary X2/X3 means only
on training compounds. A context with no training outcomes receives 0 (neutral,
fixed fallback). All ranking ties follow sorted cell IDs.

For outer fold f, use fold (f+1)%5 as inner validation and remaining three folds
as inner training. Select the candidate with most known inner-validation top-4
hits (tie order above); refit using all four outer training folds. Existing
connectivity/family components remain disjoint. This selects the comparator
without outer query outcomes; it does not qualify scaffold generalisation.

Nominate contexts from score matrices with no terminal-outcome argument, save
and hash all choices before releasing their outcomes through OutcomeSeal. The
raw development matrix is loaded centrally for CV training/evaluation, so this
is logical out-of-fold isolation, not a fresh interactive laboratory seal.
Save fit provenance, scores, locked action traces, replicate records and
per-campaign counts. Independently reconstruct utility from traces and records.

## Diagnostic controls and headroom

Compare the strong selected baseline with X1 full-profile ranking (unpriced
technical positive reference, **not** a purchasable small anchor or mathematical
ceiling). Shuffle whole X1 profiles within (outer fold, compound plate), retaining
context and X1 detection-plate strata. Seed 20261016; one permutation, fixed
points retained and reported. Exchangeability is not guaranteed; no causal
randomisation-test interpretation. This single identity-shuffle family is the
negative control. Its scalar A375 value also supplies the shuffled offset check.

The other attribution control is a common additive A375-X1 offset to baseline
scores, with missing anchor mapped to zero update but still charged. The ranking
must be invariant. It is an algebraic control, not a conditional transfer model.
No selected outcome may be removed because a reference score is missing; rank
missing X1 last, with the same fixed cell-ID ties.

Calculate outcome-informed best-known-hit opportunity bounds at 4, 3 and 2
confirmations per campaign, plus missing-result upper bounds. Report how often
known ranking improvement is possible, repeat stability, and the largest integer
anchor-cost threshold permitting the oracle to beat the no-anchor total. This
oracle sees the terminal profile and is neither deployable nor an assertion that
a scalar anchor can recover it. No search across anchor models follows it.

## Cost and statistics

Hypothetical single-line deployment B only: budget=4 confirmation units per
compound, confirmation=1 unit (assumed inclusive of two readouts and controls).
Scalar anchor ratios=.25, 1, 2 units; confirmations=floor(4-anchor cost), with
unspent fractions reported. Failures/unknowns consume the allotted confirmation
unit. Zero turnaround cost assumes no binding deadline. These prices have no
authenticated laboratory basis. Historical replay A is **not executed**; pool
members are recorded in full and cannot be priced as independent matrix cells.
No paid calls or purchased assays. Operational ceiling: 300 MB retrieval and
7,200 CPU seconds, CPU only. Use cached measurements; no broad data collection.

The minimum useful mission gain is +0.10 independently confirmed hits per
compound at budget 4 (one extra hit per ten campaigns, chosen as a modest
screening throughput improvement). It cannot be tested under the failed gate.
Report paired family/connectivity-component bootstrap intervals, 2,000 resamples,
seed 20261016, over all campaigns including zero yields. Two mission comparisons
would require familywise 95% inference (97.5% individual intervals); neither is
executed. Diagnostic intervals are nominal 95%, descriptive, not promotion tests.
Report achieved interval width rather than claiming prospective power.
Assess context-prior probability Brier/ECE separately from raw decision utility;
other model scores are LFC, not calibrated hit probabilities.
