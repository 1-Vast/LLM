# Innovation validation: do feedback-created procedures improve later decisions beyond strong conventional methods?

Package `research/innovation_validation_20261020/` (2026-10-20). Predecessors (`functional_rule_revision_20261020`, `self_improving_virtual_cell_20261020`) were read-only and are byte-identical before and after (`INTEGRITY_BEFORE.json` = `INTEGRITY_AFTER.json`); their vault state and sealed outcome files were not opened. Nothing was committed, pushed or published.

The special-mechanism claim is withdrawn for this world family: a latent-factor model with A-optimal acquisition explains the effect. What survives is narrower and true: a gate program with decision-value acquisition is a **competent** method (it recovers the planted gates with 96–99 % precision, deploys nothing in the null streams, and beats GBM, broad-capacity, direct measurement and the no-probe prior by 0.015–0.035), but it is **not better than the strongest ordinary comparator**, and its small library gain (+0.0044 over deleting the procedure) is neither inheritance-specific nor witness-specific.

## Answers A–H

**A. Was the mechanism implemented?** Yes (`iv_core.py`, `iv_gates.py`, `iv_search.py`): competing numerical programs (null, unexplained, continuous latent, gate program) each with legal probes, a state posterior, per-pair predictions, an observable applicability condition and evidence dependencies. Before every probe the run stores the competing programs and weights, their predictions for the same probe, the nominations that could change, the knowledge-gradient score and the best alternatives; after every measurement support and predictions update before the next probe; an unresolved mismatch (`U` weight > 0.5) is allowed; useless probes are replaced by direct measurement or stop. `WORKED_EXAMPLE.json` (executable, synthetic): two competing explanations, the same probe, outcome "raised" gives A 0.997 and nominates pair07, outcome "unchanged" gives B 0.991 and nominates pair09; a probe outside both gives `U` = 1.0 (mismatch flagged); the variance-only rule probes the noisy decoy and leaves the posterior unchanged.

**B. Did it beat the strongest conventional comparator?** No. Strongest comparator (chosen on dev streams before freezing): latent factors + A-optimal acquisition (`lat_aopt`). Primary H1 +0.0017 (−0.0020, +0.0056) pooled; alias +0.0028 (−0.0000, +0.0054), compose +0.0006 (−0.0062, +0.0079). Controls: lowrank −0.0045 (−0.0063, −0.0027) (the latent learner wins where it should), null +0.0025 (all arms ≈ prior; no false deployment). Transfer contract T: +0.0038 (95 % +0.0004, +0.0074), also below margin. Fixed observations FO (identical probes and history): +0.0014 (−0.0018, +0.0047). Same library, other acquisition rules: KG beats exploit by +0.0054 (+0.0023, +0.0087), below margin. Paired headroom (oracle − method, same cells): prior 0.0572, direct 0.0551, `inh_kg` 0.0225, `lat_aopt` 0.0242, GBM 0.0376.

**C. Did inheritance add value under identical history and resources?** No. H2 −0.0000 (−0.0008, +0.0006); T +0.0015 (−0.0003, +0.0034); FO +0.0002; A_new checkpoint 4 −0.00004. The restart arm keeps all raw data, has an empty library, the same validation rule, and a candidate envelope of 12 × generation (an upper bound that it used: 10 222 evaluations vs 3 004 for the inherited arm; 40 724 vs 11 972 validation replays). Because discovery is deterministic given data, restart simply rediscovers the same gates. Inheritance saved compute (CPU 15 323 s vs 35 284 s in the arm columns) and nothing else.

**D. Did a learned procedure transfer or compose, and did deleting it remove the advantage?** Partly, at sub-margin size. Deleting the deployed procedure costs 0.0044 (0.0021, 0.0068); deleting only older-generation gates costs 0.0001 (late gates are the only ones that matter, or nothing composes beyond what refit finds). Transplanting the deployed gate program into the fixed broad-capacity baseline raises it by +0.0111 (0.0064, 0.0160) (portable), but the transplanted baseline (0.8793) is still below `lat_aopt` (0.8864). In compose streams gate recall is only 0.58 (conjunction and late gates are hard to recover), against 0.92 in alias. No gain exceeds the margin, so no transfer or composition claim is supported.

**E. Did the effect survive shared-anchor/nuisance controls and old-task retention?** The nuisance (drug-cell effect shared across pairs) is in every stream; the null streams show no deployment and no gain. The witness-to-target break (decoupled streams, same anchors, nuisance and noise) does **not** remove the system's advantage: H1 gain is +0.0049 on decoupled vs +0.0017 on normal streams (difference −0.0032, CI −0.0095, +0.0033); the KG-vs-exploit advantage is +0.0101 decoupled vs +0.0054 normal (difference −0.0047, CI −0.0096, −0.0003), and deletion costs 0.0057 vs 0.0044. Whatever the system gains is therefore not specific to cross-class witness structure. Retention: library value on A_old does not fall between checkpoints 2 and 4 (+0.0010).

**F. Synthetic, exposed development or independent?** All inferential evidence is **synthetic** (planted generators written for this study; 96 normal + 48 decoupled evaluation streams, plus 16 dev streams used only for timing and comparator choice). The real-data replay is **exposed development** (18 backgrounds the predecessors already analysed, one world, 9 tasks, no inference). There is **no independent measured evidence**.

**G. What is methodologically new versus established?** Established: Bayesian experimental design with knowledge-gradient/expected-improvement acquisition, mixtures of structural hypotheses, latent-factor/matrix-completion active learning, A-optimal design, BALD-style information gain, batch Bayesian active learning for combination screens (BATCHIE: public summaries state an adaptive screen of 206 drugs exploring ~4 % of ~1.4 M experiments; only the web summary was read, not the paper or data), and program/library learning with validation. Not new here: the home-built acquisition rules are not BATCHIE and are not called that. New at most: the combination of reusable *gate procedures* (scope, effect profile, probe plan, state transform) carried across backgrounds with strict, equal validation/retirement against a restart control and deletion/transplant/decoupled controls. Effective performance (competent, not superior) and methodological novelty (modest, unproven to matter) are separate conclusions; I did not run a systematic literature search, so novelty relative to the literature is **not established**.

**H. Continue, change or stop?** **Stop** the claim that this gate/inheritance mechanism improves decisions beyond ordinary methods in this family. **Change** only if a discriminating world exists: structure that a rank-≤4 latent model provably cannot represent (many more gates than the probe budget, thresholded or conjunctive effects) *and* a measured pool in which it plausibly occurs. **Continue** only the harness (equal-envelope inheritance control, deletion/transplant/decoupled controls, strict verifier), which is reusable.

## Smallest next action
Do not scale the synthetic study. The single smallest action that could change a verdict is to obtain **one pre-measured full-pool dataset** meeting `EXTERNAL_SOURCE_QUALIFICATION.json` (same ~350-pair pool measured, in duplicate, on ≥ 12 independent backgrounds, 8 released as history) and run `inh_kg`, `lat_aopt`, `gbm_ucb`, `rst_kg` once on it with a margin fixed in advance. With the observed paired SD of ~0.011 the synthetic study separated 0.0017 from 0.01 comfortably with 48 streams; a measured pool would need the same order of independent backgrounds. No measurement was made or requested here.

One further, documented repair cycle: the real-data replay crashed in the GBM comparator (an empty real class gave an all-NaN feature column). Fix in `iv_conv.py` (drop columns without two distinct observed values), recorded in `FREEZE_AMENDMENT.json`, pre-repair file `RUN/iv_conv.pre_repair.py`, ledger note on `real` attempt 1. Synthetic evaluation streams were produced before the repair; any fit affected by it would have crashed, so their numbers are unchanged.

The strict verifier, run after the evaluation, also found one silent inconsistency in the stored records: in `rst_kg` / compose_8 the checkpoint-4 anchors ran the base library although the generation record names a deployed program (the second refit, from already-adapted gate profiles, failed the separation check; 1 of ~1 600 checkpoint records, no task rows affected). The evaluation was not re-run. The case is listed in `KNOWN_EXCEPTIONS.json`, reported by `iv_verify.py` as a NOTE, and the pooled anchor contrasts without this stream are unchanged in sign and size (`inh − rst` A_new −0.0002, A_old +0.0010). `FREEZE_AMENDMENT.json` records both changes to frozen files.

## Design, as run
Frozen before scoring (`CONFIG.json`, `FREEZE.json`, `PROTOCOL.md`): four generators (null with shared-anchor nuisance, low-rank/contextual, alias, compose with late gates and a conjunction), 24 evaluation streams each + 24 decoupled streams each for alias and compose; 8 history cells, 4 batches × 4 task cells, 4 + 4 never-released anchors, 378 pairs, shortlist 5, 4 probes per task (primary), 4 generations, ≤ 12 candidates per generation. Primary endpoint: absolute shortlist utility averaged over batches 2–4 per stream; margin δ = 0.01 absolute (engineering threshold with rationale in `CONFIG.json`, not a fraction of oracle headroom); paired stream-level percentile bootstrap, 98.33 % intervals for the three primary hypotheses. Dev streams 9001–9004 set the comparators (H1 `lat_aopt`, H1a `inh_exploit`, H3 `inh_kg`) and showed timing; evaluation endpoint, worlds and comparators were not changed afterwards.

Budget–utility curve (E, alias + compose): `inh_kg` 0.8534 (0 probes) → 0.8795 (2) → 0.8881 (4) → 0.8944 (8); `rst_kg` identical within 0.0002; `lat_aopt` 0.8789 / 0.8864 / 0.8920; `direct` 0.8537 / 0.8555 / 0.8670. Probes to half the no-probe-to-oracle gap: 4 for `inh_kg`, `rst_kg`, `lat_aopt`; direct measurement does not reach it by 8. Lifecycle (non-decoupled, all kinds): `inh_kg` proposed 1 482 candidates, 226 demonstrated, 921 noninferior, 331 retired; a program was deployed at 176 of 384 checkpoints (alias 77 %, compose 69 %, lowrank 38 %, null 0 %). Post hoc diagnostic against the evaluator-only planted truth: deployed-gate precision 0.96 (alias) / 0.99 (compose), recall 0.92 / 0.58.

Optional API arm (`API_RESULTS.json`, dev stream alias_9001, descriptive): 4 requests, 4 HTTP attempts, 2 945 tokens (limits 6 / 30 000), no credential persisted, no monetary figure asserted; all four typed proposals were valid, two were noninferior, none was promoted, utility identical to the deterministic arm. Failure of this arm could not have blocked anything.

## Limits
One synthetic family; effects are bounded by the generator's decision headroom (the oracle exceeds the no-probe baseline by 0.057, and conventional latent learning already captures ~58 % of that). The strongest comparator happens to represent the planted binary gates well (a few gates behave like a low-rank factor), which is exactly why the null result is informative about *this* family and silent about worlds where it cannot. Validation uses 4 cells per generation, so promotion is noisy by design. Novelty is judged from background knowledge plus one web summary. Nothing here is evidence about biology or about real screens.
