# Procedure inheritance audit: retain the bounded closure; scientific self-repair was not established

The frozen decision remains
`CLOSE_NO_PRACTICAL_BENEFIT_OVER_FRESH_ONE_STEP_PLANNING`. The executable plan
is an engineering improvement over the earlier prototype, but the recorded
utility does not establish a useful advantage over ordinary planning or
distillation. This audit corrects accounting, verification coverage and the
scope of that conclusion. It does not replace the original evaluation or turn
its negative result into positive evidence.

The result concerns this finite two-step-plan implementation and its synthetic
task mixture. It is not a population-level demonstration that inheritance can
never help. Drug-discovery effectiveness remains `NOT_TESTED`. In particular,
the new regime occurs only in the final batch: the study has no subsequent
learning step or later task on which to test repair of that new failure.
Open-ended evolution was not evaluated.

Three reviews covered software execution, statistics/resource accounting, and
mechanism/asset qualification. The supplemental auditors use archived records,
stored artifacts and public references. They do not fit a model, regenerate
hidden truth, score a new scientific outcome, open a real-data payload or call
an API. The 46 non-cache files of the original package remain byte-identical;
its four predecessor packages are preserved separately by its existing receipt.

The same-data comparison against ordinary planning remains the central reason
to stop promoting this implementation as the flagship innovation. A useful
cache is a possible engineering application; its economic value has not been
demonstrated.

Four conditions share each of two generator seeds (`pi_world.py:33`), so the
eight condition streams contain only two independent seed blocks. Post hoc
descriptive checks show that the primary mean is -0.011934 without the null
condition and -0.010982 over alias plus chain. The seed-block means are
-0.006550 and -0.011505. These checks show sensitivity to the frozen task
mixture; they do not authorize changing that mixture or its decision rule.
No confidence interval, significance test or support label is added.

The original statement that no stream has a large reuse effect is too strong:
`novel_71001` has plan-at-3 minus restart-at-3 of +0.013347. One such result does
not establish a general inheritance benefit or override the pooled closure.

## Why the unsuccessful behaviors do not have a threshold-only repair

**The current acquisition policy does not reliably obtain observations that
distinguish the novel regime.** The new regime changes six previously distinct
effect actions and a marker, all within the legal action pool. Probing a known
action can return a residual compatible with the null regime. The unexplained component
U is a broad zero-mean distribution, so null-compatible observations can reduce
its posterior probability. Lowering an exit threshold cannot create information
about effects that were never measured. The exit is checked before the next
action (`pi_model.py:263`); final posterior anomaly and a runtime exit are also
different outcomes.

The reported 0/32 novel tasks are 4 distinct novel backgrounds evaluated by
4 arms at 2 budgets. Plan-only coverage is 0/8 campaigns over those same
4 backgrounds. All four missed runtime detection. Known-state exits occurred
in 39/992 campaigns over 124 stream-background IDs; 12 IDs had an exit in at
least one arm/budget. Arm repetitions and condition IDs are not independent
random-world replications.

**The novel-regime self-repair sequence is absent.** `pi_world.py:57` puts
regime 5 only in batch 4. `pi_run.py:107` learns before each batch from previous
batches and ends after batch 4. Thus there is no regime-5 failure followed by
knowledge revision and a later test. Earlier generations update response
profiles and plans, but this does not establish the proposed ability to diagnose
and repair the novel failure. This is a limitation of the tested research unit,
not a reason to relabel its score or manufacture a favorable next generator.

**Applicability and content transfer are different questions.** The own-plan
range check rejects 21/112 invocations. It takes a maximum over 66 standardized
reference dimensions with a fixed threshold, using small-sample mean/variance
estimates; `rebound` retains the old range. A future applicability contract needs
finite-sample, joint predictive calibration and an explicit update policy.
Threshold relaxation on the consumed evaluation is not a validation.

All 112 transplanted-plan invocations are rejected: content transfer is
`NOT_EXERCISED`. In addition, seeds permute effect/marker assignments and change
the reference baseline. Numeric action IDs alone do not preserve a meaningful
pharmacological correspondence. Disabling the range check in the stored post
hoc analysis does not repair that correspondence or establish portability of
learned knowledge. Its +0.0003 transplant contrast cannot prove that the own
plan encodes uniquely useful transferable knowledge.

**Internal selection is not independent validation.** The shared library and
candidate construction use all released rows, including `val_ids`; leave-one-out
profiles retain labels learned using the omitted row (`pi_plan.py:285`). The
promotion/rollback checks are development selection. This does not demonstrate
leakage of future unreleased task outcomes, but it precludes treating internal
promotion as an independent generalization result. Relaxing two wins to one
happened before evaluation; stored strict-rule flags cannot reconstruct the
counterfactual downstream trajectory under the stricter rule.

## Correct the asset blocker before choosing another architecture

The original `INVENTORY_CHECK.json` combines labelled regimes, actual assay-cost
information, independent hit confirmation and unexposed backgrounds simultaneously.
That is too restrictive for every possible measured functional study:

| Intended claim | Needed evidence | What is not automatically necessary |
|---|---|---|
| Exposed-data functional development | Authorized measured actions, response endpoint, pairing and an honest exposure label | Labelled latent mechanisms or a separate confirmed drug hit |
| Relative measurement efficiency | A defensible measurement/query unit and equal-resource comparisons | Monetary prices, unless monetary savings are claimed |
| Independent functional validation | A genuinely independent evaluation resource and protected access | Resetting exposure by reshuffling or downloading old data |
| Mechanism recovery | Evidence that distinguishes the proposed mechanisms | Functional prediction alone |
| Pharmacological efficacy or economic value | Relevant efficacy or economic measurements | Synthetic utility and quantized CPU projections |

A common measured action subpool can suffice; whole-dataset completeness is not
required. Missing outcomes must not be filled by a model and then called new
observations. Replicates or other defensible uncertainty information must match
the claim, rather than being inferred from repeated policy replays.

No raw dataset is opened by this audit, so it does not qualify a measured unit.
The correct present status is: **no qualified independent validation asset has
been identified; bounded measured development may be feasible, but its observation
structure and decision opportunity still need qualification.** Existing access
restrictions and exposure history continue to apply. This amendment supersedes
the blanket claim that no measured research is possible, not those restrictions.
