# Erratum (2026-10-18): the equal-lineage bootstrap interval dropped lineage multiplicity

Status: a separate record. `analyse.py`, `FREEZE.json`, `RESULTS.json` and `REPORT.md` of this frozen study are
unchanged. The correction is computed by `erratum_equal_lineage_bootstrap.py` from the public per-lineage means in
`RESULTS.json` only and saved in `ERRATUM_equal_lineage_bootstrap.json` (write-once).

## The defect

`analyse.cluster_boot` draws 21 lineage indices with replacement, concatenates the lines of the drawn lineages and
passes them to `equal_cluster_mean`, which averages over `sorted(set(groups))`. A lineage drawn twice therefore
counts once and a lineage not drawn is dropped, so each replicate is the mean over the *distinct* drawn lineages
(13.46 of 21 on average) instead of the mean over the 21 draws. A cluster bootstrap of the equal-lineage mean must
let a repeatedly drawn lineage weigh as often as it was drawn.

It affects one secondary statistic: `RESULTS.json` `standardised.<layer>.equal_lineage_weights.ci95`, quoted in
`REPORT.md` (section 3) and `research/EVIDENCE.md`. It does not affect:

* the primary tests P1-P3 and their Holm family (`common.paired` weights drawn clusters by `sums[picks]` and
  `sizes[picks]`, so multiplicity is kept);
* any point estimate, the pooled, stratum, leave-one-lineage-out and attribution contrasts;
* the NOT_CONFIRMED verdicts or any stop decision.

## Check that the diagnosis is right

Replaying the flawed estimator on the public per-lineage means with the frozen seed (20261017) and 2,000 draws
reproduces the three saved intervals to 1e-12. The same draws with the corrected statistic, and 100,000 draws for
Monte Carlo stability, give:

| Layer | Equal-lineage mean | Saved (flawed) 95% | Corrected 95% (2,000 draws) | Corrected 95% (100,000 draws) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| policy | +0.057 | [-0.175, +0.267] | [-0.212, +0.344] | **[-0.206, +0.355]** |
| I2 | +0.100 | [+0.023, +0.176] | [+0.006, +0.194] | **[+0.002, +0.202]** |
| I1 | +0.076 | [-0.072, +0.213] | [-0.112, +0.282] | **[-0.105, +0.278]** |

The intervals widen by about a quarter; no bound changes sign. At I2 the lower bound is now +0.002, so "the
equal-lineage interval excludes zero" survives only marginally. That statement was never a registered test: the
registered primary test at I2 (pooled, Holm-adjusted) is unchanged and stays NOT_CONFIRMED.

## Consequence

None for any decision. The statement in `REPORT.md` and `EVIDENCE.md` that I2 is "more evenly spread
(equal-lineage mean +0.100 [+0.023, +0.176])" should be read with the corrected interval above.
