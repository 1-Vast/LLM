## Asset A: concrete EU-OPENSCREEN structure

Actual biological protocol:2,464 bioactive compounds distributed on seven384-well plates. Cells grow24h after seeding, then receive24h treatment at10uM. DMSO is the negative plate reference; tetrandrine and nocodazole at5uM are positive controls. Each dataset has four replicates, described by the primary methods as technical replicates. HepG2 is assayed at FMP, IMTM, MEDINA and USC. FMP also assays U2OS. Thus this is a same-background/multi-site contrast plus a same-site/two-background contrast, not a fully crossed4site×2background experiment. Four technical repeats are not four independent donors or tasks.

Microscopy quantifies9fields/well after fixation/staining. CellProfiler identifies objects, filters single-cell feature vectors using HBOS/missing/infinite filters and aggregates median features per well. The primary/code explicitly expose Metadata_Object_Count as retained detected-object abundance; the primary warns toxicity is assessed from cell number only and is not comprehensive. No measured ATP, direct live/dead fate or growth-rate trajectory is established for this asset. Detected count may change through proliferation, death, detachment, segmentation and object filtering. HepG2 clustered growth makes segmentation especially challenging. These limitations narrow the endpoint; they do not erase its value as an experimentally observed functional-abundance response.

There is explicit evidence of seeding/dispenser stripes, edge effects, microscope differences and source-specific culture/staining variation. Cells, serum and dye lots were centrally shared to reduce variation, but three imaging systems and different local practices remain. These observations motivate a test of control-conditioned transport. They do NOT prove that remaining site effects are purely measurement rather than biology.

Critical biological caution: positive controls are real perturbations, not inert instrument standards. The primary shows nocodazole has much stronger count reduction in U2OS than HepG2. Forcing nocodazole/tetrandrine responses to align across cell backgrounds could remove genuine context-dependent treatment effects. Use this as a falsifier of universal reference alignment, not justification for unconditional batch removal. The partial crossing allows limited comparisons, but no full causal identification of instrument/culture/state factors is supplied. There are no beads/calibration standards or crossed same-culture instrument rescans authenticated here.

## Asset B: specific myoblast/myotube opportunity and target gap

The primary studies30suspected myotoxicants,8concentrations10nM..30uM, C2C12 myoblasts and differentiated myotubes. Four technical wells per condition and three biological runs on separate days are described. Myotubes differentiate4days; both states then receive72h treatment. Same-plate vehicle controls occupy roughly30-40wells. NCAP/aminocaproic acid is called a control drug. Cisplatin uses DMF rather than DMSO; a vehicle-specific matching issue must not be ignored. The protocol has Cell Painting Count plus an orthogonal CellTiter-Glo ATP assay on separately processed plates, not a same-physical-well dual readout.

Zenodo10623852 concretely exposes exactly four CSVs: CellProfiler-Myoblasts.csv101,348,971bytes, CellProfiler-Myotubes.csv128,706,408bytes, Harmony-Myotubes.csv57,642,463bytes and Harmony-Myoblasts.csv63,545,089bytes. Header-only GETs confirm Harmony-Myoblasts has1075fields including Compound,Concentration,PlateName,Row,Column,Count; CellProfiler-Myoblasts has1630fields including Metadata_Well,Metadata_Concentration (Image),PlateID,Count and many morphological descriptors. Neither header contains a Viability, CTG or luminescence target. The Count field is a usable current extracted endpoint, not ATP.

Author code GETs clarify the gap: AUTO - Viability and cell count prediction.py reads CTG-fullWellNormresults.csv from a local C:/Users/Roman/...plate reader path. It groups that CTG table by Compound×Concentration, takes median across plate rows, then joins the median target onto multiple morphology wells. A random well split can therefore reuse the same aggregated drug-dose target in train/test; it cannot validate prospective across-plate functional updating.

CTG/CTGtoCSVwMetadata.ipynb source reads original local XLS files with Plate,Repeat,Well,'1s luciferase (CPS)', assigns drug/concentration from well map, normalizes using DMSO mean and blank minimum with MinMaxScaler(0,100), and outputs CTG-fullWellNormresults.csv. It contains an active special-case removal of even rows of plategT4; several upper-clip recipes are commented out, not executed by the inspected code. These are author source conditions, not corrections we executed. Raw ATP counts, blanks and matched plate identities would permit the intended assay-shift diagnostic, but they are not in the inspected Zenodo file list or GitHub tree. No author contact is allowed or attempted. Do not infer global unavailability beyond these exact inspected assets.

Thus do NOT qualify this release as an acquired per-run CTG benchmark. It may support Count transport across days/states after header/body identity qualification. A static endpoint diagnostic would be legitimate; absence of cell trajectories is not the blocking condition. The actual missing information is orthogonal per-run functional targets and control linkage, plus source preprocessing provenance.

## Minimum falsifiable next task, controls and stops

AssetA minimum: qualify raw per-well Metadata_Object_Count plus same-plate reference counts for one predeclared HepG2 held-site task before any count values. Hold whole compounds and their four replicate wells together. Source sites supply treated compound responses; target site supplies a fixed explicitly legal reference-only calibration set (DMSO and, if justified, fixed positive controls), while target non-reference compound counts remain queries. All methods receive the same source/target references and known compound identity. Because target references are measured simultaneously with target wells, this is context-calibrated endpoint transport, not early feedback predicting a later time or a wet-lab-cost reduction.

First compare untreated-reference ratio/offset, source lookup/mean, ordinary affine/reference-conditioned direct regression, ordinary pooled refit and fixed mixtures. If a small target treated-anchor budget is introduced, freeze compound identities before outcomes and give the same anchors to every direct/refit/CL/AL/search arm. Exclude query Count and any count-derived feature from query inputs. A data-calibration result cannot be called a new world-model/update mechanism; ordinary robust plate normalization is already an explicit predecessor. Do not resurrect the historical CIPA/control-information stop merely with a new name: this is a distinct observed cell-abundance/site/background asset, and any claim still must exceed same-information ordinary calibration.

A meaningful falsifier is whether reference-only calibration improves held-compound prediction beyond simple DMSO normalization in each predeclared held-site comparison, while retaining genuine same-site HepG2/U2OS response differences rather than forcing control invariance. This needs a fully frozen response-stage metric/margin and actual schema qualification before running; none is claimed frozen or passed here. Source/target site pairs share data and are not independent clinical tasks. Missingness caused by severe perturbations must not be silently changed to count0 or dropped using full-outcome toxicity/induction/replicating filters. If raw counts/controls cannot be recovered at the planned well granularity, stop that task or explicitly redesign before response values, not afterward.

Stop a measurement-versus-biological IDENTIFICATION claim if it requires assuming all control responses are invariant across backgrounds or all site effects are technical. Stop a new updater claim if its map is merely ordinary normalization/affine calibration or if same-information direct/refit/mixture/search matches it. Stop functional claims that relabel segmentation count as death/ATP. Full world/agent/inherited-U/joint/improved-improver claims remain unestablished. Qualification of a new controlled functional asset, not another numerical pilot, is this phase's positive progress.
