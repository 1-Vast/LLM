## Exact cancellation claim, and its assumptions

Let X_ghcr=log2(N_ghcr+1), with gthe left-slot guide and hthe right-slot guide. For two fixed left guides g,g'and right guides h,h', define

    R_cr = X_ghcr - X_gh'cr - X_g'hcr + X_g'h'cr.

If a nuisance has the additive coordinate form u_gcr+v_hcr+k_cr, Rannihilates it exactly. Therefore context-specific gene main effects and context-specific guide main effects are removed IF they act additively in this chosen log(count+1)coordinate and consistently by guide/slot across partner genes. They need not be invariant across contexts or repeats. Using the same guides in each corner is essential; switching the guide when its gene appears with another partner defeats this cancellation. Complete3by3edges allow equal-weight guide averaging with common sets as another linear construction. Counts pooled canonically over reciprocal orientations with9versus18constructs are not automatically a common-role rectangle: retain orientation roles and weights explicitly.

For T_r=R_A,r-R_B,r, any fixed shared construct-specific intercept b_ghalso cancels, even if bdoes not factor into row+column terms. This includes an identical log-start/plasmid term if one were algebraically included with identical linear guide weights. The actual terminal statistic needs no plasmid value for that cancellation. It does NOT cancel context-specific construct/guide-pair biases, partner-dependent cutting efficiency, toxicity interactions between guides/promoters, copy-number-dependent editing responses, different time/culture states or other nonadditive readout effects. Pseudocount1also means additive nuisance in ideal logN need not remain additive in log(N+1), especially at low counts; exact algebra applies to the declared coordinate, not an assumed physical multiplicative model.

Consequently a reproducible nonzero Tcan falsify an additive two-slot explanation of the measured coordinate under the stated mapping, but does not separate on-target cellular interaction from context-specific nonadditive guide/assay effects. If Tis weak or zero, biology is not proved absent: signal can lie in removed additive components or be poorly measured at these selected actions. Two repeat labels and shared guide sets do not authenticate independent cultures or new biological task generalization. The already exposed selected counts cannot become a new pristine confirmation merely by taking a different linear projection.

## Difference from author anchor-regression GI

Cached author fitness.py constructs common-plasmid log enrichment with pseudocount1/all-construct sum normalization, guide medians, nontargeting centering, sample-wide gene-pair scaling and essentiality-based sample filtering. Cached interactions.py fits expected fitness anchor by anchor from single-gene fitness and standardizes/mirror-averages residuals. Published GI subsequently aggregates sample/repeat residuals. A linear raw-read rectangle is not that nonlinear/context/anchor-dependent pipeline and is not a synthetic-lethality score.

Even the simple expected anchor model mu_ij=a_j*x_i+b_j gives a raw rectangle

    mu_ab - mu_ad - mu_cb + mu_cd = (a_b-a_d)*(x_a-x_c).

It can be nonzero while every author-style fitted residual is zero. Thus rectangles remove additive row/column effects but do not automatically remove an anchor-varying slope, a multiplicative single-effect response or ordinary low-rank structure. Context differences in those quantities can give nonzero Twithout a change in the author's residual interaction. Conversely an author residual rectangle can differ from the raw rectangle due to subtraction and anchor/sample scaling. Equality requires explicit additional restrictions such as the expected coordinate being purely additive and transformations sharing the required affine scale; no such equality is authenticated by the present identity audit.

Ordinary two-way fixed-effect regression on the same ordered graph, cycle projection, low-rank completion, source-conditioned residual models and anchor-regression models are legitimate comparators. Their source/search costs and legal target support must match. A cycle statistic is a particular representation of a residual after an additive model, not a novel learned updater merely because it removes nuisance algebraically. Gauge freedom in assigning row/column constants also prevents a unique causal main-effect interpretation.

## Verifiable restrictions versus a transport mechanism

Feasible finite diagnostic restriction: all selected four-corner contrasts obey (or fail) a stated additive log-coordinate null, under fixed guides/roles and the current selected-action coverage. A complementary repeated Tmeasurement could assess whether the nonadditive contrast repeats, but would be a separately frozen exposed-development diagnostic using existing counts, not authorized by this source report. No such numerical projection is performed here.

For support-to-unseen-action transport, cancellation alone supplies no prediction of an unobserved residual. Under an exact additive model one missing corner has the ordinary completion X_ab=X_ad+X_cb-X_cd; measured violations refute that completion rather than create a new rule for the missing interaction. Many nonadditive laws fit the same observed corners and differ at held actions. A proposed transport restriction needs a falsifiable common structure across unseen edges and a fair same-information ordinary completion/refit comparison. A high repeatable cycle residual may be useful measurement evidence but does not establish learnability, biological causality or inherited-U/RSI.
