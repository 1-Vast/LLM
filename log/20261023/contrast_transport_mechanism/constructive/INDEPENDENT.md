## One specific restriction worth distinguishing mathematically

Let E be a prospectively fixed graph of unordered gene-pair actions and B its unsigned action-by-gene incidence matrix. Let d_c(e) denote the change in one **consistently defined** action-level response coordinate between source and target context c, not a latent molecular state. A four-action even-cycle contrast for distinct genes a,b,c,d is

    L(d_c) = d_c(a,b)+d_c(c,d)-d_c(a,d)-d_c(c,b).

For every additive gene-main-effect response d_c=B beta_c, L(d_c)=0 exactly. Thus a reproducible nonzero L can falsify that mathematical response family without identifying latent mechanism. This is a contrast of four actual action measurements, not four free queries or an additional independent biological task. Missing cycle edges cannot be imputed by the hypothesis being tested.

The sole transport restriction examined is

    d_c = B beta_c + Z theta_c + epsilon_c,
    B^T Z = 0,  ||theta_c||_0 <= s,

where columns of Z are an **independently fixed, finite catalog of localized even-cycle response patterns**, s is frozen before target outcomes, and support feedback must identify which few patterns change in a new context. The same catalog is inherited; active coefficients/support may change by context. Z cannot be mined from exposed target query residuals. Localization/catalog and sparsity are the substantive claim; orthogonal decomposition alone is a generic reparameterization and contributes nothing.

This is narrower than arbitrary pairwise corrections and can differ from the stopped endpoint-only recipe: B beta cannot express its nonzero cycle component. It is also not automatically the same operation as a fixed Gaussian low-rank posterior or KRR: a hard sparse catalog allows context-dependent support selection, a union of subspaces with potentially high rank across contexts, rather than a single fixed-rank Gaussian state. Family labels alone therefore do not prove equivalence. Conversely low-rank/kernel methods can represent all or some of these same functions, and K=ZZ^T with quadratic shrinkage is an essential same-information control. If theta is merely fitted by ridge, the supposed new cycle updater is exactly an ordinary linear/kernel residual model and should be reported that way. Non-Gaussian sparse inference is familiar compressed-sensing/model-selection mathematics, not an invented primitive.

## Why present evidence does not justify this restriction

The successful read statistic compared hash-adjacent actions, not complete cycles; no independent cycle measurement or cycle-catalog sparsity was verified. Its reproducibility therefore does not reject B beta, support the catalog or identify any sparse theta. Stable context-dependent guide/library effects can also create nonzero cycles; cycle cancellation removes additive **gene** potentials only, not every measurement nuisance or guide-pair interaction. Calling the kernel nullspace an epistasis space would silently change measured read abundance into a biological interaction.

Sparsity is basis-dependent: a vector sparse in a fitted dictionary can be dense in another cycle basis. Choosing Z or s after target outcomes can manufacture the desired claim. No currently authenticated pathway/complex map specifies the needed cycle catalog, no source-only repeated response panel establishes its transfer, and no legal support-to-query design has yet demonstrated the necessary measurement identifiability. Five dependent exposed contexts or the two repeat labels are not a large independent meta-training population. Thus the distinction from fixed quadratic models is mathematically real but the application-specific premise is presently unsupported.

Even sparse theta is not enough. Let S be legal observed actions. The projected measurement design must distinguish the relevant Z supports after allowing the nuisance B_S beta. If B_S is sufficiently expressive, it can absorb observed sparse cycle changes, making theta unidentifiable from that support. Complete measured cycles can cancel beta but cost every constituent action; shared-action reuse must be recorded rather than claimed as new labels. A generic O(s log M) compressed-sensing slogan cannot be imported without the actual nuisance-projected design's conditioning, noise and available action mask. No such property has been established here.

## Minimal falsifiable experiment, if the missing premise is supplied

A future bounded development test would first fix a small biologically or source-data justified cycle catalog and action mask **without target query outcomes**, authenticate all required actions and one response endpoint, and freeze an action-heldout split grouping all guides/orientations/both target repeats. Use target support only to estimate beta and sparse theta; a heldout action's target replicate1 outcome must not enter features, catalog construction or selection simply because evaluation uses replicate2. Source-only covariates are lawful, shared and costed. Current exposed arrays would provide development evidence only, never fresh biological confirmation.

Primary falsifier: sparse-cycle support feedback fails to improve untouched target action contrasts/decision utility beyond both (i) ordinary gene-main-effect updating and (ii) same-catalog quadratic ridge/KRR, a competent low-rank/source-conditioned direct model and an ordinary sparse-regression control with equal source/search/support budget. Source mean/frozen prediction and scalar calibration remain controls. Count all edge acquisitions, dictionary learning, support selection and inference cost. A known sparse-regression method matching the proposed estimator does not falsify biological sparsity, but it does defeat an algorithm-updater novelty claim; the simplest ordinary implementation should then own the measured capability.

A statistically repeatable nonzero four-cycle alone is only rejection of additive potentials, not success of transport. A held-action predictive gain only against B beta is insufficient when same-information kernel/sparse controls match it. Stop if Z/s require exposed query tuning, necessary cycle edges are unavailable, nuisance-projected support cannot identify the candidate directions, or equal-cost ordinary controls explain the gain. No need for latent truth before this ordinary test, and no new reserve/data/model run is authorized by this proposal.

That would be a finite-information biological restriction, even implemented with familiar sparse inference.
