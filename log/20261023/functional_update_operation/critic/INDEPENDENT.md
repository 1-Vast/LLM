## Verdict and the scientific question

**NONE / REVISE before an experiment.** I cannot specify one presently defensible novel operation merely from the instruction “paid cell state tells us where functional feedback should propagate.” That instruction identifies a valuable learning problem; it does not yet identify an algorithmic restriction. This verdict does not follow from calling it meta-learning. It follows from an explicit operation-level ordinary competitor, an exact positive equivalence, and the absence of a cell-grounded condition that would distinguish when that competitor should fail.

The useful application would be deciding which genetic vulnerabilities to validate in a new cellular context after a small number of real functional anchors. Genetic dependency is not drug efficacy. Historical Chronos scores are a distinct assay, with screen/culture context differences from Perturb-seq; shared identifiers do not make RNA a contemporaneous mediator of the functional screen. ## A precise informed ordinary opponent

Let a source-trained base forecast be f0(x,a), and define residual r=y-f0. Let z contain only lawfully available cell state, intervention identity, context and QC. Train a conditional residual mean m_phi and a PSD residual covariance K_phi from independent source-context episodes, with both support and query labels available only in source episodes. In a held-out context reveal only support functional feedback r_S. Then the ordinary conditional empirical-Bayes/kernel update is

    delta_Q = m_Q + K_QS (K_SS + R_SS)^(-1) (r_S - m_S).

The kernel may use state differences, baseline context, action target and their interactions; kernel/hyperparameter selection must be source-only. Include fixed ridge, conditional low-rank covariance and a direct support-query episode predictor under matched source histories and selection budgets. The covariance update already routes positive, negative or zero functional corrections to different queries. Learning its gating or low-rank basis across contexts is genuine inherited updating, but does not by itself distinguish our algorithm from this comparator.

This opponent is specific enough to implement. It is stronger than an uninformed minimum-norm update or a state-only direct predictor. It must receive exactly the same source functional histories and purchased states as the proposed mechanism. Include mean/residual estimation, selector search, RNA acquisition and source assembly in costs. Its success is an empirical question; its availability is not a proof that no better nonlinear restricted updater can exist.

Exact equivalence positive, not an executed new experiment: suppose residuals have a one-dimensional inherited cellular response r_i=u_i beta with beta~N(0,tau^2). One noiseless nonzero support s gives beta=r_s/u_s and query correction u_q r_s/u_s. The ordinary covariance K=tau^2 u u^T yields the exact same correction. A proposed “state-derived shared response direction” can therefore pass an impressive support-to-query toy solely by learning u. It must show where and why its update restriction beats this informed same-u opponent; defeating isotropic regression would not do so.

## A falsifiable adverse case, with its exact scope

Consider two unobserved residual-response regimes with identical available RNA state z and identical actions. In both regimes support residual r_s=1. Regime A has query residual r_q=1; regime B has r_q=-1. All permitted observations before purchasing query function are identical. Any deterministic updater must issue the same query correction t in both regimes. Its two squared errors are (t-1)^2 and (t+1)^2, whose average is t^2+1. Zero transfer minimizes this average; positive shared transfer t=1 has average error 2. A randomized rule cannot evade the average bound, since the risk is E[t^2]+1. These are algebraic statements, not results from synthetic or biological execution.

This refutes an unconditional guarantee that measured transcriptomic agreement identifies safe functional sharing. It does **not** refute conditional sharing if a lawfully observed feature distinguishes the regimes, if source contexts establish the relevant invariant response, or if the proposed method honestly abstains. It also does not demand complete molecular occupancy identification. The candidate must name its distinguishing observable and quantify when the assumed invariance fails. Residual sign anticorrelation is relevant to synthetic-lethal/context-specific dependency; RNA similarity alone supplies no exclusion restriction.

A second necessary negative control is straightforward: preserve available state/action/context marginals while permuting **independent context-level** source residual-response assignments, before any candidate learning. If a purported state-based transport law keeps its advantage, leakage, a context/target prior, or source-gene mean is a stronger explanation. Do not shuffle wells or guides and call them independent context evidence. This is a proposed control, not a performed result.

## What would turn the verdict into GO

One explicit operation must specify which physical/assay variation is invariant, which is allowed to change function, and how this creates an update restriction beyond conditional residual covariance. Examples of a hypothesis class are not themselves an innovation. A generically learned gate or editor is insufficient unless its mathematical operation or a newly established biological law gives an identifiable additional prediction.

Before new labels: specify an independent context source, a source-only program/parameter selection procedure, purchased support/query-state costs, number of functional anchors and failure tolerances. Predeclare a target-selection endpoint such as top-k genetic-dependency regret against gene means as well as strong direct forecasts and EB. The old experiment's full-menu regret being slightly worse than gene means is especially relevant. Test support-to-query improvement from common starts; do not confound useful historical information with a new updater. Matched total cost includes fitting, source search and improvement trials. If gains come from measured RNA alone, report a representation increment. If they come from K estimated across histories, report ordinary inherited covariance value unless a distinct operation was tested.

For RSI, changing an updater is Level 2 only after independent descendant benefit. Level 3 requires a changed updater-proposal/selection rule G1, compared with G0 from the same parent/history/budget on later independent tasks, measuring subsequent improvement yield. Saving useful gates or textual diagnoses does not establish improved improvement. No agent necessity or virtual-cell necessity is currently demonstrated by this mechanism description.

## Two actual recent originals read locally

Local originals were already downloaded in another phase; I re-read method paragraphs, not that phase's review opinions. Exact original receipt/body SHA256 comparisons passed. See LOCAL_SOURCE_RECEIPTS.json and newly projected text. No original file was modified. This is a bounded proximity check, not exhaustive novelty clearance or author-performance replication.

1. **SkillGrad: Optimizing Agent Skills Like Gradient Descent**, arXiv:2605.27760v1, https://arxiv.org/html/2605.27760v1. Read Sections 3.4–3.6: trajectory failures and contrastive successes produce per-task diagnoses; persistent pattern memory records recurring and absorbed corrections; the patcher updates shared patterns rather than tasks and chooses the skill layer for each edit. This is concrete prior overlap for “inherit recurring repair strategies, selectively alter modular research procedures.” It does not learn a numerical cell-state functional-transfer covariance or prove the biological restriction above. Its textual gradient/momentum analogy is explicitly nonnumeric. Its reported performance was not reproduced here.

2. **PatchWorld: Gradient-Free Optimization of Executable World Models**, arXiv:2605.30880v1, https://arxiv.org/html/2605.30880v1. Read Sections 4.1–4.3: standalone belief-state predict/correct interface; contrastive action/outcome signature evidence; typed execution/parsing/state/readout counterexamples; diagnostic prioritization; independently sampled full-module repairs; acceptance only for improved lexicographic full-replay score, rejecting displayed-example fixes that regress elsewhere. This is concrete overlap for “counterexamples selectively repair a world-model procedure and preserve previous capabilities.” It is not an empirical-Bayes functional updater and does not establish safe cross-cell transfer or Level 3 improved-improver learning. Program repair alone cannot supply our missing novel operation. Author performance was not reproduced.

Both originals are from 2026 and are narrower, different controls than CLAW/Harness/ExpEvolve/TTT-E2E/Centaur. I do not claim either is the globally closest paper for selective biological transfer. They limit modular-repair novelty narratives; they do not rule out a genuinely new cellular transfer law.
