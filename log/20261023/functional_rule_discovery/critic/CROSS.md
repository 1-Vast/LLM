## Frontier exchange

Read `frontier/INDEPENDENT.md`: Mani, Vondrick and Zemel, *Few-Shot Design Optimization by Exploiting Auxiliary Information*, arXiv:2602.12112v1. Its AuxNP learns a posterior over functional outcomes from full contexts containing action, measured function and auxiliary observations, with query action available and query auxiliary not required. Deployment appends purchased observations while learned weights stay fixed. This is a concrete strong same-history direct predictor, not merely an abstract model-family objection.

Frontier challenged whether the proposed MGG memory has any advantage over AuxNP/DeepSets or an ordinary learned optimizer when all receive the same available observations and computable gradients. I accepted this comparator and the need to charge offline training, online gradients, memory, and inference. With K=5/10 feedback points, caching history or replay may be cheaper than large coordinate memory; no advantage has been measured. I countered that exact commutative aggregation or full-support access does not prove equal predictive utility at equal resources. Frontier explicitly agreed: these are falsifiable competitors, not an impossibility theorem or a prohibition by model-family name.

Frontier also located real project precedent in `log/20261023/feedback_law_discovery/DECISION.md`. I read it and sealed `HISTORY_SCOPE_CORRECTION.md`: prior BeatAML order/batch consistency improved its consistency measure while worsening functional MSE in all three seeds; ordinary covariance conditioning won. Two-order averaging is not full permutation invariance and no longer supplies a new cell-specific fit rationale. No BeatAML rescue, GDSC transplant, or repeated order toy is proposed.

## Constructive exchange

Read `constructive/INDEPENDENT.md` and `SOURCE_SCOPE_ADDENDUM.md`: Li et al., *Predicting drug inactivation by changes in bacterial growth dynamics*, DOI 10.1038/s44259-025-00152-w. The public panel contains exposure growth curves and a separately measured residual biological activity assay. It is not a mammalian cell-state world-model dataset, an early-prefix endpoint, proof of chemical degradation, or 38 independent environments. The published lag-based classifier is already a direct baseline.

I argued that a source-fixed small shape representation could be a useful finite-sample constraint even if Bayesian ridge/logistic calibration is conventional, and that ordinary held-compound functional prediction does not require proving cross-context inheritance first. Constructive explicitly agreed. They distinguished that legitimate ordinary forecast from the present search for a novel reusable update mechanism: opening MAT assets to confirm more labels does not change exact same-basis ridge equivalence or the existing lag method. They therefore narrowed their own next-step proposal to no blind dataset chain; authentic paired multi-compound independent contexts would be needed to change inheritance eligibility. This is not a permanent rejection of the source or ordinary replication.

Constructive independently identified commutative sum, full-support DeepSets and cached replay as essential MGG controls, and challenged the two-order invariance wording. I explicitly accepted both and confirmed that historical order consistency cannot be reused as a new fit justification.
