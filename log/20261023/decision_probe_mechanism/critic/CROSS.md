## Constructive challenge to critic

Constructive agreed that choosing RAED's target as best-drug classes fixes critic's one-probe counterexample, but challenged extending this to a sufficient sequential probe state. Its separately pre-frozen XOR example has four worlds(d,n), probes N=n and X=d xor n, each marginally uninformative about drug d, jointly identifying it. Myopic positive-gain stopping fails, and replacing the true joint law by a product of class-conditional marginals loses the pair value. Critic accepted this precise objection.

Critic's counterquestion: does class=d itself fail, or only the additional factorization/myopic restriction? Constructive explicitly answered the latter. A class-conditioned model can preserve the correct joint P(N,X|d) without keeping n as an explicit state, and then an ordinary joint planner recovers the pair. No proof requires an explicit nuisance variable or full mechanism identification. Controlled observation/planning sufficiency matters, beyond terminal action sufficiency.

Both agree Action-BED's full-history joint design/action policies can express this pair strategy given its actual simulator/training contract. RAED can consider a paid composite experiment with a correct joint observation law. Neither primary is refuted by the XOR or critic witness. Under a genuine controlled-lumpability restriction, cached ordinary direct aggregation can reproduce the compressed planner; constructive has no additional update operator or measured compute bound beyond this ordinary comparator. This is a scoped NONE, not a veto of valid biological restrictions or efficient familiar compositions.

## Source challenge to critic

Source challenged any suggestion that only paired different-modality data can provide joint probe laws. Critic confirmed that same-sample jointly covered drug AUCs can estimate a particular joint drug-response law under declared availability, overlap, missingness and patient grouping. Different modalities, full trajectories and mechanistic identification are not universally required for a lawful finite-pool acquisition diagnostic. What is missing for the present core claim is a distinctive testable restriction and independent validation beyond strong same-information joint direct planning, not a universal impossibility of estimating any joint law.

Source accepts Action-BED's joint optimization and finite-compute idea as substantive prior work, not something invalidated by a direct model's expressiveness. It also accepts RAED's decision-class target remedy and the need to preserve loss magnitudes for unequal-regret decisions. Beat AML does not automatically grant a reparameterizable simulator, score function or arbitrary assay counterfactuals; defined finite-pool replay is a different lawful contract.

Critic's counterquestion concerned computational fairness of fixed linear-Gaussian component models. Source confirmed the direct comparator may reuse identical B,C,R and Woodbury/cached sufficient statistics. It cannot be forced to materialize dense covariance to manufacture a component memory/compute advantage. The exact conditioning equality applies only to the fixed linear-Gaussian law, not every nonlinear component model or every potential useful finite-resource restriction.

Source subsequently sealed FORMULA_SCOPE_ADDENDUM.md: its original numerator B_Q C B_S' needs zero support-query measurement-noise covariance; in general T=BCB'+R and the conditional update uses T_QS T_SS^(-1). Critic read and accepted this correction. Same-T direct/component equality remains valid. The original sealed report is preserved, and the correction is not repurposed as a claim that all component models fail.
