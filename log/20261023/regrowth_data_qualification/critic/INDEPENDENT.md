## What the actual primary experiments are

1. Short-treatment organoid formation: sorted EpCAM-positive cells from PyMT/Bcl11b reporter tumors encounter Doxo for 24 hours and then washout; persister organoid formation and reporter composition are assessed later. Fig2D/E day10 images and fluorescence fractions are not a continuous per-cell treatment/regrowth series. Fig2F/G compares WT and Bcl11b KO after 24-hour treatment/washout, with day11 images and persister organoid frequency relative to control. Caption n=6 wells from three experiments is not six independent donor tumors or six source-to-query episodes.
2. Prolonged-treatment regrowth: Fig2J/K pregrows WT/KO organoids for 3 days, treats with 100 nM Doxo for 7 days, then washes out and measures actively growing organoids at day17. Authors describe size decrease in both groups during treatment, regrowth in WT and loss in KO after withdrawal. This is the clearest direct hypothesis for treatment-phase measurements versus later regrowth. Neither same early survival/size nor matched on-treatment histories is quantitatively established by those narrative statements alone.
3. Dissociation/replating/rechallenge: persister organoids are dissociated into single cells and re-embedded in fresh Matrigel to remove residual drug and generate clonal regrowth; marker composition is measured in regrown organoids and Doxo responsiveness is tested again (Fig2L and cited S3H-J). Replating changes density, matrix and population selection. This is not automatically the same intact-organoid post-withdrawal trajectory or the same exposure protocol as Fig2J/K.
4. Residual-cell transplantation: after seven-day ex-vivo treatment, equal numbers of residual WT or KO cells are transplanted to mice. This tests regenerative/tumor-initiating potential per administered residual-cell number under a new host context. It does not estimate total relapse burden from an original treated well, because residual-cell yield is normalized away. It cannot be silently joined to intact-well regrowth as if it were another timepoint.
5. TNFα protocols: Fig5C/D gives 200 ng/ml for six days before marker measurement; Fig5E/F instead pretreats for two days, then 200 nM Doxo for 24 hours and single-cell replating to measure persister frequency. These are not matched marker/functional measurements from one time series. Fig5G in-vivo three-cycle treatment differs again. The caption explicitly says WT and KO in-vivo experiments were separate and absolute tumor sizes may not be directly compared. Mouse volume measurements every three/four days do not make these curves interchangeable with organoid counts.
6. Molecular snapshots: scRNA-seq uses pregrown organoids treated with/without 100 nM Doxo for 24 hours, then dissociation and live-cell sorting. Cells are destructively sampled; untreated and treated snapshots are not tracked cell descendants. Bulk sequencing, sorted BCL11B reporter subsets, treatment-naive atlases, endpoint immunostaining and human PDX collections are additional assays/contexts. Fig6B PDX RNA is collected one day after the last of three doses, not before treatment. GSE228442 is a molecular-data accession, not evidence that each sequenced sample has a linked later regrowth query.

## ONE precise, falsifiable candidate task grounded in Fig2J/K

Application: identify a condition that appears suppressive during chemotherapy but leaves residual organoids capable of regrowth after withdrawal. Predict the well's fraction or number of actively growing organoids at day17 from that same well's measurements recorded only through the seven-day Doxo treatment endpoint, plus the known washout schedule, WT/KO identity and lawful baseline observations. Use the documented intact-organoid protocol only. A rechallenge task is separately defined and needs its own linked assay; it is not manufactured by attaching S3 endpoints to the Fig2J timecourse.

Required qualification: actual well/experiment/tumor-or-clone IDs; treatment and washout times; well-linked baseline and on-treatment observations; post-withdrawal observations and active-growth definition; denominators and exclusion rules; mapping of biological versus technical replication; and measurement units. If images are used, identity continuity and scale/segmentation must be authenticated. Representative images alone do not supply all wells or unbiased response labels. For the endpoint fraction, specify whether denominator is initial organoids, residual organoids at washout or all scored structures at day17. Those answer different questions and can diverge because organoids disappear or fuse.

Data may refute this if a simple current-burden/genotype predictor is sufficient. ## Essential safeguards and ordinary controls

All models receive the identical lawful baseline, on-treatment history, genotype, drug protocol and washout schedule. No post-withdrawal marker, day17 regrowth image, rechallenge response or post-last-dose PDX RNA enters early input. The 24-hour treated RNA snapshot may be a lawful earlier observation only for an independently authenticated paired downstream task; it cannot be relabeled as pretreatment and cannot be assigned to unrelated seven-day wells by shared condition name.

Direct baseline: genotype/condition plus baseline/current viable burden and treatment duration. Rich direct baseline: regularized on-treatment summaries or a competitive sequence predictor of the same endpoint using the same image-derived/molecular history, if such history is actually linked. Ordinary dynamics: exponential/logistic or parsimonious viable/persister mixture with identical measurements and ordinary source fit, followed by same-history refitting/sequential updating where permitted. A simple population mixture is particularly important because known WT/KO groups and surviving cell counts may explain the effect without a new model-update algorithm. No new kinetic variable or resistant-state inheritance is inferred from a gene-expression change alone.

Function-endpoint comparator: if a lawful short pilot colony/regrowth assay on source material is available at the same decision time, compare it directly with the proposed biomarker/world prediction under equal acquisition cost and delay. Do not gift that pilot to one method, and do not call its later endpoint an early observation. Its utility may reveal that direct functional measurement is a better decision than molecular-state simulation. Cell number at washout and per-residual-cell regrowth must both be reported when comparing a normalization that otherwise hides treatment killing.

Train/tune on source independent experiments and hold out complete tumor/preparation/experiment groups; wells and organoids are nested. Two validated KO clones within six passages are not a large panel of independent biological tasks. A random organoid/frame split leaks shared preparation and treatment. If only three independent experiments are accessible, report that limited uncertainty honestly; don't interpret 18 organoids as 18 donor tasks. Cross-experiment WT/KO absolute-size comparisons must honor the explicit Fig5G caveat.

Bcl11b KO organoids cluster toward Matrigel centers under control conditions, limiting individual volumetric expansion despite comparable organoid numbers. This author-noted geometry effect makes volume-only endpoints hazardous; it is a concrete reason to distinguish organoid number, volume, viable cell yield and active growth. Marker fluorescence fractions are neither cell numbers nor organoid-formation probabilities. Control-normalized frequencies are not absolute residual burden unless denominator/normalization are known.
