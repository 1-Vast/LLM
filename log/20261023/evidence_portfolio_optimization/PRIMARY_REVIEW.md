# Primary-source review of the evidence-portfolio proposal

Date: 2026-10-23. Scope: five primary sources, their relevant methods where accessible, and the closest experimental controls. This is a literature and mathematical audit, not a biological experiment or proof of RSI. No reserved outcomes were opened and no existing implementation was edited.

## Source identification and overlap

1. **Zhanran Lin, Wanteng Ma, Zhimei Ren and Yuting Wei, _Sample-Efficient Multiple Testing with Adaptive Data Collection_, arXiv:2609.26651v1, submitted 22 September 2026.** [Abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26651v1), [full method](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.26651v1). Section 3.1 already couples adaptive sampling with e-BH. Its increments must have conditional null expectation at most one given the global history and current action. The sampler uses observed average log-e growth and posterior-style randomized exploration; rejected hypotheses are frozen to obtain nested rejection sets. This is a direct control for evidence-growth allocation. The paper distinguishes valid inference from sample-complexity assumptions; Section 6 leaves heavy-tailed or misspecified-model analysis open. It does not establish our cell-assay calibration.

2. **Ziyu Xu, Catherine Wang, Larry Wasserman, Kathryn Roeder and Aaditya Ramdas, _Active multiple testing with proxy p-values and e-values_, arXiv:2502.05715v2, revised 14 December 2025; v1 submitted 8 February 2025.** [Abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.05715v2), [full method](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.05715v2). Sections 1.1 and 2 allow an arbitrarily inaccurate proxy F and an arbitrarily dependent, valid true e-value E: query with probability (1-gamma/F)+ and return F without a query or (1-gamma)E with one. The active e-BH guarantee follows from the resulting valid e-values. Appendix A already permits multilevel proxies and updating remaining proxies after observed queries. A learned world prior plus valid testing or iterative proxy repair is therefore insufficient novelty. The prerequisite true e-value still needs a justified assay model or design.

3. **Hongjian Wang, Sanjit Dandapanthula and Aaditya Ramdas, _Anytime-valid FDR control with the stopped e-BH procedure_, arXiv:2502.08539v3, revised 5 August 2025; v1 submitted 12 February 2025.** [Abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.08539v3), [full method](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.08539v3). Section 2 and Theorem 2.4 require global e-processes for arbitrary global stopping. Locally valid streams can fail after cross-stream information is used to stop them. Their causal condition supplies one sufficient route from local to global validity. Arbitrary dependence among fixed-time e-values is consequently not permission for arbitrary sequential feedback. Shared plate controls, nuisance fitting, allocation and stopping must enter the proposed filtration explicitly.

4. **Biswa Sengupta, _Self-Evolving Agents with Anytime-Valid Certificates_, arXiv:2607.00871v1, submitted 1 July 2026.** [Abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00871v1), [full method](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.00871v1). SEA uses a frozen base, steering adapter and versioned harness; Section 3 explicitly disclaims new learning theory. Sections 4.1-4.3 say their composed forgetting, performative-convergence and counterfactual-credit guarantees remain unproved; the implemented credit estimate is a recency-weighted directive reward mean. Section 9.1 supplies a useful no-op-composite control and reports that one apparently strong gate arm accepted zero edits, so its score was not an edit effect. This is a concrete warning against interpreting a certificate or an aggregate score as demonstrated improvement of the improver. Its coding-task results do not settle cell-discovery utility or inherited RSI.

5. **David Scott Lewis, _Scientific CI/CD for Self-Modifying Discovery Agents: Statistical Godel Gates, Capacity Budgets, and Domain Verifiers_.** The [official ICML page](https://icml.cc/virtual/2026/82618) resolves by ordinary HTTP and identifies a **poster in the 3rd AI for Math Workshop: Toward Self-Evolving Scientific Agents**, 11 July 2026, with [OpenReview forum 4ob0d33A2l](https://openreview.net/forum?id=4ob0d33A2l). Calling this simply an ICML 2026 paper hides its workshop status. The official abstract specifies statistical, capacity and domain gates and reports adoption-risk experiments. Full current OpenReview note/PDF requests returned a challenge/403; no access-control workaround was attempted. Search-index text of an anonymous preliminary PDF is not a verified current full method, author list or main-conference acceptance. The full-text novelty comparison therefore remains incomplete for this source.

## Corrections to the proposed guarantee

These are this review's deductions, rather than new theorems claimed by the sources.

- **Predictability is necessary but not sufficient.** For each null, each proposed increment must satisfy its conditional expectation bound under that null and the complete available history. A predictable choice of a misspecified Student-t likelihood ratio does not make the ratio valid. Empirical null calibration can diagnose violations; a finite collection of passed simulations cannot establish a uniform conditional guarantee under the real assay law.
- **Specify the capital operation.** Fixed initial mixing of globally valid e-processes preserves validity. Predictable convex mixing of individually valid next-read factors also preserves validity. Choosing a high accumulated expert wealth after observing its outcomes, or repeatedly adding new experts with free initial capital, is a different operation and needs an accounting argument.
- **The half-weight bound is path-specific.** A protected wallet E_port,t = 0.5 E_base,t + 0.5 E_adapt,t implies E_port,t >= 0.5 E_base,t. Hence e-BH contains the base wallet's alpha/2 discoveries on the same read stream and hypothesis family. Giving the base factor half the weight each round instead only implies the weaker 0.5^t factor bound. Neither statement compares against the different wells that a standalone baseline allocation policy would have purchased.
- **Separate expert scoring from causal allocation credit.** Frozen predictors can all be scored on a queried action's observed response. Their rewards on unqueried actions, and the later histories their policies would cause, are not observed counterfactuals. Logged randomization, positive action probabilities and a stated estimator can identify limited one-step policy comparisons; they do not produce an exact alternative adaptive trajectory for free.
- **Null validity is not a Goodhart-proof fitness theorem.** Even if each expert produces a valid e-value, selecting the largest of many such scores need not preserve expectation at most one. Discovery FDR does not control the chance of adopting a harmful editor or guarantee that more editor search creates more useful descendants. Retain explicit all-cost, equal-search and frozen-editor controls.
- **The KL statement has a specific likelihood contract.** For a true density q, fixed null density p0, and predictive density p on the same observation, E_q[log(q/p0)-log(p/p0)] = KL(q || p). Equal known Gaussian covariance reduces this to squared mean error in noise units. This identity does not establish validity with an estimated null, unknown heavy tails, restricted or clipped bettors, nor optimal allocation. Scoring only independently identified true hits is a possible utility analysis; selecting those hits from the same evidence being scored is circular.

## Closest controls and a falsifiable opportunity

Use one frozen observation/likelihood contract for all arms. The minimum serious comparator is **e-PS allocation plus the strongest direct predictor**, paired with the **active proxy e-value construction** if the task permits its query contract. Give the world model the identical inputs, nuisance calibration, candidate actions and acquisition budget. Include uniform allocation and a fixed ordinary expert pool as simpler controls.

For proposed portfolio learning, compare a protected-wallet portfolio against ordinary fixed mixing and an online expert-weight learner with the same feedback. For any editor, include a no-op editor and an equal-budget ordinary program search. Evaluate future-task discovery utility and independently measured calibration, not offspring count or the most successful historical e-value. An inherited editor must beat its frozen predecessor across subsequent tasks with all search and validation costs charged.

A potentially useful contribution remains: an assay-qualified implementation that improves discoveries per cost through transferable prediction and adaptive allocation, with explicit behavior under expert birth, nuisance uncertainty and feedback. The sources do not prove this impossible. They do remove generic e-BH, iterative proxy repair, e-growth sampling and statistical self-edit gates from the standalone novelty claim. The current attachment supplies a research direction; it does not yet supply a distinctive validated mechanism.
