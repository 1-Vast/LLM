# Pre-freeze revisions and rejected design

The initial root proposal counted positive final reads, scouting the original top 40% and confirming 20%, versus a direct arm buying final reads for the original top 60%. The evidence reviewer initially supported it, then identified the set-dominance counterexample on reading the actual source: direct includes every possible adaptive final candidate. With the same final observations, adaptive cannot exceed direct's positive count. No numeric outcomes had been converted and no freeze existed. Do not interpret this discarded design as a numerical experiment or negative evidence about all adaptive acquisition.

Before freeze, root and evidence reviewer explicitly changed the task to two-read repeatability: BOTH independently assigned validation-role reads must be positive. The strong fixed comparator purchases pairs for the top 30% (integer-rounded) at the same total read budget; adaptive buys 40% scouts and 20% finals. Now the possible adaptive confirmed set includes ranks outside the fixed paired set. This makes a comparison possible without supplying free feedback or weakening the fixed comparator's per-unit success definition. All final choices precede final response conversion. Neither task provides latent-hit or FDR certification. Thresholds, roles, fractions and the primary rule were not chosen from this study's outcomes.

The first metadata-only freeze command exited 1 before writing a freeze because 2,880 original rows encode two anchor concentrations (`0.125|0.003` or `0.5|0.03`) rather than one scalar; validation has zero such rows. No response field was requested by the metadata reader. The single-anchor unit parser now explicitly excludes multi-anchor or invalid/missing identity records before eligibility; their counts are recorded in the freeze. This is a schema correction before outcomes, not response-based filtering or a new source. The failed command and correction are retained here rather than hidden as a successful freeze.
