## What the success means

The revised endpoint is legitimate as a declared **purchased two-observation repeatability task**: both measurements are charged, choices precede final conversion, and an unpaired scout positive is never counted as a repeat. It is not the discarded final-positive endpoint. That initial design was set-dominated and was rejected before freeze or numeric conversion; its failure is retained in the review/revision history.

However, scout positivity is itself part of the success definition. Selecting already positive scouts can improve repeat-positive acquisition through ordinary screening even without predicting final responses better. A homogeneous independent-read example with positive probability 0.6 gives approximately 0.12K adaptive repeats versus 0.108K fixed-pair repeats under these budget fractions. This analytical example is not an executed biological experiment. Thus the measured 43-repeat gain does not isolate transferable cell information, reliability calibration, a novel allocator or an inherited strategy.

## Strong simple explanation and threshold dependence

The explicitly post-result controls use the unchanged scout set, threshold, source observations and total budget. The source reconstruction independently reproduces their nominations and newly required selected final reads:

- Gate scouts at >0.10, then rank by the old original score: **898 repeats**, versus 919 for magnitude ranking. The control-minus-feedback interval is [-52, 11]; incremental value of scout magnitudes over this simpler control is unestablished. The cost-aware feedback-minus-control contrast is +0.928 repeats per 1,000 proxy wells, interval [-0.0096, 1.877].
- Gate scouts at >0.10, then hash-order: **869 repeats**; control-minus-feedback -50, interval [-80, -21]. This post-result descriptive contrast does not rescue novelty or demonstrate a latent biological mechanism.

At the predeclared >0 sensitivity threshold, feedback has **1,061** repeats and paired direct **1,409**. Any scout policy here has at most 1,078 paired finals, so it cannot reach 1,409 at this budget. Threshold choice therefore materially changes the usefulness of scouting; it is not a minor robustness footnote. The root kept the frozen primary result and labelled this reversal rather than changing the threshold or budget.

The gate-plus-original control was proposed after primary outcomes were known. It cannot be substituted for a frozen confirmation arm or presented as having settled equality; its interval permits modest positive or negative differences. It does show that the observed result currently has a strong ordinary explanation, and no distinctive core has emerged.

A restored binding does not erase the failed earlier check.

The validation subset was selected in the original study; source files are already exposed. Barcode separation demonstrates distinct recorded plates, not independent cultures or absence of shared fitted monotherapy terms. SIDM clusters preserve observed within-line contributions but do not resolve cross-line drug effects or source preprocessing dependence. Count bootstraps also do not preserve equal cost in each resample, which is why the additional own-cost sensitivity is useful; that sensitivity still does not rerun acquisition in a new population. Hashes and local timestamps support these record bindings only, not secure blinding or repaired global provenance.

No AZ/reserve was accessed, no model was fitted, no new wet experiment was run and no production implementation was changed by this audit. The new verifier is post-freeze audit code; it does not rewrite the frozen experiment.

## Minimum next decision

Retain the ordinary positive-scout filter plus original ranking as a strong comparator/component. Do not tune this exposed pool, add editors, open the AZ reserve or promote certification infrastructure to obtain a stronger narrative from the same result. A later distinct application must first justify why this precise repeatability endpoint is useful, exhibit headroom against the simple gated comparator under all-in costs, and specify a genuinely distinguishable prediction or decision mechanism. That new task would require its own protected later observations and comparisons; the present pass alone does not authorize a claim of world, agent, inheritance, joint or improved-improver benefit.
