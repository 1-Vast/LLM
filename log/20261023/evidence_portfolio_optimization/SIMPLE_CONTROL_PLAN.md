# Post-result simple controls

The frozen primary replay has completed and its results are already known. This is explicitly a post-result diagnostic, not an independent confirmation or a revision of the frozen rule. The statistical reviewer identified that selecting a positive scout can increase the two-read repeatability endpoint even with no predictive information about final status. For example, homogeneous independent positivity probability 0.6 yields expected 0.12K repeats with 0.4K scouts/0.2K selected positive scouts, versus 0.108K for 0.3K fixed pairs (ignoring small finite-pool shortages).

Use the unchanged metadata pool, role observations, scouts, threshold 0.10 and total read budget. Add two simple ordinary controls: filter scouts positive at 0.10 then rank by original score; filter scouts positive then use a fixed hash order. In either policy, if positives are insufficient, complete the same F purchases from the remaining scouts using that policy's fixed order. No model is fitted. Persist both choices before converting their newly required final response fields; do not modify or rerun the frozen primary study. Detailed artifacts remain in `data/processed/evidence_portfolio_optimization_20261023/`.

Report repeat-positive counts at 0.10 and 0, same-cost differences versus feedback, and SIDM-cluster conditional bootstrap intervals with the same 2,000-draw seed. These are descriptive mechanism checks after exposure, without a new pass gate. A tie with the original-score gated control favors that simpler policy. Even an incremental magnitude-ranking gain establishes no novel world model, agent, inheritance or RSI mechanism. No reserve or new wet experiment is used.
