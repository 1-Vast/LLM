# Independent functional-mechanism frontier: return-path feedback

Decision: **NONE qualified for a new mechanism experiment in this bounded review.** This is a new original-methods assessment, not a repetition of the Norman attenuation diagnostic or an AML/ALL asset search. The prior STOP on causal promotion of shared attenuation was read and is retained.

## New primary evidence

Lee et al., *Sequential transcriptional waves and NF-κB-driven chromatin remodeling direct drug-induced dedifferentiation in cancer*, Nature Communications, published 15 April 2026, DOI **10.1038/s41467-026-71349-4**, PMID 41986344, PMC13083995. Original full-text XML is saved as GET2_primary.xml; GET1_europepmc.json is the targeted primary-study discovery response. Two GETs total. No matrices, source workbooks, protected values, or author code were retrieved/run.

The original experiment exposes M397 melanoma cells to 3 μM vemurafenib for 29 days, then either continues exposure through day 59 or washes out for 35 days. It measures a forward/reverse transcriptomic loop and distinguishes early chromatin-related and late lineage-related waves. Forward day 21 and reverse day 17 have similar late-module scores but different early-module scores. RNA-seq has one biological sample/time point, and ATAC-seq similarly has one; some ChIP assays have two. These are actual exposure/washout observations, not counterfactual values reconstructed from single perturbations.

Functional measurements exist: 72-hour CellTiter-Glo dose response with at least three biological replicates; washout/rechallenge is reported to recover sensitivity close to untreated cells. Long-term clonogenic assays test vemurafenib with RelA inhibitor JSH-23, KDM5B inhibitor CPI-455, or HDAC inhibitor quisinostat across five melanoma lines. Staining is stopped when vemurafenib monotherapy shows clear regrowth; its time is therefore a treatment/control-dependent stopping rule, not a uniform fixed-time outcome. Colony area is not automatically an absolute viable-cell count. The study also has cell-count assays under ROS manipulations. These distinctions matter to any proposed functional prediction target.

Data availability authenticates GEO GSE255671, ArrayExpress E-MTAB-5493 for other time-series lines, and an attached Source Data file. It does not by itself authenticate a dense joined functional-dose-time-return-path table. No claim that such a table is absent globally is made; it was not established in these original methods. No further dataset qualification loop is proposed.

## The single mechanism examined

Application: predict how a drug holiday changes sensitivity to vemurafenib, rather than merely predicting RNA after exposure. The strongest concrete lead suggested by the paper is **use real exposure–washout feedback to update a fast/slow transition model, preserving the distinction between early stress/chromatin memory and late lineage state, and transfer that update to sensitivity forecasts after an unseen holiday duration**.

Its falsifiable claim would be: a constrained update of the transition law from one complete exposure–washout cycle improves held-out rechallenge dose-response prediction and drug-holiday selection across new durations/lines, beyond updating a history-aware direct functional predictor with identical observations. A static current-dose predictor is insufficient as the principal opponent: a direct model must receive the same exposure history, washout duration, and measured modules. Endpoint return of sensitivity alone does not identify the entire return map, a persistent memory variable, or a transfer law.

The exact operation needed to distinguish this from ordinary model refitting is not presently supplied by this lead. The original paper already specifies a two-module ODE, dg/dt=b+Mg, fits forward and reverse transition coefficients, and excludes faster-than-sampling oscillations. It also augments the last observation with up to three hypothetical steady-state points; those are fitting assumptions, not independent observations and cannot become free labels for our updater. Calling the same two-mode ODE, a replay buffer, or a return-path loss a new inherited update mechanism would not establish a substantive difference. Conversely, use of ordinary ODE components alone would not veto a genuinely new restricted update operation: the missing item is a specified operation and a reason it should improve functional generalization at finite feedback budget.

## Minimum discriminating check and fair opponents

Before any claim, an authenticated functional feedback design would need measured sensitivity at intermediate forward and reverse phases, including histories with similar late state but different early state, followed by a truly held-out rechallenge. The minimal check asks whether the proposed updated state predicts that future functional difference beyond a history-aware direct model. RNA reconstruction or recovery of the known endpoint is not sufficient. No new wet-lab observations are presumed available or authorized as already acquired.

If a distinct update operation is later specified, equal-information/cost comparison must include: strong direct sequence prediction; ordinary full/refined refitting of the same two-mode model; replay/regularized continual learning; active acquisition using the same feedback menu; meta-learning the update; simple static/dynamic model mixtures; and equal-budget search over program/update candidates. Each gets identical actual outcomes, temporal history, observation costs, hypothetical-label restrictions, and tuning budget. Held-out holiday-duration/line functional errors and decision regret would test world-model usefulness. Agent acquisition, inheritance to a fresh line, and improvements in later update efficiency would require separate comparisons. No result in this paper supplies those claims for our framework.

## Success, stop, and next action

Success would require a specified non-equivalent operator, lower held-out functional prediction error/decision regret than the strongest matched opponent, and reproducible benefit across independent feedback episodes. Stop if only RNA fit improves, if the entire benefit disappears against a history-aware direct/refit model, if phase matching needs query outcomes, or if success depends on hypothetical steady-state labels or treatment-dependent endpoint relabeling.

Current next executable mechanism check: **NONE**. The fresh evidence supports biologically real history dependence and an interpretable functional task, but does not identify a new update operation or show a legal functional feedback table sufficient to test one. Do not fit the existing ODE, add a gate, download a large matrix, or reopen earlier stopped sources merely to create a positive result. Two purposeful GETs were used; the unused third request is not a reason to broaden the search.
