## What is actually being tested

Application: prioritize a separately assayed apoptosis readout after an already purchased genetic perturbation Cell Painting assay. The narrow hypothesis is that within-well morphology variation contains reproducible information about independent condition-level apoptosis beyond median profiles. The target is an independent assay **condition expectation**, not the fate of an imaged cell. Condition matching is sufficient for this hypothesis; same-cell health matching is unnecessary. It cannot establish individual-cell mediation, future fate, intervention dynamics, drug efficacy, a feedback updater or RSI.

The author reports 59 genes,119 guides,3 lines and357 consensus conditions. The measured biological unit for prediction is guide×line; dependence/inference must group the guides of a gene. Wells and cells are measurement replicates, not extra independent examples. Independent panels cannot be joined by cell/well/replicate ID. The original public drug-health predictions are not truth for this diagnostic.

## Minimum experiment and exact contrasts

1. **Retrospective reference.** Reconstruct published per-well median features and author consensus as an anchor. Reproduction tolerance and deviations are recorded. This anchor is not the causal contrast because author MODZ weights and feature selection may themselves use different profile geometry.
2. **Matched acquisition/aggregation contrast.** For every arm use the identical wells and identical selected cells, same compartments/features, QC and plate controls. Let m_w be each well's coordinate medians; let d_w concatenate coordinate q10 and q90 minus m_w. These are fixed marginal dispersion/tail summaries, not a copula or full distribution. Aggregate both m and d by the same equal-well mean within guide×line. Do not pool all cells across wells: that would weight wells by abundance. No label-dependent MODZ weights or target-specific cell filtering. Original952 median features are retained in the strong median arm; new features are selected/standardized from source training only.
3. **Median-only strong control.** Train elastic net on all available median coordinates, plus a competent nonlinear kernel-ridge predictor with nested gene-grouped tuning. Same line covariates, cell/well count covariates, control references and allowable metadata are present in every arm. Guide/gene IDs and assay infection-efficiency measurements are omitted from all arms unless bought and declared identically. Keep counts outside d so a morphology-tail claim cannot be a cell-loss/count-only claim.
4. **Equal dimension/equal downstream model contrast.** A source-training-only projection gives m and d the same predeclared width k (e.g.16, fixed before endpoint decoding). Compare ridge on [m_k,d_k] against ridge on [m_k,b_k(m)] where b is a fixed nonlinear median feature map with k coordinates. Both32-dimensional designs have identical regularization grids, fit counts and nested splits. Also report full-median952 ridge/elastic-net/kernel baselines. A gain against the32-dimensional median map alone is insufficient if full-median direct models absorb it. Projection and fixed-map choices are not retuned on outer residuals.
5. **Same raw information direct control.** A standard set/distribution predictor gets exactly the same cells/wells, no extra labels or microscopy. A small fixed random-feature mean embedding with ridge is an appropriate ordinary comparator; one modest set network may be included only within the shared tuning/compute cap. These controls can consume distribution information, so beating median does not mean beating direct prediction. If an ordinary same-input distribution predictor captures the signal, report a useful feature-information result and stop any new-world mechanism claim.

The smallest executable version needs reference + strong median elastic net/kernel + equal-width ridge pair + ordinary raw-set embedding. No LLM, agent editor, world simulator or feedback loop is needed. Do not count additional summary coordinates as free capacity: use matched widths, actual effective degrees of freedom where available, identical tuning opportunities, and the full-median anchor. A noise augmentation can check numerical artifacts but is not a strong primary comparator. Cellwise independent-coordinate permutation preserves marginals and only tests joint dependence; it cannot refute a q10/q90 mechanism. No such copula claim is proposed.

## Budget and normalization

Both median and distribution summaries are computed from the same SQLite payload and fixed cell sample, even if released medians are cheaper to download. Report separately: assay cost shared, cells/wells shared, single-cell storage/processing cost, and model fit/tuning cost. A deployed median assay may not retain single-cell tables, so a cost-performance benefit is not presumed. Prefer all authenticated eligible cells with equal well weighting for the primary matched-input experiment; a source-chosen fixed cell budget can be a later robustness test, not another chance to select an effect. If cell count is correlated with the endpoint, all arms get counts and a count-only baseline.

Plate feature normalization may use same-assay EMPTY controls available at deployment; query endpoint labels cannot normalize query inputs. Fit global scalers, feature removal, projection and imputation only inside each source-training split. Released target normalization may use all labels on each plate. If this cannot be reconstructed from lawful controls, label the task retrospective prediction of released normalized phenotypes, with no sparse-feedback/online interpretation. Do not estimate cross-assay error covariance from unmatched wells. Quantiles can reflect segmentation or batch artifacts; require control-well stability and avoid calling them biological subpopulations without separate evidence.

## Holdout and inference

Primary development is gene-blocked outer evaluation within the declared source lines, with all guides, lines and wells for one gene kept in one outer fold. Inner tuning uses the same gene grouping. Cell lines can be stratification variables, but each gene still belongs to one fold across all lines. Use a single deterministic predeclared assignment, not the best of many seed assignments. Because gene-blocked CV uses overlapping training sets, its folds are not independent biological replications.

Reserve a locked gene set before outcome inspection for the one confirmation comparison, only if59 genes leave enough source genes for training/tuning and the predeclared uncertainty criterion. Otherwise stop at exploratory evidence. For a later cross-background question, hold out one entire cell line and train on the other two; if claiming both new-gene and new-line transfer, also exclude held-out test genes from the two training lines. The resulting test is small. Three line holdouts do not create a rich sequence of inherited tasks; do not average them as independent RSI generations. Original authors already performed line holdout, so it is a control/robustness property, not novelty.

Report condition MSE and per-gene averaged paired squared-error difference, with gene-level resampling for uncertainty. Line-specific scores, coverage, counts and plate membership expose whether pooled gains are driven by one line or one batch. If replicate labels cannot identify batch confounding, say so rather than fabricate a batch-held-out proof. A triage ranking metric may be predeclared secondary, but with gene interventions it does not demonstrate small-molecule decision benefit. No statistical test over individual cells/wells is permitted as evidence of59 independent gene effects.

Resource-screen success criterion to freeze before endpoint decoding: at least5% lower held-out MSE than the strongest source-selected median model, a gene-level95% interval for paired error difference wholly in the beneficial direction, and no gain explained by counts alone or different aggregation/measurement budgets.5% is a proposed practical threshold, not a literature-derived constant; root may choose another before payload access. Small locked sample may make this unattainable, which is an honest resource stop. Do not pick another endpoint, quantile, cell fraction or split after failing.

If distribution gain fails, stop this instantiated tail-summary hypothesis without declaring all state distributions useless. If matched acquisition or joint identity cannot be qualified, stop the asset branch before a fit. If the equal-width contrast wins but a competent full-median predictor does as well, no established summary insufficiency. If distribution input wins against median but ordinary same-information set prediction explains it, retain a static independent-function diagnostic and decline the novel world/updater claim. If a signal survives, investigate a specified reproducible functional prediction error on genuinely later tasks before inventing a transferable update restriction. Nothing in this diagnostic establishes world, agent, inheritance, joint or improved-improver gains.
