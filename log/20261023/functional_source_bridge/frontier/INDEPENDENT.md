## New primary evidence, not performance reproduction

Three HTTP requests, all successful, with retained bodies and receipts: one official arXiv title query and the official original HTML for TTT-E2E, arXiv:2512.23675v2, and Centaur, arXiv:2503.11650v1. These are 2025 originals, distinct from the previously read CLAW, HarnessTTT and ExpEvolve. This bounded lookup is not an exhaustive novelty search. Paper performance has not been reproduced.

TTT-E2E, Section 2.1, Equation 2 performs W_t = W_(t-1) - eta grad ell_t(W_(t-1)), where ell is the actual next-token cross-entropy. Section 2.2, Equations 3-4 averages sequential next-token losses after previous updates and differentiates that sequence objective with respect to the initialization. Section 2.3, Equations 5-6 replaces single-token updates with minibatches for parallelism and stability. Therefore the concrete transferable operation is **optimize the deployed post-update objective, through the actual update**, not simply retain parameter changes or an updater name. This paper is not evidence that any auxiliary loss gives the same effect. In cells, RNA prediction and drug efficacy are different targets; a gradient through RNA adaptation still requires historical true functional query labels to train/validate its effect on function. That distinction is the central missing biological contract.

Centaur, Section 2 and Equation 3 initially distills candidate trajectory score features from an expert algorithm, typically simulator/world-model generated. Section 2.1 selects M candidates, chooses five direction anchors, assigns trajectories to the nearest anchor in L2 distance, sums predicted scores within clusters, normalizes those sums and minimizes Shannon entropy. Section 2.2 buffers F=4 historical-frame gradients and applies their mean in one update; the current-frame gradient is not used immediately. The implementation uses M=100 and only updates the score decoder, keeping perception frozen. Consequently this is not a learned recurrent improvement strategy and not evidence for Level 3 RSI.

Two transfer qualifications matter. First, driving trajectories have a defined geometric neighborhood and behavior directions; chemical similarity or target sharing does not establish the same functional-neighborhood property in cells. Second, the exact Section 2.1 candidate-sampling sentence says it prioritizes candidates with high average score **on the test set as per the expert algorithm**. The retrieved supplementary text did not resolve the timing/source of those aggregate scores. This is an information dependency to disclose and match, not a misconduct claim. We must never translate that operation into a drug candidate menu using hidden future functional labels. Its Figure 4 discussion also explicitly describes a failure where TTT cannot suppress a confident original prediction. Entropy alone is not a truth-feedback signal.

## Candidate decision

No new positive cell-to-function sharing law was established by these sources. A sequence loss differentiated through adaptation remains a useful strong comparator, not our contribution.

This changes the next action concretely: qualify a source with independent historical episodes containing pre-feedback cell state, actually observed functional support labels, and separate functional queries before selecting a reusable update restriction. Do not accept RNA-only residual improvement or drug-family score entropy as the eligibility signal. A summary-function catalog can test a supervised updater component, but does not automatically supply the state/function contract or dynamics.

If such a source is authenticated, the narrow first test is whether lawful state information predicts **the effect of a fixed functional-support update on unseen functional queries**, beyond equal-information empirical Bayes and direct episodic prediction. We should not yet build a learned editor. No particular dataset is nominated here, because none was newly authenticated in this review. This is a source requirement, not a numerical GO or a claim that the full goal is blocked.

## Required informed competitors and finite gate

For any later proposal, use exactly the same historical episodes, pre-query covariates, support acquisition and hidden-query split for: (1) strong direct model with state/support input, (2) ordinary full refit and continuous update, (3) conditional empirical-Bayes posterior correction, K(q)=Cov(e,r|q)[Cov(r|q)+R]^-1, (4) end-to-end meta-trained initialization with actual functional query loss, (5) random and Bayesian/active support choice, (6) fixed state/function-model mixture and (7) same-total-cost updater/program search. Include state-model pretraining, outer-loop training, candidate generation, API and source-selection costs; retain method-specific effects and any mismatch explicitly.

A new restriction must have its own exact operation and cell-grounded reason before seeing query values. Prespecified support/query episodes must be grouped by donor/lineage/assay dependence, with enough independent units for its claimed effect; repeats are not extra tasks. STOP the particular restriction if it loses to the strongest informed ordinary model on the frozen functional decision measure, or if the apparent advantage vanishes after matching information/cost. A new posterior kernel alone is not a new contribution. If the restriction survives, separately test state-model contribution, adaptive-agent choice and inheritance, then cross-history change in the improver at fixed descendant budget. Positive adaptation does not imply RSI.
