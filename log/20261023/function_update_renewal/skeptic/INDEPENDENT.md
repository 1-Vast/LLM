## Why the tempting mechanism is presently insufficient

Suppose source tasks learn a correction covariance or context map, and a later functional residual updates a context vector. If `y=f(x)+b(x)^T z+epsilon`, Gaussian residual inference or ridge in z is already a reusable update law. An inherited learned optimizer can be genuinely useful; calling it ordinary does not logically invalidate operational improved-improver capability. But no new operation beyond that comparator has been supplied. A nonlinear router among state/head/representation updates remains conventional meta-learning until its distinctive constraint/estimator and advantage are demonstrated.

One scalar residual cannot distinguish latent drug susceptibility, baseline error, assay offset and measurement artifact without source-learned structure or additional observations. Low-dimensional cell-context inference may fail when mechanism-specific response loadings change across contexts. Acquiring expression data merely to infer a direct scalar phenotype adds an expensive measurement that the direct predictor must also receive in a fair comparison; an RNA benefit alone does not establish a dynamical-world necessity.

## Application/data feasibility and the next discriminating evidence

The relevant practical application is **adaptive discovery of functional perturbation combinations across independently measured cancer-cell backgrounds**, a target-combination discovery proxy, not measured drug efficacy. This needs multiple real screens/cell backgrounds with common candidate identities and actual measured combination functions; splitting Norman's one map repeatedly is insufficient.

TDAL App. C names SCHEMATIC (Fong 2025), Simpson K562 and Horlbeck Jurkat among public combinatorial functional assets. Numerical margins must follow the selected application and measurement uncertainty, not be fabricated here.

## Inheritance and recursive test that would count

An inherited context-fit state is knowledge/model inheritance. A stored source-trained rule mapping legal feedback to an update is program/capability inheritance. A stored proposer or rule-selection policy updated by outcomes of previous improvement attempts is a candidate improved improver. These are different artifacts and must be checkpointed with applicability and rollback.

For Level 3 compare I0 and I1 from the **same fresh downstream model/program state and same initial history** on genuine subsequent tasks. Give both the same source experiences and count source learning plus deployment costs; allow fixed search and ordinary learned optimization competent training. Measure improvement search efficiency (validated useful programs/functional utility versus cumulative total cost), not merely I1's better inherited predictor. I1 may belong to ordinary meta-learning and still show operational improved-improver capability. That does not by itself establish a novel algorithm or world-model necessity. A claimed recursive sequence needs subsequent independent tasks, not resampling/seeds as generations. World, agent, inheritance, joint and improved-improver effects remain separately unestablished.
