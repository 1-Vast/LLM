## Primary1: MeRLOT, actual support-driven hidden/function updates

Wonjoon Goo and Scott Niekum, **Local Nonparametric Meta-Learning**, arXiv2002.03272v1,9February2020. Actual official HTML https://arxiv.org/html/2002.03272v1,266923bytes,SHA2563c82b3d722d49fdde8e083290d858ea470c4f66835d38438f3a0039e70443629. Read section3's meta-objective and functional-update description, section4 including equations10–13, architecture and the explicit training loss/step settings; selectively read task motivation, not full experiments/proofs or author code.

Equation1 defines context C={(x_i,y_i)} and query T={(x_j,y_j)}, minimizing sum of query losses for predictions Phi(x_j;C). The source explicitly describes its functional update, originally attributed there to MetaFun, as

    u_i(t) = u(x_i,y_i,r_xi(t)),
    delta r_x(t) = sum_{i in C} k(x_i,x) u_i(t),
    r_x(t+1) = r_x(t) - alpha delta r_x(t).

Crucial actual text: the update u_i is calculated with a parameterized neural network, “not a formal derivative”; u,k,decoder are jointly optimized with equation1. MeRLOT then IMPLEMENTS these finite updates separately for each context-seeded local functional representation r_x^i(0)=psi(c_i)(x), combines local predictions with context-conditioned attention, and trains psi,u,k,decoder with query NLL or l2. The source specifies T=3 and alpha=.01. This is direct primary evidence of a local rule driven by labeled support/context plus current hidden representation, trained end-to-end for held-out-query performance without an auxiliary true-gradient imitation objective. It is stronger operational proximity than SIB's query-transductive classifier update. We did not fetch the MetaFun original and do not falsely count that citation as another original read; the MeRLOT paper directly specifies and uses the recurrence.

This precedent does NOT establish exact equality with A+=eta e tanh(P[k;u;b]) k' and b+=eta_b e h. The published state is a query-indexed functional representation and multiple context-seeded functions, not the candidate's finite parameter matrix A and head b. Its updater inputs (context x,y,current r) do not automatically equal the candidate's key/preactivation/head input with a residual multiplier. The kernel may be nonlinear and context-dependent; the teacher's finite-rank support transfer under fixed keys has a distinct restriction already audited historically. Copying those missing details into a MeRLOT comparator would be a constructed control, not a claim those exact details were published.

The real published structural contribution is local nonparametric representation: capacity can grow with context and local functions rather than a fixed-size global task encoding, with intended scale/range shift robustness. Its source-reported benchmark benefits are not reproduced. This does not give it free runtime/storage against a fixed-size teacher. Local copies, context attention, query evaluations, all source training and unroll steps must be counted. Conversely the compact teacher cannot claim a finite-cost advantage merely by calling MeRLOT nonparametric; one must measure or derive the relevant cost/accuracy tradeoff at the same legal context/query information. No such new advantage is supplied here.

## Primary2: actual sparse-feedback plasticity optimization

Official HTML https://arxiv.org/html/2512.09366v2,186337bytes,SHA25692bbf40e22c4765378b8ad6e8c4e1a27bf7f5015d1536ade840dbe63cf071636. Its local eligibility evolves as a learned degree5 polynomial of pre/post activity followed by decay: de_ij/dt=sum_{k,l}theta_kl r_j^k (xbar_i-x_i)^l - e_ij/tau. At episode end a stochastic weight write has mean eta e_ij (R-Rbar) and matrix-normal additive noise. Outer optimization targets future cumulative reward across learning episodes, using a REINFORCE-inspired score estimator for stochastic weight writes and forward-propagated state, trace, eligibility and weight sensitivities. No true-backprop-gradient matching target is introduced in the inspected objective. Its computational motivation is avoiding reverse differentiation through hundreds of learning trials; that is a concrete algorithmic choice, not proof of a free or exact gradient. It carries tangent states and stochastic-estimator variance and does not establish unconditional lower total cost than reverse/truncated alternatives.

This is an actual learned three-factor local feedback rule and distinguishes plasticity-meta-parameters from synaptic weights, but it is NOT a held-out-label regression teacher or the candidate's exact deterministic support residual write.
