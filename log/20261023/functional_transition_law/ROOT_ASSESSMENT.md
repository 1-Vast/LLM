## Three concrete attempts and why they do not yet advance a new core

1. Shared death-hazard onset correction across doses: a real biological timing hypothesis, but fitting a common lag is exactly ordinary delay-parameter refitting. Net counts cannot distinguish equal birth/death replacement from zero birth/death. Requires genuine death increments and risk exposure, not ATP or net counts alone.
2. Post-commitment cohort-age death kernel: a more specific biological factorization, but the proposed nonnegative prior-regularized least-squares update is exactly ordinary constrained survival/deconvolution fitting. Commitment timing versus execution delay is not identified by the same viable-count trajectory. Irreversibility alone does not imply execution timing is independent of continued treatment.
3. Velocity/curvature innovation gain: the full stated operation is ordinary linear conditional/LMMSE updating. An exact stipulated moment check gives risk18 for unregularized refit and4.5 for the source-prior rule, but the same-information ordinary linear predictor also gives4.5. Exact Bayesian posterior equality additionally requires Gaussian/linear-conditional-mean assumptions, as clarified in a separately sealed cross-review addendum. Increasing target curvature variance to9 while retaining the source gain gives22.5 and reverses the advantage over18; this is not the risk of target-aware optimal Bayes. This is an algebraic necessary-condition result, not measured cellular performance or inherited-improver evidence.

These are exact-operation objections to these implementations, not a ban on conventional mathematics, nonlinear models, useful biological restrictions or a finite source-only experiment. A familiar method family does not in itself defeat novelty. No claim that a novel rule is impossible follows from these failures.

- No repeatable functional error has yet yielded a distinctive update rule that outperforms strong same-information ordinary prediction/refitting.
- Extra latent mechanisms require observable discriminating information. Counts, ATP, death events, commitment and births are different measurements; a hidden-state story does not supply the missing channels.
- Exposed-source replay and random seeds do not supply independent later tasks for inheritance or recursive-improver claims. Prior CDK Middle-feedback and matched-function tests have already been run, with weak/unstable or adverse functional results; the pasted external analysis predates them and is not a reason to rerun that branch as fresh.
