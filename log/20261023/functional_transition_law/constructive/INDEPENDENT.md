For one cell context and fixed functional horizon, let c_t be newly committed cells at actual time t, d_t newly dead cells, b_t new births, and n_t viable cells before death. ## Exact falsifiers and identifiability

1. **Additional readout can identify a kernel.** If an independently observed synchronous commitment cohort has size N and c_0=N,c_t=0 later, with background removed and actual new-death counts available, d_a/N=k_a directly. This gives an executable inference and clear measurement requirement; it is constructed algebra, not an acquired cell experiment.
2. **Viable counts alone do not supply that readout.** One observed death series can be explained by immediate commitment with a delayed kernel or delayed commitment with immediate death. For unit impulses, c_0=1,k_1=1 and c_1=1,k_0=1 both produce d_1=1. Equal viable/birth/death time series cannot identify the commitment/execution split without further lawful measurements or fixed assumptions. Thus adding a hidden c_t and fitting the same live-count likelihood cannot validate the proposed mechanism.
3. **True washout reversibility refutes the restriction.** If the candidate marker reverts and the same supposedly committed cohort survives after drug withdrawal, the assumed irreversible-entry transport is wrong. If execution hazard still depends on drug present after commitment, sharing k across dose histories is wrong. These are central falsifiers, not conditions for adding a rescue gate after outcomes.
4. **Population balance can fail.** A retained dead-cell stock with degradation is not newly dead flux; absent births make count decreases insufficient to infer death. Raw ATP, projected area and segmentation count do not become interchangeable. Unknown dead clearance or marker-to-commitment mapping blocks this particular mechanistic test.
