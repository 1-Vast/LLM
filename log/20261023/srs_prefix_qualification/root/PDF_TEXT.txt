Note S1. Instructions for device fabrication.  
Materials: 
 PLA filament 
 25×60 mm glass coverslips 
 19-gauge stainless steel tubing with 90° bend (autoclaved, McMaster-Carr) 
 Polydimethylsiloxane (PDMS, Dow Chemical Company) 
 Sterile PBS 
 75% ethanol 
 UV light source 
Equipment: 
 Bambu Lab P1P 3D printer  
 Autodesk Fusion 360 or compatible CAD software 
Instructions: 
 3D Model Preparation 
 Download the supplied file “PerfusionChamber.stl”. 
 Open the file in Autodesk Fusion 360 to verify dimensions and design integrity.  
 Export the design as an STL file for 3D printing. 
 3D Printing 
 Load PLA filament into your 3D printer of choice equipped with a 0.2- or 0.4-mm hot end. 
 Print the chamber using the exported STL file. Confirm that layers adhere properly and the 
chamber is structurally sound. 
o Recommended settings: print at >60% in-fill density, with >4 wall loops, and with an 
outer brim to ensure flat edges while printing. 
 Sterilization 
 Inside a sterile environment such as a biosafety cabinet, immerse the printed chamber in 75% 
ethanol. 
 Expose the device to UV light for 15 minutes to ensure sterility. Ensure ethanol has 
evaporated prior to next step. 
 Coverslip Assembly 
 Inside a sterile environment such as a biosafety cabinet, affix cleaned, sterile 25×60 mm glass 
coverslips to both the top and bottom of the chamber using degassed PDMS. Ensure an even, 
leak-free seal. 
 Tubing Addition 
 Prepare autoclaved 19-gauge stainless steel tubing with 90° bends for inlets and outlets. 
 Apply a thin layer of PDMS around the tubing and insert into the chamber ports. 
 Allow the PDMS to be cured at room temperature in a sterile environment for 72 hours to 
form a firm, leak-proof seal. 
 Final Rinse and Preparation 
 Thoroughly rinse the assembled chamber with sterile PBS before seeding cells.
