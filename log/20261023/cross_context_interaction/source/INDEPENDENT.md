## One honest later-context contract and its missing boundary

A possible ordinary predictive task is retrospective completion of released per-line GI scores in a held-out cell background, conditional on the publisher's completed full-screen scoring system. Source lines and held-out lines can be grouped prospectively by lineage; all pair/guide/mirror repeats must stay grouped. This need not prove molecular mechanism truth or have full cell trajectories to be a legitimate small functional prediction test. It is cancer genetic-target interaction prioritization, not direct measured drug-combination efficacy, clinical safety or arbitrary dose/sequence intervention planning.

Such a score-label replay cannot claim sparse wet-lab savings: masking an already full-screen-normalized score matrix does not undo its construction. To claim paid sparse-function updating instead, first define a count/log-fitness endpoint using authenticated baseline/endpoint/control observations and only permitted purchased pairs. Charge shared screen preparation, control/reference constructs, calibration information and any source normalization. If using published scores, explicitly charge/condition on the full target screen. This is an observation-contract gap, not proof no count-based sparse endpoint could ever be constructed.

Single-effect-dependent attenuation already overlaps the published per-anchor expectation. Correlating single fitness with already single-residualized GI cannot alone identify a new biological transport law. A distinct predictive shared restriction could still have finite-sample value, but it needs specified forecasts beyond author-style allowed-data regression, hierarchy/pooling, conditional direct prediction, covariance/low-rank adaptation and ordinary refit. Same auxiliary hierarchy/DepMap features must be shared. Absence of paired RNA is not a veto on a functional response world; absence of an actual new useful restriction is the current algorithmic gap.

There are seven named backgrounds, not thousands of independent later tasks. Repeated guides, rows, tissue pools and masking seeds cannot inflate task count. A held-out lung pair of lines, for example, could test limited cross-lineage prediction, not broad multigeneration RSI or patient therapeutic index. A nonmalignant MCF10A comparator does not establish clinical safety. No split, success margin or numerical experiment is frozen here.
