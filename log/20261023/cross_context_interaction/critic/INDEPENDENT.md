## What a transferable restriction would actually assert

One might write kappa_ab,c=B(f_ab,c)-B(f_a,c)-B(f_b,c), then propose that kappa_ab,c is shared or low rank across contexts. The coordinate definition and source kappa estimate do not establish that cross-context restriction. Constant kappa transfer is ordinary transformed-outcome pair-intercept regression; kappa_ab,c=sum_r U_ab,r V_c,r is ordinary hierarchical/tensor factorization. Source doubles plus target singles can inform either, provided labels, normalization, gene features, target covariates and source selection costs are shared. A neural state name or recursive re-estimation does not distinguish the same operation.

Target singles need not determine target mixed-effect changes. A source-trained restriction can nonetheless predict such changes statistically; lack of uniquely identified latent biology is not a veto of a small held-out functional diagnostic. What must be named is the actual conditional stability constraint, the observed recurring error it repairs, and why its finite-feedback risk/selection cost differs from a strong direct hierarchical predictor. No such new restriction or advantage is established here. Choosing parallel/serial separately after seeing every target double would just use the prediction labels; source-selected mixtures and equally budgeted target-support model selection are lawful strong controls.

## Fair information and falsification contract

Target-support singles and paid few doubles must be disjoint from scored target doubles, with source-only/control-only endpoint construction or explicitly full-screen-conditioned scope. Pairs must remain grouped across orientations and replicate wells; named source/target lineage split, assay duration/KO versus activation and gene-menu overlap must be audited. Target single outcomes are informative paid functional observations and must be charged even when reused across many pairs. They do not automatically provide molecular states or drug effects.

At minimum compare a source-only direct predictor; identity/single-feature hierarchical pair factor or tensor predictor; ordinary pooled and target-refit updates; strong transformed parallel/serial/additive controls; simple mixtures selected within the same source/support budget; continual and active acquisition when sequential claims are made; and equal-total-budget search over these programs. Share real input covariates, paid observations, validation selections and source costs. Fixed-count fits are not necessarily equal compute. If a policy selects target doubles, its advantage must be separated from the predictor's advantage using common support packages or crossed policies. At seven contexts, leave-one-context-out performance uncertainty is substantial; pair-level resampling does not create independent new cell contexts.

Before a numerical diagnostic, freeze a practically meaningful function/ranking gain margin and stop conditions against the best ordinary predictor, label construction sensitivity and true source-to-target transport. Stop the claimed structural benefit if it is reproduced by the same transformed pair-factor refit, depends on whole-test outcome normalization, or disappears against source-selected mixtures/equal-budget search. Do not retroactively tune a threshold or opening more labels until this arbitrary coordinate wins. This report registers no experiment or data access.

A successful purely functional transfer law would support a functional world component, not uniquely identified molecular dynamics. World/state contribution needs same-information direct ablation; research-agent contribution needs a policy comparison; inherited U needs independent later contexts/common starts; improved-improver needs G1/G0 future improvement from the same artifacts/history/total budget. Familiar mathematics may provide finite-sample capability, but none of those benefits has been demonstrated by this source read.
