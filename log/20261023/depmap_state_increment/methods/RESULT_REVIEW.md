## Why not salvage or erase this result

Fixed mixture G has lower descriptive line MSE than the source-selected full procedure, but choosing G now as the primary procedure would use outcomes to change the frozen question. Even descriptive best-G versus the same restricted selected procedure remains a different estimand. E has the lowest observed regret, but switching to E on that basis similarly changes the selection objective after evaluation. Do not lower 5%, remove loss-bearing lines, revise top10, add QC-RBF or optimize blend weights on these read endpoints. Conversely the 5% threshold is a resource-allocation rule, not an assay-noise bound or biological validity test. It cannot turn 3.47% with coherent direction into 'biologically absent' or statistically proven failure of all RNA mechanisms.

Both menus use measured RNA panel context; the full menu also gets target-level state. This is a fixed-menu contrast, not RNA versus no RNA and not proof that target-specific information is necessary. Nonlinear QC, global context transfer, context-conditioned low-rank and other informed ordinary explanations were not exhausted. Shared targets, lineages and batches prevent reading fourteen wins as fourteen independent confirmations; batch sensitivity is a second split of the same data, not external replication. Seventy missing-function pairs dropped under the frozen rule also condition coverage. Exact same-pair public Chronos lookup remains the practical zero-error oracle for these historic scores.

The run reports 528 eigendecompositions, 2640 elementary lambda fits and 22 outer heldout fits in 211.922 seconds. Full/restricted menus have unequal selection opportunities. G reuses fitted components with independently chosen lambda values, rather than an evaluated joint 25-pair search. Costs and selected procedure frequencies should accompany the result. No fair equal-cost new-mechanism superiority is tested or established.
