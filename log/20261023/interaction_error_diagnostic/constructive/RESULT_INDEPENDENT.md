## Positive result: feedback calibrates score error

The strongest demonstrated gain is simple scalar calibration of source mean. Equal-context mean MSE falls from1.058929 to0.947277 at128 support (10.55%) and0.940790 at512 (11.16%). Calibration improves salt-averaged MSE in all five contexts at both budgets and is below both selected direct and selected identity-aware MSE in all ten context/budget aggregates. These are arithmetic comparisons of frozen held-query outcomes, not a posthoc validation-selected deployable super-family. Source mean remains a useful low-cost ranking control: calibration preserves its bottom20 ranking, with mean regret3.284751 at both budgets. Feedback utility is real for score estimation; better decision ranking has not followed.

More feedback improves the selected ordinary recipes: identity mean MSE1.048857→0.948982 and regret3.832732→3.347103; score-direct MSE1.016490→0.948169 and regret3.610862→3.415544. Common within-salt queries make these budget contrasts meaningful. They do not distinguish extra data from inherited update knowledge; no model update or strategy is transferred between tasks here.

## Identity structure: retain local512 observations, reject broad advancement

The frozen gate fails: joint MSE-and-regret wins in0/5 contexts at128 and2/5 at512. Mean identity MSE is worse than score-direct at both budgets (128:1.048857 vs1.016490;512:0.948982 vs0.948169). At512 identity mean regret3.347103 improves over score-direct3.415544 (~2.00%), but still trails source mean/calibration3.284751. Relative source-mean improvement cannot be used to bypass the strong ordinary controls.

At512 the retained salt-averaged wins are MCF7 (MSE gain0.007240, regret gain0.000523) and MCF10A (0.004503,0.266893). MCF7's tiny ranking advantage changes sign across masks, and both contexts' MSE identity advantage changes sign across masks. MCF10A decision regret improves in each mask (0.397367 and0.136418); CAL27 also improves regret in each mask (0.027183,0.015153) while losing MSE in each. MDAMB231 improves regret in each mask but MSE is mask-unstable. CAL33 loses both metrics in each mask. Thus limited ordinary ranking structure survives at512, strongest descriptively in MCF10A, but no context shows simultaneous two-metric improvement in both masks. Do not erase these partial results, select a favorable context as confirmation, or promote them to a new cross-context update law.

Endpoint-unseen query fraction drops from51.58–51.87% at128 to4.22–6.63% at512. This supports an interpolation/coverage interpretation of the budget transition but is not a causal demonstration that coverage caused the gains. Better model selection with larger validation sets is another possible explanation; the design does not isolate it. Identity recipes choose strong-shrinkage joint ridge frequently at512. Permuted support labels are worse in aggregate, especially for512 decision regret, showing dependence on lawful feedback rather than support IDs alone; a single fixed permutation and context-level exceptions at128 do not establish statistical significance or biological identity causality.

## Decision

STOP expansion/promotion of this exact shared-endpoint recipe under the frozen gate. Retain ordinary calibration as a positive baseline and record the narrow512 ranking leads as descriptive development evidence. No extra fitting, larger search, lung opening, new architecture, confirmatory subgroup or revised endpoint is justified by this result alone. A future functional-update hypothesis would need an independently motivated restriction and a new prospectively assigned evaluation contract; it cannot inherit novelty from the fact that feedback helped scalar calibration.

Published completed-screen GI error remains conditional on normalization and finite availability; neither ordinary gain nor identity coefficient separates biological response changes from scoring/library effects. No absolute growth, drug efficacy, sparse physical-assay savings, world-model contribution, agent contribution, inheritance or improved-improver/RSI gain was tested.
