## Scientific consequence

The frozen endpoint-shared error lead gate fails. Stop this exact support/model/selection recipe as a promoted reusable law, without opening lung backgrounds or tuning a replacement on exposed outcomes. This is an executed ordinary development diagnostic; lack of novelty did not prevent learning something concrete.

The two512joint wins versus the registered MSE-selected source-score direct pipeline must be retained as actual local comparisons, not erased by the aggregate failure. They are MCF7 andMCF10A. They do not establish practical superiority: in both contexts identity-aware MSE is worse than the prespecified scalar calibration control, and ranking regret is worse than source mean. Across all five contexts at both budgets, scalar calibration has lower salt-averaged MSE than the identity-aware arm. Its achieved bottom20score equals source mean's in the saved cases, so this calibration gain is score prediction/calibration value without a ranking gain. No additional selection or model was fitted for this observation.

The source-direct comparator was tuned by validation MSE, not by ranking regret. Therefore a win against it is a win against the registered pipeline, not the strongest separately ranking-tuned predictor. Identity and nonlinear methods are ordinary models. Endpoint-sharing gains or losses cannot identify cellular interaction change; full-panel score construction remains a plausible contributor.
