## New source 1: HCC paired culture format / selective drug response

Wong et al., *Patient-derived organoids inform pharmacogenomic vulnerabilities in liver cancer*, JHEP Reports2025, DOI10.1016/j.jhepr.2025.101426, PMID40584266, PMC12205652. Actual original full text obtained from NCBI BioC; saved XML/TXT and HTTP hash receipt. No supplement payload or response matrix downloaded. The paper text contains exposed published aggregate results; they are not experiment scores.

The abstract's 23 established PDTOs must not become 23 complete drug-screen tasks. The results specify **10 tumor organoids, 8 tumor-derived monolayer cell lines, 7 non-tumor organoids, 3 normal-liver organoids and 5 ATCC HCC lines** in the 100-drug screen (85 FDA-approved+15 pipeline). Eight monolayers originate from the same tissue as corresponding organoids (literal IDs670,688,720,744,775,813,877,886). Same-donor formats are paired measurements, not independent patients. Two independent screen runs/model and early/late passage concordance are reported, not independent transfer tasks. Exact run-level arrays and matching normal-tumor donor set are not authenticated from attachments.

Application: choose drugs effective in a new patient's tumor organoid while avoiding toxicity to non-tumor/normal-liver models. Format transfer offers a meaningful restriction: scarce 3D drug feedback calibrates a prior learned in matched 2D culture; state information may capture format-dependent biology. This is materially different from assay-only CXCL9 correction, but **baseline multiomics+drug-response pairing does not itself establish drug-conditioned molecular state transitions**. A static action-conditioned viability component is legitimate if claimed narrowly; an RNA dynamics claim is unsupported here.

Potential later-task construction must group all formats, repeats, passages and tissue aliases of each donor. Source donors teach any update operation; subsequent donors supply purchased feedback and a separately sealed terminal drug menu. Ten screened tumor donors, at most eight paired format donors, make source/development/later/improved-improver separation small. One cannot manufacture new patients from100drugs, wells or cultures. Drug-held-out extrapolation is a different claim from donor inheritance. Cross-patient retrospective query replay can be legal without an author-run adaptive history; no need to impose that extra requirement.

The original data-availability statement says datasets/analytic methods are in Supplementary Tables, while physical organoids require MTA. Thus do not falsely report all data as upon-request. Primary text calls normalized percentage inhibition→AUC/IC50 and tableS7; assay controls S:B/Z' are reported. Exact concentration/window, whole-plate normalization, independent runs, row IDs, per-format transcriptome scope, finite/censored IC50 and coverage must be inspected **metadata-only before response payloads**. Mean-AUC tables might support finite response replay, but cannot be assumed raw controls or a sparse-measurement oracle. This source is not approved as an inheritance benchmark now.

Nearest simple controls for any future precise hypothesis: patient mean/offset, same-drug source2D→3D calibration, ridge on baseline genomic/transcriptomic variables, ordinary hierarchical multi-task regression, low-rank matrix completion, partial pooling and full union refit. Ordinary format-dependent residual sharing is not a new learning mechanism. Any proposed structural operator must specify a different testable restriction; a drug-specific residual cannot be interpreted as target occupancy.

## New source 2: ESCC real two-stage assay

*Organoid-based two-step drug screening for rapid identification of chemotherapy-resistant oesophageal squamous cell carcinoma and alternative therapies*, Clinical and Translational Medicine2025, DOI10.1002/ctm2.70534, PMID41267460, PMC12635425. Original full methods obtained, not supplement numeric payload.

The paper establishes26PDO models, including treatment-naive and post-chemotherapy sources. **These are not authenticated paired longitudinal patient states**. Real workflow: preliminary single-dose viability using ATP at about4days; select viability<=20% and top3agents; secondary expanded organoids receive top3plus TP, with ATP at treatment start/end and concentration-dependent GR. This is actual screening feedback, with meaningful growth-normalized function and independent patient backgrounds. It is not a complete oracle for alternate candidate-selection policies: selection determines which agents receive secondary assays. No public dense second-stage alternative menu is authenticated. Real chronology alone does not supply counterfactual query coverage.

The paper reports17named PDOs in the sensitive/resistant PTX comparison and six targeted BATF2 manipulations, not26independent perturbation-state-function sequences. RNA comparison is stratified by response, not established within-culture time-course perturbation measurements. Transcriptomic signature discovery after labeling does not become lawful prior state. Clinical response confounding acknowledged by authors (different immunotherapy/TME) prevents treating ex-vivo ranking as proven clinical treatment benefit.

Data-availability text says primary data upon request with appropriate sharing agreements and also paper/supplement contents. This is not evidence of zero public data; it does leave run-level joins/coverage unqualified. TableS5 is referenced for selected secondary response. We did not fetch it. Do not send external author messages.

**STOP AS CURRENT POLICY-INHERITANCE ORACLE.** Could support a bounded observational diagnostic after table schema audit; not worth numeric modeling without a distinct operation and lawful counterfactual menu. A model filling unmeasured secondary responses must not judge itself as real feedback. Restricting to universally measured TP would test a much smaller ordinary prediction task, not the advertised adaptive10drug agent.

## Biological criteria / falsification

An inherited updater should act on a declared prediction map with known feedback and independent endpoint audit. It need not uniquely identify physical occupancy or full GRN; demand physical identification only for a physical claim. It needs more than repeated fitting on one donor. A state-based update advantage must survive raw-state direct predictors with identical purchased labels, control costs, source search and total compute. At least donor grouping and genuine later feedback are necessary; prescribing an arbitrary number of tasks here would invent a power claim.

No mechanism GO is made. If a peer supplies a precise new restriction, the HCC source may be considered for metadata-only qualification, with an explicit pre-response sample-size/coverage gate. Stop that candidate if the shared-donor count or affordable feedback cannot identify the restriction, if ordinary hierarchical calibration implements it, or if predeclared later-donor decision gains fail. Novelty and power are unresolved, not verified negatives.
