## Scientific proposal and decision

Application remains learning a reusable way to repair intervention-to-function predictions in new cellular backgrounds from limited real functional feedback. The source evidence makes an important distinction: **learning an adapter generator, learning a research-harness proposer, and learning executable edit factors are three concrete operations already implemented by close CS prior work**. Merely combining their names or replacing transitions with molecular observations is not a technical contribution.

**NONE / NO_NEW_CORE_NOMINATED.** I do not currently have a distinct cell-grounded estimator and lawful untouched functional task that separates its mechanism from these direct controls. This is not rejection because the mathematics is conventional: finite-sample structural restrictions could be useful. It is a refusal to promote an unspecified restriction as an innovation. The current nine-feedback CXCL9 task is explicitly stopped and cannot become fresh evidence by attaching a hypernetwork or proposer.

The most precise unpromoted difference worth examining is **intervention-validated updater-edit credit**, rather than association between archived program ideas and scores. For a proposed update edit e, clone the identical starting world theta and history H; apply the same paid feedback batch D to updater U and to e(U); score both on a prospectively untouched later functional batch Q. The inherited record is (edit signature, allowed task descriptors, paired delta utility), not a raw winning program. Learn a proposer from these signed effects and compare its new and old checkpoints producing descendants from the same initial updater on subsequent tasks. This targets credit confounding by parent strength and task difficulty, not ordinary model fitting. Its falsifiable claim would be that paired-effect descriptors improve *future edit discovery per total assay/compute cost* over score-only idea factors, fixed random/UCB edit search and standard trained contextual bandits. But as stated it is a combination of classical paired causal evaluation, contextual algorithm selection and meta-learning. No new estimator, biological sharing law or special sample-efficiency theorem is supplied; therefore **do not call this our new algorithm or run a costly biological campaign merely for this description**. A contribution would have to specify the structure that lets one real functional contrast legitimately inform many new updates and then defeat a same-structure conventional learner.

### CLAW: Amortized Low-Rank Adaptation for Model-Based Reinforcement Learning

Original [2609.12278v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.12278v2). Read abstract/introduction, prior-work comparison and §§4.1–4.2 architecture/context-buffer/meta-objective, including equations3–6; did not audit every appendix or experiment. It jointly trains a base TD-MPC2 world and hypernetwork. A small MLP embeds each observed transition; averaging makes an unordered context embedding. A chunked generator maps this embedding plus chunk identity into a template-offset LoRA adapter, phi=phi0+Delta_phi. Across environments, context and update buffers are separated: context generates adapter; update samples supply the ordinary world-model loss plus adapter L2 penalty, gradients updating base and hypernetwork jointly with Adam. Deployment freezes base and generator and generates new adapters by one forward pass. Context collection is random in experiments; the explicit reason is matching context quality/distribution across adaptation baselines, not a learned agent acquisition policy.

**Substantive overlap:** a trainable reusable update operator that converts feedback into world-model weight changes is already present. Low-rank biological adapters or a DeepSet feedback encoder alone are occupied mechanisms. The source compares gradient adaptation/context conditioning and discusses joint versus post-hoc hypernetwork training; these should be strong controls if we later propose a different inherited update.

**Transfer assumptions:** repeated environment family; genuine transitions with action/reward; support/query data; common context-collection policy at training/deployment; sufficiently informative contexts. Drug endpoint panels are not robot trajectories. Exchangeable well measurements may justify mean aggregation, but time/order/history-dependent biological responses would be discarded unless encoded. An RNA-rich paid context must also go to the direct/meta residual competitor. Two pancreatic contexts cannot simply be called a large meta-training family. A virtual-cell version needs a biologically anchored adaptation restriction, not a larger generated parameter tensor. Training the generator once and reusing it is meta-learning/Level2, not automatically improved-improver Level3.

### HarnessTTT: Learn to Optimize Harness at Test-Time for Scientific Discovery

Original [2610.17370v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.17370v1). Read abstract/introduction and §3 harness/proposer/experience loop through the explicit reward, RLOO/sharpening and proposer update; examined transfer caveat. Executor weights stay frozen. A trainable proposer emits validated executable harness specifications: prompts, skills, tools, middleware and execution controls. Each candidate rollout starts from shared incumbent state; rewards improve incumbent score normalized by remaining gap to published reference (or incumbent magnitude after reference reached), with caps/penalties. Advantages compare proposals **within groups sharing parent and prompt**, specifically avoiding inherited-parent advantage. RLOO omits reward-standard-deviation division; the added centered softmax sharpening has alpha=.3. The paper explicitly calls the resulting estimator a discovery heuristic, not unbiased. Best valid solution, harness and compact digest are retained. Its text acknowledges that transfer including additional optimization does not isolate a pure transfer effect.

**Substantive overlap:** changing the improvement proposer itself using scored descendants is already an operation, not merely textual memory. We cannot assert the whole improver-learning concept is new. At the same time these author results do not prove biological function or our required later-task common-start recursive advantage. A biological variant cannot use a cell model's own score as independent truth; it needs lawful measured utility and noise-aware same-budget controls. Reference-score rewards require a legal known reference, not leaked held-out optimum. Same-parent grouping is already present, so paired-parent fairness alone is not a sufficient claimed difference.

### ExpEvolve: Structuring and Reusing Experience for Open-Ended Discovery

Original [2610.29303v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.29303v1). Read abstract/introduction, structured-experience argument and §4.1–4.2 practical idea discovery, edit control and plateau exploration; did not verify all theorem proofs/appendices. Persistent idea factors have code-pattern descriptions, binary/categorical types and value sets. An LLM annotates archived programs. A pairwise ranker identifies large-score-gap pairs unexplained by current factors; another LLM proposes new separating factors; candidates are annotated across the archive and admitted if supported and score-separating. ADD/REMOVE factor instructions form executable mutation actions, chosen with UCB on maximum normalized reward. Reward normalizes each candidate relative to random-exploration candidates of the same generation. Some slots remain random. Plateaus trigger diversification away from factors common to top programs, with dependent factors masked.

**Substantive overlap:** persistent extracted improvement factors, experience-conditioned strategy allocation and targeted diversification are directly implemented. A catalog of failed biological updates plus UCB editor selection is therefore not new. The theoretical insight that factors require variation to be identified also directly warns against passive score association. A new paired-intervention factor evaluation would be a testable departure from the specific practical archive-ranking implementation, but the source already motivates contrasts and the generic departure is classical experimental design. It needs a new estimator/restriction before promotion.

**Transfer assumptions:** repeated accurate evaluators; runnable candidate programs; enough independent variation of factor settings; reliable annotations. Sparse/noisy cellular outcomes and a shared calibration confound violate the easy score-factor interpretation. In this application a program score depends on feedback cost, assay noise, starting model and task difficulty; a factor label does not establish transferable causal benefit. Strong controls include ordinary contextual bandit/operator selection with identical task descriptors and full source-search cost.
