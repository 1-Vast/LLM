## Constructive -> support; actual response

Do not leave-one-gem and then average into a pooled predictor/target because shared single errors re-enter. I replied that A/B must remain frozen even if distributions differ; diagnostic single-expression A/B differences can be reported but cannot select a new split. Trace normalization must be training-only and penalties specified in advance; equal candidate count alone is not fair regularization geometry.

## Necessary implementation detail sent to root

If q_i=mean_g(a_i,g elementwise b_i,g), the fair full bilinear feature is Q_i=mean_g sym(a_i,g b_i,g^T). It is not sym(mean_g a_i,g times mean_g b_i,g^T). The exact dual kernel is the double g,h mean of

    .5 [(a_i,g . a_j,h)(b_i,g . b_j,h)
        +(a_i,g . b_j,h)(b_i,g . a_j,h)].

This preserves the q diagonal as a subset of the full features and uses the same per-gemgroup state payload. Aggregating singles before their products would silently change the hypothesis.
