Yes. r_ab,g = mu_ab,g - mu_a,g - mu_b,g + mu_c,g and q_ab,g = (mu_a,g - mu_c,g)*(mu_b,g - mu_c,g) reuse random means. A state-prediction gain can reflect these coupled errors and guide/batch effects. Matching the gemgroup alone fixes different weighting mixtures but not reuse.

Form predictors from within-gemgroup control-centered singles in A, using an equal-gemgroup average of the products. Do not average leave-one-gemgroup analyses and claim all shared single errors vanished, because the aggregate predictors and targets reuse observations again. Disjoint strata avoid direct reuse of cells for the predictor and response; they do not establish independent biology, eliminate guide efficacy differences or guarantee equivalent batch distributions.
