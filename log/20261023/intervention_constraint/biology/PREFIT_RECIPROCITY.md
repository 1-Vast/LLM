# Biological pre-run review: endpoint diagonal-symmetrization gate

2026-10-23. Root supplied a candidate description after my independent report was sealed: in known conjugate force coordinates, a potential yields a reciprocal susceptibility; unknown positive intervention strengths can motivate diagonal symmetrization, reciprocal-sign and three-cycle compatibility checks. I have not read the constructive role's report or new RNA values. This note is therefore a pre-run validity review of that supplied description, not an independent discovery or a result review.

## Verdict

**ALLOW_BOUNDED_DESCRIPTIVE_ENDPOINT_GATE**, subject to the exact scope below. A numerical check can assess whether the chosen observed endpoint submatrix is compatible with the precisely defined algebraic constraint. It cannot test whether cells are at equilibrium, possess a biological potential, or violate microscopic reciprocity. No significance, p-value, cross-task inheritance or state-world superiority claim follows.

The current single-RNA panel is a finite CRISPRa endpoint, logCP10K transformed and control-centered, with experiment/batch handling declared in its existing qualification. It is not a measured local derivative of a conjugate coordinate with respect to a calibrated external force. A gene-target intervention index and that gene's RNA output coordinate share a name, but this does not establish physical conjugacy. RNA abundance is not automatically the conjugate coordinate of sgRNA activation strength. CRISPRa effects may act through several molecular routes, feedback, saturation and finite-time selection. A smooth equilibrium response law can produce an asymmetric finite secant matrix when columns are evaluated at different interventions and points. Therefore incompatibility of this matrix does not falsify the underlying potential idea beyond this endpoint encoding.

## Conditions before reading values

1. Freeze orientation: rows must be measured target-gene RNA features and columns matched interventions (or declare the transpose consistently). Restrict to a square identity-qualified target-gene intersection. The 103 target labels and 33,694 RNA features are not automatically a valid square matrix. Feature IDs should be stable original gene IDs with the existing aliases applied exactly once. One-to-many symbols, duplicated features or aliases, missing targets and the RHOXF2/RHOXF2B history must be surfaced; do not choose the most convenient feature using values. Do not merge or average ambiguous rows after viewing results.
2. State the exact matrix entry, transform and centering. Keep the existing preprocessing fixed. LogCP10K is compositional: changing one transcript can shift many other relative abundances, and log transformation and centering change the effective metric. An unrestricted unknown metric could absorb asymmetry; an unknown *diagonal* metric is a substantive representation assumption. Neither success nor failure identifies the biologically correct metric.
3. Treat source/control batch adjustments as part of the observation operator. Intervention-specific guide efficacy, cell composition, gemgroup, measurement noise, heterogeneous subpopulations and timing can create asymmetric entries. Do not treat 103 interventions as independent biological replicates or cells as independent condition-level replicates. No re-centering, threshold rescue or post-hoc subgroup selection based on incompatibilities.
4. Spell out unknown force assumptions. “CRISPRa” names intended activation, not a verified positive change in every target, nor a calibrated positive physical force. Observed target self-expression does not itself identify the conjugate force sign. Positive diagonal symmetrization tests the stipulated *positive* column-rescaling model. If column signs are allowed unknown, raw pair sign disagreement no longer by itself refutes signed diagonal symmetrization. Report the distinction; do not silently flip columns to rescue the positive test. If the candidate requires positive force, failure belongs to that candidate's observed-coordinate assumption, not a general cellular-law conclusion.
5. Near-zero values and numerical tolerance matter. Relative three-cycle residuals can explode near zeros. Freeze absolute/numerical and any descriptive support thresholds before execution and report excluded counts. Without a measurement-error model these are sensitivity summaries, not evidence that noise explains violations or statistical tests of reciprocity. Do not threshold into a “biologically valid” subset after seeing the answer.
6. A general matrix-symmetry gate can be run without response-model fits or functional labels. Keep it source-feature-only and do not open new development/protected functional outcomes. No biochemical time ordering is inferred from intervention row/column order.

## Mathematical claim boundary

For a square matrix J in fixed coordinates, the candidate must declare whether it tests existence of positive D with D*J symmetric, J*D symmetric, or equivalently a transposed convention. Pairwise nonzero entries must have the same sign under positive scaling, and ratios must satisfy cycle closure on the specified support graph. Pair sign and triangles alone are not a complete global test on an arbitrary sparse support graph: longer chordless cycles can fail despite all available triangles passing. One-sided zero support is incompatible with exact positive symmetrization. Approximate/noisy compatibility needs a declared residual objective and tolerance rather than promoting pairwise sign fractions as a proof. Diagonal entries place no reciprocity constraint; negative diagonals are not by themselves a test of the requested symmetry condition.

Conjugate-force local equilibrium Hessian symmetry is a theorem under its assumptions. Mapping this theorem onto finite transcription endpoints supplies unverified assumptions. A passing endpoint matrix could arise from symmetric network structure, common normalization or ordinary direct factorization; it would not authenticate a potential or establish new algorithmic value. A failing endpoint matrix can justify **STOP_THIS_POSITIVE_DIAGONAL_ENDPOINT_REPRESENTATION** if frozen thresholds say so, while preserving the possibility of different calibrated coordinates, local small perturbations, non-equilibrium models or other biological mechanisms. Do not use the result as permission for unrestricted-metric rescue or new data acquisition without an application-driven rule and strong controls.

The H-metric / third-order curvature extension cannot be checked from these one-intervention endpoints without a specified derivative identification and additional perturbation/readout structure. A single finite response matrix does not identify all local second or third derivatives. Do not count an algebraic tensor fit to the same matrix as an independent validation of that extension.

## Necessary follow-on only if compatible

Compatibility would be a cheap feasibility signal, not a promoted core mechanism. A method must still specify a feedback update with a distinct cost/sample implication versus ordinary symmetric or symmetrizable factorization and direct regression on the same features. Functional-feedback benefit, cell-state validity, independent later-context transfer, agent necessity and RSI remain separate tests. A failed endpoint gate warrants recording the bounded failure and moving on, not claiming that equilibrium biology is disproved.
