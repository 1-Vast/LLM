## Verdict

**NO_NEW_BIOLOGICAL_CONSTRAINT_SUPPORTED / DO_NOT_PROMOTE.** There is a biologically motivated constraint worth articulating, but the exact constraint is already explicit in Norman 2019, has known biological exceptions, and functional-only feedback does not identify its state correction. I do not recommend a new biological model fit under an innovation claim on this evidence. This is a bounded failure to nominate a distinctive constraint, not a proof that all constrained interaction learning fails or that later research must await a complete RSI dataset.

## The one concrete constraint assessed

For simultaneously activated genes a and b, let d_a and d_b be their measured single-perturbation transcriptional shifts. Constrain the shared predicted combination response to

    d_ab = c_a(a,b) d_a + c_b(a,b) d_b + e_ab.

The biological claim would be that acquired functional feedback principally revises **amplification/dominance coefficients of existing single-response programs**, while unobserved new directions e_ab are not freely edited. Coefficients for the two genes need not be equal. Swapping the unordered input pair must swap the associated coefficients and leave the predicted state unchanged. This is a simultaneous-response decomposition, not a temporal composition T_a T_b. There is no justification here for a universal coefficient sign, a fixed <=1 bound, a fixed GI sign, or a shared readout-independent magnitude.

This is the narrowest credible shared-factor restriction I find in the available evidence, but it is **occupied primary prior art**. Norman's main text explicitly defines d_ab=c1*d_a+c2*d_b+epsilon and discusses dominance, suppression, synergy and neomorphism. Existing local primary text: log/20261022/norman_functional_contract/root_sources/primary_bioc.source.txt, paragraphs headed “A quantitative framework for interpreting GIs” (lines47-53 in the saved file). DOI10.1126/science.aax4438. The article reports strong average fit but also unexpected double phenotypes, including PLK4/STIL. Thus approximate low-dimensional structure is plausible; hard exact span sufficiency is not a general law. Its scalar growth map also collapses different cellular endpoints (death, differentiation, slow cycling), which is precisely why functional agreement alone cannot certify a corrected molecular state.

Reusing this equation as a feedback-editable “world factor” is insufficient distinction. With fixed linear functional head g, y=g^T d_ab gives one scalar constraint for two coefficients, and all coefficient shifts satisfying (g^T d_a)*Delta c_a + (g^T d_b)*Delta c_b=0 remain functionally invisible. A nonlinear head does not generally make a single scalar observation identify a multidimensional state; it usually leaves level sets. Shared factorization across pairs can reduce ambiguity, but ordinary joint factor regression exploits the same sharing. A module is not biologically identified merely because optimization selects one solution.

## Smallest informative falsification, if a genuinely new coefficient-update rule emerges

Before any inheritance/RSI claim, a frozen source-only feasibility test is permissible. It would require actual matched source-pair transcriptional outcomes in addition to the already available single states and source growth. First test the approximation itself on source-only acquired pairs: estimate the best two-direction projection from one biological/technical measurement set, evaluate molecular residual and coefficient stability on another, and account for replicate uncertainty. The within-pair best-fit residual cannot be called a predictive result. Cell resampling is not a new biological replicate. If matched independent state measurement is unavailable, report the identifiable limitation rather than inferring state validity from growth fit.

Then compare the proposed update against the exact Norman span model, a direct same-information scalar/factorized predictor, ordinary joint refit, and a multiview predictor receiving exactly the same acquired paired state. An identity-only/without-state ablation distinguishes information from architecture; count state acquisition and computation costs. Preserve an unconstrained residual channel baseline because neomorphic phenotypes are the biologically important counterexamples. Stop the hard-span claim if the source molecular residual exceeds the predeclared replicate-based tolerance, or the functional improvement is explained by direct factor regression at the same cost. Stop an algorithmic novelty claim if its prediction/update map is equivalent to the ordinary comparator. Success of a soft span bias would establish at most conditional finite-sample utility; it would establish neither unique cellular causality nor an autonomous agent, inheritance, or RSI.

No new coefficient-update rule with a distinct mathematical or biological justification is supplied by the current evidence, so this is a **conditional falsifier specification, not an authorized promoted experiment or a pending promise to fit**. It is not necessary to acquire all SCHEMATIC data to reach this decision.
