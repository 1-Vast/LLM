# Independent primary-source qualification: organoid scheduling

Scope: read cached primary PMC12309667 (DOI10.1126/sciadv.adt1851), its projection and original XML figure captions, without other agents' current conclusions, newly downloaded datasets, repository scripts, or newly downloaded numeric response tables. Published primary-paper values and trends are necessarily exposed. This is primary-source qualification, not new predictive evidence, a new state model, or an attempt to rescue the failed TRAIL configuration.

## Main disposition

Conditional GO for qualifying a real schedule-to-terminal-functional-response benchmark and historical generation-ahead prediction. The primary establishes a genuine experimental-feedback BO campaign with automated drug delivery and real endpoint responses. It does not establish observable intermediate cellular-state trajectories, full candidate-space counterfactual outcomes, transferable learned update rules, or recursive improvement of the improvement strategy. Source data/schema and exact campaign history must be audited before response-stage protocol freezing. No new updater/core claim is justified yet.

## Biological unit and independence

MCF-7 spheroids are one established breast cancer cell line. The organoid experiment uses one ER-/PR-/HER2- model; Methods explicitly says PDO line1 was established in EKGel from a PDX model. Therefore call it a PDX-derived breast-organoid model of patient origin, not a multi-patient PDO cohort or direct patient-specific clinical validation. Tissue consent/ethics do not establish multiple independent patients.

Each microfluidic device has12 quadruplets,100 microwells per quadruplet, four rows of25 sharing an inlet/outlet. The device can test12 regimens, not1200 independent treatment assignments. Shared fluid supply, device, suspension and model ancestry create dependencies. Fig3/6 means and SD use three-to-five independent experiments; validation sometimes uses more than four/five, and Methods says at least three independent experiments. These statements support experimental repeat claims at the paper level but do not map a released row to unique organoid, quadruplet, chip, passage, date or biological preparation. A schedule mean is not automatically a run replicate, and100 organoids are not100 independent treatment experiments. Inspect actual IDs before deciding a split. Never fabricate batch or donor IDs.

## Physical actions and clocks

MCF-7 concurrent treatment supplies DOX/5-FU/CPA for48h. Sequential experiments hold concentrations to the concurrently selected combination: DOX1.25uM,5-FU105uM, CPA94.4uM (some narrative rounds94). All six permutations are possible. The first two drug durations are inputs; third equals48h minus their sum. Initial discrete duration step4h is later refined to2h. The reported preferred GS2-7 is DOX8h ->5-FU32h ->CPA8h. A pump withdraws/discards one drug solution before supplying the next. This is actual sequential exposure of each treated cohort, not independent single-agent endpoints concatenated by a simulator. Nonetheless retention/penetration kinetics mean nominal wash/switch does not prove instantaneous intracellular drug clearance.

For the organoid model, OLA and IBET-762 are concurrently selected at116uM and0.993uM, then tested as OLA->IBET or IBET->OLA across96h total. Initial duration step4h becomes2h in subsequent generations. Fig7 validation identifies OLA30h and IBET66h; exact ordering and each candidate's timings should be obtained from design records, not guessed from sorted response plots. Dose ranges and single-agent EC50s are drug/culture-specific, not evidence of generic drug-additivity or a shared mechanism across MCF-7 and organoids.

Separate clocks are essential: spheroid formation images at24h and PDO formation images at96h postseeding describe culture maturation. Drug responses are measured after48h (spheroids) or96h (organoids) of treatment. These times must not be mistaken for longitudinal response measurements at drug switches. Paper Methods reports one-hour LIVE/DEAD staining at37C and endpoint fluorescence imaging. It also says CVexp is calculated from viability before versus after treatment and normalized against control to reduce device variation. This supplies baseline/end normalization, not a released dense intervention-state trajectory or proof that every before/after record has persistent per-organoid identity. Assay timing/normalization orientation and control availability must be fixed from release documentation before a numerical task.

The endpoint is a derived LIVE/DEAD fluorescence viability measure using calcein-AM and PI mean pixel intensities. It is not direct cell count, per-cell death timing, or exact viable biomass. Values above100% are explicitly possible; paper discusses dye permeability or biological explanations. Do not clip, infer growth/death from dye ratio, or normalize away device effects using future held-out controls unless those are legally available to every method at the stated forecast time. Ki67/H2AX/confocal and dissociated-cell flow cytometry are separate validation assays, not automatically joint per-organoid longitudinal state labels.

## What Gryffin actually learned from

The primary explicitly describes measured CVexp/CI being supplied as BO objectives and results accumulated from preceding iterations guiding the next generation. Thus real feedback is established, unlike a campaign conducted solely against a synthetic oracle. The primary does not prove every raw replicate/well or every attempted experiment enters each optimization call. Could be schedule means, retained observations, or history subsets; code/configuration/history audit is required to establish all-versus-partial feedback. The text alone cannot certify all-data ordinary retraining or a particular updater.

Concurrent searches use single-drug fitted curves to calculate theoretical viability (product of single-agent viabilities), Bliss-related synergy CI and viability objectives, and a CVtheor constraint. For MCF-7 the primary uses CI<=-0.1 and viability<=70%; sequential search instead targets viability50%. PDO concurrent targets CI<=-0.15 and viability<=50%, whereas sequential again targets viability50%. This differs by campaign, so one cannot combine rankings without accounting for objectives and constraints. CI depends on fitted single-drug reference responses; reference-fit inputs must be legal for all comparisons.

Human collaboration is explicit: exploration/exploitation starts balanced, then humans adjust strategies, constraints or termination; MCF-7 sequential later restricts to exploitation and refines duration resolution. PDO sequential also changes4h to2h. Chimera handles objectives. Generation labels are experimental search batches, not successive cellular measurements within one treated organoid. Reported campaigns take10weeks for spheroids and15weeks for PDOs, largely constrained by culture. There is no basis for treating nominal48/96h response time as total laboratory feedback latency.

The main narrative counts MCF-7 sequential GS0..GS3 as32 proposed experiments, eight per batch, and organoid GS0..GS2 as27, nine per batch. These are selected proposal counts, not independent donors or proof of exhaustive response matrices. Concurrent narrative says four generations but labels G0..G4; do not resolve that textual counting ambiguity by inventing missing data. Data release must establish actual chronological batches, repeated schedules and absent outcomes.

## What can and cannot be validated

1. Most immediate eligible task: predict unseen later-generation terminal CVexp for a complete ordered dose-duration schedule using only preceding-generation measured feedback and legally available single-drug references. Group complete schedule repetitions and chip/preparation IDs where supplied. Use actual chronological batch order, not plots sorted by response. If only means are released, restrict to schedule-mean prediction, with no false replicate independence. Fixed treatment durations mean this is schedule-to-endpoint response prediction; calling it a dynamic virtual-cell world model requires additional evidence.

2. A limited model-update test can compare a prespecified predictor before/after a real feedback batch on fixed legal future schedule queries. Strong direct models should include drug duration/exposure, order, interactions and dose; compare ordinary pooled refit, recency/persistence, simple mixtures, and equal-budget model search before claiming a special updater. Novel schedules must be held out as whole schedules; duration interpolation versus new order transfer should be distinguished. Very small samples, adaptive data collection and shared normalizations limit inference. Existing Gryffin+Chimera is an essential baseline, not a new invention to rename.

3. Offline active-learning/agent-policy superiority cannot be inferred merely by replaying a policy against historically selected schedules: unselected candidate outcomes are unknown. The final selected best and human-adjusted policy history are selection-conditioned. Need a documented premeasured candidate pool with lawful hidden outcomes, new wet-lab outcomes, or an explicitly approximate simulator whose claims remain separate. A model predicting outcomes for untested schedules is not an authenticated experimental oracle. No simulated response may be relabeled real feedback.

4. Intermediate virtual-cell state learning is presently unsupported by the primary alone: no documented repeated functional/state observations at every switch, persistent cell identities, or molecular response trajectory joins. End-to-end order effects are real biological phenomena but do not identify latent states or transition rules. Single-time Ki67/H2AX validation does not bridge that gap automatically.

5. Inheritance/RSI remains unestablished. Concurrent-to-sequential reuse of selected doses is hand-specified experimental knowledge transfer. Ordinary BO surrogate updating and human resolution/strategy changes do not demonstrate an inherited learned update law or that the strategy for improving strategies becomes more effective. MCF-7/three-drug and one-organoid/two-drug campaigns vary mechanism, duration, doses and objectives simultaneously; they are not a large independent meta-training/test task series. Paper discussion calls transfer learning a future acceleration, not a validated result here.

## Success, stops and remaining evidence

Qualification success requires actual release records mapping schedule/dose/duration, observed endpoint and units, chronological generation and replicate aggregation, device/control/batch identity when supplied, and code/configuration showing which measured history is passed to each optimizer. It also requires identifying objective/constraint/resolution changes before testing a claim. Missing device IDs limit generalization claims but need not prohibit a transparently narrow schedule-mean diagnostic.

Stop a world-state/update/RSI claim if data are only endpoint schedule means without legal future queries and distinct mechanism. Do not abandon every endpoint benchmark merely because full RSI is unavailable. Stop retrospective policy-superiority claims without outcomes for alternative actions. Do not treat source data links as proof of schemas not inspected. No new numeric response was decoded or forecast fitted in this independent review. This is a new source qualification, explicitly unrelated to repairing TRAIL weights/state assumptions.
