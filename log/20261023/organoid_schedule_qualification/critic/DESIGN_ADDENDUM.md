## Explicit GO for the smallest honest experiment

GO for raw-coordinate schedule-to-endpoint forecasting with categorical seq and released t0/t1. Full physical-hour and a..f decoding is not required to predict this endpoint when every model receives identical coordinates and their meaning is stable across generations. The category names can remain anonymous. This is a direct prediction benchmark, not physical exposure modeling or latent-cell dynamics. The source anchors support cross-generation consistency sufficiently to justify a transparent small diagnostic. If stronger unit inconsistency is found, record representation shift rather than retrospectively transforming coordinates using outcomes.

Use complete later batches; frozen G0 model versus identical model ordinary-refitted on generations<g. At g1 the two are algebraically identical: this is a sanity check, not evidence of useful update feedback. The first actual comparison is g2, and FAC also permits g3. Feature schema and regularization must be fixed before later responses; do not select the eventual winner on scored batches. A full direct predictor can use categorical order, coordinates and their interactions, with a simple mean and a fixed local predictor as ordinary checks. Tiny G0 counts do not justify unrestricted model search. Means/rows are the analysis unit when replicate records are absent.

Success is a prespecified positive paired loss reduction from ordinary cumulative feedback over the identical frozen model, reported separately for each campaign and evaluable batch. Also report absolute loss versus simple mean/local controls; a relative win from an unusable predictor is weak evidence. MAE is a natural primary metric, MSE a secondary sensitivity; ranking or best-of-batch regret is allowed only among the actually measured target batch and secondary, because response noise and few regimens can dominate. No target-response clipping, global preprocessing or target-informed hyperparameter choice.

This identifies limited utility of adding real response-labelled observations under the historical design distribution. Added observations also expand action-space coverage; it does not isolate a special updater or transfer law. A response-blind expanded-design control would require an explicit imputation method and is not necessary to establish this narrow added-feedback result. Adaptive batches and absence of biological replicate IDs prohibit an independent-task significance claim. If improvement is inconsistent or loses to simple ordinary controls, report that directly and stop any new updater claim for this configuration. Either result advances the question without waiting for full RSI assets.
