## Single proposed gate, to be frozen by root before X/Y access

Application: determine whether a pooled affine functional-feedback transport has a repeatable basal-context dependence worth studying. It tests association heterogeneity, not intervention causality or a new world model.

1. Keep historical folds1–4 only. Choose exactly one RNA sample per subject by minimum SHA256 of `functional-error-one-sample:` plus context ID. Partition subjects 50/50 by SHA256 parity of `functional-error-source-replication:` plus group ID. These salts are new, explicit and must not be retried to balance results. Source-discovery and source-replication remain exploratory subdivisions.
2. Specify the **first basal-expression principal component** as the sole axis before Y access. Retain genes finite throughout source and nonconstant in discovery; standardize by discovery mean/SD; fit PCA1 only on discovery; score both halves using that transform. Fix sign by making the largest-absolute-loading gene positive, breaking ties lexicographically. Split high/low by the discovery score median, apply that same threshold in replication. No PCA2, gene-panel, mutation subgroup or alternative threshold rescue. This is unsupervised context construction, not a prediction-model fit. If PCA/finite-gene support is unusable, stop the diagnostic rather than change the axis.
3. Prespecify **all unordered drug pairs** among the existing 165 items. Eligibility is at least25 subjects jointly measured in each of the four half-by-PC-stratum cells. Select by finite-value masks only, never correlation magnitude/sign. Require at least30 eligible pairs. Pearson correlation for a constant measured vector is defined as0 and the count reported; do not outcome-drop such pairs after looking. These count/effect floors are pragmatic exploratory gates, not externally validated biological thresholds.
4. For every eligible pair p compute `Delta_D[p] = r_D,high[p] - r_D,low[p]`, and independently `Delta_R[p]`. The **single primary quantity** is `T = mean_p Delta_D[p] * Delta_R[p]`. T is positive for aligned cross-half context dependence; pooled correlations need not reverse sign. Requiring actual opposite signs would miss genuine context modulation. No pair is promoted because it looks good in discovery; the aggregate includes every eligible pair with equal weight.
5. Bootstrap subjects independently within each fixed half-by-stratum cell,2000 replicates with a fixed documented RNG seed, recomputing the complete pair-correlation vectors and T on the original eligible list. The single continuation gate is the one-sided95% percentile lower bound for T greater than **0.01**. Report T, CI, four-cell sizes, eligible-pair count, constant-pair counts and all pair deltas regardless of sign. Do not call drug pairs independent replicates: subject resampling preserves their dependence. This finite bootstrap is an exploratory uncertainty assessment, not an exact finite-sample test or protection from historical designer exposure.

Interpretation:0.01 is an approximate squared correlation-difference effect floor; if each half estimates the same difference0.1, their product is0.01. It is **not** a guarantee of drug-ranking or clinical benefit. PCA1 may reflect batch, cell composition, disease mix or treatment history. Their predictability can still be relevant to forecasting, but the diagnostic cannot assign causality. Differences in means/variances, pairwise missingness and PC-stratum range can change Pearson correlations without a changed cellular interaction. Report these limitations without adding post-hoc modules.
