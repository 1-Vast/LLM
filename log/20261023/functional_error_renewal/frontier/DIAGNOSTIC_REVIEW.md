## Minimum statistical/interpretive contract

1. Authenticate basal RNA sample-to-function/patient joins and pretreatment status. It is not necessary to prove same-cell post-drug transitions for this predictive stratification question. One fixed score/gene-set/state threshold chosen without AUC response inspection is sufficient. If a median threshold needs fitting, fit it in discovery RNA then apply unchanged to replication. Do not tune gene, sign or threshold against response differences.
2. Split by whole patient using a frozen identity rule. Predetermine same-patient multiple-sample aggregation or representative selection without consulting function. New halves of already exposed source are an internal exploratory replication, not a new external cohort or secure untouched confirmation. Do not access development fold0 or infer independence from new salts.
3. Predeclare drug availability and minimum common-observation requirements. Correlations must use common measured patients for each drug pair and state stratum; no zero-filled drug outcomes, synthetic functional labels or complete-case-selection concealment. Report each pair's four discovery/replication-by-state counts and baseline-group/missingness frequencies. Large differences in assay availability limit transport even if association replicates.
4. If scanning many drug pairs in discovery, define one multiplicity family, threshold and a bounded selected list before replication values. Preserve all tested pairs and null failures. Freeze effect direction/pair identities and acceptance criterion before inspecting replication. If reporting any replicated pair, correct for the number selected or use a prespecified aggregate signed statistic. Correlated drug pairs and repeated patients must not inflate independent-task counts. A patient-level bootstrap or randomization procedure must respect patient units; sample-level resampling is inadequate when duplicate patients remain.
5. Pearson correlation reversal is one exact association target; it is not identical to a change in a globally normalized covariance or predictive slope. Report source-fixed scaling plus within-state covariance/slopes or explicitly limit wording to correlation heterogeneity. Mean shifts and broad sensitivity can produce pooled Simpson-type effects. Missingness/assay factors and sample composition remain alternative explanations; neither partial correlations nor a gene score alone supplies causality.
6. Discovery significance alone is insufficient. Require a prespecified practical effect size, consistent direction and uncertainty in replication rather than treating two noisy sign estimates as reliable reversal. If the sample count/CI is too weak, report inconclusive; do not chase another group or cutoff. State numerical minimum n and effect gates in the actual frozen protocol, with feasibility assessed from legal counts rather than favorable outcomes.
7. Reproducible conditional association heterogeneity would motivate a conditional updater hypothesis. It would NOT yet show the global covariance predictor makes worse held-patient functional predictions: different within-state correlations can coexist with acceptable global conditional calibration. For an actual global-model-error claim, a separately frozen direct residual/calibration check against its source-fitted linear prediction is needed; do not silently include this extra fit in a purported no-fit diagnostic. A later source-only ordinary state-conditional/refit comparator is essential before novelty promotion. No gain from omitting that straightforward comparator is acceptable.
