## Prespecified single analysis

1. Threshold is discovery patients' median CD14 expression. Apply unchanged to both halves: high strictly greater, low otherwise. No PCA, CD34, alternate marker/cut, gene search, or result-driven transformation.
2. For every unordered pair of165 drugs, compute subgroup pairwise-complete count. Eligible pairs have at least25 patients in EACH of the four half×stratum cells. Eligibility uses missingness, not correlation magnitude. Need≥30 eligible pairs, otherwise stop INSUFFICIENT_COVERAGE. Every selected patient contributes at most once. Do not combine drugs from different RNA samples.
3. Within each cell compute pairwise-complete, mean-centered Pearson correlation. This controls separate means and scales for the association statistic. Numerically constant-variable correlations (centered cross-standard-deviation denominator<=1e-12) are assigned0 and counted explicitly; no pair is removed on response variance. Report counts, correlations, covariances and conditional OLS slopes descriptively for every eligible pair. Individual pairs are NOT declared discoveries.
4. For each pair e, Delta_D(e)=r_D,high-r_D,low, similarly Delta_R(e). Unique primary statistic T=mean_e Delta_D(e)*Delta_R(e). Positive T indicates alignment of association contrasts across patient halves, averaged over the declared eligible pairs. It is NOT a prediction risk or causal interaction. Also report reversal counts in discovery, replication and both halves (r_low*r_high<0, zeros not reversals), without making separate hypotheses or selecting examples.
5. Stratified patient bootstrap: independently resample patients within each of the four cells, keeping original cell sizes;2000 replicates, NumPy default_rng seed20261023. Recompute pair correlations for the FIXED original eligible pair set; bootstrap replicates never reselect pairs. Report percentile5% one-sided lower bound and central95% interval. Weighted duplicates are repeated draws, not extra independent patients. Keep pair dependence through whole-patient row resampling. This interval conditions on the observed median threshold, strata and pair eligibility; it does not account for threshold estimation or all historical design exposure, and has no demonstrated calibrated coverage at the degenerate no-heterogeneity boundary.
