## One concrete restriction, and why it is not yet a new method

The minimal defensible biological restriction is **separate a drug's calibrated effective activity from its target's RNA abundance**. For a reversible inhibitor under a fixed exposure regime, a conventional reference is active target amount a=P(1-o), where P is target protein amount and o is fractional occupancy. A conventional downstream state equation can consume a. This is a hypothesis/reference parametrization, not a general law: ATP competition, residence time, covalent binding, degradation, allostery, compartmentalization, target multiplicity and feedback can violate its naive interpretation. Nominal dose or lysate affinity alone does not certify o in living screened cells. Phosphosubstrate abundance is not automatically a specific occupancy measurement either.

An action-specific reusable update could modify the dose/time-to-effective-activity map while holding a shared response law fixed, but **that restriction has not been authenticated in these assets**, and no estimator/decision operation beyond standard calibration, nonlinear mixed effects or ordinary conditional prediction has been supplied. Do not make a new core by adding an occupancy latent, an activity encoder, an editor or an inherited calibration table. The potentially useful hypothesis must first predict an independently measured functional outcome and outperform those controls.

## Identifying versus predictive claims

A discrepancy between RNA-edit predictions and measured drug RNA response does not identify occupancy failure: off-target effects, compensation, time mismatch and state-selection can explain it. A measured downstream phosphosite may improve prediction without uniquely identifying target activity. Conversely full mechanistic identification is unnecessary if an ordinary action-conditional predictor reliably improves the held-out drug decision. The warranted claim should follow the actual assay and comparison.

The strongest immediate simple control is a dose/time/history/chemical/context conditional model, with the same measured protein/activity proxy and all acquired functional feedback, ordinarily refit after each release. Add regularized nonlinear mixed-effects calibration and ordinary multi-output conditional inference. Shared-activity calibration must not receive exclusive phosphoprotein, affinity or target annotations. RNA-edit versus activity-edit alone would compare two candidate action maps; it would not establish a world-model or agent contribution.

## Minimal action-specific function falsifier, not authorization to run a new source hunt

Only if an actually matched corpus is authenticated and a distinct candidate operation exists: start from source-fitted shared response law; reveal a fixed budget of acute drug-specific activity/state observations; save predictions of independent later viable burden or outgrowth for withheld drug schedules before revealing their function. Test whether updating only the effective-activity map improves the *decision between schedules*, with all compared arms given identical observations and acquisition/search opportunities. Include an RNA-edit surrogate, strong direct schedule predictor, ordinary union refit, continual learning, standard active acquisition, fixed structured model, simple mixture, and equal-total-budget program search. Use whole compound/mechanism/background units rather than doses or technical replicates as independent later tasks.

Failure of the shared-action restriction would be evidenced by systematic functional errors on withheld schedules despite adequate measured acute-action fit, or no useful advantage over the same-information ordinary conditional/refit controls. A marker-only improvement is insufficient. A useful numerical decision margin, noise model and stopping rule cannot be honestly frozen now because the required matched functional endpoint and independent task family have not been authenticated. That is a concrete missing measurement contract, not permission to choose a permissive proxy or to claim data are globally unavailable.

## Cross-context/RSI path and allocation

Required later tasks would be independently assayed new chemical implementations and cell backgrounds, with known exposure/activity and later function. Current authenticated assets do not establish this family for the proposed restriction. Different doses, three immune ratios, CV folds or replaying old controls are not substitutes. Shared activity-map transfer would at most be adaptation/inheritance; improved-improver requires the requested same-start/equal-history/equal-total-budget old-versus-new improver test on new tasks. No such evidence exists here.

**Decision:** retain the action-versus-RNA distinction as a biological audit requirement. Do not promote activity/occupancy calibration itself as the new mechanism, rerun the stopped kinobeads/decryptE configurations, or launch occupancy data collection without a distinct operation and falsifiable functional utility. No new core, world-model necessity, agent contribution, inheritance, joint or RSI benefit established. This narrower negative does not rule out a future useful finite-budget activity-conditioned update rule.
