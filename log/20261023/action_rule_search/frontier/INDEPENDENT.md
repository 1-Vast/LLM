## New original evidence and transfer structure

Bounded arXiv searches (saved HTML) identify three actual originals, each retrieved as pinned v1 full HTML. Scope read: abstract/introduction, problem assumptions, method/algorithm, key data/baseline descriptions; no exhaustive appendix or author-results reproduction. Receipts bind exact bodies. The searches are relevance leads, not proof of complete novelty.

1. **SCOUT, Scouting the Dynamics Gap, 2609.36107v1 (28 September2026), §§2,4.1–4.4.** It already couples policy and action-conditioned forward model through shared q; inner loop minimizes average ||f(q,a,o)-E(o')||² with frozen weights; outer loop differentiates query-action imitation NLL through K inner updates. It uses offline histories including unsuccessful/random and privileged ground-truth actions, plus query demonstrations. This is a genuine finite-adaptation operation, beyond merely concatenating context tokens. It is also direct prior art to a proposed cell scheme adapting shared drug-response latent from molecular discrepancies then choosing drugs through a meta-trained policy. Conventional family membership alone does not reject a new rule; **this exact shared-latent inner/outer operation is already present**. Replacing robotic outcome with RNA and policy NLL with drug-function loss supplies no additional update restriction. SCOUT authors report same-framework history-conditioned and PEARL-style comparators; their numerical results are not our replication.

2. **DreamTrue, 2610.12468v1 (8 October2026), §§3.2–3.4.** Offline action calibration refines camera geometry and mounting offsets from known URDF/state plus cross-render/time/view pixel correspondences. Counterfactual trajectories are then perturbed and a human-annotated video-defect reward drives RL post-training without paired counterfactual futures. Essential extra structure: known robot geometry and observable physical defects such as unsupported rising objects. For drug action, target annotation does not provide analogous known action-to-activity geometry, nor a trustworthy RNA-only efficacy-defect oracle. The original distinguishes action calibration from outcome plausibility; a cell transfer must likewise distinguish occupancy/activity measurement from eventual function. Rewarding transcript plausibility cannot authenticate drug efficacy. This does not imply no cell consistency constraints are possible; none has been authenticated as discriminating useful decisions here.

3. **AD-WM, 2609.30264v1 (24 September2026), §§III-B–E.** Residual latent transition plus inverse action recovery from (z,predicted z') and normalized action-recovery loss regularizes predicted, not solely observed, transitions. Unit-covariance normalized recovery reduces to squared target error plus mean shrinkage. At deployment auxiliary heads are discarded; CEM is unchanged. Their planning-facing elite regrets explicitly use environment-realized shared-bank candidate outcomes outside training. Important transfer caution: drug identities/doses can be functionally indistinguishable in a measured cell state. Enforcing recoverability of arbitrary identities can manufacture action distinctions rather than resolve a drug decision. Elite regret is a useful standard endpoint, not our algorithmic innovation.
