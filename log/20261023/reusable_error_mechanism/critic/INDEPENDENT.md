## Exact feedback and update operation

Distinguish three quantities: live-cell number `N`, ATP per live cell `m`, and detector gain/background `(alpha,beta)`. A local assay model is

`A(s)=alpha*(N*m+s)+beta+noise`,

where `s` is ATP added **after lysis**, so the added material cannot change the previously living cells. This equation is a falsifiable local approximation; saturation, drug-luciferase interactions, extracellular ATP, extraction efficiency and spike/endogenous differences can invalidate it. Calibrate the valid interval rather than assert global linearity. A same-matrix blank is needed for beta; a separate viability-gated imaging/count channel supplies N with its own error model. A raw nuclei total without live/dead discrimination is not automatically N.

Let theta govern biological state→N,m, and phi govern the observation channel. Source and later biological measurements may update both. In the candidate, a declared post-lysis calibration observation can update phi and uncertainty but has **no direct parameter write into theta**. Biological feedback is then scored through the calibrated observation likelihood, with uncertainty retained. If counts are unavailable, the update concerns ATP content `N*m`, not survival. The inheritable object is the typed assignment of measurement interventions to observer parameters plus the source-trained observation-error structure, not one corrected assay value.

This is a distinct proposed constraint from unconstrained end-to-end joint error minimization. It is familiar modular/cut inference and causal measurement design, not claimed new mathematics. That fact does not decide finite-sample utility: the specific biological/measurement invariance and robustness at equal cost must be tested. Conversely the restriction is not guaranteed helpful. A correctly specified joint model with sufficient data can match it; a cut may discard useful coupling when the same drug genuinely changes metabolism and survival. Ordinary calibrated regression and a same-factorization joint/cut likelihood must get every intervention flag, spike, blank and count that the candidate receives. If they implement the operation exactly, no separate updater-primitive claim survives; a validated restriction or finite-cost training advantage might still remain.

## Concrete minimal experiment and early falsifiers

First stage is a measurement-intervention challenge, not a new drug screen. Use an authenticated existing aliquot series or explicitly acquired small matched assay package containing live counts and ATP, with sufficient material to split lysates. The current pass has **not** authenticated such an open numerical asset. Do not reinterpret old exposed CTG labels or generate simulated observations as this package.

Before outcomes, freeze the physical units, predefined2D/3D contexts, concentration/time and measurement setting; a baseline/untreated and a named treatment package; each culture's independently calibrated live count; post-lysis unspiked/spiked aliquots; same-matrix blanks and calibration range. Keep all aliquots of a culture together. Include at least two nonzero spike levels if feasible: one nonzero spike identifies a slope locally, but a second provides a real linearity falsifier. Background and extraction controls are not optional free information. The cell-count target and replicate precision must be defined by the assay, not selected after an apparent correction gain.

Step1: compare the proposed observation equation to held-out aliquot responses. Nonlinearity or unequal endogenous/spike recovery stops the simple observer law; do not add an arbitrary network to force fit. If the observer intervention does not measurably affect the raw assay beyond repeatability, there is no repair headroom for this setting.

Step2: start all methods from the identical source model/history and reveal exactly the same small calibration package. Test predictions for held-out independent cultures/treatments whose live counts remain withheld. Also check paired invariance: changing only post-lysis s must not change the reconstructed prelysis N beyond predeclared count/assay uncertainty. This provides a direct error diagnostic on real measurements; it is not a drug-response improvement by itself. If strong ordinary joint/direct methods already maintain it, reject the claim that their repeated error motivates this mechanism rather than weakening them.

Step3 is conditional: only if a model-error headroom and observer-law validity are found, test whether observer-restricted updates reduce **future independent live-count/ranking error** more efficiently than the strongest models. Freeze a meaningful assay-derived functional margin and culture-level uncertainty before that numerical access. A merely exact invariance check or lower ATP reconstruction error cannot promote the method. Current absence of an authenticated package and assay precision prevents freezing an honest useful margin; therefore no numerical launch is authorized by this report.

## Strong same-information/equal-budget controls

Direct context/drug/ATP/spike/blank/count conditional regression, including a source-trained nonlinear episodic predictor; ordinary pooled union refit; replay/continual update; explicit calibrated observation likelihood with joint inference; **the same observation law and modular/cut inference**; simple ATP+count mixture; learned-target/vector optimizer using identical flags; standard uncertainty/active allocation; and equal-total-cost program search over the same observation/update constraints. Give every arm every paid feature, context, source outcome and control. Count culture material, counts, spikes, blanks, source fitting, tuning, deployment and search. A candidate must not receive counts while its opponents receive ATP alone.

A same-law implementation can decide exact operation equality, but copying the candidate under a baseline name is not a performance proof. The useful comparison is whether restriction-led training generalizes better under actual sparse calibration versus flexible joint fitting/strong direct prediction, and whether an ordinary same-law solver fully accounts for that improvement. If the latter does, credit the observation restriction, not a novel optimizer. Measurement intervention labels must be supplied prospectively; phenotype-based posthoc routing is forbidden.

Potential inheritance test: learn observer error structure/routing on independent source contexts, reset theta to the same later-task start, and compare inherited versus reset observation rule at matched training and assay budget. A world-model contribution still needs the structured biological state representation to beat an informed direct predictor; two scalar latent quantities are not by themselves a virtual cell. Agent contribution requires better paid measurement choices; RSI additionally compares improved versus original routing/search strategy G1/G0 on fresh tasks from identical U/model/history and total cost. A better calibrated predictor is not proof of any of these.

## Decision and exact stop scope

Retain **one concrete candidate for adversarial review**: measurement-intervention-informed restriction of feedback writes. No algorithmic novelty, repeated-error prevalence, public executable dataset, performance or RSI gain is established. Do not claim it fixes past failed mixed laws. Stop it if the assay mismatch is absent in the chosen setting, the local observation equation fails, the extra calibration is not worth its real cost, or strong same-information direct/joint methods do not exhibit the proposed error and match future utility. If shared constraints explain all gain, retain a possible validated observation-law contribution and drop the updater novelty claim. If no lawful paired package exists, close this finite experiment without reopening protected labels. Global research remains active.
