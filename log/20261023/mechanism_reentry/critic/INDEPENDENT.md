## Typed levels: predictor, update rule U, and improver G

Let theta encode an agent's policy and observation-/action-conditioned predictions. Let h encode update history. The learned rule is

`targets, h_next = T_eta(predictions(theta), predictions(theta_EMA), actions, rewards, terminal, h)`;

`U_eta(theta,h,tau) = theta - alpha * grad_theta D(stopgrad(targets), predictions(theta))`, plus stated auxiliary objectives and optimizer state.

The rule's parameters eta therefore encode a **procedure for updating another model**, even though eta itself is numerically a parameter vector. The outputs y,z are learned prediction semantics, not guaranteed interpretable cell states. The meta-network takes agent predictions, not raw environment observations, and shares action-dimension weights; this is an explicit transfer bias rather than free access to biological mechanisms.

The improver `G` is the outer procedure that transforms `(eta, optimizer/meta-value state, population experiences)` into eta_next by differentiating post-update return through multiple applications of U. The article truncates the differentiable window at20inner updates. It additionally trains a meta-value function, normalizes advantages and averages separately Adam-normalized per-agent meta-gradients. The population and reset-lifetime design are part of G's effective training distribution/budget.

Improved held-out performance after discovery is evidence, as reported by the authors, of a useful inherited U_eta and transfer across RL tasks. It does not alone establish that a revised improver G1 generates better future update rules than G0 from the same starting U and same new evidence/budget. A fixed outer Adam does **not** logically preclude improvement in G: learned search proposals, a learned stateful U-generation component, objectives or curricula can be part of G. The source simply does not provide the common-start G1-versus-G0 experiment required for that stronger claim. Its meta-RNN history state can support within-lifetime adaptation; a changing hidden state by itself is not evidence of recursively improving the improver.

## Exact operation difference and a checked boundary

DiscoRL generates detached targets rather than merely a scalar differentiable objective. A precise limited distinction: with `T(theta)=(-theta_y,theta_x)`, the semi-gradient of `0.5*||theta-stopgrad(T(theta))||^2` is `(theta_x+theta_y,theta_y-theta_x)`. Cross derivatives are+1 and-1. No twice-differentiable static scalar potential has this gradient on an open set. `target_field_check.py` checks this operation and equality to an ordinary stateful vector updater at four fixed points; `TARGET_FIELD_CHECK.json` PASS. This is an analytic operation witness, not learned biological performance.

Crucially, an ordinary stop-gradient target loss implements the **same** field exactly, as does a conventional vector-valued learned optimizer. Thus “nonconservative,” “targets instead of scalar losses,” “feedback-conditioned future target,” or “learn prediction semantics” alone is not a new MAESTRO contribution. This equality is scoped to the proposed operation; it does not assert equal finite-sample training, compute, robustness, or all possible biology-aware designs. A direct same-interface Disco-style target learner must be a comparator, not an allegedly incapable baseline.

## One finite-resource hypothesis worth retaining, without promotion

Conditional hypothesis: under a fixed small number of newly purchased real functional observations in a new cell context, an inherited target-generation rule can use paired molecular-state/function residuals to improve later drug/target prioritization more efficiently than refitting the same-information predictor. This changes what the model learns from each observation, rather than attaching another final prediction head. Its still-unspecified contribution must be a testable biological restriction on target formation or feedback credit assignment, not merely inserting cell features into DiscoRL.

Concrete available functional asset from historical authenticated evidence: SCHEMATIC seven-line genetic-combination scores can support a **retrospective context-specific target-combination prediction** task. They do not currently provide authenticated paired cell-state transitions, and published score normalization depends on the full screen. Consequently they cannot support free sparse real-feedback replay, actual drug-response claims or world-model necessity as-is. This review does not reopen those outcomes. A genuine drug application needs an authenticated source/target split over cell contexts, molecular measurements with same-assay matched intervention/time, independent functional viability or drug-decision outcomes, and control-only/source-only endpoint normalization with all required measurements charged. No such newly qualified asset is established by these four literature requests.

Before any numerical opening: freeze physical task units/context holdouts, actual feedback/control cost, legal calibration and normalization, molecular observation availability at decision time, query horizon and minimal clinically/scientifically meaningful decision margin. At most propose a bounded single-update proof-of-operation using real new feedback after these are satisfied; do not simulate an assay or use hidden endpoint data as a differentiable environment and call it real feedback.

Strong controls: direct prediction with every identical purchased observation and representation; union refit; replay/continual learning; same-information active learning; simple mixture; meta-learned scalar objective; **detached learned-target/Disco-style U and vector-valued learned optimizer**; equal-total-cost program search including source learning/tuning and deployment. Compare world-state input to a matched direct residual representation; agent acquisition to fixed/random/standard acquisition using the identical U; inherited U to reset-U with equal source budget. For joint gains cross these factors. For G improvement, freeze common initial U/model/history and compare G0/G1 on later independent tasks, plotting validated improvement versus cumulative total cost; inherit an improved predictor alone only in the explicitly separate U test.

Success requires a predeclared meaningful decision benefit and uncertainty across actual independent contexts at equal total cost. Stop the narrow new-operation claim if the same-interface target comparator executes it exactly; retain possible sample-efficiency/robustness questions only if a concrete differing constraint remains. Stop sparse-assay interpretation if normalization requires unpaid outcomes; stop cell-world-model contribution if only functional endpoints exist; stop RSI claim without common-start improver comparison. Insufficient precision is inconclusive, not equivalence.

## Resource and decision implications

The paper's discovery was not low-budget biology:128agents/1,024TPUv3cores/64hours for Disco57 and206agents/2,048cores/60hours for Disco103, with tens to hundreds of millions of environment steps per agent lifetime. These are author-reported resources, not MAESTRO measurements. Porting the full training regime to scarce assays is not justified. Offline simulator training may help but would need separate simulator-to-real validation and cannot make simulated rewards actual functional feedback.

Decision: keep the source as a strong, concrete baseline for future precise cell-feedback designs; **do not launch a fit, acquire more assay data, or call the target-generation interface our innovation**. Current result is a narrower prior-art and falsifiability improvement, with no new core GO, biological performance, inheritance benefit or RSI result.
