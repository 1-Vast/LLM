## Unresolved empirical issue and minimal discriminating check

The law to test would first be frozen: within a named drug/time regime, transport of a predeclared state representation remains source-valid while a survival or explicitly net-growth component changes. Do not infer this factorization from a good fit to terminal counts.

The least ambitious useful falsifier targets transport invariance before fitting a complete killing field: prelabel or sort baseline states and track living descendants under matched treated/untreated conditions, with non-destructive temporal state/lineage measurements and death events; track divisions as needed to separate death from net growth. Systematic treated-state migration **before death**, absent from the frozen transport predictions and beyond prespecified tracking/assay uncertainty, refutes the kill-only restriction.
