## Actual application and mathematical operation already published

The application is prioritizing perturbation combinations that raise measured CXCL9 protein secretion in AsPC1/BxPC3 pancreatic cancer cells. This is an actual protein assay, not post-RNA-derived scores or an inferred snapshot trajectory. It is not proof of T-cell recruitment, anti-tumor efficacy or safe therapeutic improvement; the paper itself discusses context-dependent chemokine effects and future multiplexed outcomes.

The source uses a curated signed logic network, continuous logic-ODE simulation and context-specific parameters theta. Each model is trained by ordinary constrained parameter optimization of squared assay error plus l1 regularization on network edge strengths. Operationally its loop is:

    theta_(r+1) = optimize_theta [sum_(a,y in D_r union B_r) (F_theta(a)-y)^2 + lambda ||k||_1],
    B_r selected using ensemble mean, interval width, their sum, or random sampling.

The trained models are further optimized after adding newly measured conditions. This is real model-based active learning plus ordinary parameter reoptimization. It cannot be renamed as our novel feedback updater, and its fixed acquisition functions are not improved-improver RSI. An ODE-based operator need not be identical to a generic static KRR; the exact ordinary-retraining equivalence is instead that the source's specified update is itself optimization of the augmented dataset objective.

The paper reports initial two independent screenings with duplicates per cell line and subsequently two experimental active-learning rounds, with four chosen conditions per strategy per round. Synthetic acquisition comparisons come from simulations of a fitted AsPC1 model plus noise; they are not independent biological support for discovering the correct network. Real-round differences in ensemble interval widths are not by themselves held-out prediction calibration or a prospective all-candidate accuracy oracle.

## New substantive failure: more feedback can change the target without identifying its cause

The actual wet feedback exposed a mismatch absent from the model: some predicted strong secretion combinations gave lower measured CXCL9, and elevated Caspase-3 was associated with reduced secretion. The authors hypothesize reduced viable secreting cells. Their Methods explicitly correct by adding normalized CXCL9 and normalized Cas3 signals, then min-max rescaling the sum. They acknowledge the model currently lacks apoptosis effects. Author Figure5.R loads a precomputed rounds1_and_2_CXCL9_values.RData and plots CXCL9_correction; the inspected entrypoint does not independently validate that correction. This is a reading of source/code, not our recalculation.

At most the toy measurement equation y=N*q represents endpoint bulk secretion, with viable secreting abundance N and per-cell secretion q. Even this is favorable: a real accumulated supernatant readout can depend on their entire histories, not only endpoint values. An added apoptosis activity a does NOT identify N unless an independently justified mapping a=h(N,history,...) is supplied. For arbitrary positive c, N'=c*N,q'=q/c yields identical y; absent a known relation between a and N, the second channel does not eliminate that ambiguity. Caspase activity is not automatically a quantitative census of viable secreting cells.

Consequently the source's corrected target z=rescale(norm(y)+norm(a)) is neither established q nor preserved bulk y. Holding y fixed, increasing normalized apoptosis can increase z. That is mathematically true of the stated correction, not a claim that every observed ranking reversed or that the authors fabricated data. It is defensible only as a declared composite objective or an explicitly validated assay correction, not automatically 'rescued true secretion'. This supplies a real reason to distrust an apparently better feedback-repaired world model: it may optimize a changed endpoint while hiding the actual functional loss.

An additional operational issue is min-max normalization across assay conditions. For a legal retrospective feedback study, calibration constants must be fixed from source/support controls available at the round; an unqueried maximum cannot be a free normalizer. This is a benchmark-design issue to authenticate, not an assertion that this source's original wet procedure necessarily leaked labels.

## Why I do not nominate a two-channel updater now

A tempting mechanism would update a joint secretion/abundance model using both channels, with separately declared observation operators and a viability constraint. But the current source does not establish the required quantitative observation mapping. A shared two-output regressor or ordinary joint retraining receives exactly the same extra labels, so 'we used apoptosis feedback' alone supplies no distinct operation or finite-budget gain. A corrected target inferred from the same endpoints cannot serve as its own validation truth. The new source is direct prior art for network-constrained AL and reoptimization, while its unvalidated measurement inversion blocks promoting the obvious next elaboration.

A useful bounded next question would require independently measured secretion and viable-abundance/viability outcomes, known grouping and support/query access. Then compare direct same-information multivariate prediction, head-only calibration, ordinary joint model reoptimization and a declared constrained observation update. If deployment utility is bulk secretion subject to viability, evaluate that declared utility directly; do not replace it with an apoptosis-rewarding composite and call the latter recovered biology. No source-qualified new algorithm or actual corresponding independent endpoint panel is established here, so this remains a rejected candidate, not a request to fit it.

## Fair comparisons, success/stop and decision

If a genuinely qualified paired-function dataset later appears, all compared methods must share exact source data, purchased support endpoints, legal calibration constants, observation operators, query universe and total tuning/optimization cost. Strong direct predictors include a full-history conditional model and a multi-output predictor with the same measured channels; ordinary controls include constrained nonlinear refit of the same theta, joint retraining, residual/head adaptation, standard continuous/active learning and equal-budget update-rule search. The measurement contribution must be separated from parameter-update and acquisition contributions. More informative labels are charged and given to ordinary comparators.

Success would first be independent agreement with a declared measured utility/observation operator, then later-task decision improvement over the best same-information ordinary competitor. Stop if a composite correction changes the objective without external validation, the observation mapping remains nonidentifiable, only selected queried conditions have follow-up labels for counterfactual policy comparisons, or ordinary refitting matches utility/cost. Improving acquisition needs held-task equal-budget policy comparison; RSI needs a changed policy/updater-producing strategy to improve descendants from common starts, not merely narrower self-reported ensemble intervals.
