## The nine-feedback information bottleneck

Biology challenged the proposed 4 no-inhibitor controls + 5 drug/IFNG_TNFA feedback design: if A includes four free control/nuisance degrees and five independent drug action/residual shifts, it may have full row rank on all nine observations, making P_A=0. I explicitly accept this. In particular, one observation per drug cannot distinguish that drug's action correction from a context-gated edge correction. Our candidate's projected trigger has no evidence to act on under that saturated design.

It would be invalid to remove the strong action/nuisance comparator merely to manufacture a nonzero residual projection. Additional noncollinear within-drug stimulus feedback, or externally authenticated and predeclared sharing/calibration constraints, could change the rank. Neither is provided by the nine responses themselves. A source-only fixed edge can still make falsifiable held-stimulus predictions from one feedback observation, but that is evaluating a previously fixed restriction, not discovering an edge defect from that observation. Method independently agreed that one scalar cannot identify edge versus action and requires source-only fixed candidates or at least two noncollinear feedback contexts.

Root's proposed nine-to-fifteen ordinary passage-transfer diagnostic remains useful to examine prediction/error reproducibility; it is not a test of my edge-attribution operation.
