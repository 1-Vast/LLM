# Independent response to root's stage-3 proposal

2026-10-23. Root proposed an explicitly conventional diagnostic, before numerical response fitting: initial four raw assay files only; AL1/2 unopened; local sample×condition bead cleaning; logMFI and allowed blank/positive anchors; inhibitor-blocked direct additive/ridge-interaction/Hamming-KRR controls; passage transfer. No peer opinions were read.

**Position: reasonable exploratory baseline audit after schema-only gate; it is not a new-mechanism experiment and cannot establish RSI.**

## Minimum comparisons and precautions

- Preserve screen, sample, condition and bead-event identifiers. Estimate robust summaries separately per sample×condition; do not let held-out passage or condition determine other samples' trimming. FACS event counts estimate technical measurement precision, not independent biological sample count.
- Define target as log10(MFI_condition)−log10(MFI_blank) with blank controls explicitly available to every method and counted in any acquisition budget. A positive anchor is optional for passage-scale adjustment; do not divide by a nearly zero stimulus response. If included, predefine fallback and share that measured anchor with all comparators. Anchor measurements are observable interventions, not cost-free metadata.
- Separate two tasks: (i) held-out inhibitor response within a familiar passage, and (ii) familiar inhibitors at another passage. They answer intervention extrapolation and context transfer respectively. A P12/P14 label permits passage blocking, but metadata alone does not prove a chronological causal sequence or separate assay dates.
- For held-out inhibitor blocks, a drug identity one-hot model cannot estimate a new drug coefficient. Hamming kernels over drug identities reduce to an undifferentiated new-inhibitor prior. This is a legitimate weak-information baseline but insufficient alone to establish scaffold contribution. Include permitted target/pathway descriptors or features derived from the same fixed graph supplied to a mechanistic model, with source-only tuning. Otherwise a graph wins through privileged prior information rather than dynamics.
- Include copy-no-inhibitor/control response, additive cytokine effects, additive stimulus+inhibitor, and low-rank/mixed-effects response surfaces when information permits. Compare training-only selected ridge interactions and graph-informed kernels at matched access. Known-inhibitor passage transfer should include direct source-passage copy and simple blank/positive-anchor affine corrections, ordinary pooled refit and a source/target mixture. A source-only choice must not be replaced with the post-hoc best diagnostic method.
- The five initial inhibitor identities, not dozens of condition rows or bead events, are the main inhibitor-extrapolation units. Report per-inhibitor errors, signed functional ranking errors and passage dependence. A five-unit uncertainty interval will be weak and should not be presented as a large independent validation.
- Interpret residuals against replicate/sample variability and control uncertainty. A repeatable residual can motivate a hypothesis, but does not identify clamp error rather than scaffold misspecification, off-target effects, assay scaling or context change. Do not adapt the intervention set/endpoint based on which residual looks most favorable.

## STOP and advancement boundary

Stop before fitting if raw identities/controls cannot be authenticated, sample×condition grouping cannot be reconstructed, or schema exposes only already screen-normalized outcomes. A retrospective normalized benchmark could still be defined separately but would not pass this local-feedback contract.

After the conventional diagnostic, stop this mechanism search on these initial files if dominant residuals are non-reproducible across allowed samples/passages, matched ordinary direct/pooled methods remove them, or there are too few independent units to formulate and prospectively test a distinct correction. A small nonzero residual alone is not a GO. Exact practical effect thresholds should be fixed for the next separately reviewed mechanism test after the exploratory scale/noise audit, not retroactively imposed to call this audit successful.

Advance only to a **separate candidate proposal** if an action-conditional residual repeats outside the fitting unit, is large enough to affect secretion decisions beyond assay uncertainty, and suggests a precise low-complexity operation distinguishable from same-information ordinary calibration. Seal this exploratory diagnostic before formulation/evaluation on later AL1/2 data. AL1/2 are a potential later check, not automatically globally unseen confirmation after designers have read the paper. None of these stages supplies enough common-start updater/editor tasks for Level 3 RSI.
