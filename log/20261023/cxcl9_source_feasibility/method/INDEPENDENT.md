## What the actual primary methods permit

Application can legitimately be discovery of perturbation combinations increasing **24 h secreted CXCL9** in AsPC1/BxPC3. This is a proximal immunomodulatory output, not demonstrated tumor killing or beneficial immune recruitment. The methods use two cell lines, duplicate conditions, five initial pathway inhibitors at 5 micromolar, cytokine stimuli, an activity-clamped logic-ODE scaffold, and 33 lifetime plus 61 edge parameters before network processing. Acquisition already compares greedy, uncertainty, their sum, and random sampling. Real new measurements enter ordinary further optimization of the ensemble. Therefore “mechanistic cell model + active wet feedback + refit” is direct existing work, not an MAESTRO innovation.

Screen-wide min/max normalization (with highest training value capped .8) and normalized CXCL9 plus Cas3 correction must not be silently reused as a sparse local measurement oracle. Required source-feasibility questions are whether raw MFI, individual duplicate identifiers, screen/batch identifiers, fixed controls and correction inputs are available, and whether the published masks encode actual chronology. Full-screen retrospective prediction is still a possible study if labeled honestly; it is not prospective assay efficiency.

## A specific restricted operation, and its conventional counterpart

Let a fixed scaffold produce endpoint predictions F_c(s, a; theta_c), with cytokine setting s and target activity input a. Replace the paper's inhibitor target clamp a=0 by a residual-activity parameter eta_d, and update eta from newly allowed secretion measurements while keeping theta frozen. A scalar or low-dimensional eta shared across cytokine combinations gives a genuinely constrained hypothesis: the vector of expected secretion responses must lie on the low-dimensional response manifold { (F_c(s_1,eta),...,F_c(s_m,eta)): eta in [0,1] }. It can be falsified by failure of held-out combinations to remain on that manifold within independently estimated assay error. This is more specific than unconstrained refitting.

But this is ordinary constrained nonlinear calibration. Adding an inherited Gaussian prior gives standard MAP/hierarchical random effects; linearizing F gives ridge/Gauss–Newton. A separate eta per drug×cell line with no law tying tasks together adds no reusable update mechanism. A shared eta across lines assumes comparable effective exposure despite transport/metabolism/context differences and is empirically testable, not guaranteed by matching extracellular dose. Target-level pooling assumes implementations have shared effective action, a known fragile assumption; it cannot be imported from annotation alone.

No novel update law follows merely from imposing this restriction. The possible opportunity is empirical **manifold sufficiency under limited feedback**, with a future distinct mechanism only if a repeatable conventional failure and a new corrective operation emerge. Endpoint validation need not uniquely identify physical occupancy. Conversely, secretion-only fitted eta cannot automatically be called measured target occupancy.

## What this can and cannot establish about a world model

A held-out combination gain could support reuse of the scaffold's compositional response structure. It would not prove real state dynamics, because the described assay provides a single 24 h output and no measured intermediate state trajectory. Endpoint maps can often reproduce such data. Dynamic claims require time-resolved or intervention-order information, or an independently justified dynamic prediction task. A static endpoint limit of the same graph and a direct predictor given the same pathway/cytokine/feedback features are required controls.

There is also structural aliasing: in a simple endpoint path y = theta * eta * s, action efficacy eta and signaling gain theta are indistinguishable from outputs, even across many s. This blocks a unique physical interpretation; it does **not** prevent predictive restriction from helping. A fair study should test predictive transport of the chosen restriction, and compare an equally sized pathway/context-gain update rather than claiming action-specific mechanism from fit quality alone.

## Minimum lawful falsification design, conditional on source availability

Before any response fitting, authenticate raw observables and chronology, define one secretion utility and fixed control normalization using allowed measurements only, and freeze budget and splits. Use source settings to select hyperparameters; hold out drug implementations/combination families when available, not duplicates of the same condition. Freeze the scaffold before target feedback. At each identical feedback budget compare partial-action calibration with ordinary accumulated-data refit, scalar/mixed-effects residual calibration, equal-dimensional context/pathway-gain calibration, strong direct prediction, static graph endpoint prediction, ordinary continual updating, and published acquisition plus random acquisition. Track total initial fitting, adaptation, hyperparameter and search costs. A latent calibrated action variable need not be physically identifiable to pass this predictive test.

Success requires an independently selected restriction producing repeatable later-condition **decision** benefit beyond the strongest same-information comparator, with uncertainty grouped at the actual independent task/batch level and a predefined practically meaningful margin. A precise numerical margin cannot responsibly be set before measurement scale, noise and useful experimental hit definition are authenticated. Stop if lawful local measurement cannot be reconstructed, independent tasks cannot be separated, or the action manifold has no predictive increment beyond matched simple calibration/refit. No large program/editor stack is justified by this pilot.

## Task count and RSI boundary

Two cell lines are two biological contexts, not a large bank of independent improvement generations. Duplicate wells are not later tasks. Distinct inhibitor/combination blocks may support limited within-assay held-out prediction but do not automatically yield independent source→meta-validation→future tasks, especially when all share one batch, scaffold and normalization. The five initial inhibitors do not by themselves establish enough tasks; the exact public experiment table must be inspected without numerical outcome fitting first.

Reusing eta priors is knowledge/model inheritance and at most Level 1 adaptation. Choosing a better restriction on previous tasks can be Level 2 only if a frozen program improves genuinely later tasks. Level 3 needs old/new improvement policies supplied the same initial updater and history, allowed the same total search/feedback budget, and evaluated by the quality/efficiency of their **new descendants** on unseen tasks. The two-line source by itself does not substantiate that design. Repeated masks/seeds are dependent counterfactual replays, not new biological generations.

## Independent conclusion

This source is valuable as a direct prior-work control and potentially a narrow assay-calibration benchmark. It currently provides neither a new action-update algorithm nor evidence that dynamic state simulation or RSI is necessary. Proceed only with metadata/raw-measurement feasibility, retaining the low-dimensional manifold hypothesis as an explicitly conventional restricted model. Do not escalate it to the main research mechanism until distinct technical content, lawful feedback and sufficient independent tasks exist.
