## Application and legitimate endpoint

Selecting cytokine/inhibitor conditions that increase pancreatic cancer cell CXCL9 secretion is a legitimate proximal immunomodulatory discovery task. CXCL9 is the measured functional output; secretion is more proximal to a functional phenotype than RNA alone. It is not a demonstration of T-cell recruitment, antitumor activity, therapeutic index, or tumor killing. A useful public-data task can be ranking a declared, finite set of unobserved conditions within the original cell line/dose/time protocol. Retrospective reveal simulation is feedback from previously measured wet experiments; it is not a new prospective wet-lab discovery.

Methods describe AsPC1 and BxPC3, 24-hour exposure, inhibitor concentration 5 micromolar, 1% DMSO in inhibitor screens, blank controls, IFNgamma/TNFalpha, and duplicate conditions. Cytokine concentrations differ between the lines (10 and 1 ng/mL respectively as stated), so an across-line performance difference cannot isolate cell-intrinsic regulation from exposure differences. No extrapolation across dose or time is established by these endpoint screens.

## Pharmacology: useful prediction need not imply unique mechanism

Binary node clamping (stimulus activity 1, inhibitor target activity 0) is an explicit modeling convention. At one concentration it does not establish complete target inhibition, unique engagement, intracellular occupancy, or target exclusivity. The listed drugs target broad signaling regulators; annotations such as JAKi or RASi are action labels, not direct measurements of the cellular action actually achieved. At 5 micromolar, potency, off-target activity, permeability, feedback, and apoptosis are plausible competing explanations for discrepancies. This review does not assert that any particular off-target caused an observed response, since response values and selectivity data were not read.

A learned low-dimensional action map may nonetheless improve prediction without uniquely resolving these explanations. Test it against same-information ordinary regularized action calibration, a small function residual model, and full/partial ordinary refitting. A gain would establish useful constrained adaptation only. Do not call the map a measured occupancy or infer a corrected causal edge from that gain alone. Distinguishing a drug-action defect from an incorrect shared circuit requires predictions across multiple new contexts for the same drug and competitive model restrictions; biochemical engagement or orthogonal perturbations would strengthen a physical interpretation but are not prerequisites for a predictive test.

## Repeats, units, and information independence

“Every condition was measured in duplicates” does not specify independently cultured biological replicates, separate days, randomized plates, or technical duplicates. Until protocol metadata resolve this, the condition is the sampling/evaluation unit and duplicates stay together. Capture beads or flow events are analytical observations, not independent cells or independent perturbation experiments. The ten optimization restarts are computational restarts, not biological replication. Bootstrap parameter distributions cannot create new experimental units.

Splitting duplicate rows gives leakage. Random condition splits can be useful in-domain completion, but sharing inhibitors/cytokines is not unseen-drug generalization. With only two described lines, a leave-one-line experiment is at most two directional transfers and has a different-exposure caveat. It cannot establish broad inheritance or RSI efficiency. Train/validation/test units, original screen/batch identity, duplicate origin, and all drug/cytokine encodings must be enumerated without outcomes before a claim is frozen.

## Normalization and apoptosis: exact oracle issue

Methods set the lowest MFI condition to 0 and highest to 1, with a training maximum adjustment to 0.8. They then sum normalized CXCL9 and Caspase-3 measurements and rescale to 0–1. These are outcome-dependent transformations. An unexamined processed MIDAS table is not automatically a lawful sparse-assay oracle.

1. An anchor min/max computed over a complete screen uses unrevealed outcomes. Masking rows afterward does not remove this information. Fixed whole-screen scaling can be declared as a transductive endpoint with full-screen calibration supplied equally to all methods, but that differs from a prospective sparse-feedback claim. Do not silently use it to claim that only selected outcomes were observed.
2. Screen-specific scales can make outcomes from different screens incomparable even after row concatenation. The exact grouping and 0.8 convention must be reconstructed from code and metadata before replay; the Methods wording alone does not fix every implementation detail.
3. Recomputing min/max from each revealed subset changes previous labels and the target definition, creating artificial learning. Prefer a raw/standard-calibrated output or an affine map fixed from a predeclared initial calibration set or assay standards. Future candidates may exceed its range; avoid clipping away the evidence.
4. The apoptosis correction uses another response assay. If the oracle supplies corrected CXCL9, both CXCL9 and matched Caspase-3 measurement costs, controls, and calibration must be included. A method cannot receive all candidates' Caspase values for free while claiming sparse functional feedback. If matching raw data are absent, restrict the task accordingly or describe the preprocessed-transductive limitation.

Summing normalized secretion with normalized caspase is a phenomenological correction, not direct secretion per viable cell. It can increase the corrected target when apoptosis rises. Thus a “best corrected CXCL9” condition may differ from a desirable non-toxic secretion condition. With raw matched assays, report raw secretion and apoptosis separately and predeclare the decision objective (for example secretion subject to an apoptosis constraint). Do not reinterpret Caspase-3 as absolute viable-cell count. The original threshold 0.2 is on its chosen processed scale and is not a universal clinically meaningful CXCL9 threshold.

## What is sufficient to run a useful-update pilot

No full occupancy-state-function triple is required. Sufficient minimum is (a) recoverable condition identifiers, doses, times and cell context; (b) outcomes attached to independent condition groups; (c) either raw CXCL9 plus matched correction components/calibration or an explicitly limited fixed processed endpoint; (d) pre-outcome split and exact initial reveal set; (e) an independently withheld set of conditions with enough residual variation to compare updates; and (f) same information and total feedback/compute accounting for all competitors. Metadata audit first; fetch or inspect assay values only after a frozen oracle/split protocol. A convenient processed table alone is insufficient for prospective sparse-feedback claims.

For an exploratory finite-screen pilot, use held-out condition prediction and cumulative best-feasible-condition discovery regret/hit yield, evaluated at identical complete-assay budgets. Include naive/no-update, regularized direct response prediction, ordinary pooled refit, bounded low-dimensional action refit, fixed mechanistic-model active learning, random acquisition, and a simple hybrid. If a constrained action update is promising, compare which drug/context residuals are transferred and whether benefit survives disjoint validation conditions. Reuse of the same full candidate table across procedure editing requires a separate untouched evaluation set. Baselines need the same prior network/action annotations.

## What these data cannot establish alone

One terminal output with a curated network (33 node time-scale and 61 interaction parameters in the Methods) does not identify a unique internal trajectory, regulatory edge set, target engagement, or correction to signaling dynamics. Relative model fit cannot validate those mechanisms. The data can test endpoint decision usefulness and restricted update inductive bias, subject to the oracle qualifications. It cannot by itself establish tumor efficacy, broad multi-task inheritance, improved-improver RSI, or the necessity of an LLM research agent. A fixed acquisition function plus refitting is already implemented in this primary study; closing that loop is prior art.

## Gate and stopping conditions

GO only to a blinded schema/provenance audit, not to a core-innovation claim. REVISE the task if only normalized/corrected tables survive: explicitly transductive endpoint completion can still be an honest auxiliary task, but its weaker biological and prospective scope must be stated. STOP the sparse-feedback replay if unavailable raw/calibration/correction inputs prevent construction of the declared oracle; do not invent raw secretion or independence. STOP promotion if ordinary constrained adaptation or a direct predictor explains the same gain at matched cost. A numerical pilot, even if positive, is a feasibility result; further independent tasks and a distinct update mechanism are needed before inheritance/RSI work.

Unresolved: biological-vs-technical duplicate identity; condition counts and overlap across actual screens; raw bead/MFI and Caspase pairing availability; standards/plate batches; normalization groups and precise implementation; complete candidate-set availability; independence of original model curation from prospective replay conditions. These are inspectable metadata questions, not reasons to demand complete latent identification.
