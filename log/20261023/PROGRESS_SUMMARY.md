# 2026-10-23 research progress

## Synthesis

This cycle produced useful task-level feedback and several newly qualified public-data entry points, while repeated candidate falsifications did not support promoting a general-purpose or novel update core. Ordinary predictive or calibration utility appears in some bounded settings; it should be reported separately from evidence for transferable mechanisms or recursive self-improvement. Continue only with task-specific gates, authenticated outcomes, and stronger conventional baselines. All conclusions remain scoped to their individual studies; the overall research goal is not achieved.
