## Concrete application and evidence

Application: choose or engineer an NK-cell engager/CD16A product to preserve repeated tumor-cell killing over a 12-hour assay, rather than optimize only initial degranulation or the fraction of NK cells killing at least one target. This is preclinical functional prioritization, not a demonstrated patient or drug-discovery benefit.

The paper uses real primary human NK cells in microwells with multiple tumor targets, and a separate engineered NK92 comparison. It measures actual target/effector death with SYTOX Green plus permanent identity labels; some experiments required manual analysis because SYTOX detection or clustering prevented reliable automated counting. It reports contact times scored manually. There is no RNA, ATP, or live-count-derived death label in this proposal.

The biologically interesting separation is this: CD16A shedding inhibition can retain much initial cytotoxic activity while reducing serial killing, accompanied by longer contacts, clustering, and sometimes increased effector death. Thus good initial-kill or CD107a response need not rank sustained killing correctly. Batimastat is a broad MMP inhibitor, not a selective CD16A intervention. The authors additionally compare cleavable high-affinity CD16A and high-affinity noncleavable CD16A in NK92 cells, which strengthens but does not universalize the mechanism.

Important contrary and limiting details must remain:

- Primary NK cells with HL-60 show increased time in conjugation; that time fraction was not significantly elevated with Nalm-6.
- The engineered NK92 serial-killing difference with HL-60 was not observed with Nalm-6. Background NK92 killing of HL-60 is already high.
- Batimastat was toxic to Karpas-299, so that target was replaced for this comparison. It can affect other synaptic proteins and can increase NK death even without acimtamig.
- Noncleavable CD16A may still downregulate through internalization. Surface receptor loss does not uniquely measure shedding.
- The article itself cites earlier detachment/shedding observations (references 9 and 17). The biological connection is not invented here, and a state-machine implementation would not establish novelty over that prior work.

## What observations would distinguish update strategies?

At minimum a legal feedback episode needs donor, independent experiment, treatment, well and NK identity; initial target availability; temporal target/effector identity; contact on/off; actual target and NK death status; movie end and loss-to-follow-up; and observation cadence/quality flags. For each killed target one needs the post-death release time and whether another live target remained available. A final kill histogram and total conjugation fraction do not contain these histories.

The following patterns would separate competing explanations:

1. Lower probability or slower time to target death conditional on a live-target contact supports declining per-contact killing competence.
2. Similar first-contact death timing but longer dead-target attachment, conditional on an effector remaining alive and another target being available, supports a release/turnover limitation.
3. Similar release times but fewer new contacts while free supports search/motility limitation; the at-risk free time matters.
4. Effector death competes with every later kill and release event. Restricting to surviving NK cells can manufacture apparent preserved competence.

Finite hourly observations interval-censor death and contacts; the paper uses 1, 10, or 20 frames/hour in different experiments. These cadences cannot be silently pooled into exact event times. Several targets can contact one NK cell simultaneously, so a simplistic mutually exclusive four-state chain is an approximation, not an authenticated physical model. The reported fraction of time in conjugation stops at NK death, producing a treatment-dependent denominator. Its aggregate value alone cannot distinguish slow target killing, slow release, or changed survival.

The article excludes wells when an additional NK enters in the first two hours or the NK dies before interacting. Those exclusions may be appropriate to its biological analysis but must be accounted for in any prospective full-population prediction. Published serial-killer fractions condition on cytotoxic NK cells and use different thresholds (at least three target kills for primary NK, four for NK92). They are not interchangeable population clearance outcomes or independent task episodes.

## One operation, ordinary comparison, and why NONE

The most direct operation would update contact-to-kill, dead-target-to-release, free-to-contact, and effector-death transition distributions using observed episode risk sets, then predict remaining target clearance. With piecewise exponential hazards the update is the familiar event-count/exposure update; with a Gamma prior, posterior shape and rate add events and at-risk exposure. A semi-Markov version fits the same standard event-history likelihood with duration-dependent hazards and censoring.

This entire operation is ordinary multistate survival estimation/refitting. Calling each transition a virtual-cell module or choosing which one to refit from residuals does not itself change that operation. A transition-selection policy adds an algorithm-selection question, not automatic improvement of an improvement strategy. There is no independently identified new restriction, information reduction, or non-equivalent update algebra here; therefore no candidate core mechanism is promoted.

If the task existed, the proper ordinary comparator would use exactly the same contact/death/detachment observations and censoring, fit or update the same multistate likelihood, and tune transition complexity on the same development budget. Other required controls would include a strong trajectory direct predictor, complete retraining, sequential likelihood/continual updates, a simple mixture of kill-competence and turnover models, and equal-budget update-program selection. An active-learning comparison needs a real acquisition menu and costs; choosing which already-public well to reveal is only retrospective simulation. No favorable comparison can be claimed by giving the mechanistic model contact times while withholding them from the direct predictor.

## Public task qualification and stopping decision

The paper's Data Availability Statement reads: “All processed data are available in the main text, the supplementary materials, or upon request. Images, cell lines, and reagents are subject to materials transfer agreements (MTAs).” The associated-data listing names only eji70078-sup-0001-FigureS1-S6.pdf. The XML authenticates no public repository of per-cell time-resolved contacts, target/NK death, release events, donor metadata and censoring. The statement does not mean that every processed trajectory is public; “or upon request” is a material qualification. No author contact is authorized for this task.

Thus the genuinely public material authenticated here supports a scientific counterexample and published aggregate figures, but does **not** supply the claimed dynamic update task. Reconstructing trajectories from summaries or treating plotted donor points as serial feedback would manufacture information. The selected source implementation stops now without downloading its figures, fitting a toy NK world, or spending GET3 on a replacement source. This is a bounded source failure, not evidence that no suitable NK dataset exists anywhere.

A lawful future reopening would require independently authenticated public event-level trajectories from this source or a separately justified task, plus an update operation that is substantively specified before unsealing outcomes. The prospective falsifier would compare held-out-donor/experiment later clearance predictions after early feedback under equal information and budget. Failure to beat the ordinary event-history update and strong direct predictor, unstable gains across donor contexts, or inability to separate release from killing and survival would stop that configuration. A primary uncertainty-aware task metric and practical effect threshold must be frozen only after actual source schema and unit counts are known; this report does not invent them without an available task.
