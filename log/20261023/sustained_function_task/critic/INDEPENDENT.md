## Scientific ambiguity that must be resolved first

Later observed kill count combines at least intrinsic effector ability, target opportunity, target susceptibility, target exhaustion, time occupied in contact, and effector loss. Early first-kill speed can correlate with sustained killing without identifying intrinsic serial capacity: it can signal a target-rich well or a fragile target. Conversely a competent cell in an exhausted well can have zero later kills. Conditioning on future survival or future contacts removes part of the functional process and can select a different population. Dividing by realized future exposure is a different conditional estimand, not a free correction for unconditional sustained function.

Operational application: rank immune-cell-engager or cell-product conditions for sustained viable target clearance after an early observation window. This supports an intervention prioritization task only if multiple documented conditions exist. A single unperturbed cell population would support an initial forecasting diagnostic, not a drug decision or RSI claim. Effector identity and distinguishable target-death/effector-death observations are required; a death stain without cell identity cannot supply the intended task.
