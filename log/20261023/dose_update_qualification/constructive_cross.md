The critic challenged my proposed positive criterion: if the candidate operation is exactly same-forward same-solver MAP, a future requirement that it outperform that identical implementation would be an impossible contest. It recommended separating two claims: (i) independently establish a newly useful biological sharing law, or (ii) specify a genuinely different inference/robustness/computation operation and compare it against same-law ordinary inference.

I explicitly accepted this correction in an actual message. The original independent report's NO_CORE_GO remains; its positive criterion must not be read as permission to fit two identical algorithms hoping for a win. If a shared-potency law is the scientific contribution, give the law to an ordinary implementation to measure what it explains. Differences from predictors not using the law can establish its predictive/scientific value, but cannot establish that its MAP estimator is a novel updater. If an update-rule contribution is sought, a different restriction/resource property must exist first. This is an addendum scope correction, not a rewritten original claim.

The critic and I retain that full physical target occupancy is not universally required for a predictive curve-alignment result. It is needed only at the level warranted by a physical occupancy/engagement interpretation; conditional functional accuracy must receive its weaker honest interpretation when such anchoring is absent.

## Reverse challenge actually sent

I asked the critic to clarify its phrase that world-model necessity requires “predicted intervention state” to beat direct prediction. The full objective allows intervention-state, response, dynamics, or functional structure; it does not require every useful world model to output an explicit RNA forecast. A latent action-to-functional-response world could qualify if it supports independently verified intervention generalization and indispensable decision value beyond a same-information direct predictor. A static curve-fitting interface alone would not qualify. This distinction prevents silently narrowing the world-model goal to transcript prediction.

The critic actually replied and accepted this reverse challenge: world-model value can be established in the task-selected latent action->state/function mechanism, without mandatory explicit RNA forecasts. It also agreed that shared-control cancellation applies to the identical-centroid linear contrast, not automatically to cosine/angular targets, and that a simple covariance expression requires the stated independence assumptions. The exchange is therefore reciprocal rather than an unreturned question.

## Quota primary: what the original actually supports

Root's new primary, *How many cells resolve a perturbation direction? A closed-form, control-aware cell quota for single-cell perturbation screens*, was read as a source on measurement design, not our new algorithm. The Methods estimand is a treated centroid minus a control centroid in a fixed embedding, then its normalized direction. The first-order angular variance includes both arms. Discussion explicitly says between-well biological/plate variation is omitted, same-cell downsampling does not verify independent wells, shared-control downsampling does not exercise the control-pool term, and a replicate-well test remains to be done. Its machine-checked component is deterministic algebra, not its biological sampling assumptions or whole probabilistic result.

The original also explicitly reports that on the pool-limited Tahoe setting adaptive and flat allocation recovered the same similarity graph and neither recovered mechanism labels. That finding cannot be represented as demonstrated MoA-discovery improvement. The article's headline angular quotas are not validated functional-decision quotas.

The text extraction renders displayed equations as “Embedded Image”; I do not quote unread displayed formula strings as independently transcribed. The simple contrast identities below are my own elementary derivations, not an executed reproduction of the original supplementary calculations.

Let estimated condition displacements share the exact same estimated control centroid in the same frozen embedding:

    vA_hat = muA_hat - mu0_hat,
    vB_hat = muB_hat - mu0_hat.

Then vA_hat-vB_hat=muA_hat-muB_hat identically. Under independent treated centroids its covariance is SigmaA/nA+SigmaB/nB: the shared-control sampling error cancels. More generally, a linear contrast sum_i a_i v_i with sum_i a_i=0 cancels that one common control term. This is ordinary contrast design; it is not a new algorithm or world-model mechanism. If treated estimates are correlated, their covariance terms must still be carried; cancellation of the control algebra does not establish independence.

This cancellation **does not** hold generally for normalized directions, cosine similarities, nonlinear readouts, separately batch-corrected embeddings, different control pools, or condition-specific normalizations. For example, uA_hat-uB_hat where uA_hat=vA_hat/||vA_hat|| retains dependence on the common control. A decision that compares h(vA_hat) and h(vB_hat) inherits exact cancellation only for the appropriate common affine h (or another explicitly derived invariant), not for arbitrary functional predictions. Therefore neither “all individual directions have a control floor so all pairwise decisions have that floor” nor “shared controls cancel so controls never matter” is defensible.

The strongest design question is which *specific scientific decision functional* is required, and its full Jacobian/covariance relative to the joint estimand. If a simple raw contrast already suffices, optimize and benchmark that ordinary contrast. Do not buy extra controls to solve an irrelevant individual-angle precision problem. Conversely do not treat control-free raw differences as a valid substitute for a nonlinear mechanism-direction or absolute-functional-effect question.

## EmeraldBay: strongest source/claim challenges before any new labels

The README explicitly describes five-day transcriptomic profiles and a phenotype derived from cell-count proportions at that same endpoint. The two are not independent assays or temporally separated early-state->later-function evidence. They can still support a legitimate retrospective phenotype/representation task if named and controlled honestly. Splitting cells cannot create independent biological task replicates or a later functional endpoint.

A crucial ambiguity is absolute versus relative growth. Proportion measurements alone cannot distinguish the scenario in which all cell lines grow by a common factor from no change in their relative proportions. The README names a growth_rate field and time-zero controls but does not display a complete estimator or certify absolute total-cell recovery. Do not assert either an absolute growth-rate oracle or a particular mathematical growth formula until the original construction, controls and package normalization are authenticated. Pool competition and differential cell recovery remain alternative explanations to intrinsic drug-specific sensitivity.

RNA sample depth, surviving-cell composition, presence/missingness, cell recovery and QC can be directly coupled to that derived phenotype. Apparent RNA->function gains may be count/proportion reconstruction or survivor selection. A primary comparator should receive every legally observable count/QC/missingness/context statistic; an expression arm should be evaluated with fixed per-group cell budgets and without hidden count leakage if the claimed contribution is expression content. These are task-specific controls, not a claim that count information is illicit when the task honestly purchases it.

The README says sample distinguishes replicate treatments, but that label alone does not authenticate independent experimental runs or cultures. The raw summary's stated row count and condition dimensions include replicates/time-zero/combinations; infer neither independent sample number nor a complete dose curve from headline counts. First qualify sample/condition/line joins, explicit dose coverage, plate/run/control identity and how functions aggregate replicates. Its drug annotations were curated with Claude and checked against databases; these are annotations rather than measured selective action. Same-module potency sharing cannot be assumed from them.

The most important next-step challenge to root is to avoid using this new source merely as another large same-endpoint RNA->label screen after the old small gain. It would become materially distinct if it supplies a prespecified decision application or a testable sharing law, legally isolated context/drug families, and a functional readout/assay timing contract that can distinguish the mechanism from ordinary count/QC/direct and nonlinear curve/EB baselines. Source qualification may be worthwhile before fitting, but source size, matched IDs, a quota paper, and a readme do not resolve the missing updater law. Do not inspect the new functional payload and then choose the module or desired error signature.

## Disposition

Retain NO_CORE_GO for shared shift as an updater. Retain the possibility of a new biological sharing-law study or a different measured-function task, without reopening historical stops. Quota is relevant ordinary measurement-design evidence with a narrow estimand; EmeraldBay is a potentially useful source requiring qualification, not authenticated independent functional confirmation. World-model, agent, inheritance, joint and improved-improver gains remain unestablished.
