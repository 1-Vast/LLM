## Quota preprint: actual reading and algebra scope

Read local quota_primary.txt Methods “The angular-error model and the cell quota” and Discussion of **How many cells resolve a perturbation direction? A closed-form, control-aware cell quota for single-cell perturbation screens**, DOI10.64898/2026.10.02.754788. This is a preprint. Text extraction loses several equations as “Embedded Image”; I do not claim a complete equation-level reproduction from this text. No author empirical claim, calibration estimate or machine-checked proof was independently verified by me.

Its estimand is the direction of v=mu_t-mu_c in a fixed embedding, with centroid sampling noise and tangent normalization projector. Its shared-control issue is a legitimate assay-design consideration, but how it affects our *decision estimand* must be derived rather than copied. Root's proposed exact linear-contrast check is ordinary covariance algebra, not a new cell-learning mechanism or functional success.

Let v_i=t_i-c using exactly the same random control centroid c. If treatment centroids are mutually independent and independent of c, then Cov(v_i,v_j)=Cov(c) for i!=j. Without these independence assumptions the general covariance contains Cov(t_i,t_j)-Cov(t_i,c)-Cov(c,t_j)+Cov(c). Correlated plate effects, joint normalization or a learned shared embedding may violate the simple independent-centroid model.

For fixed linear contrast sum_i h_i v_i, the control term is exactly -(sum_i h_i)c; its standalone covariance contribution is (sum_i h_i)^2 Cov(c). The +1,-1 contrast cancels the same c algebraically, while an absolute effect (sum h=1) does not. The cancellation of the control random variable in the linear expression is exact even with correlations; the additive decomposition of total variance still needs the appropriate cross terms. Comparing two normalized directions/cosines or nonlinear functional readouts is a different statistic: each has a different normalization/Jacobian and sum h=0 does not automatically remove control noise. No claim is made that the author validated this cross-condition covariance term or our task's functional decision noise.

I sent these conditions to root and constructive; constructive agrees cancellation is limited to same-centroid linear contrasts, not automatic cosine/angle or nonlinear-function cancellation. No new run was made here; any root frozen arithmetic must remain labeled an ordinary synthetic/analytic verification.

## Emerald Bay: metadata only

The README explicitly describes the drug-phenotype measure as derived from **cell-count proportions at a five-day endpoint** in a MOSAIC pool, alongside transcriptomic profiles. This authenticates the author's stated assay type, not absolute viability or independent population growth. I read README introductory/feature prose only and did not read response arrays.

Required next metadata question: what exact formula maps observed compositional counts, baseline inoculum, controls, time and capture/cell-identity effects to the released growth measure? Is there an absolute-count/spike-in or externally calibrated total-population reference? Are controls shared, independent, pooled or matched by plate/batch? Does normalization use the whole treatment panel or future conditions? Does released capture composition represent viable cell abundance or selective cell recovery? Without these facts, call the endpoint relative pooled abundance/growth by its authenticated formula, not absolute drug killing.

Identifiability warning is scoped and algebraic: multiplying all latent absolute final cell counts in a pool by the same positive factor leaves their proportions invariant. Consequently proportions alone cannot identify common-mode absolute growth inhibition. This does not mean relative composition lacks utility; selective vulnerabilities, rank changes or properly calibrated ratios may be legitimate endpoints. Independent absolute calibration could resolve the ambiguity, and should be made available equally to direct competitors. No repeated numerical compositional counterexample was executed.
