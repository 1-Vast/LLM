# Frozen exact audit: common controls and the objective of acquisition

2026-10-23. New mathematical source-transfer check; no drug response labels, sampled cells, fits or paper-performance reproduction. Motivated by the newly retrieved quota paper, not a new biological candidate. Paper HTML equations are image-encoded and the text projection does not expose all symbols; use only the explicit stated two-arm sampling model and Discussion limitations. Do not claim to execute or falsify the author's full angular-quota algorithm.

Stipulated experiment: independent treated means T_A,T_B and one shared control mean C, each with per-cell variance1 and independent Gaussian mean noise1/n. Define V_A=T_A-C,V_B=T_B-C. Compare the exact scalar contrast D=g_A V_A-g_B V_B. Its variance is g_A²/n_A+g_B²/n_B+(g_A-g_B)²/n_C. The conventional marginal-risk surrogate omits cross-arm covariance and minimizes Var(g_A V_A)+Var(g_B V_B)=g_A²/n_A+g_B²/n_B+(g_A²+g_B²)/n_C. This is a deliberately named ordinary surrogate, not the paper's actual acquisition policy.

All allocations have the same total30cells, at least1 in each arm, and exhaustive integer search over all406allocations. Two predeclared cases: equal gradients(g_A,g_B)=(1,1), and opposite gradients(1,-1). Exact Fraction evaluation computes proxy minimizers, true-contrast minimizers and direct independent covariance-matrix recomputation using covariance matrix [[1/n_A+1/n_C,1/n_C],[1/n_C,1/n_B+1/n_C]] and h=[g_A,-g_B]. Ties use lexicographic(n_A,n_B,n_C). No parameter, tolerance, budget or case may be selected after results.

Success of this diagnostic: covariance arithmetic agrees for every allocation; equal gradients cancel the control term and the proxy allocation has strictly higher true contrast risk; opposite gradients retain4/n_C. A Gaussian ranking-error illustration uses stipulated true margin1/2 and 0.5*erfc(margin/sqrt(2variance)); this illustrates only this idealized task. No Monte Carlo or confidence significance. Conventional exhaustive c-optimal design is the correct informed control and is identical to the contrast optimizer; the check cannot establish a novel agent, world model, inheritance or RSI.

Stop/pivot: regardless of outcome, do not promote absolute angular precision as functional-decision improvement without an actual function map and decision loss. Do not claim nonlinear/angular cancellation merely from shared C. If a distinct real task is later qualified, fit its own ordinary correlated-noise/direct/EB controls before learning an agent. This check adds a concrete acquisition-contract constraint and changes whether to treat control-cell quota as a ready-made agent innovation.
