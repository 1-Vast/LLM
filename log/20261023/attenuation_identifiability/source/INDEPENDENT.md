## Decision

**GO for one exact descriptive decomposition, NO mechanism/functional/RSI promotion.** Root's identity can distinguish two mathematical contributors to the existing transformed residual and expose whether a statement about “attenuation” depends on averaging order. It cannot identify a physical attenuation mechanism, and a valid equality is not a new predictor or an independent replication. Do not select a coordinate/method based on this check or reinterpret it as reopening the stopped overlap/product law.

For each actual condition and gem, define M=mean(cellCP10K), L=mean(log1p(cellCP10K)), T=log1p(M), J=T-L. With common finite cell set and nonnegative values, J>=0 by concavity. Let D be the contrast double-singleA-singleB+control. Then exactly

    D(L)=D(T)-D(J).

This is an identity on condition-specific empirical means. All73pairs,103coordinates and8gems can be reported with no fitted parameters. Preserve pergem condition contrasts before averaging directions; mixing logaftergemmean with meanofpergemlogs introduces a separate acrossgem Jensen term. Scientific checks must match the saved extraction's cellweights and conditioning/QC rules. Technical gems do not become independent biological episodes.

## What it can rule out

It can rule out the arithmetic claim that meanpercelllog equals logofmeanCP. It can quantify exactly whether the current mixedlog residual's negative/positive component is present in D(T), offset by D(J), or reinforced by it. If D(J) is nonzero, a claim that aggregation heterogeneity makes no contribution to this specific numerical residual is false. If D(J) is zero while residual remains, **withincondition Jensen aggregation alone** cannot explain the residual; nonlinear loggeometry in D(T), CPclosure and biology remain.

If desired within the frozen check, signed projections onto the already fixed attenuation direction can be decomposed by the same linear identity, with no new fit or selection. That would explain an observed projection's arithmetic provenance only. Squared errors/norms are not additive: cross terms matter, so do not report percentages of “variance explained by Jensen” by dividing componentMSEs or subtracting norms. Opposing signed components can individually exceed the total. Perreadout cancellation does not imply no heterogeneous response.

## What it cannot rule out or certify

J is **not noisevariance, assaynoise, a removable bias, causal heterogeneity, or biological resistance**. It reflects dispersion of the normalized percell expression values, including zero mass, real cell states, sampling/readdepth, compositional normalization, survivor selection and measurement differences. A treatment may genuinely change J; subtracting it changes the estimand rather than repairing an error automatically. Condition-specific J uses observed doubles, so this is an outcome decomposition, not a way to forecast an unmeasured combination for free.

D(T) is still a contrast in nonlinear coordinates. Even if CPmeans obey additivity, logs need not. Conversely a nonzero D(CP) does not identify a molecular interaction because CP10K has percell libraryclosure and no absolute transcript/cell population calibration. Saved103selectedreadouts do not supply the full transcriptome denominator distribution or absolute counts. No pure observation decomposition can distinguish CRISPRa machinerycompetition, guideactivity, feedback regulation, cell-state redistribution and differential survival using these same conditionmeans alone.

Inputgems and targetgems differ in earlier OOFpredictions. A within-targetgem identity does not causally explain why the inputside globalattenuation predictor outperformed another fitted procedure. Predictioncovariance, finite-sample regularization and source selection also contribute. The decomposition must not be turned into a retrospective gate or fitted correction on those same exposed targets.
