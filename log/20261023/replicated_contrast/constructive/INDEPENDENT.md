# One minimal screen-replicate ordering-reversal diagnostic

2026-10-23. Independent proposal before current-phase peer reads. No new numerical outcome access, model fit, workbook/lung/FDR access, download or execution. Read cached author interactions.py, aggregate.py and scoring script only. This proposes one measurement diagnostic, not a new scientific core or a rescue of the stopped endpoint updater.

## Fixed endpoint and application

Question: can the opposite ordering of two double-KO combinations between MCF7 and MDAMB231 repeat in the second screen replicate? This asks a decision-relevant contrast without assuming comparable numerical GI units across backgrounds. It does not require latent truth, clinically meaningful drug effects or a mechanistic explanation.

Endpoint Y[p,c,r]: author-style **replicate-specific gene-pair interaction residual**, after the documented sample/anchor scoring and mirrored-role aggregation, with no pooling of screen replicate1 and2. If several sample columns/timepoints belong to a screen replicate, their fixed author-defined within-replicate aggregation must be authenticated before values. Any final context/replicate-wide positive affine standardization is immaterial to the within-context ordering below. Do not replace it postvalue with raw growth, a new residual model or cross-context z-score differences. Counts alone are not this endpoint: baseline/timepoint/guide/replicate mapping, single-effect/reference inputs and processing must first be authenticatable.

Cached code evidence: score_sample_residuals fits expected response separately per sample/anchor, optionally scales and standardizes residuals, then averages reciprocal directions; calculate_scores_and_effect_size currently averages selected columns over reps=(1,2) and standardizes the pooled result. Therefore the published aggregate must NOT be split, duplicated or treated as two repeat outcomes. The diagnostic needs the upstream per-sample residuals or sufficient authenticated inputs to reconstruct them separately. The function/pipeline existence does not prove those inputs are already available or that the published software versions will reproduce outputs. A separate authorized execution may perform prescribed endpoint reconstruction; no reconstruction/fitting occurs in this proposal.

## One identity-only selection and one held-replicate test

Use the existing frozen11621canonical nonself/noncontrol development pair IDs, preserving exact identity map/hash, without consulting pooled GI, q, nominations or replicate values. Sort pairs by SHA256 of UTF8 string `MAESTRO-replicated-order-v1|geneA|geneB` with lexicographically canonical gene order; break any hash tie by canonical ID. Take first1024pairs and form512disjoint-in-action contrasts by adjacent pairing. This fixes comparison pairs p_k,q_k before either replicate is read. Gene sharing is allowed/disclosed, and nothing treats these512contrasts as independent biological tasks. Do not replace unavailable/bad outcomes with the next hashed identity after opening values.

Contexts are fixed MCF7 and MDAMB231 only; the other three permitted development contexts are not scanned for a favorable result. A549/A427 remain numerically untouched. Screen replicate1 is discovery, replicate2 validation; no swap or cross-fold best result. Existing pooled development scores have already been exposed historically, so this is a predeclared replicate-repeatability test of an exposed panel, not a pristine independent biological confirmation. Hash identity selection prevents explicit favorable-pair mining; it does not undo historical exposure.

Define D[k,c,r]=Y[p_k,c,r]-Y[q_k,c,r]. A discovery reversal exists if D[k,MCF7,1]*D[k,MDAMB231,1]<0. Freeze that discovery set and the two signs before any replicate2 outcome is opened. An exact validation replication requires both validation signs to equal their respective discovery signs: sign(D[k,c,2])=sign(D[k,c,1]) for each fixed context. This is stronger than merely finding a reversal in replicate2: two swapped directions do not count. Equality/ties are nonreplications. No q/effect filter, top-kselection, guide-based independent votes, threshold search or subset modification enters.

The primary statistic is exact-orientation replicated reversals divided by **all discovery reversals**, retaining missing validation outcomes as nonreplications with separate missingness counts. Also report512total/usable contrasts and discovery/validation missing/tie counts. Discovery missingness means not evaluable, not evidence of no reversal; report the original512denominator so complete-case conditioning is visible. If processing/mapping cannot produce reliable identities and sample roles, stop before calculation instead of interpreting absent labels as zero.

## Prospective operational reliability falsifier

This narrow measurement GO requires at least20discovery reversals and at least80% exact-orientation replication in replicate2. Otherwise STOP the claim that this fixed, broadly sampled reversal object is sufficiently repeatable for the next functional-update mechanism study. The20/80% criterion is an explicitly chosen operational screening requirement, not a derived biological/clinical margin, hypothesis-test alpha or proof of high-probability future transfer. It is frozen here before values and must not be lowered after a failure. If fewer than20reversals occur, record insufficient support for this object, not a proof that all rare context effects are absent. If it passes, proceed only to proposing a separately evaluated contrast-transport restriction; it does not establish that feedback can predict the held contrasts or that any updater outperforms ordinary models.

Do not assign a binomial confidence interval over512contrasts: pairs share genes and author normalization/background references. The two screen replicates are the measurement repeat units; nineguide combinations are within-screen measurement structure, not nine biological experiments. Shared plasmid baseline, common guide library, processing and any shared cell preparation further limit independence. Authenticate whether rep labels mean independent cultures/screens or sequencing technical repeats; technical-only repeats can establish technical repeatability under that label, not independent screen-repeatability. Two screens alone do not estimate general across-lab biological reproducibility, and a passed sign test can retain systematic library/normalization effects.

## Why this is minimal and what it cannot establish

Within-context action differences and their signs directly address order; subtracting separately standardized scores across contexts would import a scale-sensitive biological interpretation unnecessary here. The diagnostic tests an interaction of **orders**, not significance of a difference-of-differences in a common growth unit. Rank equality survives positive final affine transforms, but anchor-specific normalization may still create consistent patterns; no causal attribution is made. A repeatable ranking object could still be unpredictable from cheap target support, and few meaningful reversal pairs need not lie near a deployable top20boundary. Neither a pass nor failure reactivates the prior fitted endpoint recipe.

Only one endpoint/contrast/split/reliability criterion is nominated. No broad literature search, new mechanism family, model fit or additional mathematical test. A future ordinary functional prediction test remains permissible even without latent truth; its response meaning and information costs must be stated. Any later model/active-learning/RSI claim needs its own fair direct/retraining/continual/GP/mixture/equal-search controls and new task roles. This proposal does not consume protected lung outcomes or claim those contributions.
