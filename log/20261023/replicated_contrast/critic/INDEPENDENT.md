# Independent measurement review: replicated contrasts require a real repeat contract

2026-10-23. Read rank_reversal_mechanism/DECISION and historical control-endpoint addendum only; no current root/peer measurement plan,raw values,workbook,lung outcomes or new fit. This review specifies a conditional minimal diagnostic,not a repaired old model or a new mechanism. Whether raw matched samples exist is delegated to source authentication; formulas alone do not acquire them.

## Endpoint and unit

For authenticated construct g,context c,repeat r,define a frozen relative endpoint

    L_gcr = log2((N_terminal,gcr+alpha)/(N_baseline,gcr+alpha))
            - median_neutral_control log2((N_terminal+alpha)/(N_baseline+alpha)).

Freeze pseudocount,baseline/terminal mapping,control membership,guide aggregation,coverage and missing policies before endpoint values. This is a control-referenced log-abundance change. It is not automatically absolute growth,per-doubling fitness,author residual GI or an interaction effect. At a minimum a baseline,terminal sample,construct identity and actual repeat lineage must be authenticated. AAVS/nontargeting names do not prove neutral invariance or equal editing burden. Sequencing-depth cancellation is exact only under the specified unregularized shared factors; fixed pseudocounts can break it for low counts.

If p=(a,b) and q=(d,e) are two distinct double-KO actions,then D_cr=L_pcr-L_qcr compares relative outcomes of those actions. It is not an estimate of gene interaction merely because each action targets two genes. Author GI subtracts an expected-fitness model and standardizes. An interaction contrast would need legal matched single/control endpoints and an explicitly specified neutrality model with its own uncertainty; do not reconstruct that as an unannounced followup if this simpler measurement diagnostic is weak.

Nine guide combinations are measurement channels sharing gene effects,library,control and culture. They are not nine independent biological repeats. Two sequencing lanes of one culture are not two biological repeats either. If actual sample lineage does not establish separate cultures/transductions as claimed,the scope becomes technical repeatability conditional on the culture. Five contexts are five dependent screen backgrounds; replication within one does not create another later biological task.

## Contrast and reversal are different targets

For contexts A,B,a contrast change is

    Delta_r = (L_pAr-L_qAr) - (L_pBr-L_qBr).

Delta nonzero can occur without reversing action order. A genuine observed reversal requires D_Ar and D_Br to have opposite signs. Replication of the reversal requires independently measured repeat2to preserve each context's repeat1sign,not merely preserve sign(Delta). Likewise positive correlation of whole pair endpoints is not evidence of stable small pairwise differences near a selection boundary.

Do not derive repeat confidence intervals from author q values. The previous q values test each residual against controls and do not supply variances or covariance for D or Delta. q must not be inserted into a Gaussian observation-noise R.

## Shared baseline/control dependence is decisive

If controls are identical within a context/repeat,their common additive log normalizer cancels algebraically in D. This can reduce one source of dependence;it does not cancel guide-specific baseline error or make p,q independent. With separate controls/pseudocount/QC or weighted aggregations,check the actual cancellation rather than assuming it.

If baseline counts are shared between repeats,their construct-specific measurement error appears in both repeat endpoints. Selecting a reversal with repeat1and confirming it with repeat2can therefore recycle the same baseline error. It is not independent biological replication of absolute action order. Report the actual shared baseline as conditioning information,not a hidden independent measurement.

If the same construct baseline is also shared between contexts A,B,it cancels in the difference-of-differences Delta under identical mapping/aggregation. That may permit a cleaner conditional contrast-change diagnostic even when each D retains shared baseline error. It does not authenticate a replicated sign reversal of both D values: sign(D_A) and sign(D_B)still depend on the common baseline contrast. Independent matched baselines,an appropriate explicit count-noise model,or a more limited conditional estimand are required. Do not infer independence merely because the word replicate appears in a column name.

## Minimal executable design, before values

Choose a fixed small panel of action-pairs(p,q)by canonical unordered IDs and a frozen hash,before reading repeat endpoints. Pair aliases/orientations and all guides stay grouped; define whether comparisons may share a gene and report that overlap. Using many overlapping contrasts is useful for description but their apparent sample size cannot be the number of all possible pairings. Use separate hash batches as selection robustness,not biological replication.

First compute D_1andDelta_1only. If the aim is to study selected inversions,freeze the repeat1selection rule in advance: for example opposite signs in A,B plus |D_A1|,|D_B1|above a declared source/measurement-motivated resolution margin. Repeat2cannot alter selection,margin,guide weights or exclusions. Keep the full fixed panel denominators,number selected and every nonvalidated selection. A repeat2dropout is an unvalidated/unknown endpoint under a frozen rule,not an outlier to delete. If no source-supported resolution margin exists,use strict nonzero signs as descriptive selection and do not manufacture a practical-effect claim.

Then compare repeat2without selecting again. Primary quantities are the fraction of selected contrasts preserving both signs,context-specific D magnitude/error,andDelta repeat concordance. Also show the unselected/full-panel results and declared reverse-direction selection as a separate fixed sensitivity if wanted; do not pick the better direction. No trainable model or target source-score threshold is needed. Avoid enormous all-pairs expansion merely to make standard errors tiny.

The literal minimal falsifier is clear: a repeat1selected reversal whose independent repeat2does not preserve both signs fails to validate that reversal. A large failure fraction or agreement confined to shared-baseline channels stops using that fixed measurement definition as reliable contrast evidence. Before root sees values it may register a pragmatic screen such as requiring>=75%two-sign validation in every included context contrast with adequate predeclared selected counts; this would be a heuristic,not a calibrated p-value or proof of transport. I do not require this arbitrary example as a universal scientific threshold. It is equally legitimate to freeze a descriptive measurement audit whose success is complete honest ascertainment and whose output supplies no model-promotion GO.

Stop before numerical execution if baseline/terminal/control/repeat mapping cannot support the declared endpoint,source columns imply unrecognized shared measurements,lung values would be required,or guide/canonical roles cannot be joined without outcome-driven choices. Stop rather than improvise a new normalization after seeing results. Do not start a raw-count acquisition cascade without source evidence that the required bounded asset actually exists.

## Uncertainty and interpretation

With only two biological repeats per context,one can report a held-repeat sign validation and observed repeat discrepancy. That does not robustly estimate biological variance,rank-reversal probabilities or asymptotic mixed-effect confidence intervals. Guide/construct bootstrap can quantify conditional measurement sensitivity under an explicitly declared resampling model,not invent culture-level N. Reusing shared controls/baselines and shared genes requires joint resampling/cluster treatment; simplistic independent Bernoulli or all-pair bootstrap intervals are not calibrated.

Even a replicated cross-context relative-abundance reversal does not identify the reason: biology,editing efficiency,initial representation,culture/library competition and readout processing may contribute. This could nevertheless be useful functional differential evidence without latent mechanistic truth. It is not yet evidence that sparse support predicts unmeasured reversals,that an inherited updater improves them,or that a world/agent/improved-improver is necessary.

Full pooled culture/library/sequencing cost remains a bundle. Reading a few archived construct counts is an information-reveal restriction conditional on that screen,not a proven per-construct wet saving. Do not call repeat1selection a learned acquisition strategy; it is an outcome-selected measurement audit with held-repeat validation. Old endpoint sharing,rank selectors and protected lung outcomes remain stopped.
