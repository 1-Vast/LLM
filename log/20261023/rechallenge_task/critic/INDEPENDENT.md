## Concrete scientific gap and current-primary evidence

The2022 primary supplies actual treatment history followed by a standardized functional dose challenge: cells cultivated continuously/intermittently in10-cm dishes are harvested each week, reseeded into96-well plates and exposed to varying LGX818 doses for72 hours. The response is CellTiter-Glo-derived viability/number estimate, not direct physical counts. This is a different experimental unit from the weekly Figure2 plate endpoint and from the independent PI assay. Both drug-exposure history and a challenge-dose response can therefore be meaningful variables without inventing a cellular latent state. Current repository schema qualification must establish source-culture/run provenance and response normalization; shared condition names alone do not join Fig2/3/PI.

The published2022 results already show that resensitized cells differ from initial drug-naive cells despite apparent sensitivity recovery; many resistance transcripts remain altered. Thus neither the observation of history dependence nor the narrative of reversible sensitivity is new. The prior four-condition diagnostic's early/late multiplier differences add a measured-proxy forecasting failure, but are ratios of group means, not a unique mechanistic identification. A useful research gap would be an update restriction that exploits available early history to improve a later actual challenge response beyond rich direct/history models and ordinary sequential refitting, on independently specified queries.

The2026 paper offers a concise mechanistic hypothesis: discrete fitness-associated phenotypes and biased/semi-biased transitions toward high growth, versus unbiased/no transitions. It assumes intermediate fitness by interpolation and a drug-naive/adapted state approximation; it calibrates500nM parameters on both four-week schedule curves jointly. Its phenotype-update rules update simulated cell states x, not the research predictor's parameters theta, not a learned feedback-update procedure U, and not an improver G. Confusing these three different updates would incorrectly convert biological adaptation into RSI.

The paper acknowledges that count dynamics do not identify biased versus semi-biased transition strategies or birth/death separately. A growing net population can have substantial death offset by greater birth. Its sign-based mapping from net growth to either division or death is a simplifying modeling assumption. Intermediate simulated phenotype distributions, predicted death spikes and fitted transition rates are not independent experimental feedback to a research agent. Published all-schedule calibration cannot become held-out rechallenge prediction without re-estimating from lawful source data alone.

## Why the closest minimal operations do not yet supply a new core

One natural operation would retain a treatment-history state h, update it using the observed assay residual and roll forward a challenge response distribution. Written as h_new=h+K*(y-yhat) or a likelihood/posterior update, that operation is an ordinary filter/state estimator when K is fixed/learned from past episodes. A direct recurrent history predictor can receive the identical histories, source challenges, measurement noise and priors. Retaining h or a covariance is memory/prior reuse, not inherited improvement of U. This specific equality, rather than membership in an ordinary family, blocks a novelty claim based only on adding h.

A second naming temptation is to learn the transition/update rule from multiple schedules and retain it. If the operation is joint maximum-likelihood training of shared transition parameters followed by per-episode state inference, ordinary joint system identification plus filtering performs the same update on the same observations. If an agent chooses which episode to replay or fit next, prioritized replay/active acquisition or ordinary equal-budget search is its direct counterpart. No concrete extra restriction, theorem, falsifier or measured resource advantage distinguishes such an operation here. I therefore do not promote an empty new module as the one candidate.

This does not imply every operation reducible to standard mathematics lacks publishable value. A new assay-qualified target, structural identification result, demonstrable efficiency tradeoff or newly justified restriction could be a technical contribution. None is established merely by giving an existing filter a cellular name or adding a learned optimizer to the exposed four curves. Historical exact LMMSE/prior, consistency, midpoint, paired-credit and semantic-witness STOPs remain scoped and are not used as a universal ban.

## ONE useful disambiguating diagnostic before an updater claim

Ask whether two source-culture histories that yield similar CURRENT measured proxy/functional summaries predict different subsequent standardized72-hour dose-challenge curves. Compare a current-summary direct model with a full legal-history direct model and a specified current-state world model on held-out complete source-culture/induction-run groups. The challenge dose and known schedule go to all models. Full-history need not mean future RNA or future PI; only actual known exposure history and measurements available before challenge are allowed. A single repeated culture's neighboring weeks are correlated history points, not independent RSI tasks.

If the richer direct model improves, the result establishes value of historical observations; it does not prove world-model necessity. If no current-summary model can distinguish genuinely aliased histories but full-history predicts their differing challenges, that falsifies that specific summary's sufficiency. A world model must either represent the distinguishing history or accept the same irreducible error. An observational aliasing example does not uniquely identify a cellular mechanism. Similarity tolerances, challenge endpoints and eligible histories must be frozen before response inspection, not selected from the already exposed four-condition panel. No such new diagnostic is executed in this phase.

A dataset containing only one curve per history can still support a narrow cohort-level forecast if provenance and independent queries are honest; it need not contain full RSI generations before any numerical work. It cannot support strong biological uncertainty or transfer estimates from replicate labels alone. Negative/no advantage remains useful functional information and should not trigger arbitrary state additions.

## Required comparisons and separable claims

Predictive controls: strongest lawful schedule/history-conditioned direct regression/sequence predictor; current-state direct predictor; persistence and schedule-specific source response averages where applicable; ordinary nonlinear state-space fit with identical history and measurement model; source joint fit plus target-prefix ordinary refit/filter; simple sensitive/adapted mixtures. Fit, tuning, failed runs and challenge purchases count. An untrained four-formula comparison does not satisfy this stronger future claim, but remains a valid earlier diagnostic.

World contribution would be a predeclared improvement from a specified compositional prediction/update restriction over these same-input controls. Net-growth fitting alone cannot establish cell-state reversibility or identifiable birth/death. Agent contribution would require a real menu of selectable challenge/measurement actions and better subsequent functional prediction or drug decision at equal total cost; masking already published wells is only a retrospective acquisition simulation, not new experimentation. Do not grant the world extra molecular channels while denying them to direct models.

Updater inheritance requires retaining a changed U and comparing it with ordinary refit/CL from a common predictor, prior and legal source history on later qualified challenges. A trained predictor theta or inferred h alone is not that contrast. Improved-improver requires G1 versus G0 from identical starting U/theta/prior/history/archive and equal budget, with higher later descendant utility; no such pair exists yet. A potential source culture's biological memory is not inheritance of the research agent's update capability.

## Success and stops

Before a future distinct method experiment: authenticate the actual culture/run units, exposure and challenge schedules, assay normalization, missingness and legal early measurements; fix response loss and an assay-relevant margin; isolate different query groups; and specify a complete concrete update operation. A limited single-system forecast is allowed with limited claims. Stop the new-operation claim if an identical ordinary implementation reproduces it, if any gain vanishes against rich direct/refit controls, or if only extra observations/prior calibration explain it. Stop a particular split if unit/response provenance cannot be authenticated, not all research worldwide.

No current candidate meets those conditions, so there is no mechanism worth a new numerical run at present. The strongest surviving positive is a biologically grounded functional rechallenge task and an explicit sufficiency question, not a proposed RSI core. Current scope ends at independent review and later reciprocal cross; no new fit, acquisition or same-panel repair is authorized by this document.
