## Positive evidence and permissible task

This source provides actual functional intervention data: dish area occupancy under repeated TRAIL versus alternating TRAIL/TBQ. It is materially more relevant than an RNA-only surrogate and establishes a plausible small, public cohort-level future-density forecasting task. Two cached model-input files labeled experiment 3 and experiment 4 contain AAAA, ANAN and Naive sheets. Hold a whole file, including all its conditions, together when studying transport; do not split condition sheets as independent runs. These are declared-experiment/file labels, not authenticated independent culture/date/plate identifiers.

Figure 5B explicitly describes one representative clonogenicity experiment composed of one independent dish for each time point; Figure 5C states at least three experimental repeats. This is destructive endpoint sampling across sibling cohorts, not a repeated measurement of the same dish. That does not disqualify a cohort endpoint forecast: early sacrificed dishes can inform later sibling-cohort endpoints if cohort assignment and observation availability are documented. It cannot support individual-cell or same-well trajectory claims, and figure-level repeat statements do not prove the exact experiment 3/4 files are independent preparations. Fresh clones and low passage number do not repair this missing mapping.

Density is quantified crystal-violet stained dish area occupancy by Fiji. It is not direct count, CellTiter-Glo, death probability, or an identified decomposition of birth/death/phenotypic states. The separately described CTG, FRET and live-death assays must not be silently joined to these workbook rows. HeLa-RIPK3-Cherry and HT-29 are reported, but workbook filenames do not authenticate every line or TCQ condition in extended figures.

## Measured versus synthetic structure

Experiment 3 sheets have A1:F8; experiment 4 treated sheets A1:F8 and Naive A1:F12. Dimensions are not verified counts of measured endpoints. The third row distinguishes Measurement Time and Cell Density from Death Modality, Drug Input Time in Simulation, Death Time in Simulation and Measurement Time in Simulation. The latter simulation columns do not establish empirical death events or extra biological observation times. The LT workbook names and 12-/20-day simulation narratives do not create observed long-term responses.

The author loader independently drops missing values in each column. A future parser must preserve actual time-density row pairing and explicitly distinguish schedule entries from assay records. Do not infer sample count or align columns solely by list position. Freeze the physical timing, observed-prefix definition, normalizations, A/N mapping and response metric before response decoding. ## Published model is not a fresh zero-fit forecast

README says PSM2D is not re-calibrated. The primary Results explicitly says all topology combinations were evaluated, the lowest-RMSE coupled model was selected, and reset speed and sensitivity rate of alpha_TSN when activated by TRAIL were estimated. Thus this is partial reuse with target topology selection and additional estimation. Do not cite the published fit as independent held-out prediction or a novel learned updater.

PSM1D was calibrated across all three regimens in the first 72 hours; simulated seven-day curves are not extra measured outcomes. EV6 sets tolerant initial mass using density after first treatment and sensitive mass from the before/24-hour-after difference. Such initial conditions use future information for a pretreatment forecast; they may be lawful only when that first 24-hour observation is an explicitly available prefix. They do not directly measure latent sensitive/tolerant compartments.

## Supplement header exposure and leakage restrictions

Root supplemental inventory has exposed the Fig3B per-cell Dying cell / Surviving cell header labels, including ordering. Therefore neither this review nor later work may claim label blindness. Those labels cannot enter predictor input; column order/identifiers may also encode fate. Numeric FRET trajectories remain unparsed in this review. The primary method subtracts a minimum over all time points when normalizing FRET, which would leak a future observation into a prefix prediction unless recomputed using the available prefix or acquired raw pre-normalization signal. Figure 5D headers Naive,N_TAKi,Surv,Surv_TAKi do not supply a Fig3-to-Fig5 cell-level linkage. SourceData metadata figure labels can mismatch filenames; route by exact archive/member name. The two root reader failures from treating __MACOSX/._ CSV resources as assay files remain recorded; excluding OS metadata is a schema correction, not a successful numeric experiment.

## Decision and falsification

Conditional GO for a minimal declared-experiment/cohort forecast protocol after metadata timing is fixed. Not GO for a novel reusable update law, biological independence, agent gain, inheritance gain, joint gain, or improved-improver/RSI claim. No new performance result has been obtained here.

A response-stage study would compare the same legal prefix and future schedule using persistence/trend, strong direct history/schedule prediction, a parsimonious on/off or event model, ordinary joint refit/continual update, simple mixtures and equal-budget model/program search. Target-selected topology, initialization, tuning and all search evaluations must share the same budget and exposure rules. File-level held-out prediction may be reported descriptively even if culture independence remains unresolved, with appropriately limited inference. Stop a reusable-law claim if apparent improvement disappears against these controls, changes with arbitrary row alignment/normalization, or relies on synthetic observation times or future labels. Two small files cannot by themselves substantiate meta-improvement over later independent tasks.

Remaining evidence gaps: precise experiment-file-to-repeat identity; preassigned sibling cohorts and real sample times; raw versus transformed trace availability; frozen independent future task bank. Public functionality is a positive acquisition result, not yet a positive predictive or RSI result.
