## Actual API proposal: revise contract, do not fit literally

One real DeepSeek response proposed a diagonal-plus-rank1 residual updater. It changed the old task from eight distinct drugs on one patient to eight patients per drug without its own resource/evaluation definition. Under the old fixed-patient disjoint support/query drug task, the diagonal query-support block is zero; the declared equal-pooling correction reduces to u_Q*g(x)*mean(r_S), an ordinary contextual rank1 residual predictor. A learned support weighting would specify a different operation. Full covariance conditioning already uses drug identities; it is not globally scalar. Unseen-drug loadings require an explicit descriptor-to-loading or feedback-based rule; freezing an absent ID loading is undefined. A per-drug paid residual mean can be lawful under the newly declared patient-support task, but cannot be presumed available for unpurchased query drugs in the old task. Offset shrinkage, contextual reduced-rank regression and full covariance conditioning are necessary competent controls.

These are concrete information/operation defects, not a proof that every low-rank updater fails or lacks possible value. No generated code, toy, predictive fit or protected labels were run/read. No new instantiation currently has enough task closure or evidence to nominate a fit. API/ROOT_ASSESSMENT.md and each role's API_REVIEW.md retain the detailed objections.
