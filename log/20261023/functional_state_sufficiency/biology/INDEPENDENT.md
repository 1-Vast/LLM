## What is really measured

The training experiment perturbs **59 target genes with119 CRISPR guides in3 Cas9 cancer cell lines: A549,ES2,HCC44**. Cell Painting uses its standard broad organelle stains and CellProfiler morphology features. Separate targeted Cell Health panels independently measure:

- cell-cycle panel: Hoechst,EdU,pH3,γH2AX; proliferation/cycle-phase and DNA-damage marker readouts;
- viability panel: Caspase3/7,DRAQ7,CellROX plus digital-phase-contrast morphology; apoptosis/death/ROS and shape/count readouts.

These are **separately acquired assay images/reagents**, not70 targets generated by applying a model to the very same input Cell Painting features. Thus a blanket accusation that every CellHealth target is a deterministic recoding of X is incorrect. Many targets nevertheless are marker/gating/count phenotypes, and some (e.g. Live Cell Area) are specifically DPC-derived shape measurements: not all70 are orthogonal physiological functions. Infection efficiency is itself a separate assay and is included as negative-control features. Caspase/DRAQ7/EdU targets are better candidates for an orthogonal health question than claiming shape→shape demonstrates a novel functional mechanism. Their marker meanings do not uniquely identify every underlying biological process or clinical efficacy.

Primary acquisition describes4-day post-guide Cell Painting fixation, parallel plates for infection-efficiency, cycle and viability, minimum4 treatment replicates for Cell Health, and independent gating per cell line/experiment. It does not supply a longitudinal same-cell transition or a feedback-followed-by-next-action experiment. We must not turn a fixed postperturbation image into a pretreatment predictor or pretend repeated assays are free.

## The decisive join limitation

The exact Methods statement is: **“We generated consensus signatures because there is no way to match replicate-level information across the assays.”** It reports3456 Cell Painting well profiles and2302 Cell Health well profiles collapsed separately by MODZ to the common **357 guide×cell-line conditions**. Final input has357×952 morphology features; outputs are70 condition-consensus readouts. Consequently:

- A lawful shared condition is **guide identity×cell line**, with separately aggregated replicate distributions.
- No authenticated same-cell, same-well or matched-replicate state/function join is supplied. Well names cannot be equated across panels to manufacture it.
- The measured association can legitimately study expected condition-level health prediction. It cannot establish an individual cell's morphology/hidden state suffices for its own later fate, nor within-run state/function error covariance.
- Raw labels are advertised publicly by panel×cell line and normalized labels/consensus are author-linked, but actual schema/IDs/payload completeness remain **uninspected**. The original source supports their existence, not an acquired verified join table.

README's two panels×three lines yields six panel/background groups, **not six independent biological contexts**, and neither70 readouts nor119 guides gives70/119 independent later background tasks. Multiple guides of one gene are dependent intervention realizations. A whole-gene split should keep all its guides together; a whole-line test has only three backgrounds. This does **not** forbid a modest three-line exploratory transfer question. It does forbid selling its repeated folds, guides, wells or endpoints as many independent inherited-task generations.

## Drug data are importantly different

The1571 compounds at approximately six doses are a separate A549 Drug Repurposing Hub Cell Painting dataset. The70 Cell Health values applied to its10,752 consensus profiles are **predictions from the genetic-trained elastic-net models**, not newly measured70-channel health truth. Hill curves and the webapp do not convert those predictions into measured labels.

An actual orthogonal drug-validation route exists: PRISM19Q4 barcoded pooled viability forA549, with1382 compounds shared with the imaging collection. But the paper explicitly states dose conditions are not one-to-one and uses **dose ranks** to compare them. It reports a Spearman rho.35 association; that is an author-reported validation result, not our numerical result. It is a different assay/culture protocol, not same-well paired morphology→health. Do not call rank-aligned doses exact exposure matches.

The additional drug “validation” of ROS,G1 counts andγH2AX uses enrichment/known drug classes and model-predicted dose responses, not a complete new wet measured function matrix for all compounds. Thus the abstract's broad orthogonal-validation phrasing cannot license treating every displayed drug phenotype as ground truth. The paper itself says **“all predictions require further experimental validation.”** No drug corpus qualifies here for a complete independent measured70-target task or cross-background RSI.

## What task is supportable and what remains missing

A scientifically legitimate narrow application is **targeted assay triage**: ask whether a paid postperturbation Cell Painting profile predicts an independently assayed apoptosis/death/proliferation phenotype for an unmeasured genetic perturbation condition. Strong direct counterparts already include the source's separately tuned elastic nets and competent nonlinear morphology→health predictors; random or gene-family/whole-gene splits must be stated distinctly. Cell-line holdout was already attempted by the authors, including limitations of shape transfer; simply repeating it is not our method innovation.

This source alone supplies no new updating operator, no actual research-agent intervention choice and no improved-improver procedure. A small **error-discovery** diagnostic could be permitted later without first inventing an algorithm, but it needs a frozen concrete missing prediction property and legal condition-level target contract. Merely acquiring another357-row asset or redrawing static mapping as a world transition is not enough reason to start downloads/fits. Root has not supplied such a diagnostic here, so I recommend **NO_AUTOMATIC_NUMERIC_ACQUISITION_CHAIN** now, rather than inventing one for a directory.

Key future controls if a real conditional task is justified:

- Shared source/support/query perturbation identities, all purchased channels and assay/computation costs; morphology measured after intervention is paid information given to the direct comparator.
- Label normalization is perplate using median/SD in the paper. A sparse-feedback claim cannot secretly require all query target labels for current-plate normalization; use a lawful fixed transform/reference design or lower the claim to retrospective prediction of released normalized labels. Feature selection uses broad profiles and consensus construction uses repeats, so all available-profile/repeat information must be disclosed.
- Distinguish genetic target prioritization from small-molecule drug efficacy. Gene knockout need not approximate a drug's target occupancy, off-target effects or dose/time kinetics.
- Stop a same-cell/dynamic interpretation immediately if only unmatched condition consensus exists; that does not stop the weaker condition-level statistical question. Stop a cross-drug independent-function claim if targets are only model predictions or unqualified dose-rank joins.
