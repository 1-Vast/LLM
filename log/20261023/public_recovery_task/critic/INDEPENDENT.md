## What is empirical and what is simulated

The primary models previously measured WM239A encorafenib data from Kavran et al. It has two distinct empirical assets: (a) 0/72-hour challenge-count fold changes at multiple challenge doses after 0/1/2/3/4-week preincubation with 500 nM, reported as quadruplicates; (b) four-week continuous/intermittent treatment count dynamics, with six in-vitro replicates in Fig2's caption. The intermittent regimen is one week on/one week off. These are real functional cell-count observations, not RNA proxies. The independent biological status and per-curve linkage of the six replicates are not established by this article's caption alone.

The discrete phenotype states, linearly interpolated intermediate-state fitness, state-density histograms, simulated individual birth/death events, plasticity-update strategies and treatment-optimization sweeps are model outputs/assumptions. One hundred simulated runs are not one hundred independent cell experiments. Their initial distribution is rule-specific and generated through assumed preconditioning; it is not an experimentally measured phenotype distribution. The statement that adapted cells recover naive-like growth after removal is attributed to the earlier experiment with results not shown here; it is not a released recovery trace authenticated by that sentence.

For 300 nM, the model uses growth-rate matrix values and sets eta_on=eta_off=2, chosen to traverse phenotype space within roughly one week. A simulated advantage at 300 nM is not automatically a measured 300 nM continuous/intermittent experiment. Although the broad Fig2 caption refers to both doses, the surrounding text explicitly describes the 300 nM schedules as predictions and the four-week fitted empirical comparison at 500 nM. Qualify exact raw series identities before assigning experimental labels to every displayed panel.

For 500 nM there is no directly measured three-day challenge fold-change at that challenge dose in the stated challenge-dose grid. The primary estimates FM(0,500), FM(1,500), eta_on and eta_off against BOTH continuous and intermittent mean trajectories at all four noninitial timepoints simultaneously. Fitnesses are bounded by the 300/1000 nM neighboring-dose values. Consequently its 500 nM curve agreement and schedule ranking are in-sample calibration/model selection, not a future-held-out or held-out-schedule forecast. Merely reusing those fitted parameters on the same curves would not test a research updater. These source results remain author evidence, not our reproduction.

## Normalization and net-growth limits

The 72-hour assay quantities are relative fold changes: fold change at dose d divided by fold change at 500 nM. Extracting absolute effective lambda_d requires a 500 nM reference growth rate, itself estimated by log-linear regression on a reported seven-day count segment. Thus the rate matrix embeds an extra empirical count segment and exponential-growth assumption. All compared predictors must receive the same lawful segment/reference estimate; a matrix is not free prior knowledge. If that segment belongs to a held-out query, only the permitted prefix can be used. A source/target provenance check is needed before caching any ProcessData-derived matrix.

The longitudinal display uses normalized cell counts and simulations seeded at 100 cells. The caption does not by itself authenticate the exact raw-data normalization denominator, passaging/reseeding correction or count units. Do not infer absolute tumor burden or death counts from normalized curves. Normalization by a query's baseline count is legal when observed at forecast time; normalization by future maxima, full-trajectory totals or a future control would leak. Metadata/header inspection must identify what was actually done. Challenge FC normalization and longitudinal timecourse normalization are different operations.

Net growth is birth minus death and does not identify them separately. The IBM maps positive net growth to division probability and negative net growth to death probability, effectively eliminating simultaneous opposing turnover within a state. This is a modeling simplification, not a measurement of division/death rates. The primary itself calls for separate division/death measurements and acknowledges counts cannot distinguish semi-biased from biased adaptation, especially without phenotype-distribution observations. Recovered population growth does not prove single-cell phenotype reversal or a unique adaptation mechanism. Predicted death spikes and simulated phenotype histories cannot serve as experimental training labels.

## ONE minimal real functional forecast

Application: predict cell-count rebound during the FIRST seven-day drug holiday after one week of 500 nM exposure in WM239A. Forecast the observed week2 normalized population count from the target replicate's baseline/week1 observations and the legally known off-week schedule. Continuous-treatment week2 count is a prespecified comparator condition, not a substitute query or a selected favorable subgroup. This is a narrow same-cell-line/same-protocol forecast and cannot be counted as multiple independent RSI tasks merely because there are several replicates or frames.

Preferred finite test, conditional on authenticated replicate identities: leave one complete experimental replicate/block out, fit/tune on complete lawful source replicate trajectories and source challenge assays, then expose only the target baseline/week1 prefix and score its week2 value. Never fit a target's week2/3/4 data, future-derived normalization or an initial state tuned to the full target. Hold out shared experimental batches together if replicate labels reveal those groups. If only group means or unpaired column blocks exist, do not pretend leave-one-replicate-out independence; either explicitly downgrade to one aggregate prefix forecast using only nonquery source information or stop that particular split. A limited aggregate diagnostic is allowed, but cannot claim replicate-generalization uncertainty.

The strongest simple opponent is source-trained schedule-specific mean/fold-change recovery, initialized at the target's observed week1 count. It already knows that identical source cultures experienced the same off week. Other ordinary controls are: persistence/current-count extrapolation; source-calibrated on/off piecewise log-linear regression; rich-history direct regression using baseline, week1, dose and known schedule; parsimonious sensitive/adapted mixture with source fit and ordinary prefix state refitting. A proposed dynamic world model gets identical source challenge data, reference-rate information, preconditioning schedule and target prefix. It must beat the strong schedule/condition baseline, not merely an on-drug exponential extrapolation that is denied the drug-off indicator.

Source-trained world parameters may use complete SOURCE trajectories, including both schedules, provided controls have identical access. The primary's published whole-data fits cannot be used as blinded-target weights. Source fitting, target-prefix adaptation and hyperparameter/program search costs all count; plain refit/continual update remains a required same-history control. With few points, a simple direct method can be the scientifically correct winner. A lack of a new algorithm does not invalidate this task; this phase has not proposed one.

Primary loss can be squared error on the authenticated normalized count or log fold change if positive counts and reference definitions permit. Fix the scale, zero handling, split and practical margin after schema qualification but before numeric query access; do not optimize normalization or endpoint for a win. Report baseline-conditioned errors and uncertainty only at the actual independent group level. A world-model finite-data contribution requires beating rich direct, schedule-condition and ordinary dynamical/refit controls with the same information. Improving only versus a stationary on-drug slope shows the value of knowing withdrawal, not a unique virtual-cell mechanism.

## Qualification and stop conditions

The primary explicitly declares both simulation and in-vitro data on github.com/SJHamis/phenotype_adaptation, making this a concrete public-data lead rather than an upon-request-only source. This independent review has not inspected repository schemas or payloads. Required next authentication: which files hold experimental versus synthetic records; exact 500 nM continuous/intermittent counts, times and schedule; normalization and any passaging correction; per-replicate/block IDs and whether measurements are longitudinal/destructive; and the reference segment used in growth-rate extraction. Raw count values need not be opened merely to establish these contracts.

Stop a purported held-out forecast if full four-week target trajectories contributed to parameters, normalization, initial states or selection; if synthetic model counts are mislabeled biological; or if there is no lawful early/late endpoint pairing for the proposed unit. Narrow the claim rather than demand a large multi-cell-line dataset before any numeric test. If a fair simple condition mean wins, retain the real forecast result and stop promoting world necessity; do not reopen old configurations or add latent/editor layers to rescue it.

No numerical experiment or new mechanistic novelty is established here.
