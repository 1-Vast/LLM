## Material correction: the reported counts are CellTiter-Glo estimates

The actual experimental primary states CellTiter-Glo 2.0 was used to determine cell numbers. Figure2 explicitly calls weekly values CellTiter-Glo measurements normalized to the initial number seeded. Thus the directly measured endpoint is a luminescence/ATP-based cell-viability proxy interpreted by the authors as cell numbers, not authenticated image/physical counts. Without calibration and additional metadata, it cannot be assumed proportional to viable cell number across changing treatment/adaptation states. A model fit to this signal does not separately validate population birth/death, absolute live burden or target elimination. This does not make the measured assay useless: a properly named held-out assay-response forecast is still real experimental prediction.

CellTiter-Glo is a lytic endpoint assay in its ordinary use, so a repeated same-well longitudinal interpretation cannot be assumed from the plotted weekly columns. The main primary says cells were seeded in 96-well dishes and measured at weekly endpoints but does not authenticate well IDs linking the same living culture over time. Exact scheduling of sister wells, destructive sampling, replicate pairing and calibration requires the SI/protocol or dataset metadata. Until then, my proposed leave-one-complete-trajectory split is conditional, not a certified existing design.

Figure2 design: cells were cumate-induced for 72 hours before reseeding; the BRAFV600E construct is an engineered overexpression system, not an arbitrary untreated melanoma background. Cells undergo 28 days continuous 500 nM LGX818 or intermittent weeks1/3 on and weeks2/4 off. Media changes occur days3/5/7 each week. BRAFV600E Figure2B reports n=5 or6 at a timepoint; empty-vector Figure2C reports n=6. These n values are not automatically independent biological runs, donors or linked trajectories. The known engineered condition/cumate protocol and baseline seeding must be available to all direct/dynamic comparators.

## Distinct assays must not be joined as single cultures

Figure3A/B begins with separate 10-cm dish cultures under the corresponding schedules; at each week cells are trypsinized, reseeded into 96 wells and rechallenged for 72 hours across dose concentrations, n=4. This is a harvested/reseeded dose-response assay, not the continuation of Figure2 endpoint wells. The captions explicitly say Figure3A/B repeat the SAME empty-vector, BRAFV600E and week1 continuous data for visual comparison. These repeated displays are not extra replicates. Both source fitting and train/test splitting must deduplicate them.

Figure3C measures a different, more direct cell-death observation: adherent and nonadherent cells are collected and PI stained for flow cytometry on days3/7 of each week across five weeks, n=2 independent experiments with range bars. During day5 medium changes, nonadherent cells are retained and returned for day7 analysis. These measurements support the author's empirical death-timing investigation, but are proportions in separate assays, not per-interval death counts aligned to each Figure2 well or birth-rate observations. They cannot silently become per-cell death labels for the model IBM. Fitting net growth alone still does not identify turnover. Do not use PI proportions from future weeks as early features.

The primary finds resensitization after withdrawal without return to the naive transcriptome, and partial rather than full sensitivity reversal. Consequently a mathematical state returning to x=0/drug-naive growth is an approximation, not a verified molecular or complete functional reset. L1CAM flow distributions supply actual population-marker evidence, but do not identify every individual cell's complete state or track one cell repeatedly.

## Updated minimal task and evidence gate

Name the proposed response accurately: forecast week2 baseline-normalized CellTiter-Glo-derived viability/number estimate after the first holiday, using permitted preholiday source observations and known schedule. This is a real drug-perturbation assay-response test, with an explicitly limited inference to cell counts. Do not promise a same-well count-growth forecast unless metadata authenticates it.

If measurements are destructive sister wells, independent run/plate grouping may support a cohort-level forecast from early cohort measurements to later cohort endpoints. Such a task does not require tracking identical cells, but does require actual run/plate/well relationships; arbitrary column1 at all weeks is not a trajectory. A conditional mean/schedule predictor and ordinary assay-response refit receive identical cohort grouping and early signals. Baseline normalization by initial seeded cells is known from the primary; whether repository values already implement it, and what numeric baseline was used, remain uninspected.

Qualification remains PUBLIC_SCHEMA_PENDING. This is a measurement-contract refinement, not a global STOP or demand for complete RSI before a small numerical test. The initial report's same-information control and full-four-week-fit leakage cautions remain. No new numerical performance, birth/death identification, world-model necessity or RSI effect is established.
