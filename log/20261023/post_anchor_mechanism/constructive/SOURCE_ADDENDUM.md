## Concrete task now possible to qualify, not yet ready to fit

The intended bounded task would be clone/founder-level **pretreatment state increment for treatment-specific future outgrowth relative to matched untreated growth**. Candidate initial inputs are the authenticated human preAraC RNA and pre-split clone sizes/counts. Candidate endpoint labels require postAraC and untreated clone counts tied to actual culture/sample IDs, plus a clear shared-ancestor/replicate contract. The paper's growth curves/dose-response readouts offer potential orthogonal function, but the source-data workbook-to-clone/condition mapping is not authenticated by these GEO series identities.

The decisive remaining metadata is narrow and concrete: (1) a published pretreatment cell barcode->founder/clone map; (2) corresponding treated and untreated sample/culture labels and clone count tables; (3) exact timepoint and split-culture relation; (4) what is biological independence versus library/technical split; (5) whether direct measured functional assays join those same clone identities. Publicly deposited matrices alone do not answer them. A source-only tree used for alignment may help label linkage, but endpoint-derived gene modules/branches cannot be fed into a prospective pretreatment predictor.

If these joins exist, a finite same-information direct state-versus-fitness/count diagnostic is scientifically meaningful without a new primitive. If they do not, do not invent single-cell futures or use author endpoint annotations as baseline features. The new evidence licenses bounded join qualification, not label opening, a new updater or RSI. Two cell systems under one principal drug do not establish a general inheritance task family, although they need not prevent a scoped component study.
