## Precise ELDA task if public data later becomes authenticated

Application: choose which patient-specific leukemia drug exposures require prolonged observation before96h nomination, after an early<=24h/36h live-imaging prefix. Article states primary ALL cells in3Dcollagen with marrow stromal cells, wells imaged every30min for80–96h, with50selected drugs tested in25samples. Fiveconcentrations in duplicate and tenvehicle wells per assay are described. These are author counts, not independently counted payload. Purchase/action unit is patient culture well/condition plus controls/repeats and imaging time, not a cell or video frame. Multiple drugs share patient material/control plate and are not independent patient replicates. Future partitions require wholepatient/plate/run and all dose/replicates together, with later patients strictly held out from both update-rule learning and policy selection.

Ordinary models trained on genotype or early mean apparent viability can fail for a particular reason authenticated in the paper: spontaneous culture death, stromal motion, and delayed drug death change what the imaging signal means. EMMA's signal is **pixel area of cells with apparent membrane motion**, not direct viable-cell counts. It detects motion over a six-image/3h sequence, first normalizes by the same well's first3h signal and then by time-matched vehicle means. The paper warns that stromal cells may move dead ALL cells, confounding fast-death traces. Some samples die rapidly in untreated culture and yield unreliable curves. This directly motivates revising an **observation operator**, not merely increasing response-predictor flexibility.

Potential restriction to falsify: an observed motion trace is a mixture of intrinsically active leukemia-cell motion and externally displaced nonviable cells. Early state feedback should update the motion-to-viability observation mapping using drug-independent stromal displacement/control information, while biological drug-state dynamics remain shared. If identifiable from lawful features, this can correct an ordinary function model's systematic error and preserve reusable calibration across drugs/patients. The inherited object would be a learned rule for when/how to revise this measurement mapping, with later improvement performance assessed separately from simply retaining fitted parameters. This is **not yet a novel algorithm**: state-space models, tracking/classification and ordinary latent-observation recalibration are nearest direct controls, and the original software already targets related image artifacts. A residual improvement over endpoint ridge alone would not establish distinctness.

Critical observability issue: motion alone cannot distinguish a dead cell transported by stromal motion from a living mobile cell. A proposed correction needs independent state evidence (e.g. membrane deformation versus coherent field displacement, morphology or orthogonal staining on an allowed subset). Article's endpoint Calcein/PI/CyQUANT checks exist in selected experiments, but matching such labels for all patient/drug trajectories is unverified. They may be destructive and must be charged/purchased; clinical or in-vivo examples do not automatically label every trajectory. No invented independent viability truth.

Potential finite falsifier after acquisition: train on sourcepatients; hide later portions/orthogonal outcomes for heldout wholepatients. Compare strong prefix-to96h direct predictor, ordinary time-series retraining/state-space smoother, raw-control normalization, simple calibrated mixture, same-information segmentation+tracking, active continuation/ordinary value-of-information and equal-budget program search. Give all competitors identical early images, controls, paid orthogonal labels and feedback. First test whether observation correction improves heldout late viable-function prediction and actual nomination rather than just fitting motion. Then test inherited correction rule on later patients; then whether inherited strategy yields better subsequent update steps at fixed feedback/compute than a frozen strategy. Without successive independent tasks, no RSI claim.
