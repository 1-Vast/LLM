## Exact synthetic witness: even tumor-killing ranking is ambiguous

Executed `research/observation_law_search_20261023/biology/witness.py` once, maestro Python -B, exit0; output `WITNESS.json`. No biological numeric file read, no fit/API/wet experiment. The witness is elementary thinning mathematics, not an original algorithm or evidence of actual source selection bias.

For each fresh intervention u, start with known N0=1000 cells. Each survives independently with probability s_u and is captured/passes QC independently with probability c_u conditional on survival. Observed counts C_u have exactly Binomial(1000,s_u*c_u); every captured cell has the same stipulated UMI profile [10,20] in both worlds. Thus raw captured UMI, normalized RNA, depth and all conditional survivor summaries have identical laws.

* World A: U=(s=1,c=.1); V=(s=.5,c=.2).
* World B: U=(s=.5,c=.2); V=(s=1,c=.1).

Both worlds give C_U,C_V ~ Binomial(1000,.1), with identical captured RNA, but the best tumor-killing intervention reverses: V in A, U in B. Equal observation distribution is stronger than equal expected counts. All already observed training tasks may be identical across these worlds; choosing a transfer law that excludes one world is an additional assumption, not information supplied by repeated source fitting.

The witness permits unknown intervention-dependent capture/QC; if capture is known constant and initial representation is known, relative survival can instead be identified in expectation. No claim is made that all platforms have arbitrary capture, or that counts can never be predictive. Random sampling uncertainty still remains.

Even supplying survival=.5 in both worlds does not identify removed cells' preselection state. With survivor state=1, removed-cell preselection state can be0 or2, giving full preselection population means .5 or1.5 while all survivors and total survival match. This concerns an interpretable state immediately before selection, not RNA expression in already dead cells. An endpoint count solves its endpoint-survival target, but does not reconstruct the missing state trajectory.

## Minimal missing measurement depends on the intended claim

* **Relative growth prediction:** matched initial/final guide representation in an independently generated, authenticated functional screen with declared reference normalization/time/replicates. This provides function supervision, not a hidden state correction for free. Give it to direct/refit/CL controls with identical budget. Norman supplies exposed source growth, not new independent later contexts.
* **Absolute live population expansion:** live-cell counts calibrated to sampling volume or beads and matched initial representation, or another validated absolute abundance assay. A known sampling/capture design can replace some calibration; scRNA observed n alone cannot.
* **Death versus arrest versus differentiation:** longitudinal absolute counts plus a death/birth/state assay, e.g. validated apoptosis/death measurement and division tracking; two endpoint live counts alone constrain net growth but cannot decompose birth and death.
* **Preselection-state correction or fate-specific dynamics:** linked early state-to-later fate/lineage or a validated observation/selection model with sufficient calibration and overlap. Merely adding terminal viability does not identify removed cells' earlier transcriptomes. Independently sampled early and late profiles can support distributional hypotheses but are not individual fate links.

These are assay contracts, not proposed wet work or a demand that every task collect every assay. Choose only the measurement needed for a particular falsifiable claim.

## Concrete application and independent later-task path

Application: rank tumor-selective intervention combinations while separating cytostatic from killing responses. The minimal currently defensible task is the existing conventional normalized-state-to-independent-growth predictor within selected K562. It cannot identify killing selectivity, a therapeutic window, or transfer of an observation correction across later biological tasks.

A genuine later-task path requires new disjoint biological contexts or independently executed intervention experiments with the same calibrated observation/function interface, grouped by complete biological task. Holding out gemgroups, genes within the same exposed panel, guide replicates, hash folds or response-space transforms is not that path. No currently inspected local source certifies the necessary matched later-task corpus for a survival-correcting updater; do not imply one exists. This is a scoped data limitation, not permission to restart huge qualification pipelines.

Then freeze a function/ranking margin on independent tasks; stop if the candidate cannot beat that ordinary envelope at matched total cost. Observation-likelihood modelling, inverse-probability weighting, multi-task fusion and state-space models are standard families; a distinct mechanism and current primary novelty review remain required.

## Remaining uncertainty

We do not know whether survivor selection explains any observed Norman, CDK or ALMANAC error; source papers' descriptive observations cannot prove it. No effect size has been estimated. A constrained population model with independent calibration might be useful; rejecting unconstrained reconstruction does not reject that possibility. Latest cross-domain papers are delegated to other independent roles; this report makes no new 2025–2026 literature-completeness claim.
