## Biology answer

Current normalized means and source function labels can support conditional function prediction, including a scalar constraint on a specified state model. This is legitimate supervision, not zero information. They do not themselves supply real/imagined transition replacement, authenticated clean counterfactual observations, or independent shared-dynamics calibration episodes. They do not identify unique transport, an unbiased population state, or a distinctive update mechanism. A nonlinear head or a renamed event statistic is not justified as a novel mechanism by those assets alone. All paid observation information must be shared with strong direct/refit controls.

## Strongest biology challenge

Does absence of SAME-CELL trajectories forbid only literal individual transition replacement, or every useful distribution-level function transfer? A randomized matched-culture experiment can measure time-specific distributions under known actions and independent later outgrowth, even though each scRNA assay destroys different cells. Such an experiment could test a distribution-level functional discrepancy without reconstructing individual trajectories. Treating complete lineage as necessary for every cell-world task would be an excessive restriction.

Also, for the root's two-element OU ±rotation family, can one known force probe and a signed cross-axis later mean identify the sign, without an entire paired trajectory? State exactly which observation resolves the proposed ambiguity rather than demanding full dynamics recovery.

## Actual frontier reply and agreed limit

Frontier accepted the distinction explicitly: same-individual state transitions are required for the literal individual transition-replacement operation, not a universal prerequisite for distribution-level functional repair. Randomized matched independent cultures with time-specific distributions, known actions and independent later outgrowth can support distribution-level discrepancy attribution. Selection/measurement assumptions still require scrutiny, and the same information goes to direct controls.

For root's OU family, frontier specified A=(-1,0) followed by signed E[x2] as an informative cross-axis probe: the two rotations produce opposite means. Thus a single suitable calibrated intervention endpoint can distinguish this two-element family. No complete lineage or continuous trajectory is needed. If only E[x1] under that A probe were observed, rotation sign would remain hidden; the choice of measurement matters. An alternative B=(0,-1) probe with E[x1] is also informative.
