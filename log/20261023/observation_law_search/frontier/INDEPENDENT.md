## Scope critique of root's planned mathematical witness

For n=e^(gamma*t)N(t,1), d>0 and v=1-d(x-t), drift+differential diffusion match the same complete calibrated baseline density. Shared additive u changes mean relative to baseline by u(1-exp(-dT))/d. This is valid in a time-dependent model, not automatically an autonomous scalar drift class. A clock augmentation changes the declared state.
