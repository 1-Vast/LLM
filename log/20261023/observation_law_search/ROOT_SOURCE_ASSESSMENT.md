## Consequence for mechanism choice

Observed distributions can ground useful predictions without identifying every latent parameter. The requirement is action-relevant identification or empirically validated action prediction, not exhaustive recovery of the whole cell. Conversely an energy-selected decomposition cannot be called the uniquely learned causal cell solely because its marginals fit. The minimum additional experiment depends on the ambiguity: calibrated state counts for capture, known asymmetric forcing for circulation, independent function response for the desired action. Existing legal pair-function feedback can help and is not ruled out by a baseline-only construction.

Learning to choose such probes is a plausible application of standard system identification / experimental design. No distinct estimator, policy restriction or repeatable real error has been supplied in this phase to elevate that application into a new core. Do not introduce a generic “identifiability agent” or editor to rename it.
