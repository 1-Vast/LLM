2. **Informationally Decoupled Trajectory Design for Sim-to-Real System Identification, [2610.10905v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.10905v1).** Read abstract, introduction and mathematical sections IV-B/C plus algorithm context. For named physical parameters it uses Schur-complement information S_i(F)=1/(F^-1)_ii, normalizes by F_ii^alpha_i, searches complementary trajectory segments, and aggregates coverage. Original text explicitly gives tr(F^-1)=sum_i 1/S_i and acknowledges A-optimal uncertainty already reflects coupling. The paper's mechanism addresses distinguishable physical parameters; a cell latent's arbitrary coordinates have no equivalent named-parameter contract. Pure decoupling is coordinate dependent even under orthogonal rotation, so it cannot be naively used as an intrinsic gate deciding whether to revise a latent cell mechanism. Our executed check below demonstrates precisely this mismatch, not a criticism of the original physical-coordinate experiment. A functional prediction/c-optimal criterion can repair the target mismatch, but that is already ordinary decision-focused OED, not our new core.

## Exact equivalence versus real finite-sample contribution

- The historical fixed-head rank-one state edit has an exact scalar-regression update realization. Its current distinctness claim is stopped regardless of biological sample size.
- A learned nonseparable state transition/interaction factor with shared feedback effects is not generally exactly equivalent to one globally fixed-feature regression update. Ordinary direct networks may share expressivity while differing in sample efficiency, optimization and measurement costs. 'A sufficiently big direct model can represent it' is not a valid impossibility argument.
- Gradient-history policies and residual covariance inference are known families, but known-family membership alone does not disprove a new constrained estimator or finite-budget advantage. A meaningful future candidate should specify a measurable repeated failure, an exact update operation, and the structural reason ordinary refitting cannot efficiently exploit the same feedback. No such extra operation passed this independent search.
- Parameter identification is neither necessary nor sufficient for a good drug decision. Functional-equivalent states may be adequate; an identifiable parameter can be irrelevant to top-hit utility. A claim about drug/target rankings must evaluate that utility under measured feedback, not simply improved latent identifiability.
