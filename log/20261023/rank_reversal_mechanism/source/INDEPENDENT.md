## What author FDR means

Cached author commit218cf1bb8c83204da846256951ba5f6b0092890b, src/decipher/scores/aggregate.py: score_interactions calls estiamte_fdrs. For each cell/context group, it concatenates guide-pair residuals over selected sample columns (reps1,2). _inner_estimate_fdrs compares each pair's residual vector against the flattened AAVS-first-position residual reference using scipy.stats.ttest_ind(equal_var=False,nan_policy='omit'), with its default two-sided alternative. statsmodels multipletests(method='fdr_bh') adjusts the resulting per-context vector of pair p-values. Thus released FDR is a BH-adjusted control-relative residual test; it is neither a posterior probability that a pair is true, an error bar on its rank, nor a test of a difference between two cell contexts. Shared controls, guide structure and processing limit naive independence interpretations. This code inspection authenticates intended implementation, not a rerun of published FDR calibration or matching software versions.

Cached score pipeline uses anchor-specific expected-fitness regression and residual standardization, mirror averaging, sample aggregation and final panel standardization. The final context-wide positive affine standardization alone cannot change within-context ordering. Anchor-specific/sample-specific scaling, underlying single-gene expected fitness and measurement noise can affect ordering before that final transform. Consequently observed cross-context ranking differences may reflect biological state, assay/power/library/normalization effects or mixtures; current data do not quantify which dominates. It would be incorrect either to attribute reversals mainly to noise or to dismiss normalization on grounds that all processing is monotone. Within-context rank differences across two biological contexts are not generated by one shared scalar monotone map in general.

## Minimal legitimate diagnostic contract

Freeze before FDR access the exact five development columns MCF7,MDAMB231,MCF10A,CAL27,CAL33, sheet2 only, pair IDs and prior prediction hashes, stable top20 memberships, case/arm menu and the q threshold. Do not select/cast lung, multi-lineage, score-sheet values again or any raw assay arrays. Pair metadata and headers are permitted to align IDs. Source/calibration/direct/identity top20 must be defined solely by the already saved predictions and oracle top20 separately identified as an evaluation-only outcome-selected reference. Retaining oracle annotations is legitimate descriptive context; it is not achievable prospective prediction.

For every frozen nomination set report denominators and counts of finite q, unknown q, q<=.1, and score<0 jointly with q<=.1. Report identical denominators even if unknown or unsupported; never drop query pairs or redefine top20 using target q. No fitting, feature addition, tuning, support reassignment, target-q acquisition policy, new regret metric or recalculation of the prior gate. The number of supported-negative nominations can be compared descriptively for frozen policies but this is not an independent new holdout, nor a calibrated policy-level significance test. Two salts and nested budgets duplicate/shared observations; show cases and equal-context summaries without treating repeats as independent trials. Calibration's nominations were independently shown identical to source mean, so their support counts must agree.

Canonical mirrors may have unequal q although mirrored GI scores agree, since guide-residual constructions/variance can differ. Preserve ordered q and mirror availability in audit output; define max available q as primary conservative descriptive aggregation and min available q as sensitivity, report both. If one orientation is missing, it remains missing-orientation metadata; do not silently claim both orientations supported. Missing overall q stays unknown and is not discarded. A max of BH-adjusted q-values is an operational conservative support annotation; do not present a newly derived FDR guarantee over canonical pairs or the selected20. Missing/invalid range behavior must be frozen before access and may stop on unexpected schema/alignment rather than reinterpret values.

Any fixed pair that is negative and supported in one context but positive and supported in another may be called double-supported opposite-sign released GI. It is not a significant A−B contrast. Significant in A and nonsignificant in B is also not a significant A−B contrast, and no positive-context q can prove absence of lethality. FDR significance is insufficient to establish pairwise rank reliability: testing two pair residuals against controls is not testing their ordering. Genome background differences, MCF10A nonmalignant identity, and differing screen power mean these comparisons cannot establish drug selectivity, causal context mechanisms or clinical benefit.

## Success, stop and scope

Success of this phase is correct complete annotation under the frozen boundary, with missingness/mirror aggregation disclosed and no alteration of predictions or previous decision. It is not a new performance gate. A high support fraction only argues selected residual departures have some author-test support in the completed-screen system; a low support fraction only flags limited such support, not proof that all nominations or biological interactions are false. Stop without extra qualification if access/schema cannot be kept to the allowed boundary or pair alignment fails. Stop after the fixed annotation either way; any rank-reversal mechanism requires separate evidence of differential response/rank reproducibility and a separately motivated prospective protocol. No mandatory raw assay reconstruction is imposed on this valid limited diagnostic.
