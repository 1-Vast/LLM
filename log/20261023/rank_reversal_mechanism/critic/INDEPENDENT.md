## Established observation and narrow implication

The completed-screen diagnostic established an ordinary score-estimation benefit: scalar calibration reduced MSE about10–11%,while exact retained-array audit found identical full rankings in all20cases. That is useful feedback utility without ranking value. It does not imply feedback cannot fix ranks: it establishes that this fitted shared increasing affine channel cannot. The failed endpoint-sharing recipe remains stopped; this phase does not retrofit its support budget,features or selector.

For minimizing outcomes, if initial prediction mu_a<mu_b, an update delta flips that pair only when delta_a-delta_b>mu_b-mu_a. A constant shift cannot do it; a common positive affine transform preserves every pair order. A negative common slope can reverse globally and a nonmonotone link can reorder,so do not call every one-dimensional update ranking-inert. Those alternatives need lawful evidence and their own ordinary controls,not an assumed cell mechanism.

Only inversions crossing the action-selection boundary can change a fixed-cardinality top20set. Reordering already selected actions alone changes an ordering diagnostic but not the mean selected outcome. Correcting a top20boundary inversion can improve decision regret; forcing a wrong boundary inversion can worsen it. A large point error or a large number of flips is not itself a utility signal. Current released-GI endpoint is a score-prioritization proxy,not therapeutic efficacy.

## A simpler actual ranking-repair control

An ordinary fixed-covariance posterior update already changes action contrasts:

    mu'_Q = mu_Q + K_QS (K_SS+R)^(-1) (y_S-mu_S).

The difference between rows a,b of K_QS determines whether their correction can cross their existing score gap. Equal rows produce equal corrections and cannot flip that pair; different supported rows can. This is a concrete operation with lawful support outcomes and source-trained covariance,not a synthetic copy of an arbitrary proposed program. In low rank K=Phi C Phi^T,use the shared r-dimensional solve/Woodbury/cache. Charge source covariance estimation and support acquisition to all arms. A world implementation may beat this under a real nonlinear structural restriction; the Gaussian identity is scoped,not a universal equivalence veto.

An alternative ordinary control fits an action-dependent low-dimensional residual delta_a=phi_a^T w from legal support,with source-learned features and shrinkage,and selects bottom20in the resulting posterior means. It costs a finite r-dimensional ridge solve plus O(nr)predictions and top-kselection; it does not require11621-by11621dense inversion. Any proposed inherited contrast updater must receive no more source/context data or selection opportunities than this solver. Boundary-weighted/ranking-loss source training may change phi or w,both of which ordinary decision-focused direct controls can also learn.

For the current expected additive fixed-cardinality decision,conditional means alone determine the Bayes action:

    argmin_|A|=20 E[sum_(a in A)Y_a | observed history]
      = bottom20 of E[Y_a | observed history].

Thus full joint diffusion is not required merely to compute this terminal action. Conditional covariance can still be valuable for deciding what to observe next,updating means,and coherent uncertainty. Joint dependence also matters for nonlinear,risk/chance objectives or pair interactions in the decision payoff. Do not extend mean sufficiency for this terminal linear utility to the whole sequential experiment design.

## What finite feedback could establish, and what it cannot

Few labels can improve ranks if source tasks supply a genuinely transferable correction geometry,and observed support identifies its action-dependent coefficients near relevant boundary inversions. That statement requires conditional stability/coverage and adequate signal-to-noise; target context shifts outside the geometry or unsupported query contrasts can defeat it. Pairwise differences formed from m scalar labels do not create m(m-1)/2independent biological observations. Published-score normalization and source feature costs remain shared.

A candidate-specific property worth testing would have to explain why one restricted contrast direction is reproducibly learnable with fewer legal outcomes or less total computation than the strongest source-trained direct/ordinary posterior/ranking-aware updater. A generic rankloss,low-rank factor or boundary sampler does not supply that property by naming alone. Conversely a new validated biological restriction or finite-cost guarantee could contribute despite ordinary mathematics. No such new supported restriction is nominated from this primary read; do not run the old endpoint recipe again to search one into existence.

When a genuinely distinct candidate exists,freeze common predictor/history/support,source labels,source search,legal query pool and total compute;compare model repair against ordinary refit/continual learning,pure acquisition against AL/joint-law design,and jointly versus crossed repair/acquisition controls. Training support-only updates with source heldout decision utility must not receive free target query labels or full-world counterfactual truth. Stop if rankings do not change,changed selections do not improve heldout utility,or a cheap matched direct/posterior control explains the result. Independent later contexts are necessary for inheritedU; changing the improver requires common-start G1/G0future improvement tests,not only a trained ranker transferred once.
