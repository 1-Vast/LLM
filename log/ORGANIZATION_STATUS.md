## Scientific gaps and restart conditions

The objective remains **ACTIVE / NOT_ACHIEVED**. No useful new Agent–virtual-cell joint-learning mechanism, general Agent advantage, causal cellular mechanism, inherited-updater benefit or recursive improvement is established. Scoped ordinary prediction/calibration findings and valid negatives remain in their original protocols.

| Gap | Minimum evidence needed before upgrading the claim |
|---|---|
| Engagement to cell function | Same cell/drug/dose/time/preparation rows linking proximal engagement to an independent functional endpoint, with an independently checked join and held-out comparison |
| Dose and time transfer | Measured functional values at multiple doses and treatment times, controls and a frozen split preventing cell/drug leakage; RNA trajectories alone are insufficient |
| Biological replication | Explicit culture/batch/plate identities and an aggregation rule; repeated rows, technical redos and database overlaps are not independent cultures |
| Genetic/target reference | Matched genetic perturbation/rescue or prespecified target-negative controls; annotations and DepMap gene effects do not establish engagement or mediation |
| Orthogonal function | A second numeric functional endpoint under the same intervention schedule with QC; viability/ATP alone does not confirm a mechanism or state transition |
| Fair Agent comparison | Equal information and total resource budgets, frozen splits and utility/regret, leakage review, strong ordinary comparators and independent implementation |
| Background/compound transfer | Reserved backgrounds/compounds, explicit identity mapping, source-level replication and calibration; source identity is not response transport |
| Data eligibility and access | Numeric response tables, provenance/license, units and preparation/randomization fields; bounded failed acquisition is not proof of absence |
