## 2. Research questions and hypotheses

Can the registered dual-core workflow execute a legal forecast/planning/measurement/decision chain without silently dropping query semantics, bypassing budget or using an unpersisted plan? Can a sufficient measured prerequisite permit the next legal action while insufficient or mismatched evidence remains blocked? Which datasets can support interface, reading-quality and terminal comparisons, given their sources and exposure?

Success requires named refusal of unsupported modes; observable shadow/active forecaster invocation; provider calls confined to affordable current plans; and an offline end-to-end measured evidence trace. Contract tests establish software behavior. They do not establish reading accuracy, calibration or improved biological decisions.
