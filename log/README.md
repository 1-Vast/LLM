# MAESTRO Research Log

This is a compact index of research directions, results, failures, and next steps. Detailed, reviewable source records remain in [`research/`](../research/); this directory keeps conclusions that should survive temporary-file cleanup.

- `EXPERIMENT_REGISTER.md`: compact historical study summaries; `research/STUDIES.json` is the current registry.
- `DIRECTIONS.md`: research directions to continue, defer, or stop.
- `RESEARCH_PROGRAM.md`: contextual early drug discovery, research-method transfer, task/capability matrix, novelty boundaries and MT-P0/MT-P1/MT-P2 validation gates.
- `program_review_20261017/`: reproducible synthetic thin-loop diagnosis, official-source receipts and preservation/validation records; no biological experiment.
- `transfer_review_20261017/`: literature review across vision, language agents, robotics, causal inference and virtual cells on what transfers across biological contexts, with recommended (unapplied) changes to the research programme; no experiment.
- `FAILURE_MODES.md`: reproduced failures/blockers and their corresponding fixes.
- `COSTS.md`: boundaries for calls, downloads, and data assets.
- `studies/`: one-page summaries; the study roots remain authoritative for freezes and verification scripts.

`data/external/` contains external data assets; `outputs/` contains only regenerable outputs with explicit research value. Before deleting temporary artifacts, confirm their conclusions are recorded here or in `research/REPORT.md` and `research/EVIDENCE.md`.
