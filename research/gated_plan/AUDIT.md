## 4. Contribution cards

### AG: prerequisite-directed repair

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Problem and failure case | A genetic dependency and a drug phenotype disagree. The readout that would separate "insufficient target engagement" from "mode non-equivalence" is uninterpretable until intact-cell engagement is measured, and the menu has no action that measures it. Example: `eng-atr-ve-821-ach000551`, where ATR gene effect is -1.51 while VE-821 is inactive in GDSC2 K-562 (IC50 37 uM). |
| What closest methods already do | **Precondition-aware planning** (STRIPS; Fikes & Nilsson 1971) plans actions whose preconditions are unmet. **Plan repair** (van der Krogt & de Weerdt 2005; Fox et al. 2006) edits a failed plan. **Model-based diagnosis** chooses the next measurement to separate candidate explanations (de Kleer & Williams 1987; Reiter 1987). **Bayesian experimental design** values measurements by expected information (Lindley 1956; Rainforth et al. 2024), as does **equivalence-class active learning** (EC2; Golovin, Krause & Ray 2010). **Closed-loop hypothesis elimination by experiment** is done by the robot scientist (King et al. 2004). **Selective decision-making** abstains when risk is high (Chow 1970; Geifman & El-Yaniv 2017). |
| MAESTRO's added operation | Name the interpretation gate that blocks a readout, not an action precondition. Map it to a typed capability (quantity, entity, context, units). Admit the result only through typed rules. Propagate the admitted premise to the downstream rule (`evidence_requirements`, `interpretation_gate`). Record the promise and score it against the later result. |
| Why it could change a decision | A conventional selector over the **unexpanded** menu cannot buy the premise, so it defers; that is what happened in 4 of 6 engagement cases. |
| Code and status | `contrast._directed_prerequisite_repair` (production; single action, most coverage then cost); `proposal_arms.RegistryRepairRulePolicy` (evaluation only); typed admission in `models.PremiseRequirement` and `outcome.InterpretationTable`. Not connected to forecasts. |
| Evidence so far | Replay rerun today (6 cases; VALIDATION.md step 6): the same-information control `registry_expanded_selection`, which admits every compilable capability and then selects once, reaches the **same licensed decision at the same cost in 4 of 4** cases where the repair decides. They differ in 2 of 6: dasatinib/ABL1, a different licensed decision; and the MCF7 case, where the repair spends 2.0 and defers while the control spends 0 and defers. |
| Refuting result | The same-information comparators (expanded selection; exact contingent optimum over the closed legal space) match A1's decisions, errors, deferrals and cost on qualified units. Current evidence already points this way on 6 cases. |
| Precise distinction from prior work | The interpretation-gate formalism and typed evidence admission for pharmacology are domain-specific constraints. The search and selection operators are established. If there is a contribution, it is the **task formalisation and reusable typed constraints**, or construction efficiency when the capability space is too large to pre-admit. Superior decisions under matched information are not supported. |

### WM: conditional world-model predictions

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Problem and failure case | Which of several legal conditions will read in a way that separates two hypotheses for an unseen compound? |
| What closest methods already do | Class-conditional empirical frequencies, nearest-reference retrieval, ridge and linear predictors. Linear baselines match deep perturbation predictors (Ahlmann-Eltze et al. 2025, in `protocol_v2/LITERATURE.md`). |
| MAESTRO's added operation | Per-hypothesis reading distributions shrunk pooled → class → structural kernel, conditioned on the real history, consumed by an exact two-step planner (`world.py`, `planning.py`). |
| Why it could change a decision | Structural neighbours might predict which condition discriminates better than the class average. |
| Code and status | Research only (wiring rows 12-13). Production `StatePrediction` does not reach the default selector (row 4). |
| Evidence so far | Kernel strength fitted to 0 on L1000. Action change at most 4.5%, decision change at most 0.6%. Real minus masked within ±0.009 (H6). Forecasts 2-5 times under-calibrated (H5). For unseen compounds no predictor beat "no change" gene-wise (2026-09-26). |
| Refuting result | Already met on development data for the current model: benefit over the masked control excluded at +0.02. |
| Precise distinction | None demonstrated beyond the simple class-conditional layer. |

## 5. Supported and unsupported conclusions

**Agent (AG)**
- **Supported (engineering):** a missing interpretation premise is identified by name. A typed capability is compiled or refused by name. The admitted result updates only its scope. The registered licence follows. There is a real trace ([DATAFLOW.md](DATAFLOW.md) section 4), and 123 invariant tests pass.
- **Supported (decision, 6 cases, not inferential):** on the engagement package, access to the engagement capability changes licensed decisions relative to menu-only arms in 4 of 6 cases.
- **Not supported:** that the directed repair operation adds value over a conventional selector given the same capabilities. No additional algorithmic contribution was identified.
- **Not supported:** any biological confirmation. Engagement is not condition-matched to the viability phenotype, and `not_engaged` has no measured sensitivity.

**World model (WM)**
- **Supported:**
  - the no-world-model fallback is executable;
  - forecasts never become evidence;
  - the class-conditional empirical layer is a usable simple predictor.
- **Not supported:**
  - decision-relevant information beyond simple predictors (excluded at +0.02 on development);
  - calibrated risk;
  - study-shift generalisation.

**JOINT**
- **Not tested and not testable yet:** no wiring and no qualified task. No interaction estimate exists.
