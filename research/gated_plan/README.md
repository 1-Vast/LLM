## What the audit found

1. **The evaluated task is not the claimed task.** Every registered protocol-v1 and protocol-v2 experiment scores
   transcriptomic MoA-class discrimination, which has no interpretation prerequisite. Prerequisite-directed repair
   runs only on the discordance packages (engagement_v1: 6 cases; real_v3: 58 cases, where the repair's certified
   value is 0.000 in 58 of 58).
2. **Historical claims, recomputed (all on exposed data):**
   - The fixed order reaches **63.3-99.4%** of the hindsight oracle's correct decisions. The recorded "99.6%" does not
     reproduce.
   - 81.6% (L1000 LT) and 75.9% (L1000 T) of episodes are unidentifiable under the registered menu and rules.
   - "345-813 units" depends on the current planner's departure rate; it is not a general sample size.
   - GSE70138 had 673 eligible new compounds in 613 units, of which 38 were label-compatible. It is consumed, and it
     was a same-study, new-compound test.
   - Forecasts were 2.0x, 2.3x and 5.0x too low.
3. **Leakage and state problems found by execution:**
   - 12 SciPlex3 conditions were profiled but dropped for having fewer than 20 cells. The v2 menu does not offer them,
     so an outcome (cell survival at toxic doses) shapes the initial legal menu.
   - L1000 development pools count detected identities of all compounds before the split.
   - Unregistered duplicate citations count as independent sources in production.
   - A lysate engagement grant satisfies an engagement premise that names no site.
   - Research scoring is closed-world: the annotated class is always in the pair.
4. **Wiring:**
   - The production default selects on declared detection power. It logs, and does not use, the virtual cell's output.
   - `plan_measurement` and `ReferenceWorld` run only as research arms.
   - Plan composition and licence certificates are implemented but not called by any runtime path.
   - No path uses a forecast to value a prerequisite measurement.
5. **Agent attribution on real data.** The deterministic replay of the engagement package, rerun today, shows the
   following:
   - Access to the engagement capability turns 4 menu-only deferrals into licensed decisions.
   - A same-information selector that admits every capability in advance matches the repair operator's decision and
     cost in all 4.
   - The two differ on 2 cases. On dasatinib/ABL1 they reach different licensed decisions, and only the selector's is
     marked consistent by the independent kinobeads test. On the MCF7 case the repair spends 2.0 and still defers.

## Answers to the four questions

1. **What does the agent accomplish beyond a conventional planner with the same knowledge?**
   - **Measured decision value:** none identified. On the only real task that exercises the repair (6 cases), a
     conventional one-shot selector with the same capability registry reaches the same licensed decisions at equal or
     lower cost. The value observed over menu-only arms belongs to the expanded action space, the registry's content.
   - **Established engineering contribution:** named prerequisite identification, typed admission with named
     refusals, scope-limited updates and contradiction handling. It is shown by a real trace and 123 invariant tests.
     It establishes implementation correctness, not a decision benefit.
2. **What information does the world model contribute beyond a simple predictor?**
   - On development data: none measurable at the decision level. The structural kernel is fitted to zero on L1000.
     Real minus masked lies within ±0.009, which excludes a +0.02 benefit. Forecasts are 2-5 times too low.
   - At the profile level, no predictor beat "no change" for unseen compounds (2026-09-26).
   - The class-conditional empirical layer, itself a simple predictor, carries the forecasting.
3. **Does their combination yield additional value?**
   - Unknown, and not yet testable. Nothing connects forecasts to repair selection, and no task has both a real
     prerequisite gap and a forecastable prerequisite or readout.
   - No interaction estimate exists, positive or negative.
4. **Smallest next falsifiable step.** E-AG1 stage 0:
   - a premise-only census, local, taking minutes and reading no engagement value, of discordance cases with a
     same-context engagement or proximal-function capability;
   - with the registered rule that fewer than 30 target-gene clusters leaves biological AG validation **NOT_READY**.

   In parallel, E-DATA1 decides whether the proxy task has any task and menu fit for planner work, after the two P0
   boundary fixes. The only candidate source of measured genetic and chemical pairing is CPJUMP1. It is not local, and its
   metadata census needs your approval.
