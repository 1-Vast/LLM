Potential contribution: a reproducible gain in selective measurement decisions
for unseen connectivity groups under actual acquisition granularity, matched
total cost and independent terminal outcomes. Falsification: a pre-frozen
selective workflow fails to improve by 0.10 hits/campaign over the best fixed
workflow on qualified independent measurements, with sufficient group precision.

Changed assumption: charge information against the same terminal-confirmation
budget and authenticate experimental bundles and terminal lineage first. This
is a new qualification/opportunity question relative to the old P0 ranking and
RNA/GCP anchor studies. The current data **do not realise the candidate
contribution**. The completed product is a blocked-endpoint audit plus a bounded
technical-repeat evaluator diagnostic. No new acquisition algorithm, LLM benefit,
biological mechanism, experimental saving or positive scientific novelty claim.

All literature byte counts, hashes, failed requests and access levels are in
`source_review/RETRIEVAL.json`; downloaded pages are evidence, not experimental
outcomes. Detailed inaccessible methods and numerical author claims are omitted.
