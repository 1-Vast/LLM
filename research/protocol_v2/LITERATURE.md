# Literature and method review for protocol external-validation-2

**Date:** 2026-09-27. Primary sources were checked by search on this date. DOIs and stable URLs are
given where the publisher lists one. Each method is judged against the data this repository
actually holds, not against its popularity. The adoption decisions themselves are in
[DIAGNOSIS.md](DIAGNOSIS.md) section 4.

**What the repository data are.**
- Two development studies:
  - SciPlex3: 3 lines x 4 doses at 24 h, plus an A549 dose-by-time tier.
  - L1000 Phase I: 4 lines x 6/24 h at 10 uM.
- One consumed external study: GSE70138, 38 label-compatible units.
- 42-256 independent units per development tier.
- Complete condition tables for almost every eligible compound, each with one realisation per
  condition.
- A deterministic registered validator that makes the terminal decision.
- Deterministic logging policies only.

## 7. What the review changes

1. **Safe improvement.** The fixed order is a strong baseline, and support is thin. That calls for
   SPIBB-style constraints: take the baseline where support is low. It does not call for a
   stronger optimiser. The repository's data cannot license SPIBB's formal guarantee, and none
   is claimed.
2. **Calibration.** Calibration across studies cannot be borrowed from conformal theory here,
   because the conditional distribution shifts. Protocol v2 therefore measures coverage failure
   directly (leave-one-study-out). It lets the world model drive stopping only through a bound
   that holds on both development targets.
3. **Evaluation.** Off-policy estimators are unnecessary while condition tables are complete.
   Exact replay is stronger. The binding constraints are independent units and oracle headroom,
   and no estimator can fix either.
4. **Virtual cell.** The virtual-cell literature agrees that simple baselines are hard to beat
   out of distribution. The virtual cell is judged on terminal decisions only.
