# Framework gaps for the mono-pretraining study

Line numbers refer to the working tree on 2026-10-05 (git HEAD `0f7a999`, with uncommitted changes elsewhere in the repository).

## Blocking gaps

| # | Gap | Evidence (file:line) | Method | Consequence for the study |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | **No representation of a drug-pair intervention with orientation.** `Intervention` is a single `identifier` with one dose, one time and targets. `for_action` accepts only the condition keys `dose, dose_unit, dose_nM, dose_uM, time_hours, exposure_hours, intervention, mode, context_identifier, readout` and raises `unexpressible_action_conditions` for anything else, so `orientation`, `anchor_conc`, `library` cannot be declared. `CompositeIntervention` (multi-component) exists but is referenced nowhere outside its own module. `EvidenceAction` has no pair, anchor or orientation field. | `virtual_cell/interface.py:52-61`, `:70-76`; `virtual_cell/biology.py:295-312`; `maestro/models.py:203-` | PROBE (G1) | Pair identity and SV/VS orientation can only be string-encoded into `intervention`, unvalidated. Condition identity (the SV verification is the VS screen) cannot be checked by the framework. |
| G2 | **Predictions for unselected candidates cannot be stored in the authority.** `record_prediction` requires an exact committed plan action and an `awaiting_result` case; the orchestrator stores envelopes only for executed actions. The study's central object, the full-menu ranking (about 158 scores per line and role) needed for menu AUC, calibration and the "which pairs would the model have bought" analysis, lives only in run-log events and the in-memory cache. | `agent/case_store.py:157-199` (refusal at `:179`, `:189`, `:192`); `agent/orchestrator.py:690-700`; `agent/prediction.py:205-240` | PROBE (G2), READ | Menu-level evaluation and the frozen-before-reveal prediction hash must be kept in a separate immutable artifact store. |
| G3 | **Production selection never lets a world-model ranking choose actions.** Prediction output becomes a priority `abs(value) x relevance x weight`, used "only after coverage, cost and set size are equal", i.e. as the last tie-break; the logged `response_priority_use` is `last_tiebreak` / `diagnostic`. A prediction with `in_distribution` None or False is excluded. `abs()` discards sign. The exact selectors enumerate subsets and refuse more than 16 candidates (`exact_candidate_limit_exceeded`); a 158-pair menu is "too_large". The research loop `agent/discovery.py` is separate: one `measure` callback, one label per candidate, fixed rounds, a terminal certification audit, no verify/stop actions and no per-candidate cost. | `agent/orchestrator.py:1759-1812` (`:1776`, `:1798`, `:1812`), `:404-411`, `:602-609`; `maestro/acquisition.py:16`, `:148-164`; `maestro/composition.py:42-63`; `agent/discovery.py:104-165` | READ | Neither production strategy can run the primary arms. The study cannot be called an agent-through-production-interfaces result. |
| G4 | **CaseStore does not enforce the purchase legality the contract requires.** A measured action identifier can be planned again in a later plan version (PK includes `plan_version`; `recorded_action_identifiers` exists and is called nowhere in `src/` or `tools/`); a verify action is accepted with no earlier screen; the budget is charged again. The research `Lab` refuses all of these (re-screen, verify without a revealed hit, last-round screens). Budget is one scalar `cost`; plates, wells and days cannot be tracked. | `agent/case_store.py:149`, `:295-357` (no check), `:437-446`, `:565-578`; `research/.../design/campaign.py:326-380` | PROBE (G4), READ | The authority is weaker than the harness: parity can fail silently in the direction of more purchases. Legality has to be asserted outside the store. |

## Non-blocking but material gaps

| # | Gap | Evidence (file:line) | Method | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| G5 | **Import idempotency needs a caller-supplied result id.** Without it a random uuid is generated; a retry then fails with "Case is not awaiting..." (and, combined with G4, a re-planned action could be imported twice under different ids). With a deterministic id the retry is idempotent (`created=False`). | `agent/case_store.py:472`, `:476-482`, `:655-672` | PROBE (G5) | Derive result ids from content (line, pair, orientation, plate set, release hash). |
| G6 | **Reliability ledger collapses candidates and is inert for uncalibrated models.** (a) Records are de-duplicated on (context, source cluster, time, condition fingerprint); the fingerprint is the result's `conditions` JSON, so two different candidates measured in the same line, source and time with the pair identity outside `conditions` collapse to one record, and the orchestrator then logs `prediction_reliability_duplicate_ignored`. (b) Only intervals that claim coverage are graded; with descriptive intervals (the case for any ranking model without a calibrated band) 10 scored records leave `miss_rate=None`, `weight=1.0`, `provisional=True` forever. (c) Scores are produced only for purchased (selected) actions, so reliability is selection-biased. (d) `ScoredPrediction` has no strategy, use or training-state field. | `maestro/judgment.py:249-265`, `:338-352`, `:437-456`; `agent/orchestrator.py:1440-1530` (`:1511`, `:1520`) | PROBE (G6), READ | Per-line "reliability-gated blending" has no usable production feedback signal; the study must compute reliability itself and may only use purchased outcomes. |
| G7 | **New cell backgrounds are out of domain by construction.** `SupportRegistry.assess` returns `context_unregistered` (in_distribution False, abstain) for any context without a registered slice, and the orchestrator ignores predictions whose `in_distribution` is not True. The primary axis of the study (held-out lines) therefore abstains unless the target line is registered with observed rows. | `virtual_cell/applicability.py:221`; `agent/orchestrator.py:1798` | READ | Needs an explicit "new context, validated transfer" level, or the pair of checks defeats the use case. |
| G8 | **Prediction cache identity excludes the backend's training state.** The key is the request payload (without tracking fields) plus the backend *name*; `model_version` is part of the payload, so different folds, history draws or seeds served under the same version string in one controller would reuse the first prediction. Also no semantic range check of a readout (a probability outside [0, 1] passes if finite). | `virtual_cell/interface.py:974-1039` (`:986-993`), `:544-632`, `:708-730` | READ | Encode fold, draw, seed and artifact hash in `model_version`, or disable the cache for the study. |
| G9 | **Strategy and model versions are not recorded with the prediction or the decision in the authority.** `runtime_contract` logs the strategy name and the backend name in the run log; the case-store envelope holds request and prediction (`model_version`, optional `artifact_sha256`) but no strategy, policy parameters, history-set digest or code hash; the "diagnostic vs decision" use appears as per-turn strings, not as a per-prediction flag. | `agent/orchestrator.py:404-411`, `:602-609`, `:693-700`, `:1731`; `maestro/judgment.py:249-265` | READ | Audit of "which model and strategy made this purchase" needs the run log plus an external manifest. |
| G10 | **Training data has no home in the request contract.** `PredictionRequest.history` entries must carry text `action` and `outcome`; hundreds of history rows cannot travel in a request (they would enter the cache key), and `ModelCapabilities` has no field for a training-set or fold digest (`calibration_basis` is free text). | `virtual_cell/interface.py:140-152`, `:206-214`, `:986-993` | READ | The n4 history and the mono-pretraining set must be pinned by an external manifest; the production record cannot attest them. |
| G11 | **The combination world is outside the world-model protocol and has no verification orientation.** `CombinationWorld` takes a numpy `CombinationScreen` of unordered pairs `a < b` with one label per (pair, line); it has no `capabilities/assess_query/predict`, no anchor/library orientation and no second-orientation label. The Jaaks panel with screen and verification labels exists only in the research builder. | `virtual_cell/combination_world.py:35-60`, `:167-` ; `agent/discovery.py:67-72` | READ | A pretrained model needs a new adapter (and G1) before it can be served through `VirtualCellWorldModel`. |
| G12 | **Independence units are caller-asserted.** `independent_units` is any positive integer; `SourceClusterIndex.cluster_of` returns the source id itself unless a cluster was registered, so each plate or file counts as its own cluster; the Jaaks hierarchy (3,106 plates, seeding events, 14 repeat lines) must be registered by the caller. | `agent/case_store.py:58-85`, `:460-464`; `maestro/handoff.py:190-220` (`:208-209`) | READ | Reliability and independence counts would overstate evidence if the plate-to-line hierarchy is not registered. |

## What this means for running the study

1. Run the study on the research harness (frozen builder, contract v2 engine, `Lab`), with legality asserted there (A2-A5 in
   `VERIFICATION_PLAN.md`), and store frozen score files outside the CaseStore.
2. A production-interface parity slice (for example 8 pairs of one line, two rounds) is worth doing only after G1 (pair/orientation
   conditions), G2 (menu-level prediction store) and G4 (legality) are fixed; until then it can only demonstrate refusals. The
   acceptance test for such a slice is 0 purchase mismatches against the harness.
3. Any statement in the report about "the agent" should name the harness policy actually run, not the production orchestrator.
4. Items owned by `src/` (not edited here): G1-G12. Suggested priority for the owners: G1, G2, G4, G6(a), G8, G5.
