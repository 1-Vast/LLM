## Prediction, action and terminal influence are separate

The frozen true-pool/cyclic-pool replacement changes the selector in4/9 questions; all four
changes involve refusal, with zero concrete top-action switches. A predicted selector versus
the fixed comparison action changes9/9 outputs, split into2 concrete changes and7 refusals.
These are prediction-based choices on historical controls. Response/reading accuracy, terminal
change conditional on action change, and actual utility remain unavailable; undefined
denominators are `null`, not zero or invented favorable outcomes.

The first-round matrix still has18/27 EGR1 zeros and7/9 refusals. Seed-average plate10 selects
0.5uM while the other pools select5uM. This does not establish repeatable individual benefit,
but it also prevents claiming that interaction or headroom is absent. Pool and plate remain
confounded. A confidence interval crossing zero indicates insufficient evidence, not proof
of no meaningful benefit; stopping needs a predeclared worthwhile-effect threshold and an
upper bound below it.

The software audit actually executes12 `DecisionPath` variants: three constructed forecasts,
two policies and two forecast timings. Every arm has the same constructed action budget1
and two constructed forecast calls. Forecast and policy swaps change actions and constructed
terminal labels; timing changes order without changing the action. This proves a software
path can use forecast content, not that STATE or WorldV2 improves biology. Constructed labels
cannot fill missing measured endpoints or demonstrate a matched-token multi-agent advantage.

## What remains research-only

Display, ranking and support for a particular risk claim are separate forecast permissions;
none is a measured-evidence category. No new risk/abstention threshold, ensemble arbitration,
amplitude priority, response-to-mechanism mapping, learning-based scheduler or e-process is
promoted. Composite routing remains explicit first-eligible engineering routing; use a pinned
single backend for attribution or existing `predict_all` for separate comparisons. No tested
ensemble improvement justifies changing its default.

The paired-baseline hook, action/double centering, residual diagnosis, matched-only permutations,
development-only shrinkage and software influence audit remain in research. A hypothetical
outcome must not update measured biology. Multiple agents and repeated analyses do not create
independent physical evidence. RNA accuracy, reading accuracy, selected-action calibration and
terminal utility must be established on their own appropriate observations.
