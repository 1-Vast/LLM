<h1 class="front-h1">Identifying and targeting cancer dependencies with small molecules</h1>

<p class="front-body"><img src="images/compound2.png" class="compound2" align="left" />The Cancer Therapeutics Response Portal (CTRP) links genetic, lineage, and other cellular features of cancer cell lines to small-molecule sensitivity with the goal of accelerating discovery of patient-matched cancer therapeutics. <br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;We generated an 'Informer Set' of 481 small-molecule probes and drugs that selectively target distinct nodes in cell circuitry and that collectively modulate a broad array of cell processes. We quantitatively measured the sensitivity of 860 deeply characterized cancer-cell lines to Informer Set compounds, and have undertaken analyses connecting sensitivity to cancer features, including mutations, gene expression, copy-number variation, and lineage. These analyses, and links to the underlying data, are provided openly on the CTRP. <br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The CTRP is a living resource for the biomedical research community that can be mined to develop insights into small-molecule mechanisms of action and novel therapeutic hypotheses, and to support future discovery of drugs matched to patients based on predictive biomarkers.</p>
<div class="content-bottom content-bottom-left">

  <img src="images/resources_gray.png" alt="" align="left">
  <p>The National Cancer Institute's CTD2 Network maintains an <a href="https://ocg.cancer.gov/programs/ctd2/data-portal/" target="_dm">Open-Access Data Portal</a> that makes available raw data downloads from member Centers, including all raw sensitivity and enrichment data and other supporting information from the Broad related to this portal.</p>
  <p>The <a href="http://www.broadinstitute.org/ccle" target="_ccle">Cancer Cell Line Encyclopedia</a> provides public access to genomic data, analysis and visualization for about 1000 cell lines.</p>
