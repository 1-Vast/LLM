## State and evidence authority

| Component | Authority | Explicit boundary |
|---|---|---|
| `CaseStore` | Current case plan, committed action, accepted result, execution status, budget | Original plan identity is mandatory when ambiguous; prediction scores are derived, not new observations |
| `MemoryStore` | Scoped auxiliary context/retrieval entries with epistemic status and provenance | Similarity or reflection cannot substitute for an execution receipt or admitted premise |
| Production `EpisodeStore` | Append-only historical scientific episodes in its own schema | History/retrieval is not the current case's instrument or budget authority |
| `EvidenceState` / admission and decision rules | Qualified mechanism updates under declared conditions | Neither RunLogger nor the model can directly eliminate hypotheses |
| `RunLogger` and round cache | Derived event and handoff views | No budget charge or Store mutation; current in-process view is not restart-restored scientific state |
| ASTRA `DecisionPath` | Opt-in component experiment using production CaseStore | Extra state/cache bindings remain research variables; not a second supported controller |
| ASTRA `FeedbackStore` | Exact research link receipts and derived categorical scores | Does not write CaseStore facts, budget or EvidenceState; no scientific default authority |

Do not replace distinct Stores with an ambiguous shared fact-update API. New
research should compose the supported planner/predictor/selector and schema;
experimental variables that production cannot yet express remain explicitly
research-only. `DecisionPath` and `FeedbackStore` were not promoted or copied into
production in this iteration. Their cache/execution-link differences require a
separate demonstrated need before any future interface change.

The [schema audit](results/20261003_responsibility_followup_v2/schema_scope.json)
records actual class/field definitions, hashes and current legacy callers. The
legacy case contains typed `problem_type`, `retrieved_knowledge` and
`virtual_cell_predictions`; the production episode has a distinct problem type
contract, `retrieved_cases`, `hypothesis_graph`, `virtual_cell_forecasts` and
`branching_interpretation_plan`, among other differences. Observation/measurement
and forecast fields also differ. Equal JSON readability would not certify a
lossless mapping. No automatic converter is introduced and the legacy sources
remain intact. A future versioned converter must retain failures, condition
matching, raw provenance, availability and physical units, or explicitly refuse
unrepresentable records.
