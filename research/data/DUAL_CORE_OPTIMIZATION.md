## Objective and ownership

The agent's proposed contribution is selecting an intervention, readout and time
that resolves a stated uncertainty. The world's proposed contribution is predicting
how an actually realised intervention changes a supported observation in a specified
cell context. Their joint contribution requires better measured decisions at matched
information and budgets. Stable predictions alone do not establish that benefit.

Keep three production packages: `agent` proposes, queries and coordinates;
`maestro` owns deterministic menu, evidence and decision rules; `virtual_cell`
answers supported prediction requests. CaseStore owns running plans and accepted
results. Memories and reliability scores are derived records. EpisodeStore remains
a historical/research case source, not a replacement for running-case execution
results or a second authority for mechanism conclusions.

## Implemented changes

| Boundary | Implementation and verified behaviour |
|---|---|
| Menu | Shared `validate_action_menu` rejects nonfinite/boolean/negative/nonnumeric costs, duplicate/blank IDs and invalid times before selection and reservation. |
| Coverage | Shared exact subset search retains deterministic/probabilistic objectives and source dependence. Canonical ties use cost, size, priority and IDs; permutations give the same choice. |
| Attempt distributions | Existing forecast validator reused in EDV and case rankings. Reject incomplete mass, duplicate branches, unsupported hypotheses, wrong-action envelopes and measurement-status predictions. Missing failure mass is not normalised into success. |
| Signed value | Harmful registered interpretation retains negative expected value, rather than being displayed as zero. |
| Response binding | Same request/version/readout validator in inference, cache, briefing and controller use. Missing requested readouts cannot produce usable briefing/priority. |
| Evidence | `EvidenceState.apply` uses admission: QC, real evidence, explicit condition match, registered rule, source and known units gate mechanism elimination. Unqualified records remain visible and add no independent support. |
| Feedback | CaseStore joins accepted result to its original plan. Immutable request/response envelopes restore scoring after restart. New results for repeated actions require explicit plan version; anonymous repeated names do not resolve to the latest prediction. |
| Execution | Planned, externally reported started, completed, QC failed and cancelled are distinct arm states. Partial QC failure leaves siblings importable. Started arms cannot be cancelled as unexecuted. Failed receipts consume declared action budget cost. |
| Budget | `budget_status` separates pending commitments from recorded use. Neither declared action budget units nor unknown monetary charges become a measured invoice. |
| Final review | Compare all declared action fields, including conditions and readouts. Same name with changed conditions is stale advisory review; no hidden second review changes selection. |
| Provider spend | Missing/incomplete usage or explicitly unpriced model gives unknown charge. Reservations remain for ceiling accounting and are labelled, not converted to known charges. Cache-hit tokens are not double-counted. |

Forecast probabilities remain planning assumptions. Structural validity does not
establish calibration, exchangeability, causal support or experimental validity.
Categorical EDV assumes exclusive registered worlds; coexisting biological causes
need composite worlds or a validated joint model. A Bayes planning choice does
not authorize mechanism elimination. Registered interpretation and evidence admission
remain separate from prediction scoring.

Final review compares fields actually declared. Missing dose, medium, intervention
or endpoint is not authenticated by equality of incomplete records. Legacy results
without start receipts remain compatible only when their plan is unambiguous;
that does not recover hardware logs. Current stored response envelopes are restored;
unsupported historical envelope schemas are left unscored.

## Scientific experiment contract

1. **Response:** predict an exact intervention/dose/time/context endpoint. Evaluate
   action differences as well as absolute error, calibration and abstention on
   held-out physical batches. Cheap background/annotations are eligible; actual
   state must be available before decision. RNA is backend-specific and optional.
2. **Mechanism:** register explanations and their contrasting observables. Include
   insufficient actual perturbation, modality-specific function, recovery and
   omitted factors; allow composite worlds for coexisting causes. A transcript-to-
   function bridge requires independently measured realisation, proximal function
   and phenotype during development. STATE RNA outputs do not supply that bridge.
3. **Joint experiment:** catalogue intervention, dose, readout and time as complete
   actions. Predict all attempted outcomes, including failure, nondetection and
   uninterpretable results. Unknown validity keeps valid-readout-only forecasts out
   of EDV. Repairs re-enter deterministic selection/admission; predictions do not
   close measurement gaps.
4. **Two rounds:** use one real feedback boundary. Scores and mechanism updates
   remain separate. Contradiction of every explanation retains the observation and
   requests a new hypothesis set. Seeds and repeated analysis are not physical repeats.
