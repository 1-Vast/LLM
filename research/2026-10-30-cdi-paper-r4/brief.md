# Research brief — paper-informed CDI revision

Date: 2026-10-30 (Asia/Shanghai)

This is a focused continuation of `2026-10-30-cdi-r3` and the earlier audit of
the user-supplied paper. The biological setting is low-cost drug discovery with
public drug × cell × dose × time × functional-readout data. The question is
whether a molecular contrast between two compounds assigned to the same target
can be transferred into a reproducible cell-function contrast, and whether an
Agent can buy the smallest observation that distinguishes target-mediated
effect from exposure, off-target pharmacology or assay error.

The paper supplies a design reference: a compact perturbation-state
representation, directional phenotype modules, and an externally checked score
revision. It does not supply the target-contrast or intervention evidence needed
for the CDI mechanism. AdaMBind and PBCNet2.0 remain optional molecular priors,
not required architecture.

Confirmed inputs are the local PRISM/DepMap/Klaeger assets, the exact FLT3
retrospective pilot, the official PRISM Repurposing 24Q2 and DepMap 26Q1
metadata/release records inspected by the resource scout, and the saved paper
audits. Potential inputs include public cellular-engagement assays and a new
matched intervention resource. Sealed or confirmation matrices, wet-lab work,
and code promotion are outside this round.

Success requires a frozen candidate, a matched row-level data contract, an
independent challenge, and a decisive test against ordinary hierarchical and
standard-VOI selectors. A masked public replay may test leakage, splits and
equal-cost selection, but it cannot establish target mediation without a linked
engagement or genetic intervention record.

The round budget is one paper evidence pass, one public-resource audit, one
independent candidate challenge, and no new scientific model run until the data
gate is closed.
