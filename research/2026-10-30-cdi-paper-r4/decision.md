# Decision — paper-informed CDI revision

The DOI paper is a useful reference for three operations: directional
cell-state modules, compact perturbation-state representation, and external
auditing of an objective. It does not establish a same-target molecular
contrast, cellular engagement, target intervention, dose/time transfer, or
equal-budget diagnostic selection. Its full CellQ training and validation
contract remains inaccessible in this round.

The surviving candidate is **intervention-gated target contrast**. It is a
conditional research hypothesis, not a validated algorithm. Its central test
is whether a molecular ordering changes selectively under target intervention
and is restored by rescue while exposure and assay controls remain balanced.
The Agent has a narrow role: choose the next legal observation by expected
separation of `H_T` and `H_N`, with random, fixed-balanced, hierarchical and
standard-VOI controls at equal cost. It must stop with `inconclusive` when the
worlds cannot be distinguished.

The public-resource audit returns `data_unavailable` for the required joined
contract. PRISM 24Q2 + DepMap 26Q1 can support a predictive ledger and masked
replay; PXD047949 and the FLT3 CETSA study are separate engagement exemplars,
not matched validation. The FLT3 PRISM r3 signal remains a weak retrospective
association with no target-mediated context effect.

The bounded PRISM 24Q2 metadata pass confirms a better dose/replicate/identity
ledger (2.5 µM screen-level dose, five-day protocol, X1/X2/X3 codes and
`depmap_id`), but exact FLT3 target-annotation joins are sparse and all target
labels remain annotations. This changes the acquisition plan, not the
mechanistic verdict.

The separate DepMap 26Q1 GeneEffect extraction also leaves the verdict
unchanged. Exact matching to the PRISM 19Q4 FLT3 table covered 395 cells and
produced a weak dependency-tertile interaction (`rho = 0.0717`, versus
expression-tertile `rho = 0.0601`) with a near-zero repeated-row direct
correlation (`rho = -0.0056`). Because this is a cross-release genetic
reference without drug dose-time engagement, it is evidence about concordance
only. A fresh engagement-resource search found CellEKT and a live-cell kinase
probe assay as near-overlap templates, but neither closes the public row-level
join.

Therefore: **conditional GO for a data-acquisition/schema gate and one
pre-registered intervention pilot; NO-GO for a full virtual-cell stack, MAML,
PBCNet2.0 or CellQ implementation.** No `src/` or `tools/` code is promoted.
