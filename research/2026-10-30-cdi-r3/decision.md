# CDI decision — 2026-10-30

Status: **CONDITIONAL GO for one intervention pilot; NO-GO for the full stack**.

The FLT3 retrospective `maestro` run is reproducible at the response-table and
correlation level and shows a small held-out association from exact same-target
relative Kd to PRISM pair response.  It does not identify a background-mediated
mechanism: the relative-Kd versus expression-conditioned `D` correlation is
`0.0601`, the screen is a documented technical redo, and target engagement,
genetic perturbation/rescue, preparation and structured exposure metadata are
missing.

The only defensible continuation is a pre-registered identifiability/falsification
pilot with matched A/B compounds, at least three measured contexts, independent
assay days, exposure control and target engagement or perturbation.  The Agent
may select observations only after the candidate pool and M1–M4 models are
frozen, with ordinary hierarchical regression, random and standard-VOI selectors
under the same budget.

Do not claim novelty, causal mediation, prospective savings, or Agent advantage.
Do not add AdaMBind, PBCNet2.0, MAML, a larger virtual-cell representation, or
model-discrimination code to `src/`/`tools/` from this round.  If the required
intervention resource cannot be obtained, park CDI as a standard conditional
regression/active-learning proposal and retain the negative result.
