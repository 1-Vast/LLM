# Cross-scale differential identification R3

**Status: exploratory retrospective association; no target mechanism, Agent advantage or promotion established.**

This FLT3 pilot tested whether exact same-target relative Kd adds predictive information for PRISM pair viability. Pair-grouped RMSE changed from 0.703181 to 0.700920 and lineage-grouped RMSE from 0.702772 to 0.700309. The expression-conditioned context contrast had Spearman rho 0.0601 with relative Kd across 1,109 pair-by-dose contrasts. These exposed retrospective observations do not establish cellular target engagement, causal mediation, independent biological replication or prospective selection benefit.

The next candidate is a conditional matched intervention design that separates target-mediated from exposure, off-target and assay explanations at equal cost. PRISM response matrices, molecule-level CSVs, detailed outputs and acquired source bodies are omitted; this published workspace supports knowledge continuity but not data-level replay.
