## Reproducibility of the typed decision model

Twelve identical calls, one unchanged state, `jev-1.13.0`:

| Question | Verdict | Agreement | Answers |
|---|---|---:|---|
| `decision_separation` | stable | 1.00 | `True` twelve times, p 0.88 to 0.90 |
| `evidence_sufficiency` | stable | 1.00 | level 2 twelve times |
| `best_separating_action` | **unstable** | **0.41** | `orthogonal_rescue` 7, `proximal_activity` 4, `engagement_shift` 1 |

The gate binds on the one question that can change which action is bought, and on nothing else.

**The reported distribution is not the sampling distribution.** The captured call's own vector
has collision probability 0.27; the measured agreement over twelve calls was 0.41. A source's
probability vector cannot stand in for how often its answer repeats.

**None of the twelve selections was executable.** `orthogonal_rescue` has an unsupplied premise;
`proximal_activity` and `engagement_shift` are three and two supplier steps away. `rna_low`, the
executable decisive action, drew 0.03 and was never chosen. The probe's state carries no supplier
topology and the critic's does, which is a confound, not a result:
[`topology_ablation.py`](topology_ablation.py) runs the comparison with that block as the only
difference.
