# Initial structural mapping — candidate-unexposed

Recorded 2026-10-30 before reading dose-resource primary sources. Reading so far: the episode brief and task instructions only. No developing model files or new outcome values were inspected. Overall research status is ACTIVE / NOT_ACHIEVED.

The domain-neutral object is a change in a response relation under an intervention. Different objects include a change in a single-gene dependency, a change in pairwise nonadditivity, and a change in the relative advantage of two compounds. These are distinct contrasts with distinct observation requirements. An observation contract must identify the intervention, matched comparator, biological unit, timing, endpoint, dose support, and uncertainty. Available task inputs limit what can be identified or predicted; missing descriptors cannot be replaced by an extrapolation claim.

Possible solving principles, with no preferred candidate: estimate a directly matched contrast; use an explicit low-dimensional intervention or dose-response constraint; condition a flexible predictor on observed context; borrow strength through hierarchical pooling; compare ordinal decisions without fitting a full curve; or abstain when measurement support cannot identify the decision. A raw measured grid can support a local comparison while leaving continuous-dose or cross-context claims unidentified.

The simplest counterexplanations to apparent changed relations include measurement noise, mismatched dose or assay timing, endpoint normalization, cell or compound identity mismatch, incomplete observed support, selection of responsive cases, and differences in batch or growth conditions. A shifted baseline, ordinary calibration, or assay-specific scaling could explain a pattern without a new interaction mechanism. Robustness should be assessed with matched observations and uncertainty before assigning mechanism or prediction benefit.

This note proposes neither a model nor novelty. Source admission will ask which of these contrasts the available measurements actually support, and will retain unresolved requirements and negative evidence.
