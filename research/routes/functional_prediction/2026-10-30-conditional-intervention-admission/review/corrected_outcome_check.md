## Partial evidence from the null, and its limit

The aligned gain of −0.02619 is greater than 48 of the 49 null maximum gains. One null gain is at least as large, so the tie-inclusive plus-one p-value is `(1+1)/50=0.04`. All 49 null gains are themselves negative; their minimum, median and maximum are approximately −0.04481, −0.03701 and −0.02446.

Thus the aligned chemistry/profile correspondence performs better relative to frequency than most coherently reassigned correspondences, under the specified scaffold-block exchangeability assumptions. This is a limited conditional alignment signal. It coexists with a clear absolute deficit to frequency, and it does not establish incremental useful prioritization beyond frequency for any tested chemistry method. It cannot be reported as a successful chemistry-gain gate, a validated physical mechanism, or a reason to release the held-out set.

There were 49 distinct mappings, 23 ordered-round/size strata, 170 movable scaffold blocks covering 177 compounds, and 11 fixed singleton strata covering the other 54 compounds. The null is conditional on this mobility and the fixed strata; it is not a biological randomization and does not remove unknown global processing or prove universal exchangeability. The 0.02 p-value resolution and single extreme null draw also limit precision. No additional permutations or altered strata are justified after seeing this result.
