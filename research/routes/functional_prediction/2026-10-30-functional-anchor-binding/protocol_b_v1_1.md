Before any CPJUMP phenotype values or B fitting. Preserve protocol_b.md and review/b_protocol_initial.md. This amendment controls conflicts. This is the first substantive revision of B, not a new budget.

T-B01: Chemical IDs stay in fixed wells and ORFs in two fixed wells across the plates. Therefore position and target identity can be confounded with apparent correspondence. Add a spatial-only ordinary Ridge100 rival using known chemical and ORF positions and their cell interactions. Position vectors contain row16/column24 one-hot indicators plus normalized row/column, their squares, their product and an explicit outer-two-rows-or-columns indicator, for46 columns. Average over a reagent's recorded wells across its two plates, separately by cell. Concatenate chemical46 and matched ORF46; append signed cell and its interactions. Add a further conditional_spatial_ridge100 with the existing conditional chemical/target inputs concatenated with the spatial-only input. This does not establish nuisance removal or independent replication; it tests important simple rival explanations. A positive result still requires later randomized layouts or independent assays.

T-B02: Append cell_mean and gene_cell_shrink10. Cell_mean is source relation mean within that cell; gene_cell_shrink10 shrinks the source target-by-cell mean toward its source cell mean using10 pseudo-observations. Source absent targets fall back to the corresponding mean. Keep pooled gene_shrink10. Metadata already shows only198/260 OOF compound IDs have another training compound for their nominal target;62 lack source target support. Report these counts, not a favorable post-score subset.

The final12-method order is fixed_same, fixed_opposite, global_mean, gene_shrink10, pooled_ridge100, pooled_rbf1, conditional_ridge100, conditional_rbf1, cell_mean, gene_cell_shrink10, spatial_ridge100, conditional_spatial_ridge100. Do not add these four values to the effective query sample size or infer a shared culture identity from plate sorting.
