# A protocol amendment v1.1

2026-10-30, before implementation/execution of the new A matrix. Preserve and read `protocol_a.md` and independent `review/a_protocol_initial.md`; these amendments control any conflicting initial wording. Development outcomes remain historically exposed, not reset by this amendment.

1. **Teacher scope:** source-response PCA uses all18,415 masked canonical genes, including common and source-ineligible genes. Source-only rarity restricts recommendation candidates and scoring, not teacher fitting. Validation/query calls never enter this teacher. Retain the declared rank32, source centering and eigenvalue rule.
2. **Strongest direct comparator:** select the maximum full-CV P@20 over ALL seven noncandidate settings, in the initial panel order: frequency, chemical-only Ridge100, knowledge-only Ridge100, joint Ridge1, joint Ridge10, joint Ridge100, joint RBF1. Identical maximum selection occurs in every coherent null. The candidate remains fixed joint rank32/alpha100. Exact integer recovered-call totals determine all winners/ties/null comparisons.
3. **Attribution:** this tests the frozen metadata package, including its availability/missingness/coverage and regularization effects. A positive gain would not separately identify pharmacological content, distinguish it from availability/maturity patterns, or show that no different chemical representation could encode those annotations. Initial review found183 of213 positive indicators are singletons and29 of54 annotated queries share no positive indicator with their source fold. Keep these limitations; no post-score eligibility or matching relaxation. Metadata-only/new-input claims are distinct from fresh independent experimental information.
4. **Inference:** the profile randomization is a conditional alignment diagnostic under the specified block exchangeability, not a causal, prospective or confirmatory test of teacher mechanism. The bootstrap is conditional on this fixed dataset, fitted/saved predictions and selected aligned direct comparator; it omits refitting, selection and shared-control uncertainty. No general population or biological confidence guarantee follows.

No endpoint, split, annotation, feature, rank, alpha, null mapping, primary denominator, budget or held-out boundary otherwise changes. The projected teacher is analytically ordinary projected Ridge. It may be a useful countertest or output regularizer, not a new algorithm. Keep all63 held-out compounds, S2 values and protected historical responses untouched.
