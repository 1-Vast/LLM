**Composition is not identified by these five maps.** Observing a control and separate ATR, etoposide or glucose-limitation endpoints can constrain environment-specific response updates. It does not observe the combined or ordered application of two environments, nor a time-resolved state transition. Many operator-composition rules agree on every observed endpoint and disagree on their composition. A genericG0+Delta_e fit is ordinary conditional response prediction; it must not be presented as validated composable dynamics. True composition needs the relevant joint/order-dependent observations or a separately justified restrictive assumption with its own disconfirming test.
