## What is admitted

The resource and frozen plate selection are coherent for the narrow question. Chemical48h and ORF48h use parental A549/U2OS, avoiding the discarded chemical48h/CRISPR96h elapsed-time and engineered-line mismatch. Original Methods establish different seeding densities and perturbation procedures, so equal hours cannot identify equivalent biological phase or mechanism. The protocol correctly limits its output to assay geometry and keeps binding, drug efficacy, knockout equivalence, unseen-cell transfer and external validation out of the claim.

The metadata-only checks reproduce 260 source chemical IDs, 256 names, 255 identity groups and 130 nominal design targets. Selected treatment support is 1,040 chemical well identities and 1,040 ORF well identities. Each chemical ID occupies exactly one well position across all four chemical plates; each treatment ORF gene occupies exactly two well positions across all four ORF plates. This confirms both the repeated-measure grouping and persistent layout confounding, without saying anything about the strength of that confound in values.

The group closure and hash folds are suitable for the stated whole-compound grouped task. They do not establish scaffold or target novelty. Of the 260 out-of-fold compound IDs, 198 have a nominal target represented by another training compound and 62 do not (by fold, seen-target counts 41/47, 40/56, 42/56, 36/52, 39/49). This is a mixture of target-support regimes under one compound-split estimand. Keep the main population fixed and describe that support; do not later discard the 62 queries or call the whole evaluation unseen-target transfer.

Prediction-time information is unusually explicit and acceptable for this assay-assisted workflow: target hypothesis, structure, cell, controls, measured ORF references in both cells and source chemical profiles. Query chemical values remain truth only. The ORF/control measurement cost and target-hypothesis assumption must remain attached to any result. The original journal split guidance independently supports keeping all perturbation repeats and duplicate-name compounds together. Conservative connectivity closure is stricter than the named duplicate pairs and is appropriately described as a split restriction rather than intervention equivalence.

## Decisive objections and bounded corrections

**T-B01 — required nuisance rival is absent (admission blocker).** The source admission contract explicitly requires a position/control-only nuisance comparator. None of the eight listed methods is that comparator. The original Plate layout design Methods say the two compounds for a target share outer/inner membership and the associated genetic reagent is assigned the matching outer/inner region. The same positions recur across plates and cells. Gene labels, structures and cell interactions can therefore predict a stable technical relation. Per-plate negative-control centering/MAD does not by itself remove well-position effects.

Bounded correction: freeze one ordinary spatial nuisance rival before values. At minimum it must use chemical and matched-ORF well-position information (including outer/inner membership), cell and their conditional effects, with source-fold-only fitting and no structure or target-identity features. State which measured controls enter it and why; a position-only fit is still useful if a defensible control-only construction is unavailable. Include it among the primary gate comparators and save its predictions. Choose its fixed basis/regularization before values. This adds a comparator, not a new representation. Even winning against it cannot fully disentangle biology from layout without independent/randomized layouts, so that remaining limit must persist.

**T-B02 — simple context means remain an uncontrolled explanation (admission blocker for an intervention-specific information gate).** `global_mean` and `gene_shrink10` pool both cells. Conditional ridge includes a cell intercept and cell-by-target terms, whereas these simpler rivals cannot express them. Conditional RBF is an equal-input nonlinear competitor but is not a substitute for low-variance cell-only or target-by-cell averaging. A positive conditional gain could be entirely a cell-specific overall sign or target-average effect, with no compound-specific conditional information.

Bounded correction: add a source-fold `cell_mean` and a `gene_cell_shrink10` that shrinks each source target/cell mean toward its cell mean, with an explicit fallback for training-unseen targets. Preserve the fixed conditional-ridge candidate and include both simple rivals in the same maximum-comparator rule. No tuning or extra matrix is needed. If only the broad question of whether these two cells differ is retained instead, state that this is the diagnostic being tested and abandon the stronger compound-specific representation rationale; this weaker question alone does not justify a larger virtual-cell experiment.

**T-B03 — lexical cross-modality pairing has no experimental pairing basis (material endpoint ambiguity).** Chemical plate 1 and ORF plate 1 are not verified paired cultures; sorting barcodes only chooses one of two possible cross-modality matchings. `mean(cos(C1,O1), cos(C2,O2))` need not equal the alternate pairing. Saving two same-pair replicate scores does not expose this sensitivity. Their shared fixed well layout also means agreement can reproduce an artifact.

Bounded correction before values: either make the primary relation the average of all four cross-modality plate-pair cosines, or retain the chosen primary summary and preregister the alternate pairing/all-four summary as a required descriptive sensitivity with the same selected comparator and no refitting. Do not select the favorable pairing after access. Calling either construction a fixed assay summary is defensible; calling the lexical pairs biological replicates is not. This issue does not by itself invalidate a plainly labeled fixed summary, but ignoring it weakens the stated continuation decision.

## Controls, endpoint and uncertainty

Per-plate control statistics and the all-eight-plate finite/MAD mask are permitted by the declared prediction-time contract; they do not use query activity. Terminal nonfinite behavior avoids outcome-based eligibility. However, ORF negative controls are 15 biological overexpression reagents selected for weak prior morphology, whereas chemical controls are DMSO. Separate modality/cell normalizations define different reference origins and feature weights. Cosine is consequently a relation between these explicitly normalized assay effects; it is not an experimentally calibrated universal same/opposite biological label. Retain control-role provenance and surviving-feature counts. Do not infer inactivity, target engagement or an agonist/inhibitor mechanism from zero/signed cosine.

`a*r` is a coherent surrogate for predicting which orientation of a reference vector is closer under squared Euclidean geometry: the difference between distance to `+O` and `-O` has the sign of the dot product. Weighting errors by `abs(r)` is also preferable to allowing weak profiles to dominate a three-class label. But the offered action is a predicted sign; the experiment does not administer a reversed ORF or select a wet-lab intervention. The current utility averages signs over all IDs and cells, rather than testing top-ranked probe selection, chemical effect magnitude, laboratory success or a decision threshold. A positive gate supports directional morphology prediction only. It does not yet show practical probe-prioritization benefit. A full practical ranking/selection study is beyond this cheap countertest and should not be added after looking at scores.

All 5,792 retained/raw-feature families are not independent and their numerical units are only header-authenticated. MAD scaling and clipping define an arbitrary but frozen geometry; duplicated/correlated feature families can dominate it. This is acceptable for the small fixed diagnostic if reported as such, and it cannot establish biological non-equivalence independently of the representation. No favorable feature-set search is warranted.

The cluster bootstrap over complete identity groups respects repeated query IDs and contexts, but shared target ORF references, shared control-derived scaling, fixed layouts and source fits couple distinct groups. The protocol correctly says the interval conditions on predictions/selected comparator and omits those errors. It therefore remains an exploratory conditional interval, not a robust generalization interval or confirmatory significance claim. The replicate-positive conjunction also is a dataset sensitivity, not independent replication. No permutation p-value should be invented.

## Ordinary rivals, novelty and final disposition

Conditional ridge, cell interactions, RBF and target shrinkage are standard direct scalar predictors. A successful held-compound conditional ridge result would establish only that this fixed surrogate contains predictable context-associated information beyond the frozen ordinary panel. The design does not learn or test a retained shared representation, distillation, a new mechanistic relation, or an Agent advantage. The CPJUMP1 resource already supplies matched chemical/genetic morphology and target-hypothesis relationships; the present original-Methods reading supports resource and split facts, not an independent assessment of its published matching/directionality results. CellCLIP/UniPert/TIDE statements in the protocol were not independently authenticated by this review, so no closest-prior novelty conclusion follows here. The protocol's explicit no-novelty/core-promotion limit is appropriate.

After T-B01 and T-B02 are frozen and T-B03 is resolved or explicitly bounded, one matrix can be admitted as an exploratory information diagnostic under the existing ceiling. Keep `robust_benefit_established=false`, `core_promotion=false` and the overall objective ACTIVE / NOT_ACHIEVED irrespective of a positive conditional gate. If ordinary context or nuisance models suffice, preserve the valid negative and park this design. No implementation was co-authored, no protocol was edited and no numerical execution is authorized by this initial review alone.
