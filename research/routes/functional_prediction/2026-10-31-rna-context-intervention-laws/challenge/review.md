## Judgment

RCI-01 is a legitimate, presently untested restriction of joint steady-state reporter-product composition. Its interesting question is whether a GT/GC complete-construct contrast acts only through a fixed donor-use group, with one odds multiplier transporting across promoter contexts. The strongest surviving claim is conditional on the assay's measurement process and is limited to synthetic HEK293 reporters. Current evidence neither confirms nor refutes it.

Scientific count analysis is possible under explicit, justified assumptions; missing upstream source code or imperfect junction detection is not an automatic veto. The frozen candidate nevertheless does not itself authorize execution: it explicitly leaves measurement, numerical criteria, protocol, and code unfrozen. A test using corrected coordinates as unquestioned exact donor identities would not yet test the stated biological law. A restricted, biologically interpretable necessary-condition test can require substantially less than a complete molecular census, as derived below.

## What is already known and what remains different

The mutation-efficiency factor in Baeza-Centurion et al. (2020), root/sources/splice_scaling_elife2020.xml s2-1, already transports a scalar inclusion mutation effect across starting PSI values. Its s2-7 also analyzes a trans/cell-type change. The exact displayed equation should be checked against definitions before implementation; the extracted text labels a transformed quantity Delta PSI, whereas the accompanying prose describes the mutation-induced change. The candidate's written probability equation itself is unambiguous. When G equals inclusion Y, the candidate is the scalar law. A new notation or multinomial implementation supplies no novelty.

Shanas et al. (2026), investigator/sources/promoter2026.xml Sec3-Sec8 and Sec29-Sec30, already establishes promoter-specific PSI, alternative effect scales, sequence-dependent response classes, cryptic splice products, retention, and donor/acceptor coupling. Sec8 specifically links first-donor fidelity to upstream intron removal and promoter context, and links downstream donor strength to acceptor choice. The candidate must not claim those observations as discoveries.

The potentially new empirical proposition is stronger: within each donor-use group the edit cannot redistribute the other joint splice choices, and its donor-use odds multiplier cannot change with context. This has new content only if there are informative products beyond a binary inclusion/skipping outcome. If G effectively equals Y, gamma is effectively one, or only one resolvable product occupies each group, the joint restriction is untested or degenerates to prior scalar scaling. Even if a flexible predictor matches its forecast accuracy, a well-powered confirmation or falsification of a biologically specified restriction could remain scientific knowledge. Prediction utility must be reported separately.

## Biological need, competing explanation, and paid information

An assay team can care about intended RNA composition rather than inclusion alone, because the source's inclusion class counts two-intron products even when their junctions are cryptic. The useful output would be an intended-product/aberrant-product forecast for an unmeasured construct-context corner. The paper documents the composition problem; demand, assay savings, and downstream drug-discovery benefit remain proposed applications.

The simplest biological competitor is coupled splice choice: weakening the donor changes acceptor recognition, upstream retention, or selection among alternative donors differently across promoters. This is directly motivated by Sec8 and does not require a new mechanism. The simplest observational competitor is a genotype-dependent correction or barcode/demultiplexing artifact. Sec23's +/-3 nt maximum-MaxEnt correction uses sequence changed by GT>GC, so treating the correction map as genotype invariant is particularly consequential here.

Every rival needs the same three paid profiles, design identities/sequences, and contexts. A family held out from supervised learning is still calibrated with its own three profiles; describe the task as within-family calibrated context transport on families outside supervised tuning. It is not zero-shot prediction. Full-outcome multiplicative transport, q_c(j) proportional to p_c(j) a_j, is the immediate less-restricted competitor: local G-only action sets a_j to one of two values. A constant no-edit forecast and a same-information ordinary nonlinear predictor are also necessary. Scalar scaling is necessary but cannot by itself produce a joint intended-product forecast without an explicit, equally charged allocation rule.

## Minimum biological measurement contract

1. **Unit and population.** Define p and q as compositions of assay-eligible steady-state reporter RNA products, with an explicit denominator and eligibility boundary. Sec23 excludes certain alignments and supports no more than two introns; therefore unrestricted whole-transcript fate or all cellular RNA is not authenticated. Production rate, degradation rate, and kinetics are not separately identified. Sec5's stability controls reduce a systematic inclusion/skipping concern but do not establish equal stability for every cryptic/retained product.
2. **Shared biological identities.** A j is a homologous, design-referenced junction configuration or a declared biological aggregate, not a library-prefixed string. Map fixed donor position and intended junction tuple from each construct design; authenticate indexing, null introns, ordering, and genotype alignment. G is use of the designed donor in either splice-junction slot, irrespective of GT/GC canonical flag. Retention can move that donor from the second to the first observed junction. An intended product needs all its required donor/acceptor choices, not just two introns or G=1.
3. **Observation assumptions.** State which junctions are physically distinguishable, which corrections can merge them, whether selected outcomes are G-pure, and whether measurement/retention probabilities are common between GT and GC within each context. Different contexts may have different depth or product-dependent efficiencies under the sufficient condition below. The edit and barcode must not introduce unmodeled differential classification for the selected biological outcomes. These can be transparent working assumptions supported by geometry and methods; they need not be falsely described as verified facts.
4. **Selection and zeros.** Use original exported counts if available under later authorization, before notebook count thresholds, `fillna(0)`, or source PSI filtering. Do not remove variants for lacking inclusion evidence and then infer completeness. Freeze treatment of absent rows, missing files, zero observations, and unclassifiable mass. A finite sample zero is not true product impossibility. If only thresholded counts are available, model or bound censoring instead of treating thresholds as genotype-independent selection.
5. **Replicates and attribution.** Promoter a/b labels support replicate structure, but exact sample provenance/batches should be frozen. Drug-dose biological replicates are not authenticated by labels alone. Reads are not biological replicates; report family-balanced and sample-aware uncertainty. All 72 pairs also change barcode (local_donor_design_pairs.json); Sec4 barcode controls are partial justification for a sensitivity assumption, not donor-only randomization. A fixed-pair law can be tested without donor-only attribution; biochemical donor causality requires barcode controls, matched edits, or a convincing sensitivity bound.
6. **Evaluation freeze.** Freeze contexts, the full design-eligible 72-pair universe, family identifiers, calibration profile access, intended/aberrant outcomes, likelihood/uncertainty model, practical tolerance, failure criteria, ambiguity treatment, and abstention rules before reading responses. No held-corner selection by favorable outcome, joint richness, or model residual. Low-information strata may be declared inconclusive with coverage reported. Promoter changes are not a promoter-by-drug factorial.

## Why incomplete recovery need not prevent falsification

Let a=0/1 denote GT/GC and let K_c(k|j) be the probability that true biological product j is retained and observed in measured category k in context c. Its columns may sum to less than one, allowing unobserved products. Suppose the same K_c applies to both constructs, and each observed k has one known G value g(k): K_c(k|j)>0 implies G(j)=g(k). Write p_tilde_c(k) and q_tilde_c(k) for observed distributions conditional on retention. Under RCI-01,

    q_tilde_c(k) = p_tilde_c(k) gamma^g(k)
                   / sum_l p_tilde_c(l) gamma^g(l).

This follows by factoring gamma^G(j) out of the sum over j for each k; normalization removes unknown total recovery. K may be outcome dependent, and may differ across contexts, while remaining common across GT/GC within each context. Thus full raw-read accounting, uniform detection efficiency, and perfect separation of all products are stronger than necessary. Merging several products within one G group can preserve the law. Merging across G, edit-dependent remapping, or data-dependent count censoring generally does not. If a measured category merges intended and aberrant products, the equation may survive while intended-product utility remains unidentified.

An even weaker necessary-condition test is available. Choose a fixed subset T of physically meaningful joint outcomes, all with the same known G, and a common genotype measurement/selection process. Conditional on observation in T,

    q_c(j | T) = p_c(j | T).

No gamma, G-group total, or omitted-product mass is needed. For two outcomes this is q_c(j1)p_c(j2)-q_c(j2)p_c(j1)=0. This is a test of biological product redistribution, not a processing-label score, if the outcomes encode authenticated retention or joint junction choices. For example, two remote, mutually distinguishable donor/acceptor configurations excluding the designed donor may provide a G=0 contrast. A correction displacement limited to +/-3 nt can exclude designed-donor origin for a detected donor farther away, provided the exported configuration contains every relevant junction and the stated correction bound applies. A category that is just a corrected donor-position label near the edited site does not meet that condition automatically.

This route can falsify the full local-only law even if many other fates are unmeasured. It cannot validate gamma transport, intended-product prediction, or the full law. Two distinguishable informative products, common measurement, and sufficient replicate precision remain empirical requirements; their presence in these pairs has not been read. A genotype-invariant map need not be proven perfectly before conditional analysis, but the specific danger from this edited motif must be addressed by the chosen outcome geometry or explicit sensitivity assumptions. The general statement that STAR was used is not by itself a justification for ignoring that danger.

## Decision-changing discriminator and outcomes

Prefer a planned sequence of necessary conditions within one frozen comparison, not repeated searches for a favorable product partition:

- First, use fixed biological within-G contrasts in the reference and target contexts. Upstream-retained versus spliced products sharing the designed donor are biologically motivated by Sec8, if mapping can authenticate them. Alternatively use remote G=0 joint products under the weaker contract above. The candidate predicts no conditional redistribution in either group. A reproducible departure across promoter replicates that exceeds a predeclared practical tolerance and survives measurement/barcode sensitivity falsifies the complete-construct G-only restriction. It can fail even if scalar PSI forecasting succeeds.
- If both G groups and their denominator are identifiable, estimate gamma only from reference GT/GC. The withheld target GC must satisfy both the same gamma and the within-G shape constraints. Compare with per-product multipliers to distinguish failed locality from failed context transport. Reference within-G violations are failures of the law, not a reason to drop those pairs.
- If exact donor/acceptor identities are ambiguous, define an allowed latent allocation set from source geometry and any authenticated error/censoring bounds. Test whether ANY allocation within that set can satisfy the restriction. Rejection for all allocations is informative falsification. If both satisfaction and violation remain possible, report measurement-inconclusive. Do not call the most favorable allocation validation. No numerical uncertainty bound is supplied by the unweighted coordinate audit, so none should be invented.

The branch decisions are distinct: (a) robust product-redistribution/odds violation refutes the declared restriction; (b) precise equivalence across genuine joint choices supports a restricted empirical law; (c) inadequate counts or ambiguity leaves it incomplete; (d) failure to beat ordinary predictors limits practical usefulness but does not itself refute a correctly tested law. Neither statistical nonrejection nor a positive corrected-label forecast establishes biological locality.

### Does the source identify a usable G-pure subset?

It gives a sharper sufficient-condition route, but does not on its own certify an actual populated biological subset among the72pairs. For designed donor D=435, a conservative candidate G=0 class could require that **no endpoint anywhere in the complete final key lies in [432,438]**, rather than checking just a retained representative donor column. This implication requires all of the following:

1. Valid pair fusion and the endpoint ordering represent the actual product's complete relevant junctions, so use of D is represented as a raw donor endpoint. The source provides the algorithm, not empirical proof of this premise.
2. D is a valid, nonminimum donor-score axis column and has more than one candidate in its +/-3 neighborhood for both constructs, so a represented D must survive correction within[432,438]. These are axis/identity checks, not requests for score values; they have not been completed in this review.
3. No later rule can remove that represented donor while retaining the row in the selected class. Under a valid ordered two-intron representation and ordinary bounded correction, the >1100 acceptor rule cannot remove a second donor at435, because its preceding acceptor would be below435 before correction. A first donor at435 is not the donor erased by that rule. This conditional argument must not be extended to malformed/crossed endpoint representations or unbounded exceptional corrections.
4. The final biological bins and their inclusion probabilities are common between GT and GC within each context. They must encode remote joint splice choices or retention, not merely distinct corrected labels. Score/correction neighborhoods should avoid the edited site and barcode, or their effects must be bounded. Mapping, barcode assignment, and amplification can still introduce differential selection despite a pure donor grouping.

If these conditions are justified, grouping remote endpoints into two fixed distinguishable biological G=0 fates permits the necessary-condition ratio test already derived: q_c(j1)p_c(j2)=q_c(j2)p_c(j1). Unknown mass elsewhere need not be recovered. The script does not require surrendering this test solely because `_o` is representative. Conversely, neither one remote representative `_o` tuple nor a final key far from D is sufficient without endpoint-survival and pair-completeness premises. A biological G=1 class, and especially exact intended-product mass, needs stronger resolution of near-D preimages; the source has not supplied that resolution.

The concrete next factual discriminator is therefore source/identity authentication of pair completeness and designed-endpoint survival for a predeclared remote-outcome partition, or an informative bound on their violation. If established, the measurement objection can become a conditional biological falsification analysis. If not, the premise remains incomplete; the local law is still not refuted.
