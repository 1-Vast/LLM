# RCI-01 E1: a proposed necessary-condition falsification test

This does not replace the frozen joint-product question with a weaker success endpoint. It would test one necessary implication of RCI-01v1. Failure could stop that restriction under explicit measurement assumptions; passing could not establish full transport, useful learning, physical donor use, assay savings or drug benefit. No counts have been exposed and no numerical execution is admitted.

For any two biological RNA products that do not use the edited donor, RCI-01 requires equal relative proportions in GT and GC constructs within each context. The gamma multiplier and the amount of unobserved G=1 RNA cancel. A common observation/selection kernel restricted to those products also cancels. Perfect recovery of every RNA fate is unnecessary. Conversely, genotype-dependent assignment, hidden-junction mixing or selection can invalidate the biological interpretation of an observed violation.

Proposed finite frame: the72design-verified GT/GC complete-construct pairs, without response-selected family exclusion. Possible contexts are untreated EF1alpha a/b and untreated full-CMV a/b, authenticated from the official SDRF and figure-code dictionary. The first acquired EF1alpha control table remains response-opaque. At most three additional processed files would be needed; no raw sequencing or author code would be run.

Proposed fixed observable bins, in source reporter coordinates: S is an exact single junction(88,1214), the cassette-skipping product. C comprises two-junction keys(88,a,d,1214), with303<=a<=420 and450<=d<=600, representing upstream excision and a remote cassette-region donor. Every boundary is separated from the designed donor435 and its+/-3correction window. The bounds are structural/source-coordinate choices, not selected using response support or effects. Nonconforming, ambiguous and absent records do not become zeros or hidden exclusions. A final protocol would need exact encoding, support/abstention rules, thresholds, loss, uncertainty and implementation review before any counts.

Within C union S, predict the target GC C-fraction from the measured target GT fraction under RCI-01. An equally informed ordinary alternative transports the source GT-to-GC log-odds shift to the target GT fraction. All three paid profiles are available to both. Source-calibration failure is itself relevant to the necessary condition. Use family units and actual a/b cultures; read-count precision cannot substitute for biological replication. No claim of zero-shot genes/drugs or independent-source validation follows.
