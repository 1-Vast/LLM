2026-10-31. Independent reviewer. Initial assessment preserved in `initial_independent.md`; no other reviewers' reports or development discussions were opened. Final review additionally incorporates the coordinator-announced static `mpra_maxent_table.py` and identity-only v3 receipt. All source code was read as text, never executed. ## Decision

**A useful conditional necessary-condition test exists, but the proposed broad C/S bins are not yet sufficiently justified for a physical donor-local falsification verdict from the processed tables alone.** Admit a narrowly scoped measurement audit and protocol construction. Hold numerical physical-law testing until the finite-bin observation premise is supported or bounded. The candidate is **untested / measurement-incomplete**, not refuted. The missing requirement is not perfect recovery or proof that the law already holds.

A statement about a processing-label C/S ratio would be executable in principle, but without the conditions below it would answer a different question. A positive or negative label result must not silently become a claim about physical joint RNA products.

## Why the necessary condition is valid

For each context, let K_g(b|j) be expected assignment/retention into observed bin b in {C,S} for physical RNA product j and genotype g. Sufficient conditions are:

- **G-pure preimages:** K_g(b|j)=0 for products using the edited donor (G=1).
- **Common relative observation:** for G=0, K_GC(b|j)=a K_GT(b|j), with one positive scalar a shared across b and j. A more permissive condition can suffice, but this one is explicit and auditable.
- **Barcode condition:** barcode substitutions do not independently change the physical G=0 product composition; any barcode-dependent observation effect is included in the preceding bin-independent scalar.

Under the candidate, q(j)=p(j)/Z for G=0. Therefore expected observed C/S ratios, and C/(C+S), agree between GT and GC. Product-specific incomplete capture and loss of all other RNA classes are permitted. The kernel and scalar may differ between contexts. Neither perfect molecule recovery nor equality of absolute expression is necessary.

Failure rejects the conjunction of the physical restriction and the stated observation/barcode assumptions. Passing cannot establish the full transport law, invariant gamma, intended-product prediction, assay savings or drug benefit. The source-shift rival is a useful empirical comparison after admission, but is not needed to derive this within-context implication.

## Smallest decisive work before counts

1. **Immediate metadata check:** authenticate the actual score-table coordinate headers, reference sequence construction and version used for these exports, including donor 435 and relevant neighborhoods. No score magnitudes are needed. Resolve whether normal local correction and endpoint survival apply. This is cheaper than raw sequencing reanalysis and directly addresses the newly visible coordinate discrepancy.
2. **Finite-bin certificate:** for contributions to C/S, obtain a provenance/QC summary of complementary mate identity, true junction correspondence, and donor-local coverage or a justified topology restriction. Full-length recovery of every RNA product is unnecessary. A count-blind structural restriction to a narrower C region whose donor-local coverage is guaranteed is a possible alternative, but must be derived and frozen before outcomes; it is not yet demonstrated by the current records.
3. **Allow bounded imperfections:** a predeclared, source-supported upper bound on G=1/discordant/erased contamination and differential selection is sufficient if it leaves a useful rejection region. For example, if contamination fractions among selected molecules are bounded by epsilon_GT and epsilon_GC, their arbitrary contribution alone can shift C-fractions by at most epsilon_GT+epsilon_GC. Add an explicit allowance for differential clean-product observation and barcode effects. A violation exceeding that measurement budget plus sampling uncertainty could falsify the restriction conditionally. No numerical bounds are presently established, so inventing a tolerance would not admit the test.

A complete raw-read trace is **not inherently necessary**: an authenticated finite-bin QC certificate, coverage guarantee, or informative error bound can replace it. Nevertheless, a source of preaggregation/coverage evidence remains necessary under the current broad-bin proposal; the final processed representative rows cannot reconstruct it. This review does not authorize obtaining raw reads, running author code or exposing counts. If that evidence is unavailable under scope, report inconclusive and preserve the candidate rather than declaring it false or validated.

## Scientific value and limits

The eLife 2020 original s2-1 already supplies context rescaling from a mutation-specific biophysical parameter. RCI-01 uses ordinary exponential tilting, and the proposed C/S invariance is a stricter joint-product implication, not an algorithmic novelty claim. Its potential use is an inexpensive stop test for an overrestrictive reporter response model. Biological donor competition could violate the restriction; that is precisely what a valid test should be allowed to discover. The 72 complete-construct pairs support a finite assay-specific evaluation, with family grouping and culture replication reflecting different dependence levels. They do not establish external transfer, general gene novelty, endogenous mechanism, or drug benefit.

## Post-initial targeted discussion: coverage guard and actual availability

After the independent record and first final draft, the coordinator proposed using read geometry to replace the hidden-junction trace requirement. This is a useful change in reasoning: a local coverage guarantee can establish the relevant G-purity without full-amplicon reconstruction.

The proposed corrected acceptor interval 416..420 was slightly too wide. Because the fixed **corrected** upstream donor 88 can have raw coordinate as high as 91 under ordinary +/-3 correction, a retained mate with at least 111 aligned bases is only guaranteed at least 20 aligned bases after that first junction. At corrected acceptor 416, raw acceptor 413 leaves 22 reference bases before donor 435; the guarantee does not reach it. A corrected acceptor interval **419..420** repairs that bound: raw acceptor is at least 416, leaving at most 19 bases before 435, while at least 20 post-junction aligned bases remain. Under accurate ordinary linear alignment, a donor-435 junction must then be visible, or an earlier intron has removed the donor so it is not used. Additional observed introns exceeding the detector's limits are filtered, rather than quietly creating the four-endpoint remote key. The argument also needs normal correction/endpoint survival, correctly paired records, and no misleading insertion/deletion/alignment representation. It is a sufficient conditional geometry argument, not evidence that its assumptions hold for every observation.

I subsequently read the identity contract, resulting JSON and its non-author checker as text. In the one pm425a export, the finite 144-construct frame contains 590 identity rows. The check reports 100 GT and 97 GC representative raw-donor-435 occurrences, all corrected within [432,438], with no exception. That is positive consistency evidence for donor survival in represented variants. It neither authenticates every raw preimage nor guarantees all construct rows, contexts, axes or mappings. The broad C identities exist (10 GT rows and 38 GC rows); these are unweighted availability observations, not counts of RNA molecules or a genotype effect.

Crucially, the check's broader 416..420 guard has **no identity rows in either genotype**: its Counter keys are absent because no entries were incremented. Therefore the corrected 419..420 guard is also empty in this acquired calibration export. Its absence is not a biological zero and must not be converted to one. The guarded bin currently supplies no observable C/S comparison for the specified first context. There is no reason to open counts merely to discover that prerequisite again.

**Updated admission conclusion:** A populated geometry-certified remote bin could support a conditional physical necessary-condition test under explicit common-kernel, pairing and barcode assumptions, without mandatory full raw reconstruction. This is stronger than the blanket position that all processed-table tests require a full read trace. Here, however, the broad proposed C is not yet G-pure, and the defensible narrow geometry guard has no exported support in the available calibration sample. The planned broad-bin numerical test remains unadmitted; the narrow-bin attempt is observationally unavailable in that sample. Neither result refutes physical donor-local action. A different justified, populated guard or restricted preaggregation coverage certificate would be decision-changing; widening the guard merely to recover observed entries would require a fresh biological/measurement justification and a frozen revision.
