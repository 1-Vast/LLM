## Judgment

The mathematical necessary-condition test is scientifically meaningful under explicit, imperfect measurement assumptions. Full recovery, equal absolute capture efficiency, and recovery of all donor-using products are unnecessary. However, the proposed broad C/S bins are not yet adequately authenticated as a test of physical donor-local action. Admit protocol development and a narrowly scoped observation audit; do not yet admit an empirical physical-law verdict from these processed tables. This is an incomplete measurement premise, not a refuted law, nor a claim that the relevant problematic branches actually occur.

For a context c, let K_c(b|j) describe assignment and retention into b in {C,S}. Sufficient conditions are: (i) K_c has zero contribution from physical G=1 products to these bins; (ii) for G=0 products, the GT and GC kernels agree up to one bin-independent scalar; (iii) the barcode change neither changes the physical C/S ratio independently of the donor nor produces differential bin capture. Then q(j)=p(j)/Z on G=0 implies equal observed C/S ratios, even with substantial, product-dependent missingness. K may differ across contexts. Thus the invariant is more permissive than complete physical isoform reconstruction. Failure challenges the conjunction of the local-action restriction and these measurement/barcode assumptions; passing only fails to falsify this one implication.

## Source-grounded concerns

1. **Mate identity.** Article Sec23 and README say mates are joined by read identifiers. The actual detector uses even/odd rows, resets indices, and joins by their new row indices. Pipeline proper-pair filtering (flag 0x2), unsorted STAR output, and two positional extractions plausibly preserve pairing, but there is no explicit name/flag comparison or name collation. Proper-pair flags alone do not prove adjacent records are complementary mates. This is checkable, not proof of erroneous pairing.

2. **Endpoint sets are not automatically physical junction graphs.** The detector takes a sorted set union of endpoint coordinates and later treats alternating endpoints as donor/acceptor pairs. This is valid for concordant, correctly paired observations of ordinary, nonoverlapping introns, but it can combine discordant observations and loses which read supported which boundary. A reported (88,1214) is biologically a skipping junction only if that junction/preimage is authenticated. Four remote endpoints do not prove no additional donor-435 junction occurred in an unsequenced interval. Two 150-cycle reads are not generally full-length sequencing of every possible cassette-containing product. The broad C interval permits retained internal sequence spanning 435, so G-purity needs local coverage/topology support, not merely endpoint separation from 435.

3. **Correction and erasure.** Ordinary interior corrections are within +/-3, but maxent() returns coordinate zero for out-of-axis or minimum-axis endpoints, and the one-candidate branch returns a score-like object in the coordinate slot. Positive-only key construction erases zeros. Later, intron-2 coordinates are zeroed when the corrected first acceptor exceeds 1100. Raw-key aggregation, corrected-key aggregation and drop_duplicates retain only representative metadata. Consequently an exported raw-coordinate row cannot certify every contribution to its corrected bin. These are reachability concerns; no claim is made that they contaminate C or S in this dataset. Under a verified interior-axis/nonerasure condition and faithful linear junctions, some branches can be excluded structurally rather than audited molecule by molecule.

4. **Genotype-dependent observation.** Donor scores use 9-nt windows and acceptor scores 23-nt windows. The remote proposed endpoints have a useful sequence-separation rationale: their local scoring windows can avoid the donor edit and barcode. This supports common local correction, conditional on correctly aligned score-table axes and identical local reference sequence. It does not establish equal STAR mapping or selection: references are genotype-specific and filters depend on read alignment. The detector's actual length/deletion/position filters differ slightly from prose (including a repeated reverse-start condition). The official IDF authenticates the mapper repository, not the particular historical execution or its selection errors.

5. **Barcode and replicates.** Every verified pair changes the barcode as well as the donor. Sec3 explicitly places barcodes 58-70 nt upstream of the constant donor to minimize their effects, and Sec15 uses common primers. These make negligible barcode influence a reasonable explicit assumption, not a validated donor-only intervention. Exact barcode demultiplexing can also produce unequal capture. Only bin-independent barcode yield differences cancel. Biological a/b cultures, rather than sequencing reads or isoform rows, must support uncertainty. The 72 pairs do not create 72 independent culture replicates. Source methods discuss UMIs, but the inspected reporter mapper does not establish UMI deduplication.

## Smallest decision-changing check

A restricted provenance certificate for contributions entering C/S is sufficient; a complete transcript census is not needed. It should establish complementary mate identities, true supporting junctions and coverage of the donor-local region where C could hide a junction, the score-table coordinate domain and ordinary correction branch, and absence or a useful upper bound on G=1/discordant/erased contributions. It must cover the preimages of both bins rather than only their retained representative rows. If a narrow design-defined sub-bin has provably complete relevant coverage, it could avoid demanding universal full-length recovery. The current corrected-coordinate identity receipt cannot supply this certificate. Barcode neutrality remains an explicit causal condition even after computational certification.

## Scientific scope

The scalar scaling prior already supplies the basic nonlinear/log-odds scaling idea. The new test's value would be empirical falsification of the richer joint-product restriction, not a new transport algorithm. The proposed ordinary source-shift rival does not repair measurement validity. Equality of C/S within one context is already necessary; cross-context comparisons provide additional opportunities to fail, while passing says nothing about gamma invariance, unobserved G=1 products, intended-product benefit, endogenous rescue, or drug utility. Low support or failed provenance should yield abstention/inconclusive status, not a favorable zero or a biological refutation.
