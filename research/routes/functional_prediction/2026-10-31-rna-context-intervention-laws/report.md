## What this round changes

The strongest new question is whether an edit's local donor action can remain stable while promoter/drug context changes the mixture of RNA products. A measured joint distribution could distinguish this from a change in the local action itself. The desired output is a held construct-context molecular-response forecast, including intended versus aberrant products rather than aggregate inclusion alone. The source is a synthetic HEK293 reporter assay; endogenous rescue, kinetics, efficacy and actual assay savings are not established.

Exported `_o` therefore cannot unmerge an RNA count into physical site-use states. This changes the proposed analysis: the full joint-product law remains unadmitted, while a weaker necessary-condition falsification could be valid with a justified finite biological subset. No amount of success on corrected-label prediction alone would establish the intended biological or drug-discovery claim.

The independent review also improves the measurement requirement. A complete molecular census is unnecessary when a selected pair of biological products is demonstrably pure for the same donor-use group and observed through a common relative kernel. In this assay, however, the broad candidate subset has an unexcluded hidden-junction route, and the geometry-certified narrow subset has no exported identity in the acquired reference sample. That is an incomplete test premise, not a negative result for the biological law.

## Candidate RCI-01v1 and closest-prior boundary

The [frozen neutral candidate](root/candidate_v1.md) asks whether

`q_c(j) = p_c(j) * gamma^G(j) / [1 + (gamma - 1) * p_c(G=1)]`

transfers between declared contexts. Here p is the GT reference product distribution, q the GC complete-construct distribution, and G indicates use of the fixed donor. Reference-context GT/GC profiles estimate gamma; a target-context GT profile predicts the held target-context GC profile. The hypothesized local action requires preservation of product ratios within each G group, stable donor-use odds shifts across contexts, and no creation of a truly unsupported outcome. These are hypotheses to test, not prerequisites that must already be true to allow falsification.

This is ordinary exponential tilting/log-linear multinomial transport. If G is the inclusion class Y, it reduces to the published scalar mutation-scaling law. The original eLife2020 [DOI10.7554/eLife.59959](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.59959), inspected at s2-1/s2-7/s2-9, already estimates a mutation parameter at one starting PSI and transfers its effect to another, including trans-context and splice-site exceptions. The originating Cell2019 [DOI10.1016/j.cell.2018.12.010](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2018.12.010) is metadata-only here. No claims about its unread Methods or universal absence of multi-isoform prior work are made.

The potentially different empirical object is the joint site-use/product channel. The identity

`DeltaP(Y) = (gamma - 1) * Cov_p(Y,G) / [1 + (gamma - 1) * P_p(G)]`

shows why even equal baseline PSI and equal marginal donor use can permit opposite aggregate PSI changes when the joint mixture differs. This is a mathematical possibility, not an observed reversal in the72pairs. If G effectively equals Y, the extra channel collapses and there is no substantive extension beyond scalar scaling. Full-outcome multiplicative transport, scalar scaling with an explicit allocation rule, a same-information ordinary nonlinear predictor and no-edit prediction are mandatory rivals for any later forecast. None was fitted this round.

The proposer preserves [initial reasoning](development/initial_reasoning.md) and a developed [mechanism analysis](development/mechanism_analysis.md). It does not identify molecular rates, factor recruitment or chronological splicing history from mature endpoints. The early scout method-read two2026causal originals: joint QoL/survival [arXiv2602.09537v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.09537v1) and independent selection [arXiv2604.21837v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21837v2).
