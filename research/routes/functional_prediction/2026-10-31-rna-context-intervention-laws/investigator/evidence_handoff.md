## Decision

**The strongest changed evidence is an authenticated finite combinatorial promoter × cassette-sequence assay, including designed motif insertions measured in matched promoter contexts.** This is a better controlled intervention resource than inferred patent ASO neighbors, and is materially different from transporting a drug response through U1C depletion. However, promoter dependence, some motif reversals, simple response-scale objections and prediction of promoter-response groups are already source-owned findings. A new model name or rediscovering these observations would add nothing. The paper leaves the operative recruitment/kinetic/chromatin mechanism unresolved, and does not authenticate a law transporting arbitrary RNA perturbations.

A second, smaller original authenticates POLR3A mutation × gRNA/ASO correction in three cell lines. It replaces inferred pairing with actual assay pairing, but has only two exon regions/four selected mutations, technical triplicates and confounded chemistry/length changes. It cannot cheaply support broad independent generalization. No candidate is admitted to response access or fitting by this handoff.
