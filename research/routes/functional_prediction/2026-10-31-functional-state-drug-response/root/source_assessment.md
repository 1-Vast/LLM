## P1: ClpP activation, recovery and combination response

Daglish et al., Cell Death & Disease2026, DOI10.1038/s41419-026-09061-w. New to this library. Official main HTML acquired through four recorded redirect/request hops; actual scientific main is `sources/clpp_original.html.hop3`, 657449B. Actual reading: Methods for culture, senescence staining, dose response/washout, ATP normalization and combination response; Results for withdrawal and venetoclax; Discussion limitations; Data availability. Local `sources/clpp_original.reading.txt` retains numbered paragraphs. SI, PRIDE matrices and individual experimental outcomes were not acquired/read.

- C1, lines108–112 and173–175: SUM159, MDA-MB-231 and4T1-Luc undergo24/48h treatment followed by drug-free medium and six-day cell-count follow-up. Fig5A reports N=3, but this alone does not identify independently prepared biological repeats. Authors already report slow recovery after24hTR-57 in two lines, sustained arrest after48hTR-57 and rapid recovery after48habemaciclib. These are source-owned observations.
- C2, lines106–107,175–178: beta-gal staining fixes the cells. Mitochondrial/protein/ATP observations are assay- and culture-specific, not a serial nondestructive measurement of the same cells. ATP is normalized by parallel average cell counts. ATP per cell can rebound in glucose after washout despite sustained arrest; it cannot be read as recovered proliferative competence or as a direct death fraction.
- C3, lines142,175,179–180: the mouse experiment injects a fixed500000treated cells and follows tumor formation, terminating at55days. This is a new handling/population denominator, not total surviving burden from the initially treated culture. Finite observed nonrecovery does not establish an infinite-time absorbing state.
- C4, lines143–148 and181–185: TR-57/venetoclax combination experiments use72h combined treatment and measured death/viability. They are not the Fig5 drug-washout population followed by a delayed venetoclax challenge. Their reported HSA combination effect does not authenticate a sequential susceptibility target or a prechallenge-state join.
- C5, lines194–195: proteomics is deposited as PRIDE PXD067842; remaining datasets are available from the corresponding author upon reasonable request. The inspected public contract does not supply a downloadable joined recovery/state/next-challenge table. This does not prove that the table cannot exist. No author contact is authorized or sent.

Practical opening: recovery competence and response to a later senolytic are different functional questions. A finite, prospectively timed recovery observation might help predict a later challenge; this is a proposal, not this paper's demonstrated transport capability. The original already establishes the broad senescence/combination biology, so neither renaming it nor replacing its encoder is a novelty claim.

## P2: multiscale persistence and intermittent therapy as an existing prior

Wang et al., PLOS Computational Biology2025, DOI10.1371/journal.pcbi.1012815. New to this library. Official main HTML `sources/persistence_prior.html`,246948B. Actual reading: data collection, model framing/parameter-source allocation, intermittent-treatment objective narrative, selected holiday results and limitations, Data availability. Exact equations/proofs, SI, author code and source data were not read; plain extraction drops math and is not used to infer an equation.

- C6, `sources/persistence_prior.reading.txt` lines147–150: Dataset1 contains clone-derived epigenetic data; Dataset2 contains mouse tumor-volume trajectories; Dataset3 is publicGSE149383 scRNA-seq. Parameter groups and model-check roles use these separate sources. Dataset3 is not itself a joined state-to-later-functional-response validation table.
- C7, lines149 and274: Methods describe11days erlotinib,8-day break,2days retreatment; Results describe a6-day withdrawal. This unresolved timing discrepancy must be settled against the original source metadata before any reuse. No schedule is silently selected here.
- C8, lines214–225,264–281 and293: a coupled epigenetic/population model, optimized drug holidays and marker-based interpretation of existing scRNA-seq are already published. The authors explicitly note cross-lineage/scale parameter limitations and absence of validation in human dynamics. Their optimization is not a newly observed independent clinical or prospective regimen benefit.
- C9, line133: original points to `https://github.com/WilliamMoriaty/Multiscale-Model-of-Caner-Drug-Resistance`. This is an official-code lead; acquisition/version/license/source review remain separate. No author code is executed.

Consequence: continuum/compartment/state models and holiday optimization are substantive existing rivals. A proposed common conditional response law needs a distinct prospective held-history/preparation test. The new original does not, on the inspected passages, meet the old study52 restart condition.
