The central eligibility question is whether a measured post-first-drug state is genuinely available before the next intervention, with preparation-level joins to a later functional response. A publication reporting drug withdrawal, molecular persistence or a fitted latent initial state does not establish this contract. Important alternative questions are (1) how long a functional susceptibility persists after washout, and (2) whether treatment-induced cell-cycle arrest versus recovery predicts a later drug response. Ordinary survival/compartment models and simple condition-level regressions are strong alternatives.
