## Finding

FSD-01 is a coherent, potentially useful empirical question, but its operative idea is established repeated observation/dynamic functional biomarker prediction. A possible substantive result would be a precisely bounded common response relationship across controlled histories, available before a useful next-challenge deadline. The inspected evidence does not establish that result or authenticate an executable test table. Missing measurement/identity support is not biological refutation.

Independent search added a particularly close target prior omitted from v1: *Novel selective strategies targeting the BCL-2 family to enhance clinical efficacy in ALK-rearranged non-small cell lung cancer*, DOI10.1038/s41419-025-07513-3, original Europe PMC XML in sources/alk_dbp_original.xml (PMC11926089). It measures induced apoptotic priming at16h, compares later96h death, and uses induced dependencies to motivate sequential treatment. This is more direct target-field overlap than general decision-sufficiency donors. It does not establish conditional-mean invariance across unseen histories, nor a general history-free cellular state.

## Strongest simpler rival and decisive test

The strongest simple rival is deadline viable abundance plus early recovery/growth and available phase/dependency measurements in an ordinary regression or low-dimensional kinetic model, alongside history/dose/calendar regression. Where affordable, a directly measured dynamic functional probe such as DBP is a substantive established comparator. Neural encoders and invariance penalties are not necessary to pose or test this question.

A planned decisive test is scientifically describable: fix one real next challenge/horizon and population, authenticate histories and sibling-preparation joins, obtain prospective S0 and a result-available-before-challenge Sd, and compare S0, S0+Sd, H+S0 and H+S0+Sd with equally available labels and strong simple kinetic/count alternatives. Test prediction transport on independent preparations/held histories separately from residual-history equivalence on overlap. Estimate precision against a biologically justified consequential-error criterion; do not invent a threshold now. Preserve a truly hidden source/preparation set because published aggregate effects have been seen.

An actual numerical experiment is **not currently scientifically definable/admissible**: the selected biological system, two observation operators/calendar, response encoding/denominator, exact row/preparation joins, common support, hidden independent histories, likelihood, useful-error precision and public processed data contract remain unspecified. No data, split, code or metric freeze is supplied. The DBP source adds strong prior evidence and real replication, but the main article's request-based availability and unverified preparation joins do not supply a new held-history test. Digitizing its exposed figures would not solve this and would convert prior observations into purported fresh validation.
