# Processed functional data and a repair-tolerance hypothesis

2026-10-31. **No new Virtual Cell mechanism is admitted for fitting.** This round recovered usable processed resources and developed a concrete drug-discovery question: can an independently measured, lesion-specific tolerance to repair-route impairment predict selective drug killing in a new cellular background? The available tables authenticate selected survival experiments, but do not yet identify that tolerance boundary. No workbook response analysis, model fit, Agent experiment or core promotion occurred. The overall objective remains **ACTIVE / NOT_ACHIEVED**.

This is a qualification result, not evidence that functional repair models cannot work. It also does not reject ordinary within-source dose/retest prediction. Those tasks are feasible in principle, but have not acquired a new biological restriction merely by naming a latent variable “repair reserve.”

## Why repair dependence is worth distinguishing from repair outcomes

Macak et al., [Nature Communications 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-67243-0), directly connect a CRISPR editing screen to synthetic-lethal drug repurposing. The original reports selected PRKDC-deficient hiPSC/eHAP1 and BRCA2-mutant/restored Capan-1 survival experiments. Raloxifene, erlotinib, gefitinib and artemether have source-reported selective effects in the hiPSC comparison, whereas only raloxifene and erlotinib transfer to the eHAP1 comparison. These are **published, selected, now-exposed source findings**, not MAESTRO predictions or fresh validation results.

The affected user would be a repurposing team selecting compounds and exposures for a repair-deficient tumor background and a proficient comparator. The proposed output is a shortlist with predicted selective survival differences and an explicit unsupported-context boundary. The concrete problem is a drug liability that fails to transfer despite a shared repair-deficiency label. The source already supplies editing-to-synthetic-lethality reasoning, genetic perturbations and selected isogenic validation. Reproducing that sequence is not a new application. A useful new capability would predict which transfers fail under a small, fixed calibration budget before the target drug responses are measured. Its economical feasibility, biological validity and advantage over ordinary prediction remain unestablished.

The source also supplies adverse examples. Cytarabine can increase the sequence class assigned to NHEJ while decreasing extrachromosomal NHEJ reporter signal. AOX1 loss increases several repair-reporter signals yet sensitizes cells to DNA-PKcs inhibition/cisplatin. These comparisons defeat simple substitutions of “editing fraction” or “reporter activity” for unused repair capacity. They do not independently prove a reserve mechanism: assay substrate, repair demand, exposure, proliferation, metabolic state and polypharmacology remain relevant alternatives. Acute depletion and chronic knockout differences likewise do not by themselves establish adaptive memory.

Precise intervention provenance matters. The hiPSC/eHAP1 NHEJ-deficient state carries **DNA-PKcs K3753R**; workbook `PRKDC (-/-)` labels do not authenticate deletion or absence of the protein. Capan-1 carries BRCA2 6174delT and was genetically corrected in a single-cell-derived line; a workbook “BRCA2 knockout” label does not replace that history. Source-reported biological replication must also be retained: Fig. 1 primary n=1; Fig. 2b/c n=2; Fig. 5c n=4, d–f n=3 and g n=2. These captions provide more evidence than column names, while preparation/clone provenance and independent error structure remain incompletely authenticated.

## The observation boundary

The sequence categories are amplicon proportions classified from sequence features, including the two-base microhomology cutoff. They are not direct repair-route flux or capacity measurements. Schematically, a recovered outcome fraction can depend on outcome production, subsequent selection/growth and amplicon recovery. Different biological processes can give the same fraction. Even a true measurement of used flux would not alone identify unused capacity.

Resazurin adds a metabolic survival proxy; cell abundance, growth and per-cell metabolism remain entangled. The Results describe a three-day assessment, while generic Methods describe one day with ROCK inhibitor plus three days in normal medium and DNA collection after at least five days. Their exact sample crosswalk remains unresolved. Fig. 5c–e uses five-day treatment, while Fig. 5f uses 14-day treatment; those are explicitly distinct windows. Multiplying normalized fractions by normalized resazurin therefore does not currently authenticate an absolute viable-edit yield. This is a measurement limitation under the allowed alternatives, not a universal impossibility theorem. Stronger matched counting, sampling and mechanistic assumptions could identify more, and would need their own evidence.

## A conditional mechanism and its ordinary equivalents

The [frozen question v1](repair_question_v1.md) specifies three objects obtained separately: a background's tolerance to a verified impairment of route `k` under lesion `L`; a compound's relevant functional impairment at a defined exposure; and its previously unobserved survival effect in that background. A route-restoration intervention should change both the calibrated boundary and the predicted liability consistently. The distinguishing observation is a prespecified transfer failure or reversal that genotype and basal fractions miss, yet the independently calibrated boundary predicts.

At use time, the candidate identity/dose/exposure, reference action profile, target genotype and fixed candidate-independent reference panel are available. Candidate survival in the target background is unavailable. Target treatment-induced molecular profiles, engagement measurements and every calibration/control assay must be charged to the workflow. A panel containing each candidate's target survival response measures the answer instead of establishing this capability.

Independent calibration must constrain the prediction. With free latent parameters, `sigmoid(reserve_c - impairment_d)` is exactly additive logistic prediction. A capacity/demand dot product is an ordinary low-rank interaction; multiplying basal category fractions by exponentiated drug shifts is compositional/log-odds transport. Naming those parameters does not establish a cellular mechanism. Parallel-pathway reliability, capacity thresholds, chemical-genetic interactions, functional phenotyping and ordinary dose-response models are substantial prior classes. A boundary alone also does not specify a continuous selective-survival difference: its scale, link/slope and impairment-transport map must be independently defined or learned under a frozen permitted protocol. They remain missing, rather than being fitted retrospectively here.

The fresh challenger recovered a close original target prior, Rajendra et al., [Nucleic Acids Research](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkad1196), issue 2024 with online publication **2023-12-18**. This older source is included for a necessary closest-prior check. Original sections SEC3-5/SEC3-6 already demonstrate titratable cellular repair inhibition, compound potency ranking, target-knockout controls, wild-type/catalytic-mutant rescue and inactive-enantiomer comparisons; SEC4 explicitly describes substrate-dependent POLQ reliance and uncertainty about physiological/chromatin recapitulation. Functional action profiles, rescue and lesion-specific assay design are therefore established ingredients. The inspected passages do not establish the proposed unseen-background survival-tolerance restriction, and a bounded reading cannot establish its universal absence. Root checked the cited original passages after the independent initial review; this verification is not a second independent review.

An important direct adverse prior is Villacampa et al., [Clinical Cancer Research 2025](https://doi.org/10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-24-3148), read at **abstract/metadata level only**. Its post hoc GeparOLA analysis uses a predefined RAD51 functional-HRD threshold in 90 evaluable, genetically preselected HRD samples and reports association with pathological complete response under both treatment arms; exploratory disease-free survival shows no RAD51-group difference. Thus “functional HR measurement improves therapy association beyond genotype” is already target-field prior. That abstract does not establish differential treatment benefit, this lesion-specific tolerance restriction, or an exhaustive novelty assessment. The original repair article's relevant bibliography supplies additional pointers; those pointers were not upgraded to independently read full-text priors.

A minimal conceptual falsification would require two independent backgrounds with relevant deficient/proficient and restoration comparisons, a fixed lesion/route calibration panel, two chemically distinct candidate inhibitors and a non-repair cytotoxic comparator. Freeze the calibration rule and second-background predictions before seeing candidate survival, use compatible survival assays and independently repeated preparations, and measure/control relevant engagement. A failure with comparable boundaries and engagement refutes the stated restriction; unresolved comparability makes it inconclusive. One successful second-background transfer is a proof of concept, not a general learned rule. Equal-information genotype, hierarchical/Ridge, nearest-profile, low-rank and flexible dose-response rivals must receive the same observations and assay budget. None of this new test was performed or qualified as available/cheap.

## Cross-field principles and archive decisions

[Its original source notes](scout/independent_handoff.md) distinguish decision-relevant ambiguity from arbitrary model disagreement. Golovin/Krause/Ray EC2, arXiv:1010.3091v2, supplies a cross-class edge-cutting rule under a specified finite hypothesis/noise/cost model. Its all-test decision-identification guarantee does not establish biological regret, a learned cellular state or unseen-context transfer. Complementary assay sets matter; pointwise disagreement is not the theorem.

Yang/Chen/Wu, DOI 10.1016/j.cma.2025.118198, arXiv:2502.05372v2, uses measured candidate outcomes before discrepancy-informativeness evaluation. It cannot provide free prospective acquisition scores; all preliminary measurements must be costed.
