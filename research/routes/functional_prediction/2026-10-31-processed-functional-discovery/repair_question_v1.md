# Repair tolerance and drug susceptibility: qualification question v1

2026-10-31. This freezes a scientific question and its admission conditions. It is not a numerical protocol, a model/data split freeze, or permission to fit. No workbook response values have been inspected. Published selected treatment findings in the original article have been read and are exposed evidence.

## Question and practical claim

Can an independently measured, lesion-specific tolerance to loss of a DNA-repair route predict selective killing by previously unmeasured compounds in a new cellular background?

The proposed user is a drug-repurposing team choosing compounds and doses for a repair-deficient cancer background and a matched proficient comparator. The output would be a shortlist with predicted selective survival differences and a declared domain of applicability. The proposed capability is reliable transfer across backgrounds under a small, fixed functional-calibration budget. Clinical benefit, broad therapeutic safety and Agent benefit are outside the claim.

The core restriction would connect three separately obtained objects: (1) a background's tolerance boundary under a specified lesion and reference route-impairment challenge; (2) a compound's independently measured impairment of that route at a defined exposure; and (3) an unseen compound-by-background survival response. A route-restoration intervention should change the relevant boundary and drug liability consistently. This is a hypothesis, not an established mechanism or a claim of novelty.

At deployment the inputs would be candidate identity/dose/exposure, its reference functional-action profile, target genotype, and a fixed candidate-independent calibration panel in the target background. The target compound's survival response is unavailable. Any target treatment-induced RNA/protein measurements, reference assays, engagement assays and controls must be counted as acquired measurements. A calibration panel that contains each candidate's target-background survival response would not qualify this use case.

## Ordinary rivals and scientific difference to establish

Compare the source's own genotype/synthetic-lethality rule; chemical-genetic interactions and rescue; conventional functional repair panels; equal-information Ridge/nearest-profile prediction, low-rank drug-by-context models and dose-response models; and ordinary potency, uptake, growth and assay effects. Existing capacity/demand or reliability models are potential principle-level priors. The word "reserve" is not a scientific difference.

If context capacity and compound impairment are unconstrained learned coordinates fitted only to survival, test whether the proposed model is an ordinary additive logistic or low-rank interaction model. The possible new contribution would be an independently supported biological restriction that predicts an intervention under stated measurement conditions. No score advantage or new architecture is presumed.

## Distinguishing observation and falsification

A useful discriminating observation would be a prespecified background-dependent liability reversal or failed transfer that genotype and basal repair fractions do not predict, but independently calibrated tolerance does. Restoration should alter the predicted liability, and two chemically distinct compounds with comparable verified functional impairment should obey the same boundary within the stated lesion/exposure scope. A non-repair cytotoxic comparator tests whether generic toxicity explains the pattern.

A conceptual falsification minimum would use two independent backgrounds with deficient/proficient comparisons including relevant restoration, a fixed route/lesion calibration panel, two distinct candidate inhibitors and a non-repair comparator. Freeze the rule and second-background predictions before candidate survival is revealed. Compatible survival measurements and independent preparations are needed. This is a proposed design; its cheap-data feasibility has not been demonstrated, and a single background transfer would not establish a general learned rule.

The restriction fails if comparable independently calibrated boundaries and relevant engagement lead to incompatible liabilities. Missing engagement, preparation or lesion comparability makes the result inconclusive. If equal-information ordinary models provide the same capability, no learning-mechanism advantage is established. No post-outcome latent-boundary adjustment is permitted.
