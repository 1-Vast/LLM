## Initial judgment

1. **Closest-prior overlap:** The candidate’s core operation—measure a small pre-action functional state, condition a two-action response contrast on it, and test whether history-by-action variation collapses—looks like a bounded conditional-effect/response-panel problem. Dynamic BH3 profiling and functional precision medicine appear to cover the early functional probe → later treatment-response link; ordinary response-panel models with history interactions appear to cover the statistical comparator. I expect no broad representation or “virtual cell” novelty to survive. A potentially significant biological object could remain only if a specific, reproducible history-invariant two-action contrast is shown under independently prepared, chemically and temporally held-out conditions.
2. **Minimum discriminator:** The minimum informative observation is a row-level, same-unit pairing of two randomized next actions after two prespecified histories, with a pre-action probe measured on exchangeable sisters, independent preparation identity, washout/carryover status, and one fixed later endpoint. Without that, the proposed law cannot be separated from history labels, carryover, or ordinary panel regression.
3. **Identifiability/leakage:** I expect positivity/common-support, destructive-sister exchangeability, carryover, and technical-versus-biological replicate issues to be decisive. Holdout tuning, action labels or endpoint-derived probes in S, or use of selected survivors will leak the target and invalidate the contrast claim.
4. **Resource outlook:** Based on the audit’s metadata-only status, PA14 may support only a metadata whitelist if the two source tables become readable; sarcoma and UPEC appear unable to support the complete all-unit A/B numerical run as currently described. No fitting should occur.
5. **Falsifier/stop rule:** A reproducible history-by-action contrast difference at fixed overlapping S exceeding a prespecified consequential margin should falsify the measured-state law. Stop for absent row-level joins, no independent preparations, no common support, carryover failure, or selected-survivor-only outcomes. Restart only after a new resource passes the same frozen admission gate.
6. **Later extension:** Organoid/Agent extensions look premature until the small two-history × two-action experiment is identifiable and beats the ordinary response-panel/history rival at matched cost on a held preparation block.

This judgment is provisional and intentionally skeptical; source reading may narrow or strengthen it but should not convert metadata into biological evidence.
