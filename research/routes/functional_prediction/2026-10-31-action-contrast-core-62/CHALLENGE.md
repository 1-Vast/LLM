# Independent challenge — I-ACT-02 v2

Date: 2026-10-31 (Asia/Shanghai)
Status: final independent review; no fitting, protected-outcome access, workbook-cell inspection, or core edits.

## Verdict

**Do not admit a numerical run. Do not claim a new representation or Virtual Cell contribution.** The candidate can retain a narrow biological question: whether one inexpensive, pre-action assay contract preserves one randomized two-action contrast across two specified histories and independently prepared blocks. That object is potentially significant only as a bounded response law if it survives the matched rival and the frozen falsification design. The current evidence does not show that law or identify a usable benchmark.

## 1. Closest-prior overlap and residual biological object

The closest practical rival is a fixed functional panel (DBP or another early response panel) followed by ordinary response-panel reconstruction with known-history and history-by-action terms. The Morales-Sánchez/Montero review states the functional-precision paradigm directly: living cells are tested functionally, dynamic BH3 profiling measures treatment-induced priming early, and later cytotoxic responses can be predicted. The ALK study in the cited passages combines short-term drug-induced priming with later Annexin-V/DAPI death and uses dependency probes to motivate sequential combinations. These sources do not authenticate the candidate's exact held-history, held-preparation contrast law, but they occupy the early functional measurement → later treatment-choice capability.

Rhaister is an additional response-panel rival: a few measured perturbations can predict unmeasured perturbations from context-level response statistics. PSR/R-PSR/PVE provide the established mathematical language for action-conditioned predictive or value sufficiency. Transportability/CRN/causal-abstraction sources show that moving an action effect across histories requires explicit treatment semantics, positivity, consistency and exchangeability assumptions. Active system-identification work already supplies the principle of choosing a small executable probe that separates response explanations. A compact encoder or history-by-action regression therefore cannot be presented as a new algorithmic principle.

The PA14 primary source does leave a real biological object: CAR→GEN negative hysteresis is order-dependent, wash/pause-sensitive, growth-dependent and decays after recovery; the paper also warns that OD can be distorted by filamentation and validates selected comparisons with CFU. This is direct prior art for history-dependent action contrast and also a counterexample to assuming a scalar cheap readout is portable. The sarcoma source leaves a temporal collateral-sensitivity trajectory after mTKI pretreatment, but its relevant screens have limited replicate structure and no authenticated pre-action state join. UPEC leaves a single-cell pre-ampicillin growth-rate probe and post-washout regrowth observations, but the later rechallenge is for selected survivors, not a complete all-starting-population A/B matrix. Thus the only remaining possible contribution is a **specific, reproducible, bounded contrast law** under a declared assay, history pair, action pair, endpoint and preparation range. It is not a universal cell state, mechanism, or organoid transfer claim.

## 2. Minimal observation that would distinguish the claim

The minimum discriminating unit is a lossless, row-level join containing:

- two prespecified histories H0/H1 in the same admissible preparation block;
- a pre-action S measured on randomized/exchangeable diagnostic sisters (or a validated nondestructive equivalent), with no history identifier encoded in S;
- randomized next actions A/B, both present for each history in every block, with physical dose, order, exposure and washout/carryover record;
- one fixed later endpoint Y and denominator/eligibility rule;
- independent preparation-block identity plus technical replicate identity.

The primary test must compare the history difference in the A-versus-B contrast within overlapping S support. A held preparation block must be scored before its outcomes are opened. To establish more than two-history scope, a third history or an explicitly frozen chemistry holdout is required; it cannot be inferred from the two-history result.

## 3. Identifiability and leakage risks

The candidate is unidentifiable or invalid when either history has no common S support, an action is absent in the overlap, or H is confounded with passage, thaw, day, plate, density or preparation. A destructive probe requires exchangeable sisters; selected-survivor rechallenge changes the estimand to the eligible survivor population and cannot support an all-starting-population claim. Technical wells and serial reads cannot be counted as independent preparations.

Residual history compound is a direct threat: PA14's physical medium exchange and pause, sarcoma's continuous replenishment/no washout, and UPEC's flow washout define different estimands. Carryover must be measured or bounded before action assignment. Endpoint scale, action dose, exposure time and missing/censoring rules must be identical across histories. OD, CTG, CFU, ATP and death are not interchangeable outcomes.

Leakage occurs if the target action, post-action endpoint, or selected-survivor status enters S; if panel choice, normalization, margin, threshold or model selection uses holdout outcomes; if MIC or dose labels are outcome-conditioned and then treated as fixed; or if a target block is used to tune the map. Balancing, embeddings and larger models do not repair absent positivity or exchangeability. A non-significant interaction is not equivalence.

## 4. Can PA14, sarcoma or UPEC support a numerical run?

**No resource is admitted now.** The resource audit is correct that all three are metadata-only leads.

- **PA14 CAR→GEN:** smallest possible bridge, because the primary paper supplies ordered exposure, medium exchange, optional drug-free pause, wash controls and CFU/OD endpoints. It may support a bounded bacterial run only after MOESM18 and the MIC table are readable and a whitelist confirms one row-level A/B/dose/MIC/order/pause/time/preparation/technical-replicate key, both actions in the same unit, and suitable controls. Until then there is no certified numerical benchmark.
- **Sarcoma temporal collateral sensitivity:** the source reports mTKI pretreatment and later infigratinib response, but no authenticated nondestructive S joined to each outcome, no complete paired A/B table, and relevant temporal panels with limited replicate information. It cannot support the candidate's contrast run.
- **UPEC CFT073 ampicillin:** the primary source supports projected-area growth-rate measurement before ampicillin, flow washout and post-washout regrowth. Its rechallenge concerns selected survivors/progeny, with chamber/cell nesting and all-cell denominators unresolved; αMG/LB/M9 conditions and CFU/real-time readouts are distinct estimands. It cannot support the complete two-history × two-randomized-action run.

No fitting, numerical admission, response-array access, or workbook-cell inspection is justified.

## 5. Explicit falsifier and stop/restart rule

For the frozen state contract, define

`Δ(s) = E[Y(A)-Y(B) | S=s,H=H1] − E[Y(A)-Y(B) | S=s,H=H0]`.

The measured-state contrast law is falsified when, within a prospectively declared common-support region, independent preparation blocks reproducibly show `|Δ(s)|` beyond the prespecified consequential equivalence margin. Absolute outcome shifts under both actions do not by themselves falsify contrast invariance. Wide intervals are inconclusive; failure of this S does not prove that no richer state exists.

Stop without fitting if any admission condition fails: no row-level join; missing MIC/physical dose or washout identity; no independent preparation IDs; only technical replicates; no same-unit A/B and reverse/no-drug controls; absent later fixed-action endpoint; selected survivors only when the claim is all-starting-population; common-support or positivity failure; carryover above threshold; or holdout leakage. Also stop if the ordinary same-cost panel/history model matches the proposed map on the frozen held-preparation action decision, if contrast equivalence fails, or if the result disappears under a declared endpoint or chemistry holdout.

Restart only after a new source passes the same metadata whitelist and a new protocol freezes histories, actions, endpoint, margin, support, replicate unit, assay cost and holdout before outcome access. A resource-access failure is a resource failure, not a biological negative result.

## 6. Organoid and Agent extension

An organoid extension is premature: the core contrast has not yet been shown to be identifiable in a simple parental model, and organoids add composition, matrix, exposure and survival-selection changes that alter the estimand. An Agent extension is also premature: no evidence shows that an Agent improves the matched ordinary panel/history decision, and active-probe selection or a learned encoder would add degrees of freedom before the biological law is established. Reconsider either extension only after a bounded primary run passes the contrast-equivalence gate and beats or matches the strongest ordinary rival at equal measured information and cost on a held preparation block, with a separate declared transport design.

## Evidence boundary

This review reuses only the four frozen candidate/audit/design reports and the cited local source passages: PA14 hysteresis, sarcoma temporal collateral sensitivity, UPEC ampicillin, the BH3/FPM and ALK DBP passages, and the local PSR/causal-transport/active-identification readings. The search was not expanded. No protected or sealed MAESTRO outcomes, response arrays, numeric workbook cells, model fits, or source-code edits were used.
