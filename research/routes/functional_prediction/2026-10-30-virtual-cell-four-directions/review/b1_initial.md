Date: 2026-10-30. Written before rebuttal and before any B grouped-model score was disclosed. Reviewer context for B is outcome-blinded beyond the specifically authorized descriptive census. Effective inherited model configuration is unknown.

## Boundary and decision

Reviewed `protocol_b.md`, `audit_b/census.json`, metadata manifests, metadata-only text/provenance audits and selected rows of `metadata_census_source.csv`, the supplied original-paper methods excerpts, and pinned original AssayBench baseline/metric/data-handling source. No training hit arrays, per-task gene-hit vectors, model predictions, or future result files were read. No experiment was run and no protocol/model/source code was edited. The explicitly authorized census contains aggregate historical-training hit statistics; these were read as disclosed prior exposure, not as a fresh validation result.

**Decision: STOP before grouped scoring pending three concrete clarifications/corrections below.** The scientific question is useful and feasible as a bounded internal development test, and most safeguards are sensible. There is a known additional cross-publication label-construction link, and the metric/gate and some model/rival definitions are not yet exact enough to freeze the conclusion.

## B1. Known label-construction dependencies need a pre-score grouping or sensitivity rule

Joining PMID27260156 to PMID29083409 correctly handles their documented GeCKOv2/reanalysis lineage, but the source metadata identify more than unspecified possible overlap:

- The notes on the CERES Avana tasks (PMID29083409) state that ORCS estimated FDR using positive/negative gold-standard gene sets defined jointly from Avana and the ten cell lines of Bertomeu et al., PMID29038160.
- The GeCKOv2 notes (PMID27260156) reuse those gold-standard sets and additionally use PMID27760321 and PMID28162770 to filter negatives.
- The metadata-only source roster shows all three additional publications are in this same official training pool (one, seven, and fourteen task rows, respectively, before primary-set exclusions).

These are documented dependencies in hit-label construction. They do not prove that every hit was copied or that model inputs contain test labels, but they do permit target/fit publication labels to depend on each other even when publication IDs differ. The existing statement that other overlaps are uncertified does not describe these known links adequately. Source-balanced weighting and excluding the two largest sources from the final score do not, by themselves, remove a target-publication label contribution to a fitted label set.

Before scores, choose either a conservative connected grouping of the known calibration/reanalysis publications, or a frozen sensitivity that removes/breaks these specific links from both fitting and scoring. For example, treating {27260156, 29083409, 29038160, 27760321, 28162770} as one connected source group is a defensible conservative rule; distinguish calibration dependence from shared cultures rather than asserting they are the same experiment. Another documented exclusion design is acceptable. Persist raw-publication-to-canonical-group mappings and the resulting fold roster, and compute the three-publication rare-gene support rule on these canonical independent-source units where appropriate. Unknown remaining donor/processing overlap still limits any result to internal evaluation.

## B2. Freeze the score, comparison and continuation gate exactly

The original paper and code expose a genuine naming trap. The supplied paper excerpt presents Precision@k divided by min(k, number of positive genes), while pinned `benchmark/metrics.py:264-299` has ordinary precision by default and the positive-count-normalized variant only when `normalization` is supplied. For the stated experimental budget of 50 picks, freeze primary **P@50 = correct hits among the 50 ranked eligible genes / 50**, using binary directional hits. Do not silently use the positive-count-normalized benchmark variant. This distinction is substantial on rare libraries having fewer than 50 hits.

Also specify before scores:

1. Whether all-library P@50 includes only the same tasks with at least 50 rare eligible genes, or additionally tasks eligible only for the all-library analysis. Apply candidate-minus-baseline comparisons to identical task/source denominators; define the all-library rule for libraries below 50 genes. Report eligibility exclusions by source without conditioning on held-out hit counts.
2. Define `non-fitness/drug`: the complement of Fitness, the Drug category, or two separately required subsets. The current slash notation and the gate's word `benefit` do not identify an exact subset, minimum denominator, comparator, or threshold. Define the required real-context-versus-permuted comparison similarly. An unscoreable required subset should be INCONCLUSIVE/NOT_PASSED, not silently waived.
3. Define `best baseline` as an exact member of the frozen panel selected by a declared aggregate metric, rather than a different oracle per source unless that is explicitly intended. State whether fold improvement is against that globally chosen rival or each fold's best rival. The primary aggregate must average source scores across all eligible source groups, not average five fold means equally when their source counts differ.
4. Specify bootstrap seed, percentile/tail and confidence level, and whether the rival is held fixed or reselected during each bootstrap draw. Keep the source resampling unit consistent with B1. A fixed-candidate comparison to every frozen rival is also acceptable. Define relative improvement at a zero baseline; the absolute +0.05 gate remains meaningful. Do not interpret the bootstrap as a prospective or biological confidence statement.

The gate is otherwise an appropriately demanding development screen: +0.05 ordinary P@50 corresponds to 2.5 additional recorded hits per 50 picks on average under the chosen source weighting. A pass would justify acquiring a separate evaluation set, not establish that utility prospectively.

## B3. Make model math and the strongest matched simple rivals reproducible

No B fitting implementation was supplied for this review. Several consequential choices are still left open by the prose and should be frozen in equations or a hashed implementation before any score:

- Name the KRR kernel and its normalization; `kernel ridge from TF-IDF` does not uniquely mean linear, cosine, polynomial or RBF regression.
- Define per-gene propensity with the observed-mask denominator, and state whether it uses screen weights or source-balanced weights. Separately keep the deliberately screen-weighted rarity definition explicit. Define source weights after primary-row exclusion, normalization to mean one, and whether directional variants receive ordinary row weights within their common source.
- Define the weighted SVD construction and the coefficients supplied to regression. If decomposing sqrt(W)R, regress the unweighted task coefficients R V (equivalently W^(-1/2)U Sigma), not U Sigma with source weights inadvertently encoded as the prediction target. Specify zero-support genes, final readout, and any score clipping consistently. Missing measurements must remain zero residual/mean imputation, not binary negatives.
- Define the 64-dimensional TF-IDF representation and ordinary residual modes for ExtraTrees as fit-only transformations, and how source weights enter its fitting. Rank, alpha and depth selection must use the inner fit-derived rare eligibility and source-macro validation metric; all propensities, modes, vocabularies, strata counts and support masks must then be recomputed on the four-fold refit partition.
- Clarify whether the three conditional-frequency rivals are separate strata or joint strata and which global propensity supplies the ten pseudo-observations. Include the source-balanced versions of these same simple conditional counts (or make those the prespecified matched versions), alongside any official-style screen-count variants. Likewise retain the original unweighted neighbor reproduction, but include a same-information source-balanced neighbor count variant if claiming benefit beyond neighbor frequency. Otherwise an apparent win over counts can partly reflect the deliberately improved source weighting rather than the low-rank conditional relation. These variants need no enlarged hyperparameter search.

Full-rank residual ridge and a nonlinear tree panel are important rivals and the bounded-panel qualification is appropriate. A matched unscaled low-rank linear residual ablation would further separate variance scaling from low-rank compression if either is later credited individually; it is not necessary merely to report that the complete candidate construction won this bounded panel. Failure of the 15-minute matrix to complete all required rivals is an implementation/resource failure, not a favorable reduced-panel result.

## Findings supporting the narrow test

The proposed practical output is clear: prioritize genes from a known screen library using prospective assay context. Training-only rarity plus at least three fit-publication measurements addresses ubiquitous-essentiality shortcuts without selecting targets on their held-out number of hits. Keeping zero-rare-hit target libraries at zero precision is essential and correctly specified. Missing measurements are explicitly not negatives. Grouped outer holdouts, a separate inner validation group, refitting transformations on the four non-test folds, deterministic ranks, and preservation of official validation/test and LaTest are the right boundaries.

Retaining dec/inc branches and excluding merged/recovered rows avoids counting a union endpoint as the same directional problem. Directional disjointness/union consistency is representation QC, not repeated experimental evidence. No source/author identifiers or raw outcome fields should enter predictors. Named reporter, drug target and engineered-background genes in phenotype/condition text are plausible pre-screen context; examples such as GFP-TAX1BP1 readouts and FIP200 knockout backgrounds do not by themselves prove leakage. The existing symbol flags are therefore not a reason to erase all gene names.

Nevertheless, these annotations were retrospectively curated with LLM assistance and the original benchmark includes outcome-dependent quality filtering. Fixed allowed-field whitelists and a metadata-only inspection for answer-like sentences are needed; neither this review nor a field-name whitelist proves every safe-field sentence was prospectively available. Candidate libraries are benchmark-processed assay masks, including score-validity filtering in the original methods. Treat them as shared evaluation information, not evidence that a future raw library will have exactly that availability.

## Interpretation ceiling

Even with B1-B3 resolved and every gate passed, the supported finding is **a known conditional residual model improves retrospective, source-grouped rare-hit prioritization over the specified baselines on historically exposed AssayBench training tasks**. The paper's temporal split, official external rows and LaTest have not been tested. Heterogeneous significance criteria make hits benchmark-specific, not a common magnitude of gene effect. Rare hit frequency does not imply selectivity, nonessentiality, drug specificity or causal dependency. Most cell-line strings occur in only one publication, so a text-model win need not establish transfer to unseen cellular backgrounds or contribution from background features specifically.

Bootstrap intervals over frozen predictions summarize conditional variability across observed source groups; folds share training data and do not supply five independent biological experiments. No outcome licenses algorithmic novelty, a new mechanism, a virtual-cell simulator, an Agent advantage, or autonomous decision/cost benefit. Numerical and leakage review of the eventual frozen implementation/predictions remains required before interpreting scores.
