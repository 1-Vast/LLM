# C1: dose-switching eligibility census (frozen before treatment outcomes)

Scope: first-layer feasibility only, not a model-benefit test. HTS001 original SHA256 e6ff575664705d6021d98e1dad41751c87d86a425a3794f7e2a7e3b12468d617; observed metadata show 7 backgrounds, 14 drugs, 10 doses each, two wells per drug-dose and 28 vehicle wells per background. One plate per background, no expt.id or certified independent preparations. HTS003 and HTS031 are exact duplicates, SHA256 2a5b5a9bed53175127cf042ae49015e590d914c703d62a9872edad5dc3f670c3, and contain HTS031/SCLC plate labels. Their outcomes are outside C1.

Endpoint: interpolate each well's observed count to 24h and 60h using only bracketing frames strictly before 72h. No interpolation beyond the observed range. If any needed count is missing, nonfinite or nonpositive, exclude that well with a reason. Endpoint is log2(N60/N24) minus the same-plate median vehicle log2 growth; more negative means stronger relative growth suppression. This is relative growth over a measured interval, not calibrated death rate, clinical efficacy or GR/IC50. Initial seeding and fluorescence calibration remain unverified.

Use only pairs of distinct drugs with at least two exactly shared numeric concentrations, sorted in Molar. Do not interpolate along dose for the census. Sort duplicate wells lexicographically; their first/second elements supply two deterministic technical pseudo-replicates. Drug-pair contrast at each dose is the difference of mean relative log-growth endpoints. A candidate dose flip needs contrast >= +0.25 log2 units at one measured dose and <= -0.25 at another. A repeat-concordant flip additionally needs both pseudo-replicates to show the same directions and >=0.25 magnitude at both witnessing doses. All four cross-well differences at each witness must agree in sign. Require both drugs on the same plate. This is a stringent descriptive technical-consistency rule, not a population significance test or biological replication certificate.

Report the full eligible denominator, flips and repeat-concordant flips per background, all witnessing dose pairs and contrasts, and fraction of repeat-concordant flips whose signs are reproduced in other backgrounds at the same doses. Do not optimize thresholds, time interval or dose range after seeing outcomes. Reuse of a drug across many pairs prevents treating pairs as independent samples; no pair-bootstrap p-values or biological CIs. Later prediction must split whole backgrounds/studies and compare to ordinary curve models; this census alone cannot justify Virtual Cell Capability or an Agent.

Compute each well endpoint once, preserve well-level endpoints and pair summaries. One execution; rerun only for documented implementation corrections without changing scientific criteria. Run in D:/MAESTRO/.venv/Scripts/python.exe. No network in this script, no installations, no core edits. Independently review the protocol and check a sample of numerical endpoints and flip counts. If stable flips lack qualified independent support, record descriptive feasibility and the missing validation rather than extrapolate.
