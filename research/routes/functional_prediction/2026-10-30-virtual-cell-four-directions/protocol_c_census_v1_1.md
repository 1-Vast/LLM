# C1 v1.1: exclusion and reproduction clarification

Read with the original frozen protocol_c_census.md. This amendment precedes treatment-outcome access and addresses review/c1_initial.md. Endpoint, doses, 0.25 margin and technical-concordance criteria are unchanged. No count-based scientific revision occurred.

Metadata reveal that MGH has no observed frame between 58.8h and 74.1h, so the 60h endpoint cannot be bracketed strictly before 72h. Its 308 well trajectories are expected to be excluded rather than extrapolated. Report all seven input backgrounds, all exclusion reasons, input/eligible well totals and exact-dose-pair denominators. Expected maximum eligible pairs is 6 x 56 = 336 before any further count-quality exclusion; original metadata contain 7 x 56 = 392 input pairs.

If a two-well condition loses either well, exclude that condition from pair analysis; retain its single surviving endpoint for the exclusion audit and record incomplete-condition identities. Conditions losing both wells are represented by well-level exclusions and the reduced eligible denominator. A cell with no valid wells remains in the background table with zero eligible pairs. Exclusions are protocol-driven and cannot select a favorable response.

Save every positive-negative dose combination witnessing a mean flip, with a flag for technical concordance. For cross-background reproduction the primary unit is one technically concordant cell x unordered drug pair. Choose its witness deterministically: the lowest numeric positive-contrast dose and the lowest numeric negative-contrast dose among technically concordant witnesses. Other backgrounds are available only when both drugs retain complete two-well measurements at both exact doses. Sign reproduction means mean contrasts >0 at the positive witness and <0 at the negative witness; separately report stricter reproduction of the +/-0.25 margins. Exact zeros do not reproduce.

Primary descriptive fraction: mean over source cell x pair units of each unit's sign-reproduced/available-other-background fraction, excluding units with no available other background and reporting that denominator. Also report pooled reproduced/available opportunities. No uncertainty interval treats reused pairs or dose witnesses as independent samples. These fractions describe observed portability across the six available assay backgrounds; they are not predictions in held-out backgrounds or independent experiments.
