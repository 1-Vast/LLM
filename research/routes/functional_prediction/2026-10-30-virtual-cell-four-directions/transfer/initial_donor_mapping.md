## Biological relation and initial decision

The observation unit is a perturbation–cell background–assay/protocol–dose–preparation outcome. The intended use is selecting interventions for an unmeasured context. A repeated gene/drug identifier or study-specific association is insufficient evidence that a relation transports. The unresolved decision is **which conditional relation has an identifiable intervention meaning and an independently falsifiable transport test, with useful intervention choices beyond a global importance score or ordinary direct regression?** No viable transfer is an allowed result.

Starting decision: do not commit to a cross-field architecture. Compare the assumptions of the following principle hypotheses first. A changed future decision will be recorded separately from an executed experiment; there will be no fitting, dataset/outcome access, installs or external model calls in this scout.
