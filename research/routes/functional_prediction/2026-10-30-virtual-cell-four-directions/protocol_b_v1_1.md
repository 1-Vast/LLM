Model equations: p_g=sum_s H_sg/sum_s M_sg on the fit partition, zero for no support. Source row weight w_s is inverse number of included fit rows in its canonical group, normalized to mean1 after endpoint-row exclusions; directional rows share their source weight with all other source rows. R_sg=M_sg(H_sg-p_g); sigma_g=sqrt(p_g(1-p_g)+.01). TF-IDF rows are L2 normalized with the frozen tokenizer/settings. K=A A^T is the linear TF-IDF kernel. Weighted ridge prediction is K_qA W^(1/2)(W^(1/2) K W^(1/2)+alpha I)^(-1)W^(1/2)R. Candidate right singular vectors V come from truncated SVD of W^(1/2)R/sigma; regress the unweighted coefficients (R/sigma)V, then read out p + predicted_coefficients V^T sigma. No clipping is applied to ranking scores; no probability calibration claim. Zero residual imputes an unmeasured gene to p, not to a negative label. The tree fits source-weighted unweighted coefficients R V_ordinary, where V_ordinary comes from SVD of W^(1/2)R; its 64 task-text features use a separate fit-only TF-IDF TruncatedSVD. All transforms, masks and propensities are recomputed on the final four-fold refit.

Add source-balanced counterparts for all three conditional-frequency rivals and for the same-information neighbor. Weighted counts use w_s in both observed-hit and measured denominators; weighted conditional-frequency pseudo-count10 uses the source-balanced global propensity. These are separate strata (type OR coarse phenotype OR rationale), not a joint cross product. Unweighted versions retain ordinary screen counts and screen-weighted global propensity. The balanced neighbor uses the same k grid[5,10,30] and validation rule; no new k, rank, alpha or depth values are added. Official-style neighbor is a reproduction of the statistical idea with deterministic lexical ties and min_df2; it is not a byte-identical official prediction run.

Exact P@50=number of true binary hits among 50 picks divided by50. All and rare analyses use the same task set: require at least50 fit-defined rare eligible measured genes. All-library predictions then rank the full measured library on that same task set. Do not select tasks on held-out hit counts; zero rare hits remain zero precision. Every actual method must have identical task/source denominators. Random expectation is the eligible library's hit prevalence and is reported as an expectation, not a fitted model.

Primary aggregate: average tasks within canonical group and then all eligible canonical groups equally, not equal weighting of five fold means. Baseline panel excludes candidate, permuted-context diagnostic and random expectation. Choose the baseline with the largest overall source-macro rare P@50; deterministic name order breaks ties. Each outer fold is compared to this globally selected fixed rival for the >=3/5 sign rule. Compute 2,000 paired resamples of canonical source groups with replacement, seed1030, 2.5th/97.5th percentiles. Rival is held fixed in these conditional intervals; model/rival selection and shared training make this exploratory, not confirmatory biological inference.

Binary labels and assay-context text are retrospectively benchmark-curated and masks include valid-score filtering. They support a retrospective ranking problem, not prospective field availability, a common intervention effect scale, causal selectivity, unknown-gene/cell generalization or a mechanistic simulator.
