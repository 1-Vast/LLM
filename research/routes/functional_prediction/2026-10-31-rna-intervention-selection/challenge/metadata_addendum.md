# Addendum 1: new raw metadata and header evidence

This addendum follows preservation of initial_review.md; it does not rewrite that assessment. Read only newly supplied root/archive_headers.json and root/sources/selected_input_bams.json plus the already inspected official splicing inventory and selected-archive mapping. No event-level values, protocol draft, fitted result or other review was read. Network use remains zero.

The new evidence materially improves the independent-unit gate. Independently joining each selected archive's four derived_from IDs to the official BAM inventory yields exactly two KD BAMs attached to its selected KD experiment, and two control BAMs attached to its selected control experiment. Within each group, the BAMs have separate advertised biological replicate numbers 1 and 2 and separate origin_batches biosample accessions. All 24 BAMs and their 24 biosample origins are distinct in this selected panel. The explicit check is saved as sample_crosswalk_check.json.

This is adequate primary metadata to support the limited statement that the source archives derive from **two separately registered biological input samples per group**, provided root verifies the meaning of the origin_batches field and the archive vectors actually contain one entry per upstream biological BAM. It is substantially stronger than merely observing two comma-separated slots. There is no need to recover whether slot0 is biological replicate1 to conduct a deliberately arbitrary-slot primary analysis with frozen reversal, if the two entries are established to correspond to the two different inputs. Label the slots by position, never as named biological replicate identities. The group provenance does not establish independent statistical errors or absence of shared culture/batch history. Additional ancestry/library checking is required only if metadata conflicts or suggests duplicated RNA, not as an endless demand for proof of independence.

The headers show exactly one skipped-exon JunctionCountOnly table in each archive, with both group's count/PSI columns and full SE coordinates/form lengths. They include duplicate ID column names, so the parser must not rely on unique ID headers or use those IDs as cross-experiment identities. Member/header evidence cannot establish count-vector length, missingness, per-slot mapping, or the treatment-independent event universe. Those remain predeclared validation gates in the execution/parser contract. Absence of b1/b2 config and GTF files makes the source-slot mapping and annotation proof unavailable inside these archives themselves.

The independent-unit concern is therefore **substantially closed at the advertised metadata level**, with a finite vector/schema verification condition and statistical-dependence caveat. The overall initial decision is otherwise unchanged: do not inspect treatment values or fit until the epsilon/null behavior, bounded comparison, eligibility/ascertainment, folds, direction firewall, metrics and execution protocol are frozen and reviewed. A documented no-run outcome is preferable to silently weakening the reference/control-only eligibility contract.
