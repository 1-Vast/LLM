# Repair 1: failure diagnosis, not a new efficacy claim

This package preserves all three predecessor study packages. Their protocols,
stored evaluations and negative decisions are not overwritten. Repair 1 was
designed after reading the innovation-validation results; every new experiment
here is a synthetic engineering diagnostic, never a fresh confirmatory study.

## Fixed scope

1. Reproduce and correct inconsistent knowledge-gradient estimation, action
   screening, lossy executable identity, repeated fitting at deployment, and
   archive count inflation. Archive reuse and branching diagnostic programs are
   explicitly NOT_IMPLEMENTED; implementing those is a separate research change.
2. Audit stored legacy outputs without fitting, API calls or sealed outcomes.
   Known checkpoint discrepancies must fail strict verification, even when the
   legacy verifier waived them. Missing anchor models remain NOT_REPLAYABLE.
3. Run an analytical probe-value regression and bounded development diagnostics
   on the existing four world families. No replacement generator is selected to
   defeat the latent comparator. The flawed legacy decoupled control is not used.

## Development diagnostic fixed before execution

- Seeds: 93021 and 93022; families: null, lowrank, alias, compose.
- Existing world generator; 12 drugs (66 pair units), 8 initial history cells,
  4 batches of 4 tasks. All methods receive the same full batches after use.
- Arms: inherited gate model + corrected KG; restarted gate model + corrected KG;
  rank-3 latent + A-optimal; rank-3 latent + corrected KG. The latter isolates the
  value of the acquisition repair from the value of adding inherited factors.
- K=5, query budget=4, contracts E and T. T's legacy within-class partition is
  used only as a transfer task, not a causal witness-control identification.
- Both library arms have the SAME ceiling of 12 candidate evaluations each
  generation. This is candidate-cap matching, NOT equal actual CPU or equal
  discovery effort. Report actual counts, process CPU, elapsed time and common
  fitting costs separately; do not claim efficiency dominance from the cap.
- Promotion rule stays gain>=0.004, wins>losses, wins>=2. These are internal
  selection heuristics, not biological validation or multiplicity-adjusted proof.
- One old and one new anchor per checkpoint are logged only to check deployment
  consistency. Anchors never alter fitting, search or policy.
- Every task/anchor row records the fitted-artifact SHA256, public base prediction,
  legal pool, observed query/value sequence and final nominations. Every fitted
  artifact is stored; replay performs no fitting and reads no unobserved outcome.
- Two sequential seeds per family, one numerical thread. No API, wet lab or real
  evaluation data. Maximum 30 minutes elapsed; fail/partial on interruption,
  never selectively discard a failed stream or change seeds/arms after results.
- No CI, superiority decision, new threshold, biological or open-ended-evolution
  success claim is generated from eight small engineering streams. Report paired
  means and counts as DEVELOPMENT_ONLY. Stop at the planned diagnostic.

## Interpretation boundaries

Legacy H1/H2/H3 numbers and registered decisions describe the old implementation.
They cannot establish how the repaired method performs. Legacy H2 did not test
equal resources; the decoupled manipulation did not isolate a witness dependency;
H3 was a conditional gain rather than a factorial interaction. Real utility is
still NOT_TESTED. Do not obtain a 350-pair/12-background dataset on that report's
unsupported sample-size argument.

Next scientific evaluation, if justified, must independently freeze the task,
actual practical margin, strong baselines, resource estimand and untouched data.
Repair success is mathematical/software correctness, not innovation success.
