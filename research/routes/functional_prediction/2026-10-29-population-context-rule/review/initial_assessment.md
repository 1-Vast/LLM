# Independent initial assessment

Date: 2026-10-30. This assessment was preserved before reading any other episode interpretations, developer/evidence/scout assessments, usage/coordination records, or preferred decisions. It is an assessment of the frozen question and original article passages, not a scientific experiment or an empirical verdict on the proposed relation.

## Assessment

The proposed restriction is mathematically coherent, falsifiable, and potentially useful: in a prespecified drug family and one fixed cell population, old-support functional response would be a sufficient coordinate for new-support response. Its empirical content is the equality of new-support responses for *different drugs/doses independently matched on old-support response*. It is more than fitting one drug, and it is not established by the supplied papers. Ordinary inference can implement this relation; that fact does not disqualify it. Conversely, the formula and the addition of an Agent do not establish novelty, biological sufficiency, or a useful acquisition advantage.

My present disposition is **retain as an unvalidated conditional relation, with novelty unresolved and a presently unqualified execution-ready scientific test**. A meaningful test can be specified in principle. The materials read do not establish that the necessary independent multi-drug/support crossing, family assignment, timing and usable endpoint calibration are currently available. This is a qualification gap, not evidence that the relation is empirically false or impossible to test.

## Mathematical scope

Write the common old response as u. If F is injective on the relevant old-support latent range, then

`F(q_d(c_d)-a_0) = F(q_r(c_r)-a_0) = u`

implies `q_d(c_d)=q_r(c_r)`. Drug-invariant support action then gives equal new responses. On the invertible old range,

`T_1<-0(u) = F(F^{-1}(u) + a_0 - a_1)`.

The implication only requires old-range injectivity; a new-support plateau can preserve equality while making a reverse inference impossible. Distinct drug dose–injury functions, slopes and potencies are compatible with the result. An identical IC50 is neither necessary nor a substitute for response matching at the actually tested doses. Old plateau equality does not identify injury, and a finite noisy match is not exact equality. Calibration and matching uncertainty must propagate to the transported prediction. Without regularity or an estimated local response sensitivity, a small old-response discrepancy need not imply a small new-response discrepancy.

If F increases with injury, the resulting common map is monotone on its valid domain. A reference measured on both supports can estimate this map only over the reference's observed old-response range that overlaps the targets. Extrapolation beyond that overlap is an additional assumption. Several matched effect levels are more informative than one: they examine a response relation rather than a single coincidence. A good fitted reference curve or algebraic consistency of invertible maps by themselves do not test cross-drug transfer.

The observable common-map claim is weaker than identifying the biological decomposition. Numerical q, a and F are not uniquely identified merely by transported response curves, and a successful common map does not prove that antioxidant margin is the causal coordinate. Likewise, equal endpoint responses do not generally imply equal latent injury without the shared-F assumption. The scalar additive model supplies a sufficient mechanism for the proposed prediction; it cannot be inferred just by naming an endpoint “injury.”

## Biological restrictions and source ownership

The model requires the chosen family to share a sufficient injury coordinate, support changes to leave each drug's dose-to-injury function invariant, and support protection to act independently of drug identity. The population, observation interval, exposure schedule and endpoint meaning must also be comparable. Support-induced uptake, state-distribution changes, cycling, drug-specific defense induction or assay metabolism can defeat that sufficiency. These are material restrictions already raised in the packet, not observed failures of the candidate family. Excluding failures after seeing target/support responses would make the claim difficult to falsify; family and reference selection therefore need independent justification.

**P1, PNAS2025, DOI 10.1073/pnas.2423894122.** S2/S5/S6/S7/S9/S12 establish the article's own tumor–lung-fibroblast model, drug-response modulation, high-content screening, autophagy vulnerability and contact/signaling experiments. In S7, physical Transwell separation removes the reported suppression of MB231 proliferation, increased autophagic flux and MCB-613 resistance; integrin and JAK-family interventions partly reverse resistance, while IL-6 neutralization/addition does not alter the reported MB231 phenotypes. These observations concern contact-dependent, multicomponent biological interaction, not a scalar extracellular antioxidant shift.

P1 also bears directly on practical need and overlap. S2 selects concentrations of five therapies using their 2D monoculture IC50s and evaluates other culture conditions. S5 screens 876 kinase inhibitors at a common concentration in monoculture/coculture and finds both resistance and distinct vulnerability, with selected differential responses independently checked. This is substantial prior ownership of context-aware screening and functional drug prioritization. It is closer to the candidate than a generic statement that “stroma matters.” Nevertheless, reported IC50-guided doses and broad differential screening do not establish that achieved old effects were matched within a specified tolerance, that a suitable family was assigned independently, or that a common cross-drug transport map was the tested relation. The opposing effects across drug targets caution against an unrestricted all-drug claim; they do not refute a genuinely prespecified narrower load-generating family. P1's autophagy/contact mechanism must not be relabeled as the proposed P2-inspired antioxidant mechanism.

**P2, Nat Commun2026, DOI 10.1038/s41467-026-75772-5.** Sec6/Fig3 report gemcitabine sensitivity on control/GIS-conditioned fibroblast-deposited ECM and on defined collagen-I-coated PDMS supports. The reported PDMS moduli are approximately 62, 164 and 486 kPa; Sec27 specifies base:curing-agent ratios 40:1, 30:1 and 25:1, curing and collagen coating. These are controlled support interventions with gemcitabine dose curves. GIS-conditioned ECM is a separate intervention, not an interchangeable stiffness-only perturbation: the article also reports changed ECM deposition/architecture.

Sec6–9/Fig3/4/6 provide biological plausibility for support-dependent antioxidant protection in selected backgrounds: stiffness-associated Piezo1 signaling, NRF2 activation and BRG1-dependent SLC7A11/redox regulation accompany gemcitabine resistance. The article reports that NRF2 loss changes ROS and gemcitabine sensitivity in MIA PaCa-2 but not comparably in PANC-1, with BRG1 perturbation/reconstitution further conditioning dependence. This argues for fixing and qualifying a background; it does not validate a universal background-invariant margin. These are the authors' published mechanism findings, not new findings of this review.

The same source gives reasons to avoid inferring the candidate from mechanistic plausibility alone: stiffness changes mitochondrial activity, ATP, membrane potential and ER–mitochondrial contacts as well as antioxidant expression. Thus q and the endpoint-generating process need not remain unchanged solely because an antioxidant pathway is involved. These findings leave a one-coordinate approximation possible; they do not demonstrate it.

Sec29 identifies CCK-8/450-nm absorbance as the sensitivity readout for gemcitabine or Erastin. CCK-8 response should be treated as that assay endpoint unless its relationship to injury/cell survival under these metabolically altered supports is qualified. The separately described colony assay involves GR lines, BRG1 suppression, Ferr-1 and prolonged gemcitabine exposure. It does not supply a colony-endpoint replicate of the entire PDMS/support/drug/genotype design. Sec10/11 describe Erastin sensitivity and ferroptosis-pathway interventions, but do not establish Erastin as a support-invariant load reference matched to gemcitabine across supports. Calling both agents redox-relevant would not establish that requirement.

Original Fig3/4/6 captions state three independent experiments for the relevant dose-response panels; Sec44 more generally says all experiments were repeated at least three times. The general statement does not add unreported well-level design, matching tolerances or exposure timing. The selected methods do not fully specify drug-sensitivity timing, support adaptation timing, plate layout or the complete drug-by-support crossing. Those details are unknown here, not proven absent from the full article/SI or the authors' experiments. No raw effects or source-data workbooks were read.

## Exact prior overlap and practical consequence

The broad need is credible and already demonstrated by P1: a hit identified in a simpler culture can change usefulness in a stromal context. The proposed narrow value would be predicting a target/support entry from its old response plus paid reference/support calibration, then making a prespecified threshold decision with uncertainty. It does not require a new class of inference algorithm. An Agent could operationally select calibration/target measurements and revise or stop using the relation when it fails. No superiority over a fixed ordinary policy follows from that implementation.

At the level of two-support observable predictions, the proposal is a shared monotone response calibration. If an ordinary shared-potency/monotone-calibration rival uses the same observations and imposes the same common-map restriction, it can make the same predictions. A claimed advantage over that rival requires a genuinely different, justified constraint, independent family qualification or demonstrated better decisions per total measurement cost. This equivalence does not erase possible scientific value in discovering that a particular independently defined biological family obeys the restriction.

Exact novelty remains unresolved. The original P1 passages already contain multi-drug functional dose selection and context screening. P2 Sec14 additionally *attributes* to LeSavage et al. resistance to several chemotherapies in patient-derived PDAC organoids in high-stiffness matrices. That is a secondary attribution within the P2 original, not an independent reading of the Nat Mater2024 original. Its original was not acquired; neither its precise dose matching, transport models nor the absence of them can be inferred. No exact-prior absence claim is justified, and no novelty claim should presently be promoted.

## What would change the material objections

1. **Qualified cross-drug test:** verified design/materials showing at least an independently assigned reference and distinct eligible target, matched old-response levels in a nonsaturated overlapping range, both controlled supports, comparable endpoint/exposure timing, independent repeats and controls, and a prespecified predictive tolerance. Target/new-support responses must be reserved before calibration and family decisions. This would qualify a test, not guarantee success. A single-drug/genotype crossing is insufficient.
2. **Assay/biological sufficiency:** endpoint qualification under the relevant support-induced metabolic changes, plus evidence that the assigned family and support intervention plausibly preserve the drug injury coordinate. Successful held-out transport could support an empirical common map even if the precise antioxidant decomposition remains unresolved. Mechanistic ownership would require stronger evidence than response matching alone.
3. **Prior overlap:** the missing Nat Mater original and a bounded comparison with existing functional-equivalence/context-calibration methods could either expose direct prior coverage or leave a defensible narrower contribution. Its unread title cannot decide this.
4. **Practical/Agent value:** matched-budget prospective decision evidence against direct measurement and ordinary shared-map/flexible response rivals, accounting for calibration, controls, repeats and failed measurements. Predefine the hit threshold and how uncertain transported predictions affect advancement. Better curve fit alone would not establish decision value.
5. **Empirical outcome:** consistent transported predictions with sufficiently narrow uncertainty on held-out family members would support the restricted relation. Reproducible departures beyond the prespecified tolerance in independently eligible members would challenge it. Wide intervals, inadequate response overlap or underqualified membership would leave it inconclusive rather than positive or negative.

These are qualification/evidence requirements for the existing proposal, not authorization to retrieve data, run tests or introduce a new hypothesis. No such action was taken.
