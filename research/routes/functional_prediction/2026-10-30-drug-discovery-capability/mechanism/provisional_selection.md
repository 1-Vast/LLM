## D4b: absolute functional burden versus pooled relative response

**One-sentence claim to investigate.** Under a documented compositional measurement regime, accurate prediction of a published response statistic can coexist with an unidentified absolute treated/control burden decision; a paired population-size factor can resolve that ambiguity when recovery is exchangeable. This is an untested application of a known identification principle, not a discovered biological law or an established flaw in Rhaister.

**Practical setting.** A screening analyst chooses which compound should advance because it lowers a target cell population below a fixed treated/control burden threshold at a specified harvest time. Relative enrichment of a cell line inside a treated pool is a different quantity from its absolute expansion or depletion relative to vehicle. Both quantities can be scientifically useful. Their interchangeability is the question.

**Virtual cell and intervention.** State is the vector of viable endpoint cell burdens `N_l` across the populations actually represented in a pool, with matched starting material and time. Intervention is a drug×dose×duration. The outcome is `g_l,d = N_l,d / N_l,0`, where the control is the matched endpoint vehicle culture. This measures endpoint burden relative to vehicle; it is not a death probability, durable clearance, per-founder reproductive success or clinical efficacy. A model that only predicts compositional response has not automatically predicted this outcome.

**Conditional identification mechanism.** Suppose the observed statistic is based on within-sample proportions `p_l,d = N_l,d / N_total,d`, with exchangeable recovery within the pool. Define `r_l,d = p_l,d / p_l,0` and `G_d = N_total,d / N_total,0`. Then `g_l,d = G_d * r_l,d`. The same observed proportions permit multiple absolute responses obtained by changing the unknown positive scale `G_d`. A fixed absolute threshold and cross-drug rankings for one population may therefore be unidentified, even when within-drug relative rankings are exact.

The inspected source equation is precisely `s_l,d = log2(r_l,d)`. A useful identifiable quantity is therefore `s_i,d - s_j,d = log2(g_i,d/g_j,d)`: the differential response of two lines under the same drug. This can support a relative preference among the cancer-cell contexts actually assayed. It does not establish normal-cell sparing, absolute tumor suppression or a therapeutic window. A time-zero **composition** adds another relative snapshot; by itself it does not supply the missing total-burden scale at either time. The public table's field name `growth_rate` should not be silently interpreted as a physical net-growth rate or a standard GR value.

A purely analytical example: for one target population, drug A has relative fold .4 and total-burden fold .9, giving absolute fold .36; drug B has relative fold .7 and total-burden fold .2, giving absolute fold .14. Relative fold prefers A, absolute burden prefers B. This example is not a simulation, source observation or empirical result. For a target comprising 10% of the control pool, the other populations can have relative folds 1.0667 under A and 1.0333 under B, satisfying the composition constraint; all resulting absolute folds remain below 1. The point does not depend on a drug increasing total growth.

The scale can already be known if the study has appropriate total viable counts, calibrated spike-ins, fixed-volume sampling with validated recovery, or another documented absolute normalization. If so, this particular ambiguity is closed. Under exchangeable recovery, one matched total-burden factor per condition suffices. Under line-specific but treatment-stable recovery, differential responses still cancel those line-specific factors; an absolute response for one reference line **in the same pooled condition** can instead anchor the other lines by `g_i,d = g_reference,d * 2^(s_i,d-s_reference,d)`. A parallel monoculture is not automatically a valid anchor because pooling can change the biology. If recovery is cell-type/drug dependent, `observed count_l,d = sample_factor_d * recovery_l,d * N_l,d`; one total count or one reference line is not generally sufficient. Drug-sensitive dissociation, genotype assignment, viability selection and quality-control filters must be part of the observation contract.

If defensible total-burden bounds `[L_d,U_d]` exist, then `g_l,d` lies in `[L_d*2^s_l,d, U_d*2^s_l,d]` under the stated observation model. A threshold decision is already determined only when that entire interval lies on one side of its threshold. This is ordinary partial identification, not a new algorithm. Without informative bounds, arbitrary positive scales leave every nontrivial absolute threshold unresolved; inventing narrow bounds to create a few attractive queries would manufacture an Agent advantage.

**Agent observation → revision/action.** Start with a model predicting the available relative statistic. For a compound whose advancement decision changes across the admissible total-burden factors, the Agent would request or retrieve an appropriate matched total-burden observation, update the absolute response, and change advance/reject/abstain. If new evidence shows differential recovery, it must revise the observation model or require an orthogonal population-resolved count. This is a possible operational role, not demonstrated Agent benefit; no LLM is needed for the algebra.

**Strongest equal-information rivals.** A deterministic compositional correction using the same calibration; a fixed protocol that records total viable counts for every pooled condition; ordinary hierarchical prediction of the corrected response; direct population-resolved count or individual-line screening. All receive identical controls, metadata, historical labels and decision-time feedback. Compare the full costs of pool preparation, sequencing or barcoding, viable counts, technical failures, historical calibration, biological time and repeats. Reusing a free public summary table makes this retrospective analysis cheap; it does not make a new sequencing-based deployment cheaper than a direct functional screen. An adaptive policy must outperform an ordinary missing-calibration/threshold rule or a fixed calibration design before claiming Agent savings.

**Falsifiable prediction and alternative outcomes.** If the actual measurement is compositional, total-burden factors vary materially between drugs, and target decisions lie near relevant thresholds, adding qualified absolute calibration should change some predeclared cross-drug advancement decisions. The hypothesis is uninformative when absolute calibration is already present; practical benefit is unsupported if corrected decisions rarely change or fixed cheap counting performs equally well. A calibration-induced change alone does not show that the corrected biological value is true: an independent absolute endpoint is required. Failure of an adaptive policy to beat a fixed protocol rejects that benefit claim, not the algebra.

## D1: rescue equivalence across distinct resistance causes

**One-sentence biological hypothesis.** Different initiating resistance changes that converge on the same causally verified bypass may share a rescue intervention that restores the original drug-response relation, even when their untreated molecular states differ. This proposes an empirical equivalence class of reversibility, not merely a predictive cluster of similar endpoint profiles.

**Virtual cell, intervention and decision.** State represents the original therapeutic dependency and a specified alternative survival route in a defined parental/resistant background. Interventions are the original drug at a dose series and a mechanistically qualified perturbation of the proposed bypass. Outcome is a specified population-resolved functional burden under the combined intervention, with baseline and pretreatment effects retained. A screening researcher decides whether a rescue discovered in one resistance cause should be advanced in a second cause before running a full rescue library there.

**Operative mechanism and enabling case.** In the proposed restricted family, resistance changes only the availability of a bypass; a qualified rescue places that bypass in the same functional state as the parent without changing drug exposure or the original target route. Under those assumptions, clamping the bypass should recover the parental response to the original drug across a non-floor dose range, after using the predeclared observation scale and matched baseline conditions. This is a conditional mechanistic implication, not evidence that any real backgrounds satisfy it. A background with an additional target mutation, altered uptake or independent downstream survival route is a boundary case: the same rescue need not restore response. A correlated RNA signature alone does not place a background in this family.

**Agent observation → revision/action.** An initial response disagreement motivates distinct hypotheses such as bypass activation, original-target loss or altered exposure. The Agent selects a perturbation that changes their predictions, observes its joint functional response with the original drug, revises family membership or mechanism, and chooses which rescue action merits follow-up in another background. It must not declare a bypass causal solely because a probe predicts the outcome. A failed rescue should revise the common-mechanism claim rather than be absorbed by unlimited background-specific parameters.

**Prediction.** Before inspecting target rescue outcomes, nominate different initiating resistance causes using independent mechanistic evidence. A single predeclared rescue should restore a defined part of the parental drug-response relation in a held-out cause, while a mechanistically distinct negative-control perturbation should not. Test the response relation across the non-floor range and report partial restoration rather than only one favorable combination score. The family is contradicted for that held-out background if restoration fails despite verified comparable bypass perturbation and compatible exposure/readout conditions. Failure to verify the perturbation leaves the result inconclusive.

**Strongest simpler rivals.** The known genotype/pathway rule with the same evidence; a directly measured rescue panel; Rhaister-style phenotype interpolation; ordinary hierarchical drug×resistance×rescue interaction models; and standard chemical-genetic epistasis/causal triangulation. Generic additive toxicity, growth slowing and baseline changes are rival explanations, so parental/resistant×rescue/control×original-drug/control conditions and appropriate dose ranges are required. The biological restriction could still be useful if an ordinary model implements it. An Agent advantage requires a separate prospective comparison against a fixed mechanistic panel and ordinary sequential design with equal initialization, information, timing and total costs.

**Data/independence contract.** Matched parental and resistant preparations, different independently generated initiating resistance causes, actual joint rescue×drug outcomes, sufficient controls to assess dynamic range and off-target/baseline effects, comparable exposure, and a justified causal assay of the bypass are necessary. Different doses of one compound, many cells from one culture, or two database names sharing experiments do not provide independent causes or validation. Leave the claimed causal family or initiating lesion out of development, not merely observations within familiar backgrounds. A second inhibitor chemotype or genetic perturbation can strengthen triangulation only when its own action and measurement conditions are qualified.

**Material prior/data gap.** Collateral sensitivity, synthetic lethality, chemical-genetic epistasis and bypass resistance reversal are extensive established literatures. The exact restriction above has not been searched to novelty completion. The current source packet lacks its complete matched intervention family. The 2026-10-28 rescue analysis already warns that marker maintenance is not state clamping and that a simple four-cell difference does not identify mediation. Current Rhaister probe prediction is a direct predictive rival, but does not by itself establish or refute the causal restriction. This branch is therefore a source-qualified research question to pursue only if a concrete matched resource changes feasibility, not a ready test or recommendation to build a new model.
