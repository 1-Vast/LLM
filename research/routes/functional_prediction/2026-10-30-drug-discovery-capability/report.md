The unresolved CTG endpoint does not automatically have that survival interpretation.

Second, the ordinary MuSyC equilibrium model with both directional potency modifiers and both cooperativity modifiers equal to one has product-state occupancy. On nondegenerate invertible axes it yields

`H(u,v)=u+v-E0+lambda*(u-E0)*(v-E0)`,

`lambda=(E3-E1-E2+E0)/((E1-E0)*(E2-E0))`.

Common state-effect values E0–E3 are sufficient for identical H despite changes in single-agent Hill potency/slopes. Shared E0 and lambda already suffice within this subfamily; common E0–E3 is not necessary. Arbitrary shared E3 permits efficacy interaction, so the overlap extends beyond the Bliss null.
