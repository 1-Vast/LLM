## A. Three-generation EGFR resistance: good compact mechanism precedent, incomplete public functional data

**Unmasking the common enemy: drug resistance mechanisms across three different EGFR inhibitor generations are associated with co-targetable alterations in extracellular matrix signaling.** DOI10.1186/s12964-026-02927-8; PMC13312578; PMID42087187;2026-05-06. XML acquired `request02-egfr-primary.body`,194464B, SHA256 fbce047da74f90aeac347bceefe4e9077e87ea5d34b1650f6767c4b3af9f9942.

- **Independent derivations/backgrounds:** Par11–13 (text lines30–32): HCC827 parental has EGFR exon19deletion; H1975 parental has T790M+L858R. “Gefitinib-resistant C827 (C827/GEF), dacomitinib-resistant H1975 (H1975/DAC), and osimertinib-resistant H1975 (H1975/OSI) cell lines were established ... using a standard stepwise selection method.” Ten-month escalating selection with72h exposure/recovery cycles; drugs withdrawn at least5days before assays. These are three separately selected sublines from **two** parental backgrounds. No multiple independent evolution replicates per selecting drug were verified. Distinct selected backgrounds are NOT the same as independently engineered single causes; generation and background are confounded.
- **Original drug × rescue controls:** Par21–22 (lines43–44) explicitly select Ko1431µM (ABCG2), venetoclax1µM (BCL2), sonidegib5µM (Hedgehog), RO4929097 10µM (Notch), defactinib2.5µM (FAK), verteporfin0.05µM (YAP1). Quote Par22: “EGFR-TKIs or the modulators alone, or their combinations.” ABCG2/BCL2 modulator preincubation2h then48h combination; Hedgehog120h; Notch24h preincubation then72h; FAK/YAP1 48h. Selecting-drug plus modulator crosses are real, not merely collateral single-agent sensitivity. Dose-response serial dilutions specified, exact full TKI grids only indicated in figures, not transcribed here.
- **Functional endpoint and basic controls:** Par18–20 (lines39–41): MTT with96well densities dependent on parent/resistant line and assay; vehicle/40%DMSO define100%/0% viability controls. This is a metabolic viability proxy, not direct cell count. Par34 (line63): “All experiments were independently performed at least three times.” This is assay repetition, not three independent resistance derivations. Well/batch identifiers and raw replicate pairing unverified.
- **Genetic target reference:** Par24 (line47): FN1 siRNA25nM24h, scrambled negative siRNA at same concentration; then dacomitinib/osimertinib at varying concentrations48h, in parental H1975 and H1975/DAC or H1975/OSI, MTT endpoint; knockdown Western validated. This is an actual target-intervention/control comparator. Only one siRNA sequence verified; not a large genetic-cause library or rescue add-back.
- **Claim ownership:** Abstract/Par72–79 already own shared ECM-associated rescue and background-dependent failures of otherwise expression-upregulated targets. The study itself explicitly notes two-background/generation confounding. Inferring a resistance cause solely from increased expression is already challenged by this primary. Generic “infer mechanism, choose matching inhibitor, resensitize original TKI” is an established prior, not new here.

No direct functional response workbook link was verified. Supplemental media are DOCX figures/antibody table and original Western blots, not an advertised raw response matrix. Zenodo was not queried. Public expression/proteomics does not substitute for publicly available matched rescue outcomes.

**Assessment:** actual small resistance-reversal panel suitable for source-grounded biological feasibility and strong ordinary rivals. Not admitted as ready cheap transferable-cause dataset: response-level downloads, full grids, independent-cause labels and replicate schema remain unresolved; author-request availability fails an automatic public-data assumption.

## B. Daraxonrasib resistance: distinct causes and source-data links, incomplete common rescue cross

**Acquired resistance to the RAS(ON) multi-selective inhibitor daraxonrasib guides rational combination therapy strategies in pancreatic cancer.** DOI10.1038/s41591-026-04537-w; PMC13472880.1; PMID42581230;2026-08-11. XML acquired `request03-ras-primary.body`,232416B, SHA256 00c61dc9d8e1ff2a7426e7592e2759e64f84d32ec9c998620853bcca3654ef79.

- **Distinct cause development exists:** Par17 (text line32): “(1) targeted genetic engineering introducing candidate genomic resistance drivers into treatment-naive models, and (2) isolation of resistant clones ... under sustained in vivo daraxonrasib (or a relevant RAS(ON) inhibitor) treatment pressure” then characterization/passaging. Key modeled mechanisms include mutant KRAS amplification, RTK alterations and MYC amplification. Par68–70 (lines116–118) verify doxycycline-inducible KRASG12R in HuP-T3 and lentiviral KrasG12D/AP009 clones. **Multiple expression doses or AP009 clones are not multiple molecular resistance causes.**
- **Original-drug identity matters:** Par49/56 (lines88/98) specify that the HPAC resistant line came from **RMC-9945**, a G12D-selective tool inhibitor, not daraxonrasib selection; cells subsequently show daraxonrasib resistance. Capan-1 was selected on daraxonrasib, HPAF-II on RMC-7977. These must remain separate history/drug identities; no silent pooling as “same original drug.”
- **Matched four-arm functional cross:** Fig4c/d caption (line52) documents parental **and daraxonrasib-resistant Capan-1**, each with vehicle, daraxonrasib25mg/kg daily, T-DXd4mg/kg weekly, or combination, n6/group, longitudinal tumor volume. This is an actual rescue/control×original-drug/control cross with matched parental/resistant model pair. It is an animal endpoint and cannot be called cheap to regenerate; reusing public source data may still be economical.
- **In-vitro matched rescue:** Fig5a/b caption and Extended Data Fig8c/d (lines58–62/209–212) describe parental/resistant HPAC5day 2D viability matrices for daraxonrasib+zoldonrasib and daraxonrasib+azenosertib/BBI-355, including singles. One of two independent experiments shown, technical duplicates; no raw values read. Again HPAC's selecting drug differs from measured daraxonrasib.
- **Timing/readout and normalization:** Par67 (line114), “ten serial dilutions”;384well cells overnight then compound/0.1%DMSO5days; T0 and T5 luminescence, CellTiter-Glo2.0, normalization against DMSO and baseline. Baseline information exists in protocol, but per-well T0/T5 raw fields are not confirmed. Exact concentrations are in figure matrices/source data, uninspected. No unexposed precision contract can yet be frozen.
- **Target/control limits:** KRAS induction/clonal engineering and sequencing provide a causal resistance perturbation reference. MYC/RTK findings guide cause-specific combinations, but I did not verify a uniform panel where every rescue is crossed against every independently derived cause in the same background and assay. Fig4 multiple treatment-naive KRAS backgrounds and cause-specific efficacy do not automatically meet this contract. It also mixes prevention of resistance with reversal of established resistance; those are separate discovery tasks.
- **Exact public-data availability:** statement (line244): “All data supporting the findings ... within the article and its Supplementary Information”; HPAC parental/resistant DNA sequencing **GSE335219**; “Source data are provided with this paper.” Source links authenticated from XML media, contents unconsumed: `41591_2026_4537_MOESM7_ESM.zip` **Source Data Fig4: Inhibitor response source data**; `...MOESM8_ESM.zip` **Source Data Fig5: Inhibitor response source data**; `...MOESM15_ESM.zip` **Extended Data Fig8: Statistical source data for matrix combo response, unprocessed blots**; MOESM3 XLSX Supplementary Tables1–5. No archive sizes, accessibility status after direct GET, table schema, replicate completeness or matrix coverage verified. Public linked archive ≠ confirmed downloaded cheap functional dataset. GEO sequence accession is not functional rescue data.

**Assessment:** strongest concrete continuation from this batch: public-labelled matched functional response source files accompany independently derived/engineered resistance biology. Enough to develop a **conditional feasibility question**; insufficient to claim a transferable resistance-cause benchmark or admit a test. ## Decision boundary

The search changed the resource assessment from speculative to **specific public-linked candidates with known protocol contrasts**. It did not establish multiple identifiable resistance causes, common rescue coverage, economical extraction, unexposed holdouts or an Agent benefit. Generic resistance reversal, collateral sensitivity, model-guided double binds and bypass combinations are already owned principles. A virtual-cell model would still need to predict functional consequences under a new cause/background; an Agent must change a discovery action based on observations, beating strong ordinary diagnostics/combination selection under total costs. The present resource audit does not co-design or judge the coordinator's separate pooled-scale/Rhaister candidate.
