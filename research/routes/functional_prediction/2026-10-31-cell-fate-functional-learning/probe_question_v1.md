# Cancer-cell history and future susceptibility: qualification v1

2026-10-31. A scientific question and data-admission record, not a numerical model protocol. No response, fate, fluorescence, fitted-parameter or time-of-death values have been projected from source data. Published selected findings have been read and are exposed literature.

## Proposed capability and operative restriction

Can a small observation made after a treatment history but before a specified next challenge predict that challenge's functional consequence across previously unmeasured histories or preparations?

The practical user is a cancer-screening team deciding which next intervention to test after a population becomes tolerant to an initial treatment. The output would be a predicted directly measured functional response for a declared next intervention and horizon. The desired advance is a transportable restriction connecting an observable response state to future intervention consequences, under a small specified measurement budget. It is not a claim of a newly invented state-abstraction principle, pathway mediation or clinical efficacy.

An inspectable version would fix a state observation `S` before the future probe, learn a probe-specific map `f_a(S)` on allowed source histories and then predict `Y` in a held-out history/preparation without a new history-specific coefficient or target-outcome calibration. The distinguishing prediction is that histories mapped to the same `S` have compatible future probe responses within the declared conditions. A reproducible history-dependent residual rejects this particular sufficiency restriction. Agreement for one probe/horizon does not establish a universal cellular state or dynamics.

Candidate observable inputs include current population burden and a reporter measured before the next treatment, such as baseline RIPK3-Cherry where actually available. A fixed independent functional-probe panel is an alternative future acquisition design, not an already available free feature. It must not contain the outcome of the candidate intervention being predicted. Complete future FRET trajectories, their maxima, future minima used for normalization, retrospective fitted initial conditions and final fate labels cannot be prospective inputs.

This question does not yet specify a new learning algorithm. Ordinary conditional regression, history interactions, survival models, state-space/compartment models and predictive-state sufficiency are serious alternatives/equivalences. To become a useful Virtual Cell mechanism, an empirically supported restriction must constrain an unseen intervention consequence beyond merely fitting a renamed latent state. Equal-information ordinary history-aware functional models must receive the same observations, assay budget and exposure limits. A score advantage alone does not establish a new biological mechanism.

## Distinguishing test and confounding limits

An informative test needs independently prepared cancer-cell cultures under documented histories, compatible baseline observations and a common future probe/horizon. State definition, observation window, prediction map, selection/censoring, treatment/preparation split, assay costs and ordinary rivals must be frozen before effects are inspected. A held-out history must overlap the observed state support sufficiently to test the restriction; interpolating a regimen label is not independent biological transfer.

The functional estimand must distinguish whole-population burden from response per surviving/replated cell. Reseeding equal survivors changes the intervention and drops initial depletion unless its denominator is separately retained. Post-treatment matching can select different survivors; a history-by-probe interaction is not automatically cell-intrinsic memory, a pure death/growth pathway decomposition or causal mediation through RIPK3. Shared wells, repeated fields, related cells and pooled errors are not independent biological repeats. A current reporter association can be biologically useful without meeting the stronger transport restriction.

Required before a numerical test: actual joint input/probe/future-outcome records, verified units and timing, risk set/non-events/censoring or an explicitly narrower valid outcome, preparation identities and enough intervention/history support for the stated holdout. No complete numerical input/support/split/label/cost/rival/metric/code freeze exists yet. Missing data leave the hypothesis undecided; they do not falsify it. Source-owned alternation success cannot validate a model selected to explain it.
