# Initial mechanism alternatives, before scout synthesis or response access

2026-10-31. These are proposed tests, not findings or novelty claims. The root has read source identities/Methods, prior-round reports and abstracts, but no EGI count or GI numerical responses. The structural scout has not received these notes.

The practical question is whether a measured untreated functional relationship gives a useful coordinate system for predicting which whole gene-pair relationships change under a known environment. The intended user is a functional-screen laboratory choosing double-perturbation follow-ups after measuring an untreated map, environmental single effects and a fixed subset of environmental double perturbations. This is a retrospective screening capability, not a prospective assay-saving result or an arbitrary unseen-drug claim.

One possible endpoint is a preregistered per-culture four-term difference of log count ratios, constructed directly from authenticated counts, same-library single-target/NTC constructs and matched untreated samples. A difference of environmental-vs-untreated log abundance ratios can avoid the author's query-wide quadratic fitted-label ancestry. It would be a newly defined relative-abundance endpoint, not a reproduction of published standardized GI and not a rate, death mechanism or physical-time trajectory. Exact source timing, identity and control compatibility still control admission.

Three explanations must remain distinguishable. Most apparent changes could be negligible/noisy, ordinary gene-specific environmental sensitivities could explain them, or baseline functional-neighborhood structure could predict additional pair-specific changes. The minimal candidate would smooth only residual changes over neighborhoods derived from the untreated functional map, shrinking unsupported changes to zero. Ordinary conditional linear regression, sparse regression and low-rank matrix completion could already explain the same pattern. Such a smoother is an ordinary graph regularizer; novelty would require a consequential capability and evidence beyond that construction.

A counterexample is immediate: identical untreated profiles plus arbitrary independent environmental pair effects make neighborhood transfer fail. A favorable constructive case has environment changes shared by a functional module while the untreated profiles identify that module and measured anchor pairs cover it. Then unseen pair effects can be estimated from compatible anchors. These are algebraic thought cases, not empirical or simulated results. Independent cultures are needed to distinguish this structure from common noise; two cultures remain two, regardless of pair count.

Before any numerical access, fix one environment/batch, held unordered-pair identities across cultures, available baseline/single information, count eligibility, response formula, model grids and a strong-effect discovery gate. The primary gate must require more reproducible, sign-correct strong interactions among a fixed Top-K than ordinary rivals; an MSE improvement alone will not suffice. A failed prerequisite parks the experiment without choosing a favorable substitute from response values.
