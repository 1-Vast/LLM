## The conditional analytical result

The [frozen candidate](mechanism/candidate_v1.md) and [freeze receipt](mechanism/freeze_receipt.json) precede the [fresh independent initial assessment](review/initial_assessment.md). The [developer's bounded response](mechanism/response_to_review.md) clarifies an observation-unit ambiguity without changing the frozen files or the theorem. Count this conservatively as one post-review assay clarification, within the two-revision ceiling.

Let stable latent types have a common reference distribution pi. Let w be their nonnegative pretreatment descendant weight, bounded by U, and b their invariant additive challenge output per challenge-entry unit, bounded by V. Define

`m = E[w] > 0`, `q = E[b]`, `r = E[wb]/m`.

For finite U,V>0, require `m<=U` and q,r in [0,V]. Every such arbitrary static mixture obeys the sharp interval

`max(0, V-U*(V-q)/m) <= r <= min(V, U*q/m)`.

For 0<q<V, the smallest maximal amplification consistent with m,q,r is

`U_required(V) = m * max(r/q, (V-r)/(V-q))`.

The proof uses the four elementary product inequalities for bounded w and b. A complete constructive rival uses only two immutable types: fraction q/V at b=V with weight mr/q, and fraction 1-q/V at b=0 with weight m(V-r)/(V-q). It attains every feasible observation. This is inherited bounded-moment/Fréchet reasoning, not a new probability theorem. At q=0 or q=V, only r=0 or r=V respectively is compatible; then the minimal U is m. V=0 is trivial; m=0 leaves r undefined. Rounded zeros do not establish exact endpoint support.

The thought example m=.95, q=.8, r=.2, V=1 requires U>=3.8. A qualified U<=2 would exclude this static-mixture model; U=4 admits an exact two-type explanation. These are analytical illustrative numbers, not source values, simulations or an empirical test. Net retention of 95% cannot certify small U: hidden birth and death can cancel. For sensitization r<q, increasing V without bound drives U_required down to m. Thus qualifying pretreatment amplification alone does not repair an unbounded bulk-growth challenge endpoint.

The common reference is equally necessary. If pi is the matched vehicle challenge distribution, w and U must describe relative A/vehicle lineage weights on common support. An absolute division cap under A is not automatically that relative cap. Equal aggregate vehicle counts do not establish equal hidden composition.

## The independent correction: define where the binary endpoint starts

A binary outcome for **each individually isolated cell entering challenge B**, summed over entry cells, can have V=1. A pretreatment founder may supply several such cells. Its summed successes can exceed one; V still bounds the output per challenge-entry cell. Additivity requires each descendant's conditional mean response to remain b under the null, with handling, density and sampling factors fixed. Mutual independence of all successes is not required for the expectation, but family/preparation dependence remains relevant to uncertainty.

By contrast, scoring whether an **entire original pretreatment family** has at least one successful descendant is nonlinear. For fixed n descendants with independent success probability b, its mean is `1-(1-b)^n`; for random N it is `E[1-(1-b)^N]`. Neither is generally E[N]b. Without conditional independence even that family formula needs the joint failure probability. The frozen candidate's ambiguous “founder” wording must be interpreted through this correction. Whole-family binary survival is outside the present bound.

Clumping, filamentation, competition, secreted factors, inoculum shifts and nonlinear assay signals can also break the additive unchanged-response model. Excluding it would show failure of the specified contract, not uniquely prove induction, molecular memory or Cpx action. The inspected papers' bulk endpoints do not currently qualify the joint contract. A workbook's existence would not by itself supply a universal cap; supplementary evidence may still change that assessment if actually inspected.

## Co-learning assessment and the decision

The proposed shared object is the set of static-mixture explanations still compatible with measurements. One could choose short, weakly depleting A pulses and B challenges whose baseline q is near V, then update that feasible set. This is informative only if the priming effect there remains detectable, all bounds are credible, and all uncertainty is retained. It has no established sample-saving guarantee.

A fixed panel plus the same bound calculator and an ordinary robust sequential-design policy implement the same operation with the same information. The developer and independent challenger both identify this concrete ordinary rival, but agent agreement is not the evidence: the explicit two-type construction and operation-level correspondence are. No extra scientific state, update rule or demonstrated capability distinguishes an Agent implementation here. Algorithm novelty, practical explanatory value and biological benefit therefore remain separate and unestablished.

**Decision: park the current main-candidate claim; do not spend the remaining scientific run on unqualified OD/CFU/CTG values.** No empirical result fell inside or outside the envelope, because none was evaluated. No new architecture, latent-rate model, generic history learner or outcome-driven rescue was added. The round learned a real intervention source and an exact failure boundary; it did not achieve the user's requested joint-learning mechanism.

The already recorded restart check is a concrete cheap protocol/source contract, before any new response access: additive challenge-entry endpoint with finite informative V; independent evidence for the full-window U on the chosen reference; known dilution/reseeding and common support; matched B conditions and handling/carryover controls; biological preparation identities. An observed maximum among finitely sampled lineages is not a universal bound. A probabilistic/tail allowance would need its own justified model rather than silently substituting for U.

Only after that qualification should one freeze a single condition, the joint uncertainty region, practical discrepancy and stop rule. Selection is excluded only if every admissible parameter point violates its region, including uncertainty in U,V and reference handling. A crossing interval is inconclusive; a point inside admits static selection but does not disprove induction. Any co-learning claim additionally needs a prospective same-information/same-total-cost comparison with the fixed panel and ordinary design policy, including qualification costs and independent transfer conditions. Those are planned checks, not runs performed here.
