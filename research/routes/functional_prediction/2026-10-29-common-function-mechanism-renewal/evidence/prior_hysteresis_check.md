# Roemhild2018 closest-prior check — 2026-10-29

Original: **Cellular hysteresis as a principle to maximize the efficacy of antibiotic therapy**, PNAS115,9767–9772(2018), DOI10.1073/pnas.1810004115, PMC6166819. Saved original `p6_hysteresis_prior.xml`; exact text by XML locator is in `p6_hysteresis_prior.xml.selected.txt`. Main original inspected; no supplement, dataset or code acquired.

## Decision

**Yes, the original explicitly distinguishes rapid induced physiological response from acquisition of resistance mutations and then studies the distinct selection exerted by that induction over long-term evolution. No, the inspected original does not supply a distribution-free mass/selection bound for arbitrary static latent mixtures.** Its experiments provide compelling biological evidence for an induced effect, including short nonlethal pretreatment, CFU killing, direct microscopy and reconstructed mutant/flow-cytometry contrasts. They do not establish the per-latent-type pretreatment expansion upper bound U or per-initial-lineage challenge-output upper bound V needed for the proposed mathematical exclusion. Aggregate viable or OD counts alone cannot establish those two bounds.

This is a source-check finding, not a novelty judgment on an unseen candidate. The prior already claims induced sensitization, distinct from resistance evolution, so a generic selection-versus-induction distinction cannot be claimed as a new biological principle here. A rigorously specified new exclusion theorem would still need valid assay-specific assumptions; this paper neither provides nor validates them.

## Exact source passages and their scope

XML paragraphs have no IDs; use section IDs plus direct `p` ordinal. Quotes below are from the acquired original, not its abstract.

1. **body/p[2], introduction:** “We here use the term ‘cellular hysteresis’ ... to summarize the inducible and long-lasting physiological effects on cellular integrity and function, spanning both discrete and quantitative changes.” It explicitly discusses inheritance of stable proteins and autoregulatory physiological responses across generations. This is a broad inducible-state concept, not a no-selection theorem.
2. **s2/p[1], Results and Discussion / Cellular Hysteresis Depends on the Order of Drug Switches:** “Hysteresis is also distinct from collateral sensitivity, as it occurs immediately, i.e., without acquiring resistance mutations.” Earlier in the same paragraph it describes “short exposures to nonlethal concentrations” followed by time-kill experiments, order dependence and separation from simultaneous drug interaction. **This excludes a need for newly acquired resistance mutations as the authors' interpretation; it is not an explicit exclusion of enrichment of pre-existing non-genetic states.** No latent-state census is supplied in the inspected main text.
3. **s2/p[2]:** “pretreatments as short as 15 min (equivalent to 1/3 generation time) and concentrations as low as 3 µg/mL [1/32 of the minimal inhibitory concentration (MIC)] were sufficient for the induction of accelerated killing.” It further reports400-fold higher bactericidal activity for15min2×MIC. Low-dose/short-duration conditions limit the plausibility of extensive evolutionary selection but **1/3 average generation time does not prove a hard maximum number of divisions for every latent subtype**. The “as short” and “as low” facts also must not be silently combined into a claim that the identical single15min1/32MIC condition was effective without checking its figure grid.
4. **Fig1 legend within s2:** A uses “concentration of viable cells (cfu)” and n=6; C shows control and CAR-pretreated bacteria in GEN with dead cells stained by propidium iodide; D measures15minCAR pretreatments followed by GEN **and drug-free media**. These are relevant viability and background controls. The original main text does not declare all pretreatments to conserve identical numbers of every lineage, quantify a universal per-type expansion bound, or track all descendants of individually identified founders.
5. **s9/p[1], Time-Kill Experiments:** “Exponential phase cells (5 × 10^7 cfu/mL) were pretreated with sublethal antibiotic concentrations for the indicated time. The medium was exchanged to expose cells to a second antibiotic at IC75, followed by cfu counting for 6 h.” Hysteresis is “the average log10 difference in cfu counts of pretreated and control cultures.” Killing confirmed by time-lapse microscopy on agarose pads. **CFU is a viable-reproductive output, not a bounded death probability per starting lineage. Six-hour challenge permits lineage proliferation/recovery; no V bound is established.** Exponential cells and nonlethal pretreatment do not impose U=1. Medium exchange is documented; thorough double-wash residual-carryover test belongs to P52026, not established here from the inspected main methods.
6. **s3/p[3–5] and Fig2D context:** Model without hysteresis predicts coexistence under rapid switches; adding hysteresis increases selection and reduces diversity. Authors say “inducible physiological effects act as a strong selective constraint.” Thus induction and selection are interacting processes, not competing exhaustive labels. The long-term experiment is190populations,96transfers,12h intervals,≈500generations; it explicitly evolves resistance/tolerance and is distinct from acute short-exposure assay.
7. **s4, Fig5 and paragraphs on cpxS/mexR:** Reintroducing cpxS T163P into ancestral background abolishes CAR-induced GEN sensitization whereas similarly reconstructed mexR T130P does not; ordinary GEN resistance unchanged in either mutation. Fig5C uses OD and flow-cytometry dead-cell measures. The source also reports an evolved ispA-linked tolerance isolate no longer sensitized. These interventions distinguish susceptibility to induction from canonical resistance, a direct prior biological result. They do not identify all hidden phenotypic types or prove arbitrary-mixture mass inequalities.
8. **s16/p[1]:** Defined-mutant growth measured by OD every15min; independent flow-cytometry hourly samples, Live/Dead stain and **three technical replicates**. Do not label those three biological replicates or interpret OD as survival.
9. **s15/p[1], Mathematical Model:** Explicit deterministic logistic growth with mutation, four genotypes (WT and single-drug-resistant types), genotype/drug growth table R, and hysteresis represented by multiplying growth rates by experimentally inferred table S. Parameters K=10^8, dilution50 every12h, cutoff10. **This is a specific growth/mutation model, not a distribution-free derivation over arbitrary latent mixtures, and its carrying capacity does not bound descendants per initial lineage.**

## What must remain unverified

- No source-backed universal U or V for arbitrary static latent types; no exhaustive no-division or per-lineage proliferation measurement.
- “Nonlethal/sublethal” is not identical to zero turnover, no enrichment, or exact preservation of all initial lineages. Stable netcounts would also not establish those statements.
- Immediate response without new resistance mutations does not exclude pre-existing physiological heterogeneity or occupancy/carryover mechanisms.
- Direct microscopy is relevant to actual killing, but the inspected main text does not provide complete lineage accounting through pretreatment and challenge.
- No algebraic total-variation, survivor-mass or selection-only exclusion bound was located in the main original. Supplements were deliberately not acquired; absence there is not asserted.
