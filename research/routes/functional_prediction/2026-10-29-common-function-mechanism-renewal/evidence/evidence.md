## What scientific opportunity survives, and what does not

The original supports **inducibility and its decay under recovery as action-dependent functional properties**, with examples of genotype-level failure and an OD observation bias that can reverse interpretation. This is more informative than a fixed drug-response matrix: switch order, dose and pause physically change later killing. The wash/pause/growth/Cpx/uptake controls give concrete reasons a scalar functional trace might fail to specify a portable susceptibility state. It does not show that one can infer the molecular state from OD alone, that a single latent coordinate suffices, or that useful biology must be learned jointly with an Agent.

The key opportunity is a question, not a proposed algorithm: can inexpensive *legal perturbations* distinguish a background's **ability to be induced** from its current sensitivity, and can an acquired conditional rule transfer across backgrounds/pairs after accounting for readout distortion and physical MICs? Generic history-conditioned prediction, adaptive scheduling, active system identification, calibrated state-space models and mechanism-informed kinetics are strong routine rivals. A gain from more probe wells or a more accurate growth fit alone is insufficient. Any later test must freeze outcome access and transfer units before examining Source Data, and must compare equally informed ordinary models under the same full assay cost. No advance is established in this note.

Direct prior warning from P5's own original references: Roemhild et al.2018 **Cellular hysteresis as a principle to maximize the efficacy of antibiotic therapy**, DOI10.1073/pnas.1810004115, PMC6166819 (CR12); Batra et al.2021 **High potency of sequential therapy with only β-lactam antibiotics**, DOI10.7554/eLife.68876, PMC8456660 (CR13). These bibliographic entries are verified in P5 references; their full methods were not acquired in this worker round. Generic “discover history dependence” or “prime then kill” is already directly pre-empted.
