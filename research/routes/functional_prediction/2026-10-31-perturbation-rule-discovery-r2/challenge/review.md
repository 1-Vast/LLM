## Decision

Park v1 as an unresolved scientific lead. Do not recommend it as a new useful Virtual Cell mechanism, even conditionally in a way that implies its novelty or feasibility is already supported. A conditional recommendation for a narrowly scoped *future feasibility investigation* is defensible, but it is not the research advance requested by the brief. The candidate itself largely acknowledges this distinction.

The biological question is worthwhile: can an invariant, measured cell-response relation transfer across changes in composition/environment and support intervention decisions? The present proposal has not specified which relation is invariant, which measurable history is sufficient, what existing approach fails, or a qualified public observation design. Its remaining contribution is a hypothesis about an unidentified system, rather than an identified learning principle. This is not evidence that such a relation cannot exist.

## Mathematical and information assumptions

1. The count and vehicle-normalization equations are correct for the stated fixed-environment, composition-invariant cohort responses. Independence between cells is stronger than needed for the accounting identity: expectations of descendant counts add even when cells interact. The falsifiable null is that each cohort's expected descendants is unchanged by mixture composition after the specified conditioning. If G is allowed to depend arbitrarily on composition, the weighted sum is bookkeeping and does not test cell autonomy. Make this invariance explicit before any implementation.
2. The covariance identity is correct under conditional drug independence and common weights. It does not establish conditional independence or identify combination outcomes from single-agent marginals. If q is a survival probability of a randomly selected initial cell, its weights are that population's initial cohort probabilities. The earlier untreated-growth weights can be reused only for a separately justified sampling/estimand definition. Avoid presenting the same symbol as automatically having both meanings. This is a notation/estimand clarification, not a refutation of the algebra.
3. A cell's drug response can depend on exposure history, accumulated damage, recovery and time-varying oxygen, not merely the current or terminal local concentration. PhysiCell's original anti-cancer example already uses damage accumulation and repair driven by drug concentration (equations 23-24). A scalar exposure or oxygen value is not a demonstrated sufficient state. Prespecify the observed trajectory or justified summary; do not choose it after inspecting held-condition failures.
4. The prediction-time contract is unresolved. Measuring a target organoid's treatment-induced exposure/oxygen trajectory can yield a valid *conditional response prediction*, but may require administering that intervention first. It therefore does not automatically enable pretreatment intervention selection. If the intended use is selecting an unadministered drug, supply exposure from an independently calibrated, frozen prediction/experimental design with its uncertainty and cost, or restrict the claim to a workflow in which these measurements are genuinely available before the decision. Never feed terminal target-state measurements back as if they were baseline inputs.
5. Environment is partly produced by the living cells. Treatment-induced death changes oxygen consumption, uptake and spatial structure. Conditioning on an observed environment alone does not identify a causal cell response or allow arbitrary environment interventions. The held-condition experiment must separate predictive transport from causal attribution. A measured association that survives transfer may still reflect unmeasured geometry or cell history.
6. Stable pretreatment lineages can define descendant burden even if molecular state switches, provided all viable descendants are assigned to their initial cohort. Terminal marker-defined states alone cannot do this. Stable lineage identity also does not ensure that each initial cohort is homogeneous or that its internal distribution transports between preparations.

## Closest prior and exact residual difference

The inherited claims are substantial. Palmer, Chidley and Sorger's original eLife article explicitly distinguishes cross-resistance from pharmacological interaction and states that superiority of a fixed-dose combination over monotherapy is insufficient to establish synergy. Its Bliss definition uses independent death probabilities. Thus, explaining apparent pooled synergy with heterogeneous sensitivities and designing complementary killing are established foundations.

The DMG original already targets complementary states; its original methods authenticate separate subcutaneous DIPG17 state-depletion and orthotopic DIPG4423 treatment experiments. The AML original already fits sample-specific drug sensitivity models from targets/transcriptomes and nominates combinations through a target-union encoding. These sources own the broad state-complementarity application, though they do not, in the inspected methods, establish the candidate's particular held-mixture invariance claim.

Bradley's original HTML, Definition 2 and paragraph S3.SS0.SSS0.Px2.p2.1, requires independent marginals on disjoint feature subsets. This is not a theorem about sums of cell cohorts, interacting populations or drug independence. Removing that analogy from the scientific claim is correct; no transferred diffusion principle currently remains necessary to v1.

A targeted final-pass original-source check strengthens the prior concern beyond the AIVOs review. **Ghaffarizadeh et al., PhysiCell (2018), DOI 10.1371/journal.pcbi.1005991**, already couples diffusing oxygen/drugs to individual cell processes. Equation 13 specifies oxygen-dependent cell-cycle entry. Section `sec032`, equations 23-24, links local drug concentration to accumulated damage/repair and apoptotic probability. Section `sec033` includes heritable cell heterogeneity, oxygen-dependent growth and selection. The drug-delivery and heterogeneity examples are separate demonstrations; this review does not claim that paper validated the proposed measured transfer law or its public-data benchmark. It does establish that environment-conditioned cellular action, population aggregation and a temporal damage state are old modeling components.

The only defensible residual difference is **empirically learning and independently validating a particular response relation that remains invariant across specified composition/environment shifts with a practical information advantage**. No exact system, sufficient relation, measured advantage or fair operational comparator is yet fixed. The existence of a new validation setting alone would not establish a new general learning mechanism. Compare against a calibrated conventional exposure-response model with cohort effects, exposure history and flexible within-cohort combination effects; do not reserve the useful exposure measurements for the proposed method. A full simulator is not automatically the strongest practical baseline under the same data and resource limits.

This is a bounded prior finding, not a claim that all relevant multiscale pharmacology has been searched. Two actual HTTP attempts, including one redirect, acquired the PhysiCell original; 228,883 known decoded body bytes were used. No further searches are necessary to justify parking this version. The remaining allocation is not a reason to keep searching without a decision-changing premise.

## Feasibility of the joined frame

The original DMG methods and GEO metadata support a genuine structural mismatch, not an absence of public functional data:

- DMG `Sec37` uses subcutaneous DIPG17, five days of treatment and terminal scRNA-seq. `Sec42` estimates treated/vehicle changes in state *proportions*. GEO GSE262775 lists 24 samples and this terminal monotherapy design. Neither source supplies independently assigned initial composition contrasts or stable per-cohort absolute descendant counts in the inspected schema.
- DMG `Sec44`/`Sec49` establish orthotopic DIPG4423 with monotherapies/combinations. `Sec45` describes untreated tumor profiling from separately killed animals. Similar state labels across these experiments do not make an initial-composition-to-functional-outcome pairing. Different models, sites, schedules, sampling times and functional endpoints cannot be silently joined. Tumor volume itself is not absolute viable cell burden without a measurement model.
- AML `s2-8` authenticates four selected combination matrices in paired diagnosis/relapse material of the index patient, with a 72-hour flow assay and doses differing from the single-drug CTG screen. It supplies biological population readouts, but no controlled mixture intervention, independent donors for this validation, local-exposure field or stable pretreatment lineage assignment is authenticated by those methods. Diagnosis and relapse differ in more than composition; they cannot serve as an isolated composition perturbation.

Consequently no currently authenticated matrix tests v1. This conclusion concerns the inspected metadata and methods; response workbooks were not opened. There is no reason to expose outcomes merely to resolve missing design variables. A future metadata-only crosswalk could revise feasibility, but it must identify the same preparation/arm/time, initial allocation, absolute endpoint/denominator, lineage observation or justified alternative, exposure timing, and independent replication. Freeze the permitted projection and outcome boundary before opening any such file. Missing stable lineages are not an absolute prohibition if another defensible observation model identifies the chosen estimand, but terminal fractions alone do not suffice.

## Smallest decisive test if the necessary observations become available

First choose one biological system and a single operational decision. Freeze the conditional relation, exposure-history definition, observation model, eligible shifts, assay endpoint and clinically/preclinically meaningful utility threshold before selected outcomes. Use independent preparations as replication units; cells from a culture are not independent test replicates.

Fit on source preparations with controlled mixture contrasts at fixed total density, geometry and schedule. Hold out entire preparations and a prespecified composition/environment regime, not random cells or wells from the same preparation. Distinguish novel joint combinations of already observed cohort/exposure conditions from extrapolation outside observed exposure support. A held mixture lying inside the training mixture range supports only that interpolation claim; it does not establish a broad unseen-environment capability.

The core test has two separate parts: (a) cohort-level response invariance or a prespecified violation pattern across compositions after conditioning; (b) absolute burden prediction or an intervention decision improved over equally informed direct regression, growth-adjusted cohort weighting, and a conventional exposure-response/mechanistic comparator. Include within-cohort combination measurements equally when available. Evaluate the value of exposure measurements for all methods, not just the proposed method. Use preparation-level uncertainty intervals and prespecified effect sizes; without enough independent preparations to distinguish the threshold, report inconclusive evidence.

Prediction gain alone cannot establish the proposed mechanism. A conditional-invariance claim needs its own contrast. If the same baseline response law fails at matched exposure/initial state across preparations, the assumed sufficient relation has failed even if a flexible condition-specific predictor fits. If ordinary models explain the benefit, retain the useful measurement/analysis finding and stop the asserted new Virtual Cell advance.

These are premise-level requirements, not a request to add modules.
