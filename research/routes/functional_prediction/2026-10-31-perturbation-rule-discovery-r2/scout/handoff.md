### Strong ordinary null for a changed state mixture

The following is a transparent law-of-total-expectation comparator derived here, **not a new donor theorem, algorithm, or proven biological model**. Let k be a *pre-treatment* stable type/lineage, pi_k the measured initially seeded proportion, N0 the seeded count, and g_k,D(t) the expected absolute descendant/live count per initially seeded type-k cell under a fixed drug D and assay. Under cell autonomy and no mixture-dependent exposure/resource effect,

`N_D(t; pi) = N0 * sum_k pi_k * g_k,D(t)`.

g can exceed one because cells proliferate; it is not automatically a survival probability. If untreated type-specific growth g_k,0 differs, normalized viability is

`R_D(pi) = [sum_k pi_k g_k,D] / [sum_k pi_k g_k,0]`,

not generally `sum_k pi_k [g_k,D/g_k,0]`. Equivalently, ratios can be averaged using the *untreated endpoint* weights proportional to pi_k g_k,0. Per-cell luminescence/metabolic signal must be known additive/calibrated before substituting an assay signal for count. A changing mixture response or ranking can therefore be wholly owned by ordinary weighting, growth normalization and measurement physics.

Even under this autonomous null, the endpoint survivor/descendant fraction is

`pi'_k(D) = pi_k g_k,D / sum_l pi_l g_l,D`.

Thus a post-treatment change of state composition is expected from differential survival/growth. It does not by itself demonstrate a state transition, cell-cell interaction, or a newly learned intervention rule. If state labels themselves change with drug, endpoint conditioning additionally conflates transitions, marker changes and selection; baseline lineage/state information or a separately identifiable transition/observation design is necessary.

For drug combinations, there are two separate null layers. (a) Cell autonomy across **population members** permits weighting any measured or ordinary fitted type-specific combination response g_k,AB. It does NOT imply drug independence within a cell. (b) If a genuine killing-probability endpoint and within-type independent drug action are additionally defensible, then s_k,AB = s_k,A s_k,B, giving pooled survival `sum_k pi_k s_k,A s_k,B`. This differs from pooled Bliss `(sum_k pi_k s_k,A)(sum_k pi_k s_k,B)` by `Cov_pi(s_k,A, s_k,B)`. The covariance identity is direct algebra, not empirical evidence. A mixture can therefore appear synergistic/antagonistic relative to pooled Bliss while every type obeys its within-type independent-action null. A strong comparator conditions on known types and allows cross-drug sensitivity covariance/cross-resistance rather than setting it to zero by convenience.

If only pooled single-drug margins are measured, the cross-drug coupling is not identified. Neither changing pi nor observing two single-drug endpoint distributions reconstructs the same cell's joint counterfactual sensitivity without additional stable lineage/repeated-clone assumptions or informative combination observations. With unresolved coupling, report an explicit sensitivity/bound family instead of treating independence as established. No claim is made that this finite scout establishes the globally strongest existing model; these are the minimum scientifically necessary ordinary comparators established by the checked source and elementary identities.

### What a composition change could actually distinguish

A reweighted mixture can test cell autonomy only if initial mixture variation is externally assigned or otherwise justified, types are defined before treatment, their observation/labeling is stable, total density/seed count and dose/media/time are controlled, and there are independent culture repeats with state-resolved **absolute** endpoint counts (plus untreated controls). Monocultures and/or sufficient known-mixture designs are required to identify type-specific g without letting each mixture have arbitrary free responses. Controls must distinguish total density from relative composition and account for proliferation, state switching, uptake/exposure, survival and capture. These are proposed necessary identification checks, not a claim any public resource satisfies them.

Then ask whether the *same type's* drug response g_k,D changes with partner proportions after those factors are controlled. A reproducible change can reject the specified autonomous weighting null; it does not alone identify a signaling mechanism, distinguish all possible exposure artifacts, establish drug-drug synergy, or establish hit-selection value. Distinguishing drug interaction from population interaction further needs both singles and combinations across mixtures and a declared within-type dose-response reference. The scientifically informative extra observable is stable type/lineage-resolved absolute response across controlled initial mixtures, not merely another pooled expression vector or post-treatment state fraction.

### Relation to Bradley and concrete decision

Bradley's CPC replaces disjoint **feature blocks within a joint observation** and assumes their distributional independence. The mixture null sums over mutually exclusive **population members/types** and can retain arbitrary correlations among a cell's features. They are different probability operations (product vs mixture) and different independence assumptions. Feature-block sparsity supplies no reason for mixture weights or type responses to remain constant; mixture-level weighting supplies no disjoint-feature theorem. Neither should be relabeled as the other or advertised as novel biology.
