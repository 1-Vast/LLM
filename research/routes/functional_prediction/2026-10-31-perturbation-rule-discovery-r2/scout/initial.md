## Original donor: conditional projective composition (CPC)

Reading: original abstract; sections 1-3.2; Definition 1; Definition 2; Lemma 1 and Appendix B proof; Appendix D Definition 4/formal Corollary 1 statement. Section 2 and its experiment-description passages were read, not empirical plots independently measured or code reproduced. Approximate-CPC relaxation in Appendix C was not audited; do not inherit an approximate guarantee from this note.

Passage locators (stable HTML ids; derived txt is also line-addressable):

- `S2.p1.1`: experiments with identical training data/architecture but all-object versus one-object condition labeling; two strategies can fit one-labeled-object training observations and disagree at multi-condition inference. This is a concrete observation-model ambiguity, not proof that architecture identifies the biological rule.
- `S3.SS0.SSS0.Px1.p1.1`, `Thmdefinition1`: a local conditional score depends on selected variable and conditioner subsets; a distribution for unseen conditioner combinations is assumed to exist. "Local" means sparse dependencies, not necessarily spatial neighborhoods (`S3.SS0.SSS0.Px1.p2.1`).
- `Thmdefinition2`, Eq. (2): for disjoint variable sets M_j, the distribution conditioned on a set J factorizes into the no-condition distribution on the remaining variables and one marginal p(x_Mj | c_j) for each active condition. `S3.SS0.SSS0.Px2.p2.1` explicitly calls this condition strong.
- `Thmlemma1`; Appendix B `A2.p3.1`-`A2.p5.1`: under this CPC factorization, independent isotropic Gaussian noising preserves block independence and yields an exact score whose variable/condition dependencies follow the block structure. The proof inspected establishes the displayed forward implication. The paper uses "equivalence" in prose; I did not independently establish a broader converse/identifiability theorem.
- `Thmdefinition4`, Eq. (8), and `ThmC1x1`: the feature-space extension assumes an invertible transform and the same disjoint-block factorization. Section 3.2 `S3.SS2.p3.1` and its footnote say learning the feature map/inverse is difficult, technically requiring a map at each noise level. `S3.SS2.p4.1` calls orthogonality of mean differences necessary but not sufficient for the feature-space structure. A convenient low-dimensional encoder or orthogonal mean shifts do not certify this assumption.

Source-native operation: replace autonomous marginal blocks according to their corresponding conditioners, while leaving the remaining distribution at baseline. A score representation can implement the resulting sparse conditional dependence. This is a particular distribution algebra; it is not a generic promise that sparse networks generalize, that perturbations act independently, or that the correct factorization can be identified from singles alone. Diffusion time here is artificial noise level, never biological time.

## Biological mapping and likely failure

Proposed mapping, not a target finding: x is a same-cell joint functional/state measurement vector; c_j is a fully recorded intervention; background b is stratified explicitly. A CPC-style rule would predict in an unseen combination A+B that the A-controlled marginal is the one observed under A alone, the B-controlled marginal is the one under B alone, and the unaffected marginal is baseline. It also predicts the required cross-block independence, not just additive means. No same-cell trajectory or paired counterfactual is needed to state this distribution comparison.

The extra observable is the joint within-cell distribution, plus reliable intervention labels and well/culture-level replication, not another average response score. Marginal shifts can match while covariance, tail mass or conditional dependence violates the rule. Per-cell samples within one well are not independent interventions; repeated source disclosures are not independent replications. If modalities are measured on different cells, their marginals do not identify their joint coupling.

Critical mismatches: drugs often affect shared pathways, so the donor's disjoint autonomous blocks may be false precisely where synergy/resistance matters. Variable cell-cycle/state mixture, survival-dependent selection, capture/segmentation, baseline differences and batch can mimic apparent block replacement or destroy it. Single-condition data alone cannot decide between competing composition rules, as the donor's own labeling example illustrates. A learned invertible representation cannot be presumed to solve those ambiguities. The source provides no cross-cell-background transfer result; stability across backgrounds is an additional test, not a consequence. It supplies no causal biochemical mechanism or drug discovery benefit.

## Concrete decision changed and limits

Before: the episode's choice of object/information is open. After this original passage: propose a *measurement/stop gate* for any compositional route: require source-authenticated same-cell joint measurements, complete intervention/background labels, and independent experimental units spanning singles plus at least some combinations or other informative variation. Test intervention-block autonomy and cross-block dependence before proposing a diffusion/representation architecture. This is a changed future plan suggested by the scout, not an executed coordinator selection or demonstrated advance.

The falsifiable question becomes: does changing one intervention preserve the other intervention's specified marginal and cross-block dependence structure within a fixed background, and does any such relation survive a genuinely different background? Explicitly decide whether the scientific target is autonomous composition or the informative *failure* of autonomy; do not silently treat interaction residuals as evidence for CPC.

Strong comparisons must include the direct empirical/product-of-block-marginals rule, additive/linear composition and the strongest feasible ordinary conditional predictor with the identical information and split. CPC implemented by a neural model alone would be a reimplementation of an existing principle. If this premise leaves no headroom over direct distribution composition, it is not a novel useful cell mechanism. If only univariate means/static endpoints are available, labels are incomplete, repeat units cannot be reconstructed, or sampling/selection cannot be separated from joint state, stop this route before outcome inspection. If an interaction-focused route is selected instead, a new identifiable necessary condition is required; this donor does not supply it.

For drug discovery, a source-authenticated functional endpoint or validated state-to-function link is still necessary. Only a predeclared held-out intervention/background decision such as actual functional hit enrichment under equal assay budget could establish independent selection value. Neither source theorem nor a state-distribution reconstruction can substitute for that test. Benefit remains untested.
