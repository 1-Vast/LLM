## What changed through the investigation

The initial question was whether complementary cellular states can be combined into a transferable functional intervention rule. Two original studies already address state-aware combination design: the 2026 DMG study, DOI [10.1038/s41588-026-02550-w](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-026-02550-w), and the 2025 AML study, DOI [10.1158/0008-5472.CAN-24-3840](https://doi.org/10.1158/0008-5472.CAN-24-3840). This changed the proposed advance from complementary-state targeting to predicting functional response when population composition changes. It did not reopen the earlier ASO restriction or its exposed queries.

The separate [AIVOs reading](../../literature_cross_domain/2026-10-31-virtual-organ-paper-reading/report.md) sharpened a second explanation: drug distribution, oxygenation and spatial context can alter the action experienced by a cell. The frozen [candidate v1](root/candidate_v1.md) therefore asked whether a measured conditional cell-response relation can transfer to an unseen mixture or organoid condition. This was an untested hypothesis, not a claim that exposure-conditioned modeling is new.

The [fresh independent challenge](challenge/review.md) found a direct earlier mechanism in the PhysiCell original, DOI [10.1371/journal.pcbi.1005991](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1005991). Its equation 13 models oxygen-dependent proliferation; equations 23-24 link local drug concentration to accumulated damage, repair and apoptosis. Its drug-delivery and heterogeneity demonstrations are separate examples, not validation of our proposed transfer test. They establish that coupling local environment to cellular rules and aggregating cells is mature prior art. The candidate does not yet specify a distinct learnable relation, a sufficient observation history or a qualified independent test. The reviewer therefore supports parking it, while retaining the scientific question for a future feasibility investigation.

## The ordinary explanation that must be beaten

For stable pretreatment cohorts k, initial proportions p_k, total seeded cells N0 and time T, let G_k,d(T) be viable descendants per seeded cohort member under intervention d. Expected total count is a sum over cohorts. The falsifiable restriction is **composition invariance of the cohort response under the declared conditioning**:

    N_d(p,T) = N0 * sum_k p_k G_k,d(T),
    with G_k,d unchanged when only the initial mixture changes.

Additivity of expectations alone is bookkeeping and also holds when cells interact. If G is allowed to vary arbitrarily with p, the equation cannot test cell autonomy. Different untreated growth matters: a count normalized to its matched vehicle uses weights proportional to p_k G_k,0, rather than initial fractions alone. State-conditioned ordinary regression/mixture models must receive the same source-combination information as a proposed method; population independence does not imply drug independence inside a cell.

Palmer, Chidley and Sorger's original eLife study, DOI [10.7554/eLife.50036](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.50036), makes cross-resistance, independent drug action and independent culture replication concrete. Under an additional justified within-cohort drug-independence assumption for **binary survival of an initially sampled cell**, use its initial cohort weights p. Then

    Q_AB - Q_A Q_B = Cov_p(q_k,A, q_k,B).

A heterogeneous mixture can therefore depart from pooled Bliss even when each cohort satisfies its own independent-drug-action reference. This is an elementary identity, not a new mechanism. The initial-cohort survival estimand must not be silently replaced by untreated-growth weights; reusing those weights requires a separately justified estimand. Cell counts, survival probabilities and ATP luminescence are also distinct. Drug/state-dependent ATP per cell and proliferation can invalidate a probability interpretation of CellTiter-Glo.

Post-treatment state fractions change under ordinary differential survival and growth, as well as state switching, marker changes and sampling. They do not identify state-specific killing or transitions. Stable baseline lineage cohorts can define descendant burden if descendants remain assigned to their initial cohort; endpoint marker states alone cannot. Bradley's [conditional diffusion original](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.16447), actually read at v1, composes disjoint feature blocks under strong factorization assumptions. A product over those coordinates is not a mixture over mutually exclusive cell populations; this transfer correspondence fails without further information.

These findings changed the actual future design: a pooled Bliss comparison and post-treatment fractions are insufficient. A proposed exposure-conditioned response law would need to outperform both a growth-aware cohort mixture and a conventional exposure-response model with cohort effects, exposure history and flexible combination action under identical information. No such comparison has been run.

## The remaining question and the conditions for reopening

The retained question is whether a **specified conditional functional response relation** remains invariant across a defined composition/environment shift. A worthwhile positive result would explain a reproducible functional phenomenon or enable a useful held-condition decision beyond ordinary cohort weighting and calibrated exposure-response modeling. The current record supports neither novelty nor feasibility of that advance.

The independent review adds three necessary corrections to the proposed use. First, terminal concentration is not a demonstrated sufficient state: exposure trajectory, accumulated damage, recovery and oxygen history may matter. Second, a target drug's measured treatment-induced exposure may only become available after administering it. A conditional post-measurement prediction is different from pretreatment intervention selection. The latter needs an independently frozen/calibrated exposure forecast or an explicit paid-probe workflow, with uncertainty, costs and the same information supplied to rivals. Third, environment is partly produced by the living tissue. Conditioning on observed exposure/oxygen does not identify a causal cellular response or justify arbitrary environmental interventions.

Reopen only when an exact source-authenticated preparation/schema establishes compatible pretreatment composition or stable cohorts, functional intervention identity/dose/time, untreated denominators or a justified burden measurement model, independent preparation replication and the intended held-condition split. Where exposure is central, freeze its trajectory/summary, availability time and measurement/prediction cost before held outcomes are opened. A metadata-only projection, if necessary, requires a reviewed sheet/column whitelist before semantic output; no response-dependent selection.

The already proposed distinguishing test then changes composition while controlling total seeding/density, media and geometry, measures the environmental process actually claimed, and holds out complete preparations/donors and a specified mixture/condition. Compare the ordinary and mechanistic rivals under the same observations and source-combination information; remove exposure conditioning and coupling in separate comparisons. Report functional prediction and any prespecified intervention-selection benefit with uncertainty. A gain is not unique mechanism identification; an inconclusive result remains possible. Task-specific utility thresholds must be fixed before response access, not invented now without an admitted task. No LLM Agent is needed for the physical model. Agent-based simulated cells in AIVOs/PhysiCell obey cellular rules; a scientific LLM agent remains a separate downstream question.
