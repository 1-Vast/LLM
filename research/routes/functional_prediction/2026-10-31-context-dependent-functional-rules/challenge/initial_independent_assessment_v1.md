## Finding

A meaningful and inexpensive **internal falsification experiment is possible**. Its object is the operational claim that one source-selected Yeo–Johnson coordinate, shared across these two K562 culture ancestries, carries a pair's latent residual from UT through ETOP to glucose deprivation using measured target singles and a measured UT pair. This hypothesis need not already be established to merit a test. Its one-parameter restriction and zero target-double fitting cost are useful, testable properties.

The candidate is not a new algorithm or architecture. It is an ordinary transformed fixed-effect model / additive difference-in-differences restriction. A useful empirical transport regularity could nevertheless be a legitimate contribution. Neither the law's predictive usefulness nor a reduction in real screening cost has yet been shown. This initial assessment does **not** admit response-value access or numerical execution: several material code/protocol choices remain unfrozen, and the coordinator has said a separate freeze will follow.

## Ordinary equivalents and exact boundaries

Write `U=T(Y)`. The proposal is exactly

`U_pair,e - U_pair,UT = (A_e-A_UT) + (B_e-B_UT) - (N_e-N_UT)`.

Thus each ordered guide pair has an unrestricted intercept measured in UT, the two singles provide environment-varying main effects, and there is no additional pair-by-environment effect in U. This is a transformed additive contrast, equivalently fixed pair effects plus observed single shifts. The use of ordinary Yeo–Johnson transformation and source error selection supplies no new algorithmic principle. The unusual empirical question is whether that restriction transfers here without target-double calibration.

At identity T, `N=mean_NTC Y=0` by construction. Substituting the prediction into the defined Q cancels target and baseline single shifts and all `d_N` terms, giving exactly `Q_hat=0`. Identity-coordinate transport, persistent raw pair residual, and Q=0 are the same comparator. The candidate correctly states this; code must preserve that identity numerically.

The rank paper's scalar model and rank restriction are not the same hypothesis as this candidate. Its one-dimensional additive model assumes a shared monotone readout and preserved mutant ordering across genetic backgrounds. I054 allows arbitrary measured single shifts between environments and arbitrary baseline pair residuals. Consequently, a rank reordering alone cannot refute I054, and success of I054 would not validate the scalar rank model. The shared overlap is nonlinear coordinate removal of apparent raw-scale epistasis. The rank paper explicitly explains heteroskedastic noise/saturation and, in s11, shows that extra additive latent dimensions can produce apparent specific epistasis without direct coupling. That directly defeats an interpretation of I054 failure as identified molecular rewiring, or success as a uniquely identified molecular trait.

The functional-bottlenecks Methods define `E(a)=sum_i h_i a_i` and sigmoid `F_B(E)`, `F_R(E)` readouts with opposite signs. This is prior support for ordinary nonlinear readout explanations, not evidence that the same invertible coordinate must hold across these stresses. The multienvironment-fitness Methods describe each environment's own supervised genotype-to-fitness model. That is an ordinary regression precedent and potential resource-based comparator, but it does not establish the same target-single-only, zero-target-pair transfer contract.

## Specific objections and resolution conditions

**O1 — Empirical coordinate transport is the unsupported premise.** Independent pair-by-environment deviations are unidentifiable from these prediction inputs, exactly as the candidate's boundary example shows. The proposal acknowledges this and is not invalid merely for being restrictive. What remains to resolve is whether a source-identified member of this small family survives target prediction within meaningful error. Keep distinct outcomes: source prediction failure; source-identifiable but target failure; source-unidentifiable; target variation too small to assess; useful target prediction. Do not convert every failure into evidence for direct biological interaction change.

**O2 — Algorithm novelty is already unavailable; scientific usefulness remains open.** The transformed fixed-effect equivalence above is exact. Source selection of one power parameter is conventional. Resolve by keeping the main claim empirical and resource-conditional. A convincing result could be a useful functional restriction, a clear capability boundary, or a well-supported negative result. Beating a weak model would not establish algorithm novelty. Conversely, matching a flexible regressor can leave a parsimonious descriptive relation or lower-cost implementation worth reporting, provided its advantage is actually measured and not inferred from simplicity alone.

**O3 — Equal information must include the distribution used before averaging.** `mean T(Y)` generally differs from `T(mean Y)`. The candidate uses all guide–NTC outcomes and all NTC–NTC outcomes before transformation. A rival given only single means is missing information used by the candidate, even if it sees the same named biological categories. NTC nonlinear mean and guide/control dispersion can change the output. The numerical freeze must specify the rival's complete feature map, orientation symmetry, whether moments/quantiles or complete allowed distributions are accessible, and source-only tuning. A second-order approximation with the corresponding control moments is an especially relevant ordinary explanation. This is an information-accounting objection, not a request to invent a replacement core mechanism.

**O4 — The coordinate depends on assay conventions and shared baseline noise.** Original S31 uses depth normalization, a negative-control median, per-doubling normalization and pseudocount 10. The proposal deliberately uses a different endpoint estimand: mean-NTC-centered log change without division by doublings. That is defensible if frozen and labelled accurately. With additive pseudocounts, scaling raw sample counts before addition is not innocuous; NTC centering does not generally undo it. Nonlinear `T(Y)` also keeps T0 error and UT-baseline error, shared across the ETOP and glucose arms. An apparently transferable coordinate can partly describe this observation process. Freeze exact count-depth/pseudocount order and quantities, and quantify dependence on baseline/read-depth strata and ancestry using source-side diagnostics. Do not interpret common T0/UT ancestry as external replication or automatically subtract measurement noise using technical guides.

**O5 — Selecting one lambda does not establish an identified coordinate.** The source loss profile can be flat or several near-equivalent lambdas can yield different glucose predictions. A deterministic tie-break does not resolve that scientific ambiguity. Freeze a source-only compatibility criterion, the units/size of a materially different target prediction, and what makes the result inconclusive. The candidate's `T(0)=0`, `T'(0)=1` prevent affine scale collapse, but they do not establish empirical identifiability or error stability after inverse transformation. Also freeze overflow handling and any forbidden clipping. With lambda in [0,2] the mathematical whole-line inverse is valid; numerical saturation is a separate implementation issue.

**O6 — An empirical min/max support flag can be scientifically weak.** A single extreme source point can create broad nominal support with little information in the region where the inverse is sensitive. The candidate also uses both observed endpoint inputs and a predicted transformed endpoint; their support rules cannot be left discretionary after target scoring. Freeze which quantities define support and the exact source set used. Report the full error and coverage distribution and source occupancy/sensitivity within the admitted range. Do not infer broad interpolation reliability merely from being inside global min/max. This need not expand the model or impose a new test population.

**O7 — Prediction success and “law holds” are different claims.** Lower average Q error can occur with systematic pair-by-environment residuals remaining; failure can arise from measurement error or this narrow transform family. Prespecify predictive utility as the primary object. For a claim that the latent residual is invariant to practical precision, report source-held and target residual distributions against an explicit error tolerance, with uncertainty and ancestry visibility. A source-trained scalar that beats Q=0 alone cannot rule out ordinary flexible mapping or count-scale explanations.

**O8 — Practical relevance is conditional and narrower than drug discovery generally.** The actual task concerns pairwise CRISPRi growth selection in an ATRi-enriched gene panel in K562, not drug–drug response, a general cell simulator, or patient efficacy. Original S23 documents phenotype-selected guide/gene inclusion; S24 documents two culture ancestries and different ETOP/glucose exposure schedules. Measuring target singles in the full pooled screen retrospectively is not equivalent to physically screening a reduced library at equal precision. No-prospective-target-pair fitting is a useful assay-information property, but the UT and ETOP full-map input costs, target single/control channels, and any validation pair assays must be counted. A real restricted-library experiment or an external resource is needed for demonstrated savings/generalization, not for this first internal test.

## A feasible meaningful falsification test and remaining admission details

The proposed UT→ETOP development, held-pair source check, and UT→glucose single-only transfer can distinguish this v1 restriction from Q=0 and ordinary prediction. It is feasible in principle without author code, model weights or new wet experiments. Pair-level holdout is legitimate for a claim about new pairs among these genes; it is not held-gene generalization. Reusing the same held pairs for source and target diagnostics is acceptable if both responses are opened only after fitting and no source-held response affects target predictions.

Before opening numerical responses, a complete independently reviewed freeze still needs:

1. Exact allowed files/columns and parse rules; guide-to-gene mapping; distinct unordered pair/diagonal exclusions; pseudocount and depth convention; equations and every averaging/weighting level; numerical inverse/finite-value behavior. Preserve missingness. Test the algebra on artificial constants/symbolic examples without accessing data responses.
2. Target-independent eligibility using identities/T0/UT only, with exact thresholds. No target double abundance/missingness-based filtering before prediction. Define how unobservable target labels are reported at scoring rather than silently removed as bad predictions. Single-control availability requirements are distinct from target-double label access.
3. Deterministic pair hash, namespace/seed, fractions, source internal validation and refitting rules. Keep guides/orientations/cultures for a pair together. Freeze one lambda shared across ancestries, tie rule, source loss aggregation, source-compatible set, and input-support rule before glucose double labels.
4. Fully specified equal-information rivals and hyperparameter search with comparable source-only access. Include Q=0 once. Keep a legitimate ordinary nonlinear regressor if available; a missing dependency must not trigger unapproved installation or a post-outcome comparator change.
5. Primary Q-unit loss, aggregation weights, practical error/advantage tolerance, source identifiability rule, ancestry-consistency criterion and target noise/headroom interpretation. Ranking is secondary unless its assay budget and success criterion are frozen in advance. Avoid the previously excluded impossible extra-hit criterion.
6. Uncertainty that respects shared genes and paired ancestries; do not bootstrap individual guides/constructs as independent biological experiments. Specify precisely whether inference concerns this finite gene panel/pair distribution, or a broader population not supported by two cultures. The two ancestries are both development inputs, so concordance of their held-pair results is an internal robustness check rather than an independent biological replication of model training.
7. A staged access mechanism: source development responses only for training/tuning; allowed UT baselines and target single/NTC observations for feature construction; persist hashes/provenance of frozen source and glucose predictions before source-held ETOP and target glucose double labels are exposed to scoring. Exact parser restrictions must prevent target doubles from entering aggregate normalization/support/features. Global count-depth estimates would themselves require declaring whether they inspect forbidden double counts; raw-count convention avoids that particular hidden access but retains the pseudocount/depth caveat.
8. Stop rules and reporting for family failure, no headroom, weak source identification, low support, or a simple rival matching it. No spline enrichment or target calibration as a silent rescue. Any later change to the core relation needs a new candidate version and relevant review.

None of these requires inventing a novel architecture or proving the hypothesis before testing. They make the proposed experiment interpretable. A meaningful target failure with a source-identified coordinate can reject this particular common-coordinate transfer rule on this panel. A meaningful successful result can support internal functional utility under the specified assay inputs. Neither outcome alone establishes external biological validation, molecular mechanism, novel algorithm, economical physical screening or general Virtual Cell capability.

Initial decision: **scientifically testable, conditionally worth a narrow falsification experiment; numerical execution not yet admitted pending the promised complete protocol/data/code freeze.** The unresolved strongest prior (Otwinowski 2018 original method) limits novelty claims, not the possibility of testing empirical usefulness.
