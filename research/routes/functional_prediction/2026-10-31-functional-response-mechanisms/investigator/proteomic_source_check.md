## Observation and endpoint contract

- **Molecular endpoint:** PTDS is temporal protein abundance, with63FDA-approved drugs grouped by target/pathway (Extended Data1). Its caption gives2-4 biological replicate samples per cell-line/drug/time-point condition. Pools and technical runs are QC objects, not additional independent treatments. Exact cell-line list, dose, time grid, protein filtering and train/test allocation require Methods/schema. The mtPTDS caption distinguishes instrument batches and durations, but does not establish longitudinal non-destructive measurement of the same cells. Mass spectrometry trajectories are molecular response, not independent efficacy.
- **A real independent functional assay exists in the reported study:** Extended Data3D describes capecitabine dose-response in TYMS-depleted versus control HCC1143, with n=3 biological replicates from independent cultures per concentration, sigmoid fitting and IC50; replicate-level measurements are advertised in Supplementary Information. Extended Data3C separately measures siRNA knockdown efficiency by MS. This authenticates a genetic-plus-drug validation, not yet a large paired intervention/function resource. It does not establish that every PTDS row has a matched viability outcome, nor that IC50 is the training target rather than a validation output. Exact viability technology, timing, doses, negative controls, preparation mapping and unaggregated export are unknown.
- **Clinical readout is different:** Extended Data9 describes survival curves stratified by model risk scores for a multi-drug-treated cohort. These are patient-outcome associations, not randomized causal effects of choosing a drug, and not a cheap matched culture-functional panel.
- **Transfer is source-owned:** Extended Data8 explicitly describes proteomic pretraining followed by transcriptomic PDX-data refinement and comparison with from-scratch training on the same subset. Temporal-pretraining transfer, predicting clinical responses, TYMS sensitization and generic proteomic virtual-cell functionality are existing claims of this work; using them is not a newly discovered mechanism. No reported benchmark/model values were extracted.

True experimental units must therefore be separated: independent culture/preparation nested within cell-line×drug×dose×time for in-vitro efficacy; MS sample/run for molecular QC; donor/organoid or patient for external outcomes; model training run for algorithm uncertainty. Available captions do not qualify all nesting/matching needed for a new split.
