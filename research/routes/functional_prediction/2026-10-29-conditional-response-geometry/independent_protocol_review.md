### Algebra and baselines

For centered prediction error e, the unnormalized pair loss satisfies

`(1/28) sum_(q<r) (e_q-e_r)^2 = 2 ||e||^2 / (d-1)` for d=8.

With the frozen `D = 2 tr(C0)/(d-1)`, the scored loss is exactly `||e||^2/tr(C0)`. Thus, with the correct conditional mean and covariance, the expected normalized decrease from noiseless difference observation is `V_s(a)/tr(C0)`. The line-10 proportionality and the mean/scalar-covariance invariances are correct. The covariance must be for the centered vector z; line 27 supplies that centering. One may also substitute a full-y covariance only after projecting it with P. No new theorem or mechanism identity follows.

The fixed estimator is mathematically coherent: pooled within-class covariance, sample-covariance trace normalization, fixed count shrinkage, and ridge on the centered subspace. Since `a' P a=2`, a strictly positive epsilon makes every action denominator positive. Ridge must be included in both V and the update. C0 in D is the pre-ridge pooled covariance as written. Numerical symmetrization/projection may handle roundoff; it must not change the estimator or tune thresholds.

B0 and B1 are useful strong comparisons here. B1 versus C shares the full conditional prediction model and differs only in the fixed versus class-dependent action. B0 versus C additionally changes the covariance model. The conjunction of both tests is more informative than C versus a no-query mean alone. Separate LOO-selected fixed pairs for B0/B1 are legitimate. ### Interpretation that must survive a positive result

- **Restricted information at equal retrospective cost:** two fitted curves produce two absolute values in reality; this protocol deliberately reveals one signed difference. A pair average can contain predictive information about a relative spectrum when nuisance levels are not arbitrary, so difference-only is not generally a sufficient statistic for every real two-curve procedure. All three policies can validly be compared under the same restricted interface, but passing does not establish optimal use of two curves, real assay savings, or superiority over methods allowed both absolute values. This is an interpretation boundary, not a request to replace the main task.
- **Oracle versus plug-in geometry:** the exact expected-decrease argument assumes the true conditional mean/covariance. Finite class samples, shrunken means, pooled covariance, and ridge make V an estimated score. Biased means and estimation error can affect realized choices even though the oracle ranking is mean-invariant. Passing supports useful class-conditioned correction/selection in this frozen proxy. It does not identify the true biological covariance, prove anisotropy as a causal mechanism, or prove that sparse feedback can learn it. Using at least two pairs is a necessary action-variation check, not independent evidence of a biological relational mechanism.
- **Stability, not population coverage:** the stipulated cluster bootstrap resamples fixed development errors with the model held fixed. It captures neither training-model uncertainty nor independent chemical/biological replication. The unequal-cluster-size weighted mean is coherent with the frozen per-drug estimand. Requiring both marginal lower bounds above zero is a conjunction of improvements; lack of multiplicity correction is not by itself an error for an intersection-union success criterion. Historical development exposure and unverified structural identities still prohibit a confirmatory generalization claim.
- **Fit provenance:** shared NLME fitting and extrapolated IC50 values can induce relationships in these labels. Removing a common drug offset does not remove cell-dependent fit errors, batch structure, off-window extrapolation, or class-associated fitting artifacts. The current protocol appropriately calls the output a fitted proxy. A positive local decision benefit may justify the next mechanism-discriminating question; it does not answer it already.
- **Analytic toy construction — line 12:** identical marginal distributions require identically distributed a,b, not merely independent zero-mean unit-variance a,b. Minimum textual fix: say a,b are iid N(0,1), or explicitly claim only identical first two marginal moments. The crossed-pair zero-information/perfect-centered-reconstruction argument itself is sound for iid a,b and is only a possibility example, not data evidence.
