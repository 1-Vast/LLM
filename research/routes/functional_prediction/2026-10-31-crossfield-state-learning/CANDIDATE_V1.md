# Candidate v1 and independent challenge contract

Question: Does a finite, standardized functional challenge panel serve as a predictive cell-state measurement that preserves later treatment choices across unseen preparation histories and chemotypes, beyond equally informed ordinary response-panel reconstruction?
Inputs: baseline/early observations, previous exposure history H, diagnostic probe responses P measured before action A on exchangeable sister cultures, physical dose/time and independently prepared later function Y. First action menu is one fixed dose/time or one second-drug challenge; not all combinations/washout at once.
Rule: learn a low-dimensional map z=f(baseline,H,P), explicitly supervised by future action-outcome vectors and fixed-budget selection regret; recurrent update only when real repeated measurements exist. Compare direct full history, plain response-panel ridge/covariance, supervised reduced-rank/PLS, passive-state model, chemistry and matched-cost fixed/random probes. No LLM required.
Proposed scientific contribution: discover and delimit a transferable operational susceptibility state and a challenge that exposes otherwise hidden history-dependent response. PSR/VIB/PVE/decision-focused learning and response panels are inherited methods, not novelty claims. Biological early functional profiling is existing prior art, including dynamic BH3; no broad novelty asserted.
Falsifier: at overlapping z, a held-out history under the same action or a complete randomized action contrast has reproducibly different future outcome not explained by measured exposure/preparation. Distinguish rejection of this map from proof that no possible state exists. Mere different-action divergence is not a sufficiency violation.
Payoff: lower decision regret at equal total assay cost, or fewer assays at prespecified noninferiority/coverage, across independent preparations and unseen chemistry; representation dimension alone is not a saving. Compact z cannot beat a Bayes-optimal predictor with the same full H,P by adding information; finite-sample regularization/inductive bias or reduced measurement cost must explain a practical gain.
Boundary: a finite reference action menu cannot identify novel interventions without structure/support. No prediction model invents absent sequential outcomes. Agent/organoid deferred until core evidence.
Review request: independently assess novelty over PSR/R-PSR, ordinary response panels/Rhaister, dynamic BH3/functional precision medicine; determine which part is potentially significant, minimal feasible differentiating experiment, and which proposed claim must be stopped. Do not seek a positive verdict. Inspect raw source notes and originals as necessary. <=4 HTTP requests/no fitting/protected outcomes/core edits.
