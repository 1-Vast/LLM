The article itself states n=2 biological replicates and that statistics using technical duplicates are qualitative; future broader replication is needed. This is a close, source-owned ordinary rival for mechanistic functional action selection. ## One feasible falsifiable question (conditional, candidate-free)

Can a fixed, cheap functional panel selected from a training pool improve a pre-registered held-out cell-specific drug-ranking decision over (i) global drug means, (ii) ordinary fixed-panel ridge/GBM, and (iii) the same-budget random panel, when all compared rows share cell identity, drug identity, physical dose and endpoint/time, and evaluation contains an untouched cell×drug set? This is a narrow capability question, not a novelty claim. It is falsifiable by no gain or by failure to preserve rank/calibration under a second compatible resource. A dynamic/Agent claim would additionally require feedback changing the next assay action and independent biological preparations.
