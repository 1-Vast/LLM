## Object A: ATP versus reproductive survivor burden

The [independent observation development](observation_branch/developed.md) distinguishes the action objective from a surrogate. Under a qualified additive founder-colony protocol, let `N` be live entry cells, `p` their average probability of producing an assigned colony by a fixed post-washout deadline, and `C=Np` the conditional expected successful-founder count. Define blank-corrected ATP `A=mN` for positive `N`. Then `C=A(p/m)` where `m>0`. These are definitions/expectation identities, **not a discovered biological law**. Independence of founder successes is unnecessary for expectation additivity; across random wells the target retains `E[Np]`, generally not `E[N]E[p]`.

The hand example `(A,N,p)=(20,20,.9)` versus `(30,60,.1)` gives expected C18 versus6 and reverses the ATP-based ranking. It proves possibility only. A common positive `p/m` is sufficient for equal rankings, but not necessary for a particular finite pair. Identical ATP/count snapshots can coexist with different future reproductive capacity when `p` is unrestricted. This refutes universal snapshot sufficiency, not the usefulness of a specified probabilistic predictor.

For oncology screening teams advancing schedules for suppression after withdrawal, a transferable restriction on `p/m` could change an early advance/reject decision. The missing contribution is that restriction and its domain: neither a source-supported direction/invariant nor matched ATP/live-count/delayed founder observations has been identified. ATP lysis requires appropriately randomized sister cultures; assay interference, plating loss, founder assignment, merging, density and deadline censoring matter. P3 occupied area and P4 long counts are neither `N` nor the defined `C` by naming convention.

Mandatory rivals are direct delayed `C` measurement when available before the decision and a full-history joint model receiving the same observations, labels, timing and total acquisition budget. If a decision must precede `C`, any speed advantage is prospective and must pay for historical paired learning and calibration. Ordinary inference could implement a useful biological restriction; that expressibility does not refute its value. No such restriction or measured timing/decision benefit is established here. **Park A before effect testing.**

## Object B: finite reusability of a complete treatment cycle

The [mechanism development](mechanism/pass15_human_alternative_check.md) asks whether a successful complete schedule remains effective on its resulting population. With the same positive burden endpoint at the same phase before/after two identical complete cycles, the candidate scalar rule is `Y2=Y1^2/Y0`. Identical treatment input alone does not justify that rule: composition, state, density, carryover, handling and observation scale can change the effective gain.

A stronger ordinary rival already breaks scalar reuse: a nonnegative static multiplier mixture has `Yk=integral(g^k dmu(g))`. With total initial mass `Y0>0` and finite second moment,

`Y0*Y2 - Y1^2 = Y0^2 Var_w(g) >= 0`.

For initial masses90/10 and cycle multipliers.1/1.1, the hand values are Y0=100,Y1=20,Y2=13, while scalar reuse predicts4. The algebra follows directly from the weighted first and second moments and was independently checked. This is standard moment mathematics, not a new theorem or biological finding. Upward departure is compatible with static heterogeneity/selection. A reliable downward departure rejects **that static nonnegative-mixture family** under a valid common-scale observation contract; it does not distinguish ordinary switching, density effects or treatment history, and it does not establish acquired resistance or Agent advantage.

P3 already observes two alternating cycles and uses coupled drug/sensitivity/memory dynamics. The currently proposed constant-gain law has no independent biological motivation and is not a deployed assumption of this project. Its rejection alone would mainly support retaining the already-present dynamic rival; non-rejection with a few source repeats would not justify durable renewal or unseen-background reuse. A fair early-to-later prediction must freeze outcomes unavailable to fitting for **both** rules, and disclose exposure to published conclusions. It would remain a reanalysis, not an independent biological experiment. **Do not spend the remaining scientific-test quota merely to reject this unused scalar simplification.**

### Corrected estimand and pairing requirement

Root directly reread P3 Sec15: density change is `(CD_t2-CD_t1)/CD_t1 *100`, despite the caption's loose word “difference.” A mean of preparation-level normalized changes generally differs from a normalized change of mean occupied areas. Multiplying published mean percent gains cannot automatically reconstruct a population-mean trajectory.

The [post-initial independent clarification](review/estimand_clarification.md) corrects an overly broad possible reading of the earlier pairing concern. Independent destructive endpoint cultures **can** estimate schedule-specific population means at0/48/96h and support a prespecified null `mu2=mu1^2/mu0`, with `mu_k=E[Y_k]`, positive `mu0`, valid matched/randomized preparation, correct complete schedule prefixes and comparable observations. Longitudinal measurement of the same dish is not universally required. This mean null differs from `E[Y2-Y1^2/Y0]=0` and means of within-preparation ratios. Preparation IDs and actual sampling dependence determine covariance and resampling units; within-preparation ratios need their own paired-data contract. No source layout is qualified or disqualified merely by this clarification.
