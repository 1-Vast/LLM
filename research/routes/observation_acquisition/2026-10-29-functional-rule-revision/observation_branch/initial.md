# Initial alternatives — independent functional-observation branch

2026-10-29. Preserved before reading any parallel candidate or agent verdict. This branch uses the innovation-research workflow. Scope: public human tumor-cell/drug/physical-dose/time functional assays only; source metadata, published text, protocols and captions; no response workbook values, new HTTP, tests or model calls. It is an alternate scientific development branch, not a challenger. Requested native gpt-6.1-sol/high; accepted backend/settings/tokens/cost unexposed.

Question: when can a precise relation among cheap functional observations change an actual intervention decision, rather than improve the fit of a scalar sensitivity label?

Three initially live alternatives:

1. **Metabolic suppression versus loss of recoverable cells.** A fall in ATP/CTG may reflect fewer viable cells, reduced ATP per viable cell, or both. Relate ATP, viable-cell abundance and growth after drug removal. The consequential action is whether to continue the same cytotoxic exposure or remove/switch it when a favorable CTG readout hides recoverable cells. A universal ATP-to-cell calibration cannot settle this. However, an unrestricted ordinary multivariate time/history model can express the relation; scientific value would require a transferable intervention-conditioned constraint, not a new estimator name.
2. **Suppression now versus irreversibility later.** A low on-drug cell count may be reversible arrest, slow continued death, or durable loss of reproductive capacity. Ask whether a measured relation between short-term suppression and post-washout regrowth transfers across backgrounds/drugs and reverses a drug/schedule ranking. A directly measured regrowth outcome is a strong equal-cost rival; this is only worthwhile if a learned relation applies on held-out actions under honest cost accounting.
3. **Alternative death-mode schedules and survivor response.** Similar early killing need not imply similar survivors; a second drug or death mode may act differently after the first. A sequence-specific observation could revise the rule connecting survivor abundance to later challenge response. This is adjacent to prior selection/history work and requires stronger original-source checks before reuse. An ordinary state/history-dependent response model is mandatory, and aggregate endpoints cannot identify induction versus selection.

Initial priority is alternative 1 integrated with the action distinction of 2: **can two regimens with equal measured ATP imply different recoverable burdens, and can an agent learn when the ATP readout ceases to order recoverable burden?** This is not yet a novelty claim or a recommendation. The first source decision is whether P3/P4 provide matched ATP/count/recovery observations at crossed doses/times, rather than merely several assays in the same paper. If not, the source cannot distinguish the proposed relation from assay/preparation/time differences and the branch should stop before values.

Retained archive conditions: prior control-mean curvature does not identify turnover, metabolism or recovery; earlier equal-budget calibration/probe failures remain binding for those exact task/cost contracts. A different biological endpoint could be meaningful, but does not erase a strong direct-target or joint-inference rival. No architecture, acronym or positive result is presumed.
