## A: ATP, live cells and reproductive founders

The hand construction is correct: the two actions yield 18 and 6 expected successful founders, respectively, while their ATP readings are 20 and 30. With positive ATP and a qualified observation contract, `C = A(p/m)`. A common positive `p/m` is sufficient for identical ATP and founder rankings, but it is not necessary for a particular finite ranking to coincide. The construction shows that disagreement is possible, not that it occurs in either source or that the discrepancy will be predictable.

`m=A/N` is a derived mean measurement per live cell, not automatically a metabolic mechanism. `C=Np` is a conditional expectation under an additive protocol: with cell-specific probabilities, it can use their entry-population average. Independence among colony successes is unnecessary for expectation additivity, although it matters for variance. Across random wells, one must retain `E[Np]`; multiplying separately averaged `N` and `p` generally loses their covariance. Colony merging, paracrine cooperation, density dependence, ambiguous founders, treatment carryover and plating losses can invalidate the proposed per-entry-cell probability or change its domain. The model must specify whether the target is successful founders, descendant cell mass at a deadline, or regrowth probability of a whole culture; these targets are different.

ATP needs blank, interference and non-live/background contributions addressed. The live-count identity must include the intended entering population and its actual handling. ATP lysis is destructive, so a matched sister-culture design, randomization and biological preparation identifiers are essential; nominally equal wells are not measurements of the same cell population. Washout must be operationally defined. A live count or immediate death assay is not a post-washout reproductive assay. A delayed colony deadline also censors slowly recovering competent cells, so the learned relation will initially be deadline-specific.

The packet leaves `p` unrestricted conditional on the observed snapshot. Identical `A,N` with distinct `p` proves that no universally valid snapshot recovery of future competence is available without additional assumptions or observations. It does not show that a probabilistic predictor cannot be useful in a specified population, or that physical history and state measurements cannot resolve some ambiguity.

The missing scientific object is an explicit, testable restriction linking competence to an observed biological state/history and delimiting transfer across actions or backgrounds. For example, a proposed direction or invariant of `p/m` would need a defined intervention domain and evidence that it predicts delayed competence outside the fitted action. No such direction, invariant, causal mediator or transfer domain has yet been identified in the packet. An observed ATP–colony rank inversion alone would establish endpoint mismatch, not that transferable relation.

The strongest direct rival measures the actual target `C` under the same assay availability, waiting time, budget and preparation constraints. If the research action can wait for that assay, the benefit of inferring `C` indirectly requires justification. If decisions must precede delayed readout, then historical paired data could support a faster surrogate, but that is a prospective timing advantage to test. A full-history joint response model receiving the same counts, ATP readings, delayed labels and physical history is also essential. It can implement a real biological relation; being expressible in that model does not refute the relation. Any claim for an Agent needs a separate, budget-matched acquisition/revision comparison against an ordinary design policy, rather than attributing new functional measurements to agent reasoning.

## B: complete-cycle reusability

The scalar arithmetic and two-type counterexample are correct. Identical physical treatment cycles define repeated application of a treatment map. They do not imply that the endpoint alone is a sufficient state or that the map is scalar multiplication. Even time-invariant biology can change composition after each cycle and therefore change the effective endpoint multiplier. Recovery timing, residual drug, handling, density and observation normalization must also be included in the full-cycle definition. A drug-free recovery phase cannot be inserted in one cycle and silently omitted in the next.

A useful stronger rival yields a precise diagnostic. For a nonnegative static multiplier mixture with finite first two moments,

`Y0*Y2 - Y1^2 = Y0^2 Var_w(g) >= 0`,

where `w` is the normalized initial mass measure. Equality is obtained only when `g` is constant on the initially present mass, apart from degenerate zero-mass qualifications. Thus an upward departure from `Y1^2/Y0` is already explained by static heterogeneity and selection. It cannot on its own establish acquired resistance, active memory or a new cycle law. A reliable downward departure would reject that particular static nonnegative-mixture family under the observation assumptions. It would still be compatible with ordinary multitype switching, density effects or history-dependent dynamics; those are stronger rivals. The mixture inequality is mathematical prior knowledge, not a newly discovered biological law here.

Two cycle endpoints do not identify mixture components, a switching mechanism, or a durable rule. Exact equality at the available times is finite compatibility, subject to uncertainty and assay resolution. It does not prove permanent homogeneous susceptibility. If `Y1` is near a detection floor, ratios and squared extrapolations become especially unstable; uncertainty in all endpoints and shared references must propagate into any decision. Independent destructive endpoint dishes are not longitudinally paired cultures. Preparation-level clustering and reference covariance need explicit treatment.

An independently frozen early-to-later prediction could add an honest finite prediction assessment if later observations were not used to select the relation, tune it or choose the test. It would be new analysis of the existing source experiment, not an independent biological experiment or external validation. Because later outcomes and qualitative source conclusions are already published and read, the split must also disclose this design exposure. A meaningful claim would compare the proposed reusable law with the source model/history rival and ask whether its uncertainty changes a specified next-cycle choice. Rejecting an unused scalar model alone has little research value.

## Practical decision and experiment gate

There is no current qualified numerical experiment that I can endorse from this packet alone. For A, the next useful work is to identify a real action choice for which ATP-guided and reproductive-target-guided decisions could differ, then qualify matched observations and state the proposed transferable relation. The present P3 source uses a compartmental model and already observes two alternating cycles, so such reliance cannot be assumed. A source-metadata clarification could make a finite reanalysis possible, but cannot itself create the missing scientific relation or decision.

No additional public request is necessary for this initial judgment. A network request would be worthwhile only for a precise unresolved source fact that changes one of these gates; it should not be spent on generic prior-work expansion here.
