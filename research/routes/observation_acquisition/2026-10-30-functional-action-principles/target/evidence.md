## What changes the decision

MultiVCDiff does not establish paired single-cell multimodal response learning. Its CPgenes construction averages all corresponding L1000 expression profiles per perturbagen/genetic target, then assigns that one vector to every morphology sample associated with the perturbation (STAR Methods, Construction of the CPgenes datasets, p0310). Morphology contexts are MCF7 for BBBC021, U2OS for CDRP/JUMP, A549 for LINCS (p0305). Consequently, perturbation-identity matching alone cannot establish RNA/image agreement at matched dose, time, cell background or biological replicate. Those matching fields are not specified in these inspected construction paragraphs. This is an inference about what the described construction supports; it is NOT a demonstrated failure of all uses of CPgenes. A cross-modal benefit may still be real for predicting a perturbation-level average signature.

The systematic comparison already owns the decomposition into basal state, shared shift, perturbation effect, combination residual and context-specific residual. Its DISCUSSION explicitly calls this an evaluative scaffold rather than a mechanistic linear gene-regulatory model (XML element944; Fig5 caption988). It also already owns the proposal to distinguish basal-state similarity from response conservation across contexts, including four similar/distant × conserved/divergent transfer regimes (1069,1097). Rebranding these diagnostics or adding an Agent narrator would not establish a new capability.

A consequential unresolved question exposed by both originals is whether an inexpensive measured response panel can support a discovery action under correct context/exposure/replicate units when surrogate reconstructions or background similarity are insufficient. This is a question, not a new method or novel candidate. It needs an independently measured task endpoint appropriate to the intended claim. Neither paper supplies inspected evidence of an Agent changing drug-selection actions, saving assays, demonstrating target engagement, durable therapeutic benefit or independently validated efficacy.

## MultiVCDiff: source claims and resource conditions

Original: DOI10.1016/j.crmeth.2026.101459, PMC13390005, fullTextXML received with matching title/DOI/PMCID. Locators below are original paragraph ids, preserved in multivcdiff.passages.txt.

- Source-owned capability: p0155 claims structure/gene-representation-only inference jointly generating transcriptomic and morphological endpoints without measured post-treatment inputs. This is a useful information-access distinction from RNA-conditioned image generation; it is not a functional-efficacy result.
- p0165 states static endpoints (example24h), no explicit dosage or combinatorial encoding, no continuous temporal kinetics. Proposed dose embeddings, aggregate perturbation embeddings for combinations, and biological-prior integration are already author's suggested extensions. A time-stamped endpoint is not a trajectory.
- Chemical perturbations use Morgan fingerprints; genetic perturbations use scGPT target-gene embeddings (p0300). The inspected construction does not establish genetic intervention type, degree/efficiency, target engagement, gene-specific biological replication or matched genetic-versus-chemical exposures. Matching a gene target is not equivalent to matched perturbation mechanism.
- p0300 reports eight NVIDIA A10080GB GPUs,100000 steps,approximately3–4days. This is an author-reported training resource condition, not measured here. Public data availability does not imply economical reproduction of this backbone.
- p0115 describes a held-out chemically divergent drug split for transcriptomic assessment; p0070/p0105 describe morphology-only held-out compounds lacking transcriptomic matches. These are different readout/holdout contracts, not independent functional validation.
- p0125 uses a known compound's annotated MoA and predicted-GO enrichment for biological interpretation. Neither pathway enrichment nor MoA-consistent morphology proves efficacy or causal mechanism. p0160's actionable-hypothesis claim remains a source claim.
- Definition of sample size n, sec8.3.2/p0415: morphology n counts generated or ground-truth samples per condition, while RNA n counts perturbation conditions. This does not establish independent biological assay replication; in particular many generated samples are not new biological replicates. CPgenes RNA averaging makes this distinction consequential for claims about distributional response calibration.
- p0185 gives CPgenes data DOI10.5281/zenodo.19640132 and four upstream public resources. p0190 gives official source URL https://github.com/prsigma/MultiVCDiff and code archive DOI10.5281/zenodo.19639206. Code not acquired or executed.

## Systematic comparison: original diagnostic and data contract

Original: DOI10.1126/sciadv.aed3414, PMC13557097(.1), matching title/DOI/PMCID verified. Many XML paragraphs have no id. Exact locators use section name plus element ordinal in comparison.passages.txt, generated by enumerating ElementTree root.iter() from zero.

- DISCUSSION936–937 separates absolute reconstruction from perturbation-specific changes; small effects can look accurate under basal-expression metrics and remain hard to distinguish from sampling/technical noise.1049 discusses variance compression and distributional calibration for DE validity. These are authors' diagnoses; no published outcome table is reproduced or independently recomputed here.
-1052 and1070 explicitly require evaluation of nonadditive combination residuals beyond additive expectations and control of response magnitude before attributing differences to interaction type. Gene-expression nonadditivity is not drug efficacy synergy under a pharmacological null. Simple additive/linear comparators are already prominent in the original (Results895).
-1069 and Cell state transfer909ff distinguish basal differences from conserved versus rewired perturbation responses. Correlation or close background alone does not identify transportability. These are source-owned explanations/hypotheses; no causal transport proof was inspected.
-1096 notes technology/protocol/batch heterogeneity, insufficient matched controls/replicates/perturbation-efficiency measurement, destructive scRNA-seq and lack of dense time sampling. Models infer population counterfactuals, not paired before/after same-cell trajectories.

- Sample multiplexing1130: KHP HDF/PBMC donors mixed before oligo labeling; two pooled samples per cell-system preparation; cmo pooled at viability-adjusted numbers across treatment groups. - Datasets and preprocessing1157: several drug/immune datasets were reduced to strongest/weakest ranked conditions using TRADE, including in-house datasets. ## Useful openings versus claims already owned

Potentially useful source-grounded openings: (1) preserve the true assay/exposure/context unit across public resources instead of treating perturbation-identity joins as paired biology; (2) determine when a small response panel supports a specified discovery choice rather than transcriptomic reconstruction; (3) distinguish useful transport from basal similarity and support that choice with independent compatible observations. These are directions for inquiry, not selected candidates, validated innovations, or proof of novelty.

Already-owned/routine: joint conditional diffusion, structure-only phenotype inference, dose-embedding and combination-embedding extensions, additive-residual diagnostics, effect-size-aware metrics, distribution-calibration checks, basal-distance versus response-conservation framing, and choosing more informative assays through ordinary decision rules. An Agent requires feedback that changes a concrete discovery action under a fair ordinary rival; this packet establishes none.
