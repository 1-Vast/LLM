## Selection after the archive

The first note's **access-enabling rescue** is not developed further now. Its simplest operational form is a shared potency shift, which directly inherits the functional-abstraction comparison, while generic efflux/access rescue and its adaptation boundary already appear in the archive. It could reopen only with a distinct source-qualified function and a concrete transfer/decision claim beyond re-demonstrating a known sensitizer.

**Readout/function decoupling** stays a useful measurement gate, not the next central discovery mechanism. The 2013 assay-format evidence and the pooled-response analysis already establish the relevant semantic issue. A cheap calibrated counterassay is a mandatory strong rival; adding an Agent does not make calibration a new biological law.

**Demand-dependent selectivity** remains scientifically important but undeveloped. It currently names an objective rather than a new learnable biological restriction, and no matched normal/diseased or host/pathogen exposure resource is qualified here. A tumor-only table cannot be relabelled as a therapeutic-window dataset.

**Decision-relevant functional witnesses** is also not developed as a standalone candidate. In its current generic form it repeats the archive's state-abstraction and ordinary-VOI skeleton. It can be an implementation ingredient only after a specific biological restriction and its discriminating observation exist.

Two questions merit more precise conditional development because they introduce actual interventions or population functions absent from the failed scalar-screen premises: environmental bypass of a necessary function, and transferable protection supplied by another population. Neither is a positive winner, neither has a verified new public resource here, and neither is claimed novel. Their usefulness must survive existing metabolic/chemical-genetic and ecological explanations, respectively.

## Conditional question A: can a functional bypass map identify which otherwise active hits need a route-closing partner?

### Intended knowledge and action

Consider a screening group with a drug that inhibits growth in one defined environment but loses activity when a physiologically plausible nutrient or metabolite is available. The useful decision is whether to discard the hit as environment-fragile, restrict its claimed context, or seek a partner that closes the particular rescue route. A model that merely predicts the second growth score supplies less knowledge than a validated rule saying which environment-drug interaction can be neutralized and when that rule can be reused for another inhibitor of the same necessary function.

This is not a new therapy claim. It is a source-conditional discovery capability. The cheapest conceivable setting is a microbial or yeast growth/rescue screen; an actual public resource, permission, compatible controls and usable preparation identities are not yet established.

### Observation -> operative rule -> new action

The proposed observation is **matched intervention data**: a drug loses activity upon addition of a defined rescue input, and a second perturbation selectively removes that rescue while leaving growth with an intact alternative supply route possible. This is stronger information than two monotherapy curves but can be much smaller than a global drug-by-drug factorial.

The functional virtual cell represents availability of one necessary output through alternative supply routes. In the simplest constructive world let endogenous supply be `S`, external uptake-dependent supply be `U`, and functional growth competence be `S OR U`. Drug `d` disables S, a rescue input enables U, and partner `b` disables U. Then the conditional rule is: **closing U restores d's function-blocking effect when U is the active bypass; b alone can remain functionally innocuous because S remains available.** Feedback changes which route is considered usable and therefore changes the next drug/partner/environment action.

The prospective transfer restriction is deliberately narrower than a universal pathway model: within one stated organism/background, assay, exposure and functional-effect range, drugs independently qualified as blocking the same supply function share the rescue/closure relation for the named input and partner. An empirical rescue signature alone is only a candidate membership signal, not proof that two drugs block the same molecular target. A held-out drug must test that membership and transfer; measured-drug refitting cannot establish it.

The Agent keeps the currently admissible route restrictions and drug membership, then chooses an actual allowed condition that can change the pursuit/discard/partner decision. A contradictory rescue or closure result splits or withdraws the relevant restriction instead of silently improving its regression fit. This is ordinary model criticism and experimental design, not a claimed new algorithm. An LLM is optional and supplies no scientific identification by itself.

### Constructive enabling example and boundary

This table is symbolic reasoning in the explicit two-route world, not data or a numerical test:

| Condition | No rescue input | Rescue input present |
|---|---|---|
| Vehicle | Growth competence from S | Growth competence from S or U |
| d only | Low function because S is blocked | High function because U bypasses S |
| b only | High function because S is available | High function because S is available |
| d+b | Low function | Low function because both S and U are unavailable |

The joint condition distinguishes this world from an alternative in which the added input merely changes drug uptake or chemically inactivates d and b does not reverse that process. The point is not that the OR rule is new; it is that the corresponding joint intervention can discriminate a repairable functional bypass and supply a partner decision unavailable from the drug axis alone.

An equally important boundary is a third usable supply route V: closing U no longer restores drug activity. Other failure boundaries include direct drug destruction by the rescue input, partner effects on d exposure, a baseline change in growth rate, incomparable endpoint normalization, and a rescue input that changes the readout without changing the declared function. Matched functional data may support the conditional predictive rule without uniquely assigning a molecular route. Calling it metabolic mediation would require appropriate additional interventions or measurements. No invariant is implied across unrelated media, species, doses or times.

The joint response cannot be deduced from the two single-agent axes alone. The archive's nonidentification result still applies. At least one actual local rescue-by-partner interaction must be observed before using a route-closure rule, and a separate interaction must remain for its meaningful prediction check. If the only useful decision is one joint condition, directly assaying it may be cheaper than learning a transferable model.

### Strong ordinary rivals

- A known pathway or flux-balance model with the same usable nutrient/route annotations and constraints.
- Ordinary chemical-genetic rescue/epistasis rules and standard synergy or dose-response-shift analysis on the same matched screen.
- A hierarchical interaction model that receives the same drug/environment/partner labels and previous joint feedback.
- A fixed rescue/closure diagnostic panel and direct measurement of the target combination, including all controls and preparation costs.

General-model expressibility does not refute an empirical relation. However, if the only content is the known S-OR-U logic applied to already annotated drugs, this is ordinary assay-guided follow-up rather than an innovation. A plausible substantive contribution would have to be an experimentally supported, bounded reuse rule that predicts which previously unmeasured hit/partner decisions succeed or fail, together with a real limit case and useful cost or coverage. Those are requirements, not present results.

### Smallest actual-source check

First inspect one original microbial/yeast rescue-screen Methods and one corresponding data dictionary or file header for: exact medium/input identity, compound and partner identity, simultaneous versus sequential exposure, a local drug x input x partner crossing, functional endpoint/control denominator, and independent preparation versus technical-well IDs. Do not read response values at this gate. A simple local crossing is sufficient for the local question; a full population-wide factorial is not.

Next identify one withheld drug or context with the same qualified endpoint and a real joint outcome that could falsify the shared restriction. If only monotherapy-by-medium data exist, limit the possible task to environmental fragility; do not claim partner selection. If the original paper already demonstrates the exact reuse rule and action, treat it as prior or a baseline. If the crossing or usable response resource is unavailable, park this public-data route without declaring the biology false. This assignment has not performed that source check.

## Conditional question B: can a function supplied by neighboring cells explain a drug-screen failure and identify a protection-blocking partner?

### Intended knowledge and action

A monoculture-active drug can fail in a mixed population even if the target cells themselves have not acquired intrinsic resistance. The useful discovery action is to identify a partner that removes the relevant protection, potentially without requiring killing the protecting population. This differs from the archive's stable latent-subtype overlap question: one population now changes another population's exposure or support, so cell-independent killing hazards are no longer the intended biological restriction.

The economical setting could be a microbial coculture and conditioned-medium screen. That is a possible source category, not verified available data. A local target-specific functional readout can be sufficient for the local question; molecular omics or permanent lineage tracing is not a universal requirement.

### Observation -> operative rule -> new action

The necessary observation is target-population function after drug exposure under a matched neighbor/medium manipulation. Bulk growth alone is insufficient when a surviving neighbor can replace killed target biomass. A source must distinguish target function, such as a qualified target-selective colony count or a validated target-specific signal, and control whether the medium manipulation changes the readout.

In a constructive world the neighbor produces an extracellular drug-inactivating activity. The target is sensitive in fresh medium, survives drug exposure in appropriately controlled neighbor-conditioned medium, and loses that protection when a nonlethal activity-blocking partner is added. The operative virtual-cell restriction is that the target's drug response depends on the particular supplied protection function, within a named exposure window, rather than on neighbor identity as an uninterpreted label. The Agent uses a failed prediction to test transferability of that function or select a partner that removes it.

A possible empirical reuse restriction is local and explicit: for a fixed target/drug/protocol, neighbor preparations matched on a validated functional drug-protection assay produce equivalent target responses within a prespecified tolerance, and a partner's effect on that protection function predicts the direction of its target-rescue reversal. This needs an actual calibrated functional assay. Merely matching bulk growth, a molecular marker, or a model embedding does not establish the restriction. Transfer to another drug needs its own scope and test.

### Constructive enabling example and boundary

In the extracellular-inactivation world, a partner that blocks the inactivating activity can restore target susceptibility even if it does not inhibit either monoculture's growth. A screen ranking partners solely by their own cytotoxicity would miss that action. This is a genuinely different action criterion, although the biological example and inhibitor concept have extensive prior art and are not claimed new here.

A rival world gives identical monoculture growth and the same bulk mixed endpoint because the target is killed and the neighbor fills the population. Target-specific endpoint measurement separates that world from actual target protection. Another rival world has live-neighbor contact-dependent protection and will not necessarily reproduce in cell-free conditioned medium. A transferred-medium result has its own alternatives: residual live cells, nutrient changes, pH, pre-existing drug products and chemical inhibition of the reporter. These are hypotheses to address with source-qualified controls, not an assertion that any existing study is invalid.

Conditioned-medium transfer can demonstrate a transferable protective activity under the defined protocol without identifying its molecule, proving durable resistance, or guaranteeing the same effect in a growing coculture. A target-only assay's relative label also must not be confused with an absolute target burden unless its calibration supports that interpretation. The archive's pooled-scale warning therefore remains active where the source uses compositional counts.

### Strong ordinary rivals and source gate

Ordinary ecological interaction/growth models, extracellular-drug depletion models, known beta-lactamase/efflux or support-factor inhibitors, standard conditioned-medium follow-up, and a direct combination screen are mandatory rivals as applicable to the source. No new algorithm follows from adding a protection coordinate or an Agent that asks the next condition. The unresolved contribution would be whether a small measured functional protection coordinate actually supports useful new target/partner decisions across a declared set of preparations and where it fails.

The smallest source check is one original coculture/conditioned-medium drug screen with metadata for target-specific versus bulk endpoint, starting composition, conditioning and filtering/transfer protocol, drug/partner timing, matched vehicle/nutrient controls, and preparation identities. The next check is whether at least one unconsumed joint target outcome exists to test the proposed reuse relation. A public mono-culture drug table, ecological abundance survey or only total mixed OD does not qualify this particular question. No such resource has been verified here; do not start a data-mining benchmark from an incompatible table.
