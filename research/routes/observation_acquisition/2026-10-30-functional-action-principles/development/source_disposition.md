## The exact question worth challenging

For one source-qualified organism/background, assay endpoint and exposure window: **can a response pattern measured using an ordinary drug plus a defined environmental rescue input identify which previously unmeasured drug–partner combinations recover the desired functional inhibition?** The practical action is partner follow-up for an environment-fragile hit, versus direct combination measurement or abandonment of that reuse rule.

The indispensable empirical restriction is not the established `S OR U` pathway logic. It is **prospective reuse of a local rescue/closure relation across drugs from information that excludes their target joint outcomes**. Drug membership must be frozen from allowed calibration observations/annotations before the withheld drug–partner response is read. For example, comparable baseline and rescue effects could nominate membership; they cannot establish it. The operative prediction is that the named partner removes rescue for that held-out member under the same qualified protocol, within a predeclared endpoint tolerance. One observed drug–input–partner closure can train the local rule; a different drug's actual joint outcome must be reserved to challenge reuse. A known target label is optional rather than an automatic requirement for all functional work.

This is one substantive tightening of A's after-archive transfer question, not a new algorithm or an empirical discovery. A positive example would distinguish a useful functional membership rule from a direct retest of an already measured pair. A boundary example is a second compound with the same calibration rescue pattern because the input chemically inactivates the drug: the partner may fail to reverse it. That counterexample would withdraw/split the reuse rule and change which combinations the Agent recommends. Neither outcome identifies molecular mediation from the endpoint alone.

The Agent's potential role is to choose a decision-changing joint condition and revise this explicit reuse restriction from feedback. Standard experimental design can implement that loop. Fixed diagnostic panels, direct target-combination assays, a same-information hierarchical drug–environment–partner model, known-pathway models with the same annotations, and standard rescue/chemical-genetic methods must receive equal observations and total costs. If they already supply the same useful capability, or if calibration costs exceed directly measuring the relevant combinations, there is no demonstrated extra Agent contribution. Ordinary mathematical expressibility by itself is not a rejection of the empirical restriction.

## Admission evidence still missing

1. **Source eligibility:** an actual local drug x defined input x partner crossing, its matched controls and timing, compatible endpoint normalization and dose units, and the distinction between independent preparations and technical repeats. A global factorial, omics or molecular mediation assay is not mandatory for the local functional claim.
2. **A falsifiable reuse target:** an additional source-supported drug/condition with a joint functional outcome that can remain outside rule fitting and acquisition. Drug aliases, related chemical structures, prior published findings and response-based condition selection must be recorded. A known published outcome makes later reanalysis exploratory, not untouched confirmation.
3. **Scientific and practical prior:** whether the source or closest metabolic/chemical-genetic work already establishes this exact cross-drug rescue/closure reuse or the proposed partner action. Generic OR logic, environmental sensitivity, conventional beta-lactamase inhibition or a new Agent label are insufficient novelty. Neither a positive nor negative exact-prior conclusion is established here.
4. **Test contract:** an explicit endpoint-specific predictive contrast/tolerance and discovery decision, compatible calibration/diagnostic/evaluation roles, meaningful uncertainty at the actual replicate level, and a direct/fixed ordinary rival. The role of the Agent must be tested separately from whether the biological restriction holds. No numerical threshold, sample size, savings or independent replication is invented now.
