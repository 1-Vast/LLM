## Findings first

The concrete screening problem is choosing compounds and doses that cause net population decline or death rather than merely slowing expansion. A low normalized endpoint count can represent different division/death processes and changes with assay stopping time. However, inferring division/death or plasticity from ordinary longitudinal counts is already an explicit target-field capability. Neither that object nor a generic active learning loop is new here.

The strongest incremental evidence in this worker pass is a 2025 original not named in the frozen brief: **A statistical framework for detecting therapy-induced resistance from drug screens**, DOI10.1038/s41540-025-00560-8, published6August2025. It already fits a multitime-point bulk-count vector using a branching-process covariance plus independent observation noise, and uses AIC to compare plasticity and no-plasticity explanations. This directly threatens a claimed innovation based on adding temporal process covariance, separating independent measurement noise, or selecting among kinetic explanations. It does not establish arbitrary-noise identifiability or effective active acquisition.

BESTDR and HTS001 were initially rediscovered in this pass, then correctly identified by the coordinator as already investigated in the unfinished `../2026-10-29-functional-joint-discovery/` episode. **They are reused archive evidence, not new discoveries.** Its metadata qualification is materially stronger than public deposit prose: seven cell backgrounds ×14 drugs plus controls, physical doses in M, two technical wells per noncontrol condition, longitudinal trajectories, missing experiment identifiers. No new count/noise/rate qualification was performed here.

A useful remaining question is therefore narrower and falsifiable: *does a permitted additional ordinary observation change a consequential compound/dose decision by resolving uncertainty that remains after an honest full-history kinetic refit and simple net-growth analysis?* The inspected evidence motivates this question but supplies neither a new algorithm nor a demonstrated positive answer. It may end as a measurement-boundary/negative result.

## Concrete workflow pain and current alternatives

User: a preclinical screening lab with inexpensive already-collected cell counts or luminescent viability and limited follow-up wells. Workflow: compare candidate compounds/doses across backgrounds; decide which deserves dose refinement or orthogonal death/lineage follow-up. Documented pain: endpoint viability depends on time; proliferation suppression and death are different population effects; plate artifacts and measurement noise can distort inferred differences. Proposed practical output: a dose/compound retain, reject or abstain decision, with one precisely costed next observation if genuinely consequential. Clinical efficacy, resistance eradication and target mechanism are not established outputs of ordinary counts.

Current alternatives are strong and should not be weakened: direct full continuous response at the desired target; whole observed dose/time curves; Thunor DIP/net-growth fits after a stabilization window; BESTDR full-history hierarchical rate fitting; Wu et al. whole-trajectory covariance likelihood plus model selection; ordinary Bayesian/model-discrimination acquisition; and extra independent lineage/death measurement when the decision actually requires it. A net-growth decision may already suffice without separately identifying birth/death. If so, mechanistic ambiguity has no demonstrated decision cost.

## Closest-prior threats and archive matching

1. **Kinetic latent objects:** BESTDR already separates rate mechanisms using mean/variance and hierarchical sharing. Wu2025 already uses the full multitime covariance and observation noise plus AIC. A covariance-aware update alone is therefore an implementation of a known principle unless a distinct operation and scientific consequence are demonstrated.
2. **Noise identification:** archive functional-joint-discovery explicitly raised observation error and read older pinned BESTDR code; formal appendix was blocked. Do not convert that version-specific static-code reading into a defect claim against the formal method. Wu2025 strengthens the ordinary full-trajectory rival.
3. **Same-price direct targets:** H_FI's trained reference mapping failed its training admission against continuous direct-target comparators. One reference cannot gain by impoverishing direct measurements. Whole control/training/repeat costs matter.
4. **Dose transport / local interaction:** A.v1 is shared potency/curve registration; H_L lost to full-history additive regression. Merely replacing fitted IC50 by raw timepoints does not create a new transfer principle.
5. **Molecular alias boundaries:** B2 is already conditional robust model discrimination with strong target-deconvolution priors; no single-action witness does not imply joint-menu impossibility. Kinetic model selection needs the same distinction.
6. **Expensive functional state / rescue:** prior pharmacology-structure-extension found missing paired rescue/functional design. nELISA and CellHit do not fill that hole: they measure other systems/endpoints, not the missing same-sample rescue experiment.

## Feasible decisive uncertainty

The smallest useful next step is conditional, not an endorsed experiment. Freeze a compound/dose decision that genuinely differs between net-growth and kinetic explanations, then state which legal count/time/repeat observation can change it. Compare a new operation against same-information full-history BESTDR, a Wu-style covariance likelihood, simple DIP/net-growth and ordinary sequential design. Include direct target measurements and all learned-rule training/audit costs. Count intervals are not independent cultures; drug replenishment and early delay cannot be silently absorbed into stationarity.

An empirical necessary test on HTS001 may assess whether the proposed uncertainty decomposition or next-observation choice survives two technical wells and plate/cell confounding, without claiming molecular death truth. The existing metadata qualifies time triplets but not independent segmentation-error replicates, independent culture noise, or a separate endpoint. If the proposed rule needs those absent units, stop at a data-support failure. If kinetic decisions never differ from net-growth decisions, stop at no consequential capability. If ordinary covariance-aware refitting makes the same choice with the same data, record a useful negative/incremental result rather than rename it co-learning. No test was frozen or executed by this worker.
