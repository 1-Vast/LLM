## A useful biological question, with a known simple learner

A drug-screening team can know how each drug changes total live-cell count and still not know whether a pair removes the same cells or complementary cells. The developed [conditional packet](conditional_v1_1_frozen.md), read with its [scope corrections](assessment_clarifications.md), makes that distinction exact. In a balanced, stable two-subtype construction, the entire joint history of any adaptive monotherapy-only policy has the same law in two worlds, while their pair-exposure outcomes differ. This is a full-menu coupling argument, not an inference from identical single-action marginals.

With within-sensitive-type survival 1/4, the two worlds both have single-pulse expected survival 5/8, but two-drug expected survival is respectively **17/32=0.53125** and **1/4=0.25**. A prespecified expected-survival threshold of0.30 therefore changes the pair-retention decision. The example is mathematical, not a simulated or observed drug result. It assumes equal stable subtype fractions, additive killing hazards, no growth/conversion, a qualified switching protocol and no latent-label anchors in the monotherapy menu.

For D active drugs, known marginals and exact noiseless pair means, the shared object is a rank-one sign matrix of relative susceptibilities. **D−1 connected pair conditions** identify all pair means; this is not a count of physical wells. An elementary signed-graph learner already implements the update and acquisition. A direct continuous measurement of the sole target pair may be cheaper. This exact simple rival, the untested biological structure and the missing resource contract stop promotion of this line as a new co-learning algorithm.

The smallest overidentifying test after learning two pair edges is an untouched third edge of a three-drug triangle. Its residual is a relation among expected means, with paid marginal/pair-estimation uncertainty propagated. A fully calibrated prediction for a new noisy well requires additional process/measurement calibration. Current metadata do not supply a justified margin, power or physical cost, so none is invented. The [fresh initial review](challenger/joint_initial_review.md), [version clarification](challenger/joint_v1_1_review.md) and [resolution review](challenger/joint_resolution_review.md) retain all objections and their disposition.
