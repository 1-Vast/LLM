# Data gate for a paper-inspired virtual-cell loop

The paper suggests a closed loop, but its own perturbation matrix is not currently an open reproduction resource. A candidate dataset must expose the same condition identity at the proxy and functional levels: drug, cell or donor/background, dose, exposure time, vehicle, preparation/plate, independent replicate, proxy readout, and an independent function endpoint. A target or genetic reference is required when the claim uses mechanism or intervention equivalence. Technical duplicate counts cannot substitute for independent preparations.

| Resource | What is locally verified | Gate result for the proposed loop |
|---|---|---|
| You et al. 2026, OMIX017117 | 110-compound PBMC perturbation accession; official page snapshot says controlled-access and not currently downloadable. T-cell follow-up is described, but exact drug list, dose, donor/well pairing and raw values were not recovered. | Design reference only; no reproduction or effect test admitted. |
| Rhaister 2026 Emerald Bay | 52 cell lines and 93 conditions in a released identity summary; 19 non-control drug names, literal µM conditions, five-day pooled cell-proportion readout. Sample/plate/preparation identifiers and an independent function endpoint are absent from the inspected summary. | Economical response-prediction baseline and relative-response task only; not a proxy-to-independent-function loop. |
| eLife 2015 RPPA/phenotype resource | One SkMel-133 background, 12 drugs and 89 nominal conditions; 24-hour RPPA and 72-hour resazurin endpoint. Source text reports three biological RPPA replicates and triplicate cell-cycle assays. Four downstream RPPA readouts are shared by two chemical interventions, but target labels are one-to-one with compounds, the condition table has a missing index, and raw pairing/scale and independent functional repeat structure remain incomplete. | A retrospective contract check or bounded descriptive test may be possible; no Agent savings, transfer or mechanism claim is admitted. |
| LINCS/L1000 joined to PRISM/GDSC | Existing project checks find nominal drug/cell joins, but collection-specific exposure, dose, time, preparation and endpoint semantics are not automatically matched. | Do not treat cross-database joins as paired proxy/function evidence without a row-level contract. |

The practical consequence is that the paper supplies a **design pattern**, while a qualified cheap public matrix is still the limiting resource. If no row-level paired resource passes the gate, stop at an information audit and do not train or promote a mechanism.
