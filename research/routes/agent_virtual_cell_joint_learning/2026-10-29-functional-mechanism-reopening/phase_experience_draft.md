# Lessons from common functional-screen research

**Scope and status.** This is a research handoff for the 2026-10-28/29 common, inexpensive public functional-screen program, synthesized from the linked episode records. The objective remains **ACTIVE / NOT_ACHIEVED**: no new Agent–virtual-cell co-learning algorithm, biological mechanism, independent drug-discovery benefit, or real measurement saving has been established. Ordinary cell, drug, physical dose and functional response are legitimate inputs for bounded prediction; expensive omics, imaging or genetic rescue are not universal prerequisites. Claims about mechanism, independent function or actual cost require the corresponding additional evidence. The sources below retain detailed protocols, exposures, failures and corrections; they are not replaced by this report.

## What information can change a decision?

An observation can be informative without paying for itself. In the [C6 paid-feedback study](../2026-10-29-paid-feedback-reuse/report.md), six initially high-priority curves served as both candidate output and feedback for seven later choices. On 243 model-unseen but historically exposed development cell IDs, with 566 training IDs and 13 attempts per cell, the rank-8 strategy had candidate-count identification bounds 7.024691–8.234568. The same-first-six blind continuation had 6.967078–8.181070; static global and tissue lists had 6.897119–8.111111 and 7.131687–8.341564. The endpoint was a finite fitted LN_IC50 within the recorded concentration window and <=0, a fitted candidate proxy rather than verified killing. Unknown attempted results remain unknown, not negative.

The paired lower-bound improvements over global, tissue and blind were 0.020576, -0.213992 and 0.008230 candidates per cell, respectively; their 95% SIDM bootstrap intervals were [-0.078189, 0.111111], [-0.312757, -0.123457] and [-0.082305, 0.098765]. Common unknowns cancel in the paired difference bounds; these bounds are not obtained by subtracting the two marginal candidate bounds. None met the prespecified >=0.5 and positive lower confidence-bound gates. Feedback changed the chosen set for 204/243 cells, but changing actions did not establish useful gain. These intervals concern the exposed development archive and do not account for research selection or upstream fitting. Stop this fixed 6+7 claim, not all possible uses of feedback. An independently written calculation reproduced the saved results from the same source; it is computational verification, not a new biological replicate.

The same distinction appears in [functional abstraction A.v1](../2026-10-29-functional-abstraction-discovery/report.md). A constructed shared dose-scaling versus amplitude-change example permits the hypotheses to be distinguished, but a fixed top-k menu can still have zero expected decision value before cost. In an ideal positive two-parameter curve, two distinct doses identify intercept and slope of `1/y` versus `1/x`; separating dose scale requires a calibrated reference K. Low-dose approximate degeneracy and saturation make precision a practical issue. No real shared transformation, cost saving or experimental result was established. Standard hierarchical curves, full-history updating and target-directed value of information already implement the stated operations. The fixed example does not rule out multi-step value in another task.

**Decision rule:** freeze the final decision, menu, loss, observation and cost before feedback. Compare the same information, not merely model names; distinguish information gained, optimization behavior and downstream decision benefit. A positive point estimate, changed action, or improved fit to the measured curve alone is not evidence of an independent benefit.

## Why direct targets and continuous values matter

In [B1](../2026-10-29-cheap-structure-function/report.md), a structure-dependent choice of a reference cell was motivated by a construction where the best reference changes with a free category. Yet for one directly measurable target at equal price, observing the target itself dominates the indirect reference in that idealized label model. With noisy measurement of a latent target, direct measurement must instead be compared at equal cost, noise and replication. A real reason such as inaccessible target, lower reference cost, faster turnaround or one reference serving several targets is needed before claiming savings. Ordinary conditional feature acquisition already implements the proposed choice; the construction does not establish a new algorithm.

The [assay-relation construction](../2026-10-29-assay-relation-identification/report.md) shows why a multi-target exception can exist. Four equally likely reference regions can encode two binary target labels, while either directly measured target gives only one bit under the stipulated distributions and error <=0.04. If a stable mapping is learned with paid linked target observations, a feasible policy costs `Q + 2K_Q <= Q + 8` versus `2Q` direct measurements for Q backgrounds; this idealized bound excludes qualification, exceptions, controls and failed measurements. For a fixed rule, eight region/target error checks each with 49 independent zero-error cases satisfy `8 * 0.9^49 = 0.04581134 <= 0.05`; an ideal balanced design requires 196 reference and 392 target readings, before other costs. This is a conditional union-bound calculation, not a universal sample lower bound or a posterior probability that the rule is true. Ordinary joint models and conditional acquisition can also learn and reuse the mapping.

The subsequent [H_FI pilot](../2026-10-29-functional-index-pilot/report.md) tested the necessary empirical premise with one complete continuous exported value per action, rather than weakening direct measurements to binary labels. From 732 cross-supported cell IDs, 529 were training and 203 development. On 55 drug pairs, only three met the four-state >=10 training support gate, yielding 27 non-target reference candidates. The selected `(a,b,q)=(2426,2428,2431)` had 430/27/45/27 training states. Across 1,058 training out-of-fold target labels, reference made 115 errors, direct-a 54, direct-b 65 and blind 126. The minimum reference improvement was -61/1058 = -0.057655954631379965. Model and triple selection used these same OOF results, so this is a training admission criterion, not an unbiased effect estimate. The specified training precondition failed: **development was not scored**, the 1,999 bootstrap draws and development effect/interval were not run, and H_FI is `not_evaluated_on_development`. The 203 development IDs were parsed for QC and are exposed, although not used for selection or scoring. Do not call this a negative held-out trial or make it fresh confirmation.

**Decision rule:** always offer a same-cost direct target, including its full continuous value as input for other targets, plus a two-target direct benchmark at its actual higher cost. A multi-target reference must earn its learning, audit and deployment costs against strong ordinary joint and acquisition methods; task-specific positive constructions do not establish real support.

## Which observed relation survived strong comparators?

[G1 conditional response geometry](../2026-10-29-conditional-response-geometry/report.md) examined whether a free drug category should select two cells for prediction of relative responses across eight cells. It used 125 training and 48 historically exposed development drug conditions in the GDSC2 fitted table, with 18 category clusters. All methods received the same measured pair difference; the experiment did not establish that discarding the absolute pair mean is sufficient for a real two-curve workflow. Normalized mean losses were 1.030736111 for the global-shape fixed pair B0, 1.021238650 for conditional-shape fixed pair B1, and 1.014232347 for category-selected C. C improved on B0 by 0.016503764, interval [-0.060953292, 0.084451615], and on B1 by 0.007006303, interval [-0.051072786, 0.065227691]. Both prespecified category-resampling gates failed; intervals are stability summaries for fixed development errors, not independent biological confidence intervals. The result is unresolved, not proof that geometry is absent. Same-CSV independent implementations agreed numerically.

The [local-relation pilot](../2026-10-29-local-relation-pilot/report.md) used a fixed 20,000-row GDSC007 export prefix: 342 directed cell pairs, 3,582 leave-one-drug tasks and 11 drugs. Given the source curve and one fixed target-dose feedback, dose-local interaction L had drug-equal decision loss 0.1420083 versus 0.1301313 for full-history additive regression A; A-L = -0.0118770, with prespecified 2,000-drug-resampling interval [-0.0230697, -0.0040388]. L also lost to the primary strong comparator despite beating the simpler T/C/D diagnostics. This is a valid negative for the fixed prefix, representation, regularization, endpoint and one-query setup; the interval describes drug-composition sensitivity, not population biological uncertainty. Independent reconstruction of the same source agreed on all 17,910 method choices. Neither A nor L thereby acquired independent discovery utility.

**Decision rule:** do not reopen these exact claims by scanning dose menus, thresholds, seeds or model families after seeing outcomes. A changed question needs a stated reason, frozen inputs and a prediction that discriminates it from full-history refitting and ordinary conditional acquisition.

## Physical measurement, export rows and qualification

A table row, deduplicated coordinate, attempted curve and physical well are different accounting units. The [GDSC007 qualification](../2026-10-29-functional-index-pilot/qualification_sources.md) identifies Figshare v2 and member `combo_well_stats_GDSC_007_05May22.csv`. The containing ZIP has SHA256 `e8cb50f6ce860fa17586a555130cb3b019f6011ed1976c0f0d3e00fb072d3259`; the member's SHA256 is unknown. The member contains 1,013,803 rows, 733 project-local cell identifiers, 11 drugs and 8,058 project/cell/drug groups. Of the H_FI selected values, 8,052 coordinates appeared 56,364 times, exactly seven export references per coordinate; these are copies, not seven independently cultured measurements. `ref` selection was lexicographic within each cell/drug; it does not establish interchangeable replicates. The paper-level protocol reports 24-hour preculture and 72-hour treatment with CTG2.0 luminescence, while a glossary fluorescence wording conflicts; per-coordinate timing, QC and source-well identity remain unverified. The exported `libN_y` is an inhibition output, not a raw plate-reader truth.

The separate [GDSC2 fitted table](../2026-10-29-conditional-response-geometry/report.md) is Figshare v1 `GDSC2_fitted_dose_response_25Feb20.csv`, 20,355,833 bytes, SHA256 `0883b3ef6e440603094c43c74044e1d333fce61e92e3efa374ea3a54f0b50302`. Fitted IC50/AUC cannot reconstruct missing raw dose points, independent cultures or the original cost. Shared fitting, extrapolation, time and replicate provenance constrain interpretation. A functional label can support the declared fitted-label task without proving cell death, selectivity or target mechanism. ATP/MTS and cell-count endpoints can differ by drug and cell context; see the [2013 assay comparison](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0063583) and the limited reading in [source notes](neutral_sources.md).

Physical accounting must include reference and target wells, controls, repeats, failed attempts, plate/batch setup, turnaround, and all data used to learn and audit the rule. H_FI actually searched 529 x 11 = 5,819 training coordinates; a retrospective three-column minimum for a preselected reference is not that search cost. No study here establishes a conversion from export coordinates to saved physical wells or money. Source-well identity, time, controls, independent culture, dose/exposure matching, noise and independent endpoint remain explicit qualification tasks, not invented exclusions from ordinary prediction.

## Transfer assumptions and candidate ancestry

The [multiscale analysis](../2026-10-29-multiscale-common-function/report.md) used H-Net, DC-DiT and SimMLM as inspiration, with important source assumptions: the first two process already available fine inputs, while SimMLM assumes complete training modalities and randomized missingness. Saving computation is not saving a paid assay; selective missingness is not random modality dropout. Dose changes intervention strength, not image scale, and cell lines are not a tissue hierarchy. Its S1 dose/retest/stop proposal reduced under its specified bounded-noise formulation to robust sequential design; its proposed eight-well, two-batch test was never made executable or run. Retesting does not necessarily shrink a worst-case bound, and per-well intervals do not automatically give sequential coverage.

The [transfer-branches record](../2026-10-29-transfer-branches/report.md) developed E.v1 after the local-relation negative: the same chemical edit might transfer a two-cell functional contrast across parent molecules. Four observations define a cell-by-edit interaction; ordinary interaction or hierarchical regression and model trees can express the proposed sharing and exceptions. The later [edit support audit](../2026-10-29-edit-support-audit/report.md) found exact-name structure matches for only 1 of 11 drugs in the chosen small metadata table, giving one mapped molecule and zero edit edges. This shows a mapping coverage gap, not that the other ten have no public structures or that the scientific hypothesis is false. The independently written audit exited before reading the well member because `GDSC007` did not match `GDSC_007`; its recomputation is incomplete. This engineering failure is distinct from the main metadata result and from a biological negative.

The [reopening neutral initial](neutral_initial.md) revisited a shared dose mapping across cells and also considered distinguishable intervention programs and paired-assay measurement mappings, all untested in this reopening. The first is the existing shared-potency functional question, not a new candidate or independent branch: the old A.v1 form `f_cd(z)=A_c*z/(s_c*K_d+z)` yields `T_ab(z)=(K_b/K_a)*z`. Its conditional functional question remains available for study against standard relative-potency, curve-registration and multi-output comparators, with held-out cells. Distinguishing intervention programs requires a credible dose-specific drug dictionary and actual discriminating drugs; paired-assay mapping requires independently traceable matched endpoints. A mapping fitted separately in each cell is not evidence of shared function, and a shared function does not identify molecular mechanism. Target annotations are hypotheses, not verified target engagement. Ring consistency constructed from a common reference can be algebraic rather than independent evidence. The [source notes](neutral_sources.md) document partial readings and current novelty gaps; these questions are not a selected winner or proof of novelty.

## How to restart without losing the evidence

1. Audit ordinary public data first: link drug identity and structure with provenance; identify actual dose, time, endpoint, controls, source-well and repeat units; distinguish matched observations from copied export rows. Missing qualification means unknown or parked, not a negative biological result.
2. Choose one concrete future decision and independently assess whether the needed cross-cell, cross-drug or paired-endpoint support exists. Freeze selection, exposed versus held-out roles, matched physical costs, strong direct/full-continuous baselines, error tolerance and stopping rule before outcome access. Historically exposed data cannot become fresh confirmation by relabeling.
3. For E.v1, recover traceable identities for the 11 drugs and independently audit repeated edits across different parents and compatible functional rectangles. For A.v1, qualify raw multi-dose measurements and test propagation to drugs not used for calibration. For its related shared-potency question, check same-pair multi-dose support across cells and compare standard relative potency and curve registration. For intervention dictionaries or assay mapping, first find credible discriminating interventions or matched independent endpoints. These are concrete questions, not authorized experiments in this organization pass.
4. Separate a data-support result, a completed but unsuccessful hypothesis test, an implementation failure, and an unrun or inconclusive test. Freeze a new test only when it can distinguish an explanation or deliver a measurable decision advantage; if the claim is algorithmic, specify the operation or capability that the same-information full-refit baseline cannot reproduce.
5. Evaluate generalization at the intended unit: unseen cell, drug, scaffold, assay or independent culture are different splits. A second implementation of the same measurements verifies calculations, while an independent endpoint or culture addresses a different scientific claim. Record both the observed result and its uncertainty, and do not infer a mechanism from a functional association.

Historical research/action caps in individual reports describe their actual use; the current [reopening record](record.md) supersedes those caps for future authorized research and lawful downloads. It does not turn old results into new measurements or authorize using protected material. This synthesis itself performs no network access, experiment, model training or response-data reading.
