# Independent initial scientific/design assessment

Recorded 2026-10-30 before communication with the developer/proposer. Reviewed protocol_v1.md, original article XML evidence/active_mechanistic.body (especially Par18–19 and Par57–60), literal evidence/cxcl9_metadata.body and evidence/schema_only.json. No response-valued raw file was decoded or inspected; no experiment, target selection, author-code modification or network request was performed. Effective model/settings are not independently observable; requested Sol/high is not claimed as verified.

## Judgment

Control-only processing may proceed. The proposed target evaluation is feasible and worth one bounded exploratory execution **as an empirical MFI prediction/restriction audit**, provided the clarifications below are frozen before target decoding. It can compare the stated common conditional gain against additive response, constant absolute interaction and unchanged untreated response. It cannot distinguish that algebraic restriction from an ordinary proportional-response/low-rank model, or identify a biological amplification mechanism rather than known nonlinear biology/readout. This is not certification of the overall research objective or a positive result.

No critical information leak is apparent in the declared split: untreated joint controls legitimately calibrate each preparation, while all inhibitor joint targets remain excluded. The source directions are already exposed, so this is not a blind confirmation. Four preparations with local calibration are not four independent biological contexts or unseen-preparation transfer. All five interventions must stay in the report, including apparent null and strong suppression conditions.

## Precise objections and resolvable corrections

1. **Nonlinear fluorescence is not linear concentration.** Par59 describes a sandwich capture-bead assay, not a linear secretion standard curve; Par60's 0.8 cap prevents saturation of a logic-model state and is not evidence that the physical immunoassay avoids saturation. A common nonlinear assay map can violate or approximate a common-gain law even with ordinary latent secretion biology. A single cytokine dose per line and no assay standard curve do not identify either explanation. Affected claims: “cytokine amplification,” biological restriction, distinguishing known nonlinear biology. Correction: explicitly call this conditional amplification *of background-corrected MFI* throughout, state that B_add/B_const comparisons do not resolve nonlinear biology/readout, and do not interpret a failure as disproving a concentration-level law. Do not add an arbitrary fitted Hill exponent or tune a transformation from target responses.

2. **Ordinary proportional response is algebraically the hypothesis.** With D_d=y10d−y00d, N_d=y11d−y01d, H_gain says N_d/D_d=N0/D0 (where division exists), equivalently D0*N_d−N0*D_d=0, a rank-one cross-product restriction. A proportional inhibition/readout model therefore is an ordinary explanation of any success, not an omitted distinct method that can be defeated by this prediction. When TNF-only offsets are negligible, a baseline-corrected fractional IFNG response transferred to the joint response is the same or nearly the same predictor. Affected claims: mechanistic/algorithmic originality or evidence against generic low-rank response. Correction: report this equivalence and use the cross-product interpretation even when D_d≤0; do not invent a gain for each inhibitor. Existing B_const is the critical distinct comparator: its difference from H_gain is correctly derived in the protocol. B_add/B_control also protect against easy null cases.

3. **Discrimination can be absent before any targets are revealed.** Small I0 or D_d≈D0 makes H_gain and B_const coincide; strong IFNG suppression can also make trivial low predictions easy. Affected claim: a small mean error “supports common gain over constant interaction.” Correction: retain and report all frozen contrasts, model errors and pairwise error differences by drug and preparation. If contrasts are small relative to propagated technical envelopes, label the comparison uninformative, without choosing a favorable subgroup or a new numeric threshold. Do not claim that beating B_add alone demonstrates inhibitor-invariant gain.

4. **The word “already bead-gated” is stronger than the available schema proves.** Par60 requires APC-based gating; exported files expose only PE. Original metadata says each row is a FACS measurement and names settings/passages, but does not itself document which gate was exported. Affected claim: exact reproduction of source gating. Correction: identify PE exports as presumed capture-bead-selected according to source processing, with gate provenance not independently reconstructible from these files. The empirical audit remains admissible on the exports; it is not a fresh gating verification. IQR trimming then arithmetic mean is consistent with Par60; NumPy's exact quantile convention is an explicitly chosen reproducible convention, not something established by the article.

5. **Calibration and technical envelopes need literal reporting, not qualification by favorable performance.** Dlow>0 and Nlow>0 are reasonable prespecified gates ensuring an interpretable positive control gain, not biological law tests. A weak denominator can yield a broad finite gain range despite satisfying >0. Correction: report the numerical control denominator and gain envelope at the checkpoint, freeze qualification from those gates only, and retain failures as calibration failures. Use all endpoint combinations for interval products, particularly when an inhibitor IFNG contrast crosses zero; do not multiply only matching low/low and high/high endpoints. A wide or non-discriminating interval is a result, not a reason to fit/clip/refine the model.

6. **Biological/technical hierarchy is correctly restricted, but source statistical results must not become extra validation.** Par18/Fig1 describe two independent wet-lab screenings with technical duplicates; metadata maps A/B to P12/P14 within each line, and schema has 24 conditions × 2 named wells per file. PE event counts are bead counts, not cells or cultures. Par19 explicitly combines technical and biological replicates for its qualitative synergy analysis and already reports inhibitor-specific loss of synergy. Affected claims: population inference, independent discovery, cell-number-adjusted secretion, cell-line effects. Correction: preserve preparation-level descriptive reporting; describe the exposed inhibitor-specific synergy as prior knowledge rather than an independent outcome. Different cytokine doses (Par57; schema 1 vs 10 ng/mL) preclude attributing between-line differences solely to cell identity.

## Normalization, offsets and scope checked

The proposed formulas subtract the matched inhibitor baseline y00d and use matched TNF-only offset y01d; they do not silently substitute untreated background. Under a common affine fluorescence transform y'=a*y+b with a>0, gain and normalized residuals (scaled by S0) are unchanged. The all-condition minmax transformation in the source is unnecessary, while target-informed minmax choices would compromise the split. Nonlinear assay transforms do not share this invariance. Equally averaging trimmed well means avoids weighting a biological condition by bead acquisition counts. No standard-curve or apoptosis-adjusted claim is warranted.

The local original source already establishes cytokine synergy and context/inhibitor-specific modulation (Par18–19), and explicitly connects logic-ODE models with active acquisition (Par68–79). The narrow value is testing whether a simpler, constrained predictor survives particular excluded joint contexts. It does not supply a new algorithm, biological causality, efficient acquisition or an Agent advantage; the protocol correctly admits that three calibration drug conditions to avoid one joint measurement are not a savings demonstration. No public-prior search was needed to establish these material objections; this review does not certify exhaustive novelty clearance.

## Admissibility recommendation

Proceed with control-only checkpoint now. Target evaluation may follow after freezing the above interpretation/gating-provenance/interval clarifications, keeping the same targets, equations, processing, normalization and thresholds. No new fitted comparator is required for the narrow empirical audit. If the intended claim is instead to distinguish common biological amplification from known nonlinear biology, the present design cannot do so and target arithmetic will not repair that identification gap. A positive exploratory result must remain a local descriptive restriction check, and a negative result must not be recast as inhibitor-specific gains or evidence that a new Agent mechanism succeeded.
