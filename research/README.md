# Research workspace

Keep experimental implementations and research tests here. This workspace may be cleaned or removed; preserve key protocols, reports, result summaries, failures and decisions in [log](../log/README.md), and store inputs and detailed outputs in [data](../data/README.md) before removing code.

Promote an implementation to [src](../src/README.md) or [tools](../tools/README.md) only after validation relevant to its intended use, with a promotion decision and supporting evidence recorded in `log/`. Repository structure or storage checks alone do not establish scientific validity.

- [Scientific report](REPORT.md) and [evidence register](EVIDENCE.md).
- [Registered studies](STUDIES.json).
- The registry's separate `workspaces` list identifies current experimental and maintenance working areas. Inclusion is a storage declaration, not study completion, scientific validation or runtime promotion.
- [Retained historical reports](../log/research_records/README.md).
- [Cleanup and migration record](../log/20261023/report_consolidation/README.md).

Record removed paths, retained report/data locations and reproduction limitations in the corresponding log record. Follow the [storage policy](../log/STORAGE_POLICY.md).
