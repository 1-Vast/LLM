# Data strategy for budgeted biological feedback

Source audit: 2026-10-11, Asia/Shanghai. This is a targeted source and existing-evidence audit, not a new biological experiment. It changes no existing study, production component, or archived verdict. Official metadata and small input tables were retrieved; no new phenotype matrix was opened. The practical recommendation is to develop a maximum-two-round policy on existing exposed PRISM data, retain basal/transcriptional transfer as the strongest prior, and reserve a separately measured, condition-qualified panel for confirmation. Adding more modalities without paired support does not solve the decision problem.

## 2. Distinguish three kinds of independence

| Property | What qualifies | What does not qualify |
|---|---|---|
| Algorithm holdout | The whole policy, tuning, sources, and stopping rule are frozen before accessing held-out outcomes | A new seed applied after all compounds informed earlier development; hiding one CSV while equivalent outcomes remain in another release |
| Independent biological measurement | A documented separate culture/plate/measurement run, with its relation to the original biological unit recorded | Cells randomly divided within one well; reprocessing the same RNA; a bootstrap; a second verifier; a new Agent conversation |
| Domain transport | Performance tested in a new declared cell/dose/time/platform context, with enough paired support to calibrate transfer | Matching a compound name across assays; calling a prediction a measured observation; pooled performance hiding unsupported contexts |

These properties are orthogonal. PRISM secondary is separately measured and can test agreement with primary; it is still historical, activity-selected, incomplete, and uses a different dose-selection protocol. Tahoe same-well RNA and cell shares provide a stronger correspondence between readouts but not a second biological replicate. A checkpoint-held-out cell line can still have an exposed phenotype or exposed RNA. A frozen public-data replay is useful algorithm evaluation when labeled honestly, but does not establish prospective laboratory improvement.

## 5. How to exploit scarce data without manufacturing evidence

**First retain a cheap transferable prior.** Basal-context transfer has positive support in the Tahoe study, and transcriptional-neighbor transfer has positive support in PRISM. Use a hierarchical residual model that shrinks a new context toward supported reference contexts; start with mean, basal kNN, transcriptional kNN, ridge and low-rank multitask controls. Fit source weights on training contexts only. The candidate contribution is improved context-aware shrinkage or feedback allocation, not the mere use of transfer learning.

**Use knowledge as a bounded prior.** The user's [MAP paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-026-01286-w), *A knowledge-driven framework for predicting single-cell responses for unprofiled drugs*, was verified through Crossref and the existing official-source audit. Its multimodal/graph representation offers a way to share information with unprofiled compounds. MAESTRO's existing audit does not certify its native RNA decoder or show general decision gains. Keep edge evidence type, direction, source, publication/release time, species, assay and missing condition fields. Unknown is not negative. Compare no-KG, typed-KG, generic text retrieval, graph-neighbor and degree/popularity controls. Delete/hold out target-compound edges when testing unseen-edge generalization, and report known-entity versus unseen-entity regimes separately.

Do not let a knowledge-based critic suppress transcriptional neighbors merely because they cross annotated mechanisms: Block N's critic harmed precisely that useful signal. A graph prior can identify an informative measurement or an unsupported extrapolation; a real result must determine whether the prior survives.

**Use cheap modalities as observations with their own error model.** CV embeddings, cell counts, a small flow panel, or a few concentration points may be cheaper than RNA. Their value must be learned against the selected terminal phenotype in matched cultures. Estimate noise and missingness from independent wells/plates; bootstrap at the culture or campaign level. A crop, an image augmentation, an extra cell, a sentence paraphrase and an in silico response are not new biological replicates.

**Borrow data-efficiency practices across fields under explicit limits.** CV self-supervision and pretrained representations reduce feature-learning demand; domain/plate-held-out testing checks whether they retain biological signal. NLP retrieval and weak supervision reduce annotation effort; source-deduplicated labels and blinded measured endpoints prevent circular validation. Robotics-style simulation/pretraining and domain randomization can stress-test an observation policy before expensive experiments; synthetic trajectories remain stress tests until a real feedback transfer is shown. Simulated outcome noise should be fitted from measured replicates, and mismatched regimes should cause abstention. These are adaptation strategies and controls, not already demonstrated MAESTRO improvements.

## 6. Minimum condition and leakage contract

Every measured row should retain `compound_identity`, `cell_identity`, genotype/basal context, concentration with unit, elapsed exposure time, culture geometry, platform/readout, plate, well, biological-culture ID, technical-replicate ID, source version, QC rule and previous outcome exposure. A cross-source identity join is insufficient when one of the condition fields is absent. A learned cross-condition bridge is allowed only as an explicitly trained and validated transport model; it is not automatically a same-condition observation.

Splits must match the claim:

1. For unseen drugs, group all aliases, salts/parent forms where biologically justified, doses and technical replicates before splitting; report an additional scaffold-held-out sensitivity analysis.
2. For unseen cell backgrounds, hold out the complete line across RNA, morphology, viability, basal fitting and model adaptation. Public unlabeled basal features may be permitted transductive input only if that is declared for all methods.
3. For cross-lab claims, hold out source, batch and independent culture together. Training on one well and testing on its cells/images is pseudoreplication.
4. Fit scaling, nuisance regression, feature selection, graph features and source mixtures without test outcomes. Mask a query's own profile across LINCS releases, PRISM releases and cached neighbor matrices.
5. Freeze the exact foundation-model checkpoint and pretraining disclosures. Name masking bounds one kind of LLM recall; it does not exclude knowledge of the underlying assay or graph.
6. Keep final validation outcomes out of Agent retrieval, KG claims, training labels, reward construction and early-stopping decisions. Matching a test drug to a graph edge extracted from its evaluation paper is leakage even without loading the result CSV.

## 7. Smallest next study worth paying for

**Development now.** Reuse the exposed 701/281/421 geometry as explicitly retrospective development. Compare zero-feedback/strong one-shot, one informative batch, and at most one update after a small initial batch. Keep the same candidate menu, posterior inputs and **total resource cap**. Measurement counts must differ when turnaround spending differs: with a cap of 18 read equivalents and a cost of two read equivalents per turnaround, the one-shot arm can buy 16 measurements while the two-round arm can buy 14. Equal paid measurements would give the latter an extra budget. Optimize final utility minus assay and turnaround cost, not entropy reduction alone. Calibrate selected-set error and full-policy risk on whole development episodes, with support/uncertainty strata defined from pre-reveal inputs. Do not tune an active-only stratum using hidden outcomes. If evidence for another round is weak, deploy the strong batch policy.

**Source qualification in parallel.** BATCHIE's 6.7 MB triplet table is a reasonable first distinct public source after checking its schema, condition support and coverage; it can test cost-aware scheduling but not transplant the PRISM biological conclusion. JUMP/BBBC/RxRx offer input representation work and missing-context diagnostics. The CXCL9 packet can exercise a real measured-endpoint contract immediately. None supplies a free, complete, independent cross-modal viability dataset by itself.

**Independent confirmation.** A prospective matched panel should contain candidates chosen by the strongest batch policy and the proposed policy, plus blinded random/negative controls and shared positive controls. Randomize positions and repeat cultures on separate plates/days. Predefine one target cell context, dose/time window, readout and an intention-to-test rule for failures. Include intermediate cheap observations only if they can be collected without invalidating the terminal assay: longitudinal live imaging plus a terminal readout may share a well; destructive RNA and ATP assays generally require explicitly matched sister cultures. Same culture allocation and independent remeasurement should have different IDs.

Set the minimum useful **net** improvement before measuring anything, obtain real per-well and per-turnaround prices, and power the study from independent culture/plate variation. No sample-size number is justified from per-cell counts. A selective-hit confirmation panel alone estimates performance in a selected population, not library-wide false discovery; a blinded random sample is needed to estimate that broader quantity. If the available independent units cannot resolve the chosen net effect, label the study feasibility and keep the empirical policy in research.

## Reproduce this audit

From the repository root, in the existing `maestro` environment:

```powershell
& 'D:/anaconda/envs/maestro/python.exe' research/budgeted_feedback_20261011/data/retrieve_sources.py
& 'D:/anaconda/envs/maestro/python.exe' research/budgeted_feedback_20261011/data/audit_assets.py
```

The retrieval tool limits each response to 2 MB and downloads no large outcome matrix. Rerunning retrieval records the source bytes at that later time; it is not a claim that mutable URLs are pinned forever. `ASSET_AUDIT.json` records the observed counts, source-hash checks and strict structure join. The tools provide source feasibility evidence only; they do not promote a model or produce a new positive scientific result.
