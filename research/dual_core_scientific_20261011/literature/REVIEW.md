# Targeted literature review for MAESTRO's scientific decision interface

Reviewed 2026-10-11, Asia/Shanghai. The 16 records in [citations.json](citations.json) contain verified titles, authors, primary identifiers, exact local source hashes, supported claims and explicit claim limits. [source_catalog.json](source_catalog.json) preserves searches, successful retrievals and failures. This is a targeted review of the repository's demonstrated gaps, not an exhaustive novelty search. References and language-model outputs are priors or constraints; they are never measured endpoint truth.

The current repository supports transcriptomic transfer for selective killing, but does not establish an independent LLM contribution, an advantage over conventional LLM-plus-tool integration, or broadly valid cross-assay/time transport. Block P's 266 turnarounds versus a one-shot comparator's 38 makes cost part of the scientific question. Block M's loss of coverage on active drugs makes selection-conditional validity part of the question. These local findings motivate a narrower intervention: bind the world model's observation distribution to a terminal decision, and evaluate whether feedback-directed observation purchases earn their cost.

## The closest prior art

**BATCHIE** already combines probabilistic response models, information-theoretic adaptive batches, retrospective replay and prospective combination-screen validation. It also accounts for the physical experimental design. Its drug-combination work is close to MAESTRO's proposed belief-update loop; active acquisition alone is not new. See Tosh et al., *Nature Communications* 2025, [10.1038/s41467-024-55287-7](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-55287-7), local primary full text `raw/active_combo_2025.html`.

**DrugReflector** already combines transcriptomic profiles, active learning and lab-in-the-loop signature refinement for measured phenotypic campaigns. The reported 13- to 17-fold hit-rate gains, followed by an additional refinement gain, are its results and should not be repeated as expectations for MAESTRO. See DeMeo et al., *Science* 2025, [10.1126/science.adi8577](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adi8577). Crossref metadata and the indexed original abstract are retained; a publisher full text was not retrieved.

**CXCL9 mechanistic active learning** already integrates curated prior knowledge, interpretable logic-ODE response models and real feedback-guided perturbation experiments. This directly overlaps claims about mechanism-state models plus experiment design. Its public measured data provide a small distinct endpoint that can check assay-bound contracts, but cover only two pancreatic-cancer backgrounds. See Wang et al., *Molecular Systems Biology* 2026, [10.1038/s44320-026-00221-w](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44320-026-00221-w), local primary full text `raw/active_mechanistic_2026.html`.

**BioDiscoveryAgent** already uses literature, code and critique for closed-loop genetic experiment design and compares against Bayesian optimization. An agent that proposes hypotheses and experiments is consequently not sufficient novelty. Conversely, **Gupta, Hartford and Liu** report that the tested LLM design agents were insensitive to permuted feedback and were beaten by classical linear bandits and GP optimization. Their hybrid separates LLM prior knowledge from quantitative acquisition. Both lines of evidence support testing the agent's distinct contribution, rather than presuming benefit from more reasoning calls. See [BioDiscoveryAgent, ICLR 2025](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/4252dc94531833029000f85dc5fac792-Abstract-Conference.html) and [LLMs for Bayesian Optimization in Scientific Domains: Are We There Yet?, arXiv:2509.21403](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.21403). The latter's arXiv abstract and full HTML were verified; its EMNLP venue was not independently verified in this search and is not asserted in the citation record.

## Use decision value rather than generic information gain

For belief state `b`, terminal actions `d`, proposed observation action `a` and its yet-unseen result `y`, the relevant acquisition value is

`EVSI(a) = E_y[max_d E[U(d) | b, a, y]] - max_d E[U(d) | b]`.

Its net value additionally subtracts the actual observation cost. This is established decision theory, not a new MAESTRO algorithm. Prediction improvement, posterior entropy reduction and mechanism-set shrinkage can be valuable secondary outcomes, but may have zero value for the terminal action. The no-purchase action must remain eligible. If the objective is to predict or select *unmeasured* lines, the same eligible terminal set must be used on both sides of the comparison; otherwise acquisition can falsely appear beneficial merely by removing a hard or low-value line from the evaluation menu.

Strong et al. provide fast EVSI estimation from a probabilistic sensitivity sample; Frazier et al. provide sequential acquisition under correlated Gaussian beliefs. These are appropriate conceptual and strong-baseline references. A particle-library posterior should be compared with a correlated Gaussian/ridge posterior and a well-tuned fixed design, using the same observed values, budget and permitted endpoint. Its computed EVSI is conditional on the model and its assumptions. A positive computed EVSI is not a certificate of positive realized utility or a lower confidence bound on real experiment value. See [10.1177/0272989X15575286](https://doi.org/10.1177/0272989X15575286) and [10.1287/ijoc.1080.0314](https://doi.org/10.1287/ijoc.1080.0314).

## Calibrate the selected policy and its claimed loss

Fannjiang et al. show why prediction-driven biomolecular design creates statistical dependence between training data and chosen test inputs. Their feedback-covariate-shift method addresses that dependence under explicit assumptions. Ordinary marginal coverage from an unrelated random split does not automatically protect the agent's selected top candidates. Conformal Risk Control extends calibration to an expected monotone loss; Learn then Test frames policy tuning as multiple testing. None of these results gives arbitrary cost-adjusted EVSI, mechanism causality or every subgroup a guarantee by default.

For MAESTRO, a certificate should name its loss, calibration population, policy version and selection process. Policy-level calibration and evaluation should replay the entire adaptive episode on separate calibration and test units, with selected-set and activity/context-stratum checks. Previously exposed PRISM/L1000 units can support a labeled retrospective replay. They cannot become a new biological holdout merely through a new random split. See [Fannjiang et al., PNAS 2022](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2204569119), [Conformal Risk Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.02814) and [Learn then Test](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.01052).

## Preserve biological condition and measurement boundaries

State and LPM motivate explicit context-aware perturbation models. LPM's perturbation/readout/context factorization is especially relevant to the interface. A shared representation does not authenticate an RNA-to-viability bridge across different doses, times, assay platforms or culture units. The verified final State article is [10.1016/j.cell.2026.07.052](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2026.07.052); the earlier source is [10.1101/2025.06.26.661135](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.06.26.661135). LPM is [10.1038/s43588-025-00870-1](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43588-025-00870-1).

Perturb-Multimodal demonstrates a stronger future multimodal data design: morphological cell segments and RNA molecules are assigned to the same cells in intact tissue under genetic perturbation. The original paper's data availability points to GEO **GSE275483**, [Hugging Face PerturbMulti](https://huggingface.co/datasets/xingjiepan/PerturbMulti/tree/main), and [the original analysis repository](https://github.com/weallen/InVivoMultimodalPerturbation). This is a valid source for studying correspondence, but it does not establish a drug-viability bridge in PRISM. Large image archives were not downloaded in this task. See [10.1016/j.cell.2025.05.022](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2025.05.022).

Ahlmann-Eltze et al. and PertEval-scFM show why mean, additive/linear and other simple baselines must remain. The 2026 in-the-wild benchmark additionally tests unseen contexts, unseen perturbations and cross-dataset transfer and finds that common setups can overestimate performance. These are prediction benchmarks; MAESTRO still needs its own decision endpoint and deployment-matched evaluation. See [10.1038/s41592-025-02772-6](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-025-02772-6), [PertEval-scFM](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/wenteler25a.html) and [arXiv:2604.27646](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.27646).

## A defensible next contribution

The immediately testable contribution is an **assay-bound interface between a response model and a feedback-driven observation policy**, with terminal utility, genuine stopping, equal information, matched budgets, and an auditable measured-result ledger. A positive replay result would establish that bounded algorithmic improvement on reused public measurements. It would not establish LLM superiority, a full mechanistic virtual cell, or a broadly valid multimodal bridge.

The stronger scientific contribution would require a registered result showing that the same observation budget and model support yield better cost-adjusted terminal decisions than strong deterministic acquisition and fixed designs, replicated on distinct measured data. An LLM claim additionally requires a same-tool, same-information language-agent ablation against the deterministic planner and the conventional LLM-plus-tool loop. Feedback permutation must change realized value in the expected direction. Mechanism-discrimination claims require real interventions or independent measured mechanism labels; text plausibility and reduced posterior entropy are insufficient.
