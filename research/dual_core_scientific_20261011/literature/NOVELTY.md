# Existing work and the proposed contribution boundary

This targeted search does not establish worldwide priority. The rows distinguish implementable improvements from claims that would require new scientific evidence.

| Proposed component | Verified overlapping prior art | What MAESTRO may test | What cannot yet be claimed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Transcriptomic transfer plus phenotypic feedback | DrugReflector; State; existing local Blocks N/P | Whether observation selection adds to the unchanged transfer model for its measured endpoint | A new transcriptomics-guided active-learning workflow |
| Mechanistic belief model plus active experiments | CXCL9 logic-ODE active learning; BATCHIE | Whether a bounded, context-aware belief representation earns its complexity over Gaussian/ridge controls | A novel mechanism-aware experiment-design paradigm or causal mechanism discovery from labels alone |
| Terminal EVSI and stopping | Strong et al. 2015; correlated knowledge gradient | Whether endpoint-bound purchases improve net realized decisions and correctly stop when they cannot help | EVSI or correlated-belief sampling as a new algorithm; positive model-estimated EVSI as biological proof |
| LLM proposals with quantitative acquisition | BioDiscoveryAgent; Gupta et al. LLMNN | Same-tool, matched-information incremental proposal value beyond deterministic and ordinary LLM-plus-tool policies | Agent superiority from a deterministic planner or from more language calls |
| Adaptive risk and uncertainty gates | Feedback-covariate-shift conformal; Conformal Risk Control; Learn then Test | Whether the deployed selected policy satisfies a registered, useful endpoint-risk limit on proper units | Coverage for selected high-score drugs from ordinary random-split marginal calibration |
| Context, readout, time and dose contracts | LPM factorizes perturbation/readout/context; State models context | Whether explicit refusals and source binding prevent actual wrong-assay decisions and preserve valid actions | A shared embedding as proof of cross-assay or cross-time biological transport |
| Same-cell RNA and image state | Perturb-Multimodal | A future measured correspondence study with matched culture/cell IDs and interventions | Viability or apoptosis from unvalidated image/RNA semantics |
| Strong baselines and strict perturbation splits | Ahlmann-Eltze et al.; PertEval-scFM; in-the-wild VC benchmark | Matched-budget terminal utility and cost on explicitly declared exposure and shift regimes | Novelty from benchmarking alone or a fresh holdout from previously exposed measurements |
| Complete dual-core evidence packet | Existing MAESTRO negative/positive studies provide local context; targeted search only | A reproducible factorial attribution of model, policy, real feedback, costs and stopping, linked to measured endpoints | A scientific gain before the registered contrasts pass; global priority based on this targeted search |

The formalizable near-term claim is a verified interface and a bounded policy comparison. A broader method claim should depend on positive incremental decision value over strong existing methods, honest uncertainty, and distinct measured validation. Negative results remain relevant evidence and should constrain promotion.
