# Small publicly measured CXCL9 dataset

The packet under `datasets/cxcl9_2026/` is downloaded from the source repository for Wang et al., *Molecular Systems Biology* 2026, [10.1038/s44320-026-00221-w](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44320-026-00221-w), pinned to revision `b654a45faf5a80cdb1b514188cc90af8c9565b85`. Exact file sizes, hashes and URLs are retained in [dataset_card.json](dataset_card.json), `data_download_log.json` and `verification_log.json`. Failed transport attempts are preserved; successful retries do not silently replace the receipt.

The author-processed `Raw_data_statistics_AsPC1.csv` and `Raw_data_statistics_BxPC3.csv` each contain 24 cytokine-by-inhibitor conditions with measured intracellular CXCL9 fluorescence summaries. The assay metadata states incubation for **24 hours**. JAK, IKK, RAS, MEK and PI3K inhibitors are momelotinib, BMS-345541, salirasib, trametinib and taselisib, respectively. The downloaded raw FACS files are BxPC3 E3CA (P12) and E3CB (P14); their rows are nested single-cell events. The AsPC1 raw files remain available upstream and were not downloaded. Each cell event is not an independent culture replicate.

The `MIDAS_*.csv` files have 144 rows per background, constructed as 24 conditions across a six-point `DA:ALL` model time grid. These are **model inputs**, not a measured six-timepoint multimodal experiment. Many `DV:*` columns are initial state or constraint values rather than observed protein assays. Only the actual measured CXCL9 endpoint may be used as outcome evidence. The `.sif` network and named biological targets are prior knowledge, not mechanism truth.

The author's preparation script log-transforms fluorescence and applies min-max normalization across conditions. A deployment or sequential replay must not reveal the full-outcome normalization range as an initial input; use a fixed external scale, the raw/log endpoint, or explicitly restrict that scaling to evaluation. Author-processed summaries are useful, but the local packet does not certify full independent reconstruction of their cleaning and normalization. The downloaded original R script is retained for inspection and has not been executed.

This dataset is eligible for an additional separately frozen measured-endpoint example, such as observation-versus-stop decisions in a known assay. It has only two cell backgrounds and does not supply an L1000 world-model bridge, viability, causal ground-truth mechanism labels, or a broad new biological holdout. No positive benchmark result is claimed by the download.

Other verified public candidates were retained without bulk download:

- BATCHIE's retrospective measured combination packet: [Zenodo 12764821](https://zenodo.org/records/12764821); prospective combination measurements: [Zenodo 13871987](https://zenodo.org/records/13871987). The original paper authenticates these availability links, but the datasets have not been downloaded or audited here.
- Perturb-Multimodal: GEO **GSE275483**, [Hugging Face images](https://huggingface.co/datasets/xingjiepan/PerturbMulti/tree/main), and [analysis code](https://github.com/weallen/InVivoMultimodalPerturbation). These preserve a stronger basis for cell-level RNA/image correspondence than unrelated assay joins; bulk data remain unaudited locally.
- GDSC2 release 8.5: the official Sanger URL `https://cog.sanger.ac.uk/cancerrxgene/GDSC_release8.5/GDSC2_fitted_dose_response_27Oct23.xlsx` returned HTTP 200 to a HEAD request and reported 21,330,376 bytes. No response bytes were downloaded. The fitted dose-response endpoint is distinct from PRISM single-dose viability.
