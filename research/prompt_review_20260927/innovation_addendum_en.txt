MAESTRO v2 ADDENDUM: TWO-CORE INNOVATION AND CONTRIBUTION ATTRIBUTION

Append this addendum to the previous MAESTRO v2 execution prompt. It adds research priorities and contribution tests without changing RUN_MODE, resource authorization, sealed-data restrictions, evidence boundaries, or deployment requirements. AUDIT_AND_PLAN remains the default. This addendum does not authorize additional training, dataset downloads, or access to sealed outcomes.

1. CORE POSITIONING AND TESTABLE CLAIMS

MAESTRO's first core is the mechanism-evidence agent. Its second core is the virtual-cell world model.

The agent owns the scientific objective, mechanistic contrasts, interpretation-prerequisite checks, measurement-plan construction and repair, action selection, interpretation of real results, and stopping or deferral. The world model forecasts supported candidate measurements and supplies conditional predictions for planning. Only qualified real measurements, admitted through independent interpretation rules, may establish measured prerequisites or update the compatible mechanism set.

Audit and test three separate claims without assuming that any is true:

AG: Explicitly identifying a missing interpretation prerequisite and directing measurement acquisition toward it improves evidence-supported decisions relative to conventional prerequisite-aware planning or reactive selection under matched knowledge, rules, and resources.

WM: With the agent and candidate actions held fixed, conditional world-model predictions provide additional decision-relevant information beyond appropriate simple prediction baselines.

JOINT: The agent's prerequisite-directed repair can exploit world-model information to produce attributable decision value. A useful combined system, independently useful components, and a positive statistical interaction are distinct findings.

Architectural importance does not depend on whether a particular experimental model is currently enabled by default. Retain an executable no-world-model fallback for operation and attribution, while preserving world-model development as a research objective. Failure to qualify WM data must not automatically block an otherwise qualified AG audit or experiment.

Do not equate the agent with an LLM. Identify the separate roles of the deterministic controller, LLM proposals, search/planning, knowledge and tools, and execution feedback. More agent roles, larger networks, and the name "world model" are not contributions by themselves.

3. DEFINE INNOVATION AS A FALSIFIABLE METHOD DIFFERENCE

For AG, WM, and JOINT, produce one compact contribution card containing:

- The scientific problem and concrete failure case.
- What the closest existing methods already accomplish.
- The additional decision operation proposed by MAESTRO.
- Why that operation might change an action or conclusion under matched information.
- Corresponding code and implementation status.
- Required real evidence and the result that would refute the claim.
- Closest prior work and the precise distinction from it.

Prioritize this candidate operation: map a missing interpretation prerequisite to an executable measurement, propagate the resulting evidence permission to downstream readout interpretation, and use sufficiently supported forecasts to assess that measurement and its possible follow-up actions.

Compare against prerequisite-aware planning, diagnosis and plan repair, Bayesian experimental design, active measurement selection, and selective decision-making. Use verifiable primary sources and state adaptation limits. Do not claim priority from terminology. If the contribution is a domain-specific combination of established methods, describe it accurately and identify the new task formalization, reusable constraints, or empirical evidence that makes the combination valuable.

Do not describe the current missing-field-coverage-then-cost heuristic as globally minimum-cost repair without establishing its search space and optimality conditions. A licence object records rule execution; it does not independently prove a biological mechanism or the scientific value of buying the repair measurement.

4. THE PRIMARY TASK MUST EXERCISE THE CLAIMED OPERATION

On development data, qualify a small real task with competing explanations, a specifically missing interpretation prerequisite, an action that measures it, a downstream readout whose interpretation depends on that prerequisite, and legitimate decisions that differ with the observed results.

One candidate is genetic efficacy with weak pharmacological phenotype, where a proximal-function measurement helps distinguish insufficient intervention realization from alternative explanations requiring further testing. Determine whether condition-matched measurements actually support this replay task. Do not invent assays or outcomes to complete the narrative.

The same readout may have different admissible interpretations before and after its prerequisite is established. Demonstrating this behavior establishes contract correctness only. To establish decision value, compare prerequisite acquisition against other legal strategies on correctness, errors, deferral, and cost.

Transcriptomic mechanism-class discrimination and dose/time selection can evaluate planning within their scope. They do not directly establish the full genetic-pharmacological mechanism-repair claim. Synthetic gaps, shuffled fields, and constructed cases are contract or stress tests, not substitutes for biological confirmation.

If qualified paired data are unavailable, mark biological AG validation NOT_READY, specify the minimum missing data, and finish the executable plan and justified contract checks. Do not manufacture an advantage by hiding a real prerequisite from a comparator, giving MAESTRO exclusive knowledge, changing outcome rules, or selecting favorable sealed cases.

5. ADD AGENT-SPECIFIC GATES AND FAIR ATTRIBUTION

Add the following gates alongside the existing WM-G0 through WM-G4:

AG-G0: The selected task has a genuine interpretation-prerequisite gap, and a feasible measurement can affect an evidence-supported decision. Distinguish development observations from constructed examples.

AG-G1: An execution trace demonstrates prerequisite identification, plan repair, verification against real results, and scope-limited updates. Contract-test success supports implementation correctness only.

AG-G2: Under matched knowledge, primitive capabilities, composition rules, interpretation rules, and resources, the method meets a preregistered decision or cost endpoint and its quality/risk constraints against strong baselines.

AG-G3: The frozen result is confirmed on eligible unopened evaluation units under the original sealing requirements and a precisely specified generalization claim.

Consider fixed expert order, reactive selection, and a conventional planner that handles the same prerequisites and legal compositions. For small spaces, use exact enumeration as a same-information baseline. Do not establish an agent contribution using weaker action permissions, exclusive shared-control discounts, or artificially incomplete comparator knowledge.

Separate selection value over a fixed plan set from plan-construction value. For the latter, generated plan sets may differ, but all arms receive the same primitive capabilities, composition rules, and cost accounting, with explicit controls for construction ability. Do not attribute all action-space expansion value to ranking.

Audit the definition of menu-only optimum in licence.incremental_utility. An optimum over a restricted menu, a same-information optimum over the complete legal space, and a hindsight oracle with realized future outcomes are different objects. Under the same model, objective, and complete legal space, do not require a new heuristic to outperform the exact optimum. A certificate threshold does not replace measured policy comparison.

6. USE EXPLICIT INTERVENTIONS TO TEST THE TWO CORES

Plan a development-stage 2 x 2 attribution experiment:

A0: A conventional prerequisite-aware planning baseline without the specifically defined MAESTRO repair operation.
A1: The same setting with that MAESTRO repair operation.
W0: The simplest qualified prediction baseline behind the same interface.
W1: The candidate virtual-cell conditional predictor.

Report A0W0, A1W0, A0W1, and A1W1. A0 retains the same facts, evidence rules, prerequisite-handling capabilities, and primitive actions. Do not deliberately make it execute invalid actions or omit essential knowledge. If A0 and A1 are behaviorally equivalent, report that no additional algorithmic contribution was identified.

Name the completely prediction-free operational fallback separately from W0. When masking or permuting predictions, preserve comparable applicability handling, legal menus, and evaluation rules, and specify the condition structure preserved by the permutation.

All primary arms share an independent authoritative result validator. Removing planning-side prerequisite awareness is different from disabling final evidence admission. The latter belongs in a separate stress test, not in a primary comparison whose apparent gain comes from weakening the comparator's validator.

For a preregistered comparable endpoint J, report agent and world-model simple effects and the interaction:

I = (J_A1W1 - J_A1W0) - (J_A0W1 - J_A0W0).

Specify the direction, statistical scale, and constraints of J. Use the same independent units across all four arms, paired cluster-aware estimation, and registered comparison priorities and power assumptions. The best joint arm does not automatically establish positive interaction. An inconclusive interaction does not automatically negate independent component value. Mark interaction analysis exploratory when power is insufficient.

If additional LLM value is part of the claimed contribution, compare LLM proposals against template/deterministic proposals under the same controller and rules. Do not attribute deterministic-controller gains to the LLM. Run this comparison only when LLM-specific value is actually claimed.

Do not add modules solely to make the framework appear more innovative.

Use structured fields and verifiable explanations; do not request hidden chain-of-thought.

Does their combination yield additional value? If not, what is the smallest next falsifiable research step? Do not reframe negative evidence as confirmed innovation through promotional wording.
