# Review: when a virtual-cell discrepancy warrants investigation

Reviewed 2026-10-11, Asia/Shanghai, at repository `bf6adafa`. This packet reviews the two supplied research recommendations and develops a candidate P0 plan. It records source inspection, literature checks and an audit of existing summaries. It does not execute Experiment Q, register Block Q, introduce a predictive policy or reopen the failed fixed-eight-probe branch. [P0_PLAN.json](P0_PLAN.json) separates proposed work from completed checks; [REVIEW_RECEIPT.json](REVIEW_RECEIPT.json) identifies the inputs and reading limits.

## Recommendation

Retain the question in the attachments: can the agent identify a failure of a declared biological transfer claim and choose evidence that improves a final scientific decision? Narrow the first commitment to **whether the available evidence can answer that question**. Neither a discrepancy record nor a new name establishes an algorithmic contribution.

The mechanism examples in the attachments are presently unevaluable with the audited menu. Compensation, generic stress and platform failure can all be consistent with one L1000 signature and one PRISM viability result. No currently qualified, condition-matched action was found that distinguishes these explanations. An independent viability repeat can establish reproducibility of a prediction error; it does not identify the pathway responsible for that error.

The next commitment should be a condition and evidence-design qualification, followed by a small functional-decision diagnostic only if it supplies a genuinely different evidence channel or justified task. Changing the number of probes, selecting attractive error cases retrospectively or relabeling the fixed-eight-probe experiment does not meet that requirement.

## What the existing evidence says

The [fixed-probe study](../context_probe_20261011/README.md) remains `NOT_ESTABLISHED`. On 101 of 106 intended evaluation contexts, the low-rank correction changes top-10 utility by -0.01193 [-0.03530, +0.01025], and the conditional update changes it by -0.00582 [-0.03152, +0.02206]. With the declared 0.001 price, low rank versus zero purchase is -0.02193 [-0.04530, +0.00025]. These are exploratory intervals on observed pairs, not independent culture evidence.

Existing ledgers show that low rank changes the selected set in 83 complete contexts: 36 improve and 47 worsen. The conditional update changes it in 89: 44 improve and 45 worsen. Thus the difficulty is not simply that feedback leaves every action unchanged. Feedback changes actions without producing positive average measured utility. The global MSE reduction is a separate result.

Development selection also had little numerical separation: the chosen ridge prior exceeds the drug-mean prior by 0.002376 in mean top-10 utility, and the chosen low-rank update exceeds that ridge prior by 0.001288. These are descriptive differences from an exposed development set, without uncertainty. They motivate checking boundary decisions and generic potency, but neither prove saturation nor bound the value of other observations.

[Block N](../astra/mechanism_routing_20261011/README.md) and [Block P](../astra/selective_discovery_20261011/README.md) support the scoped value of measured transcriptional-response transfer. They do not validate basal-expression transfer to a new functional context or a general mechanism investigator. Agent additions were null or harmful in Block N; Block P did not establish the loop's advantage over the strong one-shot model or conventional agent. Their formerly sealed compounds are now exposed. The prior dose-purity audit also limits how literally the old primary profile can be described as an exact-dose endpoint.

## A substantial overlap with existing Block O

The repository already contains a [two-modality observation environment](../astra/adaptive_investigation_20261011/environment.py): a query compound can reveal recorded L1000 line/time signatures and discovery-half PRISM responses, then predict the held-out lines. Its [headroom analysis](../astra/adaptive_investigation_20261011/feasibility/headroom.py) is explicitly exploratory in the [research index](../INDEX.md). A new generic agent/acquisition framework would duplicate much of this design.

Its results cannot serve as the lawful P0 information-value benchmark:

- `environment.py:69-72` builds generic response, mean and residual statistics from all open profiles, including the query's complete profile. Removing the query from the later neighbor pool does not remove that influence from fitted statistics.
- `headroom.py:51-58` selects active compounds and constructs parts of the action menu using query outcomes or their availability. Activity can define a prespecified evaluation subgroup; it cannot secretly select deployment cases. Archival record availability is permissible only when explicitly declared public and shared by all methods.
- `environment.py:92-98` restricts predicted top-10 selection to test lines whose hidden outcomes are finite. This describes an observability-conditioned ranking diagnostic. A deployable study must commit identities first and retain missing terminal outcomes rather than replace them.
- `full_info` releases every recorded source option and discovery-line response beyond the ordinary budget. `oracle5` chooses reference compounds by correlation with hidden test outcomes. Neither establishes achievable, cost-effective feedback value; neither is exact terminal perfect-information utility.
- A full RNA vector and one functional scalar each cost one action in that analysis. Turnaround and actual assay/API costs are not authenticated.

Preserve these historical artifacts and their exploratory status. Reuse the idea of an explicit release menu, with new isolated source statistics and commitments if a separately justified study is run. This review does not repair or rerun Block O.

## Data that can and cannot adjudicate the proposed explanations

| Evidence source | Admissible role | Qualification still required |
|---|---|---|

| MAP-KG, literature and CCLE | Provenance, transfer premises and hypotheses | Associations, mechanism annotations and basal RNA are not measured occupancy, pathway activity or causal truth |

These conclusions follow the [condition manifest](../context_probe_20261011/data/MANIFEST.json), [official-source audit](../budgeted_feedback_20261011/data/DATA_STRATEGY.md) and [engagement evaluation](../../tools/evaluation/engagement_cases.py). The latter explicitly marks matched target-engagement observations unavailable.

The metadata-only secondary audit finds 1,489 strict 2.5 uM treatment columns, 1,281 complete Broad IDs overlapping primary HTS at that dose, and 102 drug-dose groups represented in multiple secondary screens. These are potential join/design counts, not qualified cell-drug pairs, independent cultures or a promised confirmation sample size. Candidate secondary outcome values were not opened in this review. MTS010 is documented as a technical redo; its existence alone cannot identify biological versus technical effects.

The cheapest next source check is the actual repeated-measurement design: inspect official replicate treatment and pool metadata, exposure protocol and culture provenance. Proceed to a response panel only if those records establish a useful crossed design or a distinct, paired evidence channel. Otherwise seek a different small source or end the mechanism claim for this menu. A complete atlas or new image model does not solve this qualification problem.

The proposed decomposition `y = drug effect + context effect + drug-context interaction + source effect + noise` is a candidate statistical model. When contexts and sources are confounded and independent repeats are absent, those components need not be separately identifiable. Fitting a named interaction term does not identify a pathway or separate biological from technical causation.

## Separate three meanings of information headroom

| Reference | What it answers | What it cannot establish |
|---|---|---|
| Exact best terminal action using all hidden terminal outcomes, on a fully observed fixed menu | Retrospective realized perfect-information regret of the committed baseline | Whether useful information can be purchased; cause of a discrepancy |
| A frozen updater supplied every permitted observation | Performance of that model with that observation menu, often beyond the deployment budget | A mathematical upper bound over all models, or budget-feasible gain |
| A frozen policy receiving only a qualified budget-feasible action | Realized decision value of that action and policy on the stated evaluation units | Prospective benefit or causal explanation without the required independent evidence |

For a specified state distribution and utility, EVPI is the expectation of the optimal full-state utility minus optimal expected utility without revelation. EVSI instead averages the optimal expected terminal utility after a particular legal observation. The attachments' standard EVSI expression is appropriate under its declared distribution. Positive model EVSI is not a lower confidence bound on realized biological benefit.

Large perfect-information regret is a necessary opportunity, not proof of informative legal actions. Failure of one full-menu updater does not prove zero information in the menu. Missing terminal responses prevent an exact full-library optimum; ranking only the observed subset changes the estimand. No informative label imputation or clipping bound should be invented to resolve that limitation.

## Candidate P0 plan and stopping conditions

This is a design proposal, not a frozen execution protocol. Useful-benefit margins, sample size and real cost conversion remain unset until the evidence design is qualified.

1. **Choose one claim and endpoint.** The initially supportable claim is correction of functional predictions or final actions within a declared assay, not recovery of the cause of every prediction error. Keep cytotoxic efficacy, lineage selectivity, RNA response and target engagement as distinct endpoints. Freeze an eligible terminal menu and the treatment of already measured hits.
2. **Qualify the evidence channel.** Record canonical compound identity, cell identity, dose, exposure time, platform, measurement unit, source snapshot, failure/QC rules and the relationship between acquisition and terminal measurements. Cross-condition evidence needs an explicit reference-fitted bridge; a matched causal comparison needs its own control/intervention design. Absent support yields `NOT_EVALUABLE` for that claim.
3. **Require observable separation.** For every pair of candidate explanations, name at least one legal action and the distinct measurable consequences it predicts, including noise and unknown alternatives. Mere differences in hypothesis wording or MoA labels do not qualify. LLM-supplied likelihood numbers are not calibrated likelihoods. If no registered action separates the pair, retain `NON_IDENTIFIABLE` relative to the present menu and assumptions.
4. **Check decision space on exposed development data.** Evaluate baseline error and boundary losses using archived predictions first. Distinguish exact outcome oracle, unlimited-menu model reference and a paid observation. The fixed-eight-probe outcome remains a negative diagnostic; a new menu or endpoint is a new study and requires a stated reason, not another parameter search on its evaluation set.
5. **Freeze a minimal functional diagnostic only after the first four steps pass.** Include the strongest supported prior/zero purchase, matched-cost no update, correctly associated observed evidence and a prespecified shuffled-evidence control. Additional models need a concrete hypothesis. Recruit/trigger cases from pre-action inputs and model outputs, retain every intended episode, isolate all outcome-derived statistics, commit final decisions before scoring and charge failed measurements. Previously exposed data provide development evidence only.

Stop progression when no condition-qualified separating observation exists, required terminal outcomes or unit provenance cannot support the proposed estimand, decision headroom is below a justified useful margin, or the selected paid feedback does not improve final decisions enough to cover its declared costs. Do not conclude that all feedback is useless. A narrower negative result or a source qualification failure is an acceptable output.

An exploration measurement and a high-confidence biological assertion have different requirements. A valid, budgeted confirmation may be executed despite model uncertainty, using a separately qualified observation contract. It must not bypass a refusal of an unqualified likelihood or convert the resulting data into an unsupported mechanism verdict.

## Minimum framework changes if a later study qualifies

FDC can be a research-side record linked to the existing `case_id`, `plan_version` and result identifiers. Keep the question/endpoint, declared transfer premise and source conditions, versioned predictions, bought observations, competing explanations, hypothesis-by-action consequences and bounded conclusion. `MODEL_MISMATCH` identifies evidence inconsistent with the registered predictive model; it does not identify a biological cause. `EVIDENCE_INSUFFICIENT` and `NON_IDENTIFIABLE` must remain available outcomes.

Reuse [CaseStore](../../src/agent/case_store.py), [orchestrator](../../src/agent/orchestrator.py), [outcome admission](../../src/maestro/outcome.py), [frozen decision policy](../../src/maestro/decision_value_policy.py) and [virtual-cell routing](../../src/virtual_cell/routing.py). The current decision-value policy's loss is hypothesis wrong/defer loss, not automatically PRISM top-10 utility. The total-resource checker is an opt-in arithmetic contract; actual purchase reservations still need complete measurement, turnaround and tool costs. Changed hypotheses or interpretation rules require a new evaluated version rather than silent rewriting of a pinned receipt.

A later Agent experiment must give the agent an identifiable remaining choice: selecting supported transfer premises or genuinely different qualified control/measurement bundles, for example. Compare it with a same-information deterministic planner and ordinary LLM tool loop, alongside the strongest batch policy. If the evaluator exhaustively chooses the same best action from a complete menu, asking the LLM to propose that action adds no distinct decision mechanism.

Only after these pieces work should the two-core factorial test and independent confirmation run. The interaction contrast is scale-dependent and secondary to performance against the strongest usable baseline. Trace fluency, citations and changed actions are useful process audits; measured terminal benefit and justified conclusions determine scientific value. No production module is proposed for promotion from this review.

## Literature corrections and plausible contribution

The supplied direction is consistent with prior work, so the novelty burden is substantial. [SOURCES.json](literature/SOURCES.json) records exact primary references, dates and reading scope. Several important distinctions are:

- TxPert concerns gene-perturbation transcriptomic prediction and batch-matched controls. Its treatment of common stress/fitness signals motivates a baseline; it does not establish drug-functional transfer.
- PRESAGE's "functional" evaluation includes effect-size, phenocopy and gene-set metrics. These are not independent measured survival or pathway-intervention endpoints. Its multi-background conclusions should not be imported as proof of a sparse functional bridge.
- POPPER already addresses automated sequential falsification, and BioDiscoveryAgent already studies language-guided biological experiment design. FDC plus hypothesis generation and experiment selection is not sufficient novelty.
- GeneAgent supplies a relevant source-validation precedent. R-IDeA addresses active learning under model misspecification. BiomniBench evaluates scientific trajectories; it does not supply matched experimental truth for this project.
- The 27-method/29-dataset perturbation benchmark concerns transcriptomic generalization. A graph or knowledge-rich method's score in that benchmark cannot replace the functional decision test.

The specific "JUMP 2024 Nature Methods" attribution and filtered consensus-profile file named in the attachments were not verified. The public JUMP preprint and dataset repository are real; the missing publication/file identification must be resolved before treating that proposed evidence action as available.

[Vanlier et al.](https://doi.org/10.1186/1752-0509-8-20) already select biochemical model-discrimination experiments from predictive distribution differences. [Pearl and Bareinboim](https://doi.org/10.1214/14-STS486) formalize causal transportability under explicit graphical and identification assumptions. Both are prior work to account for, rather than guarantees supplied by this framework.

The credible candidate contribution is a demonstrated coupling: a **pre-action, source-qualified diagnosis of a failed transfer premise leads to a better evidence choice and lower terminal decision loss**, at the same information and resource budget, with useful abstention and independent confirmation. Causal transportability would require additional causal assumptions and design; the current proposal is empirical evidence-transfer assessment. No novelty or effectiveness claim is established by this packet.
