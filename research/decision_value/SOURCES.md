# Decision-value study: sources and scope

The user-described EVSI/safety-gate package and 45-row nested table were not
available as provenance-pinned executable inputs when this investigation began.
The new study must identify its own implementation, protocol and existing source
assets; it cannot claim reproduction of an unseen package. This audit opened
source code, manifests, publications and the existing availability masks, not
new biological response arrays.

## Reference coverage and validation

Reading only `availability_A` and `availability_B` from the authenticated
boundary packet confirms a 45-by-146 menu. There are 43 contexts with every A
and B available. Index 31 lacks 2 A and 5 B entries (7 distinct candidates);
index 34 lacks 21 A and 27 B entries (44 distinct candidates). The previous
modulo-three outer folds contain 15, 13 and 15 complete contexts.

All 45 can contribute masked training observations. Only 43 can support the
unchanged full-menu policy/terminal-utility replay. Preserve two explicit
unevaluable records rather than filling missing outcomes or shrinking their
menus. Results conditioned on complete support do not represent all 45 source
contexts or all attempted physical experiments.

Strict whole-context leave-one-out (LOO) trains on 44 contexts, while the
predecessor three-fold study trains on 30. All static, old KG and new policy
comparators must therefore be refitted under the same new split, information,
menu and resource rules. A numerical improvement over the predecessor alone
cannot identify a policy effect. Inside each outer split, forecasts used to
estimate calibration residuals must exclude both the outer-held context and
the residual-held context from means, gain selection, centering and covariance.

These references overlap STATE pretraining and have been used locally for
development; the five other target profiles are already exposed. Strict LOO
can test a newly fitted readout/acquisition policy on held-out contexts. It
does not establish independent STATE generalization. There are 43 evaluated
contexts, not contexts multiplied by policies, genes, doses or replay seeds.
Source wells/plates do not establish independent culture starts.

## LCB, cost and functional meaning

A Gaussian lower confidence bound (LCB) is model-based until its coverage is
checked. Marginal candidate coverage does not automatically protect a selected
top-five set or an adaptively chosen query trajectory. A safety claim needs a
predeclared error/loss, whole-context calibration of the actual selected policy,
separation from policy/model selection, and justified calibration-to-deployment
exchangeability. Multiple policies, thresholds and adaptive looks require an
appropriate simultaneous guarantee. Cross-fitting arithmetic alone supplies
none of these assumptions. Report bound failures, decision coverage and
abstentions rather than relabeling an empirical gate as a certificate.

Even with ideal independent units, 43 accepted zero-error calibration decisions
give a one-sided 95% exact binomial upper error bound of about 6.73%; at least
59 are needed for a single 5% conditional-error claim before multiplicity.
This illustrative calculation is not a guarantee for the current exposed,
cross-fitted data. Any gate in this study concerns the declared RNA-selection
loss, not biological or clinical safety.

The endpoint is signed mean change in 39 verified Hallmark Apoptosis RNA genes.
It is neither measured apoptosis nor viability. No authentic monetary prices,
waiting costs or conversion between credits and RNA utility are available in
the inspected inputs. A price multiplier is a declared design assumption or
sensitivity parameter; it must not be presented as a measured economic cost.

Local `data/processed/maestro_vc_v1/bridge_transcriptome_viability.csv` exists,
but its builder (`research/maestro_vc_v1/bridges.py`) joins SciPlex/L1000 RNA at
24 h and 10 uM to PRISM dose-integrated, five-day pooled-viability AUC by compound
connectivity and cell-line identity. These are cross-assay aligned records,
not matched-dose/time/batch sister samples. PRISM/GDSC/Jaaks functional releases
likewise do not identify functional endpoints for the present Tahoe A/B wells.
The ReSisTrace freeze explicitly defines its follow-up as deposited descendant
RNA, not death/survival. In the inspected local manifests/builders, no qualified
same-condition RNA-to-functional panel has been identified; this is not an
exhaustive proof that none exists. A functional bridge needs authenticated
linked assay measurements and independent validation before changing the
utility interpretation.
