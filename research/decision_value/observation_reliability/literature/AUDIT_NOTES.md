The source and prospective-design audit passed. `verify.py` reconstructs the retained byte hashes, official sample identities, condition joins, primary-text quotations and design-only payload arithmetic without network access. New source evidence is stored in new receipts; no v3 study file was revised.

The verified chronology is:

1. The historical scalar/v3 receipt lacked authenticated source duration and joined sample membership. The first metadata-only proposal selected exposed c40/c44 source strata without reading treated RNA.
2. Four published papers were retrieved from proceedings/publisher pages. BioPert's September 29, 2026 v1 and abstract were authenticated directly by bioRxiv API; full text remained rate-limited. STATE #279 was verified as an unanswered user issue, not a mapping certificate.
3. The original Tahoe v2 full text and PDF were successfully recovered through an alternate official publisher route. Methods specify 24-hour drug exposure at 37C and 5% CO2, and results describe plate14 as a biological replicate of plate6. The published Cell successor was verified bibliographically; its full Methods were inaccessible.
4. The official Tahoe sample table was downloaded at a pinned revision and matched its official LFS SHA256. All 24 initial treated-sentinel strata and eight P0.5 control strata join sample/plate/condition records. Those eight controls represent four distinct pooled samples, not eight biological replicates. The initial sentinel panel represents 12 pooled sample IDs across 24 cell-line strata.
5. The full methods also disclosed low-count exclusion of NCI-H661 and a 50-cell condition threshold. Before any treated RNA freeze, the design changed to c44/c45 with at least 50 full-QC cells per stratum. The outcome-free hash rule retained the same six 5-uM sentinel labels. The initial proposal remains available as provenance; the updated plan was not selected using current prediction utility.

Global protocol time and author-described plate repeat status are now qualified. Per-sample exposure timestamps, preparation dates, exchangeability of independent cultures, a third source repeat and untouched terminal confirmation are not supplied by those records. Different cell-line partitions in one pooled treatment sample are dependent units.

It reproduces 27/39 authenticated endpoint coordinates for c40 and 34/39 for c44 across 224 controls each. The correct overall result is `BLOCKED/AXIS_AUTHENTICATION`. It made zero network calls and imports no producer. The missing coordinates are not inferred from zeros. The 250-cell noise run remains blocked and no empirical noise ratio is available.

Read-only review of the control runner and verifier found independent covariance and complete-vector bootstrap arithmetic. Suggested checks for complete group/key sets, full metadata equality and exact source-range provenance were incorporated before the control freeze. The deterministic shadow router uses strict typed receipt records, rejects evaluator/private fields, makes no purchase and returns no predicted benefit. With the actual blocked axis receipt it must recommend axis authentication. This is contract validation, not a new biological result or an agent advantage.
