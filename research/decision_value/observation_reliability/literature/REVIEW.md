## Useful ideas and boundaries

rMFBO protects a primary-source optimization baseline when auxiliary sources are unreliable. Its theorem assumes a target/source multi-output GP with known covariance, a compact convex domain, differentiability and derivative-tail controls, and a sufficiently smooth acquisition with a definite Hessian at its optimum. A final pseudo-observation must be converted to an actual primary observation. Crucially, Theorem 1 compares robust budget `Lambda + lambda_primary` against primary-only budget `Lambda`: it is not a same-budget theorem. A finite action menu, learned misspecified residual covariance and unknown shared plate noise do not automatically satisfy its assumptions. The transferable design is baseline preservation plus source rejection and explicit verification costs.

Input-dependent MFBO models fidelity through source-specific input-dependent Gaussian noise and marginalizes uncertainty in the noise. Its guarantees assume a correctly specified GP target, compact convex domain, derivative bounds and bounded source noise; derivations use independent observation noise. The transferable design is to track source and condition reliability separately and compare against constant-noise models. A systematic plate shift or a shared DMSO reference is correlated bias/covariance, and cannot silently become independent heteroscedastic noise.

muscat states that multi-sample analyses seek sample-level inference because the experimental units are samples, accounting for sample-to-sample and cell-to-cell variability. This supports preserving source sample membership and using pseudobulk or explicit sample effects. It does not authenticate experimental samples as independent cultures or justify increasing the replicate count by splitting cells.

Systema shows that genetic perturbation benchmarks can reward systematic shifts captured by simple average-treatment baselines. It motivates strong mean/matching baselines and perturbation-specific diagnostics in MAESTRO. Its genetic perturbation setting does not establish chemical-drug action gains, biological mechanism attribution or source-repeat calibration.

BioPert's authenticated abstract describes a measured gene-expression response in a reference cell line as a transferable biological compound representation, used with a small network to predict target-cell transcriptional changes. It explicitly compares against chemical structure representations and copying the reference response. This suggests a relevant future control once affordable matched reference responses exist. It is a recent preprint; full methods, train/test exclusions, transformation details and replicate structure were not verified here. Abstract performance numbers are author claims and are not copied into MAESTRO result tables.

STATE #279 reports a missing ordered 2,000-HVG name list and positional consumption of `X_hvg` in ST-HVG inference. This is a reproducibility report by an issue author, not an upstream release certificate. An independent numerical audit may establish the original dataset's 39 endpoint coordinates while leaving all-2,000 identity and checkpoint output semantics unresolved.

## Metadata source and repeat meaning

The [Tahoe release README](https://huggingface.co/datasets/tahoebio/Tahoe-100M/blob/2dc57900b7981cfcf5e211527169a0b006546a95/README.md), freshly pinned at revision `2dc57900b7981cfcf5e211527169a0b006546a95`, describes the plate as a plate containing a seeded/treated mixed-cell spheroid and the sample identifier as distinguishing treatment replicates. Official sample metadata is only 65,636 bytes and its SHA256 matches the release LFS hash. Every proposed treated sentinel and registered P0.5 control sample/plate/condition joins exactly. The filtered row selection remains the pinned/hash-checked `pairwise_v3/data_availability/AVAILABILITY.json`.

The original [Tahoe v2 full text](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/biorxiv/early/2025/04/23/2025.02.20.639398.full) was retrieved through an alternative publisher URL after the canonical route was rate-limited. Methods specify 24 hours of drug-media exposure at 37C and 5% CO2. Results explicitly describe plate14 as a biological replicate of plate6. `SOURCE_QUALIFICATION.json` retains exact quotations, primary byte hashes, official sample joins and the distinction between global protocol time and absent per-sample timestamps. The published successor is [Cell DOI 10.1016/j.cell.2026.08.035](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2026.08.035); its bibliographic metadata was verified, but its full Methods were inaccessible. The time/repeat qualification therefore cites the verified v2 methods rather than pretending to inspect the successor's Methods.

`METADATA_REPEATS.json` independently counts every candidate stratum in the three exposed complete-menu files. Each file has 146 labels, exactly two distinct plate groups per label and one sample ID per treated stratum. The two selected cell-line partitions reuse sample IDs because cell-line-specific subsets come from the same pooled source sample. The proposed six-sentinel, two-line, two-plate panel contains 24 cell-line strata but only 12 distinct plate/sample IDs. The eight control strata represent four distinct pooled samples. Neither cells nor line strata define independent culture counts. Global protocol time and author-described plate6/14 repeat type are now verified; culture preparation, per-sample timing and new independent confirmation remain unresolved.

The prospective sentinel and shadow designs are in `../P06_DESIGN.md`. They require a separate freeze before treated response acquisition. This review requested no treated RNA, target B outcomes, LLM calls or STATE inference.

The authenticated Tahoe Methods also excluded NCI-H661, NCI-H596 and NCI-H2122 from subsequent analyses because of low counts, and required at least 50 cells per condition. This source quality finding motivated a **design-only** change from c40/c44 to c44/c45, keeping the same metadata hash rule and requiring at least 50 full-QC cells per selected stratum. `UPDATED_SENTINELS.json` preserves that chronology and supersedes the initial proposal before any treated RNA is read. It does not retroactively remove low-count contexts from v3 or claim that every retained cell in those lines is invalid.
